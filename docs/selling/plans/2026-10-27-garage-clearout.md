## How it's packaged — and why
- **Singles, each with its own listing** (6 listings) — six search surfaces instead of two; each title carries the
  brand, line, material, colour, size. Small pieces (loveseat, rug, side table, nightstand) are SUV-haulable and say so
  in the title — that is the no-truck buyer pool, and it's bigger than the truck pool.
- **Two set listings** on top ("Ashley Porter Living Room Set — 3 Pieces" and "Hollywood Sofa Set + 8x11 Rug") for
  the room-furnishing buyer (first apartment, rental, STR host). They pay set price for one trip.
- **One "moving sale — everything must go this weekend" post in the neighbourhood groups** (not Marketplace) with all
  photos and the three bundle prices. Group buyers are local, have trucks, and buy multiples.
- **A pickup window:** "Pickup Sat 9–1 or Sun 1–5 in Keller — come see everything." Truck owners are free on weekends;
  a fixed window turns lookers into a line and kills the no-show problem. Weekday evenings by appointment.
