## 2. Limits and traps

| Carrier | Max weight | Max size | Over-limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| USPS Ground Advantage / Priority | 70 lb | 130 in L+G (Priority 108 in) | refused |
| UPS / FedEx Ground | 150 lb | 108 in length, 165 in L+G | "Over Maximum Limits" $1,875/shipment (UPS) — may be returned or sent to freight ([Sifted](https://sifted.com/resources/ups-large-package-surcharges-in-2026-how-to-cut-costs/)) |

Girth = 2 × (W + H). L+G = L + 2W + 2H.

**Dimensional weight.** Billed weight = max(actual, L×W×H ÷ divisor), inches rounded *up*. Divisor is now **139 for USPS, UPS and FedEx** (USPS was 166 before Jul 12, 2026; UPS retail counters still 166). USPS applies it only above 1,728 in³ (1 cu ft); UPS/FedEx apply it to everything ([DCL](https://dclcorp.com/blog/shipping/usps-dim-weight-changes-2026/), [Quadient](https://www.quadient.com/en-us/postage-rates/ups)). **Worked example:** a 24 × 18 × 12 box = 5,184 in³ ÷ 139 = 37.3 → **bills as 38 lb** at 6 lb actual (would have been 32 lb under 166). Z8 that is ≈$95 Ground Advantage or ≈$75 UPS — a lamp in a big box costs more than a 30 lb anvil in a small one. Shrink the box before anything else.

**Cubic pricing (USPS).** Ground Advantage and Priority have volume-priced tiers for boxes ≤ 1 cu ft, ≤ 20 lb, longest side ≤ 22 in (was 18 before Jul 2026): 10 lb Z5 is $9.37 cubic vs $16.76 weight-based ([OnlineShippingCalculator](https://onlineshippingcalculator.com/guides/ground-advantage-retail-vs-commercial-guide)). Pirate Ship applies it automatically; eBay Labels generally does not (general knowledge, verify) — for dense small boxes (books, coins, cast iron, tools) buy the label on Pirate Ship.

**Oversize surcharges.** USPS "nonstandard" fees $4.50–$21 for length over 22 in, over 30 in, or volume over 2 cu ft ([Shippo](https://goshippo.com/blog/usps-parcel-select-ground-cost-sizes-and-how-it-works)). UPS Additional Handling 2026: $30–40.50 for longest side > 48 in or second side > 30 in (40 lb minimum billable), $46.50–58.75 over 50 lb, $26.75–33.75 for non-corrugated/odd packaging — tubes, wood crates, anything not in a box ([Refund Retriever](https://www.refundretriever.com/blog/ups-additional-handling-charge)). UPS Large Package 2026: longest side > 96 in, L+G > 130 in, > 17,280 in³ or > 110 lb → **$219.50–286 commercial, $254.50–331 residential**, 90 lb minimum billable ([Sifted](https://sifted.com/resources/ups-large-package-surcharges-in-2026-how-to-cut-costs/)). FedEx uses the same triggers; Oversize Z3–4 Home Delivery $275 ([Supply Chain Dive](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/fedex-2026-gri-shipping-rate-increase/760011/), [ParcelPath](https://parcelpath.com/shipping-companies/fedex/fedex-shipping-fees/)). Practical ceiling for a parcel: **~48 in longest side, ~50 lb, ~130 in L+G**; above that you are paying $30–330 in surcharges and should be on freight or local.

**When freight starts.** Anything over 150 lb, 108 in, or 165 in L+G, or any furniture piece over the ~130 in L+G surcharge line. Playbook §3 says "$150–300 cross-country dresser" — **that is low.** 2026 sources: small 3-drawer dresser $300–600, 5–6 drawer $500–800, LTL/uShip ≈ $0.60–1.00/mile for the first 500 mi ([Red Stag](https://redstagfulfillment.com/how-to-ship-a-dresser/)); uShip single pieces listed $125–160 (mirror), $300 (sideboard KC→Georgetown TX), $650 (one piece Aubrey TX→GA), $750 (tansu CA→MN) ([uShip](https://www.uship.com/cost-to-ship/household-goods/page/6)). Reconciled rule: **$150–300 is a regional (<500 mi, uShip/Facebook hauler) number; cross-country single piece is $300–600 blanket-wrap uShip, $500–900 LTL palletized.** Update playbook §3 accordingly.

Moving boxes are 32 ECT single-wall — fine as an outer box, not as the only box for glass. Uline/ULINE 200#/275# double-wall for heavy or fragile.

**Stock six sizes:** 8 × 6 × 4 (jewelry, coins, small electronics) · 12 × 10 × 8 (books, cameras, shoes) · 14 × 14 × 14 (ceramics inner box, small lamps) · 18 × 18 × 16 (outer box for the 14-cube, turntables, quilts) · 24 × 18 × 12 (framed art flat, large textiles) · 20 × 20 × 20 double-wall (outer box for large glass). Plus poly mailers 10 × 13 and 14 × 17, and #0/#2 bubble mailers.

**Double-boxing rule:** inner box sized so the wrapped item has 1–2 in of fill on all six sides and cannot move (shake test); outer box 4–6 in larger in every dimension so the inner box floats in **2–3 in of fill all round** — bottom layer first, inner box centered, fill sides and top, then press-test: if you feel the inner box through the wall, add more ([LateShipment](https://www.lateshipment.com/blog/shipping-fragile-items/)). Three strips of tape on each seam, H-pattern.

**Fill:** kraft paper for void fill and wrapping anything hard (cheap, recyclable, doesn't scratch); bubble wrap 2–3 layers directly on glass/ceramic/lacquer; foam sheet or polyethylene foam between stacked plates and as the first wrap on anything with a finish bubble can mark; air pillows only for light goods (they pop under 20 lb+ loads); peanuts only inside a sealed inner box (they migrate). For ceramics and art glass: fill hollows with paper, bubble-wrap with handles/spouts padded separately, then paper, inner box, then peanuts or crumpled kraft between boxes. Never let two hard items touch.

## 5. Insurance and claims

- Ground Advantage, Priority and Priority Express each include **$100**; up to $5,000 purchasable on any of them; Registered Mail to $50,000 ([ParcelPath](https://parcelpath.com/shipping-companies/usps/usps-claims-and-support/)).
- Add insurance when (item price − $100) × probability of damage > the premium — in practice everything fragile over $150 and everything over $300. Through eBay Labels, USPS/UPS insurance is bought on the label; eBay's own ShipCover is no longer offered (general knowledge, verify).
- **Signature confirmation is mandatory at $750+ total (item + shipping + tax)** or eBay Seller Protection does not cover an Item Not Received claim ([eBay](https://export.ebay.com/en/shipping/signature-confirmation/)).
- Claims: damage — file immediately; lost — after 15 days (7 for Express), **no later than 60 days** from mailing. Needs the label record, proof of value (the eBay order), photos of item and packaging; the buyer must keep the box until USPS inspects. Decision in 5–10 days, payment 7–10 business days after ([ParcelPath](https://parcelpath.com/shipping-companies/usps/usps-claims-and-support/)). UPS/FedEx via eBay: open the claim from the order page; same evidence.
- **Photograph every pack job**: item, wrapped item, inner box in fill, closed box on the scale showing weight, label applied. Four photos, 30 seconds, wins every INAD and claim.

## 7. Local alternatives

**Say "local pickup only" when:** over 30 lb actual *or* DIM; over 108 in L+G (surcharge territory starts at 130, but 108 is where the box is already $40+ and a single dent is a claim); any glass or mirror over 20 in on a side; marble, slate or stone tops; upholstered or case furniture; anything where the Z5 label exceeds 25% of the expected price (e.g. a $60 lamp that costs $22 to ship). Local buyers pay 20–40% less than shipped buyers (playbook §3) — that discount is still cheaper than a $300 freight bill on a $400 dresser.

**DFW movers for the rare high-value piece:** uShip (post the item, blanket-wrap haulers bid; $150–300 regional, $300–750 cross-country single piece); Lugg / Dolly / TaskRabbit for same-day in-metro moves, roughly $80–180 for a two-person pickup truck within 25 mi (general knowledge, verify); LTL via Freightquote/uShip Freight for palletized $500+ items — palletize and shrink-wrap yourself at ~$30 or pay a terminal; FedEx/UPS Store pack-and-ship takes big fragile items but charges $50–150 for the packing alone (general knowledge, verify).
