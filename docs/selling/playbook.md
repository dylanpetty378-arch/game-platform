## 1. Why a listing gets no messages (diagnose before touching price)
Read the listing's stats first (Marketplace → Selling → Listings: Clicks / Saves / Messages; or the Meta "Seller" app).

| Clicks after 24–48 h | Saves | Messages | What it is | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ~0 | 0 | 0 | **Not being shown** | Account Status + Support Inbox; search the exact title from another account; delete & relist with a new first photo; post to groups |
| dozens–hundreds | some | 0 | **Seen, not trusted or wanted** | Real photos, honest specific flaws, dimensions, price near comps (not suspiciously low), profile looks established |
| many | many | 0 | **Interest, no trigger** | 5–10% price drop (notifies everyone who saved it) |

Rules of thumb from sellers: <2 views 12 h after posting = dead listing; a few dozen clicks and no message is normal
(rental listings run ~0.6% click→message); hundreds of clicks and nothing = price/trust/photos.

**Under-priced + zero messages is almost never a price problem.** Cheap items get flooded. Zero means invisible or
untrusted. A price far under market on nice furniture reads as a scam (BBB names furniture as a top Marketplace scam
category) — and since March 2026 buyers see an AI summary of the seller's tenure, friends, history and ratings.

## 2. Facebook Marketplace mechanics (what the platform rewards)
- **Freshness is ranked.** New listings get the strongest exposure, then it decays (Meta publishes no curve). **Delete
  and relist beats Renew**: Renew (available at day 7, ≤5 times) keeps the ID and gives a smaller bump; a fresh listing
  resets age. Delete the old one first (two live copies = duplicate flag). Apartment Therapy's sectional sat 9 months
  through renews and cuts, sold a month after a plain repost.
- **Title = search surface.** Buyers search brand, material, colour, style, size: "West Elm", "walnut", "velvet",
  "mid century", "blue sofa", "sofa set", "8x10 rug". "Couch, Loveseat, Ottoman" matches nothing anyone types.
  Pattern: **Brand + line + type + material/colour + size + condition word.**
- **First photo is the thumbnail.** Whole piece, centred, filling the frame, straight-on or 45°, window light, 1× lens
  (never 0.5×), camera 3–4 ft high for a sofa, clutter gone. Then 5–10 more: fabric/finish close-up, legs, back,
  underside/label, every flaw. Label flaws in the text ("see photo 4").
- **Body answers every question before it's asked:** dimensions first line; what it is exactly; condition with named
  flaws; smoke/pet status (if true); reason for selling; pickup area; "needs a truck / fits an SUV with seats down";
  "we'll help load"; cash/Venmo/Zelle on pickup; "first to pick up gets it, no holds."
- **Price position:** at or just under the current local asks for the same thing, padded 10–15% for haggling; round
  numbers; "firm" when already at market (OBO at market invites lowballs); OBO after 2 weeks. A 5%+ drop notifies savers.
- **List in more places** (three-dot menu) → 8–12 local buy/sell groups (Keller, Southlake, Colleyville, NRH, Roanoke,
  Trophy Club, Haslet, Watauga). Vary the opening line per group; identical text posted fast gets spam-flagged. One
  seller: 3 inquiries in 10 days on Marketplace alone → 14 sales in 9 days after 11 groups.
- **Spam/automation triggers that cut whole-account reach:** 10 listings in an hour, identical text, frequent edits,
  stock photos, phone number in the body, automation tools. (The Facebook sweep extension runs on the home computer
  with Dylan's account — keep selling listings few and hand-written, and post them slowly.)
- **Settings:** Message Requests = Everyone; turn on Meta AI auto-reply for "Is this available?"; location = the
  actual pickup area so radius filters include you.
- **Boost:** $5–30 fixed-budget local boost, only after the listing is fixed, only on bulky local items ≥ ~$150.
  Never the "Cost per Sale" shipped boost (ran $400–600/day in an Oct 2026 test). Check daily budget on the confirm screen.
- **Timing (seller opinion, not data):** Thursday 7–8 am or 5–6 pm for weekend buyers; Sunday afternoon/evening.
- **Fees:** local pickup 0%; shipped 10% (min $0.80), and FB no longer sells labels. OfferUp local 0%, local-only
  since Oct 2025. Nextdoor For Sale & Free: free, verified neighbours, 10 photos.

dresser). Local buyers pay 20–40% less than shipped buyers — use local for time and zero fees, not for top price.

## 4. Pricing and markdown rhythm
- **Commodity items with many comps** (mass-market sofas, IKEA, kitchen, tools): price at/just under sold comps on
  day 1, pad 10–15%. Views spike at listing and taper — early viewers never see a later cut.
- **Unique / collector items**: anchor high, enable offers from day 1, cut in big steps (collectors ignore small ones).
- **Facebook cadence:** day 7 check stats (table §1); day 14 delete & relist with a new first photo; 5%+ cut to ping
  savers; day 21 bundle; day 30+ delete & relist again or move channel; past 60 days it's dead stock — lot it, consign
  it, or donate it.
- **eBay used stock:** −10% at 45 days, −10% at 90.
- **Household clear-out floor:** anything worth under $25 → donate or "free, must pick up by Saturday" (bed-bug
  wariness still applies to free upholstery). Donation has **$0 tax value unless you itemize** (2026 non-itemizer
  deduction is cash-only); itemizers deduct thrift-store FMV, Form 8283 above $500.

## 5. No-truck, no-delivery sellers (us): making the buyer's haul easy
The #1 reason couch sales fall through is the buyer arriving alone or without a truck. We can't deliver, so:
- Say exactly what fits what: sofa 80" needs a pickup bed or trailer; a 61" loveseat fits most SUVs with the seats
  down; an 8x11 rug rolls to ~100" and fits in a sedan diagonally or any SUV.
- "We'll help load" and "two of us here" in the body. A $19.95 U-Haul / Home Depot truck rental is the buyer's
  delivery option — mention it ("Home Depot in Keller rents trucks by the hour").
- Price so a $40–60 rental still leaves a deal.
- Split big sets into haulable pieces with a set price — a loveseat alone opens the SUV-owner pool.
