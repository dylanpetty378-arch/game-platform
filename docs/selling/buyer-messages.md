# Buyer messages — scripts, scams, no-shows, pickup (Oct 28 2026)

How to answer any buyer message fast and safely. Listing, pricing and channel rules are in
[`playbook.md`](playbook.md); platform mechanics and sources in
[`../../research/selling/marketplace-mechanics-2026-10-27.md`](../../research/selling/marketplace-mechanics-2026-10-27.md).
Standing constraints: Keller TX, no truck, no delivery, full-time job, pickup times set one buyer at a time.

Voice for every reply: short, plain, friendly, no exclamation marks. Keep the thread on the platform
(Messenger/OfferUp/eBay) so there is a record.

## 1. Reply scripts

| Message | Reply | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| "Is this available?" | "Yes, still available. Pickup is in Keller, cash or Venmo/Zelle at pickup. When could you come by?" | Meta AI auto-reply (on since Mar 2026) sends "Yes, it's still available, do you have any questions?" to every availability ping; edit the draft to add pickup area and the hours you can't do. It handles only the first message. [aphnetworks](https://aphnetworks.com/news/31470-still-available-facebook-marketplace-now-lets-meta-ai-respond) |
| "What's your lowest?" | "The price is $X. If you can pick up this week I can do $Y." (Y = accept price, §5) | Never answer with a number lower than the accept price. Decide the floor before listing. [Karrot](https://blog.karrotmarket.com/2024/10/07/lowball-offers-response/) |
| Lowball (under floor) | Day 0–2: "Thanks, I'm firm at $X for now." Day 3+: counter once at accept price: "I can do $Y if you pick up by Sunday." | Counter once, then stop. Never reply to insults or "that's not worth that"; archive. |
| "Can you deliver?" | "I can't deliver. Home Depot in Keller rents trucks and vans from $19 for 75 minutes, or Lugg will pick it up for you (two movers, about $75 for a short run). We'll help load here." | Buyer books Lugg, not us. [Home Depot](https://www.homedepot.com/c/Truck_Rental), [Lugg Dallas](https://lugg.com/dallas-tx-facebook-marketplace-delivery) |
| "Can you hold it?" | "I don't do holds, first person to pick up gets it. If you can come at [slot], I'll put you first." | Exception: a deposit through Venmo/Zelle that we would accept anyway; then it's a sale with a pickup date, not a hold. Never "pay extra to hold" (§2). |
| "Does it fit in an SUV?" | "It's L x W x H in. Loveseat length fits most SUVs with the seats down; an 84 in sofa needs a full-size SUV, a truck bed or a trailer." | Always answer with the numbers; buyer decides. Fit table in §4. |
| Scheduling | "I can do Thu 6–7 pm or Sat 9–11 am. Which works? I'll send the address once we confirm a time." | Two slots, buyer picks. Address only after a confirmed time. |
| Morning-of confirm | "Still good for 6 pm today? Address is [street], gray house, pull into the driveway." | Send the morning of; if no reply by 2 h before, offer the slot to the next buyer. |
| Day-of reminder | "See you at 6. Cash or Venmo/Zelle at pickup, and bring straps or blankets if you want them." | |
| No-show | "Looks like today didn't work. I'm offering it to the next person; message me if you still want it and can come tomorrow." | One message. No second chase. Block repeat flakes. |
| After sale | Mark sold immediately. "Thanks, enjoy it. If you have a minute, a rating on Marketplace helps me a lot." | Ratings feed the AI seller summary buyers see since Mar 2026 (playbook §1). |
| Bundle upsell | "Also listed: [item] $X and [item] $Y. Take both with this and I'll do $Z for all." | Send when a pickup is confirmed, not before. |

## 2. Scam patterns (2025–2026) and the one-line tell

The rule that beats all of them: **cash, Venmo or Zelle at pickup, in person, confirmed in our own app; never ship a local item; never send money to receive money; never give codes.**

| Pattern | Tell | Reply |
|---|---|---|
| Zelle/Venmo "business account upgrade" | An email "from Zelle" says you must pay $300+ to upgrade before funds release; buyer offers to refund it. Zelle never sends this. [WJAR Jun 2025](https://www.turnto10.com/i-team/consumer-advocate/expert-warns-of-new-scam-on-facebook-marketplace-zelle-email-business-account-check-difference-sellers-buyers-payment-june-17-2025), [WRDW Aug 2026](https://www.wrdw.com/2026/08/17/what-tech-facebook-marketplace-scam-tricks-sellers-into-refunds/) | "Payment at pickup only." Then block. |
| Overpayment / refund the difference | "I accidentally sent too much, please send back $X." The original payment never existed or will bounce. [Commerce Bank 2025](https://www.commercebank.com/personal/ideas-and-tips/2025/online-marketplace-scams) | No reply. Never refund anything. |
| "My mover/courier will pick up, send me your email" | Pays full price, no questions, wants your email to "send payment", third party collects. [MalwareTips](https://malwaretips.com/blogs/facebook-marketplace-scam-buyers-how-fake-buyers-trick-sellers/) | "Local pickup by the buyer, payment in person." Block. |
| Google Voice verification code | "To prove you're real, read me the code I just texted you." It links a Google Voice number to your phone for their scams. [Commerce Bank](https://www.commercebank.com/personal/ideas-and-tips/2025/online-marketplace-scams) | Never. Block and report. |
| "Send a photo of the serial / receipt" | On electronics: serial can be used to report the device stolen or to file warranty claims; receipts get reused for return fraud. [MacRumors thread](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/selling-on-craigslist-should-i-give-out-the-serial-upon-request.974369/) | "You can check the serial in person at pickup." Share purchase date only. |
| Fake payment screenshot / "pending" | Screenshot or email says paid or pending; your app shows nothing. "A screenshot of a payment receipt means nothing." [Commerce Bank](https://www.commercebank.com/personal/ideas-and-tips/2025/online-marketplace-scams), [MalwareTips Venmo](https://malwaretips.com/blogs/?p=409011) | "It's not showing in my app. Cash at pickup is fine." Item stays until money is in our balance, not pending. |
| Move to text/WhatsApp at once | First message asks for your number or email. Takes the thread off the platform record. [WRDW](https://www.wrdw.com/2026/08/17/what-tech-facebook-marketplace-scam-tricks-sellers-into-refunds/) | "Happy to keep it here." If they insist, block. |
| "I'll pay extra to hold it" | Offers above asking for a hold or a deposit via a link or app you didn't name. Any fee or link is the hook. | "No holds, first to pick up gets it." |
| FB "Is this still available? Please contact me at <email>" | Boilerplate first message with an email address. It's the courier/upgrade scam's opener. [MalwareTips](https://malwaretips.com/blogs/facebook-marketplace-scam-buyers-how-fake-buyers-trick-sellers/) | No reply. Archive. |

Profile check before giving the address: account age, friends, prior ratings, real photos. A new, empty profile that
agrees to full price with no questions is the standard fake-buyer shape. [WRDW](https://www.wrdw.com/2026/08/17/what-tech-facebook-marketplace-scam-tricks-sellers-into-refunds/)

## 3. No-show prevention

No hard no-show statistics exist; seller consensus on r/FacebookMarketplace is "most people don't show up or stop
responding," and the tactics below are what sellers report actually cut it.
[r/FBM Jan 2026](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1q8f7oa/snapshots/2026-01-10T04%3A10%3A48.144662Z),
[r/FBM Feb 2026](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1qxu7d7/snapshots/2026-02-07T05%3A54%3A53.787812Z)

- **No holds, no deposits.** The item goes to whoever confirms a specific day and time first; "pending" means a
  time is set, not that it's sold. Mark sold only with money in hand.
- **Prioritise the buyer who names a time.** "Is this available?" is a one-tap default; the buyer who writes a real
  question or proposes a slot is the one who shows.
- **Address only after a confirmed time**, and send the cross streets or a nearby intersection until then. The seller
  who gave the address freely "regretted it every time"; those who release it when the buyer is on the way report
  rare no-shows.
- **Confirm the morning of** (script §1). Silence by 2 h before = slot goes to the next buyer.
- **Listing states the exact pickup area** (Keller, 76248, near [landmark]) so buyers self-select by drive.
- **Porch pickup for items under ~$50**: item on the porch, buyer pays by Venmo/Zelle before you set it out, or leaves
  cash under the mat / in a box. Several sellers run most sales this way with "the occasional no-show." Risk is on the
  buyer side (fake sellers send people to strangers' porches), so a buyer who hesitates to prepay is being sensible;
  offer in-person instead. [Fox 5 Atlanta](https://fox5atlanta.com/news/woman-warns-others-about-porch-pickups-after-caught-up-in-scam)
- **Safe-exchange lots for small or high-value items** (phones, tools, jewelry). Cameras, no address given:

| City | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Keller | Keller PD, 330 Rufe Snow Dr. Station open 24 h; lobby Mon–Thu 7:30 am–5:30 pm, closed Fri | No marked exchange zone found on the city site; use the front lot or lobby. [City of Keller](https://www.cityofkeller.com/services/police) |
| Southlake | DPS HQ, 600 State St — two marked spaces, SE corner, far-right row on Division St, 24 h cameras; lobby Mon–Fri 8–5 | [Southlake Style](https://www.southlakestyle.com/community/city/southlake-police-create-exchange-zone) |
| North Richland Hills | NRH PD, 4301 City Point Dr, north parking lot — two marked spaces | [NRH Exchange Zone](https://www.nrhtx.com/892/Exchange-Zone) |
| Grapevine | Public Safety Bldg, 1027 Ira E. Woods Ave — marked "E-Commerce Exchange Zone" spots out front, 24 h cameras | [Dallas Morning News](https://dallasnews.com/news/2020/06/30/online-buyers-and-sellers-welcome-at-police-hq-grapevine-says) |

Furniture stays at the house; a police lot doesn't help with a sofa.

## 4. Pickup logistics when we can't deliver

**Vehicle fit** (tell the buyer the item's dimensions and let them check; interior figures from
[itemfits SUV](https://itemfits.com/fit/vehicle/sofa-suv) and [itemfits pickup](https://itemfits.com/fit/vehicle/couch-pickup)):

| Vehicle | Usable length | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Sedan | trunk + folded seat, ~60 in diagonal | rolled rugs, boxes, chairs, small tables |
| Compact SUV, seats down | 70–75 in × 44 in wide | loveseat (52–60 in), dresser on its side, nightstands |
| Mid-size SUV, seats down | 75–85 in | sofa under ~80 in, most dressers |
| Full-size SUV, seats down | 85–95 in | 84 in sofa |
| Pickup 6.5 ft bed | 78 in; tailgate down adds 18–24 in | 84 in sofa with overhang, strapped |
| Pickup 8 ft bed | 97 in | 84 in sofa flat |
| Trailer | anything | sectionals, sets, mattresses + frame |

**At the house:**
- "Two of us will help load" means carry to the vehicle and lift in. The buyer secures the load. Say in the
  listing and the confirm message: **straps and blankets are not provided, bring your own.**
- Stage the item in the garage or driveway before the slot so the buyer never comes inside; disassemble what we can
  (legs off, drawers out) and bag the hardware to the piece.
- Dog put up, kids inside, driveway clear so they can back in.
- Payment before loading: count cash; for Venmo/Zelle, open our own app and see the amount in the balance. A buyer's
  screen, a "pending" line, or an email is not payment. Decline checks and gift cards.
- Mark sold in the app before they pull out, then cancel any other threads with one line.

## 5. Negotiation rules

| Days listed | Accept | Reply to offers |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | list price (firm) | Decline below list; "firm for now." |
| 3–6 | 10–15% under list | Counter once at accept price; take first offer at or above it. |
| 7+ | mark OBO; accept = floor set at listing | Take any offer ≥ floor; counter once below it; relist per playbook §4. |

- **Bulky items (sofas, dressers, exercise gear): take the first offer at or above accept, every time.** The next
  buyer with a truck may be two weeks away.
- **Bundles:** 10% off the sum for two items, 15–20% for three or more, only when it clears the garage in one pickup.
- **Free with purchase:** attach anything worth under $25 (playbook §4 floor) to close a stalled bulky sale instead of
  cutting price.
- **"What's your lowest?"** always gets the accept price with a pickup condition, never the floor.
- Never negotiate at the door. The price in the thread is the price; a buyer who arrives and asks for less is told
  the agreed number once, then thanked for coming.

## 6. Response time

No platform publishes a reply-speed-to-sale curve. What exists:

- Facebook's "Very responsive" badge requires replying to ~80% of messages within an average of one hour; the badge is
  shown on the commerce profile buyers check. [OneShop](https://oneshop.com/blog/facebook-marketplace-badges)
- A high-volume reseller (1,300+ listings) reports responsiveness as a local-feed ranking factor, a 40% reach drop
  after three days of ignored messages, and that engagement speed "seems to stay the strongest predictor of sales."
  Personal observation, not measured data. [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/optimization-growth-strategies/fb-marketplace-app-complete-guide-what-high-volume-resellers-really-need-to-know)
- Vendor claims elsewhere (replies under 10 minutes win 2–3× more) are unsourced. [GoFindPart](https://gofindpart-marketing.pages.dev/blog/response-speed-impacts-seller-win-rate)

Working rule: first reply within the hour during the day, within 15 minutes in the evening when buyers plan weekend
pickups; turn the Meta AI auto-reply on so the first touch is instant; a scheduling reply with two slots beats a fast
"yes" with no next step.
