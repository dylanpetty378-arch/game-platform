Dylan (Oct 28): "continue the encyclopedia of resell, with a focus on the selling aspect; first strategize on what
would improve it the most and what it's missing." This is that audit. The buy side is 663 type entries; the sell side
is a playbook, a procedure, three research briefs, two plans and a 12-row log. The first live test (nine pieces,
garage clear-out) exposed the gaps in the order below.

## What's missing, ranked by how much it would have changed the last 24 hours
1. **A sell-side encyclopedia for the things Dylan actually sells locally.** The buy side excludes everything bigger
   than a passenger seat, so sofas, beds, rugs, dressers, appliances, exercise gear, kids' gear — the household
   families — have no entry at all. Every clear-out piece was researched from scratch on the night. →
   `household-families.md` (16 families: search words, title formula, % of retail, haul, photos, flaws, channel,
   clock, legal).
2. **Buyer-message handling.** Selling fast is mostly answering fast and safely: scripts for the ten messages every
   listing gets, the 2026 scam patterns, no-show prevention, pickup logistics for a seller who can't deliver,
   negotiation rules by day. Nothing existed. → `buyer-messages.md`.
3. **Platform gotchas learned the hard way**, which cost half an hour on Oct 27–28: Facebook title limit 100
   characters ("Input Title is invalid"); a set listing reusing the singles' photos is rejected as a duplicate;
   location must be picked from the suggestions; "List in your groups" is empty until the account joins groups and
   some groups never appear in the share dialog; nine posts in an hour is the spam line. → `playbook.md` §2 now
   carries a "hard limits" table; new ones go there the day they bite.
4. **The "Selling it" section in every type entry is thin.** It should carry what the listing needs: the title
   formula (which 5–8 words buyers type), the platform and price position, the photos that matter for that type,
   packing/haul, and the one fact to quote to a buyer. → template upgraded (README) and `enc.py lint` now flags
   sections under 60 words; entries get deepened as they're touched, highest-traffic types first.
5. **No feedback loop.** The log exists but nothing reads it. → `scripts/sell.py stats` (days listed, clicks per
   day, click→message rate, % of list achieved, by family) so the playbook's numbers come from Dylan's own sales,
   not forum anecdotes. The first real rows are the nine clear-out pieces.
6. **Seasonality.** When each family sells: toys Oct–Nov, exercise gear January, patio March–May, dorm/apartment
   furniture July–August, moving season May–September. → calendar table in `playbook.md` §4.
7. **Shipped-sale packing and cost reference** (box sizes, dimensional weight, USPS Ground Advantage vs UPS by
   weight, what to double-box) for the eBay side — the entries say "$35–60 to ship" without saying how. → next
   session; needs a current rate table.
8. **Photo and staging guide** as its own page (driveway daylight, 1× lens, the edit recipe used on Oct 27). →
   next session; the recipe is in `plans/2026-10-27-garage-clearout.md` for now.
