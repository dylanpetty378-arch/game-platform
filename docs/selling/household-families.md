# Household families — the sell-side encyclopedia (bulky goods, local DFW)

Written 2026-10-28. Companion to `docs/selling/playbook.md` and the briefs `research/selling/by-item-family-2026-10-27.md` (BIF) and `research/selling/dfw-channels-2026-10-27.md` (DFW); cited by section, not repeated. Constraints everywhere: Keller TX, no truck, no delivery, full-time job, Dylan's FB account.

**Applies to every family (not repeated below)**
- Title ≤ 100 characters or Facebook rejects it ("Input Title is invalid"). Pattern: brand + line + type + material/colour + size + condition word (playbook §2). No "OBO", emojis, "must go".
- First photo = thumbnail: whole piece, centred, 1× lens, window light, clutter gone (BIF §15). Flaw shots last, named in the body ("see photo 5").
- Body: dimensions first line; haul line ("needs a pickup; two of us to help load"); named flaws; smoke/pet status only if true; pickup area; "cash/Venmo on pickup, no holds".
- Price at or just under live local asks, padded 10–15%, round numbers. Cadence: day 7 read stats, day 14 delete & relist with a new first photo, day 30 change channel, day 60 lot/donate (playbook §4). Under $25 → donate or "free, pick up by Saturday".
- Haul reference (general knowledge, verify): SUV seats-down cargo floor ≈ 70–75"; Home Depot Keller rents a truck by the hour; Lugg ≈ $130 for a 20-mile sofa run, buyer books (DFW §6).
- Brand tiers: **T1** IKEA/Wayfair/Ashley/Home Zone/Amazon; **T2** Pottery Barn/West Elm/Crate & Barrel/RH/Article/Ethan Allen; **T3** designer or collectible (Herman Miller, Knoll, Lane/Broyhill MCM, Brown Jordan).
- DFW exits (DFW §3–4, playbook §6): Salvation Army / Goodwill Fort Worth pickup (ZIP-based), Tarrant County Furniture Bank (Saturday pickups), Habitat ReStore (no stained/torn upholstery), Rusty Estate Sales "Sell a Few Things" (cash buyout or consignment), Tyler Grace Auctions (Addison, HiBid).

### Sofas & sectionals
- **Search words:** sofa, couch, sectional, loveseat; brand (Ashley, West Elm, Pottery Barn, La-Z-Boy); leather/velvet/linen/microfiber; colour; L-shaped/chaise/sleeper/reclining; width. Junk: "nice", "comfy", "furniture", "set" with no pieces named.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Line] [Material] [Colour] [Sofa/Sectional] [W"] – [condition]`. Ex (61): `West Elm Harmony Grey Velvet 3-Seat Sofa 82" – clean, no pets`.
- **Price vs. new:** by age — <1 yr 40–60%, 1–3 yrs 25–40%, 3–5 yrs 15–25%, 5+ yrs 10–20% (BIF §1). T2/T3 excellent 40–50%; T1 30–40%; pets/smoke −20% (DFW §7). A 3-yr branded $1,800 sofa listed $600–750, sold $450–650 (https://latestcost.com/how-price-furniture-facebook-marketplace/, Oct 2026). **DFW asks:** Craigslist sofas/sectionals mostly $65–375; leather sofa+loveseat $600 (DFW §5). New IKEA 3-seat is $449 delivered — a used T1 must sit well under it.
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — neutral grey/beige, leather, under 84", sectionals that split, T2 names. Sits — floral, U-sectionals, sleepers (weight), pilling, pet hair. Weakest resale family (DFW §7).
- **Haul:** 80"+ = pickup or trailer, 2 people, 100–200 lb; loveseat ≤ 62" fits most SUVs (playbook §5). Legs unscrew (say so). Sectionals split — set price in each single's body; a set listing needs its own group photo (playbook §2).
- **Photos:** straight-on at seat height first; then 45°, side, cushions lifted, deck under cushions, zippers open, legs, back, tag, every stain.
- **Flaws:** kill — odour, stains, pet damage, sagging frame, dead recliner mechanism, torn leather. Fine — pilling, leg scuffs, faded arms. "Non-smoking, no pets" only if true.
- **Channel:** FB + 8–12 local groups; free cross-post Craigslist/OfferUp/Nextdoor. Consignment rarely takes T1 and nets ~25% of tag (DFW §1). Exit: Salvation Army/Goodwill if clean; else haul-away.
- **Clock:** inquiries within 48 h if priced right; 3 weeks silent = overpriced (DFW §7). 2-week clock, then donate.

### Recliners & accent chairs
- **Search words:** recliner, accent chair, armchair, wingback, swivel, glider, power recliner, lift chair; brand (La-Z-Boy, Ashley, Pottery Barn); leather/boucle/velvet; colour.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Type] [Material] [Colour] [Power/Manual] – [condition]`. Ex (59): `La-Z-Boy Brown Leather Rocker Recliner – works, non-smoking`.
- **Price vs. new:** sofa age table; La-Z-Boy and power/lift chairs hold the top of it. Asks (general knowledge, verify): T1 accent chair $30–100; La-Z-Boy manual $75–200; power/lift $150–400; T2 accent chair $100–300.
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — single chairs (fit an SUV, so the pool is big), lift chairs (caregivers), matching pairs. Sits — stained fabric recliners, oversized "big man" recliners, jewel-tone wingbacks.
- **Haul:** one chair fits almost any SUV; 60–120 lb; power recliner backs lift straight off the base — say so.
- **Photos:** 45° front first; then reclined, mechanism/underside, cushion lifted, fabric close-up, cord/remote, flaws.
- **Flaws:** kill — broken mechanism/motor, odour, cracked seat leather. Fine — worn piping, faded back.
- **Channel:** FB + groups; Nextdoor for lift chairs. Exit: Salvation Army, Habitat if clean.
- **Clock:** 7–14 days; −10% at day 14; donate at day 30.

### Beds & bed frames (incl. Murphy/wall and platform)
- **Search words:** bed frame, platform bed, queen/king/full/twin, headboard, upholstered, storage bed, bunk bed, Murphy bed, wall bed; brand (IKEA Malm, Pottery Barn, West Elm, Zinus); solid wood/walnut/oak/metal.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Size] [Material/Colour] [Platform Bed/Bed Frame/Murphy Bed] – [condition]`. Ex (62): `Queen Solid Pine Platform Bed Frame – disassembles, slats incl`.
- **Price vs. new:** quality frames hold value better than upholstery (BIF §5); use the case-goods rule — T2 solid wood 40–60%, T1 $20–120. Asks (general knowledge, verify): IKEA Malm queen $50–120; metal/Zinus $30–80; T1 upholstered queen $80–200; T2 solid wood queen $300–700; Murphy bed with cabinets $500–1,500 vs $2–4k new, niche and slow. Antique Murphy beds are a separate collector market (https://appraisily.com/antique-murphy-bed-value/).
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — queen, neutral, shown disassembled with the hardware bag; bunk/twin kids' beds in August. Sits — king (fewer buyers, bigger haul), custom/unbranded, Murphy (buyer needs the wall and the skills), missing slats or bolts.
- **Haul:** disassembled queen fits any SUV; king headboard 76"+ needs a truck. Murphy: 200–400 lb, 2–3 people, unbolted from studs — "you remove from wall, bring a drill".
- **Photos:** assembled and made with a plain cover first; then headboard, slats, hardware bag, joints, the disassembled stack, flaws. Murphy: open, closed, mechanism.
- **Flaws:** kill — cracked rails, missing hardware, bed-bug signs, broken slats. Fine — scuffed legs, faded fabric.
- **Channel:** FB + groups. Exit: **Tarrant County Furniture Bank** (beds are a top need). Murphy beds: Habitat ReStore.
- **Clock:** queen T1/T2 7–14 days; king/custom/Murphy 30+. Cut at 14, give up at 30.
- **Legal:** none for frames. Bunk beds: check cpsc.gov recalls by brand (general knowledge, verify).

### Mattresses
- **Search words:** mattress, size, memory foam/hybrid/pillow top, brand (Tempur-Pedic, Purple, Casper, Sealy, Saatva), firmness, year. Junk: "barely used" with no year.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Model] [Size] [Type] Mattress [year] – clean, no stains`. Ex (53): `Tempur-Pedic ProAdapt Queen Memory Foam Mattress 2022`.
- **Price vs. new:** 20–30% of original, adjusted for age (https://puffy.com/blogs/best-sleep/sell-used-mattress-guide). Only premium names under ~3 yrs clear $150+; T1 queens are $0–80 against free competition.
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — premium, <3 yrs, protector history, under $300. Sits — any stain, sag, 5+ yrs, box springs (near zero), king.
- **Haul:** queen 60×80", 60–110 lb; foam folds into an SUV, springs don't; box spring needs a truck.
- **Photos:** bare top in daylight first; then each side, law tag (brand/model/date), corners, any mark. It's a trust sale — show the whole surface.
- **Flaws:** kill — any stain, sag, odour, missing law tag. Nothing is "fine".
- **Channel:** FB allows used mattresses "adhering to local laws" (Puffy). Exit: no DFW charity takes them (general knowledge, verify); City of Keller bulk pickup or a hauler.
- **Clock:** 7 days; then free-by-Saturday; then bulk trash.
- **Legal — verified 2026-10-28:** Texas H&SC ch. 345 (Bedding) was **repealed effective 2015-09-01** by SB 202, 84th Leg. (https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/84R/analysis/html/SB00202H.htm; https://d1tvxue8o4w0fl.cloudfront.net/statute/texas-codes/health-and-safety-code/title-5-sanitation-and-environmental-quality/subtitle-a-sanitation/chapter-345-repealed-bedding/subchapter-a-general-provisions/section-345001-repealed). Texas no longer licenses bedding sellers or requires a "secondhand" tag; the cleaned-and-tagged rule in BIF §5 and playbook §3 is stale. Federal flammability rules bind manufacturers/renovators, not a household selling its own (general knowledge, verify). The project rule stands on business grounds: don't *flip* mattresses (bed-bug liability, no margin); selling your own with full disclosure is fine.

### Dressers, nightstands, chests
- **Search words:** dresser, chest of drawers, tall chest, highboy, nightstand; brand (IKEA Hemnes/Malm, Pottery Barn, Lane, Broyhill, Bassett); solid wood/walnut/oak/maple; mid century/farmhouse/modern; drawer count; colour.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Line] [Material] [N]-Drawer [Dresser/Chest] [Style] – [condition]`. Ex (67): `Lane Rhythm Walnut 5-Drawer Tall Chest Mid Century 1960s – original`.
- **Price vs. new:** T2 solid wood 40–60%; T1 $20–80 (BIF §3). A $250 particleboard 6-drawer lists $75–100, sells $50–80; national dresser asks low $40 / avg $140 / high $450 (latestcost, Oct 2026). Exception: discontinued IKEA Hemnes dressers resell ~$350 against $400 retail because they're out of stock (https://www.aol.com/yuen-seller-beware-youre-hoping-144700962.html) — check IKEA stock before pricing any IKEA case good. T3 MCM: Lane Staccato/First Edition $200–800, Broyhill Brasilia $150–900, Drexel Declaration $400–1,500 (BIF §4).
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — solid wood, neutral, dovetailed drawers, nightstand pairs, MCM with a maker name. Sits — swollen particleboard, dark-cherry 2000s suites, armoires and china cabinets (consignors stopped taking them, DFW §1).
- **Haul:** nightstand any car; dresser ≤ 60" wide fits an SUV seats-down, else pickup; 100–200 lb, 2 people, drawers out to carry.
- **Photos:** straight-on, drawers closed, first; then every drawer open, drawer joints (dovetail = value), back/maker stamp, top in raking light, hardware, flaws.
- **Flaws:** kill — water rings through the finish, broken glides, bubbled veneer, missing hardware, smoke smell in drawers. Fine — top scratches, one chipped corner, replaced knobs (say so).
- **Channel:** FB + Craigslist (no Lane competition in DFW, DFW §5). T3: Mid Century Warehouse cash offer, Again & Again, Chairish — in parallel (DFW §2). T1/T2 unsold: Rusty or Kiss It Good Buy. Exit: furniture bank.
- **Clock:** T1/T2 7–21 days; T3 anchor high 2 weeks on FB, then the dealer channels.
- **Legal:** pre-1978 painted pieces may carry lead paint — don't sand, disclose chipping, never sell as nursery furniture (https://www.bobvila.com/diy/secondhand-shopping-risks, May 2026).

### Dining tables & chairs
- **Search words:** dining table/set, kitchen table, farmhouse, extendable/leaf, round, counter height, bar stools; seats N; solid wood/oak/walnut/marble/glass; brand; chair count.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Material] [Shape] Dining Table [L"] + [N] Chairs – [condition]`. Ex (59): `Solid Oak 72" Farmhouse Dining Table + 6 Chairs – seats 6-8`.
- **Price vs. new:** solid wood $900 table listed $350–450, sold $250–375; national asks low $60 / avg $220 / high $800 (latestcost, Oct 2026). T1 sets $50–150; T2 sets $400–1,200; a $2,000 1stDibs-class table goes ~$400–500 on Marketplace (BIF §1).
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — complete sets of 4–6, table + bench, counter-height, round under 48", neutral wood. Sits — glass tops, tables without chairs, 8+ chair formal sets, dark cherry, missing leaf.
- **Haul:** chairs in any SUV; unbolt table legs beforehand and show the bolts; 72" top needs a pickup; marble/glass rides flat, 2 people; 60–200 lb.
- **Photos:** full set at 45°, bare table, first; then top in raking light, underside/apron, leg joints, leaf in and out, each chair seat, flaws.
- **Flaws:** kill — wobble, delaminated veneer, cracked glass, broken stretchers, water damage. Fine — surface scratches ("can be refinished"), one re-glued chair (say so).
- **Channel:** FB + groups + Nextdoor. T2/T3 sets: Kiss It Good Buy or The Consignerie (current styles only). Exit: furniture bank, Salvation Army.
- **Clock:** complete sets 7–14 days; at day 21 split the chairs off (chairs sell alone; tables don't).

### Desks & office chairs
- **Search words:** desk, standing/sit-stand, L-shaped, executive; brand (Uplift, Jarvis, Flexispot, IKEA Bekant); size (60x30); office chair, ergonomic, mesh; brand (Herman Miller Aeron/Embody, Steelcase Leap/Gesture, Haworth, Secretlab); Aeron size B/C.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Model] [Size/Type] [Desk/Chair] – [condition]`. Ex (59): `Herman Miller Aeron Size B Fully Loaded Office Chair – 2019`.
- **Price vs. new:** desks $30–120 worn, $150–500 solid wood or office brand (latestcost); electric standing desks ~40–60% if the motor works (general knowledge, verify). Used Aeron $300–400 is "spot on" locally (https://www.fishbowlapp.com/post/been-needing-an-office-chair-for-my-home-office-is-this-a-good-deal-local-marketplace); Leap similar; T1 mesh $20–60, gaming chairs $50–150 (general knowledge, verify). Herman Miller resells near retail on curated platforms (https://www.domino.com/content/kaiyo-best-furniture-brands-to-resell/, 2021).
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — Aeron/Leap/Gesture in any condition, electric standing desks, desks ≤ 48" in Aug–Sep. Sits — executive desks, L-shaped particleboard, glass desks, sinking gas lifts.
- **Haul:** chairs fit any car (pull the base off the cylinder); desk tops unbolt; executive desks 150–300 lb, truck + 2.
- **Photos:** desk set up / chair 45° first; then frame underside, control panel lit, mesh close-up, casters, levers, size/serial tag, flaws.
- **Flaws:** kill — desk won't raise, gas lift sinks, torn mesh, cracked arm pads undisclosed. Fine — scuffed arm pads, missing lumbar (price it), top scratches.
- **Channel:** FB + groups; Craigslist draws office buyers; Aeron/Steelcase also eBay local-pickup. Exit: Habitat ReStore takes desks; chairs under $25 → free.
- **Clock:** premium chairs and standing desks 3–7 days; T1 desks 14 days then free.

### Rugs
- **Search words:** area rug, size (8x10, 5x8, 9x12), Persian/oriental/Moroccan/shag/jute/wool/vintage/washable, colour, brand (Loloi, Safavieh, nuLOOM, Orian, Ruggable), hand-knotted vs machine-made. Junk: "nice rug", "carpet".
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Size] [Material] [Style] [Colour] Area Rug – [condition]`. Ex (59): `Loloi 8x10 Wool Vintage Persian Style Blue Area Rug – clean`.
- **Price vs. new:** machine-made under 50%; hand-knotted holds value (BIF §2). Asks (general knowledge, verify): T1 8x10 polypropylene $40–120; Ruggable 8x10 $100–200; Loloi/Safavieh wool 8x10 $150–400; hand-knotted 8x10 $400–2,000+.
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — 8x10 and 5x8 neutrals, washables, named jute/wool. Sits — 9x12+, bold colour, visible pet stains, runners.
- **Haul:** 8x10 rolls to ~96–108", 20–40 lb, sedan diagonal or any SUV, one person (BIF §2).
- **Photos:** whole rug flat, shot from a ladder, first — never rolled; then pile, back, fringe/binding, label, every stain.
- **Flaws:** kill — pet odour, stains, moth damage, curling corners on hand-knotted. Fine — minor fade; say if professionally cleaned.
- **Channel:** machine-made local (FB, Nextdoor, Craigslist); hand-knotted/vintage eBay/Etsy shipped (5x8 ~$25–50) or The Consignerie (DFW §1). Exit: Salvation Army / furniture bank.
- **Clock:** 7–14 days; −15% at day 14; free at day 30.

### Patio furniture & grills
- **Search words:** patio set, outdoor sectional, dining set, chaise, Adirondack, fire pit, umbrella; teak/wicker/wrought iron/aluminum/resin; brand (Brown Jordan, Tropitone, Gloster, Hampton Bay, Polywood); grill: Weber (Genesis, Spirit, Performer, kettle), Traeger, Big Green Egg, Blackstone, propane/natural gas.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Material] [N]-Piece Patio Set + cushions – [condition]` / `Weber Genesis II E-310 Propane Gas Grill – works, with cover`. Ex (59): `Hampton Bay 7-Piece Wicker Patio Dining Set – cushions incl`.
- **Price vs. new:** like-new with cushions 50–65%; good 40–55%; frame only/faded cushions 25–40%; needs restoration 15–25%. Hampton Bay sets $50–250; Brown Jordan $500–3,000; Gloster teak table $800–1,500; complete sets earn 30–50% more than parts (https://www.underpriced.app/blog/outdoor-patio-furniture-flipping-guide, Feb 2026). Grills: a 5-yr Weber gas grill ~40% (30–50%) — weak source (https://wildvisualizer.com/conversation/lmsyschat/4ad4d24792b64636adb45c0dfce0b837); kettles/Performers nearer 50% (general knowledge, verify).
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — complete sets March–June (peak April–May), Adirondacks (a week in season), Weber anything, Blackstone, Egg. Sits — July–Sept dead zone, outdoor sectionals (haul), rusted iron, cushion-less sets, off-brand gas grills with rusted grates (underpriced).
- **Haul:** chairs stack into an SUV; glass tops flat, 2 people; gas grills 100–150 lb, roll, pickup + 2; include the propane tank locally.
- **Photos:** full set on a swept patio, cushions on, first; then frame/welds, cushion zippers, umbrella open. Grill: lid open with clean grates, burners lit blue, grease tray, igniter.
- **Flaws:** kill — structural rust, cracked welds, brittle plastic, mold smell, broken glass top, burners rusted through. Fine — faded cushions (−30–40%), surface rust, grill exterior scratches.
- **Channel:** FB + groups + Nextdoor. Exit: Habitat ReStore takes outdoor furniture; grills → scrap/bulk trash.
- **Clock:** in season 3–10 days; off season list at −25% anyway (no storage).

### Major appliances (fridge, washer/dryer)
- **Search words:** refrigerator, French door, side by side, top freezer, counter depth, washer, dryer, set, front/top load, gas/electric dryer, stackable; brand (Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, GE, Maytag, Speed Queen); cu ft; stainless/white; model number.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Model] [cu ft] [Type] [Colour] [Appliance] – works, [year]`. Ex (71): `LG 26 cu ft French Door Refrigerator Stainless – works, 2021, ice maker`.
- **Price vs. new:** fridges 1–3 yrs: top freezer $300–600, side-by-side $600–1,200, French door $800–1,500 (https://fridge.com/blogs/news/how-much-should-you-pay-for-a-used-refrigerator, 2025/26). Washer/dryer sets 2–6 yrs $300–700, singles $100–350, Speed Queen holds value, 10+ yr units $50–150 (general knowledge, verify). Rule: ~30–50% of new under 5 yrs, under 20% past 8.
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — working sets under 6 yrs, stainless, French door, Speed Queen, anything working under $200 (landlords, flippers). Sits — 10+ yrs, white top-freezer, dented doors, gas dryers, "worked when unplugged".
- **Haul:** 2 people + appliance dolly + pickup/trailer; fridge 200–350 lb, washer 150–230, dryer 100–150 (general knowledge). Fridge rides upright or sits 24 h before plug-in. "Buyer disconnects and hauls; we help load."
- **Photos:** front, closed, clean, first; then interior lit, model/serial plate, back (coils, hoses), control panel lit, drum, flaws. Keep it plugged in until pickup.
- **Flaws:** kill — not cooling, compressor noise, error codes, leaking seals, dryer squeal, mold in a front-loader gasket. Fine — side dents, scratches, missing shelf (say so).
- **Channel:** FB + Craigslist (appliance flippers) + OfferUp. Exit: Habitat ReStore takes working units under ~10 yrs; dead → scrap haulers (free for metal) or city bulk pickup.
- **Clock:** working units 2–7 days. Dead at day 14 → free "for parts" → scrap.
- **Legal/safety:** EPA §608 puts refrigerant recovery on the *final disposer*; selling or giving away a working fridge isn't restricted (https://www.epa.gov/section608/appliance-disposal). Never cut a refrigerant line. Check cpsc.gov recalls by model.

### TVs & monitors
- **Search words:** TV, size, 4K, OLED, QLED, smart TV, brand + model (LG C2, Samsung QN90B, TCL, Hisense); monitor: size, 1440p/4K, 144Hz/240Hz, ultrawide, brand (Dell, LG UltraGear, Samsung Odyssey, ASUS ROG).
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Model] [Size]" [Panel] [4K/1440p] [Smart TV/Monitor] [Hz] – works, [year]`. Ex (52): `LG C2 65" OLED 4K Smart TV 2022 – works, with remote`.
- **Price vs. new:** 1–3 yrs good condition 30–50% of retail; 5+ yrs little value except premium OLED/QLED; a $800 65" → $300–400; 32" $40–60 (https://climbtheladder.com/how-to-sell-tvs-pricing-listings-and-safe-handoffs/, Apr 2026). Monitors: value sits in gaming/pro panels (OLED, 240Hz+, 34" ultrawide, 4K 27"+); plain 1080p office monitors $20–60 (https://paymore.com/sell-computer-for-cash/sell-monitor; general knowledge, verify).
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — 55–75" 4K under 4 yrs, named brand, remote + stand; high-refresh monitors. Sits — 1080p/non-smart, 75"+ (haul, cracking fear), monitors without stands, any line on the screen.
- **Haul:** 55" rides upright in an SUV (never flat); 65" tall SUV or truck, 2 people; stand screws bagged and taped to the back.
- **Photos:** powered on with a bright clean image first; then ports, model label, remote, stand, scratches, a white-screen shot for dead pixels (climbtheladder).
- **Flaws:** kill — cracked panel, lines, dead pixels, undisclosed backlight bleed, no remote. Fine — scratched bezel, scuffed stand.
- **Channel:** FB + OfferUp + Craigslist. TVs under 43" / monitors under 27": local, under $60. High-end monitors can ship on eBay (BIF §7); TVs never.
- **Clock:** 3–10 days; −10% at day 10; under $50 → free at day 21. The "pay to unlock Zelle" scam targets electronics (BIF §7).

### Exercise equipment (treadmills, bikes, racks, Peloton)

- **Channel:** FB first; Craigslist for racks/plates; Peloton Repowered (70% to seller) if local stalls; $20–50 deposit to hold (BIF §6). Exit: Buy Nothing (plates always go); scrap dead treadmills.
- **Clock:** 1–2 weeks; −15% at day 14; treadmills free at day 30.

### Baby/kids gear (cribs, strollers, car seats)
- **Search words:** crib, convertible/mini crib, bassinet, SNOO, stroller, double/jogging stroller, travel system, car seat, convertible car seat, booster, high chair, pack n play, glider; brand (UPPAbaby Vista/Cruz, Nuna, Bugaboo, BOB, Graco, Chicco, Britax, Stokke Tripp Trapp, Babyletto); colour/fabric name.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Model] [Year] [Type] [Colour] – [accessories], in date to [YYYY]`. Ex (57): `UPPAbaby Vista V2 2022 Stroller Grey – bassinet, adapters`.
- **Price vs. new:** premium keeps 40–60% after 2–3 yrs, mid-tier 25–40%, budget 10–20%. UPPAbaby Vista $450–750 (retail $1,400); Nuna Mixx $220–400; Graco/Evenflo travel system $30–70; Nuna RAVA (4+ yrs left) $140–250; Graco 4Ever $40–80; seat with <2 yrs left $40–90; SNOO $500–800; Tripp Trapp $120–200; mini crib $80–180 (https://itemvaluechecker.com/baby-items, 2026). UPPAbaby/Bugaboo hold 55–65% when maintained; strollers sell in 1–3 days on FB (https://getsnapprice.netlify.app/baby-stroller-resale-value.html, 2026).
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — UPPAbaby/Nuna/Bugaboo/BOB, SNOO, Tripp Trapp, Babyletto cribs, pack-n-plays under $40. Sits — budget travel systems, seats with <2 yrs left, cribs missing hardware, mystery stains.
- **Haul:** strollers fold into any trunk; cribs disassemble (show hardware bag + manual); gliders need an SUV.
- **Photos:** assembled/open, clean, first; then the label sticker (model, manufacture date, expiry — mandatory), harness/buckle, wheels, fabric, folded, all accessories in one shot, flaws.
- **Flaws:** kill — any recall, expired/crashed seat, cracked plastic, frayed harness, drop-side crib, missing crib hardware, mildew. Fine — faded fabric, worn wheels, missing cup holder.
- **Channel:** FB + local moms/kids groups (fastest, names unverified) + Nextdoor. Exit: family/crisis centers (call first; most refuse car seats and cribs).
- **Clock:** premium 1–7 days; budget 14 days then free.
- **Legal/safety (strict):** car seats expire 6–10 yrs from manufacture (shell sticker); never sell expired, recalled or crashed seats; model + date in the body (https://www.safeintheseat.com/post/can-you-resell-car-seats). FB bans recalled items (BIF §15) — check cpsc.gov/recalls by model before every listing. Drop-side cribs: manufacture and **resale** banned since June 2011 (https://www.retailconsumerproductslaw.com/2010/12/new-cpsc-crib-standard-bans-dropside-cribs/). Don't resell crib mattresses, bottles, pacifiers. Pre-1978 painted cribs: lead risk.

### Pianos & large instruments
- **Search words:** upright/console/spinet piano, baby grand, digital piano, 88-key weighted; brand (Yamaha, Kawai, Steinway, Baldwin, Clavinova, Roland, Casio Privia); "tuned"; serial/year; drum kit, cello, upright bass.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Model] [Size] Piano – tuned [YYYY], buyer arranges movers`. Ex (63): `Yamaha U1 48" Upright Piano – tuned 2025, buyer arranges movers`.
- **Price vs. new:** old uprights/spinets (pre-1980 Baldwin, Kimball, Wurlitzer, no-name) are **$0 or negative** — tuners won't take them free, repairs run $1,000–5,000, free posts sit for weeks (https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7405348, Dec 2024). Yamaha/Kawai uprights 1980s+ $1,500–4,000; name-brand baby grands $3,000–10,000+; digital pianos 30–50% (general knowledge, verify). Piano movers ~$250–500 for an upright (general knowledge, verify) — the buyer's cost, and it caps the price.
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — digital pianos, Yamaha/Kawai uprights, drum kits at half retail. Sits — every no-name upright.
- **Haul:** upright 300–500 lb, grand 500–1,200 lb (general knowledge): professional movers, buyer books; digital pianos one person, any car.
- **Photos:** full piano with bench first; then all 88 keys, inside with lid open (strings, hammers, serial), pedals, finish, veneer damage.
- **Flaws:** kill — cracked soundboard, loose tuning pins ("won't hold tune"), sticking keys, water damage, mice. Fine — cabinet scratches, yellowed keys, needs tuning (say when last tuned).
- **Channel:** FB + Craigslist + local piano teachers/churches; piano-store consignment for Yamaha/Kawai/Steinway (call). Band gear: FB + Guitar Center trade-in. Exit: "free, you move it" 14 days → junk hauler; "most charities no longer accept pianos" (https://1800gotjunk.com/us_en/blog/dispose-remove-piano); budget $200–400 (general knowledge, verify).
- **Clock:** name-brand uprights 2–6 weeks; no-name — free for 14 days, then book removal. Don't anchor high; there is no buyer.

### Lamps, mirrors & wall art
- **Search words:** floor/table/arc lamp, pair; mirror (full length, leaning, round, arched, gold, vanity); wall art, canvas, framed print, oil painting, signed, original; brand (Pottery Barn, West Elm, Arhaus, Anthropologie, Studio McGee); size; mid century/boho/farmhouse/vintage brass.
- **Title:** `[Brand] [Style] [Material/Colour] [Size"] [Floor Lamp/Mirror/Print] – [condition]`. Ex (60): `Anthropologie Gleaming Primrose 72" Gold Floor Mirror – mint`.
- **Price vs. new:** T1 lamps $10–30; T2 lamps $40–120, pairs sell better; mirrors T1 $15–50, large arched/leaning T2 $100–400 (Primrose ~50%); mass-market prints $10–60; signed originals by auction comps only (general knowledge, verify). Decor peaks in dorm season (https://blog.vendoo.co/top-selling-items-on-facebook-marketplace-in-2024). Under $25 → bundle or donate.
- **Fast vs. sits:** fast — oversized leaning mirrors, brass/MCM lamps, large neutral canvases, pairs. Sits — small framed prints, hotel art, lamps without shades, cracked mirrors (zero), singles under $15.
- **Haul:** all fit a car; mirrors over 60" flat on a blanket, 2 people; lamps — shade and bulb off for transport.
- **Photos:** lamp lit in a dim room / mirror reflecting a clean neutral scene (not you) / art hung on a bare wall with scale, first; then base, cord/switch, shade interior, frame corners, signature/label, flaws.
- **Flaws:** kill — cracked glass, frayed cord, torn canvas, desilvering on a cheap mirror. Fine — shade yellowing (price it), frame chips, desilvering on an antique mirror ("patina").
- **Channel:** FB + groups; Nextdoor for mirrors; eBay shipped only for signed art and designer lamps (BIF §3). T3 art: Tyler Grace or an art auction. Exit: Goodwill drop-off; small decor as one "$40 for everything" post.
- **Clock:** 7–14 days; bundle at 14; free/donate at 21.
