# Brand assets

**`VECTUS Brand Page.png`** — the master identity board, committed by Dylan, August 18 2026. It is the visual source of truth behind `../brand-identity.md`.

Provenance rule for color: the board's **printed hex labels are authoritative; its rendered pixels are not** (the render carries a dark grade). The platform tokens were settled by max-zoom reads of the labels — void `#080D11` · paper `#F3F2EE` · graphite `#21242A` · steel `#39414B` · signal `#22D3EE` — and the seven Dynamic Signal swatches, which carry no labels, were pixel-sampled with a normalization caveat recorded in `brand-identity.md`.

Planned residents of this folder: the mark as clean SVG (redrawn from the board, line art), the wordmark, and eventually **the custom Vectus typeface** — the fonts, like everything ours, live in the repo. Nothing here ships to players in AI-generated form; shipped art follows the no-AI rule.
