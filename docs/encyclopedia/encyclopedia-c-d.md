# California art pottery (Metlox Romanelli, Bauer, Catalina, Brayton Laguna, Hedi Schoop, Kay Finch)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay listings (Metlox Romanelli nude "water bearer" vase 9" – $99 ask, Des. Patent 125,594), collector references for Bauer ringware and Catalina Island, eBay sold

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1930s–50s California makers with marks on the base: "METLOX Poppytrail", "Metlox Made in USA" + "Des. Patent 125,xxx" (Carl Romanelli figural vases/nudes/Zodiac, 1939–42), "BAUER" (ringware in orange-red, jade, cobalt – oil jars and large bowls are the money), "Catalina Island/Catalina Pottery" (1927–37, red/brown clay, Descanso green, Toyon red), "Brayton Laguna" (figurines, Disney licence 1938–40), "Hedi Schoop", "Kay Finch" (hand-painted animals), "Franciscan" (Gladding McBean), "Pacific Pottery", "Vernon Kilns" (Rockwell Kent/Disney plates), "Roselane", "Sascha Brastoff" (signed, gold). Later Metlox dinnerware (Poppytrail, Red Rooster, California Provincial, 1950s–80s) is $5–20 a piece. Sellers' words: "vintage California pottery", "Metlox vase", "nude lady vase", "Bauer bowl".

## What sellers usually miss
"Romanelli" and the design-patent number on the base; Bauer vs lookalikes (Pacific, Garden City); Catalina Island (pre-1937, island clay) vs Catalina Pottery (GMB, post-1937). Chips/repairs at the base halve value.

2–8 lb, fragile – double-box; $15–30. eBay ~13%.

Buy when: marked Romanelli/Catalina Island/Bauer large forms at ≤ 35% of sold; unmarked-looking "nude lady vase" at ≤ $40 (check base in a second photo). Pass when: seller names the maker and prices at ≥ 60% of sold. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

# California pottery ringware (Bauer, Pacific, Garden City)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: maximalist.org Bauer piece list (1941 catalog sizes), eBay listings via search (ringware #9/#18 bowls listed $45–120), knowledge of sold ranges

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1930s–40s Los Angeles solid-colour kitchenware. Bauer "Ringware" mixing bowls: heavy, flared, with raised concentric rings on the outside (and three inside rings on early ones). Colours: orange-red, jade green, cobalt, yellow, ivory, black (black and burgundy are the scarce ones). Nesting mixing bowl numbers: #9 (1 gal, 10–11"), #12 (9"), #18 (8"), #24 (7"), #30 (6"), #36 (5"). Mark on bottom is often just an impressed number or "Bauer Los Angeles"; many are unmarked, which is why sellers just write "California pottery" or "orange bowl". Pacific Hostessware and Garden City look similar; Pacific is marked "Pacific" in a circle, Garden City is usually unmarked with sharper rings.

## Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | chipped, crazed small #30/#36 bowls | $10–25 | |
| Decent | #18/#24 orange or yellow, clean | $35–60 | |
| Collectible | #9 or #12 orange/jade/cobalt, clean | $60–120 each | the 10–11" #9 is the money bowl |
| Top | black or burgundy ringware, full nesting sets, early inside-ring bowls | $150–400 | verify colour, not repaint |

## Comps (sold, dated)
- 2026 (eBay listings found via search, asking) – Bauer ringware #9 orange inside-rings 11" – $89–120
- 2026 – Bauer ringware #18 orange – $45–65
- 2026 – Bauer butterscotch #18 beehive nested bowl – $55

## What sellers usually miss
The word "Bauer" (and the size number). Listings titled "California pottery bowl", "orange mixing bowl", "Fiesta-style bowl" are the ones to open. Chips on the rim knock 50% off; crazing is tolerated.

## Shipping / handling
A #9 bowl is ~5 lb; double-box, $18–25 to ship. Sell on eBay (13%) or Etsy. Breakage is the main risk.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a #9/#12 ringware bowl in a good colour is under $25, or two or more bowls for under $40. Pass when: chipped, or already titled "Bauer" at $50+. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.

Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold browse Oct 2025 (Justrite, Auto-Lite, Guy's Dropper); sweep listing 2318

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Small brass/steel two-chamber lamp (water on top, carbide below) with a round reflector and a cap hook. Makers stamped on the top: Justrite (most common), Auto-Lite, Guy's Dropper, Dewar, Baldwin (early, big reflector). # Cash registers (NCR / National)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: agent knowledge of eBay sold bands and NCR model-number conventions (verify on eBay sold filter before buying); run12 sheet 56

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
NCR "National" registers. Valuable ones are the brass-cased 1890s–1920s models: ornate cast bronze/brass scrollwork, marble change shelf, glass-topped pop-up price flags, model numbers 3xx–4xx (313, 317, 442) and the small candy-store 313/310/311 (the "nickel-plated" and "brass" finish). 1930s–50s models (Class 100/125/700/1000-series, e.g. "125(4)-2", "2000") are painted steel or chrome with push keys – working office machines, not decorator pieces. Tell in the photo: ornate cast scrollwork and a marble shelf = pre-1925 brass; smooth painted/chrome body = post-1935.

## Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | 1950s–70s steel NCR Class 100/125/2000, keys missing | $40–120 | 60–100 lb |
| Decent | 1930s chrome/wood 700-series, working | $150–300 | |
| Collectible | brass candy-store 313/311, working flags, original | $400–900 | |
| Top | large brass 442/452 multi-drawer, restored | $1,500–4,000 | |

## Comps (sold, dated)
- 2025 – NCR 313 brass candy register, unrestored working – $450–700 band (eBay sold, agent knowledge)
- 2025 – NCR 1950s Class 125 steel register – $50–100 band

## What sellers usually miss
Model number plate (under the drawer / front edge) – a 313 listed as "old brass cash register" at $100 is a buy. Reproduction brass registers (1970s Taiwan, "Model 313" copies) exist: thin castings, no serial plate.

## Shipping / handling
Brass 313: ~50 lb, $60–90 freight each way. Steel 1950s: 60–100 lb, worse. Only local/regional buys make sense; profit rule means ≥ $500 sale for a $150 purchase shipped.

## Buy rule
Buy when: brass candy-store model with serial plate under $150. Pass when: any painted/chrome post-1935 model (6755 type) regardless of price. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

## Seen in sweeps
- 2026-10-12 – craigslist chillicothe – "Vintage NCR National Cash Register", model 125(4)-2, 1950s, no keys – $40 – passed – steel era, sells ~$75 and weighs 70 lb

1900–1940 painted cast iron figurals: flower baskets and urns (most common), dogs (Boston terrier, Scottie, fox terrier, setter), cats, ships, cottages, people (Aunt Jemima, Colonial ladies, Mammy, golfer). Tells of originals: two-piece or flat-back casting with a worn, chalky **original paint** showing iron through at high points; crisp detail; base often marked "HUBLEY" with a number (e.g. 456 Petunias and Asters, 221 Nasturtium basket), or "B&H" (Bradley & Hubbard – the best), "Albany Fdy", "Judd", "Spencer", "National Foundry". Reproductions (1970s–today): shiny bright paint, grainy rough casting, loose fit between halves, Phillips screws, light weight. Sellers write "antique doorstop", "cast iron flower basket", "Victorian".

# Clarinets
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: alamomusic.com (pre-owned Selmer Paris Series 9 $2,000); palenmusic.com (restored Selmer USA Signet Soloist $495); reverb.com Selmer Centered Tone listing; run12 sheet 63

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Bb soprano clarinet in a case. Value is all in the maker + model stamped on the upper joint/bell, and wood vs plastic:
- Selmer **Paris** (stamp "Henri Selmer Paris" with the crossed-S logo): Centered Tone (1950s), Series 9 / 9*, 10G, Recital, Signature – professional, $800–2,500 used.
- Selmer **USA** (Elkhart; stamp "Selmer USA" or "Selmer Signet / Bundy"): Signet Soloist/Special (wood intermediate, $200–500 restored), Bundy/CL300/CL301 (plastic student, $60–150). Modern black "SELMER" plastic cases with a blue logo badge = student line.
- Buffet Crampon R13 / Festival / Prestige: pro, $900–2,500 used; Buffet E11/E12 wood intermediate $250–500; B12 plastic $80–150.
- Leblanc, Yamaha YCL-650/CSV (pro $600–1,200), Yamaha 250/255 student $80–150. Vintage Conn, Penzel-Mueller, Noblet wood: $80–250.
Tell: grenadilla wood is dull black with visible grain; plastic/resonite is glossy uniform. Nickel vs silver keys matters little; cracks and missing pads matter a lot.

| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | Bundy, CL300, Yamaha 250, B12, no-name Chinese | $50–150 | most Craigslist listings |
| Decent | wood intermediates: Signet, Buffet E11, Noblet, Vito wood | $150–400 | |
| Collectible | Selmer Paris Series 9/10, Buffet R13 playing condition | $800–1,800 | needs overhaul check |
| Top | Centered Tone, R13 1960s, A/Bb matched pairs, bass clarinets | $1,500–4,000 | |

## Comps (sold, dated)

- 2025 – Selmer USA Signet Soloist 1960s, professionally restored – $495 (Palen Music, sold out)
- 2025 – Selmer CL300/Bundy plastic – $75–130 band (eBay sold, agent knowledge)

## What sellers usually miss
"Selmer" alone – they don't distinguish Paris from USA/Bundy. The Paris stamp and the model name are on the upper joint; always ask for that photo. Also "Buffet" student B12 vs R13 (R13 has the model stamped under the Buffet logo).

## Shipping / handling
~4 lb with case, $15–20 each way. Sell on Reverb/eBay; many buyers want a tech's play-test, so budget $100–150 for a repad on pro horns.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Selmer Paris or Buffet R13 stamp visible under $400, no cracks. Pass when: plastic student line at any price over $40, or "Selmer" with the modern blue-badge case (7613). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

# Coleman gas lanterns (US, 1920s–1980s)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: terry-marsh.com Coleman US lantern model histories (1946–60, 1961–70), Coleman Collectors Forum, eBay sold listings

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Date stamp on the bottom of the fount: month and 2-digit year ("3 56" = March 1956). Model stamped near it or on the fount side decal. Shapes: single-mantle 200/200A (red, small, most common 1951–83); two-mantle 220/228 (green, bigger, 228 has a wide "big hat" ventilator); 242/243 single-mantle 1930s–40s (nickel founts); 236/237 kerosene; 202 "Professional" (chrome fount, 1954–64). Colour tells: 200A is red (most), green 200A pre-1952 and the red/green "Christmas" 200 (1951) are prized; black-band 200A (1951–52) prized; chrome/nickel founts earlier. Globes: Pyrex with Coleman logo; "Sunshine of the Night" early. Sellers' words: "old Coleman lantern", "camping lantern", sometimes with the date.

## Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)

Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: Invaluable "What makes duck decoys valuable" (Guyette & Deeter auction data), Appraisily decoy ID guides, eBay sold listings (unmarked glass-eye decoys)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Old hunting ("working") decoys vs. modern decorative carvings. Species matters: canvasback, redhead, bluebill, black duck common; shorebirds (plover, yellowlegs) and oversized/ sleeping forms much rarer. Factory decoys: Mason (Detroit, glass eyes, swirl paint, premier/standard grades), Dodge, Stevens, Peterson, Evans, Animal Trap/Victor (stamped on belly), Herter's (cork/balsa, later). ## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- **Modern hunting decoys** (GHG/Avian-X, Flambeau, Herter's foam/plastic): hollow plastic or foam, moulded feather detail, keel with line tie. $5–15 each used. 90 % of Craigslist "duck decoys".
- **Herter's (Waseca MN)**: cork 1936–39 ($200–500), hollow wood/balsa 1946–50s ($100–200), Model 72 oversize balsa (collectible, $75–150), Durlon foam and Tenite plastic 1950s–60s ($25–100 for nice ones), "Herter's Inc. 1893" bottom stamp on 1960s decoratives.
- **Factory wood**: Mason (Detroit, glass eyes, premier grade $300–3,000), Evans, Dodge, Stevens – look for painted or glass eyes, worn wood, lead keel weight, no seam line.
- **Hand-carved regional** (Ward Bros, Crowell, Illinois River, Louisiana): auction only, $1,000–100,000+.

## What sellers usually miss
Wood vs plastic (look for a seam line and hollow moulded underside = plastic). Glass eyes + ballast weight + brand stamp on bottom. "Old decoys from grandpa's shed" lots occasionally hide a Mason.

## Shipping / handling
Each decoy 1–2 lb; a dozen is 20–30 lb and bulky ($50–80). Sell singles on eBay; lots locally.

## Buy rule
Buy when: wood body with glass eyes and a bottom stamp (Mason, Evans, Herter's cork/balsa) at ≤ $40 each. Pass when: plastic/foam lots, modern brands, anything "flocked". Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

# Carved santos (polychrome wood religious figures)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold Jul–Oct 2026 (Chrome), Google Lens match to a Menino Jesus de Praga auction lot, Run 13

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hand-carved wooden saints, Virgins and Christ-child (Niño Jesús / Santo Niño / Menino Jesus) figures from Spanish, Mexican, Spanish-colonial (New Mexico, Philippines, Guatemala) and Portuguese/Brazilian churches and home altars, 17th–early 20th c. Tells of a real one: gesso ground with chipped/flaking paint showing white gesso and bare wood underneath; gilt on robes (bole-red showing through worn gilt); glass or painted eyes; hands carved separately and sometimes missing fingers; square-nailed or doweled bases; worm holes; dull matte surface. Titles sellers use: "antique wooden statue", "old Catholic statue", "church statue", "Jesus figure", "saint carving", "Santos". Reproductions: glossy even paint, uniform machine-sanded surface, "Made in Mexico/Philippines" stamp, resin (check for mould seams), stylised "Santa Fe" folk carvings from the 1980s–2000s.

## What sellers usually miss
Size and gilt. They describe condition ("badly chipped") as a negative; buyers pay for exactly that age evidence. They never say "polychrome", "gesso", "Santo Niño", "Spanish colonial" – search by those to sell. Ask for base/underside and back-of-head photos to confirm hand carving and old nails.

## Shipping / handling
Fragile (fingers, flaking gesso). Double-box with the figure wrapped loosely in tissue then bubble; 25" piece ≈ 8–12 lb boxed, $45–60 UPS Ground. Sell on eBay (religious antiques category) or to a Santa Fe / Tucson dealer; auction houses (Invaluable/LiveAuctioneers) for the top tier.

## Buy rule
Buy when: an 18"+ carved, gessoed figure with gilt and honest paint loss is under $200 – expect $400–800 sold. Pass when: under 12", glossy/even paint, resin, "Made in" stamp, or the seller already lists it as "antique santos polychrome" at $300+. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
