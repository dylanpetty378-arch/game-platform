## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Pocket-watch-sized cases (45–55 mm) with crown/pusher on top, a large sweep seconds hand and a small minute register (15, 30 or 60 min). Dials often marked in 1/5, 1/10 or 1/100 second, decimal minute (1/100 min for industrial time studies), tachymeter or telemeter scales. Brand on dial or caseback; Swiss quality marked "Swiss", "7 jewels"/"17 jewels" on dial or movement. Split-second (rattrapante) has an extra pusher and two sweep hands. Regatta/yacht timers have colored 5- or 10-minute countdown segments. Dashboard timers (Heuer Monte Carlo, Master Time, Autavia rally) are in a black mount bezel.
**Minerva:** Swiss (Villeret, 1858–2006, then Montblanc). Stopwatches marked "Minerva" with a stylized arrow logo; 30-min register common; chrome or nickel case; 1950s–70s; caliber with column-wheel in better examples.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Heuer** (Bienne/St-Imier; Heuer-Leonidas from 1964): stopwatches (Trackmaster, Microsplit later quartz), dashboard timers (Monte Carlo 12-hr, Master Time, Super Autavia, Sebring); dashboard pairs $1,000–3,000+.
- **Minerva**: stopwatches and split-seconds; 30-minute register most common ($80–180).
- **Hanhart** (Germany): stopwatches, many with red pushers; German Olympic/sport timers $80–250.
- **Gallet** (Clebar/Gallet, Geneva/NY): Gallet stopwatches including "Clebar" and Gallet split-second; $80–250.
- **Meylan** (Swiss/US distributed): Meylan sport timers; 1/5 and 1/10 sec; $50–150.
- **Excelsior Park, Lemania, Breitling, Junghans, Smiths (UK), Waltham, Elgin (US)**: Elgin/Waltham "Jewel" stopwatches $40–120; Breitling sports timers $150–400.
- **Low**: Japanese (Seiko S-series mechanical are mid at $60–150), Chinese Shanghai, Russian Agat ($20–60), Hanhart/Junghans pin-lever cheaper versions, Robert H. Ingersoll, Westclox/Timex pin lever ($10–30).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Jewelled (7–17 jewels) Swiss lever movements = quality; "no jewels"/"pin lever" = cheap. Chrome-plated brass cases wear to brass; stainless/nickel better. Enamel or lacquer dials. Look for: brand on dial and movement, jewel count, column-wheel, two pushers for split.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Non-running or reset not returning to zero, broken staff, missing crystal, dial staining, plating wear, dents. Service costs $80–150 — Dylan's rule: only buy running examples that start/stop/reset correctly (simple seller video test).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Low risk at stopwatch level; "Heuer" redials exist on dashboard timers and franken parts. Modern quartz "vintage-style" timers. Heuer dashboard timers above $500 can need authentication of dial/hands — treat as authentication-sensitive.

## What sellers usually miss
Brand names (Minerva, Gallet, Hanhart) treated as "old stopwatch"; split-second second pusher; dashboard timers removed from rally cars; drawer lots of coach stopwatches.

## Shipping / handling
Tiny; padded box $5–6. Ship stopped (not running) to avoid wear; protect crystal.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** brand + type (stopwatch, split-second/rattrapante, dashboard timer) + register (30-minute) + scale (1/10 sec) + "Swiss" + "mechanical" + "running" + model (Trackmaster, Monte Carlo). Junk: "old stopwatch", "pocket watch", "untested". Example: `Minerva Swiss Mechanical Stopwatch 30 Minute Register 1/10 Sec Running Vintage`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (watch and motorsport collectors; Swiss branded running examples sell in 1–3 weeks); Heuer dashboard timers also do well at watch auctions; FB local does not beat it.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median (Minerva 30-min ~$90–160, Heuer Trackmaster ~$150–300, Monte Carlo dashboard ~$600–1,200, Elgin/Waltham $40–120); accept −10%, floor −20%; OBO; Heuer firm; cut 10% after 30 days.
- **Photos:** 1) dial straight-on, hands at zero; then case back and any engraving, movement if the back opens (caliber, jewels), crown and pushers, crystal at an angle for scratches, side profile; video of start, stop and reset to zero.
- **State:** brand, caliber/jewel count, register type, scale, "running, starts/stops/resets to zero", timing accuracy if checked, crystal and case condition, not serviced unless it has been, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** tiny; ship stopped (not running), crystal faced into bubble, small padded box, under-1-lb class, $5–6.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "A [Minerva / Heuer Trackmaster] Swiss jewelled stopwatch, [caliber], running and resetting to zero in the video — the pin-lever Chinese and Timex ones are the $15 watches; running [brand] examples sell for [$X]."
