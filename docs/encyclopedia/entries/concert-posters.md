# Concert posters (1960s–1990s originals)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: knowledge of eBay sold ranges for 1980s Pacific Northwest telephone-pole posters; no exact comp found for The Firm 1985 Seattle

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Originals are printed for the show: 1960s Fillmore/Family Dog numbered posters, 1970s–80s "boxing style" letterpress and silk-screened telephone-pole posters on thin card (often 11x17 or 15x22 with pinholes/staple holes at corners), 1990s Hatch Show Print and art-print gig posters. Reprints are glossy, larger, or have "reproduction" in the margin. Seattle/Portland poster shops (e.g. Art Chantry era) silk-screened on cardboard – holes in corners are normal and prove it hung outside.

## What sellers usually miss
That it's an original (they say "old poster"), the artist/printer, and the Zeppelin connection in the title. Corner holes do not hurt value much; water stains and tape do.

## Shipping / handling
Flat, in a rigid mailer, $8–12. eBay (13%). Photograph front and back to show the card stock.

## Buy rule
Buy when: an original 1980s or earlier poster for a name act is under $25. Pass when: reprint, or heavily damaged. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
