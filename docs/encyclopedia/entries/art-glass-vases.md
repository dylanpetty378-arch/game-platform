## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hand-blown or mould-blown decorative glass vases beyond plain pressed glass.
- **Depression-era optic glass (1920s–40s)**: thin, mould-blown green/pink with spiral optic ribs, crimped rim; Fenton, Imperial, Hazel-Atlas; $10–30.
- **Cased/opaline with trailing**: white or coloured cased glass with applied trails or pulled swirls; Kralik/Welz Czech (1920s–30s, often with polished pontil), Hartley Wood (UK), modern Polish Krosno, Asian decor glass.
- **Scandinavian modern (1950s–80s)**: Orrefors, Kosta, Holmegaard, Riihimäki, Nuutajärvi; heavy, simple forms, engraved signatures with designer/model numbers.
- **American mid-century**: Blenko (WV) crackle and bold colours, sand-blasted "Blenko" mark only 1959–61, otherwise labels; Viking (epic line, labels); Pilgrim crackle; Fenton (moulded oval logo from 1970s).
- **Bohemian/Czech iridescent (1890s–1930s)**: Loetz, Kralik, Pallme-König, Rindskopf; oil-slick iridescence, applied threads, polished pontil.
- **Studio glass (1970s–)**: signed and dated with diamond-point or engraving tool on the base (Lundberg, Orient & Flume, Correia, Abelman, local artists).
- **Decor brands (2000s–)**: Palmero, Global Views, Pier 1, Z Gallerie, HomeGoods hand-blown "Made in Poland/China/India" vases; often large, heavy, recycled glass; sold new $80–300, resale $20–60.

## Makers, brands and variants
- Orrefors (Sven Palmqvist Ravenna, Ingeborg Lundin), Kosta (Vicke Lindstrand, Mona Morales-Schildt, Bertil Vallien), Holmegaard (Per Lütken), Riihimäki (Tamara Aladin), iittala (Tapio Wirkkala, Timo Sarpaneva): signatures engraved with numbers.
- Blenko (Winslow Anderson, Wayne Husted, Joel Myers): crackle, decanters, large floor vases.
- Fenton: Hobnail, Silver Crest, Burmese, hand-painted signed; logo dated by decade.
- Mdina (Malta, 1968–): "Mdina" scratched signature, sea-and-sand colours.
- Czech: Kralik and Welz (cased spatter, applied loops), Loetz (Phänomen, papillon), Moser.
- Steuben (Aurene, Calcite, later crystal; script "Steuben" or fleur-de-lis acid mark).
- Murano: see murano-glass entry.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Pontil: polished concave pontil = quality older hand work (Czech, Scandinavian); rough pontil = hand-blown; flat ground base with no pontil = mould or decor production.
- Signatures: engraved, not stickered; Scandinavian with model numbers; Steuben acid stamp.
- Weight and wall thickness: Scandinavian/studio heavy; depression optic very thin.
- Iridescence: real Loetz shows layered rainbow sheen; spray-iridised modern pieces look uniform.
- Uranium content: depression-era green fluoresces under UV (small premium).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rim chips and ground rims, internal stress cracks, sick glass (cloudy interior that will not wash out), scratches on the base, lost labels. Damage cuts value 50–90%.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake Loetz, Tiffany Favrile and Gallé signatures on Chinese/Romanian glass are common; modern Fenton reissues; "Blenko" attribution to any colourful vase. Decor brands imitate mid-century and Murano styles.

## Market
High supply, high liquidity at low prices. Scandinavian and mid-century American sells quickly when signed/labelled; decor and depression pieces are slow.

## What sellers usually miss
- Engraved Scandinavian signatures and numbers on heavy "glass vases".
- Polished pontils on Czech 1920s pieces listed as "vintage glass".
- Steuben acid fleur-de-lis mark.
- Reverse: decor-brand and swirl vases priced at their original retail ($100+).

## Shipping / handling
Fragile, often 8–15" tall; double box with 2–3" padding; $15–25 each way; heavy decor vases $25–35.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Photograph the base, pontil, signature and rim edge under raking light; give height and weight; attribute only with a signature/label or documented form.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − ~$18 to buyer − ~$18 to Dylan − price; pass unless ≥ max($50, price).
- Unsigned optic/swirl, decor brands: pass above ~$10.
- Signed Scandinavian/studio, Czech decorated with polished pontil: buy at ≤ $30 if resale ≥ $120.
- Loetz/Tiffany/Gallé/Lalique: need authentication: pass unless trivially cheap and labelled with provenance.
