## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- **Bed warmer / warming pan**: shallow round copper or brass pan (10–13 in) with a hinged, often pierced or engraved lid, on a long (30–45 in) wooden or iron handle. Filled with embers to warm beds.
- **Chestnut roaster**: smaller (7–10 in) pan with a lid pierced with large round holes, short iron or wood handle (12–20 in); 19th–early 20th c.
- **Foot warmers**: boxes or pans with pierced tops.
Old tells: dovetailed (brazed toothed) copper seams, hand-hammered surfaces, hand-forged iron handles with rivets, turned hardwood handles with wear, dark patina; reproductions are spun copper with no seam, machine-stamped piercing, lacquered bright finish.

## Makers, brands and variants
Mostly unmarked. English 18th c. (brass lids engraved with birds, flowers, initials and dates), American (Pennsylvania) 19th c., Dutch and French. 20th c. reproductions from England (e.g. "Made in England" stamps), Colonial Williamsburg-licensed copies, and 1970s decorators.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Engraving and piercing quality, thickness of metal, seams, handle construction. Tinned interior on some. Dated lids are premium.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dents, splits, holes burned through the base, loose hinges, replaced handles, verdigris, over-polishing (removes patina, collectors prefer patina).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Many 20th c. reproductions; married pieces (old pan, new handle); fake engraved dates.

## Market
Slow; fireplace and farmhouse decor buyers; regional auction staple. Long items are expensive to ship.

## What sellers usually miss
Dates and initials engraved on lids; that a short-handled piece is a chestnut roaster (smaller but more shippable).

## Shipping / handling
Bed warmers: 36–45 in long box, $30–45 — usually not worth it. Chestnut roasters: 4–6 lb, $18–25.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Give dimensions, construction (seams, rivets), handle material, any engraving, and describe dents/patina.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold × 0.87 − ship to buyer (~$22) − ship to Dylan (~$20) − price ≥ max($50, price). A $100 roaster resale gives $45 margin before purchase → never. Long-handled warmers fail the size rule. Only engraved 18th c. pieces at resale ≥ $300 for ≤ $75 and short enough for the car seat.
