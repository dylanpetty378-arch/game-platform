## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Wall-mounted small shelves with a scroll/acanthus support, for candles, busts and vases.
- **Carved wood (Florentine/Italian, 1940s–70s)**: carved linden or poplar with gesso, water gilding or gold leaf mixed with polychrome (silver-gray, red, cream), often distressed. Chips reveal **white gesso over wood grain**; back is unfinished wood, often with "Made in Italy" stamp or paper label and a keyhole hanger.
- **Antique (pre-1900)**: deeper hand-carving, hand-cut nails, aged gesso with craquelure, oxidized gilding, sometimes oil gilding on gesso.
- **Molded resin / Syroco (wood fiber composite)**, **Homco/Home Interiors (plastic)**, **Burwood**, **Dart**: crisp identical molded details, hollow backs with molded maker marks and copyright dates (1960s–80s), uniform gold paint, lightweight.
- **Chalkware/plaster**: heavy, chalky white at chips, often painted gold.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Italian Florentine** workshops (unnamed), sold through US department stores; labels "Made in Italy", "Florence".
- **Syroco** (Syracuse Ornamental Co., 1890–present; "Syroco Wood" composite 1940s–70s) marked on back.
- **Homco/Home Interiors** (1960s–90s plastic, molded mark, date).
- **Burwood Products** (Traverse City, MI, 1960s–80s).
- **Universal Statuary** (Chicago chalkware).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Wood grain or carving tool marks on back = wood. Mold seams and hollow back with molded numbers = resin/plastic.
- Weight: carved wood medium-light; chalkware heavy; plastic very light.
- Real gold leaf shows square leaf overlaps and red/ochre bole beneath; gold paint is uniform and brassy.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Gesso losses and flaking (common, acceptable on Florentine), broken carved scrolls and drops, re-gilding with gold paint (reduces value), missing hangers, insect damage in wood.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern resin "Florentine" brackets (Chinese, 1990s–now) mimic aged gilding but have mold uniformity and resin backs. Period-style reproductions are common at HomeGoods.

## Market
Decorator demand (Hollywood Regency, grandmillennial); Florentine pairs sell in 2–4 weeks. Resin/Syroco abundant and slow.

## What sellers usually miss
Florentine carved wood listed as "plastic shelf"; 19th-century giltwood with original gesso called "old shelf". More often they over-ask on resin.

## Shipping / handling
Light (1–4 lb each); fragile carved scrolls — wrap, box with fill, $12–20 for a pair.

- **Photos:** first photo the pair hung on a plain wall; then back (wood grain vs resin, labels, hangers), carved scrolls and drops, gesso losses, gilding close-up (leaf vs gold paint), top shelf surface, tape for height/depth.
- **State:** material (carved wood with gesso vs composition vs resin), measurements each, gilding type, labels/marks, losses and repairs by photo number, hangers present, pair matched or near-pair.
- **Pack / haul:** 1–4 lb each; fragile scrolls — wrap each bracket, box with fill, no contact between the two; $12–20 for a pair.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Carved wood with gesso and gilding, [Made in Italy] label on the back — the Florentine originals, not the resin copies; a pair in this condition sells around [$165] and these hang ready."
