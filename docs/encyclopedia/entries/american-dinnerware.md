## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Mass-produced earthenware from the Ohio River Valley potteries (East Liverpool, Sebring, Wellsville OH; Newell WV; Steubenville) plus California (Bauer, Franciscan/Gladding McBean, Metlox, Vernon Kilns) and the South (Blue Ridge/Southern Potteries, TN). Body: cream or white semi-vitreous ("semi-porcelain", "semi-vitreous porcelain", "hotel ware"), opaque (not translucent like porcelain), often crazed with brown age staining. Decoration types:
- **Decals (transfers)** of florals and roses, often with silver/platinum or gold trim; embossed scroll shapes (Homer Laughlin "Virginia Rose" shape 1929–1970s – embossed scrolls at four points on the rim, e.g. example 7975237695; Knowles "Deanna", Taylor Smith & Taylor "Laurel").
- **22K gold filigree / "warranted 22 karat gold"** rims with maroon, cobalt or green bands and a centre Fragonard-style courting scene (Sebring Serenade, Royal China, Salem, Paden City, Edwin Knowles) – 1940s–60s mass decorative ware (example 7971053337).
- **Solid-colour glazes**: Fiesta (1936–73; post-1986 revival), Harlequin, Riviera (Homer Laughlin), Bauer ring ware (1932–62), LuRay pastels (TS&T), Russel Wright American Modern (Steubenville, 1939–59) and Iroquois Casual.
- **Hand-painted underglaze**: Blue Ridge (1930s–57), Red Wing (1940s–60s), Purinton, Stangl, Franciscan Apple/Desert Rose (embossed and hand-painted).
- **Stoneware 1960s–70s**: Marcrest (Marshall Burns, Chicago; made by Western Stoneware, Monmouth IL) "Daisy Dot" brown and white ovenproof bowls; mark is a circle "Marcrest Stoneware – Warranted Ovenproof" (example 7952808731 shows the circular stamp).
- **Early 1900s semi-porcelain** with colourful transfer "chinoiserie" or Imari-style designs (US Pottery Co., Wellsville OH, c.1900–1930; Knowles Taylor Knowles; Homer Laughlin) – example 7943777386.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Vintage solid colours have a thick, slightly uneven glaze with dry or wiped foot rings; post-1986 Fiesta is lighter weight, whiter body, uses new colours (sunflower, lemongrass etc.) and an inkstamp "FIESTA ® HLC USA" with lower-case style. Date codes on Homer Laughlin backstamps are reliable. Gold trim wears quickly; worn gold halves value. Crazing and brown staining are normal on 1900–40s semi-vitreous ware and cut value modestly; heavy staining kills it. "22 karat gold" marks are about decoration, not intrinsic metal value (no melt value).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips on rims and foot rings, utensil marks (grey scratches) on plates, crazing and staining, worn decals and gold, cracks (hairlines visible as dark lines), dishwasher dulling. Restoration on Fiesta/Bauer (filled chips) is detectable under UV.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Post-1986 Fiesta (Homer Laughlin, now Fiesta Tableware Co.) in vintage-like colours – check backstamp and colour list.
- Harlequin was reissued (1979 for Woolworth) in the old shape.
- "Hall" teapots reissued (China Specialties, 1990s) – marks differ.
- Russel Wright American Modern reissued by Bauer Pottery Co. of LA (2000s) – marked.
- Decal ware isn't faked – it's just common.

## Market
Enormous supply, shrinking buyer base (estate dinnerware is the most over-supplied category in resale). Only Fiesta/Harlequin, Franciscan, Russel Wright, Bauer, Blue Ridge and Hall have active collector clubs. Replacements.com buys at a fraction of its retail. Single decal pieces sell slowly on eBay; Facebook local or not at all. Slight bump in November (holiday table) for platters.

## What sellers usually miss
- They over-price ordinary decal platters "1940s" and "22K gold" plates as antiques – the low tier.
- They miss rare Fiesta forms/colours (medium green, turquoise vases, cobalt sugars) in mixed boxes and sell them at $5–10 each; also Harlequin animal figurines and Bauer in "colourful old bowls" lots.
- Russel Wright pieces look "plain 1950s" and get lumped into $1 boxes.

## Shipping / handling
Plates and platters ship in plate boxes or double boxes: 13 in platter 3–4 lb, $15–22; sets heavy ($30–60). High breakage risk; wrap individually. eBay 13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Give maker, shape and pattern name (with HLC date code if present), diameter/length, and show the backstamp. Disclose crazing, staining, utensil marks and gold wear. For Fiesta, state vintage vs post-86 and the colour name. Note "not microwave/dishwasher safe" for gold and old glazes (some old glazes – red Fiesta – contain uranium oxide; mention as collectible, not for food use).

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − ship to buyer (~$18) − ship to Dylan (~$18) − price ≥ max($50, price). A piece must sell for ~$100+ even at $10, so single decal plates/platters can never qualify. Buy only: vintage Fiesta/Harlequin rare colours or forms (pitchers, vases, casseroles, teapots, relish trays), Bauer ring ware large pieces, Russel Wright pitchers/carafes, Blue Ridge figural/china pitchers, Vernon Kilns Rockwell Kent – at ≤ $30. Pass all decal florals, 22K gold-band plates, platters, Marcrest, Scio, Sebring, US Pottery. All fit a passenger seat.
