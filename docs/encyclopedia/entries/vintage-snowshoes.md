Buy rule (short): Buy only labelled Tubbs/Snocraft or Native-made pairs under ~$40; common pairs at $90 are a pass.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Bent ash frames, rawhide (or neoprene) lacing, leather bindings. Shapes: Michigan/Maine (long tail), bear paw (round), Alaskan (long, upturned).

## Makers, brands and variants
Snocraft (Norway, ME), Vermont Tubbs, Iverson (Michigan), Faber (Quebec), Chestnut Canoe Co.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Rawhide vs neoprene lacing; stamped/decal crossbar labels.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken lacing, cracked frames, dry rot leather.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern décor snowshoes.
