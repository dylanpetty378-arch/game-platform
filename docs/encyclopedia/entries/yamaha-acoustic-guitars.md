# Yamaha acoustic guitars (FG / A-series / L-series)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: themusicden.com used A1M listing ($439.98, excellent, sold out), Guitar Center used A1M listings (~$300–380), Reverb/eBay sold knowledge

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Yamaha headstock is a narrow three-point crown with the tuning-fork logo. Tells for the valuable lines: **A-series** (A1M/A1R/A3M/A3R/A5) = dreadnought or concert with a single cutaway, tortoise pickguard, onboard SRT preamp on the upper bout, often "Vintage Natural" amber finish; **FG red-label** (1966–72, "Nippon Gakki" red label inside soundhole) = 1960s Japanese dreadnought; **L-series** (LL/LS/LJ 6/16/26) = high-end solid wood with a wider headstock; **FG-180/FG-300** = early Japan FGs. Sellers write "Yamaha guitar", "acoustic guitar with case".

## What sellers usually miss
The model name (on the label inside the soundhole) and that A-series has electronics and a solid top. A cutaway + preamp + amber "vintage natural" finish is the A-series tell from across the room. A hard case adds $60–100 of resale.

## Shipping / handling
Dreadnought in hard case ~15 lb, 48×20×8 box: $50–80 ground, double-box. Resell on Reverb (~8%) or eBay (13%). Loosen strings for shipping.

## Buy rule
Buy when: A-series (any) with case under $120; A3/A5 or L-series under $200; red-label FG under $120. Pass when: F3xx/FG7xx/FG8xx at any price (retail goods); "needs work" or cracked. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
