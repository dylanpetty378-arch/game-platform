# Sombreros (Mexican palm and felt hats; charro hats)
Updated: 2026-10-13 · Sources: general knowledge (verify); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sombrero ; no comps in db/comps.jsonl
Slug: sombreros · Aliases: Mexican hat, charro hat, mariachi hat, vaquero hat, palm hat, straw sombrero, sombrero de charro, jarano
Summary: tourist/party straw sombreros $10–30; working palm vaquero hats $20–50; embroidered felt/suede charro hats (mid-century) $60–250; fine gold-bullion embroidered antique charro sombreros $250–1,000+
Buy rule (short): Only felt/suede charro hats with heavy metallic embroidery under $60; plain straw sombreros are pass (bulky, low value).

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Wide-brimmed Mexican hat with a tall conical crown. Value ladder by material:
- **Palm straw (palma)**: woven spiral of palm, cord hatband; cheapest; tourist ones have painted/sequined designs.
- **Felt or suede charro sombreros**: stiff, heavily embroidered with silver/gold metallic thread (bordado), often in fitted boxes; makers' labels inside (e.g. Sombrerería labels from Mexico City, Guadalajara, Jalisco).
- **Antique 19th-c. charro hats** with real silver bullion embroidery and silver toquilla bands — museum-grade, rare.

## Makers, brands and variants
Sombrererías in Mexico City, Guadalajara and León; "Tardan" (Mexico City, famous hatter), Marquez; mariachi suppliers. Tourist straw from Mexican markets (1950s–present).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Real metallic bullion (tarnishes dark, coiled wire) vs. modern lurex/plastic sequins; hand-stitched embroidery; leather sweatband with maker stamp. Tight fine weave in palm = better; coarse weave = tourist.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Brim edge fraying (very common on palm), crushed crowns, moth damage on felt, rusted/tarnished thread, missing chin straps. Repairs not worthwhile.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern mariachi hats with synthetic embroidery are sold as "antique". Tell by synthetic thread, plastic sweatbands, hot-glue.

## Market
Slow; demand bumps around Cinco de Mayo and Día de Muertos/Halloween. Big boxes make shipping costly.

## What sellers usually miss
Maker labels in felt charro hats; real metallic bullion embroidery.

## Shipping / handling
Large (20–30" brims) — needs a 24–30" box, $25–50 dimensional. Brim may exceed comfort for a passenger seat but usually fits.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Give brim diameter, crown height, interior size, material, label, embroidery type, damage.

## Buy rule
Profit = median × 0.87 − ~$35 − ~$35 − price ≥ max($50, price). Straw hats never qualify. Charro hats need sold median ≥ $160 at $20 price. Pass by default.
