# Stoneware crocks & jugs (Red Wing, Greensboro PA, salt-glaze)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: crockerfarm.com auction archive (Hamilton & Jones lots 2007–2013), redwingcollectors.org, eBay listings Oct 2026, agent knowledge

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Grey/tan cylindrical stoneware with a gallon number and often a cobalt-blue mark. The money is in the mark:
- **Red Wing (MN)**: red "wing" stamp + oval "Red Wing Union Stoneware" ink stamp; earlier ones have a hand-drawn cobalt "birch leaf" or "elephant ear" and "Minnesota Stoneware" stamp. Size (4", 6", large wing) changes value.
- **Greensboro / New Geneva PA** (Hamilton & Jones, Jas. Hamilton & Co., T.F. Reppert, Williams & Reppert): stencilled cobalt lettering, often with stencilled flowers/eagles and freehand stripes. Sellers write the name off the pot ("TR Ruppert", "Jas Hamilton") without knowing the market.
- **Freehand cobalt birds, deer, flowers** on salt-glazed crocks (NY/PA/OH, e.g. Cowden & Wilcox, White's Utica, Fort Edward) are the top tier.
- Plain "Western Stoneware", "Robinson Ransbottom", unmarked bristol-glaze crocks: decor only.

## What sellers usually miss
They copy the stencil text but not that "Greensboro, PA" stoneware is a collected category. Freehand cobalt decoration (bird, flower) vs stencil; large vs small wing on Red Wing; "Minnesota Stoneware" early marks.

## Shipping / handling
15–40 lb, fragile; shipping $40–80 double-boxed. Cracks cut value 50 %+. Resell on eBay or consign to Crocker Farm for decorated pieces.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Greensboro/New Geneva stencilled piece under $100 with no crack, or any freehand-decorated crock under $150. Pass when: cracked, pickup-only, Red Wing under 10 gal at > $40, plain/unmarked. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.

## Additional research (earlier entry: Stoneware crocks, jugs & salt cellars (Red Wing, Western, Monmouth, decorated salt-glaze))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold memory 2025, Red Wing Collectors Society knowledge

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Grey/tan salt-glaze or Bristol-glaze pottery: crocks (numbered gallon size), shoulder jugs ("whiskey jug", brown top/white bottom), hanging salt crocks (6" blue-sponged or embossed "SALT"). Marks: Red Wing wing/leaf (Minnesota), Western Stoneware maple-leaf/"Monmouth Ill.", Ruckels, Uhl, Robinson-Ransbottom (blue crown). Value tells: cobalt blue freehand decoration (birds, flowers, numbers), maker stamps, large sizes (6–30 gal), rare advertising text. Seller words: "antique crock", "whiskey jug", "stoneware".

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | Unmarked 1-gal jug, RRP blue crown crocks, chipped/cracked anything | $15–40 | heavy |
| Decent | Marked Red Wing/Western 2–5 gal, blue-sponge salt crock w/ lid | $50–120 | |
| Collectible | Cobalt bird/flower decorated NY/PA salt-glaze, Red Wing 10–20 gal w/ wing, advertising jugs | $150–600 | |
| Top | Elaborate cobalt scenes, signed potters (Cowden & Wilcox, Remmey), 30+ gal Red Wing | $1,000–10,000+ | auction |

### Comps (sold, dated)
- eBay sold memory 2025: unmarked 1-gal brown-top whiskey jug $25–40; Western Stoneware blue sponge salt crock with lid $60–90, no lid/hairline $20–30

### What sellers usually miss
Cobalt decoration and maker marks ("old jug $40"). Advertising text on a jug (merchant name/town) is the biggest multiplier.

### Shipping / handling
Heavy and chip-prone; 1 gal ~5 lb ($15 ship), 5 gal ~20 lb ($40). Sell local for big pieces.

### Buy rule
Buy when: cobalt-decorated or advertising-marked piece ≤ $40, no cracks. Pass when: plain, unmarked, cracked, lidless, or anything over 5 gal that must ship.

## Additional research (earlier entry: Stoneware jugs and crocks (salt-glaze, Albany slip, blue-band))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: knowledge of eBay/auction sold ranges (Red Wing, Western, Monmouth, decorated cobalt); no dated comp pulled this run

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Heavy grey/tan stoneware jugs (brown Albany-slip shoulder = 1880s–1920s "whiskey jug"), crocks with cobalt capacity numbers, blue-banded yellowware/stoneware bowls and pitchers (1920s–40s). Value is in: a maker's stamp (Red Wing wing, Western Stoneware maple leaf, Monmouth, Uhl, Hamilton & Jones), cobalt decoration (birds, flowers, slip-trailed script), merchant advertising stenciled on the jug, and size (5+ gal).

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | unmarked 1–2 gal jug, chips | $25–50 | heavy, local only |
| Decent | Red Wing/Western marked 2–3 gal, blue-band pitchers | $50–120 | |
| Collectible | cobalt-decorated birds/flowers, advertising jugs, 5+ gal with wing | $150–500 | |
| Top | early slip-decorated NY/PA pieces, signed potters | $1,000+ | auction |

### Comps (sold, dated)
- (knowledge-based: unmarked 2-gal Albany-slip jug $30–60; blue-band 1-gal jug $25–45; Red Wing 4" wing 3-gal crock $80–150)

### What sellers usually miss
The maker stamp near the base and any stenciled merchant name. Photo the whole jug – a stencil on the back is the money.

### Shipping / handling
8–20 lb, fragile, $25–45 to ship. Almost always local-only unless decorated.

### Buy rule
Buy when: decorated/advertising/large-wing piece under $60 and local. Pass when: unmarked or blue-band at any price above free. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
