Buy rule (short): Buy only tested space-age flip/cube designs and colored 1950s tube sets under ~$30; pass travel clock radios and anything untested.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Radio + alarm clock. Tube era (1948–1962): Bakelite/plastic cases, analog Telechron movements, pastel colors. Space age (1968–1980): ball shapes, flip-number (Copal), cubes, Panasonic RC-6015 "Spheres", Sony 8FC-59 Digimatic, Braun. 1980s: LCD travel clock radios in flip cases — the example (Magnavox D1858 / Philips folding FM travel clock radio with velvet pouch).

## Makers, brands and variants
GE (C-series), Zenith, Telechron (Warren Telechron), Sylvania, Westclox; Sony, Panasonic, JVC Videosphere (TV), Braun ABR 21 (Dieter Rams), Bang & Olufsen; Magnavox/Philips/Sharp/Sony travel.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Bakelite (heavy, phenol smell) vs thermoplastic (bright colors, prone to cracks). Space-age: chrome, white/orange ABS. Value from design not tech.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Untested electronics risk; cracked cases, warped plastic, burnt-out dial lamps, scratchy pots, battery corrosion in travel units.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Retro-styled reissues (Crosley).
