Buy rule (short): Only buy branded vintage hardshells (Lifton, Geib, Fender tweed, Gibson brown/pink) that fit the passenger seat at ≤ 1/3 sold; chipboard cases are freight losers.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Cases are valued by original-instrument pairing. Tells:
- **Chipboard (cardboard composite)**: textured faux-leather or tweed paper over pressed board; stitched edging, simple latches, thin; Harmony, Kay, Stella, Silvertone. The example is a gray textured chipboard parlor-shape case.
- **Geib/Lifton** (1930s–60s): plywood shell, leather/pebbled covering, plush lining (green, purple, pink, brown), "Geib" or "Lifton" label in the storage lid.
- **Gibson 1950s–60s**: brown with pink lining (Lifton/Stone), "Gibson" logo; Fender tweed with red plush or brown with orange plush.
- **Martin**: brown Geib with green lining (1930s–50s), black Ambassador (1960s–70s).

## Makers, brands and variants
Lifton, Geib, Stone, Victoria Luggage, Ampeg, Kay/Harmony chipboard, Silvertone. Modern: Hiscox, SKB, Gator, Calton.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Plywood vs chipboard: knock test; edges. Original lining and hardware; manufacturer label.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken latches/hinges, torn covering, mildew smell, collapsed chipboard, crushed ends.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reissue Fender/Gibson cases (2000s+) look like vintage but have modern labels, hardware.

## Market
Branded vintage cases sell quickly on Reverb; chipboard cases sell locally or not at all.

## What sellers usually miss
Lifton/Geib labels hidden inside; owners think empty cases are worthless.

## Shipping / handling
Oversize box ~42×17×6, 8–12 lb: $40–70. A full-size guitar case is borderline for passenger seat (needs reclined seat).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Interior label, lining, hinge/latch condition, interior dimensions and which guitar it fits.

## Buy rule
Profit = median × 0.87 − ~$55 − ~$55 − price ≥ max($50, price). Chipboard: never. Lifton/Geib (median $300): ≤ $60. Gibson brown/pink 50s (median $800): ≤ $200.
