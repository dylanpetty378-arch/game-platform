## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
A 1960s–70s Italian or Philippine bent-rattan child's rocking horse. It has a looped bamboo neck and head with a dowel "ears" cross-bar, a woven cane saddle, bent-rattan rocker loops and rattan-peel wrapped joints. Sold as "Franco Albini style": Albini never made one, so it is a trade attribution. There are no maker labels. Typical size about 31×17×22 in. Look-alikes: modern Pier 1/import repros (thinner rattan, staples, glossy lacquer) (general knowledge for construction tells, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- No documented maker. The "Albini" name is a sellers' attribution and adds nothing provable.
- Italian vs Philippine origin is usually unknowable from photos.
- Size matters most. Large (36"+) sculptural versions are top tier. The common child size is ~31".
- Modern Pier 1-style imports are junk tier.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Period pieces use thick bent rattan poles with natural nodes, rattan-peel bindings (not glue or staples), a hand-woven cane saddle, and an aged honey colour.
- Repros use thinner poles, visible staples or nails, a fresh glossy finish and a uniform pale colour.
- Painted white examples are worth much less (general knowledge on construction, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Condition drivers: an unbroken cane saddle, intact rocker bends, and the original colour (not painted).
- Common damage: unravelled peel wraps, cracked rocker loops, a broken saddle cane.
- Rewrapped joints with new, lighter peel are visible as colour mismatches.
- Ask for photos of the rocker undersides, the saddle and every wrapped joint.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern Pier 1-style and import repros exist. The "Albini" attribution is the usual over-claim, not a fake per se.

## Market
Bought by decorators, nursery stylists and mid-century fans. The boho/rattan revival has kept demand up (general knowledge, verify). Sales are slow. Chairish and Etsy reach the best buyers, and FB Marketplace is fine for local sales. Holiday gifting gives a mild bump.

## What sellers usually miss
Nothing. They usually over-attribute (Albini). The margin problem is shipping, not identification.

## Shipping / handling
~8–10 lb, but 31×22×17 in, so dimensional weight applies: $45–70 each way by UPS. Only sensible as a local buy or when a Chairish-level buyer pays shipping.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Title: "Mid-Century Bent Rattan Bamboo Rocking Horse Franco Albini Style 1960s". State the dimensions, original finish, saddle condition and any rewraps. Use "style", never a direct Albini attribution. Photograph it in profile, the saddle, and the joints. Offer local pickup.

## Buy rule
Buy when: ≤ $40, excellent condition and local pickup, or when a Chairish sale at $250+ is realistic. Pass when: $50+ and it has to be shipped; net is about $0 after two-way freight. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat; the standard ~31" size does, but 36"+ versions are likely bigger and excluded.
