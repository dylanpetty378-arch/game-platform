# Rattan / bamboo rocking horses (Albini-style)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: Chairish listing 7887095 ($275 ask, 31×17×22 in); 1stdibs "mid-century stylized rattan rocking horse" listings (asks $400–900); run12 sheet 56

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1960s–70s Italian/Philippine bent-rattan child's rocking horse: looped bamboo neck and head with a dowel "ears" cross-bar, woven cane saddle, bent-rattan rocker loops, rattan-peel wrapped joints. Sold as "Franco Albini style" – Albini never made one; it's a trade attribution. No maker labels. Condition drivers: unbroken cane saddle, intact rocker bends, original colour (not painted).
