## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Graduated sets of 3–5 lidded containers (Flour, Sugar, Coffee, Tea, sometimes Cookies/Salt/Shakers). Photo tells by family:
- **Depression/milk glass (1920s–50s)**: Jeannette jadite (soft jade, slightly translucent, raised or black-printed "SUGAR/COFFEE" on square jars with glass lids), Jeannette Delphite (opaque pale blue), McKee Seville yellow/jadeite (round or square, black lettering), Hocking/Hazel-Atlas green transparent and "Criss Cross" or ribbed jars, Hoosier-cabinet 1910s–20s clear jars with spice decals and aluminium/tin lids, Fire-King jadeite (later, glass lids and fewer forms). Uranium glass glows under UV.
- **Aluminium (1945–65)**: Kromex (spun, satin brushed, black/copper-tone/colored plastic knobs, often with matching shakers), Mirro (Manitowoc WI; round with wooden or Bakelite knobs, sometimes copper-anodised), West Bend (spun "Penny" shapes, pink/turquoise anodised), Lincoln Beautyware (painted tin and aluminium, pink/turquoise), Tipp City/Ransburg hand-painted tole tin. Example 7979835654 (round, satin aluminium, flat lids with turned wooden disc knobs, nesting 4) fits the Mirro/West Bend 1950s family.
- **Painted tin (1940s–70s)**: Ransburg (Indianapolis, hand-painted fruit/flowers on red, black, yellow), Lincoln Beautyware, Decoware, Chein (litho), Kreamer.
- **Ceramic (1930s–90s)**: Hall China (Chinese Red, Autumn Leaf), Holt-Howard pixies/Tomato, McCoy, Shawnee, Japan "red tomato" and Lefton, Pfaltzgraff, Treasure Craft, Metlox (Poppytrail), Sears Harvest; 1980s–2000s Portuguese/Italian imports (R.B. Bernarda for Williams-Sonoma Grande Cuisine; examples 7979371758 and 7979371392 – same set listed twice).
- **Wood (1960s–80s)**: Americana/eagle decals or applied cast eagles, tole-painted pine, hen-and-rooster themes; 1970s–80s gift-shop or craft-fair work (example 7940754842).
- **Modern (2000s–now)**: clear glass with brushed-stainless lids (Anchor Hocking, Target, HomeGoods – example 7974660003), farmhouse enamel, galvanized "FLOUR" tins from Hobby Lobby.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Old jadeite has a soft, slightly uneven colour and mould seams; reproductions (1990s+, Chinese) are uniform, often too green or too bright with crisp new lettering and no base wear. Old aluminium has mellow oxidation and stamped maker marks; lids fit snugly. Ceramic: maker stamp or incised mould number, crazing consistent with age. Wood: hand-cut joints or factory staples; cast eagle appliqués on 1970s–80s sets are usually resin/plastic gilt. Completeness (all lids, original labels) matters more than any finish.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Missing or mismatched lids (lid often worth 30–40% of a jar), chips to lid edges and square corners, worn lettering (cuts jadeite 50%+), cracked glass, water stains, dents and corrosion pitting on aluminium, rust and paint loss on tin, crazing and hairlines on ceramic, broken finial knobs (glued). Repainted/re-lettered glass exists.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Jadeite: large volume of 1990s–2020s Chinese reproductions and Martha Stewart/Pottery Barn "jadeite" (2000s) – brighter colour, crisp, sometimes marked "Martha by Mail". Fire-King jadeite is also reproduced (no Fire-King mark or a fake mark).
- Hoosier jars reproduced with new decals.
- Ransburg-style hand-painted tins reproduced as decor.
- Holt-Howard Pixieware reproduced (lighter, cruder paint).
- Rustic wood sets are made today in quantity – distressing on all sides, Phillips screws.

## Market
Liquid at the top (jadeite has a large collector base, dozens of sales per month), sluggish for everything else. Sets ship heavy and bulky, so local Facebook sales often beat eBay for anything under $60. Holiday/decor season (Oct–Dec) and spring kitchen refresh. Etsy works well for MCM aluminium and farmhouse wood.

## What sellers usually miss
- A jadeite jar described as "green milk glass canister" – check square ribbed shape and black/embossed lettering.
- McKee Seville yellow and Chalaine blue described as "yellow glass" or "blue milk glass".
- Uranium content (glows under blacklight) adds buyers.
- Kromex/Ransburg sets broken up – the matching shakers, breadbox or stool doubles the value.

## Shipping / handling
A 4-piece metal set packs nested in one box ~6–8 lb, $18–25. Glass/ceramic sets 8–15 lb, double-boxed, $25–40; lids wrapped separately. Breakage risk high for glass and ceramic. eBay 13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Give each piece's height and capacity, list every lid, photograph lettering, base marks and any chip with a fingernail test. For jadeite, state "authentic vintage Jeannette" only when ribbed square body, worn base ring and correct lettering style are shown; mention UV glow if tested. For aluminium name the maker stamp. Don't call 1980s imports "antique".

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − ship to buyer (~$22) − ship to Dylan (~$22) − price ≥ max($50, price). That needs a sale of ≥ ~$130 even at $10. Buy only: lettered jadeite/Delphite/McKee coloured glass canisters (≥ 2 pieces with lids) at ≤ $40; Hall/Holt-Howard/complete Ransburg or Kromex with accessories at ≤ $25. Pass: all modern glass/stainless, wood Americana, Mirro/West Bend aluminium, Portuguese/Italian floral ceramic, anything with missing lids. Fits a passenger seat easily.
