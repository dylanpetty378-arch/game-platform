Summary: Modern Chinese plastic tourist sets $15–40; 1960s–80s American plastic sets $50–120; 1920s bone-bamboo and 1930s–50s Bakelite 152+ tile sets $200–500; two-tone catalin / deco-case top sets $600–1,500.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Tiles in a case. The money is in 1920s–50s AMERICAN sets: Bakelite/catalin (butterscotch, two-tone "French ivory", cherry), 152–166 tiles incl. 8 jokers/flowers with ARABIC NUMERALS on the craks and bams, 5-drawer wooden or leather case, racks, Chinese bettors. 1920s bone-and-bamboo (dovetailed two-tone tiles) in wooden box. Modern Chinese tourist sets: shiny cream plastic, no Arabic numerals, vinyl case printed 中国旅游, 144 tiles — $20–40. Sellers write "mah jong", "mahjongg", "Chinese tile game".

Look-alikes: dominoes and Rummikub-type tile games; modern American sets (white melamine/acrylic, often with "NMJL" jokers) are the decent-tier sets (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **1920s import bone & bamboo sets** (China, for the 1920s American craze): two-layer tiles, bone face dovetailed to bamboo back, in a 5-drawer hardwood box with brass fittings. $150–300 complete (knowledge); $200–500 band for collectible tier.
- **Pung Chow** (1920s American seller/brand): sets in deco cases are listed in the top tier ($600–1,500).
- **1930s–50s American Bakelite/catalin**: butterscotch, two-tone "French ivory", cherry; 152–166 tiles with jokers; racks and bettors. "Enrobed" two-tone catalin and Rottgames-type sets sit at the top.
- **Royal Depth Control / Cardinal** (1960s–80s American plastic): complete with racks $50–120.
- **Modern Chinese tourist/travel sets**: 144 tiles, cream plastic, vinyl case — $15–40.
- Value multipliers: two-tone or "enrobed" catalin, deco/leather cases, full jokers, matching racks and bettors, original rule booklet (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Bakelite/catalin: thick, heavy, warm to the touch, butterscotch/amber colour that has yellowed with age; carved and painted pips; dovetail-free (one-piece) tiles. Test (general knowledge, verify): hot-water or Simichrome test gives a formaldehyde smell / yellow residue on catalin; acrylic and melamine do not.
- Bone & bamboo: visible dovetail joint between white bone face and brown bamboo back; bone shows tiny pores/grain lines.
- Modern plastic: shiny, uniform cream, light, crisp moulded engraving, no Arabic numerals.
- Arabic numerals in the corner of craks and bams = American-market set.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Missing tiles kill play value; count tiles from photos or ask (144 base, 152–166 for American sets with jokers/flowers). Bakelite tiles sell as replacements $3–8 each, so a short set still has parts value. Cracked or chipped tiles, worn paint in the engravings, broken case drawers/latches, and missing racks reduce value (general knowledge, verify). Repainted pips and mixed tiles from different sets ("married" sets — differing colour or thickness) are the main hidden issues (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern "bone" sets made in China are often resin or cow bone sold as antique; the dovetail join on fakes can be glued-on veneer (general knowledge, verify). Modern acrylic tiles are sometimes sold as "Bakelite" — use the warmth/weight/test above. Married sets (tiles from several sets) are common (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Steady eBay and Etsy demand from American mahjong players (strong National Mah Jongg League player community) who want vintage Bakelite sets to play with, plus collectors of Bakelite (general knowledge, verify). Complete Bakelite sets sell reasonably quickly at fair prices; tourist sets are plentiful and slow. Gift season (Nov–Dec) likely helps (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
"Bakelite" vs plastic (sellers say "old plastic tiles"); Arabic numerals = American set; the racks and bettors. Missing tiles kill play value but Bakelite tiles sell as replacements $3–8 each.

## Shipping / handling
5–12 lb in case, $20–35 each way. Wrap tile drawers so tiles don't rattle and chip; tape drawers shut. eBay.
