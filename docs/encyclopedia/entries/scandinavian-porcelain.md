## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Porcelain and stoneware from Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Finland, 1880s–1980s. Recognisable by underglaze blue painting (RC "fluted"), cool palettes, mid-century modern shapes and glazes.
Marks: Royal Copenhagen = three wavy lines under a crown; B&G = three towers "B&G"; Porsgrund = anchor or "Porsgrund Norway" with crown and model; Figgjo = "Figgjo Flint Norway"; Arabia = crown/"Arabia Made in Finland"; Gustavsberg = anchor + "G" hand; Rorstrand = three crowns.
The example: a blue-and-white Porsgrund decorative plate (1968) showing a child skier near a ski-jump tower — a commemorative/annual design.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Royal Copenhagen** (1775): Blue Fluted, Flora Danica (very high), figurines, Christmas plates since 1908.
- **Bing & Grondahl** (1853–1987): Christmas plates since 1895, figurines.
- **Porsgrund** (Norway, 1885): tableware, Christmas plates (1909, and series from 1968), "Jule" and mother's day plates; designers Tias Eckhoff (Det Riflede), Nora Gulbrandsen (Art Deco era, high value).
- **Figgjo** (Norway): 1960s–70s hand-painted "Lotte", "Market" etc.; Turi Gramstad Oliver designs collectible.
- **Arabia** (Finland): Birger Kaipiainen birds, Ruska tableware, Paratiisi; Kaipiainen art pieces $500–5,000.
- **Gustavsberg** (Sweden): Argenta (silver-inlaid green stoneware, Wilhelm Kage), Stig Lindberg designs.
- **Rorstrand**: Gunnar Nylund, Carl-Harry Stalhane stoneware.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Underglaze hand painting with painter's mark/number on RC/B&G; printed transfer on plates. Studio stoneware signed by incised initials.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips, hairlines, grade "2nd/3rd sorting" marks (RC scratches through the wave lines = seconds, lower value).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Few fakes; main issue is reissues and seconds. Some modern Asian copies of Blue Fluted exist.

## Market
Plates: huge supply, slow. Studio pieces: fast, strong collector demand (EU and US).

## What sellers usually miss
Gustavsberg Argenta and Lindberg pieces sold as "green ashtray"/"funky plate"; Kaipiainen birds sold as "ceramic bird".

## Shipping / handling
Plate: $10–14 in a double box.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Back-stamp photo, year, dimension, designer if known, chips and seconds marks.

## Buy rule
Plates never clear $50 profit. Studio pieces with medians $200+: pay ≤ $60.
