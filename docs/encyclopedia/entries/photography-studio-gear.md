## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Light stands, backdrop support systems (two stands + crossbar in a long bag), collapsible pop-up backgrounds (round zip disks), reflectors, softboxes, strobes/monolights. Budget kits come in black nylon bags printed EMART, NEEWER, LimoStudio; collapsible backgrounds labelled with SKU numbers like "BCK 150200DUB(G/BU)" (5×6.5 ft double-sided green/blue chroma). Pro gear: Profoto (grey heads), Elinchrom (blue-grey), Broncolor, Paul C. Buff (Einstein/AlienBees), Westcott (orange accents), Lastolite (round disks with tabs), Manfrotto/Avenger stands (black anodized, heavy, C-stands).
- **Where the model is**: monolights carry the model name on the rear panel or side label (Profoto D1/D2/B1/B10, Elinchrom BRX/ELC, Einstein E640, AlienBees B800/B1600); pack-and-head systems have the name on the pack face.
- **C-stand anatomy**: chrome or black steel column on a three-leg "turtle" (removable) or sliding-leg base, plus a grip head ("knuckle") and a 40 in grip arm. Matthews, Avenger (Manfrotto group) and Kupo are the main pro brands; the brand is cast or stamped on the base and knuckle.
- **Vintage film lights**: Mole-Richardson ("Mole") fresnel spotlights, e.g. Type 407 Baby Solarspot and Type 414 Solarspot, with cast housings and a type/serial plate. Decorators buy them; the floor-stand versions are usually bigger than the passenger-seat rule.
- **Title words**: monolight, strobe, flash head, pack, C-stand, century stand, turtle base, grip head, softbox, octabox, beauty dish, backdrop stand, muslin, chroma key.

## Makers, brands and variants
Budget: Emart, Neewer, LimoStudio, Fovitec, Insignia (Best Buy house brand). Mid: Westcott, Lastolite, Impact, Savage, Godox. Pro: Profoto, Broncolor, Elinchrom, Avenger/Matthews C-stands ($60–150 each used).
- **Profoto (Sweden)**: AC monolights D1 (500 W) and D2; battery lights B1 (launched 2013), B1X, B2 (head + external pack), B10, B10X, B10X Plus; older pack systems (Acute, Pro series). Used retail bands from GearFocus: D1 $800–1,000, D2 $1,400–1,700, B1 $1,000–1,300, B1X $1,400–1,700, B2 head $800–1,000 and pack $300–400, B10 $1,300–1,500, B10X $1,400–1,600, B10X Plus $1,650–1,800 (dealer used prices, not eBay sold; verify).
- **Paul C. Buff (Nashville)**: AlienBees and White Lightning (older, cheaper), Einstein E640 (better); sold direct, so used units turn up locally.
- **Elinchrom, Broncolor**: Swiss pro systems; Broncolor packs and Para reflectors are the top of the market.
- **Matthews Studio Equipment**: the reference C-stand ("Century stand"); Avenger and Kupo are pro alternatives; generic Amazon C-stands copy the look.
- **Mole-Richardson (Hollywood)**: vintage studio spots; 1stDibs shows vintage Mole pieces offered from about $485 up (asking prices).

### Quick ID checklist
- Read the brand on the head, pack or stand base; no brand = budget.
- Strobes: model name on rear panel; ask for a photo of the display or a test-fire video.
- Battery strobes (Profoto B-series): ask for battery age and number of full-power pops.
- C-stands: steel, heavy, with knuckle and grip arm; look for "Matthews", "Avenger" or "Kupo" cast in.
- Softboxes: speed ring brand matters (Profoto, Elinchrom, Chimera); generic rings = budget.
- Backdrop kits in printed nylon bags (Emart, Neewer, LimoStudio) = budget tier.
- Vintage Mole-Richardson: cast housing, fresnel lens, type plate; measure the head only.
- Check what is missing: power cords, sync cords, reflectors and remotes are often absent.

### Golden-egg tells
- **Profoto B1/B1X/B10 battery monolights** — $1,000–1,700 used (GearFocus dealer bands) vs $15–60 for a budget kit lot — grey Profoto head with integrated battery and "B1"/"B10" on the side.
- **Profoto D1/D2 AC monolights** — $800–1,700 used (GearFocus) — grey round head with rear-panel "D1"/"D2" and an AC cord.
- **Broncolor or Elinchrom packs and heads** — $200–2,000+ (existing ladder) vs budget strobes $20–50 — brand on pack face, blue-grey (Elinchrom) or black Broncolor housings.
- **Genuine Matthews/Avenger/Kupo C-stands** — $60–150 each used (existing entry) vs a few dollars for budget aluminium stands — steel turtle base, knuckle and grip arm with cast brand.
- **Vintage Mole-Richardson fresnel heads (Baby Solarspot, Solarspot)** — 1stDibs asking from ~$485 (asking, verify sold) vs generic lamps — cast housing, fresnel lens, "Mole-Richardson Co. Hollywood" type plate. Floor-stand versions often fail the size rule.
- **Paul C. Buff Einstein E640** — mid-pro tier (no comps fetched; est. $150–300, verify) vs AlienBees lower — "Einstein" on the rear LCD panel.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Budget: thin aluminum tube stands, plastic locks. Pro: steel C-stands, metal knuckles, engraved brand, chrome-plated. Strobe brand plate and model are the value.
- Strobe health (GearFocus): fire ~20 rapid full-power shots and listen for misfires or slowing recycle; tubes last roughly 50,000–100,000 pops; capacitors 10–15 years; modelling lamps can cost $150+ to replace; budget for a battery on units over three years old.
- C-stand knuckles: Matthews uses a metal disc and toothed sockets; Avenger uses rubber grips; Kupo has a hex-end grip arm and larger knobs (FredMiranda forum opinions).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Bent stand legs, broken locks, torn fabric, frayed spring wire in pop-ups. Strobes: flash tube and cap failures — untested is a gamble.
- Loose umbrella-shaft holes and stand adapters on monolights are common wear (GearFocus).
- A Profoto B1X that gives fewer than ~200 pops at 1/4 power needs a new battery (GearFocus says healthy is 300+).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Godox rebrands; counterfeits not significant.
- Look-alikes: budget C-stands copy the Matthews shape; read the casting. Many budget strobes are rebadged Godox units.

## Market
Used budget gear competes with $40–80 new Amazon kits: slow, low. Pro strobes sell fast to photographers.
- Dealer used markets (Adorama, GearFocus, KEH-type sellers) set the ceiling for pro strobes; eBay and local sell below them.

## What sellers usually miss
C-stands and pro strobes mixed into "random photography stuff" lots.
- Speed rings, grids and remotes (e.g. Profoto Air Remote) are sold separately at good prices and get thrown into lots.

## Shipping / handling
Stand kits are long (36–48 in) and 10–20 lb: $25–45. Pop-ups ship flat-ish (30–60 in round when folded).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Brand, model, sizes, completeness, tested functionality.

### What to tell the buyer
State brand and exact model as printed on the unit, what is included (cords, reflector, battery, charger, remote, case), and how it was tested: number of test fires at full power, recycle behaviour, modelling lamp working, battery pop count for battery units. For stands, give maximum and folded height and the brand cast on the base. Photograph the model plate, the display, and any cracks or loose mounts.

## Buy rule
Profit ≥ max($50, price). Budget lots fail. Pro strobes are untested electronics → excluded unless seller demonstrates firing. Long stand bags may not fit passenger seat comfortably.
