Buy rule (short): Skip budget kits; only pro-brand strobes/modifiers that are tested and shippable, and most strobes are untested electronics — so in practice pass.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Light stands, backdrop support systems (two stands + crossbar in a long bag), collapsible pop-up backgrounds (round zip disks), reflectors, softboxes, strobes/monolights. Budget kits come in black nylon bags printed EMART, NEEWER, LimoStudio; collapsible backgrounds labelled with SKU numbers like "BCK 150200DUB(G/BU)" (5×6.5 ft double-sided green/blue chroma). Pro gear: Profoto (grey heads), Elinchrom (blue-grey), Broncolor, Paul C. Buff (Einstein/AlienBees), Westcott (orange accents), Lastolite (round disks with tabs), Manfrotto/Avenger stands (black anodized, heavy, C-stands).

## Makers, brands and variants
Budget: Emart, Neewer, LimoStudio, Fovitec, Insignia (Best Buy house brand). Mid: Westcott, Lastolite, Impact, Savage, Godox. Pro: Profoto, Broncolor, Elinchrom, Avenger/Matthews C-stands ($60–150 each used).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Budget: thin aluminum tube stands, plastic locks. Pro: steel C-stands, metal knuckles, engraved brand, chrome-plated. Strobe brand plate and model are the value.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Bent stand legs, broken locks, torn fabric, frayed spring wire in pop-ups. Strobes: flash tube and cap failures — untested is a gamble.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Godox rebrands; counterfeits not significant.

## Market
Used budget gear competes with $40–80 new Amazon kits: slow, low. Pro strobes sell fast to photographers.

## What sellers usually miss
C-stands and pro strobes mixed into "random photography stuff" lots.

## Shipping / handling
Stand kits are long (36–48 in) and 10–20 lb: $25–45. Pop-ups ship flat-ish (30–60 in round when folded).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Brand, model, sizes, completeness, tested functionality.

## Buy rule
Profit ≥ max($50, price). Budget lots fail. Pro strobes are untested electronics → excluded unless seller demonstrates firing. Long stand bags may not fit passenger seat comfortably.
