## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
A clock under a glass dome (or in a glass case) with a pendulum that rotates slowly back and forth, hung on a thin torsion suspension spring; runs about 400 days per winding.
- **Mechanical 400-day**: brass plate movement, keyhole in the back, four-ball (or disc/pinwheel) rotating pendulum, white/silver dial with baton or Arabic numerals; dome on a round or oval wood/brass base.
- **Quartz "anniversary" clocks (1980s–2000s)**: AA battery, often "Quartz" on dial, rotating pendulum driven by a motor, sometimes musical with dancers/carousel (Rhythm, Japan). Low value.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Kieninger & Obergfell "Kundo" (St. Georgen, 1949–1990s)**: most common post-war; also Kundo Electronic (battery transistor) and miniatures.
- **August Schatz & Söhne (Triberg)**: Schatz "1000-day" (49 and 53 models).
- **Koma (Konrad Mauch), Kern & Söhne, Herr, J. Kaiser (globe clocks — a collectible variant, $100–300), Henn, Hermle.**
- **Pre-WWII**: Gustav Becker (eagle mark), Jahresuhrenfabrik (JUF, Aug. Schatz predecessor, 1900s–1920s), Badische Uhrenfabrik, Kieninger & Obergfell early; disc pendulums, heavy cast bases. $100–600 (general knowledge, verify).
- Imported/branded post-war: Elgin, Forestville, Welby, Seth Thomas (German-made).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original domes are hand-blown, thin, slightly wavy; replacements are thicker plastic or straight-sided. Brass plating wear and silvered dials. Check back plate stamp: "Made in Germany (US Zone)" = 1945–49 (desirable for some); "West Germany" = 1949–90.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken suspension springs (most common; a repair), missing pendulum lock, cracked domes, missing keys. Running "untested" clocks are often non-running.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not faked. Quartz clocks imitating 400-day look; check for battery compartment.

## Market
Huge supply, buyers mainly decorators and hobby repairers. Glass domes break in shipping; many sellers ship without domes.

## What sellers usually miss
J. Kaiser globe clocks and miniatures; pre-war disc pendulums; "US Zone" marks.

## Shipping / handling
Dome + clock 3–6 lb; must remove/lock pendulum and ship dome separately wrapped; $18–25.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Back plate marks and plate numbers, suspension condition, whether it runs, dome original, height.
