## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Shallow boxes with a central divider/handle used to carry cutlery or tools. Age and value tells:
- **Construction**: hand-cut dovetails (few, uneven, thin pins), cut/square nails, wooden pegs = 19th c; machine dovetails/wire nails/staples = 20th c.
- **Surface**: original dry paint (milk paint, worn through at handle and rims), grime-darkened patina, consistent oxidisation inside and out. Refinished or stained surfaces reduce value sharply.
- **Handle**: heart, tulip or scalloped cut-outs are folk-art pluses — but the handle must show the same age and wear as the box. A pale, crisp-edged handle in a dark box is a replacement.
- **1970s décor**: stencilled words ("CRACKERS", "NAPKINS"), dark stain, gold paint, hinged handle, staples, plywood bottoms.

## Makers, brands and variants
Mostly unmarked American country (New England, Pennsylvania German, Midwest). Shaker cutlery trays (fine, thin, dovetailed, pale finish) are the premium. Mid-century: Treasure Craft, Baribocraft (Canada), Woodcroftery (Holland MI) produced varnished novelty trays; new farmhouse repros (Hobby Lobby, Antique Farmhouse).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Pine (most common), poplar, maple, oak, chestnut. Old pine is oxidised orange-brown on exposed surfaces and pale under fresh scratches. New "distressed" pieces have uniform fake wear and fresh saw marks.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Replaced dividers/handles (common, -50% or more), refinishing, split bottoms, missing nails, insect damage, overpaint. Small shrinkage cracks are normal.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Large reproduction market: distressed, painted "primitive" trays with heart handles sold by décor shops; also old boxes fitted with new handles (as in the example here).

## Market
Steady among country/primitive collectors and farmhouse decorators (Etsy, eBay, antique malls). Painted examples sell in weeks; plain ones slowly.

## What sellers usually miss
Original paint is the money: sellers often clean or paint over it. Owners with genuinely old painted trays sometimes price them as "old wooden box $20".

## Shipping / handling
Light (2–4 lb), 12–18" long: $12–18 boxed. Fits easily.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Dimensions, wood, construction (dovetails/nails), surface (original/refinished), handle original or replaced, age estimate.
