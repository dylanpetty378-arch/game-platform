## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Lights required by collision rules: **port (red, left)**, **starboard (green, right)**, **masthead/steaming (white)**, **stern (white)**, **anchor (white, all-round)**. Two families:
1. **Small-boat running lights** (1920s–1970s): chrome-plated bronze or brass, often teardrop or box shape, screwed to the hull/cabin; Perko, Wilcox Crittenden, Attwood, Aqua Signal. Small, 3–8 in.
2. **Ship lanterns** (1890s–1960s): 12–24 in copper/brass with a ring handle on top, cylindrical or fan-shaped body, thick **ribbed Fresnel glass** lens (red, green or clear), vent chimney, a nameplate ("PORT", "STARBOARD", "MASTHEAD") and maker plate. Originally oil, many later electrified or converted to table lamps.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Perko** (Perkins Marine Lamp & Hardware, founded Brooklyn 1907, later Miami) – the biggest American name; cast plate "Perko" or "Perkins Marine Lamp Corp. Brooklyn NY"; bronze running lights, masthead lights, ship lanterns.
- **Wilcox Crittenden** (Middletown CT, 1847) – boat hardware; running lights marked "W-C" or full name.
- **Davey & Co. London** (and Davey "Ship Lamp") – British, much-faked name.
- **Seahorse**, **Tung Woo** (Hong Kong), **Den Haan Rotterdam**, **Meteorite**, **Griffiths**, **Eclipse**, **Nippon** (Japanese) – real makers of ship lanterns, mid-century; Tung Woo and Den Haan common on salvaged ships.
- **Attwood**, **Aqua Signal** (Germany) – later small-boat lights, low value.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Genuine: heavy cast or thick spun copper/brass, riveted/brazed construction, real Fresnel glass (cold, heavy, mold-ribbed, often with maker name molded in), removable burner/fount or bulb socket, USCG/approval markings on later ones, patina consistent with use. Repros: thin pressed brass, glossy lacquer, plastic or thin flat colored glass, "badge" plate stamped with "Nippon 1937" / "Davey & Co London" / "Ship Lantern 1942" in generic font, Phillips screws, made in India (Roorkee/Moradabad) 1990s–present.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Cracked or replaced lenses (big value hit), missing burner/fount, dents, crude electrification (drilled holes), green corrosion, chrome pitting on small lights. Polished-to-death brass is acceptable to decorators but reduces collector value.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Indian decorative "ship lanterns" are the dominant supply on Craigslist and Facebook. Fake maker plates are the tell; real plates are integral or riveted with period typography and no "made in India" sticker. Perko still makes running lights – a modern Perko is just a parts item ($20–60 new). Converted lamps (lantern on a base with a cord) are decor only.

## Market
Nautical decor buyers (coastal homes, bars) plus boat restorers. Moderate volume; genuine pairs sell in 1–3 weeks, repros sit. Mild spring/summer bump.

## What sellers usually miss
Real ship lanterns from estates are listed as "old brass lamp"; the molded maker name on the glass lens and riveted plates go unmentioned. Also: vintage Perko bronze running light pairs from boat cleanouts sold cheap in "boat parts" lots.

## Shipping / handling
Glass lenses break; remove and wrap lens separately if possible. Large lanterns 8–15 lb packed, $20–35. Small boat lights ship for ~$10–15. Fits a passenger seat.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker (Perko / Wilcox Crittenden / Tung Woo / Davey) + position (port / starboard / masthead / anchor) + "ship lantern"/"running light" + copper/brass/bronze + "pair" + oil/electric + height. Junk: "nautical decor", "antique brass lamp", "Nippon" repro as antique. `Perko Port Starboard Running Lights Pair Bronze Fresnel Glass Brooklyn NY Vintage`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (coastal decor buyers, restorers; genuine pairs sell in 1–3 weeks, spring/summer bump); Indian repros FB local.
- **Price position:** at the sold median ($20–60 single boat light, $60–150 pair; $100–250 ship lanterns; $200–500 Davey/matched pairs), accept −15%, floor 75%; OBO; cut 15% after 30 days.
- **Photos:** lantern on plain background; maker plate; lens with moulded maker name; burner or socket; rivets and seams; back and mount; pair side by side; dents; next to a tape.
- **State:** maker from plate and lens marks, position, oil or electric, original vs replacement glass, dimensions, electrified or not, dents and repairs by photo number, repro if thin brass.
- **Pack / haul:** carry-on, large lanterns 8–15 lb $20–35, small boat lights $10–15; remove and wrap the lens separately when possible, burner immobilized, double-box.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Genuine [Perko] [port/starboard] pair, Brooklyn-made [era] bronze with the original Fresnel lenses marked on the glass; the Indian decor copies sell for [$] and matched working pairs bring [$]."
