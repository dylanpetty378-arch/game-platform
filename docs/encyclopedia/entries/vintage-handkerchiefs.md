## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Cotton/linen/silk squares (10–16 in). Collectible eras: 1930s–1960s printed cottons. Designer signature printed in a corner (Tammis Keefe, Kimball, Burmel, Pat Prichard, Faith Austin). Souvenir hankies print fair buildings, logos and dates ("A Century of Progress 1933–34 Chicago", 1939 New York World's Fair).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Tammis Keefe (1940s–50s)**: whimsical prints, signed; $10–40 each.
- **Kimball, Burmel, Pat Prichard, Carol Stanley, Faith Austin, Jeanne Hamm**: $8–25.
- **World's Fair**: 1933 Chicago Century of Progress, 1939 NY $15–50; 1904 St. Louis earlier/linen $40–120.
- **Lace/whitework**: Appenzell, Brussels, Marghab (Madeira) $30–150.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Hand-rolled hems on quality; machine overlocked hems on cheap. Signatures and corner labels. Color fastness.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Stains, foxing, holes, fold-line yellowing; washing can remove.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Few; modern Chinese reprints of 1950s designs exist.
