# Hickory-shaft golf clubs (pre-1935)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: knowledge of eBay/Green Jacket Auctions sold ranges; George Nicoll "hand" cleekmark reference; no dated comp pulled

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Wooden (hickory) shafts, leather wrap grips, forged iron heads with a "cleekmark" stamp: Nicoll (hand), Gibson (star), Stewart (pipe), Spalding, MacGregor, Wright & Ditson. Putters, mashies, niblicks. Smooth-faced (pre-1905) and rut irons are the early valuable ones; 1920s–30s dot/line-faced irons are common. Reproduction "hickory" clubs (St Andrews Golf Co, modern) have perfect finishes and no pitting. Woods: Tom Stewart, Auchterlonie, Forgan – look for a maker stamp on the crown.

## Additional research (earlier entry: Hickory-shaft golf clubs (pre-1935))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: caddiehq hickory value guide, hickory-golf.co.uk sales, eBay listings Oct 2026

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Wooden (hickory) shaft, leather wrap grip, forged iron head with a "cleek mark" (maker's symbol) on the back and often "Hand Forged in Scotland"; or persimmon long-nose/bulger woods. Makers to look for: Tom Stewart (pipe mark), Gibson of Kinghorn (star), George Nicoll of Leven (hand), Spalding, MacGregor, Forgan, Anderson. Seller words: "antique golf club", "wood shaft putter", "hickory".

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | Burke, Kro-Flite, unmarked, rusty, replaced grips | $20–75 | most listings |
| Decent | Stewart/Spalding/MacGregor/Nicoll mashies, niblicks, putters, clean | $75–200 | playable "hickory golf" market |
| Collectible | Jiggers, cleeks, rut niblicks, matched sets, good persimmon woods | $200–750 | |
| Top | 19th-c long-nose woods, patented specialty clubs, player provenance | $1,000–10,000+ | |

### Comps (sold, dated)
- caddiehq tiers above; eBay Oct 2026 listings: George Nicoll "Gem" No. 9 putter hickory 34.5" – asking ~$80–120; Nicoll niblick – asking ~$60

### What sellers usually miss
The cleek mark and the model name ("Whippet", "Gem", "Zenith"). Most list "old wooden golf club $20".

### Shipping / handling
~1 lb, 40" long – tube, ~$20 in. eBay 13%.

### Buy rule
Buy when: recognised maker mark visible, original grip, ≤ $30. Pass when: priced $75+ (seller knows) – resale ceiling for a single common-maker club is ~$100.
