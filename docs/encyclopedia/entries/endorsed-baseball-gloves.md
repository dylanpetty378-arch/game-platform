## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1940s–70s store-model gloves with a facsimile player signature stamped in the palm/pocket and a model number (Rawlings: MM, MM5, MM9, MMF, DF1, XPG; Wilson A2000 series; Spalding; MacGregor). Rawlings "Personal Model" or "Professional Model" and "Made in USA" on the back. Sellers write "old baseball mitt", "<player> glove", "vintage Rawlings glove".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Rawlings** (St. Louis): Mantle MM/MM5/MM9 Professional (collectible), DF1/MMF (decent), XPG; "Big Leaguer" youth line ($50–100 for a 1964 Mantle youth).
- **Wilson**: A2000 (introduced late 1950s; 1957–60s examples collectible), A2000 later years are user gloves.
- **Spalding, MacGregor**: endorsed store models; value follows the player.
- Player hierarchy: Mantle, Mays, Clemente, Koufax top; Kaline, Killebrew mid-tier; minor players junk tier.
- Top: 1950s Mantle "Triple Crown" box set, pre-war Ruth/Gehrig/DiMaggio, game-used gloves.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
USA-made steerhide/cowhide gloves are thicker with sharper stamping; 1970s–80s Japanese/Korean imports thinner and lower value. Professional Model vs youth/"Big Leaguer" stamped in the palm or heel. Original lacing has even age; patent numbers and logos changed over time and help date a glove (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dry/cracked leather, broken or replaced lacing, ink names written on the glove, mould, flattened pocket. Condition drives price more than player. Relacing is common and lowers collector value (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Rawlings and Wilson have issued heritage/reissue gloves; added "autographs" on facsimile-stamped gloves are a risk (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Steady eBay market among sports-memorabilia collectors and nostalgic buyers; Mantle gloves are plentiful. Spring (opening day) and Father's Day may help (general knowledge, verify). Top pieces go through REA/Heritage.

## What sellers usually miss
The model number and the signature in the palm; "Professional Model" vs youth; USA-made; original box multiplies value. Craigslist sellers often DO name the player — then price is usually fair.

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb, $10–12 each way. eBay. Condition (lacing, cracking, writing) drives price more than player.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "vintage" + maker + model number + player + size + "Professional Model" + "USA" + hand (RHT/LHT) + era. Junk: "old glove", "baseball mitt lot", "antique". `Vintage Rawlings MM9 Mickey Mantle Professional Model 12in Glove USA RHT`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (list before opening day / Father's Day); REA/Heritage for boxed 1950s Mantle and pre-war Ruth/Gehrig.
- **Price position:** at the sold median for the model (DF1/MMF $50–120; MM/MM5/MM9 $150–400), accept −10%, floor 80%; OBO; cut 15% after 30 days.
- **Photos:** palm with signature stamp; model number; back with "Made in USA"/patent marks; lacing and web; heel and finger stalls; any writing or cracking; box if present; next to a ruler.
- **State:** maker, model number, player, size in inches, hand, country of manufacture, lacing original or replaced, leather condition (dry/cracked/supple), writing, box, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** shoebox, 1–2 lb, $10–12; stuff the pocket, wrap in paper, no double-box.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Rawlings [model] [player] Professional Model, USA-made [1960s], original lacing and supple leather; the Japanese store models go for [$] and USA [player] pro models bring [$]."

## Buy rule
Buy when: Mantle/Mays/Clemente/Koufax USA-made pro model ≤ $40, or boxed example ≤ $100. Pass when: youth models, repaired lacing, or price ≥ $45 for DF1/MMF class. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Fits a passenger seat.
