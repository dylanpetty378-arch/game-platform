# Metal lunch boxes (Aladdin, Thermos/King-Seeley, Universal, ADCO), 1950–1985
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay listing 176418919499 (1959 Aladdin Gunsmoke, ask $1,150 OBO), appraisily.com lunch box value guide (auction lots of 2–4 boxes $300–425, mint = 2–3x good), history.com "nine most collectible lunch boxes", tastingtable value lists, general collector knowledge (Scott Bruce price guide tiers).

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Steel box ~8x7x4 in, lithographed on all sides, wire-and-plastic handle, two clasps; dome-tops (1950s–60s) look like a loaf of bread. Maker is embossed on the side or bottom: **Aladdin Industries** (Nashville), **American Thermos / King-Seeley Thermos (KST)**, **Universal (Landers, Frary & Clark)**, **ADCO Liberty**, **Ohio Art**, **Okay Industries**. Matching steel-and-glass thermos (pre-1970s) or plastic thermos (1970s+) roughly doubles value. Sellers' titles usually just say "vintage lunch box" + character name.

Key year tells: **Gunsmoke** – 1959 Aladdin (Matt Dillon standing, Dodge City street scene, embossed) is the valuable one; 1962 "double-L" and 1972/73 (Marshal Dillon on a grey horse with Festus, yellow prairie) are common. **Star Trek** 1968 dome = top; 1979 flat = common. **Beatles** 1965 Aladdin blue = top.

## What sellers usually miss
The year/version. Same character, different decades, 10x price difference (Gunsmoke 1959 vs 1973; Star Trek 1968 dome vs 1979). Sellers also don't photograph the bottom (rust) or the inside (stickers removed, scratches). A box with the original thermos AND cup is listed as "lunch box" with no mention of thermos surprisingly often.

## Shipping / handling
~1.5 lb, 12x10x6 box, $12–18 each way. eBay is the market (13%). Wrap the thermos separately; glass liners break.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 1950s–60s box with intact graphics under $75, or any dome-top under $50, or a known Top/Collectible title under 25% of its sold range. Pass when: 1975+ flat boxes at any price over $15, rust-through, or seller already names the year and prices at eBay level. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
