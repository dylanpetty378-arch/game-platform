# Vintage & fashion wristwatches (Elgin, Bulova, Hamilton, Tissot, Seiko, Citizen, Invicta, Swatch)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay DE listings 2026-10 (1956 Bulova 23j 10BPAC 10K RGP "for repair" bidding €36; 1960s Elgin automatic gold-filled running listings €40–90), EBTH lot description (Hamilton Thinline 10K GF + 2 Bulova, non-running, typical estate lot), sweep listings 2026-10 (Elgin self-winding 17j diamond-dial $200 ask, Tissot PR50 $50, 1940s Swiss mechanical $125, Hamilton & Bulova ladies pair $50, Invicta 38491 $80, Citizen Skyhawk $200, Pop Swatch $100, Phillip Stein $200, Nixon Time Teller $100).

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Dial and caseback are the whole story. Tells:
- **American mid-century (Elgin, Bulova, Hamilton, Gruen, Benrus, Waltham, Wittnauer)** 1940s–60s: 10K gold-filled or rolled-gold-plate cases, 17–23 jewels, small (31–34 mm). Running men's examples **$40–120**; "diamond dial" Elgin/Bulova (tiny chips at the markers) $60–150; **Hamilton Electric** (Ventura, Pacer, Altair – asymmetric cases) $300–1,500; **Bulova Accutron** tuning fork (Spaceview skeleton dial) $150–500; **Hamilton 992B/railroad pocket watches** $250–500. Solid **14K gold** cases: melt value (~$300–600) sets the floor – look for "14K" not "14K GF/RGP".
- **Swiss**: Tissot PR50/PR100 quartz $50–100; Tissot/Omega/Longines mechanical 1950s–60s $150–600 (Omega Seamaster/Constellation $500–2,000); **Rolex/Tudor** at any CL price = authenticate (dial, caseback, reference, serial between lugs).
- **Japanese**: Seiko 6139/6138 chronographs ($250–600), 6105/6309 divers ($400–1,500), Seiko 5 ($30–80), Grand Seiko/King Seiko ($500–2,000); Citizen Eco-Drive Skyhawk/Promaster $120–250; Orient $50–120.
- **Fashion/junk**: Invicta (any model) $20–60 regardless of "retail $495"; Fossil, Guess, MK, Nixon $15–60; Swatch standard $15–40 (early 1980s rare models and Pop Swatch $40–100; Kiki Picasso/art specials $500+); Phillip Stein $60–150; "Railmaster homage" / Steeldive / Pagani $40–80.
- **Military**: WWII A-11 (Elgin/Bulova/Waltham) $150–400; Vietnam-era Benrus/Hamilton GG-W-113 $300–600; Rolex/Panerai/Omega military – auction.

## What sellers usually miss
Solid gold vs gold-filled (14K stamp inside caseback), Accutron tuning-fork movement in a plain Bulova, Hamilton Electric asymmetric cases listed as "old Hamilton", Seiko chronograph references. Conversely, sellers of Invicta/Phillip Stein always overprice vs retail.

## Shipping / handling
Tiny: $6–10 each way. eBay 13% (or Chrono24 for Collectible+). Non-running = pass (Dylan: no repair).

## Buy rule
Buy when: Accutron/Hamilton Electric/Seiko 6139/Omega/Longines mechanical under $75 and described as running; solid 14K case under melt; WWII A-11 under $60. Pass when: gold-filled Elgin/Bulova/Hamilton over $40, anything "not running", Invicta/Phillip Stein/Nixon/Citizen/Tissot quartz at any price (no spread). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.

## Additional research (earlier entry: Vintage mechanical wristwatches (Bulova, Elgin, Hamilton, Seiko, Omega))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold listings (agent knowledge Oct 2026)

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Small (30–36 mm) gold-filled or steel cases, manual wind. Brand on dial; the movement and case-back stamps ("10K RGP", "14K", "Swiss", movement calibre) decide value. Craigslist sellers price from the brand name alone.

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | 1950s–60s Bulova/Elgin/Benrus/Gruen gold-filled dress, running | $40–120 | $200 asks are common and wrong |
| Decent | Bulova Accutron 214/218 (tuning fork, running), Hamilton Electric, Seiko 6139/6138 chronograph, Elgin/Hamilton WWII military (A-11, 987A) | $150–400 | |
| Collectible | Omega Seamaster/Constellation 60s, Rolex Oyster Precision, Longines, Heuer, Seiko 6105 diver, Bulova Spaceview | $500–2,500 | authenticate movement |
| Top | Rolex Submariner/GMT, Omega Speedmaster, Heuer Autavia/Carrera, Patek, 14K/18K solid gold cases | $3,000+ | outside scope unless under $200 and obviously genuine |

### What sellers usually miss
Model names (Accutron, Spaceview, Seamaster, Electric) and solid gold ("14K" vs "10K RGP/GF"). Military dial markings. Chronograph pushers on Seikos. "Vintage men's watch – see pictures" with no brand is where the finds are.

### Shipping / handling
Ounces, $8 insured. eBay authenticity guarantee kicks in over $2,000.

### Buy rule
Buy when: Accutron/Seiko chrono/Omega/Longines/military-marked at ≤ 30 % of tier, running. Pass when: generic 1950s Bulova/Elgin asking over $60, non-running, "law enforcement" or quartz novelty watches. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
