## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Ceiling and pendant lights. The photo tells you most of it:
- **Builder-grade (low)**: white "alabaster swirl" acid-etched glass bowls, faux-bronze chain and canopy, "Tuscan" scroll arms, brushed-nickel 3-light bars. Made in China for Home Depot/Lowe's brands (Hampton Bay, Portfolio, Progress, Sea Gull).
- **Mid-century originals (high)**: spun aluminium in bright enamel colours, layered shades (PH), bubble-plastic over wire frame (Nelson), brass-and-walnut Lightolier/Thurston, Italian Stilnovo with coloured perspex, Danish Raak/Fog & Mørup glass. Labels inside canopy or on socket: "Lightolier", "Howard Miller", "Louis Poulsen", "Made in Italy".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Louis Poulsen (Denmark)**: PH5, PH Artichoke, Toldbod.
- **Howard Miller / Herman Miller (George Nelson Bubble lamps, 1950s–70s originals have Howard Miller label)**.
- **Lightolier, Gerald Thurston for Lightolier, Lightcraft of California, Moe Light, Prescolite (US)**.
- **Stilnovo, Arteluce, Artemide, Flos, Fontana Arte (Italy)**; **Koch & Lowy, Laurel, Kovacs (US 1970s)**.
- **Builder-grade**: Hampton Bay, Portfolio, Sea Gull, Progress, Westinghouse, Kichler (some Kichler better).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Heavy spun metal, real brass, hand-blown or cased glass = quality. Stamped thin steel, "oil-rubbed bronze" paint, acid-etched swirl glass = mass.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Brittle cloth wiring on vintage pieces (rewire needed), dents in spun aluminium, cracked glass, missing canopy hardware, paint loss.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Licensed reissues (Modernica Nelson Bubble, Louis Poulsen current production) and Chinese replicas of PH5/Artichoke/Sputnik/Bubble are everywhere — check labels, weight and wear.

## Market
Builder-grade: slow, local-only economics. MCM designer: strong demand, fast.

## What sellers usually miss
Unlabelled-looking Lightolier/Thurston pieces sold as "retro lamps"; real Nelson bubble lamps in garages. Lens matches to $1,000+ designer pieces for builder-grade glass are a common false positive.

## Shipping / handling
Glass shades: double box, 8–15 lb boxed, $20–35. Spun aluminium dents easily.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker (Louis Poulsen / Howard Miller Nelson / Lightolier / Stilnovo) + model (PH5 / Bubble saucer / Thurston) + "pendant"/"flush mount"/"sconce" + diameter + era + "label". Junk: "retro lamp", "Sputnik style" (unless marked), "MCM" alone for builder-grade. `Louis Poulsen PH5 Pendant Light Poul Henningsen Denmark Original Label 20in`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for labelled mid-century pieces; 1stDibs/Chairish for Nelson/Poulsen/Stilnovo at higher decorator prices (slower, higher fee). Builder-grade FB local only.
- **Price position:** at the sold median ($100–400 Lightolier/Thurston/Lightcraft; $200–2,000 Nelson/PH5/Stilnovo), accept −10%, floor 80%; OBO; cut 15% after 30 days; builder-grade $10–40 local, firm.
- **Photos:** fixture lit; label inside the canopy or shade; shade diameter and drop against a tape; canopy and chain/cord; socket and wiring; dents in spun aluminium or glass chips close up; underside.
- **State:** maker from the label, model, diameter and drop, wiring original or rewired, canopy and hardware included, bulb type, dents/chips/repairs by photo number, "no label — attributed" where that's the case.
- **Pack / haul:** carry-on, 8–15 lb boxed; glass shades double-boxed with the shade separate from the canopy, spun aluminium padded (dents easily), $20–35.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Louis Poulsen PH5 / Howard Miller Nelson Bubble] with the original label, [era], rewired and complete with canopy; the current production is [$] new and labelled originals bring [$] on 1stDibs."
