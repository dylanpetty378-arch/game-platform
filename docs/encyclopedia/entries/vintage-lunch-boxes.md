# Lunch boxes (metal 1950s–80s, vinyl, 1990s character)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: knowledge of eBay sold ranges; no dated comp pulled

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Metal boxes (Aladdin, Thermos/King-Seeley, ADCO) with matching steel/glass thermos are the collectible tier: 1950s–60s Westerns (Roy Rogers, Lone Ranger), Disney, Beatles, Star Trek 1968, Jetsons. Vinyl boxes 1960s–70s (Barbie, Monkees) $50–200 if not cracked. 1980s metal (Dukes of Hazzard, Pac-Man, ET) $30–80; 1985 Rambo was the last metal. 1990s plastic/vinyl (Godzilla 1998, Jurassic Park, Power Rangers) $15–40 with thermos. Condition rules everything: rust, dents, missing thermos cut 50–70%.

## What sellers usually miss
The thermos (worth 30–50% of the set) and the 1950s–60s dates. "Old metal lunch box" titles with a visible Western/space theme are the ones.

## Shipping / handling
2–3 lb, $10–14.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 1950s–60s metal box with thermos under $60, or a 1960s vinyl under $25. Pass when: 1990s boxes at any price (ceiling $40). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
