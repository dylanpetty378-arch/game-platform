## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Small ceramic or glass mugs, plates, bowls and divided feeding dishes for children, decorated with transfers/decals of children at play, animals, nursery rhymes, alphabets, comic characters.
- **ABC plates (1820–1900, Staffordshire)**: alphabet moulded or transfer-printed around the rim, central transfer scene.
- **American semi-vitreous (1900–1940)**: Homer Laughlin, Knowles Taylor Knowles, Smith-Phillips, Salem — decal of children, gold trim, cream/ivory body.
- **Roseville Juvenile (1916–1930s)**: creamware with stencilled chicks, rabbits, Sunbonnet babies, "Rv" ink stamp.
- **Buffalo Pottery Deldare/Campbell Kids** (1910s): marked "Buffalo Pottery".
- **Royal Doulton Bunnykins** (1934–present, Barbara Vernon signature on early pieces), **Wedgwood Peter Rabbit** (1949–2000s).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Early transfers have fine stippled printing with hand-tinted colour (ABC plates); 1920s–30s decals show halftone-free flat colour areas; later decals (1950s+) are crisp and bright. Crazing and toning indicate age (but also reduce value). Gold trim wear is normal.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rim chips, hairlines (most common, check with light), handle cracks, decal wear from washing, crazing and staining. Restorations on Roseville/Shelley.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reproduction ABC plates (1970s–present); Royal Bayreuth fake marks; Bunnykins reissues (dates in backstamp).

## Market
Low-moderate; buyers are collectors of specific makers and nursery decorators. Sell-through ~35%; 3–6 weeks.

## What sellers usually miss
Roseville Juvenile "Rv" stamp on plain-looking chick/rabbit mugs; Buffalo Pottery marks; Shelley's Attwell; "Barbara Vernon" signature on early Bunnykins.
