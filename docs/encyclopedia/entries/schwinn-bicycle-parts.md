## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Original parts from 1930s–70s Schwinns. **Chainguards**: stamped steel with Schwinn script or model name (Phantom, Jaguar, Corvette, Sting-Ray, Typhoon, Fastback) — original paint, model decal or embossed script; Sting-Ray chainguards "Sting-Ray" script; Krate chainguards with "Krate" decal; Phantom chainguards with gold "Phantom" script. **Saddles**: Mesinger, Persons and Troxel made Schwinn saddles — look under the pan for "Mesinger" or "Persons" + "Schwinn Approved"; studs around skirt (1950s); Sting-Ray "Solo Polo" banana seats (white/silver striped, Schwinn script); Krate seats with sissy bar. **Phantom/Black Phantom**: 1949–59 balloon tire — tank with horn, chrome fenders, springer forks, front carrier. **Sting-Ray**: 1963–81, high-rise bars, banana seats, slick tires, Stik shifters.

**Krate series** (BikeHistory.org catalogs): 1968 Orange Krate, Lemon Peeler, Apple Krate; Pea Picker added 1969; Cotton Picker (white) 1970–71; 1972 four models; 1973 Orange Krate, Lemon Peeler, Apple Krate only. Krate-specific parts: spring-suspension front fork, 16 × 1¾" front tire, 20 × 2.125" rear Slik tire (Gripper Slik 1969), 5-speed Stik-Shift (numbered knob on later versions), large aluminium front drum brake on 5-speeds, rear caliper (1972–73 rear disc brake), "full-floating" striped bucket saddle with spring strut (deeper bucket from 1970), chrome fenders (1969–70), "MAG" sprocket (1968–70). Coaster-brake Krates offered from 1970. The 1973 5-speed weighed 49 lb.

**How a part tells its bike**: chainguard script names the model; Krate seats are short "bucket" bananas, standard Sting-Ray seats are long Solo Polo bananas; 16" front rims mean Krate; Phantom parts are balloon-tire (26 × 2.125) era with chrome and accessories.

Dating: serials sit on the bottom of the crank (bottom-bracket) shell, the left rear dropout, or the head tube under the badge, depending on era; from the 1960s the handlebar stem and crank are often stamped with a two-digit year (e.g. "67"), which can be off by a year; pre-1960 stems/cranks generally weren't year-stamped (RatRodBikes forum, not factory records). Some years carry a four-digit day code by the "I" on the head badge.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Schwinn (Chicago)**: date codes on parts (stamped two-letter codes on bars/stems, Schwinn script stamps); serials on dropouts/bottom bracket. (Forum consensus: stems/cranks from the 1960s show a two-digit year stamp — see above.)
- **Mesinger** (NY): saddles (1940s–70s), "Mesinger" stamped pan.
- **Persons Majestic** (Worcester, MA): saddles, Sting-Ray seats (Persons stamped).
- **Troxel**: lower-grade saddles.
- **Bendix**: kickback 2-speed hubs (Sting-Ray), coaster hubs.
- **Delta** (lights, horns), **Stewart-Warner** (speedos), **Torrington** (pedals).
- **Sturmey-Archer** 3-speed hubs on Sting-Ray/middleweights, **Huret/Schwinn-approved** derailleurs on 5-speeds (general knowledge, verify).
- **Reissues**: Schwinn Krate revival 2020 (Coal Krate, Orange Krate, Grape Krate; Amazon-exclusive, runs of ~500; the Coal Krate never existed as a 1960s model — BikeHistory.org). Revival parts can turn up in "vintage Krate parts" lots.

Value nuances: Krate parts over plain Sting-Ray; 5-speed Stik-Shift pieces (shifter, drum brake, correct hub) over coaster; Cotton Picker and Pea Picker (shorter runs) over Orange/Apple; Phantom/Black Phantom chrome pieces; original paint colour matching the model (Lemon, Orange, Apple Red, Pea Green, white).

### Quick ID checklist
- Underside of seat: maker stamp (Persons, Mesinger) and Schwinn approval mark; pan shape and hardware.
- Chainguard: script/decal names the model; check mounting tabs are intact and no overspray.
- Stem and crank: two-digit year stamp (1960s on) — does it fit the claimed model year?
- Front rim/tyre size: 16" front = Krate; 20" Slik rear with correct lettering.
- Stik shifter: Schwinn script on the housing, knob style, cable intact.
- Chrome: deep and even with age-consistent wear vs bright thin repro chrome; fresh chrome next to oxidised hardware = replaced parts (Appraisily).
- "Made in Taiwan/China", no stamps, lighter gauge steel = reproduction.
- Paint: overspray at joints or over serial stamps and decals under fresh clearcoat suggest a repaint (Appraisily).

### Golden-egg tells
- Original 1968–73 Krate springer fork arms with the Atom drum-brake peg hardware — $425 asking vs $75 for a generic Schwinn springer assembly (~5×) — brake-stop peg on the fork arm, Schwinn "S"-head bolts, deep aged chrome (https://neloscycles.com/Vintage-Bicycle-Parts-Krate-Springer/)
- 1972–73 rear disc-brake Krate parts (caliper, disc hub) — a disc-brake Apple Krate made $1,000 vs $250–325 ordinary Sting-Rays — rear disc rotor and caliper on the 20 in wheel, 1972–73 serial (https://appraisily.com/articles/schwinn-krate-bike-value-color-year-parts-and-restoration-clues/)
- 5-speed Stik-Shift with numbered knob — $150–300 vs $40–120 standard Sting-Ray parts — Schwinn script on the shifter housing, T-handle knob numbered 1–5, intact cable (entry comp)
- 16 × 1¾ in front wheel with the cast aluminium Krate drum brake — Krate-only, $200–500 — small front rim and a large finned alloy hub marked Atom/Schwinn Approved (entry comp)
- Cotton Picker (white, 1970–71) and Pea Picker (green, 1969–73) colour-matched parts (chainguard, fenders, seat) — ~1.5–2× the same Orange/Apple Krate part (short runs) — the "Cotton Picker"/"Pea Picker" chainguard decal and factory white/pea-green paint with age-consistent wear (https://bikehistory.org/bikes/krates/)
- Black Phantom tank with working horn and chrome fenders (1949–59) — $250–800 vs $10–40 middleweight bits — balloon-tire 26 × 2.125 era, horn button on the tank, gold "Phantom" script chainguard (entry comp)
- Mesinger or Persons "Schwinn Approved" studded saddle with original cover — $40–120 vs $37 plain leather saddle median — maker stamp under the pan, nickel studs around the skirt, heat-sealed vinyl seams (entry comp)
- Year-stamped originality marks that prove the part: "7893-68" on bars, two-digit year on stem/crank, "S7"/"S2" on rims, seat tag "D-2xxxxx" — a dated original sells over an undated repro of the same part — read the stamp in the photo (https://neloscycles.com/Vintage-Bicycle-Parts-Krate-Springer/ ; https://ratrodbikes.com/threads/how-do-you-put-a-production-date-on-a-schwinn.11491)

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original chrome is deep and bright; re-chromed or repro chrome is thinner. Original paint colors (Coppertone, Flamboyant Red, Radiant Blue, Sky Blue, Violet). Original decals vs reproductions (repro decals bright vinyl). Original seats: stamped pans, original stitching; repro seats lighter and have Taiwan/China marks.

More: original seat covers are vinyl with heat-sealed seams and correct stripe pattern for the year; a new cover can look convincing in one photograph (Appraisily), so ask for the pan and the cover's underside. Original tyres carry Schwinn lettering (Slik, Typhoon, Westwind). Aluminium Krate front drum hubs are cast and marked; aged parts should age together.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chrome pitting/rust, dented guards and fenders, cracked/split saddle covers, rusted springs, faded/sun-bleached paint, broken mounting tabs. Restoration reduces purist value; "survivor" originals are the target. Tyres and cables are wear items — replacements should be disclosed (Appraisily). Re-covered seats are common and sell below original-cover seats.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Massive reproduction market: Sting-Ray seats, Krate shifters, sissy bars, chainguards, decals, Phantom tanks. Repro tells: no date code, lighter metal, flatter stamping, "Made in Taiwan". Schwinn 2000s reissue Sting-Ray/Krate bikes (2020 Krate revival documented by BikeHistory.org). Repro graphics do not prove original paint (Appraisily).

## Market
Deep and knowledgeable; Krate/Sting-Ray parts sell fast; middleweight slower. Spring/summer peak. Buyers are restorers completing bikes — they pay for correct-year, correct-model originals.

## What sellers usually miss
Mesinger/Persons stamps under saddles; Schwinn date codes; Bendix 2-speed hubs; original Phantom chainguards on "old bike parts" lots. Also: 16" Krate front wheels/drum brakes and Stik shifters mixed into generic lots.

## Shipping / handling
Chainguards and saddles ship $10–20; fenders sets $20–35 (long box); forks $20–30. All fit a passenger seat; whole bikes do not.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Model, stamps and date codes, original vs repro, chrome grade, mounting tabs intact, photos of underside.

### What to tell the buyer
Name the part and the model/years it fits (e.g. "fits 1968–73 Schwinn Krate"), quote every stamp exactly (maker, Schwinn mark, year stamp), state original vs reproduction and original vs re-covered/re-chromed, describe chrome (pitting, rust) and paint, confirm mounting tabs and threads are intact, and give measurements (rim size, seat length). Photograph the underside, stamps and every flaw. Keywords: Schwinn, original, vintage, Sting-Ray, Krate, muscle bike, Chicago, plus the model name.
