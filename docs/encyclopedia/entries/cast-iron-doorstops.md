# Cast iron doorstops & figures (Hubley, Bradley & Hubbard, National Foundry)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: craigslist 1836 photos; eBay sold pattern (knowledge 2024–25); Bertoia Auctions doorstop sales (reference)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1900s–40s painted cast iron: Boston terriers (Hubley, two-piece joined with a slotted screw, 8–10"), Scotties, cats, flower baskets, ships, cottages, Aunt Jemima. Hubley marks are inside/under the base ("HUBLEY" and a number); Bradley & Hubbard "B&H". Reproductions (1970s–today) are rough-cast, lighter, with Phillips screws and bright fresh paint. Sellers write "cast iron dog", "antique doorstop", "Hubley".

## What sellers usually miss
Original paint vs repaint (everything is in the paint); Hubley mark; two-piece construction with slotted screw = period. Weight (repros are lighter).

5–10 lb, $15–25 each way; wrap well, ears snap. eBay.

## Buy rule
Buy when: original-paint Hubley/B&H figure ≤ $50, or flower-basket doorstop ≤ $80. Pass when: repainted, replacement Phillips screw, or chips through to bare iron on the face. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

## Additional research (earlier entry: Cast iron doorstops (Hubley, Albany Foundry, Bradley & Hubbard))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: chairish.com Hubley listings (dog figurals $225–975 asking), 1stDibs Hubley flower-basket listings, frenchgardenhouse.com, eBay sold knowledge

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1900–1940 painted cast iron figurals: flower baskets and urns (most common), dogs (Boston terrier, Scottie, fox terrier, setter), cats, ships, cottages, people (Aunt Jemima, Colonial ladies, Mammy, golfer). Tells of originals: two-piece or flat-back casting with a worn, chalky **original paint** showing iron through at high points; crisp detail; base often marked "HUBLEY" with a number (e.g. 456 Petunias and Asters, 221 Nasturtium basket), or "B&H" (Bradley & Hubbard – the best), "Albany Fdy", "Judd", "Spencer", "National Foundry". Reproductions (1970s–today): shiny bright paint, grainy rough casting, loose fit between halves, Phillips screws, light weight. Sellers write "antique doorstop", "cast iron flower basket", "Victorian".

### What sellers usually miss
Maker mark on the back/base and subject rarity. "Two old doorstops" lots sometimes hide a B&H or a figural dog among baskets.

### Shipping / handling
3–8 lb each, Priority Flat Rate medium fits most: $17–25. eBay 13%. Wrap paint surfaces – flaking loses value.

### Buy rule
Buy when: figural dog/cat/ship/cottage with original paint under $40; flower basket 8"+ with good paint under $30; any B&H-marked piece under $100. Pass when: baskets priced $40+ each (margin gone after shipping), repro tells. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
