## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1900–1940s painted cast iron figurals: flower baskets and urns (most common), dogs (Boston terrier – Hubley, two-piece joined with a slotted screw, 8–10" – Scottie, fox terrier, setter), cats, ships, cottages, people (Aunt Jemima, Colonial ladies, Mammy, golfer). Tells of originals: two-piece or flat-back casting with a worn, chalky **original paint** showing iron through at high points; crisp detail; base often marked "HUBLEY" with a number (inside/under the base; e.g. 456 Petunias and Asters, 221 Nasturtium basket), or "B&H" (Bradley & Hubbard – the best), "Albany Fdy", "Judd", "Spencer", "National Foundry". Reproductions (1970s–today): rough grainy cast, lighter, loose fit between halves, Phillips screws, bright shiny fresh paint. Sellers write "cast iron dog", "antique doorstop", "Hubley", "cast iron flower basket", "Victorian".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Hubley (Lancaster, PA)**: the volume maker – flower baskets (Lilies, Poppies, Petunias and Asters #456, Nasturtium #221, "French flower basket"), Boston terriers, Scotties, fox terriers, cats, cottages.
- **Bradley & Hubbard (Meriden, CT)** – "B&H": best quality; cats, parrots and other figurals command the top.
- **Albany Foundry, Judd, Spencer, National Foundry**: good period makers, mid value.
- Rare subjects: Halloween cat/girl, Mammy with broom, Topsy, Parrot, Giraffe, large ships, golfer.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Everything is in the paint: original paint (60–90%+) with natural wear at high points and chalky surface is what sells; repaint (especially silver/gold spray) drops to junk. Period construction: two-piece halves joined by slotted screws or rivets, tight seams, crisp casting; flat-back single castings also period. Weight – repros are lighter. Old paint has fine crazing and dirt in recesses; new paint is glossy and even (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Paint loss/flaking (chips through to bare iron on the face hurt most), touch-ups, replaced screws (Phillips = replaced or repro), broken/welded ears, tails and handles. Ask for a close-up of the face, the back/inside base with mark, and the screw heads.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Large 1970s–today repro market, often from original doorstops used as patterns (slightly smaller than original because of mould shrinkage – general knowledge, verify). Tells: grainy rough casting, loose fit, Phillips screws, bright fresh paint, light weight, fake "aged" wash.

## Market
Established collector field (Doorstop Collectors of America; Bertoia holds dedicated doorstop sales – general knowledge, verify). Common baskets and terriers sell steadily on eBay; dealer asks on Chairish/1stDibs run 2–3x eBay sold. Buyers: collectors, country/cottage decorators. Rare figurals go to auction.

## What sellers usually miss
Original paint vs repaint (everything is in the paint); Hubley mark on the back/base; two-piece construction with slotted screw = period; weight (repros are lighter); subject rarity. "Two old doorstops" lots sometimes hide a B&H or a figural dog among baskets.

## Shipping / handling
3–10 lb each; Priority Flat Rate medium fits most: $15–25 each way. Wrap paint surfaces – flaking loses value; ears snap. eBay 13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Maker and mark/number, subject, height, original paint percentage (be honest), construction (two-piece, slotted screws), any touch-ups or chips. Photograph front, back, base/mark, screw heads, face close-up. Title: "Antique Hubley cast iron doorstop [subject] original paint".

## Buy rule
Buy when: figural dog/cat/ship/cottage with original paint ≤ $40 (write-up 2) to ≤ $50 (write-up 1); flower basket 8"+ with good paint ≤ $30; any B&H-marked piece ≤ $100. Write-up 1 also allowed flower baskets up to $80, which cannot clear Dylan's rule at $90–200 sold – use the $30 cap. Pass when: repainted, replacement Phillips screw, chips through to bare iron on the face, baskets priced $40+ each (margin gone after shipping), repro tells. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat.
