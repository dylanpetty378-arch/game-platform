## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Lidded figural or decorated jars, mostly American art-pottery/production pottery 1930s–1980s plus modern licensed and import jars. Tells:
- **Mark** on the base: "McCoy" (in-mould, often "USA"), "Shawnee USA" or "Patented Muggsy U.S.A."/"Patented Smiley U.S.A.", "Brush" or "W" + number, "Hull Ware Little Red Riding Hood Pat. Des. No. 135889", "American Bisque" (often unmarked, "USA" plus wedges on the base), "Metlox Calif.", "Regal China", "Red Wing", "Twin Winton © Calif. USA", "Treasure Craft", "Cal. Orig.".
- **Finish**: underglaze paint (colours under the gloss) is factory; cold paint (painted after firing, rubs off) is hobby or factory secondary; "wood-stain" brown matte = Twin Winton or hobby copies; gold trim = factory-decorated premium versions (Shawnee).
- **Hobby/ceramic-class jars** (1960s–80s): molds from Holland Mold, Atlantic Mold, Duncan copied Twin Winton and McCoy shapes; signed with a painter's name and date, uneven glaze, often stain-painted; worth $15–40 (example 7956647751's realistic flesh-painted Friar Tuck could be a factory Twin Winton or a hobby-ceramic copy – the base mark decides).
- **Modern** (1990s–present): Fitz & Floyd, Department 56, Warner Bros. Studio Store, Pier 1/Home Goods imports (example 7977386679 polka-dot fish jar is a modern import).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **McCoy** (Roseville OH, 1910–1990): hundreds of jars; common $25–80; Mammy, Kookie Kettle low; rare (Clown in Barrel, Hobby Horse, Pepper) $150–600.
- **Shawnee** (1937–61): Muggsy dog, Smiley pig, Winnie pig, Puss n Boots, Dutch Boy/Girl, Jack/Jill, Owl, Lucky elephant. Plain decoration $100–300; gold trim and decals $300–1,500. Muggsy with blue bow plain ~$150–300 (general knowledge, verify); matching small Muggsy salt and peppers $30–60, large Muggsy S&Ps $100–200.
- **Brush / Brush-McCoy** (1907–1982): Formal pig, Cinderella pumpkin, Elephant with ice cream cone, Peter Pan, Squirrel on log – $200–1,500; heavily faked.
- **Hull**: Little Red Riding Hood (1943–57, made by Regal China for Hull) $200–800; reproduced.
- **American Bisque** (1919–1982): flasher (lenticular) jars, cartoon characters; $50–600.
- **Red Wing**: King of Tarts, Friar (monk) and Katrina $100–400.
- **Metlox** (California): Poppets, Mrs. Rabbit, Rose Bud $100–500.
- **Twin Winton** (1946–77): Friar Tuck "Thou Shalt Not Steal!", Hillbilly bear, Cop, Squirrel – wood-stain finish; $40–150; Goebel also made a Friar Tuck (double-face), and Cardinal/Treasure Craft made friar jars.
- **Regal China**: Hubert Lion, Davy Crockett, Churn Boy, Quaker Oats – $200–1,000+.
- **Modern licensed**: Warner Bros, Disney, Harley-Davidson, Star Wars – $25–150.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Full size matters: reproductions are ~10–15% smaller because they are cast from originals (mould shrinkage). Measure height against reference.
- Crisp moulding and sharp mark = original; blurry, shallow mark and soft details = later copy.
- Weight: repros often heavier and thicker-walled.
- Gold trim: original gold is softly worn on high points; new gold is bright.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips on lid rims and inner flanges (check the lid flange – most damage hides there), hairlines, crazing, cold-paint loss, restored ears/noses (airbrushed repairs under UV), replaced lids (lid from a different jar). Paint loss on cold-painted jars cuts value 30–60%.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Mid-1990s onward: large-scale fakes of Shawnee Muggsy/Smiley/Winnie, Brush jars, Hull Little Red Riding Hood, McCoy (with fake "McCoy" marks – after Nelson McCoy's trademark lapsed someone registered "McCoy" and produced copies), Black Americana Mammy jars. Tells: smaller size, crisp new paint, wrong colours, too-bright gold, flat-bottom or modern unglazed bases with even white clay. Expert buyers ask for height and mark photos.

## Market
Collector base aged; prices fell 50–70% from the late-1990s peak. Rare jars still sell; common McCoy and hobby jars are slow. Large and heavy to ship.

## What sellers usually miss
Factory gold-trim Shawnee versions sold as plain; Regal/Brush jars sold as "unmarked jar". Sellers usually over-estimate hobby copies as factory Twin Winton/McCoy, and price reproductions as originals.

## Shipping / handling
Large (10–13" tall) and fragile, 4–6 lb, needs double boxing: $20–30 to ship. Wrap lid separately.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Show base mark, measure height, photograph lid flange, disclose chips, crazing, paint loss; state reproduction status honestly.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − ship to buyer (~$25) − ship to Dylan (~$25) − price ≥ max($50, price). Needs a ~$115+ median at $0. Buy only full-size marked Shawnee (especially gold trim), Brush, Regal, Metlox, Red Wing, or rare McCoy/American Bisque at ≤ 1/3 sold median, after height and mark photos rule out reproductions. Pass hobby jars, modern imports, Twin Winton commons, and chipped lid flanges.
