# Compound bows (used, 1995–2015 era)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Machined aluminium riser (cut-outs, camo or anodised), two cam wheels, cables and a cable slide. Brand is machined or decalled on the riser (Hoyt, Mathews, Bowtech, PSE, Bear, Elite, Prime). Hoyt target/hunting line 2002–2008: UltraTec, ProTec, Vectrix, Trykon, with "XT2000/XT3000" limb lengths printed on the limbs. Sellers typically say "compound bow", "hunting bow", "Hoyt", list draw weight, sometimes "needs string".

## What sellers usually miss
Model name is only machined into the riser; sellers list "Hoyt bow" with no model. Extra limb sets and target accessories (Doinker, Sure-Loc, Spot Hogg) are worth more than the seller thinks. "Strings need replacing" is normal and buyers expect to restring ($60–90 at a pro shop) – list it honestly as "needs string".

## Shipping / handling
Bow in soft case ~6–8 lb but 40" long: ships UPS Ground/USPS in a long box $30–50. Do not dry-fire. Resell on eBay (13%) or ArcheryTalk/Facebook archery groups (no fee).

## Buy rule
Buy when: named Hoyt/Mathews/Bowtech/Elite under $60 complete with sight and rest, or under $100 with spare limbs/case. Pass when: no-name, 1980s wheel bow, cracked limbs or cam, or price over $120 unless < 5 yrs old. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
