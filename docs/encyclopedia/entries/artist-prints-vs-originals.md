## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Framed, matted image behind glass with a flat surface = almost always an offset print. Originals on canvas have texture and are rarely under glass. Pauline Eble Campanelli (Bucks County PA, 1943–2001) painted still lifes of baskets/dry sinks; her originals sell $300–2,000 at auction but her images were mass-printed – CL "original Campanelli" is a $15–40 print unless you can see brushwork. Gerald Putt (PA wildlife) signed/numbered prints $20–60. Thomas Kinkade "canvas lithographs" $30–150 despite certificates. Gaston Bussière (French symbolist, d.1928) – any "print" is decor, $20.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Pauline Eble Campanelli** – originals $300–2,000 at auction; prints $20–45.
- **Gerald Putt** – S/N wildlife prints $20–60.
- **Thomas Kinkade** – paper prints, S/N canvas lithographs with COA ($30–150), "highlighted" canvases.
- **Gaston Bussière** – only decor reproductions appear; $20.
- **Formats**: open-edition offset print, poster, signed/numbered limited print, giclée on canvas, "canvas transfer", hand-embellished print, original oil/acrylic/watercolour (general knowledge, verify on the last few).

### Golden-egg tells
- Thomas Hart Benton or Grant Wood AAA lithographs (1930s–40s, pencil-signed) hiding as "old framed print" — $1,200–18,750 sold (Benton "The Race" $18,750 Swann 2021) vs $10–40 open-edition print (~100–500×+) — pencil "Benton" or "Grant Wood" lower right in the margin, rich black stone-litho grain with no dot screen under a loupe, full margins, AAA (Associated American Artists) label on the backing (https://thehotbid.com/2021/02/23/a-thomas-hart-benton-lithograph-could-sell-for-15000/ ; see signed-limited-prints)
- Hiro Yamagata hand-signed serigraphs — Terapeak sold median $750 (p25 $300, p75 $1,350, n=15) vs $10–40 (~20–35×) — dense colourful crowd/street scenes, glitter or metallic inks, pencil signature and x/xxx fraction in the margin; a printed signature inside the image = poster (see serigraphs)
- Norman Rockwell hand-signed limited lithographs (Circle Fine Art / Eleanor Ettinger, 1970s, editions ~200) — Terapeak sold median $255 (p25 $85, p75 $825, n=50) vs $10–40 plate-signed print (~10–20×) — pencil "Norman Rockwell" plus fraction in the lower margin and a publisher blindstamp; the image itself carries no extra signature; beware: forged pencil signatures on offset prints, check the loupe for a dot screen (see norman-rockwell-collectibles)
- Period Mucha lithographs (Champenois, c.1896–1902) vs reproductions — Terapeak sold median $143.50 (p75 $400, n=50) vs $10–40 (~4–10×) — printer's imprint "Imp. F. Champenois Paris" in the lower margin, stone-litho texture (no rosette dots), aged paper with folds; 1960s–80s reprints are slim, glossy and dot-screened (see art-nouveau-poster-reproductions)
- Pauline Eble Campanelli or Gerald Putt actual paintings — originals $300–2,000 vs $20–45 Campanelli prints / $25–60 Putt S/N prints (~10–40×; general knowledge, verify) — raised brushstrokes catching light, paint wrapping onto the canvas edge, no glass, no pencil fraction; a Campanelli under glass with a mat is the print (entry comps)
- Thomas Kinkade "original oil" listings — Terapeak sold median $175 (n=50) but the sales are mostly Collectors Society member prints and highlighted canvases, not originals — real Kinkade originals almost never appear in our lane; the tell of the common version is a COA card, "Standard Numbered"/"S/N" canvas with a few raised highlights, gallery frame with brass plate (~1–3× an open print) (entry comp)
- Open-edition offset prints, posters, Kinkade paper prints, printed signatures — ~1× ($10–40; Terapeak "decorative prints" sold median $11.79) — regular rosette dot pattern under a loupe, signature printed inside the image, no plate mark, matted under glass (entry comps; see decorative-prints)

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Offset prints show a regular dot (rosette) pattern under a loupe; giclées show fine random spray dots; originals show brush strokes, raised paint, pencil underdrawing, and paint over the canvas edges. Pencil signature + edition number (e.g. 107/650) = limited print, not original. A printed signature is part of the image. Canvas texture "embossed" into a print is uniform and doesn't follow the brushwork (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Foxing, fading, water stains, and acidic mats hurt prints; for originals, flaking paint, tears, and overpainted repairs (visible under UV). Frame damage matters little except for shipping.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
The core risk of this type: prints sold as "originals". Also canvas transfers and hand-embellished prints passed off as paintings, and forged signatures on decorative oils (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Prints are abundant and slow; buyers are local decorators and fans of the specific artist. Originals by regional artists sell at regional auction houses and to collectors of that region (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
The reverse: sellers call prints "originals". Our risk is overpaying, not missing value. Check for the dot pattern under a loupe and the flat surface.

## Shipping / handling
Framed 20×24 with glass – $30–45 and breakage; ship unframed when possible.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** artist name, title, medium (offset print / giclée / oil on canvas), "signed" + how (pencil, printed), edition fraction, framed, size. Junk: "original" unless brushwork is visible, "rare", "museum". `Pauline Eble Campanelli Signed Print Basket Dry Sink Framed 20x24`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for prints; originals by listed regional artists sell for more at a regional auction house than on eBay (best_platform).
- **Price position:** prints at the sold median ($20–45 Campanelli, $25–60 Putt S/N, $30–150 Kinkade S/N canvas), OBO, accept −20%, floor $15; originals at 80% of the low auction estimate, firm; cut 15% after 30 days.
- **Photos:** full front straight-on; signature close-up; back with labels/gallery stamps; loupe shot of the surface (dot pattern or brushwork); raking-light shot for originals; frame corners and any foxing/fading.
- **State:** medium honestly, signature type, edition number, image and frame size, condition (foxing, fading, mat burn, flaking), provenance, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** ship unframed when possible (tube or flat between boards, $8–12); framed 20×24 with glass $30–45, glass taped in a grid, corner protectors, double box; frames larger than a passenger seat are local only.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "This is a [pencil-signed] [S/N] [Campanelli] print, [107/650], in the original frame; her prints sell at $[25–45] and originals start at $300, so this is priced as the print it is."

## Buy rule
Buy when: texture/brushwork visible, signed, by an artist with auction records, at ≤ 20% of the low auction estimate. Pass when: under glass with a mat (print), or seller can't say "oil on canvas/board". Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Frames bigger than a passenger seat are excluded by the size rule.
