## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Framed, matted image behind glass with a flat surface = almost always an offset print. Originals on canvas have texture and are rarely under glass. Pauline Eble Campanelli (Bucks County PA, 1943–2001) painted still lifes of baskets/dry sinks; her originals sell $300–2,000 at auction but her images were mass-printed – CL "original Campanelli" is a $15–40 print unless you can see brushwork. Gerald Putt (PA wildlife) signed/numbered prints $20–60. Thomas Kinkade "canvas lithographs" $30–150 despite certificates. Gaston Bussière (French symbolist, d.1928) – any "print" is decor, $20.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Pauline Eble Campanelli** – originals $300–2,000 at auction; prints $20–45.
- **Gerald Putt** – S/N wildlife prints $20–60.
- **Thomas Kinkade** – paper prints, S/N canvas lithographs with COA ($30–150), "highlighted" canvases.
- **Gaston Bussière** – only decor reproductions appear; $20.
- **Formats**: open-edition offset print, poster, signed/numbered limited print, giclée on canvas, "canvas transfer", hand-embellished print, original oil/acrylic/watercolour (general knowledge, verify on the last few).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Offset prints show a regular dot (rosette) pattern under a loupe; giclées show fine random spray dots; originals show brush strokes, raised paint, pencil underdrawing, and paint over the canvas edges. Pencil signature + edition number (e.g. 107/650) = limited print, not original. A printed signature is part of the image. Canvas texture "embossed" into a print is uniform and doesn't follow the brushwork (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Foxing, fading, water stains, and acidic mats hurt prints; for originals, flaking paint, tears, and overpainted repairs (visible under UV). Frame damage matters little except for shipping.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
The core risk of this type: prints sold as "originals". Also canvas transfers and hand-embellished prints passed off as paintings, and forged signatures on decorative oils (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Prints are abundant and slow; buyers are local decorators and fans of the specific artist. Originals by regional artists sell at regional auction houses and to collectors of that region (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
The reverse: sellers call prints "originals". Our risk is overpaying, not missing value. Check for the dot pattern under a loupe and the flat surface.

## Shipping / handling
Framed 20×24 with glass – $30–45 and breakage; ship unframed when possible.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State medium honestly (offset print / giclée / oil on canvas), signature type (pencil, printed, painted), edition number, image and frame size, condition, provenance. Photograph front, signature close-up, back (labels, gallery stamps), and a raking-light shot for originals. Keywords: artist name + title + medium + "signed" + "framed".

## Buy rule
Buy when: texture/brushwork visible, signed, by an artist with auction records, at ≤ 20% of the low auction estimate. Pass when: under glass with a mat (print), or seller can't say "oil on canvas/board". Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Frames bigger than a passenger seat are excluded by the size rule.
