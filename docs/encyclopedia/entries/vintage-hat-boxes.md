## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Round (14–18" diameter) or oval drum-shaped cases with a top handle, used for travel or as store packaging. Luggage-type: vinyl/leatherette over fibreboard, contrast stitching, brass latch or zipper, satin/rayon lining, sometimes a mirror and hat form inside lid. Store/milliner boxes: printed cardboard with string handle and store name (Stetson, Knox, Dobbs, Marshall Field's) — collected for advertising.

## Makers, brands and variants
- Lady Baltimore (Baltimore, 1950s–70s): vinyl and tweed hat/train cases, tag inside.
- Samsonite (Shwayder Bros., Denver): 1950s–60s round cases, molded or vinyl, Samsonite label on latch.
- Amelia Earhart luggage (Lowell, MA, 1940s–60s), American Tourister, Hartmann (leather/tweed, higher), Oshkosh, Mendel, Belber, Ventura, Skyway.
- Store hat boxes: Stetson, Knox, Dobbs, Mallory, Neiman Marcus, Saks; graphic printed boxes 1920s–50s sell well to decorators ($30–100).
- Early American wallpaper bandboxes (1820s–1850s), Hannah Davis etc.: museum-grade, $500–5,000.
- Luxury: Louis Vuitton "Boîte Chapeaux", Hermès, Goyard: require authentication — excluded.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Real leather (grain, edge wear showing brown) vs. vinyl (uniform, cracks like plastic). Tweed/canvas with leather trim = better maker. Original lining and key adds value. Hardware stamped by maker (e.g., "Samsonite" on latch) helps ID.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Crushed sides, torn linings, musty smell (big killer), rusted latches, missing keys, handle broken (handles of the example are visibly rotted/peeling). Odor cannot be shown in photos — ask.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern decorative hat boxes (Lierys, home-decor nesting boxes) are common and cheap. Luxury-brand hat boxes are faked; never buy without authentication.

## Market
Decor and vintage-luggage buyers; moderate volume (~100–200/month across all hat boxes, general knowledge), slow 30–60 days for common ones. Bulky to ship.

## What sellers usually miss
A good vintage hat sitting inside (Stetson, Borsalino, Dobbs fedoras $50–300); printed 19th-century bandboxes listed as "old round box".

## Shipping / handling
16" drum ~4 lb but box dimensions 18×18×12 → dimensional weight $20–35. Fits passenger seat.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State maker (inside label), dimensions, lining condition, key/no key, odor (smoke-free statement), all wear.
