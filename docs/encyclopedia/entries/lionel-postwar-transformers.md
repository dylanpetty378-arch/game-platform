## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Black (or brown) Bakelite/steel-cased transformers with red or chrome handles. **Lionel ZW** (1948–66): the big black 250/275 W transformer with FOUR handles (two large, two small) — runs 4 trains. **KW**: two handles/levers, 190 W. **1033/1044**: single lever, 90 W (1033 1948–56, the common starter). **RW/LW** mid-size. Modern ZW-L/ZW-C are silver/grey. Condition tells: cord cracked (should be replaced – treat as repair = pass for Dylan), whistle/direction buttons work. HO transformers and AHM/Bachmann rolling stock are $5–15. Postwar locomotives: diecast steam (671/681/726/736 Berkshire, 773 Hudson, 2026/2037 steamers) and F3 diesels (Santa Fe 2343/2353, NYC) are the money; 1970s–90s MPC/LTI plastic is common. Sellers write "Lionel transformer", "ZW", "train power supply".
- Note: one earlier write-up described the ZW as having "two big handles"; the ZW has four handles (two large, two small) – the two-handle unit is the KW.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Lionel postwar (1945–69)**: ZW (250 W early "A"-type, later 275 W "R"-type with circuit breaker), KW, RW, LW, 1033, 1044 transformers; locomotives and 6464 boxcars; boxed sets.
- **Lionel prewar** (pre-1942): standard gauge (400E, Blue Comet), 700E scale Hudson – top tier, auction (Stout).
- **MPC/Fundimensions (1970–86)** and **LTI/Lionel LLC** – mostly common plastic.
- **Modern power**: Lionel ZW-L (modern, rated ~620 W in an earlier write-up; general knowledge suggests 630 W – verify), ZW-C, MTH Z-4000 – $300–500+.
- Other: AHM, Bachmann, N-scale – junk tier.
- Valuable variants: uncatalogued/colour-variation 6464 boxcars, original boxes (double value).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Transformers: cast/steel case, Bakelite top, carbon roller contacts inside, original cloth or rubber cord. Serviced units have a new 3-prong cord, cleaned rollers, tested output. Locomotives: diecast boilers on premium steam engines vs plastic MPC; cab-side numbers identify the model. Old vs new: postwar vs modern "Lionel" logo style, Bakelite vs plastic. (general knowledge, verify)

## Condition, damage and repairs
Cracked/frayed cords (safety issue – price in $20 to record; buyers expect a replaced cord), worn rollers, dead circuit breakers, broken handles; locomotives with broken e-units, missing parts, repaint, rust, cracked shells; boxes torn. Ask whether it has been tested on track and for photos of the cord and the cab number.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Lionel reissued many postwar designs (Postwar Celebration series, LTI) and repro boxes exist; reproduction parts are common on restored locos. Modern ZW-L is a reissue in name only. (general knowledge, verify)

## Market
Large, steady market of O-gauge operators and collectors (TCA members), mostly older buyers; eBay is the main venue, plus train shows. Peaks Nov–Jan (holiday layouts). Transformers sell steadily; prices flat to slowly softening as the collector base ages. (general knowledge, verify)

## What sellers usually miss
275 vs 250 W (later R-type with circuit breaker); working rollers; cord condition. ZW vs 1033 (size/handles) – a ZW in a "train stuff" lot is the target. Train LOTS: look for postwar engines (2343 Santa Fe F3, 773 Hudson, 736 Berkshire), numbers on the cab side, MPC-era boxes, and original boxes (double value).

## Shipping / handling
Weights disagree across write-ups: ZW ~14 lb / ~15 lb / 25 lb packed ($25–35); KW 15 lb ($25); 1033 ~7 lb ($15). eBay 13%. Trains ship well if locos are wrapped individually. Buyers expect a replaced cord.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State model, wattage (250 vs 275 W ZW), tested on track (all throttles, whistle, direction), cord original/replaced, rollers serviced. For locos: number, diecast vs plastic, runs/smoke/e-unit, box. Photograph the nameplate/label, cord, handles, underside; cab numbers. Title keywords: "Lionel ZW 275 watt transformer tested new cord", "postwar", "KW 190W", "2343 Santa Fe F3 AA".

## Buy rule
Buy when: working ZW ≤ $50–80 (write-ups said ≤ $50, ≤ $60 and under $80) or KW ≤ $25–45 with good cord; Berkshire/F3/Hudson loco under 40% of tier. Pass when: ZW at $100+ (dealer-level); 1033-class transformers at any price over $15; cracked cords; MPC plastic; N-scale freight lots; KW at $145 (market). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Fits a passenger seat.
