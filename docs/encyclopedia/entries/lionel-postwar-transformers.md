## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Lionel ZW (1948–66): the big black 250/275 W transformer with FOUR handles (two large, two small) — runs 4 trains. KW: two handles, 190 W. 1033/1044: single lever, 90 W. Modern ZW-L/ZW-C are silver/grey. Sellers write "Lionel transformer", "ZW", "train power supply".

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Black Bakelite/steel box with a red handle(s): 1033 (90 W, 1948–56, one handle), 1044, KW (190 W, two handles), ZW (275 W, four handles, the big one). ZW/KW are the ones with money; 1033 is the common starter. Condition tells: cord cracked (should be replaced – treat as repair = pass for Dylan), whistle/direction buttons work. HO transformers and AHM/Bachmann rolling stock are $5–15.

## Additional research (earlier entry: Lionel transformers & postwar O-gauge trains)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold listings (agent knowledge Oct 2026), cs.trains.com forum

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Black or brown cased transformers with chrome handles: **ZW** (275 W, two big handles, the classic), **KW** (190 W, two levers), **1033/1044** (90 W, one handle), **RW/LW**. Postwar locomotives: diecast steam (671/681/726/736 Berkshire, 773 Hudson) and F3 diesels (Santa Fe 2343/2353, NYC) are the money; 1970s–90s MPC/LTI plastic is common.

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | 1033/1044 transformers, MPC-era plastic cars, Scout sets, N-scale lots | $20–60 | |
| Decent | KW transformer working ($90–150), ZW working ($150–250 – cords usually need replacing), 2026/2037 steamers, 6464 boxcars | $80–250 | |
| Collectible | 726/736 Berkshire $200–400, F3 Santa Fe AA $250–500, 773 Hudson $600–1,200, boxed 1950s sets | $250–1,200 | |
| Top | prewar standard gauge (400E, Blue Comet), 700E scale Hudson, uncatalogued/color-variation 6464s | $1,500–10,000+ | auction (Stout) |

### What sellers usually miss
Numbers on the cab side; "box of old trains" lots; original boxes (double value). Transformer cords are a safety issue – price in $20 to record.

### Shipping / handling
ZW 25 lb ($35), KW 15 lb ($25). eBay; trains ship well if locos are wrapped individually.

### Buy rule
Buy when: ZW under $80, KW under $45, Berkshire/F3/Hudson loco under 40 % of tier. Pass when: 1033-class transformers, MPC plastic, N-scale freight lots, KW at $145 (market). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
