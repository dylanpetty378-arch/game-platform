# Porcelain figurines: Lladró, Hummel, Precious Moments, Cherished Teddies, collector plates
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold listings (agent knowledge Oct 2026)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Market for all of these collapsed 2005–2020; sellers price from 1990s book values. Treat as pass by default.
- **Lladró**: glossy pastel blue/grey, blue backstamp with tulip logo + number. Most $40–120 sold; large groups/limited editions $300–1,500 (e.g. "Old Man with Violin" 5x= depends on number; nuns ~$60–100).
- **Hummel (Goebel)**: brown-eyed children, bee/V mark on base. TMK-1/2 (1935–59 "crown"/"full bee") $80–300; TMK-5+ $15–40. Large (7"+) and early = only ones worth it.
- **Precious Moments**: teardrop-eye children; $5–25 except first-year (1978–79, no mark/triangle) rare pieces $50–200.
- **Enesco Cherished Teddies / Boyds / Willow Tree**: $3–10 each; lots of 20 go $40–60.
- **Collector plates** (Bradford, Hamilton, Davey Allison, Terry Redlin, Norman Rockwell): $5–15 each. Royal Copenhagen/B&G Christmas plates pre-1930 $50–200.

## What sellers usually miss
Hummel base mark era; Lladró edition size. Almost never the other way.

## Shipping / handling
Fragile; $15–30 double-boxed. eBay returns on breakage are the risk.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Hummel full-bee/crown mark piece under $40, Lladró limited edition under 25 % of comps. Pass when: everything else, including Lladró at $100+ singles, any plate, any Cherished Teddies lot. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
