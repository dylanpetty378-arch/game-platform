## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Market for all of these collapsed 2005–2020; sellers price from 1990s book values. Treat as pass by default.
- **Lladró**: glossy pastel blue/grey, blue backstamp with tulip logo + number. Most $40–120 sold; large groups/limited editions $300–1,500 (e.g. "Old Man with Violin" 5x= depends on number; nuns ~$60–100). [Note: the "5x=" fragment is garbled in the original text; it likely meant the value depends on the model number – verify.] Elongated, slender figures with pale faces and closed or downcast eyes are the house style.
- **Hummel (Goebel)**: brown-eyed children, bee/V mark on base. TMK-1/2 (1935–59 "crown"/"full bee") $80–300; TMK-5+ $15–40. Large (7"+) and early = only ones worth it. The base also carries the incised mould number ("Hum" number) and the "M.I. Hummel" signature incised on the back or base edge.
- **Precious Moments**: teardrop-eye children; $5–25 except first-year (1978–79, no mark/triangle) rare pieces $50–200. [Note: the triangle annual mark dates from 1981; 1978–80 pieces have no annual mark ("original 21" are the 1978 issues) – general knowledge, verify.] The 1978 "original 21" carry the "Jonathan & David" signature and an E- catalogue number (e.g. E-2013 "Unto Us, A Child Is Born", E-2012 "Jesus Is Born", E-1372B "Jesus Loves Me" boy); production marks appear on the base or back of the neck from 1981 (LoveToKnow). LoveToKnow reports the original 21 currently selling for no more than about $50, so the $50–200 upper range is optimistic for common titles.
- **Enesco Cherished Teddies / Boyds / Willow Tree**: $3–10 each; lots of 20 go $40–60.
- **Collector plates** (Bradford, Hamilton, Davey Allison, Terry Redlin, Norman Rockwell): $5–15 each. Royal Copenhagen/B&G Christmas plates pre-1930 $50–200.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Lladró** (Valencia, Spain, 1953–): glossy and matte finishes; limited editions numbered; Nao is Lladró's cheaper sister brand (a reader comment on Antique HQ says Nao pieces were made by trainee sculptors and are less refined; unverified). Mark history (Antique HQ):
  - pre-1960: no official mark (some unofficial "Valencia"/"Espanna" marks);
  - 1960–63: first official impressed mark with "Espanna" and "Made in Spain";
  - 1964–70: impressed mark without "Espanna";
  - 1971–74: blue-black stamp, bell-flower logo appears;
  - 1974–77: accent over the "o" and a trademark symbol;
  - 1977–84: stamp includes "DAISA" (Lladró's design-rights subsidiary);
  - 1984–89: DAISA plus a copyright date;
  - 1990–present: simplified logo (tulip) and new font;
  - from 1986 the 4-digit model number is embossed on the base (earlier only on the box).
  - Impressed early marks (1960s) are the scarce, earlier pieces.
- **Hummel / Goebel** (Germany): trademark (TMK) eras on the base – TMK-1 crown (1935–49), TMK-2 full bee (1950–59), TMK-3 stylized bee (late 1950s–60s), TMK-4 three-line (1960s–70s), TMK-5 last bee (1970s–80s), TMK-6+ missing bee and later (general knowledge for the exact year splits, verify). Antique Trader's dates:
  - TMK-1 Crown (crown over "WG"), 1934–1950; a "double crown" (incised plus stamped) marks a transition period;
  - TMK-2 Full Bee (large bee inside a V), c. 1950–1959; bee shrinks and drops into the V around 1956; incised, then black, then blue stamped;
  - TMK-3 Stylized Bee, c. 1960–1972 (large version with "W. Germany" to the right c. 1960–63; small version with "W. Germany" centred under the V);
  - TMK-4 Three Line Mark, 1964–1972;
  - TMK-5 Last Bee, 1972–1979 (under glaze 1972–76, over glaze 1976–79);
  - TMK-6 Missing Bee, 1979–1991 (artist's mark adds the painting date);
  - TMK-7 Hummel Mark, 1991–1999 ("West" dropped);
  - TMK-8 Millennium Bee, 2000–2008;
  - TMK-9 Manufaktur Rödental, 2009–present.
  - 1948–49 pieces may be marked "U.S. Zone" or "Germany"; "First Issue" (from 1990) and "Final Issue" (from 1991) backstamps and a 1996 125th-anniversary backstamp exist. The crown-WG decal was reused 1969–72 as a small blue copyright decal, so a crown alone on a later mark is not TMK-1.
- **Precious Moments** (Enesco, Sam Butcher, 1978–): annual symbol marks under the base; first-year unmarked pieces are the only value.
- **Enesco Cherished Teddies, Boyds Bears, Willow Tree (Demdaco)**: junk tier.
- **Collector plates**: junk tier except early Royal Copenhagen / Bing & Grøndahl Christmas plates.

### Quick ID checklist
- Photo of the base: maker stamp, mould/model number, any date or edition number.
- Hummel: which TMK (crown, full bee in V, stylized bee, three-line, last bee, missing bee, later)? Look for a double mark and "U.S. Zone".
- Hummel: incised "M.I. Hummel" signature and Hum number with size designator; height in inches.
- Lladró: impressed vs stamped mark, DAISA, accent on the "o", 4-digit number embossed (1986+); limited-edition number and certificate.
- Precious Moments: annual symbol (none = 1978–80) and E- number; original box.
- Check every protruding point (fingers, flowers, hat brims, petals) for chips and glue lines.
- Original box and certificate (Lladró numbered pieces, Hummel limited editions).
- Lladró: logo scratched or ground off on the base = second.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Lladró: hard-paste porcelain, crisp delicate fingers/flowers, incised number and blue backstamp. Hummel: earthenware with hand-painting, incised model number and size designator plus the TMK stamp. Precious Moments: cast porcelain bisque. Look for the base mark first; artist signatures and "limited edition" numbering add (general knowledge, verify).
- Lladró glossy (glazed) vs matte (gres or bisque) finishes; matte and gres pieces are a different look, not a defect.
- Hummel underglaze vs overglaze marks help date TMK-5; painted artist date appears from TMK-6.
- Old vs new: early marks are impressed or incised; modern marks are printed decals with registration symbols.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chipped fingers, flowers, hats and petals (Lladró), restored breaks (glue lines, colour mismatch under UV), crazing, scratched or ground-off backstamps (Lladró seconds have the logo scratched out – general knowledge, verify). Any damage takes most of the value.
- Hummel: chips on the base edge and the tips of umbrellas, baskets and instruments; flaking paint; repaired heads.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Hummel reissues of old models with later marks; Chinese Lladró look-alikes and fakes with painted logos; Hummel-style figurines by other makers (general knowledge, verify).
- A Lladró made after 1960 with no mark is likely counterfeit; check the limited-edition number against the stated edition size (Antique HQ).
- Hummel: a crown decal reused 1969–72 can mislead; read the whole backstamp.

## Market
Huge supply from estates and few young buyers; many listings, low sell-through, slow sales except rare marks and big Lladró groups (general knowledge, verify). Christmas plates sell seasonally. Disney-park-exclusive Precious Moments (2005+) are a small bright spot, about $100–150 boxed (LoveToKnow).

## What sellers usually miss
Hummel base mark era; Lladró edition size. Almost never the other way.
- Impressed 1960s Lladró marks; Hummel "double crown" and "U.S. Zone" marks.

## Shipping / handling
Fragile; $15–30 double-boxed. eBay returns on breakage are the risk. Wrap protruding parts separately; ship in the original box inside a second box when available.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State maker, model name and number, height, base mark/era, edition number, box/certificate, and any damage or restoration. Photograph the base marks clearly and every delicate point. Title: "Lladró #[number] [name], [height]in, box" / "Goebel Hummel [name] #[mold] TMK-2 full bee".

### What to tell the buyer
Give maker, model name and number, height, and the exact backstamp (e.g. "TMK-2 full bee, incised and stamped"; "Lladró blue stamp with DAISA, 1977–84 mark"), with photos. State limited-edition number and edition size if present, and whether box and certificate are included. Say plainly whether there are chips, repairs, crazing or a scratched-out logo, and that each fingertip, petal and edge was checked. Avoid book values; quote condition and mark.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Hummel full-bee/crown mark piece under $40, Lladró limited edition under 25 % of comps. Pass when: everything else, including Lladró at $100+ singles, any plate, any Cherished Teddies lot. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Fits a passenger seat.
