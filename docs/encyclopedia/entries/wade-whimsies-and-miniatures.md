## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Tiny (0.5–2 in) slip-cast porcelain/bone-china-body animals and characters, glazed in muted browns, greens, blues and greys with soft airbrushed shading. Solid, heavy for their size, flat unglazed or glazed base. Most marks are moulded on the back of the base or the rim: "WADE ENGLAND" (raised or incised), sometimes only "Wade" or nothing. Red Rose Tea (Canada/US) figures are mostly reissues of English Whimsies moulds. In a photo, a pile of tiny brown/grey glazed animals in a bowl or ziplock = Wade until proven otherwise. Look for the brighter, more detailed, larger 1950s pieces and anything Disney-character shaped (Lady and the Tramp, Bambi, Dumbo) – those are the money.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **First Whimsies (1953–1959)**: ten boxed sets of 4–5 figures (e.g. set 1 English animals – leaping fawn, horse, spaniel, poodle, squirrel; later sets include farm animals, polar animals, African jungle, Pets, Zoo). Larger, more detailed, often marked "Wade England" in black transfer or unmarked. Boxed sets carry the most value.
- **Hat Box series (1956–1965)**: Disney figures sold in round cardboard "hat boxes" – Lady, Tramp, Jock, Trusty, Peg, Bull, Si & Am, Scamp, Dachsie, Boris etc. Marked "Wade" / unmarked; sought by Disney collectors.
- **Disney Blow-Ups (1961–1965)**: larger (4–6 in) versions of Hat Box characters – $75–300 each (general knowledge, verify).
- **English Whimsies (1971–1984)**: 60 figures in 12 sets of 5, each "WADE ENGLAND" on base; the common ones. Tons in circulation.
- **Red Rose Tea premiums**: Canada from 1967 (Whimsies reissues, Nursery Rhymes and others), US 1983–1985 and later series (Nursery Rhyme, Circus, Noah's Ark, Calendar, etc.). Very common.
- **Tom Smith Christmas cracker figures (1973–1990s+)**: sets themed (Safari, Survival, Wildlife, Village of Broadlands, Birds). Similar value to Whimsies.
- **Whimsie-land (1984–1988)**, **Whimsey-on-Why** village houses (1980–1987), **Nursery Favourites** (1972–1981, larger nursery-rhyme figures $8–25), **Happy Families**, **Tortoise family** (Wade tortoise trinket/lidded tortoises $10–40), **Disney Hat Box**.
- Modern Wade Collectors Club limited editions (1990s–2010s) – small runs, some $20–100.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Old (1950s): more hand-applied colour, glossier, finer moulding, often black "Wade England" transfer mark or no mark; sometimes slightly larger.
- 1970s–80s English Whimsies: embossed "WADE ENGLAND" on base edge, matte-ish glaze, browns/greys; mass-produced.
- Red Rose: same moulds, sometimes colour variants (colour variants are a niche collectible).
- Hat Box Disney: bright colours, detailed faces, sometimes paper label.
- Weight/feel: dense and solid; plastic or resin knock-offs are light and warm.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips on ears, tails, horns and bases; glaze flakes; ink marks. Nearly all value is in mint pieces; chipped commons are worth near zero. Repairs (glued horns/tails) show as glue lines – reduce to near zero. Original boxes/hat boxes add 30–100%.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not heavily faked (too cheap). Confusion comes from Wade's own reissues (Red Rose reusing English Whimsies moulds) and from other makers' look-alikes: Sylvac, Szeiler, Goebel minis, Japanese bone-china miniatures, "Whimsies" by others. Unmarked Japanese porcelain minis are thinner, glossier and often have a red/black "Japan" stamp.

## Market
Steady, high-volume, low-price. Hundreds of eBay sales monthly, mostly $5–40 lots. UK buyers are the deepest pool (eBay UK). Sells in 2–6 weeks at fair price. Hat Box/Blow-Up cross into Disney collector demand, which is faster.

## What sellers usually miss
- They price Whimsies as "rare English porcelain" ($10 each) – usually over-priced, not under.
- The under-priced pieces are the larger glossy Disney dogs (Lady and the Tramp) mixed into a generic "small figurines" lot, and boxed 1950s sets sold as "old miniature animals".
- Unmarked pieces in a Wade lot can be first-series (1950s often unmarked).

## Shipping / handling
Light: a lot of 50 weighs ~1–2 lb; ships in a small box, each figure wrapped or in bubble sleeves. USPS Ground Advantage $6–10. Low breakage if wrapped individually.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State series (English Whimsies 1971–84, Red Rose, first series 1950s, Hat Box), count, every chip, mark photos, and dimensions. Group by series in photos. Lots sell better titled "Wade Whimsies lot of 40 English Whimsies Red Rose Tea".

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − ship to buyer (~$8) − ship to Dylan (~$8) − price; pass unless profit ≥ max($50, price). A common lot ($40 resale) never qualifies. Only a mixed lot that clearly contains Hat Box / Blow-Up Disney dogs or boxed 1950s sets, at ≤ $40, can hit the rule. Size fine (pocket/shoebox). No authentication issues.
