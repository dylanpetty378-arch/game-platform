## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Slip-cast earthenware with the signature green-black (or blue-black) "reduction drip" glaze in streaky vertical runs. Common shapes: stylised animals, swans, fish, tall bottle vases, jugs, cream and sugar sets. Marks: impressed "BMP Canada" or a paper label/hang tag (later "The Blue Mountain Collection, Collingwood, Ontario").

## Makers, brands and variants
Founded by Jozo Weider (Blue Mountain ski resort), with Denis Tupy designing many shapes; production through the 1990s; closed ~2004. Glazes: classic green drip, cobalt blue, mocha/brown, harvest gold, "Canadiana", red/orange — the green is commonest. Late "Heritage"/"Blue Mountain Collection" pieces have printed tags.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Lightweight red/buff earthenware body; glossy drip glaze. Better pieces have deep, striking streaks; later pieces are thinner/smaller.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips on ears/tails of animals, rim chips, crazing, glaze bubbles.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not faked; other Canadian potteries (Canuck, Evangeline, Royal Canadian Art Pottery) and Japanese imports have similar drip glazes.
