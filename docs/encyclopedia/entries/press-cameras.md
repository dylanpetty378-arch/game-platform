## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Folding metal/wood cameras with a drop bed, leather bellows, lens board on rails, and a ground-glass back, used by newspaper photographers 1912–1970s. Speed Graphic = has a focal-plane cloth shutter in the body (knob and tension window on the side); Crown Graphic = no focal-plane shutter (lighter); Century Graphic = 2x3 plastic body.
- **Pre-Anniversary (1928–1939)**: black hardware, no "Anniversary" plate.
- **Anniversary (1940–1946)**: black-painted metal (wartime), wooden body, no rear tilt.
- **Pacemaker (1947–1970)**: chrome hardware, body release, top rangefinder (Kalart then Graflex), lens board "Graflex" nameplate.
- Sizes: 4x5 (most desirable), 3¼x4¼, 2¼x3¼ ("Miniature" Speed/Crown — less valued).
pid 7953090854: "SPEED GRAPHIC" nameplate on front standard, black leatherette wood body with black hardware (pre-Anniversary/Anniversary era), wire-frame viewfinder, lens with aluminum hood in a compound/Supermatic-type shutter, side-mounted Graflex 3-cell flash with bowl reflector and cord. Proportions look like 3¼x4¼ or 2¼x3¼ (small for a 4x5) — likely a Miniature or 3x4 Speed Graphic, 1938–1946.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Bellows pinholes (shine a light inside), focal plane shutter curtain cracks, rangefinder alignment, ground glass intact, rails smooth, shutter (Graphex, Supermatic, Synchro-Compur, Copal) firing on all speeds. Chrome Pacemaker hardware clean = well kept.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Torn/pinholed bellows ($100+ repair), sticky leaf shutters (CLA $80–150), FP shutter curtain rot, missing ground glass/film holders, separated lens elements, flash guns frequently lack batteries/corroded contacts.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
None meaningful; prop/movie replicas exist but obvious. Watch for franken-combos (wrong lens board) — lower value, not fraud.

## Market
Strong steady demand from large-format shooters and décor buyers; 4x5 sells in 1–3 weeks. Small formats slower.

## What sellers usually miss
Aero Ektar or Ektar lenses on otherwise plain bodies; included Grafmatic/film holders ($15–40 each); Kalart rangefinder; 4x5 vs. smaller size (sellers price small ones as if 4x5).

## Shipping / handling
4x5 Speed Graphic ~6 lb; 12x10x10 box, double boxed, $20–30. Lens removed or protected. Fits passenger seat.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Graflex" + model (Speed Graphic / Crown Graphic / Super Graphic) + format "4x5" + era (Pacemaker, Anniversary) + lens (Optar 135, Ektar) + "tested"/"display". Junk: "antique camera", "press camera" alone, "untested" in title. Example: `Graflex Pacemaker Speed Graphic 4x5 Press Camera Optar 135mm Shutter Tested`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (large-format shooters, 1–3 weeks); nothing beats it; untested bodies still sell to decor buyers.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median (4x5 Pacemaker with lens ~$180–300 untested, $200–450 tested; Super Graphic/Technika $400–900); accept −10%, floor −20%; OBO; cut 10% after 21 days.
- **Photos:** 1) camera open, bellows extended, lens forward; then lens/shutter face, FP shutter knob and dial, bellows back-lit for pinholes, rangefinder, ground glass, body folded, holders/accessories; video of the shutter firing at several speeds.
- **State:** format, era, lens and shutter model, which speeds fire, FP shutter status, bellows light-tight or pinholes, rangefinder present/calibrated, holders and Grafmatics included, "display/untested" if so, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** ~6 lb; lens removed or padded, cock shutter released, 12×10×10 double-boxed, 6–8 lb class, $20–30.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "A [Pacemaker Speed Graphic 4x5] with the [Ektar/Optar], shutter firing at all speeds and bellows tight — complete tested 4x5 kits sell for [$X], and the small 2x3 ones are the $100 cameras."

## Buy rule
Profit = median × 0.87 − ~$25 − ~$25 − price ≥ max($50, price). A $300 4x5 kit allows ≤ ~$100. Shutters are mechanical (not electronics) but untested shutters count as risk: require a video of the shutter firing. Pass on 2x3/3x4 minis above $50.
