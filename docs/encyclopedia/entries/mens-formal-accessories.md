## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Tuxedo accessories sold in boxed sets. Fabric sets: pleated cummerbund (pleats face up), pre-tied bow tie, pocket square; mostly polyester from rental houses (Selix, After Hours, Gingiss). Jewelry sets: shirt studs + cufflinks in velvet box; maker marks on backs.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Fabric**: After Six (1960s–80s colorful and paisley sets $20–50), Lord West, Selix, Ike Behar, Pierre Cardin, Ralph Lauren.
- **Jewelry**: Swank, Anson, Hickok (gold-tone/gold-filled $15–60); Krementz (14k rolled-gold $30–80; solid 14k $150+); Larter & Sons; Cartier/Tiffany/David Webb ($500–5,000).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Silk vs polyester (fiber tag); mother-of-pearl/onyx inlays on studs; marks "14K", "STERLING", "1/20 12K GF".

## Condition, damage and repairs
Stains, fading, broken stud backs, missing pieces.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake hallmarks on luxury studs; high-end needs authentication (excluded).
