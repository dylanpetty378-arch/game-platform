Buy rule (short): Pass reproduction bar mounts and modern openers; buy only marked original pre-1930 US bar mounts (Yankee/Arcade, Chief, Quick & Easy, Swift, Champion) or English mechanicals at ≤ 1/4 comps.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Bar-mounted cork pullers clamp to a counter; a lever handle drives an internal worm in and pulls the cork. Originals: cast iron, nickel-plated, embossed maker/patent ("Yankee No. 7 Arcade Mfg Co Freeport Ill", "Chief", "Quick & Easy", "Phoenix", "Swift", "Champion", "Rapid"). Repros: embossed grape-vine relief, names like "Estate", "Brunswick", "Napa", gold-tone/antiqued finishes, wooden handles, generic C-clamp.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Arcade Mfg (Yankee No. 1/7/8)**, **Gilchrist (Yankee)**, **Enterprise**, **Chief**, **Quick & Easy**, **Swift**, **Champion**, **Phoenix**, **Rapid**: US bar-mount originals 1890s–1930s.
- **Thomason, Heeley, Lund, Dowler, King's screw**: English mechanical corkscrews 1800s.
- **Williamson, Clough, Walker**: US T-handle/wire corkscrews (common).
- **Modern**: Screwpull (Le Creuset), Rabbit, Laguiole (French knife-makers; authentic Laguiole en Aubrac/Forge de Laguiole).
- **Repros**: "Estate", "Brunswick", Chinese "King's" rack-and-pinion repros sold as "antique style".

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original bar mounts: nickel plating wear to iron, crisp patent dates, heavy. Repros: rough castings, grape motif, antiqued paint, Phillips screws, bright brass-tone thumb screws.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Missing clamp, broken worm, replaced handle, re-nickeling (lowers collector value), rust.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
High: decorative bar mounts sold as antiques, Chinese mechanical repros of English King's screws.

## Market
Active collector base (ICCA), originals sell quickly; repros slow and cheap.

## What sellers usually miss
"Yankee" and patent embossing on dirty cast iron bar mounts at garage sales.

## Shipping / handling
Bar mounts 3–6 lb, $12–18; T-handles small.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Photos of every embossing, function demo, original vs repro stated.

## Buy rule
Repros: median ~$40 → pass. Originals: buy when marked Yankee/Arcade/Chief and price ≤ $60 vs median ≥ $150.
