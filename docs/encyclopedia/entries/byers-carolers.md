## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- Byers' Choice carolers (10–13" tall) have hand-painted sculpted heads with characteristic round "O" singing mouths, wire armature bodies with fabric costumes, a wooden or felt base with Byers' signature/date on the bottom, and a hang tag.
- Construction per the maker: the body is tissue paper wrapped around a bent wire skeleton; heads are cast by filling moulds with clay, then nostrils and lips are added and the face hand-painted; fabric is cut to Joyce Byers' patterns (Wikipedia). Joyce sculpts an original head for each line, which becomes that line's mould (Wikipedia).
- Base: since 1986 figures reportedly have a green felt ring and a dated gold seal on the bottom, and each is signed by a member of the Byers family (blog source; verify against a photographed base).
- Look-alikes: Possible Dreams "Clothtique" (resin), Annalee dolls (felt with painted felt faces), Department 56 porcelain, and flat die-cut painted metal ornaments (as in the example, which are ornaments, not Byers').

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Byers' Choice** (Bob and Joyce Byers): Joyce began making carolers as gifts in 1965 at her kitchen table; first sold at The Woman's Exchange in Wayne PA in the 1970s; Bob Byers Sr. took a dozen to the New York Gift Show in 1978, which led to national distribution; incorporated as Byers' Choice Ltd. in 1981 per the company (Wikipedia says 1978); Byers Foundation 1986; first *Caroler Chronicle* collector newsletter 1988; current Chalfont facility and Christmas museum from 1994 (Byers' Choice history; Wikipedia). Over 500,000 carolers a year, sold by 3,000+ retailers (Wikipedia).
- Lines: Traditional Carolers, Cries of London (1980s–2000s, collected; examples dated 1994, 1996, 1999, 2001, 2002 on eBay), Dickens / *A Christmas Carol* characters, *The Nutcracker*, Santas, children with toys, nativity, special occasion figures, Salvation Army carolers (from 1992), Colonial Williamsburg Revolutionary-era figures, antique-automobile pieces, and later Halloween/Thanksgiving/Easter lines (Wikipedia; first-run notes). Signatures on the base help date (Joyce Byers signed early ones; later dates/years).

### Quick ID checklist
- Round "O" open singing mouth on a painted clay face.
- Base underside: Byers' signature, year, and (1986+) green felt ring with gold seal.
- Wire-armature body you can pose; fabric clothes sewn, not moulded.
- Height 10–13" adult, smaller for children.
- Props present (songbook, lantern, basket, wreath, kettle/bell for Salvation Army).
- Original box and hang tag.
- Plastic or resin face, felt face, or flat metal = not Byers'.
- Smoke staining, fading, moth damage on the clothes.

### Golden-egg tells
This type has few true golden eggs; most Byers' sell $22–60 (Terapeak).
- Halloween witches and other non-Christmas seasonal figures — ~2–3x a Christmas adult caroler (Terapeak example $99.99 for a 2022 Halloween witch vs $39 overall median) — witch hat, pumpkins, orange/black costume, Byers' base.
- Special pieces such as the "Caroler Condo" display — ~2x (Terapeak example $76.02) — a building or display piece rather than a single figure.
- Early figures before the 1986 gold seal, with Joyce Byers' signature — premium for early pieces (general knowledge, verify) — handwritten signature and early date, no gold seal.
- Complete sets/scenes with original boxes (Dickens *Christmas Carol* casts, Nutcracker, Salvation Army band) — sell as sets above single-figure prices (general knowledge, verify) — matching boxes, full cast.
- NOT golden: Cries of London (Terapeak median $28.73, n=50) and "retired" carolers generally (median $34.95) — the first-run belief that these reach $80–250 is not supported by Terapeak.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Clay heads cast from Joyce Byers' sculpts and hand-painted; tissue paper over wire bodies; hand-sewn fabrics (Wikipedia). Look-alikes have plastic faces or flat metal.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Fading, dust, moth damage, missing props (songbooks, lanterns), bent wires, smoke smell, chipped paint on faces/hands.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No real forgery market; many inexpensive look-alikes. Byers' continues to make similar figures each year, so date the base.

## Market
Seasonal Q4 peak; lots of supply from estates; moderate prices. Buyers are Christmas decorators and long-time collectors (the maker has run a collector newsletter since 1988).

## What sellers usually miss
Early 1980s signed pieces; Byers' with original boxes; Halloween/seasonal figures mixed into Christmas lots.

## Shipping / handling
12" figure ~1 lb, $10–14 in box. Protect faces and props; wrap separately.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** Byers Choice Caroler + line (Cries of London / Dickens / Salvation Army / Halloween) + character + year + "signed base" + height. Junk: "Christmas figurine", "doll", "Victorian". `Byers Choice Caroler 1999 Cries of London Gingerbread Man Signed 13in`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (Q4 only; singles sell slowly); lots of 4–8 do better on FB local to decorators. Halloween/seasonal pieces sell for more than Christmas adults on eBay.
- **Price position:** adults at the sold median (~$25–35), accept −15%, floor $20; seasonal/Halloween at the sold median $60–100, OBO; cut 20% if unsold by Dec 10.
- **Photos:** full figure front on plain background; base underside with signature/year/gold seal; face close-up (O mouth); props; clothes for moth damage/fading; hang tag/box.
- **State:** line and character name, year from base, signature, height, props listed as complete or missing, smoke-free, fading/moth/paint flaws by photo number, box/tag.
- **Pack / haul:** shoebox, ~1 lb, $10–14; wrap face and props separately, immobilize the figure, no double-box needed.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Genuine Byers' Choice [character] caroler, [year] on the signed base with gold seal, handmade in Chalfont PA with a hand-painted clay face and sewn clothes; current retail is [$] and this one is complete with [props]."
