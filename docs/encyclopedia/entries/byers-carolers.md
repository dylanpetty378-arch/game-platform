## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Byers' Choice carolers (10–13" tall) have hand-painted papier-mâché/clay heads with characteristic round "O" singing mouths, wire armature bodies with fabric costumes, a wooden or felt base with Byers' signature/date on the bottom, and a hang tag. Look-alikes: Possible Dreams "Clothtique", Annalee dolls (felt), and flat die-cut painted metal ornaments (as in the example, which are ornaments, not Byers').

## Makers, brands and variants
Byers' Choice (Bob and Joyce Byers, from 1978): Traditional Carolers, Cries of London (1980s–90s, collected), Dickens characters, Santas, children with toys, nativity, special occasion figures, and later Halloween/Thanksgiving/Pilgrim lines. Signatures on the base help date (Joyce Byers signed early ones; later dates/years).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Papier-mâché/sculpted heads, hand-sewn fabrics, wire bodies. Look-alikes have plastic faces or flat metal.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Fading, dust, moth damage, missing props (songbooks, lanterns), bent wires, smoke smell.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No real forgery market; many inexpensive look-alikes.
