## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Folding pliers with tools in the handles. Leatherman PST (1983–2004): plain stainless, no blade lock, "Leatherman Tool / Portland OR" stamp – the original; early 1980s "Pat. Pend." versions and ones with the original leather sheath are collected. Modern Wave+/Charge+/Surge sell $60–120 used; Skeletool $35–50. Victorinox SwissTool $50–80. Gerber MP600 $30–45 (Gerber's sliding "pull-out" pliers). Model name is stamped on the handle on modern Leathermans. Seller words: "Leatherman", "multi tool".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Leatherman** (Portland, OR; founded by Tim Leatherman, first tool 1983): PST, PST II (added scissors/serrated blade; general knowledge, verify), Super Tool, Wave (1998; redesigned 2004), Charge (titanium handles; TTi = titanium + premium blade steel), Surge, Skeletool, Micra (keychain), Free series.
- Collector money is in discontinued/limited: Leatherman Charge TTi, Wave 2004 first run, Skeletool CX in colours, 25th anniversary PST.
- **Victorinox SwissTool** – quality, mid value.
- **Gerber** (MP600 etc.), **SOG**, **Buck** – lower resale.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Stainless steel (420HC on most Leathermans; S30V/154CM on premium Charge models), titanium handle scales on Charge Ti/TTi. Genuine: crisp stamps, smooth action. Old vs new: "Pat. Pend." stamp (earliest PSTs), "Portland OR USA" stamp, sheath style. (general knowledge, verify)

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken tips on pliers/screwdrivers, rust, stuck tools, sharpened-down blades, missing bit kits, worn/missing sheath. Leatherman's 25-year warranty means some buyers send used tools for replacement (general knowledge, verify). Ask for photos of all tools open and the stamp.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Leatherman Waves/Charges sold online (poor stamping, rough finish, wrong logo font). The 25th anniversary PST is a reissue – note in title. (general knowledge, verify)

## Market
Huge, liquid eBay market (users, EDC enthusiasts, a smaller collector niche for PST variants). Sells in days at fair price; Christmas/Father's Day bumps. Pawn shops and estate sales supply the market cheaply. (general knowledge, verify)

## What sellers usually miss
"Pat. Pend." stamp on early PSTs; model names on modern ones (they write "Leatherman").

## Shipping / handling
<1 lb, $6. eBay (13%).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State model, stamps (Pat. Pend., Portland OR), all tools present and working, blade sharpened or not, sheath. Photograph the handle stamp, all tools open, sheath. Title keywords: "Leatherman PST Pat Pend original leather sheath", "Charge TTi", "Wave", "USA".

## Buy rule
Buy when: Charge/Surge/TTi under $30, Pat. Pend. PST under $35. Pass when: standard PST at $45. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Fits a passenger seat.
