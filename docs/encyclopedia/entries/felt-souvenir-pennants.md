## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- Triangular wool or rayon felt pennants with screen-printed scenes, flocked letters and a contrasting header strip with ties.
- Age tells: 1910s–40s wool felt with sewn-on felt letters or tassels; 1930s–60s printed graphics on felt, sometimes with factory colour embellishments (AntiqueSportsShop); 1950s–60s screen-printed colourful scenes (roadside attractions, turnpikes, parks); 1970s+ thinner synthetic felt, simpler prints, plastic sticks.
- Sewn felt lettering is the c. 1910 construction and is "extremely hard to find" on sports pennants (AntiqueSportsShop). Museum examples of wool felt school pennants date 1920–1960 (The Henry Ford).
- Standard sizes for baseball pennants: full size ~29"; ¾ size ~22–26.5"; c. 1910 oversized ~33–42"; miniatures ~8.75–11.75" (AntiqueSportsShop). Travel pennants are commonly 12–30".
- Maker tags/stamps: a sewn or printed tag on the back (e.g. Chicago Pennant Company tag on a Harvard pennant; Made in Chicago Museum); "Keezer" stamp on 1940s Athletics, Cubs and Braves pennants; "Asco" mark on an early-1960s Koufax pennant; a "patent pending" maker tag on a c. 1910 pennant (AntiqueSportsShop).
- Title words: "vintage felt pennant", "souvenir pennant", "1950s", "wool felt", "full size", "roster", "World Series", "World's Fair".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Chicago Pennant Co.** ("ChiPenCo", est. 1910, 6542 S. Cottage Grove Ave., Chicago): college and souvenir felt pennants; tag on the back (Made in Chicago Museum). Other Chicago felt novelty makers: **Advertising Flag Co. (ADFLAG)** and **WGN Flag & Decorating Co.** (Made in Chicago Museum comments, citing the Pennant Fever site).
- **Trench Mfg** (Buffalo — sports pennants, "Trench" tag), **Crown Pennant**, **Mohawk**, **Keezer** (1940s MLB stamp), **Asco** (1960s MLB), **Wincraft** (modern sports), unmarked souvenir makers.
- High-value subjects: World's Fairs (1933 Chicago, 1939 NY), early MLB/NFL team pennants (1940s–60s), Route 66, defunct attractions, bands (Beatles), college pre-1940 with sewn letters, political/civil-rights events (1stDibs lists a March on Washington felt pennant as "rare").
- Reference: Mike Egner's Pennant Price Guide (cited by AntiqueSportsShop).
- Team-name era tells (AntiqueSportsShop): Phillies "Bluejay" mascot 1943–c.1950; Boston Braves with Indian chief, 1940s; Milwaukee Braves, 1950s; Atlanta Braves from c.1966; "Cincinnati Redlegs", 1950s ("Reds" again 1961); Brooklyn Dodgers 1930s–50s; Los Angeles Dodgers 1960s; Old English lettering on 1930s Reds and Pirates pennants.

### Quick ID checklist
- Letters sewn on (raised felt, stitching visible) or printed? Sewn = early (c. 1910s–30s).
- Felt: thick, slightly fuzzy wool vs thin, shiny synthetic (1970s+).
- Maker tag or stamp on the back or header (Chicago Pennant, Trench, Keezer, Asco).
- Length: measure along the top; full 29" sports pennants are the collected size.
- Team name and logo against the era list (Brooklyn Dodgers, Boston/Milwaukee Braves, Redlegs, Bluejay).
- Printed player names (roster), "World Series", "Champions" or a team photo.
- Original tassels/streamers and ties present; tip intact; tack holes and moth holes counted.
- World's Fair year, defunct attraction name, or Route 66 in the graphic.

### Golden-egg tells
- 1940s–60s MLB roster / World Series / championship pennants, full 29" — ~15–40x the generic $11 MLB median; $150–500 asks (e.g. 1949 Phillies roster with Bluejay $475, 1962 SF Giants NL Champions roster $500; AntiqueSportsShop, asking) — a list of player names printed down the pennant, "Champions" or "World Series" wording.
- Defunct team names (Brooklyn Dodgers, Boston or Milwaukee Braves, Redlegs, Phillies Bluejay era) — ~10–20x generic; $150–250 asks for 1940s–50s examples (AntiqueSportsShop) — city/name in the title, period logo.
- c. 1910–1920s sewn-letter college or event pennants, oversized 33–42" — top of the $40–500 band — raised felt letters with stitching, wool felt, very long.
- World's Fair 1933–34 Chicago and 1939 New York — $30–100+ each vs $8–25 travel — fair name and year, Trylon & Perisphere (1939).
- Political / civil-rights event pennants (e.g. 1963 March on Washington) — collector/museum interest, listed as "rare" on 1stDibs (asking) — event name and date printed.
- Mixed travel lot with a sports or fair pennant inside — turns a $15 lot into $100+ — scan every pennant in lot photos.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Bright colours, sharp graphics, no moth holes, intact ties/streamers. Original tassels add value; tassel loss is common (AntiqueSportsShop).
- Wool felt is denser and fuzzier; rayon/acetate felt (1950s+) is smoother; 1970s+ synthetic felt is thin and stiff.
- Flocked letters raised above the felt; screen prints flat; sewn letters show stitching on the back.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Moth holes (very common), fading, staining, creases, tape residue, torn ties, tack holes, felt loss, dulled tip (AntiqueSportsShop notes felt loss and tip sharpness as condition points).
- Ask for a back photo (moth damage and repairs show there) and a ruler photo.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Modern reproductions of sports pennants (Mitchell & Ness, Wincraft throwbacks) exist; usually labelled. Some reproduction World's Fair pennants.
- Modern repros use thin synthetic felt, bright digital prints and modern tags.

## Market
- Steady decorator and collector demand; lots sell well. Single common pennants are slow. Sports collectors pay for era, roster and size; decorators pay for graphics and colour.

## What sellers usually miss
- Sellers sell "assorted pennants" in bulk without checking for sports/World's Fair pieces mixed in.
- Maker tags and size; the difference between a full 29" and a ¾ pennant.
- Defunct-team names and roster pennants listed as generic "baseball pennant".

## Shipping / handling
- Flat, light: poly mailer or flat box, $6–15 for lots. Do not fold wool pennants hard; roll or ship flat.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- Length, subject, condition (moth holes), era; group into lots by theme. Photograph the back and any maker tag.

### What to tell the buyer
"Vintage [wool/rayon] felt pennant, [subject], [length] in [full size/¾], c. [decade] — dated by [sewn letters / team name / maker tag]. Original [tassels/ties] [present/missing]. Condition: [n] small moth holes, [fading], no tears; back shown in photos. Maker tag: [name or none]."

## Buy rule
- Price ≤ $200; profit = sold median × 0.87 − ship to buyer (~$8) − ship to Dylan (~$10) − price ≥ max($50, price).
- A lot reselling at $100 allows max buy ≈ $30. Single common pennants never.
