# Wooden (woodie) hand planes & moulding planes
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: craigslist 1094 photos (3 unmarked jack/jointer/smoother planes, $70); eBay sold pattern (knowledge 2024–25); extends existing hand-planes entry (metal Stanley/Lie-Nielsen)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Beech bodies: coffin-shaped smoothers (7–9"), jack (14–16"), jointers (22–30") with a front "horn" or tote; iron wedged in. Maker stamps on the front end (toe): Ohio Tool, Sandusky, Auburn, Greenfield, Union Factory/Chapin, Scioto, Marples/Mathieson (UK). Moulding planes: narrow (~9.5" × 1–3") with profiled soles, stamped on the toe; sets of hollows & rounds. Transitional planes (wood body, Stanley metal frog) = cheap. Sellers write "antique wood plane", "old wooden planer", "primitive tools".

## What sellers usually miss
Toe stamps; plow planes (fence + screw arms) mistaken for "weird old tool"; boxwood/rosewood screws; complete iron sets.

## Shipping / handling
Jointers 26–30" are oversize, 5–8 lb, $25–35 each way. eBay.

## Buy rule
Buy when: screw-arm plow plane ≤ $60, H&R set ≤ $100, named 18th-c. maker at any price ≤ $200. Pass when: unmarked bench planes in lots over $20/plane. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
