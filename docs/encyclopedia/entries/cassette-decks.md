## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
19"-wide (430 mm) component decks, 1970s–2000s. Value tells visible on the front panel:
- "3 HEAD", "Dolby S", "dbx", "Dual capstan", "Direct drive", "Closed loop", bias/level calibration knobs, "ES" (Sony), "Dragon"/"NAAC" (Nakamichi), "Auto Azimuth".
- Single decks are usually better than dual "dubbing" decks; dual decks with "Playback only" Deck A are budget models.
- Brushed aluminium faceplates, VU meters (1970s top-loaders) are attractive; black plastic 1990s = budget.

## Makers, brands and variants
Top: Nakamichi (Japan, 1972–; 480/580/582/680/BX-300/CR-7/ZX-7/Dragon/1000), Tandberg (Norway, TCD-3014/3034), Revox B215/B710, Aiwa XK-S7000/S9000, Sony TC-K777ES/TC-KA7ES, Technics RS-B965, Pioneer CT-S920/S830, Denon DR-M44HX, TEAC V-8030S/C-3X, Akai GX-F95/GX-R99, Harman Kardon CD491. Mid: Yamaha K-series (K-903 dual deck is budget), Onkyo TA, JVC TD-V. Budget: Sanyo, Fisher, Realistic, Emerson.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Heavy (>12 lb), metal chassis, discrete transports, multiple motors = quality. Light (<8 lb), plastic fronts, "Synchro dub", "High-speed dubbing", "Continuous play" = budget.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Almost all 30–40-year-old decks need belts (melted/stretched), idler tires, pinch roller replacement, head cleaning/demagnetising; eats tapes; doors stuck; speed drift. This is "repair" under Dylan's rules unless the seller demonstrates playback.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not applicable (no fakes). Watch for "serviced" claims without proof.

## Market
Cassette revival keeps demand steady (2019–); working decks sell in 1–3 weeks; non-working mass-market decks languish. Nakamichi sells fast at any condition.

## What sellers usually miss
Nakamichi and Tandberg priced like ordinary decks at estate sales; Aiwa XK-S, Sony ES and Technics RS-B decks in "stereo lot" listings.

## Shipping / handling
17×6×12", 8–15 lb, double-box with foam, $20–35; transport screws/locks not usually needed. Fits passenger seat.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State tested functions (play, FF/REW, record, both decks), belt status, speed, and include video of playback; say "sold as-is" otherwise.

## Buy rule
Budget dual decks: resale $25–70 working; profit = 50 × 0.87 − $25 − $20 − price < 0 → pass, even at $10. Untested electronics are excluded. Only buy demonstrated-working Nakamichi/Tandberg/Revox/Aiwa XK-S/3-head decks at ≤ 25% of resale.
