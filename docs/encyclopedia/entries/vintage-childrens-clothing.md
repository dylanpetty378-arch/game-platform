## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Pre-1990 kids' clothing. Era tells: union labels, single-stitch hems on tees, metal snaps/zippers (Talon), bold 1970s colors, smocking.

## Makers, brands and variants
Florence Eiseman (Milwaukee, couture kids, $40–150); Feltman Brothers (heirloom); Polly Flinders (smocked $20–50); Health-tex/Carter's ($10–30); OshKosh Vestbak ($30–80); handmade ($8–25).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Cotton/wool natural fibers; acrylic yarn = 1970s–80s handmade.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Stains, yellowing, pilling, holes.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not significant.
