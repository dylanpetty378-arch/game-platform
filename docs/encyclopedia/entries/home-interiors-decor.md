## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Direct-sales home decor sold at home parties by Home Interiors & Gifts (founded by Mary Crowley). Framed prints in ornate resin or wood frames with oval double mats (lighthouses, florals, Victorian scenes, country), mirrors, brass sconces, Homco porcelain/resin figurines (Bisque children, animals, Native American, Masterpiece Porcelain), candle holders. Back of frames carry "Home Interiors" stickers with stock numbers.

## Makers, brands and variants
Homco (figurines, made in Taiwan/Japan), Masterpiece Porcelain (Homco premium), Burwood Products (resin frames/plaques), Interior Specialty items; Gifts by Celebrating Home (successor).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Offset-litho prints (not originals), resin or composition frames with gilt, glass glazing. Low quality uniform production; little variation.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Frame corner chips, faded prints, sun-yellowed mats; figurines chipped.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
