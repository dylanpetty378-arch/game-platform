# Surveying transits, levels and drafting instruments (K&E, Gurley, Dietzgen, Berger)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay listings Oct 2026, journalofantiques.com, agent knowledge

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Brass or black-crinkle instrument on a 3-screw/4-screw base in a wooden dovetailed box. Maker on the horizontal circle or a plate: **Keuffel & Esser (K&E)**, **W. & L.E. Gurley (Troy NY)**, **Dietzgen**, **Berger**, **Buff & Buff**, **Brunton** (pocket transit). Serial number dates it (K&E serials under ~50,000 = pre-1920, brass, higher value).

## What sellers usually miss
Maker and serial; "antique surveying tool" with brass showing. Tripod + box + plumb bob add 30 %.

## Shipping / handling
15–25 lb with box, $40–60 (double box, remove from base). eBay.

## Buy rule
Buy when: brass Gurley/K&E/Young with box under $120, or Brunton under $50. Pass when: black 1950s–70s K&E at $150+ (market), levels. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
