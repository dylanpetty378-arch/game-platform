# Royal Doulton character (Toby) jugs
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold/active listings Oct 2025 (Beefeater, Bobby, Guardsman D6755, Monty), general collector knowledge (D-numbers)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Glazed ceramic face jug with the handle forming a prop (Beefeater's halberd, Bobby's helmet strap, Guardsman's busby). Base is stamped "Royal Doulton" lion-and-crown with a D-number (D6xxx). Sizes: large (~7"), small (~4"), miniature (~2.5"), tiny. Sellers say "Toby jug", "Royal Doulton mug", "character jug".

## What sellers usually miss
D-numbers and colour variants; otherwise the common ones are simply worth little. A box of 4 common large jugs is a ~$100 retail lot, not a find.

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb each, breakable – double box, ~$12–15 each. eBay ~13% fees. Slow sellers.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a rare D-number/colourway is visible under $50. Pass when: common subjects at more than $8 each. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
