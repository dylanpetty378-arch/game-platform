# Royal Doulton character (Toby) jugs
Updated: 2026-10-12 · upgraded 2026-10-13 · Sources: eBay sold/active listings Oct 2025 (Beefeater, Bobby, Guardsman D6755, Monty), general collector knowledge (D-numbers)
Slug: royal-doulton-character-jugs · Aliases: Toby jug, Royal Doulton mug, character jug, Royal Doulton jug, D-number jug, Beefeater jug
Summary: Common large subjects $15–40 each and small/mini $10–25 each; limited editions and colourway variants $100–400; pre-1950 rare colourways and prototypes $500–5,000.
Buy rule (short): Only a rare D-number or colourway visible under $50; pass on common subjects over $8 each.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
A glazed ceramic face jug whose handle forms a prop (Beefeater's halberd, Bobby's helmet strap, Guardsman's busby). The base is stamped with the "Royal Doulton" lion-and-crown and a D-number (D6xxx). Sizes: large (~7"), small (~4"), miniature (~2.5"), tiny. Sellers say "Toby jug", "Royal Doulton mug", "character jug". A strict Toby jug is a full seated figure, while a character jug is head and shoulders only, but sellers mix the terms (general knowledge, verify). Look-alikes: unmarked English and Japanese face jugs, Sandland, Beswick, Kevin Francis and Wood & Sons (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Royal Doulton (Burslem)**: the character jug range ran from 1934 into the 2000s (general knowledge, verify).
- **Common large subjects** ($15–40 each), made for decades: Beefeater, Bobby, Guardsman (D6755), Monty, Falstaff, Old Charley, Sairey Gamp, Poacher.
- **Small and miniature sizes** of common subjects, and Dickens sets ($10–25 each).
- **Collectible** ($100–400): limited editions, the Williamsburg series, and colourway or rare variants such as the white-handle Beefeater, "Mephistopheles" and "Clown" with red hair. Check the D-number against Doulton guides.
- **Top** ($500–5,000): pre-1950 rare colourways and prototypes; need proof.
- "A" mark and early backstamps indicate early production of some subjects (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Glazed earthenware, hand-painted. The printed backstamp includes the character name, D-number and copyright date.
- The copyright date is the design registration date, not the production year (general knowledge, verify).
- Quality pieces show crisp moulding and careful colour. Seconds and later pieces may be blurrier.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Chips on noses, hat brims and handles are common.
- Hairlines and restored handles cut value hard. Restoration shows as a matte or colour-mismatched patch.
- Crazing is common and mildly negative.
- Ask for the backstamp photo and close-ups of the handle joins and rim.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fakes of common subjects are not worth making. The risks are fake "rare colourways" (repainted common jugs) and mis-attributed non-Doulton jugs (general knowledge, verify). Doulton reissued some subjects in new sizes and colourways, so the backstamp details matter.

## Market
Oversupplied. The collector base is ageing and has shrunk a lot since the 1990s peak (general knowledge, verify). Common jugs are slow sellers on eBay. Rare variants still sell to specialist collectors and through UK auctions. No real seasonality.

## What sellers usually miss
D-numbers and colour variants. Otherwise the common ones are simply worth little. A box of 4 common large jugs is a ~$100 retail lot, not a find.

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb each and breakable. Double box, ~$12–15 each. eBay ~13% fees. Slow sellers.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Title: "Royal Doulton [Character] Character Jug D#### [Large/Small/Mini] [year]". State the D-number, size and height, backstamp date, colourway and condition (no chips, cracks or restoration). Photograph the backstamp clearly and every side, including the handle.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a rare D-number or colourway is visible under $50. Pass when: common subjects at more than $8 each. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat.
