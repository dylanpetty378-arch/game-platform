## Additional research (earlier entry: Guitar effects pedals (boutique vs mass-market))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: Reverb price guide knowledge, eBay sold knowledge, retail prices (Sweetwater) for current models

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Small metal boxes with a footswitch. Value is brand + model + era. **Mass-market, no margin** (sell at 50–70% of retail, retail is $60–150): Boss compact (SD-1, DS-1, CE-5, TU-3), Behringer, Mosky, Tone City, Donner, Joyo, Zoom/Line 6 multi-effects. **Boutique / sought-after** (hold value, $150–400): Keeley (Compressor Plus, Katana), Wampler (Tumnus, Plexi-Drive), EarthQuaker Devices, Strymon, Chase Bliss, JHS, Analog Man, Fulltone (OCD, Full-Drive), Red Panda (Context, Particle), Walrus Audio, Eventide H9. **Vintage money** ($300–3,000): Ibanez TS-808/TS9 1980s (Japan, "R" logo, JRC4558D chip), Boss CE-1, DM-2, SD-1 "Made in Japan" black label, Pro Co RAT "whiteface"/big box, MXR script-logo Phase 90/Dyna Comp, Electro-Harmonix Big Muff "Ram's Head"/"Triangle", Klon Centaur (gold horsie – $3,000–5,000; clones are $50–300), Dallas Arbiter Fuzz Face, Univox Super-Fuzz. Sellers usually name the pedal – the information gap is in vintage vs reissue (serial, label colour, "Made in Japan"/"Taiwan").

## Additional research (earlier entry: Guitar effects pedals (Boss, Ibanez, MXR, Electro-Harmonix, Dunlop, boutique))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: Reverb listing 97818150 (Boss DM-2 MIJ, $573 ask, Reverb used-low $160 from 60 listings), equipboard Boss CE-2 black-label page, guitarpedalx Boss modulation guide, sweep listings 2026-10 (ODB-3 $40, RC-3 $95, DC-2w $175, JHF2 Fuzz Face $175, SubDecay Super Spring Theory $80, Behringer VP1 $75).

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Small stomp box with 1–4 knobs and a footswitch. The maker and model name are printed on the face – always legible in the photo. Valuable tells:
- **Boss**: "Made in Japan" on the back + **black label** (1977–~1988, silver thumbscrew) = vintage. Models that matter: **CE-1** (big grey box, $500–900), **DM-2 / DM-3** analog delay ($250–450), **SP-1 Spectrum** ($600+), **DC-2 Dimension C** ($300–450), **CE-2 black label** ($200–300), **OD-1** ($150–300), **SD-1 black label** ($100–150), **VB-2 Vibrato** ($500–800), **PH-1** ($100–150), **SG-1 Slow Gear** ($400+). Taiwan "Made in Taiwan" pink/blue label 1990s+ = $30–80 (except DD-3/DD-2 $80–120). **Waza Craft** (W-suffix, DC-2w, CE-2w, DM-2w) = modern, $150–200 used.
- **Ibanez**: TS-808 narrow box (1979–81) $600–1,200; TS9 original (1982–84) $200–350; reissues $60–90. Maxon-made 0-series (AD-9, FL-9) $150–300.
- **MXR**: script logo (no LED) 1970s Phase 90/Distortion+/Dyna Comp $150–400; block logo $80–150.
- **Electro-Harmonix**: 1970s big-box Big Muff (Ram's Head, Triangle) $500–1,500; Deluxe Memory Man original $300–500; Russian Sovtek Big Muff green/black $150–300; modern nano $40–80.
- **Dunlop Fuzz Face** JHF1/JHF2/FFM: $80–175 used; original 1960s Arbiter Fuzz Face $800–3,000.
- **Boutique** (Klon Centaur $3,000–6,000; Klon KTR $300–400; Analogman, JHS, Strymon, Chase Bliss, EQD) – Strymon Timeline/BigSky $250–350; most others $80–200.
- Wah: Vox/Thomas Organ Clyde McCoy 1960s $400–1,000; Cry Baby $40–60.

Black label / silver screw / "Made in Japan" on Boss; script vs block logo on MXR; narrow box on Tube Screamers. Sellers list "Boss chorus pedal $40" without the label photo – ask for the back. Also pedal *lots* ("box of guitar pedals $100") frequently hide one MIJ pedal worth the lot.

Small metal boxes with a footswitch. ## Additional research (earlier entry: Guitar effects pedals (Boss / MXR / Ibanez compacts))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: audiofanzine.com PS-3 market value ($142, dated); Reverb/eBay sold ranges from agent knowledge (verify on Reverb price guide before buying); run12 sheets 56–63 (five pedal listings)

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Compact stomp boxes. Boss: rectangular, one colour per model, model code printed under the footswitch (DD-3, PS-3, CS-2, DS-1, MT-2, OD-1, CE-2, SD-1). MXR: smaller plain block with a script "MXR" and a model name (Phase 90, Carbon Copy, Dyna Comp, Bass Octave Deluxe). Ibanez TS-808/TS-9 Tube Screamer: green. Sellers almost always name the model in the title, so the "unknowing seller" angle is rare – the edge is lot pricing (several pedals for one price) and old-stock variants (Made-in-Japan Boss, script-logo MXR, black-label DS-1).
Key tells for the valuable versions: Boss "Made in Japan" on the back plate and a silver screw (pre-1990 = Japan, 1990+ Taiwan); MXR "script logo" (1974–81) vs block logo; Boss CE-1 (big grey chorus box) and DM-2 (analog delay) are the big ones.
