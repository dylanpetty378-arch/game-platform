## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
A jointed stuffed bear (head and four limbs on disc joints — visible as a flat circle at each shoulder/hip under the fur, limbs swivel), 8-30 in tall, mohair (wool-goat fibre with a glossy, slightly wiry look that mats and thins with age) or synthetic plush, stuffed with wood wool/excelsior (crunchy, firm, old), kapok (soft, 1920s-50s British) or polyester (modern), with glass eyes (pre-1950s: shoe-button black boots-button eyes before ~1910, then glass on wire), plastic safety eyes (1960s+), a stitched nose and mouth in black/brown thread (shape and stitch direction identify makers), felt or velour pads, a growler or squeaker. Steiff tells: a small metal button in the left ear (sometimes with a paper/cloth ear tag beside it), a paper chest tag on the front (bear-head shape, colours by era), long arms with curved paws, a hump on the back on early bears, a pointed "shaved" muzzle. Raikes tells: a carved and finished wooden face (muzzle) and wooden paw pads on a plush body, signature "Robert Raikes" burned/stamped on a foot pad, a numbered/edition hang tag by Applause. Merrythought: a label on the foot pad ("Merrythought Ironbridge Shrops Made in England"); "Cheeky" bears have bells in the ears and a wide grin. Hermann: red/green triangle or round tag "Hermann Teddy Original". Schuco: miniature (2.5-3.5 in) bears, often "Yes/No" tail-moved heads; perfume-bottle and compact bears. Chad Valley/Chiltern: British, "Chad Valley Hygienic Toys" celluloid-covered button or label, Chiltern "Hugmee" with shield-shaped noses. Ideal (US, 1903-): no labels, pointed snouts, long bodies. Title words: Steiff, button, mohair, jointed, antique, "growler", Raikes, "Applause", artist bear, limited edition. Look-alikes: Steiff-style Chinese "mohair" bears with a fake button, artist bears (one-of-a-kind modern, $50-300, signed), and Boyds/Bearington "antique-style" bears with sewn-on fake buttons.

## Makers, brands and variants
**Steiff (Giengen, Germany, 1880-; bears from 1902, PB55).** Button-in-ear from 1 Nov 1904 (Wikipedia). Button/ear-tag eras (collector references, approximate): 1904-05 elephant button (tiny, elephant with S-shaped trunk) — rarest; 1905-c.1909 blank button; c.1905-1950 "STEIFF" with the final F underscored and trailing ("FF underscored" button), with a white ear tag (1908-25) then red (1926-34) then yellow (1934-50) printed with the article number; 1952-c.1970 raised script "Steiff" button with yellow tag; c.1970-77 incised script (chrome); 1977-c.1986 brass incised button; 1986-2000s brass with white (limited/replica), yellow (standard) or red (club) tags; 2000s-present gold-plated button; "Steiff" with a red cloth tag = 1990s-2000s club bears. Chest tags: 1928-50 red/blue/yellow bear-head tags; 1950-72 "Steiff Original Teddy" bear-face tag with a yellow background and later white; 1972+ split yellow/red oval. Mohair bears 1902-1930s with the hump, long arms, boot-button or glass eyes and FF-underscored button: $800-5,000 for ordinary 10-16 in examples, $5,000-50,000+ for large, rare colours (white, cinnamon, black 1912 "mourning" bears, "Teddy Girl" 1904 $165,000) — auction territory. 1950s-60s mohair "Original Teddy" with raised script button, chest tag and ear tag: $80-400 (5 in $60-120; 14 in $150-400); Zotty (open-mouth, tipped mohair, 1951-) $60-250; 1980s-2000s replicas (1902 "PB 28", 1906, "Teddy Rose", "Teddy Clown"), club bears and limited editions, boxed with certificate: $60-250 (some larger/early replicas $300-600); 1980s-2020s synthetic "Cosy"/"Petsy" plush and the modern open-edition mohair: $10-60. Steiff animals (lions, dogs, rabbits) follow the same tag logic.

**Robert Raikes (US artist, 1945-2016) / Applause (1985-1990s).** Hand-carved wooden-faced bears made by Raikes from 1982, then licensed to Applause in 1985: the first Applause edition (Spring 1985, "Original Collection": Sherwood, Jamie, Tyrone, Bentley, Rebecca, Chelsea, Eric etc., numbered to 7,500 each with signed certificate; "Christmas 1985" editions; subsequent 1986-1992 sets of 5,000-15,000 each). Common Applause editions $25-80 each; 1985 first-edition bears with box and certificate $60-150; complete first-edition sets $300-600; hand-signed early Raikes originals (pre-1985 all-wood or Raikes-signed one-offs, "Woody Bears") $300-2,000. Estimates; the market softened since the 2000s.

**Merrythought (Ironbridge, England, 1930-2006, 2007-).** Foot label; "Cheeky" (1957-) with ear bells and a grin, mohair $150-600 (1950s-60s), later synthetic Cheekies $40-120; 1930s "Magnet" and "Bingie" bears $400-1,500. **Chad Valley** (1920s-70s, celluloid button or label) $150-800 1930s mohair. **Chiltern "Hugmee"** (1920s-60s) $150-700. **Farnell** (made the original Winnie-the-Pooh; 1908-1930s, often unlabelled, webbed-claw stitching) $800-5,000.

**Hermann (Gebrüder Hermann, Hirschaid; Teddy-Hermann Sonneberg) and Schuco (Nuremberg, 1912-76).** Hermann 1950s mohair with the red-green-white tag $80-300; Schuco miniatures 1920s-50s $100-400 (Yes/No bears $300-1,200; perfume/compact bears $400-1,500); Schuco "Tricky" 1950s $200-500.

**Ideal (US)**, Knickerbocker (US, 1920s-60s, label on side seam) $80-400 for mohair examples; Gund 1930s-60s $40-200, modern Gund $5-30; Dakin, Boyds, Bearington, Vermont Teddy Bear, Build-A-Bear: $5-40 except boxed Boyds first editions $20-60. Artist bears (one-of-a-kind, signed, mohair, 1980s-2020s): $50-400 by artist reputation.

### Quick ID checklist
1. Button in the left ear — style (elephant/blank/FF-underscored/raised script/incised/brass), and ear tag colour (white/red/yellow) and the printed article number (e.g. "5325,2").
2. Chest tag style and text; foot-pad labels (Merrythought, Hermann, Knickerbocker, Chad Valley, Raikes signature).
3. Fur: mohair (sheen, backing weave visible in bald spots) vs synthetic (uniform, plastic shine) vs "distressed" modern mohair (even thinning).
4. Eyes: boot-button (pre-1910), glass (1910-60s), plastic safety (1960s+).
5. Stuffing by touch/description: excelsior (crunchy, firm) = pre-1960s; kapok (soft, British); foam/polyester = modern.
6. Shape: hump, long curved arms, large feet, pointed muzzle, "center seam" head (Steiff 1904-1915 every seventh bear) = early; chubby short-armed = post-1950.
7. Growler/squeaker working; pads original (felt, worn) vs replaced (new felt, mismatched).
8. Raikes: carved wooden muzzle with eyes set in wood, wooden paw pads, foot signature, Applause hang tag/box/certificate.

### Golden-egg tells
- Steiff with an elephant button or a blank button (1904-09) and a hump, long arms, boot-button eyes, centre-seam head — $3,000-50,000+ vs $100-300 for a 1950s-60s raised-script bear — the button is a plain pewter disc or shows a tiny elephant; shoe-button eyes; excelsior-hard body.
- Steiff "FF underscored" button with a WHITE ear tag (1908-25) on a mohair bear — $800-5,000 vs $100-300 (yellow tag 1950s-60s) — the last F of STEIFF trails under the word; the tag is white with the article number; long arms and large feet.
- Rare colours on early Steiff: white, cinnamon, black (1912), "Teddy Clown"/"Teddy Baby"/"Petsy" (blue eyes, 1928) — 2-10× the same-age brown bear — colour plus the button; blue glass eyes on Petsy.
- Merrythought mohair "Cheeky" 1957-1970s with ear bells, open felt mouth and foot label — $150-600 vs $40-120 synthetic 1980s+ — bells inside the ears (ask seller to shake), mohair sheen, label on foot.
- Schuco "Yes/No" bear (tail moves the head) and perfume-bottle/compact miniatures — $300-1,500 vs $30-60 ordinary 1950s miniature — tail is a lever; head opens to reveal a bottle/compact.
- Farnell (1908-30s) with webbed-stitch claws across the paws and kapok stuffing, unlabelled — $800-5,000 vs $100 unlabelled unknown — four claws joined by a stitched web; long shaved muzzle; "Alpha" label if present.
- Raikes 1985 Applause first "Original Collection" bear with box, certificate and the original wrist tag, numbered ≤ 7,500 — $60-150 (set $300-600) vs $25-50 loose later editions — certificate reads "First Edition 1985"; name on the hang tag (Sherwood, Jamie, Tyrone, Bentley, Rebecca, Chelsea, Eric).
- Pre-Applause Raikes "Woody Bears" with fully carved wooden faces and bodies/signed one-offs — $300-2,000 — heavier carving, no Applause tag, hand-signed and dated.
- Steiff 1980s-2000s replica/limited bears with WHITE ear tag plus the yellow-boxed certificate (e.g. "Teddy Bear 1906 replica", "Teddy Rose", "Alfonzo" red bear) — $150-600 vs $30-60 for the same-era yellow-tag open edition — white tag = limited; look for the numbered certificate and box.
- Knickerbocker / Ideal 1920s-40s US mohair with the original label — $150-500 vs $40-80 unlabelled — side-seam or chest label; Ideal has no label (shape only).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Mohair: goat fibre woven on a cotton backing; glossy, slightly coarse, pile can be long/curly (1920s), tipped (Zotty), distressed; bald patches show a woven cotton backing. Synthetic plush: nylon/acrylic, uniform, no backing weave, flat shine, often "Dralon" (1960s-80s Steiff) — fine for play, low value. Alpaca/wool plush on some 1930s-50s bears. Pads: felt (pre-1950s, wears through), velveteen/velour (1950s+), leather/ultra-suede (artist bears), wood (Raikes), plastic "Cosy" pads (1970s). Eyes: boot-button, glass (hand-painted backs, on wire loops), plastic. Joints: cardboard/wood discs with metal pins (early), later plastic. Stuffing: excelsior (wood wool) is the pre-1950s standard — a stiff, crunchy, slightly heavy bear; kapok lighter; polyester modern. Construction quality: hand-stitched noses with even stitches and the maker's characteristic shape (Steiff vertical nose stitches, Chad Valley horizontal, Chiltern shield); hand-closed back seam. Old vs new: genuine age shows uneven wear concentrated at nose, paws and belly, a dusty smell, dull wire eye loops, "mohair thinning" with intact backing; artificially aged modern bears have even thinning and new felt pads.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Common: moth/mohair loss (bald patches — −30-60% depending on extent), replaced eyes (mismatched or modern, −30%), replaced pads (new felt, −20-30%), re-stitched nose (−20%), broken growler (minor), loose joints (minor), odour/mould (serious), missing button/tags (Steiff without button −40-60%; a bear with button + ear tag + chest tag is top), restuffed/opened seams, crayon/ink stains, "surgery" (re-sewn ears/limbs). Acceptable: light wear, worn pads, sun-fading. Fatal for value: heavy bald, wet/rotted excelsior, head re-attached, re-furred. Ask: button and tags present (photos), stuffing type, any repairs/replacements, growler working, smell, height.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Steiff replicas (1980s-2020s) of early bears are legitimate but priced far below originals — the white tag/brass or gold button and certificate identify them. Fake Steiff buttons (pewter copies, stitched-on buttons on Chinese bears) exist: a genuine button is riveted through the ear with a back washer and the ear tag sits beside it; the script and font match the era. "Antique-style" artist and Chinese mohair bears are sold as "old" — new backing cloth, bright pads, polyester stuffing give them away. Raikes: reproductions are rare, but Applause editions are often sold as "first edition" when they are 1986-90 sets; the certificate wording and edition number tell. Ideal/Farnell unlabelled early bears are the attribution risk: many "Farnell" or "Ideal" bears are unknown-maker; price only on the shape tells with photos reviewed by a specialist.

## Market
Steady collector market with an ageing buyer base: Steiff vintage/antique sells in days to weeks on eBay (sell-through ~50% for tagged mohair), best at specialist auctions (Teddy Bear auctions at Vectis UK, Morphy's, Bertoia) for pre-1930 bears; Raikes and modern limiteds are slow (weeks to months) and have declined since the 1990s-2000s speculative peak; plush is unsellable at scale. Buyers: collectors (older, Steiff club), grandparents (gifts), decorators (large antique bears). Seasonality: November-December strongest, also Valentine's. Trend: early Steiff stable/strong; mid-century Steiff steady; 1980s-90s limiteds and Raikes falling slowly.

## What sellers usually miss
The button and tags (they say "old Steiff bear" without photographing the ear or chest tag; or "teddy bear collection" with a Steiff in the pile), mohair vs synthetic, excelsior stuffing (sign of age), the height, growler, and that Raikes 1985 first editions differ from the later sets. Conversely, sellers overprice Boyds/Gund/Steiff synthetics as "collectible" and Raikes at 1990s retail ($100-200).

## Shipping / handling
A 12-16 in bear in a box ~2-3 lb, $12-20 USPS Ground Advantage/Priority; large (24-30 in) bears 5-8 lb and oversized boxes $25-45. Pack in a poly bag against moisture, with soft fill, do not compress excelsior bears; do not use mothballs (odour) but do check for moths/webbing. Fragility low except old felt pads and glass eyes. Fits a passenger seat up to ~30 in.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Title: maker – era – material (mohair) – size – button/tag status – model name (Original Teddy, Zotty, Cheeky, Raikes "Sherwood" 1985 first edition) – jointed – growler. State: height, fur fibre, stuffing, eyes, pads, joints working, growler/squeaker, button and tags with transcription of the article number, box/certificate/wrist tag for limiteds, all wear and repairs, smell-free/smoke-free. Photograph: front, back, each paw pad, ear button close-up with tag, chest tag, nose stitching, bald spots, label on foot, certificate. Keywords: button in ear, FF underscored, raised script, white tag, limited edition, replica, excelsior, glass eyes, hump, Cheeky, Yes/No, first edition.

## Buy rule
Buy when: (a) a mohair bear shows a Steiff button (any era) with ear/chest tag, a Merrythought/Hermann/Knickerbocker/Chad Valley label, or a Schuco mechanism, is largely intact (no heavy bald, original eyes/pads), and is priced ≤ 30% of a conservative sold value; or (b) a Raikes 1985 first-edition set or signed pre-Applause original at ≤ $40 per bear with box/certificate. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after 13% fees and ~$15 shipping each way. Pass when: tagless "antique" bears (attribution risk), synthetic plush, Raikes loose later editions above $20, bears with mould/odour/heavy loss, replicas priced as originals, and any pre-1915 Steiff above $200 where authenticity needs a specialist. Must fit a passenger seat (≤ 30 in).
