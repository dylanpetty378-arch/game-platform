## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Figural glass/ceramic bottles holding Avon aftershave (Wild Country, Spicy, Tai Winds, Everest) or cologne, often with original boxes. Shapes: Model T and classic cars, pipes, barrels ("After Shave on Tap"), presidential busts/portraits, Indian-head penny, mailboxes ("First Class Male"), stagecoach, marlin, Dutch pipe (Delft-look). Base embossed "Avon" with a date/fill-level marking.
Cape Cod 1876 (1975–1990s): ruby red pressed glass with diamond and circle pattern — decanter, goblets, candlesticks.

## Makers, brands and variants
Avon Products (NY); predecessor California Perfume Co. (1886–1939; labels "CPC" or "Perfection" brand). Regional similar: Wheaton "presidential" bottles (not Avon).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Amber/frosted glass with plastic or metal caps; painted caps rub. Original boxes add little.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Evaporated contents, missing caps/labels, box wear. Most buyers want empty.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not faked; Avon reissued many shapes.

## Market
Oversupplied, very slow; collectors aging.

## What sellers usually miss
Sellers overvalue; CPC-era bottles are the only real find.

## Shipping / handling
Each piece ~0.5–1 lb; lots ship $15–25.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Name, year, contents level, box condition; group into themed lots.

## Buy rule
Profit = median × 0.87 − shipping − price. Lot of 20 at $60: resale ~$50 → loss. **Pass.**
