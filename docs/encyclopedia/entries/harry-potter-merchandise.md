## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Licensed merchandise from 1999 onward (Warner Bros. licence). Everything is mass-produced and recent; value comes from scarcity of specific editions, boxes and sealed condition.
- **Noble Collection** (1990s–present): heavy, quality die-cast and resin replicas — wands in boxes with Noble labels, Time-Turner (gold-plated with turning hourglass), Golden Snitch, Horcrux replicas. Look for "Noble Collection" stamp and a numbered card on limited editions.
- **Hallmark Keepsake** ornaments (2017–present): chibi-style figures (Harry on broom), dated, in boxes with Hallmark labels. Loose $5–15, boxed $12–30.
- **Funko Pop** (2011–present): box condition matters; exclusives (Convention, Hot Topic, Barnes & Noble) and chase/flocked versions.
- **LEGO**: 2001–2011 sets (Hogwarts Castle 4709, 5378), 2018+ sets; sealed retired sets have collector value.
- **Mattel 2001–02** figures and Mattel "Hermione" dolls; **Universal Wizarding World** exclusives (interactive wands, Butterbeer mugs).
- **Generic merch** (Silver Buffalo mugs, Bioworld luggage tags, Loungefly purses, Spencer's, Primark/Hot Topic keychains, Cinereplicas scarves/ties).

## Makers, brands and variants
- Noble Collection (US/UK), Cinereplicas (Vienna; scarves, ties), Hallmark, Funko, LEGO, Mattel, Jakks Pacific (2018–), Enesco (resin figures), Silver Buffalo, Bioworld, Paladone, Loungefly, Wizarding World Universal Orlando/Hollywood/Japan exclusives.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Noble: heavy metal, crisp casting, packaged in branded boxes; knockoffs are light zinc/plastic, blurry engraving, no branding (the Time-Turner, Snitch and Chocolate-Frog keychains in our sweep look like unbranded keychain-grade pieces).
- Official merch carries a "TM & © WBEI" copyright line; unlicensed Etsy/AliExpress items don't.
- Boxes, tags and inserts drive value (Hallmark, Funko, Noble).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Box wear (Funko), missing wand box or card (Noble), tarnished plating, chipped mugs, cracked ornament attachments.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Massive volume of Chinese knockoffs: Time-Turners, Snitches, Deathly Hallows necklaces, wands, Funko fakes (wrong font, poor paint), LEGO knock-offs (Lepin/"Bela"). Noble re-releases many items.

## Market
Very liquid, enormous supply, mostly low value. Lots sell slowly; fans prefer to buy individual pieces. Holiday bump in Nov–Dec. LEGO and Funko have their own price-guide-driven markets.

## What sellers usually miss
- Sealed retired LEGO and Funko exclusives hidden in "HP stuff" lots.
- Noble Collection boxed items vs unbranded copies.
- Reverse: sellers price mugs/keychains at retail.

## Shipping / handling
Mugs heavy and fragile ($12–18 each); small items $5–8; mixed lots with mugs 8–15 lb, $25–35.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Brand (Noble, Hallmark, Funko number), year, box condition, licence line photo, all included pieces. Sell high-value pieces singly, the rest as a lot.

## Buy rule
Lots with mugs ship heavy; profit rule fails unless the lot hides LEGO, Noble or Funko exclusives. Buy only sealed retired LEGO, Noble boxed limited editions, or rare Funko at ≤ 30% of sold. Pass on keychains/mugs/ornament lots unless ≤ $20 and the lot includes ≥ $150 of boxed items.
