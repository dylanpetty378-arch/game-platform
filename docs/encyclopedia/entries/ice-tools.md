## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Tools of the ice trade (1850s–1950s), from pond harvesting to home iceboxes.
- **Tongs**: scissor-like, pointed hooked jaws, wrought or forged steel, from 10" household to 30" harvest/2-man.
- **Picks**: single steel spike in a wooden handle with a steel ferrule and often a cap; advertising picks have the company stamped or printed on the handle ("Coolerator", "Coca-Cola", "XYZ Ice & Fuel Co.").
- **Chippers**: 4–6 prongs on a cast head (like our sweep's example) for breaking blocks; **shavers**: a hand-held box with a blade for shaved ice (Gilchrist, Enterprise).
- **Harvest tools**: ice saws (4–6'), ice plows (horse drawn), chisels, pike poles, ice augers.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Gifford-Wood Co.** (Hudson NY, 1814–1950s): the major ice-tool maker; stamped "Gifford-Wood Co Hudson NY", often with a model number. Tongs $30–80, saws $60–150 (estimate).
- **Woodruff & Co.**, **W.T. Wood & Co.** (Arlington MA), **North Bros. Mfg.** (Philadelphia; "Ice Shredder"), **Gilchrist Co.** (Newark NJ; ice shavers and ice-cream scoops — the No. 31 scoop is collectible), **Enterprise Mfg.** (Philadelphia), **Sargent**, **Clyde Cutlery**.
- **Advertising ice picks**: 1920s–50s give-aways from ice companies, coal & ice dealers, Coca-Cola, Pepsi, refrigerator makers. $10–40, Coke more.
- **Multi-prong chippers**: generic household 1920s–50s, $10–25; stock-number-stamped hardware-store examples (our sweep's handle shows "…ONES" and stock numbers) are common.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Forged/wrought tongs show hammer marks, tapered square stock, a rivet pin. Cast tongs are thick and blunt with a pebbly cast surface — many décor reproductions are cast iron painted black (like the tongs in our sweep).
- Marked tools: stamped maker on the arms or handle.
- Advertising picks: legible stamped or painted text, original ferrule and cap.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rust and pitting, loose handles, replaced handles, worn-off advertising, bent points. Light surface rust is expected; buyers dislike heavy grind/paint "restorations".

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Cast-iron décor tongs and hooks (2000s–present, India/China), painted black or "rust" finished, often sold as "antique" — rough cast texture, no wear on the pivot, perfect symmetry. Reproduction Coca-Cola ice picks with plastic handles.

## Market
Primitives / farmhouse décor; a steady trickle of buyers. Plenty of supply; tongs and picks sell slowly at low prices. Local and antique-mall pricing often exceeds eBay sold.

## What sellers usually miss
- Gifford-Wood stamps on tongs and saws.
- Advertising text on pick handles (needs a close-up).
- Reverse: they price generic tongs as "antique ice block tongs" at $40–60.

## Shipping / handling
Tongs 2–5 lb, $14–20; picks/chippers under 1 lb, $6–9. Durable; wrap points.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "antique" + maker (Gifford-Wood / Gilchrist / North Bros) + item (ice tongs / ice pick / ice shaver) + advertising name (Coca-Cola, ice company) + "forged" + length. Junk: "rustic decor", "ice block tongs" for cast repros, "farmhouse" alone. `Antique Gifford-Wood Co Hudson NY Forged Ice Tongs 16in Marked Ice Harvest`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for marked tongs, advertising picks and Gilchrist shavers; FB local for generic tongs and chippers (ship heavy for $15–30).
- **Price position:** at the sold median ($25–70 marked/advertising; $60–150 Coke picks), accept −15%, floor 75%; OBO; cut 15% after 45 days; generic $10–30 local, firm.
- **Photos:** item on plain background; maker stamp close up; advertising text on the handle; points/jaws; forge marks vs casting seams; rust close up; next to a ruler.
- **State:** maker stamp, length, forged vs cast (cast décor tongs are not antique), advertising text transcribed, rust level, points intact, repairs by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** shoebox; tongs 2–5 lb $14–20, picks/chippers under 1 lb $6–9; wrap the points in cardboard so they don't punch through.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Gifford-Wood] forged ice tongs stamped Hudson NY, [era], [length]; cast reproduction tongs sell for [$] and marked harvest tools bring [$]."

## Buy rule
A $40 sale nets ~$35 before shipping ~$15 each way: a loss. Buy only advertising picks (Coca-Cola and named ice companies) and marked Gifford-Wood tools at ≤ $10 where sold ≥ $80. Pass on generic tongs/chippers. All fit a passenger seat except saws.
