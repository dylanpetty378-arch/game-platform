# Carved santos (polychrome wood religious figures)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold Jul–Oct 2026 (Chrome), Google Lens match to a Menino Jesus de Praga auction lot, Run 13

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hand-carved wooden saints, Virgins and Christ-child (Niño Jesús / Santo Niño / Menino Jesus) figures from Spanish, Mexican, Spanish-colonial (New Mexico, Philippines, Guatemala) and Portuguese/Brazilian churches and home altars, 17th–early 20th c. Tells of a real one: gesso ground with chipped/flaking paint showing white gesso and bare wood underneath; gilt on robes (bole-red showing through worn gilt); glass or painted eyes; hands carved separately and sometimes missing fingers; square-nailed or doweled bases; worm holes; dull matte surface. Titles sellers use: "antique wooden statue", "old Catholic statue", "church statue", "Jesus figure", "saint carving", "Santos". Reproductions: glossy even paint, uniform machine-sanded surface, "Made in Mexico/Philippines" stamp, resin (check for mould seams), stylised "Santa Fe" folk carvings from the 1980s–2000s.

## What sellers usually miss
Size and gilt. They describe condition ("badly chipped") as a negative; buyers pay for exactly that age evidence. They never say "polychrome", "gesso", "Santo Niño", "Spanish colonial" – search by those to sell. Ask for base/underside and back-of-head photos to confirm hand carving and old nails.

## Shipping / handling
Fragile (fingers, flaking gesso). Double-box with the figure wrapped loosely in tissue then bubble; 25" piece ≈ 8–12 lb boxed, $45–60 UPS Ground. Sell on eBay (religious antiques category) or to a Santa Fe / Tucson dealer; auction houses (Invaluable/LiveAuctioneers) for the top tier.

## Buy rule
Buy when: an 18"+ carved, gessoed figure with gilt and honest paint loss is under $200 – expect $400–800 sold. Pass when: under 12", glossy/even paint, resin, "Made in" stamp, or the seller already lists it as "antique santos polychrome" at $300+. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
