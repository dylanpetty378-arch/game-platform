## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Home-canning glass jars, 1858–present. Dating Ball jars by logo (Ball heritage chart): "BALL" block letters (1892–1896); "Ball" script with underline / triple-L (1900–1910s); script without underline, "dropped A" (1910–1923); script underscored "Ball" (1923–1933); no underline, modern (1933–1962); post-1962 rounded. "Ideal" lightning-type bail jars with glass lids ran 1915–1962; "PAT'D JULY 14 1908" is on many Ideals made long after 1908 (it is a patent date, not a manufacture date). Aqua ("Ball blue") glass is typical 1909–1937.
Other tells: ground lip (pre-1915, hand-finished) vs. smooth machine lip; "Mason's Patent Nov 30th 1858" (made for decades, 1858 is patent date); mould number on base; zinc lid with milk-glass liner (1869–1940s); wire bail "Lightning" (1882–).

## Makers, brands and variants
Ball (Muncie IN, 1880s–; Perfect Mason, Ideal, Improved, Sure Seal, Special, Eclipse), Atlas (Hazel-Atlas, Wheeling WV: Atlas Strong Shoulder Mason, E-Z Seal), Kerr (Self Sealing, 1903–), Drey (Square Mason), Root (Muncie), Lightning (Putnam), Hero, Crown (Canada), Kilner (UK), Woodbury, Trademark Lightning, Globe, Mason's Patent, Hemingray, Boyd, Clyde, Gem, Cohansey, Swayzee. Rare: "The Gem" amber, "Ball Perfect Mason" amber/olive, "Mason's Patent" cobalt, early wax-sealer jars, Ball "Buck" special editions.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Colour value ladder: clear < aqua/"Ball blue" < cornflower blue (light blue) < green/olive < amber < cobalt < milk glass/black glass. Bubbles ("seeds"), whittle marks, and ground lips add age and value. Reproduction colours (modern Ball heritage collection green, purple, blue 2013–2018) are flat, saturated colours with modern embossing "Ball ® Heritage Collection" and a smooth modern lip.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Cracks, chips at the lip, "sick" glass (cloudy haze, devalues 50%+), stains, missing or replaced lids/bails. Polishing to remove haze is a repair (excluded).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Ball's own 2010s Heritage Collection coloured "Perfect Mason" (blue, green, purple, amber, rose) are widely sold as "vintage" – check for "Heritage Collection" and the modern Ball logo and lack of mould seams over the lip. Chinese decorative "antique" jars in blue/green glass with bails (no maker embossing or odd fonts). Also 1976 Bicentennial and 1980s commemorative jars.

## Market
Enormous supply; aqua Balls sell slowly at $8–20 mainly for décor and weddings. Off-colour and rare jars sell quickly to fruit-jar collectors and at specialist auctions.

## What sellers usually miss
True olive/amber/cobalt jars mixed with aqua; sellers often call any coloured jar "rare" though – the edge is small-maker embossing (e.g. "The Gem", "Cohansey", "Woodbury") nobody recognises, and ground-lip pre-1900 jars.

## Shipping / handling
Glass, 1.5–3 lb with lid; double-box with bubble, $12–18. Breakage risk moderate.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker + embossing + colour + size + lip/closure: "Ball Perfect Mason olive green quart", "Mason's Patent 1858 cobalt", "The Gem amber", "wax sealer", "midget pint", "ground lip"; Junk: "vintage jars lot", "rustic wedding", "rare" on aqua Balls, "Heritage Collection" (modern). Example: `Antique Mason's Patent Nov 30th 1858 Cobalt Blue Quart Jar Ground Lip Zinc Lid`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for off-colour, small-maker and ground-lip jars (collectors buy fast); aqua Ball/Atlas quarts local in lots (decor and weddings); true rarities to Glass Works / Heckler auction.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (aqua Ideal $13, p75 $16; cobalt 1858 $45, p75 $77; wax sealer $30, p75 $86), accept −15%, floor p25; OBO; cut 10% after 3 weeks.
- **Photos:** first photo against white in natural light showing true colour; then embossing straight-on, lip from above (ground vs smooth), base marks and mould numbers, lid/bail, held to light for haze/cracks, ruler for height.
- **State:** exact embossing, colour in natural light, lip type, base marks/mould number, lid and bail original or replaced, haze/chips/cracks by photo number; note patent date ≠ manufacture date; modern Heritage check.
- **Pack / haul:** 1.5–3 lb with lid; bubble, double-box; $12–18.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Mason's Patent 1858] in true [cobalt], ground lip — the pre-1900 jar, not Ball's 2010s Heritage colours; no haze, original [zinc lid]; aqua ones are [$8–20], this colour is [$45–300]."

## Buy rule
Common aqua jar resale $10–20 ⇒ profit negative after ~$15 shipping each way. Pass. Buy only off-colour or rare embossed jars where Red Book/eBay sold ≥ $100 and price ≤ $25. Passenger-seat sized. No haze polishing.
