## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Flat black top-loading CD/USB deck with a large central jog wheel, pitch fader on the right, "Pioneer" bottom-left. Model name is printed top-right ("CDJ-1000MK3", "CDJ-2000NXS2"). CDJ-800/CDJ-1000 (2001–2008) load CDs only (MK3 adds MP3 CD); CDJ-900/2000 (2009+) add USB/rekordbox; Nexus/NXS2 are the club standard and hold most value. XDJ = no CD slot.

Note: the CDJs are slot-loading (front slot) rather than top-loading lids (general knowledge, verify — the original text says "top-loading").

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Pioneer DJ** (now AlphaTheta, general knowledge, verify): CDJ-200, CDJ-400, CDJ-800 (junk/common $80–200); CDJ-1000 MK1/MK2 ($150–250); CDJ-1000MK3 and CDJ-900 ($250–400, still used by scratch DJs); CDJ-2000, 2000NXS, NXS2, CDJ-3000 ($500–1,800).
- **XDJ** series: USB/media only, no CD slot.
- Value is per unit; matched pairs sell well (dealer pair $700).
- Competing brands (Denon DJ, Numark) sell for less (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Plastic and metal chassis, large jog wheel with display, pitch fader, USB port on 900/2000+. Check: firmware version, display working, jog wheel responsiveness, all buttons, disc reading (laser), USB read. Hours-of-use not shown; worn button labels indicate heavy club use (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Laser pickup wear is the failure mode — "skips" or "doesn't read discs" = pass. Also: jog wheel drift/insensitive, scratchy pitch fader, sticky buttons, cracked displays, USB port failures, missing power cable. Units must be tested.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No fakes. Stolen club gear is a risk with too-cheap listings; check serials are intact (general knowledge, verify).
