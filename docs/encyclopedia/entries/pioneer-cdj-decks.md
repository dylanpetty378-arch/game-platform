# Pioneer CDJ / DJ media players
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: 10kused.com dealer listing CDJ-1000MK3 set; eBay/Reverb used listings 2025

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Flat black top-loading CD/USB deck with a large central jog wheel, pitch fader on the right, "Pioneer" bottom-left. Model name is printed top-right ("CDJ-1000MK3", "CDJ-2000NXS2"). CDJ-800/CDJ-1000 (2001–2008) load CDs only (MK3 adds MP3 CD); CDJ-900/2000 (2009+) add USB/rekordbox; Nexus/NXS2 are the club standard and hold most value. XDJ = no CD slot.

## What sellers usually miss
Little; DJs know. Arbitrage comes from sellers dumping a single unit ("serious inquiries only", no price research) and from untested estate units.

## Shipping / handling
~10 lb, 12×15×5 in; jog wheel and faders are fragile – double box, ~$30–40 ground. Must be tested (laser pickup wear is the failure mode; "skips" = pass). eBay fees ~13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: CDJ-1000MK3/900 ≤ $120 tested; CDJ-2000 ≤ $250; NXS2 ≤ $600. Pass when: untested, "doesn't read discs", or CDJ-200/400/800 at any price over $50. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
