## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Fender-owned Guild sub-brand, 1998–2003, reissuing 1960s Guild shapes. "DeArmond" script on headstock; back of headstock says "Crafted in Korea" (1998–2000, better, set-neck, Bigsby on T models) or "Crafted in Indonesia" (2000–03, cheaper bolt-on, chrome covered pickups). Models: M-75 / M-75T (single-cut, 2 pickups, Bigsby on T), M-77T, M-72, M-65, M-66 (solid), Starfire / X-155 / X-135 (hollow), Jet-Star, Pilot basses. Sellers write "DeArmond", "Guild DeArmond", "Fender DeArmond".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **DeArmond** name comes from Harry DeArmond / Rowe Industries pickups, which Guild used in the 1950s–60s (general knowledge, verify).
- **Korean production (1998–2000)**: better build, set neck (per entry; verify per model), Bigsby on T models – M-75T, M-77T, X-155, Starfire Special, Jet-Star Special, sparkle finishes = collectible/top.
- **Indonesian production (2000–03)**: bolt-on, chrome covered pickups – M-65, M-72, M-75, M-77 player grade.
- Hollowbodies: Starfire, X-155, X-135. Basses: Pilot.
- Original 1950s–60s **Guild M-75 Aristocrat/Bluesbird** is a different, much more valuable guitar (general knowledge, verify) – don't confuse.
- DeArmond hardshell case adds $60–80.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Headstock back stamp "Crafted in Korea" vs "Crafted in Indonesia" is the key tell. Mahogany/agathis bodies, rosewood boards, Bigsby vibratos on T models, DeArmond-branded pickups. Check neck joint (set vs bolt-on) and serial number on headstock/neck plate (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Neck relief/truss rod issues, fret wear, Bigsby missing parts, electronics crackle, headstock cracks (major value hit), refinishes, missing original case. Ask for headstock back photo, neck joint, serial, and a playing video.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not a fake target. Confusion risks: Guild-made originals vs DeArmond reissues, and Korean vs Indonesian (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Thin on eBay (Terapeak shows zero M-75 sales in 12 months); Reverb is the real market. Buyers: players wanting affordable Guild-style guitars, Bigsby fans. Slow sellers – expect weeks to months (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Korea vs Indonesia; Bigsby T models; hardshell DeArmond case adds $60–80. Sellers often list as "starter guitar" or in a bundle with a practice amp.

## Shipping / handling
Guitar in hardshell ~15 lb, $50–75 each way (UPS). Fees 13% eBay / 8% Reverb. Shipping both ways eats ~$120, so needs a ≥ $300 spread. Loosen strings and pad the headstock.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Model, country (Korea/Indonesia), year/serial, finish, Bigsby, pickups, case, setup and playability, any flaws. Photograph headstock front and back, serial, body front/back, neck joint, Bigsby, case. Title: "DeArmond M-75T Korea Bigsby Guild design w/ case".

## Buy rule
Buy when: Korean M-75T/M-77T/X-155 ≤ $200, or Indonesian M-75 w/ case ≤ $120. Pass when: Indonesian over $150, missing case, bundles padded with amps. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (a guitar in hardshell does).
