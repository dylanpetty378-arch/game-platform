# Darkroom equipment (GraLab timers, enlargers, Metz/Vivitar flashes)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: knowledge of eBay sold ranges for GraLab 300/450, Beseler 23C/45, Omega D2, Metz 45CT/32CT flashes; no dated comp pulled

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
GraLab 300: grey/black box with a big luminous 60-second dial – the standard enlarger timer, still used, sells steadily. Enlargers: Beseler 23C (grey, bellows), Omega D2/D5 (black, 4x5), Durst. Lenses matter more than the enlarger (Schneider Componon-S, Rodenstock Rodagon 50/80/150 = $60–150 each). Metz Mecablitz 45CT/60CT "potato masher" handle flashes sell; the small 32CT3 hot-shoe units are $20–35. Expired photo paper (Ilford, Kodak, Agfa unopened) sells $15–40 a box to lith/alt-process people.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a darkroom lot with a Componon-S/Rodagon lens or a Metz 45/60 is under $40. Pass when: GraLab alone at $50+, 32CT3 at $60. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

## Additional research (earlier entry: Darkroom equipment (enlargers, cold-light heads, Zone VI, timers))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: photrio / largeformatphotography forum threads on Zone VI cold light heads; eBay sold pattern (knowledge 2024–25); craigslist 1020

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Zone VI Studios (Fred Picker, Vermont): grey boxes labelled "Zone VI" — Cold Light Stabilizer (compensating timer box with probe), Cold Light Head (for Beseler/Omega), Compensating Enlarging Timer, Zone VI print washers (acrylic slot washers), 4×5/8×10 field cameras (walnut/mahogany with gold hardware). Enlargers: Beseler 23C/45M, Omega D2/D5 — heavy, cheap. Lenses: Schneider Componon-S, Rodenstock Rodagon, El-Nikkor. Sellers write "photo enlarger", "darkroom stuff", "Zone VI".

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | Beseler 23C/Omega B22 enlargers, trays, tanks | $30–100 | local-pickup only, too heavy |
| Decent | Zone VI cold-light stabilizer, Beseler 45 timer, Gralab 300, 50 mm Componon-S | $80–150 | |
| Collectible | Zone VI cold light head (Aristo V54), Zone VI 16×20 print washer, 150 mm Componon-S/Rodagon, Jobo CPP-2 | $200–500 | the ones worth buying |
| Top | Zone VI 4×5/8×10 field cameras, Jobo CPP-3, Heiland splitgrade | $600–2,000 | verify working |

### Comps (sold, dated)
- 2025 – eBay – Zone VI Cold Light Stabilizer – $90–140 (knowledge)
- 2025 – eBay – Zone VI 11×14 print washer – $200–300 (knowledge)

### What sellers usually miss
"Zone VI" brand on a timer box; Aristo/Zone VI heads vs stock condenser heads; enlarging lens brand (sellers call them "camera lens"). Washers and film cameras are the money.

### Shipping / handling
Stabilizer 3 lb, $12 each way; washers 15–30 lb (oversize). eBay / photrio classifieds.

### Buy rule
Buy when: Zone VI washer or cold-light head ≤ $80; Zone VI field camera ≤ $200; stabilizer ≤ $30. Pass when: enlarger bodies (freight), untested heads. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
