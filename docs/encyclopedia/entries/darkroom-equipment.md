## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
GraLab 300: grey/black box with a big luminous 60-second dial – the standard enlarger timer, still used, sells steadily. Enlargers: Beseler 23C (grey, bellows), Beseler 45M/45MX, Omega D2/D5 (black, 4x5), Omega B22, Durst, Leitz Focomat. Lenses matter more than the enlarger (Schneider Componon-S, Rodenstock Rodagon, El-Nikkor; 50/80/150 mm = $60–150 each). Zone VI Studios (Fred Picker, Vermont): grey boxes labelled "Zone VI" — Cold Light Stabilizer (compensating timer box with probe), Cold Light Head (for Beseler/Omega), Compensating Enlarging Timer, Zone VI print washers (acrylic slot washers), 4×5/8×10 field cameras (walnut/mahogany with gold hardware). Metz Mecablitz 45CT/60CT "potato masher" handle flashes sell; the small 32CT3 hot-shoe units are $20–35. Expired photo paper (Ilford, Kodak, Agfa unopened) sells $15–40 a box to lith/alt-process people. Sellers write "photo enlarger", "darkroom stuff", "darkroom lot", "Zone VI".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Timers**: GraLab 300/450 (standard), Zone VI Compensating Enlarging Timer and Cold Light Stabilizer, Beseler 45 timer.
- **Enlargers**: Beseler 23C/45M/45MX, Omega D2/D5/B22, Durst, Leitz Focomat V35/IIc (top).
- **Heads**: Aristo/Zone VI cold-light heads (V54), dichroic colour heads, Heiland splitgrade (top).
- **Lenses**: Schneider Componon-S, Rodenstock Rodagon, El-Nikkor – 50 mm common, 150 mm worth more.
- **Processors / washers**: Jobo CPP-2/CPP-3, Zone VI print washers (11×14, 16×20).
- **Zone VI field cameras** 4×5/8×10 (top).
- **Flashes**: Metz 45CT/60CT handle flashes vs 32CT3 (low); Vivitar 283 (low here; see camera-speedlights entry, which values the 285HV at $40–80).
- **Consumables**: unopened Ilford/Kodak/Agfa paper.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Lens brand/focal length engraved on the front ring; check for haze, fungus, scratches. Zone VI gear is labelled and grey; Aristo heads have a cold-cathode grid lamp. Enlargers are cast aluminium/steel – heavy. Expired paper: unopened factory seal, storage history (cool/dry) matters.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Lens haze/fungus, stuck apertures; cold-light tubes dim or dead (untested heads are risky); timer dials stuck, cords frayed; washers cracked acrylic; enlarger columns bent, missing negative carriers/condensers. Ask for working test video for timers/heads and flashlight-through photos of lenses.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not a fake category. Risks are mis-identification (Metz 45CT vs 32CT; generic "camera lens" vs Componon-S) and missing parts (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Small but steady film revival market: buyers are film photographers, schools, alt-process printers; venues eBay, photrio classifieds, FB film groups. Lenses, Zone VI and Jobo move; enlarger bodies are nearly free locally (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Enlarger lenses (Componon/Rodagon) buried in "darkroom lot" listings (sellers call them "camera lens"); Metz 45CT vs 32CT confusion; "Zone VI" brand on a timer box; Aristo/Zone VI heads vs stock condenser heads. Washers and field cameras are the money.

## Shipping / handling
GraLab ~4 lb, $12; Zone VI stabilizer 3 lb, $12 each way; lenses <1 lb; washers 15–30 lb (oversize). Enlargers are local only.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Brand, model, focal length/aperture for lenses, tested working (timer runs, head lights), glass condition (haze/fungus/scratches), included parts (carriers, condensers, probes, cords). Photograph labels, lens front ring, glass with a light behind it, and the item working.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a darkroom lot with a Componon-S/Rodagon lens or a Metz 45/60 is under $40; Zone VI washer or cold-light head ≤ $80; Zone VI field camera ≤ $200; stabilizer ≤ $30. Pass when: GraLab alone at $50+, 32CT3 at $60, enlarger bodies (freight), untested heads. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat – full 4×5 enlargers (Omega D5, Beseler 45 with column) are "bigger" and excluded.
