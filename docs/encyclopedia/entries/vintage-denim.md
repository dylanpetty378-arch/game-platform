## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Denim jeans, jackets and work wear. Value comes almost entirely from age and US manufacture; visible tells:
- **Red tab**: "LEVI'S" with capital E = pre-1971 ("Big E"). Small "e" = 1971 onward.
- **Selvedge**: turn up a cuff; a clean white edge with a red line ("redline") on the outseam = pre-~1985 Levi's.
- **Back pocket arcuate**: single-needle chain stitch and hidden rivets (pre-1966) vs bar-tacks (post-1966).
- **Care tag** inside: "Made in USA", style and date code; 1990s tags often show the date (e.g., "0397").
- **Patch**: leather (pre-1955), cardboard/paper later; a "501 XX" patch with "Every Garment Guaranteed" = 1950s–60s.
- **Top-button back**: factory number stamped (e.g., "555" Valencia St, "524" El Paso). Single digit numbers on 1970s buttons; "16" or "6" often 1970s–80s.
- **Jackets**: Levi's type 1 (one pocket, cinch back, 506XX), type 2 (two pleated pockets, 507XX), type 3 (V-shaped seams, 557XX, 70505 with small e); Lee 101-J (zig-zag stitch, side pleats), Wrangler 11MJ/24MJZ.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Levi Strauss & Co.**: 501 (button fly), 505 (zip, 1967+), 517 bootcut, 646 bell bottoms (1970s), orange-tab lines (cheaper, 1960s–2000s). Production moved overseas 1990s–2003; US-made 501s end ~2003. LVC (Levi's Vintage Clothing) reproductions are premium modern, not old.
- **Lee** (H.D. Lee Mercantile, Kansas City): Lee Riders 101, 101-J jacket, "Union Made" tags 1940s–60s.
- **Wrangler** (Blue Bell): 11MWZ (1950s+), 13MWZ, 27MW; "W" stitch back pockets.
- **Sears** (Roebucks, Hercules, Western Wear labels), **JCPenney** (Foremost, Big Mac, Pay-Day), **Montgomery Ward** (Powr House): store brands; 1940s–60s pieces now collectible, 1970s–80s "Western Wear" jackets $25–50.
- **Big Yank, Blue Bell, Headlight, Stronghold, Can't Bust 'Em**: 1930s–50s work wear, very valuable.
- Designer/fashion labels (Gap, Guess, Calvin Klein, small labels) usually $15–40 unless 1980s Guess with triangle tag (modest demand).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Old denim: heavier, unsanforised, irregular "slubby" texture, honeycomb/whisker fading, chain-stitched hems (roping).
- Modern: lighter, uniform, stretch content (elastane), printed care labels with multiple languages, made in Mexico/Asia.
- Thread: orange/yellow cotton thread on old; poly-core on modern.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Crotch blowouts, holes, frayed hems, stains, missing tabs, broken zips. Collector buyers accept honest wear and even patches on Big E; ordinary 1990s jeans lose value with holes. Measure: tagged size is usually larger than actual after shrinkage and age.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Levi's Vintage Clothing (LVC) and Japanese repro brands (Warehouse, Studio D'Artisan) copy Big E details; they are legitimately valuable but must not be sold as vintage. LVC tags read "Levi's Vintage Clothing" and have modern care labels. Fake Big E tabs are sewn on to newer jeans: check stitching around the tab and the care label.

## Market
Very liquid on eBay, Depop, Grailed and Etsy. Common USA-made 501s sell in 1–3 weeks; 1970s and Big E pieces sell in days. Sizes 30–36 waist sell fastest; very large waists (40+) sell slower and cheaper.

## What sellers usually miss
- Redline selvedge and capital-E tabs are only visible if you ask for close-ups; owners often list them as "old Levi's $20".
- 1950s–70s store-brand jackets (Sears Hercules, Penney's) with blanket lining or sanforised tags are wanted by collectors.

## Shipping / handling
Light (1–2 lb), ships $6–10 in a poly mailer. No fragility. Ideal shipped category.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Give actual flat measurements (waist, rise, inseam, leg opening), tag size, era tells (tab, selvedge, care tag date, button number), country of manufacture, and every flaw photographed.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − ~$8 shipping to buyer − ~$8 to Dylan − price ≥ max($50, price). Common USA-made 501s ($50 sold) never qualify. Buy when photos or seller answers show a capital-E tab, redline selvedge, single-stitch pockets or 1960s–70s labels, price ≤ $40 (resale must be ≥ $110). Ask the seller for tab, cuff-turned and care-tag photos before buying anything that looks old. Pass on paper-patch 1990s–2000s 501s, women's and stretch.
