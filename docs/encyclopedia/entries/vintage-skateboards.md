# Collectible skateboard decks (Powell Peralta, Santa Cruz, Vision)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay/Reverb-style browse Oct 2025 (Powell Peralta Ripper reissue, Santa Cruz Everslick, 1980s originals); sweep listings 2360, 2036

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Shrink-wrapped new-old-stock decks or used 1980s "Bones Brigade" shapes. Powell Peralta reissues (2005–present) are dated under the logo (e.g. "© 2010") and have the winged-skull/P-P logos; originals (1978–91) have a "Powell Peralta" stamp and are pig-shaped with a short nose. Santa Cruz Everslick = slick plastic bottom, 1990s; TMNT collaborations are 2010s. Sellers say "rare", "limited edition", "vintage".

## What sellers usually miss
Reissue vs original (the © date under the graphic). Most "rare limited edition" CL listings are reissues.

3–4 lb, needs a 33×10×4 box, $18–25. eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: an original 1980s Powell/Santa Cruz/Vision ≤ $100, or NOS reissue ≤ $40. Pass when: reissue priced ≥ $60, TMNT Everslick ≥ $40. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).

## Additional research (earlier entry: Vintage skateboard decks (1980s Vision, Powell-Peralta, Santa Cruz))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge 2024–25 (Vision Double Vision / Gator originals $150–400; Powell Ripper/Skull & Sword originals $300–1,000; 2000s–2020s reissues $60–120); Art of Skateboarding price references.

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1980s shapes: wide (10"), fish tail, concave with a nose kick, rails/tail bone screw holes, "pig" shape. Originals: 7-ply maple with thin (not glossy) screen print, wear through the graphic, old-style four-hole truck pattern, stamp on top ("Made in USA", Vision). Reissues (Powell Bones Brigade series, Santa Cruz reissues, Vision reissues 2000s+): glossy, crisp print, "Reissue" or modern bar code on the deck. Sellers write "OG", "vintage deck", "rare", "NOS".

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | Reissues, Nash/Variflex department-store boards | $30–100 | |
| Decent | 1980s Vision Double Vision, Psycho Stick, Sims, Schmitt Stix rider decks | $120–300 | |
| Collectible | Powell-Peralta Hawk/Caballero/McGill originals, Santa Cruz Roskopp, Alva | $300–800 | |
| Top | NOS 1980s Powell/Santa Cruz in shrink, Dogtown originals, Z-Flex 1970s | $1,000–5,000+ | |

### What sellers usually miss
Original vs reissue; seller here cited eBay comps, so they know. Complete setups with Independent/Tracker trucks and era wheels add $50–100.

### Shipping / handling
Deck 4 lb, $20–25 to ship (long box). Resell on eBay or Facebook vintage-skate groups.

### Buy rule
Buy when: confirmed 1980s original Vision/Powell/Santa Cruz ≤ $60. Pass when: reissue, or seller quotes eBay. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.

## Additional research (earlier entry: Vintage skateboards (1970s fibreglass / early urethane era))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: surfertoday.com and concretewaves.com Fibreflex history; eBay listings for G&S Fibreflex Bowlrider/Teamrider decks (asks $250–600, 2025); run12 sheet 58

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Gordon & Smith Fibreflex (1964–80s): laminated maple/fibreglass deck, reddish-brown epoxy top with a shield-shaped "Fibreflex skateboard by G&S" decal; models Bowlrider, Teamrider, Pipeline (kicktail), Cutaway. Period hardware adds a lot: Tracker Fultrack/Midtrack trucks (cast aluminium, "Tracker" on the baseplate), Kryptonics (clear amber/translucent 65–70 mm wheels with rainbow lettering), Road Rider, Sims Snakes, Powell Bones. Other 1970s names: Logan Earth Ski, Bahne, Sims Taperkick, Z-Flex, Alva. 1980s Powell Peralta/Santa Cruz/Vision decks are a separate market (originals $300–2,000 for Hawk/Caballero/Ripper first runs; reissues $60–120).

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | Nash, Roller Derby, Free Former plastic boards | $20–40 | |
| Decent | Fibreflex deck only, worn; 70s no-name with Trackers | $100–200 | |
| Collectible | complete Fibreflex with Trackers + Kryptonics, original | $300–600 | |
| Top | Fibreflex Bowlrider mint, Sims Taperkick, Alva signature, 1st-run Powell Hawk | $600–2,000 | |

### Comps (sold, dated)
- 2025 – G&S Fibreflex Bowlrider red, complete – eBay asks $350–550 (sold band ~$300–450, agent knowledge)
- 2025 – Fibreflex Teamrider caramel deck only – ask ~$250
- 2025 – Kryptonics 70 mm wheel set alone – $80–150; Tracker Fultrack pair – $60–120

### What sellers usually miss
Wheel brand (Kryptonics clear amber) and truck brand (Tracker) – a "vintage 70s skateboard" with those is a $300+ board. Fibreflex decal is usually named.

### Shipping / handling
~5 lb, 30" long – $20–30 each way. Sell on eBay; 13% fees.

### Buy rule
Buy when: complete Fibreflex/Tracker/Kryptonics under $120, or any Kryptonics-wheeled 70s board under $60. Pass when: $200 complete (7033 math: $450 × 0.87 − $200 − $25 − $25 ≈ $140, below the price-floor rule). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

## Additional research (earlier entry: Vintage skateboards (1980s decks & reissues))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: skateone.com reissue pricing, eBay/Reverb sold (agent knowledge Oct 2026)

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1980s Powell Peralta (Caballero dragon, Hawk skull, McGill, Mountain), Santa Cruz (Roskopp, Kendall), Vision (Gator, Psycho Stick), Alva, Sims, Dogtown decks. **Originals**: 7-ply maple, screened graphic with 1980s copyright line, "Made in USA" by Taylor Dykema/NHS stamp, wear consistent with age. **Reissues** (Powell started ~1999, continuous since): the word "reissue" or a modern Skate One/Bones Brigade series stamp, crisper graphics, modern wheelbase; retail $70–90 new, used $50–120 complete.

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | Reissue completes, 1990s shop decks, Nash/Variflex toy boards | $40–150 | most Craigslist "vintage skateboard" |
| Decent | 1980s rider decks (Variflex pro models), used Independent 169 trucks ($40–80), 1980s Bones wheels | $100–300 | |
| Collectible | Original 1980s Powell Caballero/Hawk/McGill, Santa Cruz Roskopp, Vision Gator, complete w/ Indy + Bones | $400–1,200 | |
| Top | NOS 80s decks in shrink, Dogtown/Alva 70s, Z-Flex, Powell "Bones Brigade" first runs, Hawk Chicken Skull NOS | $1,500–10,000 | |

### What sellers usually miss
Whether it is a reissue – ask for the bottom graphic edge/copyright. 1980s Independent trucks and Bones/OJ wheels on a junk deck are worth more than the deck.

### Shipping / handling
Complete 8–10 lb, $25–35 long box.

### Buy rule
Buy when: original 1980s pro deck (no "reissue", period copyright) at ≤ $150, or a complete with Indy/Tracker/Gullwing + Bones at ≤ $100. Pass when: seller says reissue, or price ≥ $150 on a 2000s build. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
