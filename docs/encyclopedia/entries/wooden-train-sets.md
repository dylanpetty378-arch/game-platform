## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Wooden track-compatible trains. Learning Curve Thomas (1992–2012) — wooden bodies with round magnets, painted faces; later Fisher-Price (2013–) changed to different face style and some plastic parts. BRIO (Sweden; "BRIO" stamped on wheels/underside); KidKraft, Melissa & Doug, IKEA, Imaginarium are low value.

## Makers, brands and variants
Learning Curve / RC2 (Thomas Wooden Railway), Fisher-Price Thomas Wood (2017–), BRIO, KidKraft, Melissa & Doug, Imaginarium, Chuggington wooden.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Solid wood bodies, metal axles; dates moulded "©Gullane", "©HIT" on bottom help date Thomas pieces.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Paint chips (big value hit on faces), chewing, missing magnets.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Chinese knock-offs ("Thomas" wooden fakes), re-releases of popular engines.
