## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Cardboard retail box of 36 wax packs, lid flaps often printed with the set logo. Years 1987–1993 (Topps, Donruss, Fleer, Score, Upper Deck) were overproduced – "junk wax". Value is in a handful of years/brands: 1986 Fleer basketball (Michael Jordan rookie, $20k+), 1989 Upper Deck baseball (Griffey, $300–500), 1990 Leaf, 1984 Fleer Update, 1980 Topps. Sellers write "wax box", "sealed packs", "unopened". Look-alikes: rack/cello boxes and vending boxes (different pack counts and values), and repacked/resealed boxes (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Topps** – 1980 (collectible), 1986–87 (decent), 1988–91 (junk).
- **Donruss** – 1986 (collectible, Canseco rookie), 1987 (decent, ~$70 sealed), 1988–91 (junk).
- **Fleer** – 1984 Fleer Update (factory set, collectible), 1986–87 Fleer basketball (Jordan rookie – top), 1988–91 baseball (junk).
- **Upper Deck** – 1989 low series (Griffey rookie) $300–500.
- **Score, Leaf** – 1990 Leaf (Frank Thomas) collectible; Score junk; 1990 Score football decent.
- **Box types**: wax (36 packs), rack, cello, vending, factory sets – values differ (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original wrap (factory cellophane on some years; many wax boxes were never shrink-wrapped – an un-wrapped box is not automatically resealed). BBCE (Baseball Card Exchange) authentication tape/wrap adds $30–50. Tells of tampering: loose or re-glued flaps, packs with uneven wax seals, wrong pack count (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Crushed corners, water damage, sun fading, torn wrap. Box condition matters less than seal integrity unless it is a high-end box. Any sign of opening/resealing kills value (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Resealed boxes and searched/repacked packs are the main risk for valuable years (1986 Fleer basketball, 1989 UD, 1980s Topps). Only buy high-end boxes with BBCE wrap (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Large, liquid hobby market on eBay; prices for junk wax swing with hobby hype cycles (spiked 2020–21, settled since) (general knowledge, verify). Buyers are rippers/breakers and nostalgia collectors.

## What sellers usually miss
Nothing to miss – sellers over-value junk wax from AI/Gemini quotes. The real edge is unauthenticated boxes under $40 that BBCE will wrap.

## Shipping / handling
Box ~1 lb, $8–10 to ship. Resell on eBay; fees 13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State year, brand, sport, box type (wax/rack/cello), pack count, sealed status and any BBCE wrap. Photograph all six sides, flaps, and the seal/wrap. Keywords: year + brand + "wax box" + "factory sealed" + "36 packs" + "BBCE" if applicable.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 1986–87 Donruss/Topps ≤ $30/box, 1989 Upper Deck ≤ $100. Pass when: 1988–92 anything over $20, or priced off an AI quote. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Must fit a passenger seat (it does).
