# Sealed baseball-card wax boxes (1980s–90s "junk wax")
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay 156943607183 (1987 Donruss sealed wax box, $69.99, 2 sold, Oct 2025); eBay 266407483854 (BBCE-authenticated 1987 Donruss boxes); hobby knowledge of BBCE premiums.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Cardboard retail box of 36 wax packs, lid flaps often printed with the set logo. Years 1987–1993 (Topps, Donruss, Fleer, Score, Upper Deck) were overproduced – "junk wax". Value is in a handful of years/brands: 1986 Fleer basketball (the partner rookie, $20k+), 1989 Upper Deck baseball (Griffey, $300–500), 1990 Leaf, 1984 Fleer Update, 1980 Topps. Sellers write "wax box", "sealed packs", "unopened".

## What sellers usually miss
Nothing to miss – sellers over-value junk wax from AI/Gemini quotes. The real edge is unauthenticated boxes under $40 that BBCE will wrap.

## Shipping / handling
Box ~1 lb, $8–10 to ship. Resell on eBay; fees 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 1986–87 Donruss/Topps ≤ $30/box, 1989 Upper Deck ≤ $100. Pass when: 1988–92 anything over $20, or priced off an AI quote. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
