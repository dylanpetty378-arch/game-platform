Buy rule (short): buy only canvas/linen-backed pull-down school charts or pre-1950 lithographs at ≤ $60; paper posters and 1970s shiatsu charts are a pass.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Teaching charts. Value tells: cloth (linen/canvas) backing, wooden or metal roller and header bar, publisher name (Denoyer-Geppert Chicago, A.J. Nystrom, Jung-Koch-Quentell, Hagemann), chromolithograph colour, series number. Paper posters (offset printed, glossy, copyright 1970s–2000s) are low value. The Iokai Shiatsu Center (Tokyo) "Shiatsu Meridian Chart / 経絡指圧診断治療要図" by Shizuto Masunaga ©1970 was sold to students for decades, so many survive.

## Makers, brands and variants
Denoyer-Geppert (1916–), A.J. Nystrom, Jung-Koch-Quentell (German), Hagemann; Iokai Shiatsu Center; Chinese acupuncture charts (Foreign Languages Press, 1970s); pharma company posters (Ciba Netter).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Cloth-backed > laminated board > paper. Lithographed (dot-free colour) older; halftone dots = offset.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Tears, flaking paint on cloth charts, water damage, broken rollers.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Many reprint posters of vintage anatomical art; check for halftone dots and modern paper.
