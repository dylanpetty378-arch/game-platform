# California pottery ringware (Bauer, Pacific, Garden City)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: maximalist.org Bauer piece list (1941 catalog sizes), eBay listings via search (ringware #9/#18 bowls listed $45–120), knowledge of sold ranges

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1930s–40s Los Angeles solid-colour kitchenware. Bauer "Ringware" mixing bowls: heavy, flared, with raised concentric rings on the outside (and three inside rings on early ones). Colours: orange-red, jade green, cobalt, yellow, ivory, black (black and burgundy are the scarce ones). Nesting mixing bowl numbers: #9 (1 gal, 10–11"), #12 (9"), #18 (8"), #24 (7"), #30 (6"), #36 (5"). Mark on bottom is often just an impressed number or "Bauer Los Angeles"; many are unmarked, which is why sellers just write "California pottery" or "orange bowl". Pacific Hostessware and Garden City look similar; Pacific is marked "Pacific" in a circle, Garden City is usually unmarked with sharper rings.

## What sellers usually miss
The word "Bauer" (and the size number). Listings titled "California pottery bowl", "orange mixing bowl", "Fiesta-style bowl" are the ones to open. Chips on the rim knock 50% off; crazing is tolerated.

## Shipping / handling
A #9 bowl is ~5 lb; double-box, $18–25 to ship. Sell on eBay (13%) or Etsy. Breakage is the main risk.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a #9/#12 ringware bowl in a good colour is under $25, or two or more bowls for under $40. Pass when: chipped, or already titled "Bauer" at $50+. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
