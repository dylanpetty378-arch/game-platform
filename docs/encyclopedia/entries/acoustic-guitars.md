## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Steel-string guitar. Value tells: headstock logo and shape (Martin square with decal; Gibson open-book; Guild "chesterfield"), label through the sound hole, solid top (grain runs uninterrupted through sound-hole edge), binding, inlay quality.
Esteban (Home Shopping Network guitars by Esteban/Stephen Paul) American Legacy series: many limited "Fireworks", "Starlight", "Midnight Steel" editions, abalone rosette, Martin-like D-45 styling, preamp. The example is an American Legacy AL-100 natural-finish dreadnought with abalone purfling and black pickguard.

## Makers, brands and variants
- US: Martin (D-18, D-28, D-35, 000), Gibson (J-45, Hummingbird), Guild (D-25, D-40), Taylor.
- Japan 1970s: Yamaha FG red/black labels (Nippon Gakki), Takamine F-series "lawsuit", Aria, Ibanez Concord, K. Yairi, Morris, Alvarez Yairi.
- Budget: Esteban, Rogue, First Act, Johnson, Mitchell.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Solid spruce top vs laminate; solid rosewood sides/back on high end; bone nut/saddle; neck angle and action (straight-edge test). Abalone trim on Esteban is cosmetic.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Top cracks, bridge lifting, neck reset need, fret wear, broken pickup preamp. Electronics must be tested.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Chinese counterfeit Martins/Gibsons ("Chibson") exist; check serials and construction.
