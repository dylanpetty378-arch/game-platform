## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Pitching shoes: ~7 in. long, 2.5 lb, cast or forged, with "calks" (toe points) and a brand cast on the face ("RINGER", "DIAMOND", "GIANT GRIP", "OHIO"); often letters "A/B" stamped to tell pairs apart. Farrier shoes: nail holes, worn, uneven.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Diamond Calk Horseshoe Co. (Duluth MN, 1908–)**: Diamond Super Ringer, Eagle Ringer, Official.
- **Ohio Horseshoe Co.**, **Giant Grip (Oshkosh WI)**, **St. Pierre (Worcester MA, since 1920s)**, **Gordon Spin-On**, **Lattore**, **Allen**, **Royal**.
- **"Ringer"** brand: several makers used "Ringer"/"Double Ringer" — Diamond is most common.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Drop-forged steel (crisp edges, heavier) vs cast iron (cheaper, may chip). Paint remnants indicate original colour codes.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rust (acceptable to rustic buyers), chipped calks, mismatched sets.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Cast-iron decorative "antique" horseshoes from China for crafts.
