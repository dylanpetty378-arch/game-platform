## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1950s lamps placed on or near the TV to soften glare: ceramic or chalkware figure on a base with a small bulb behind a pierced back panel, or a fiberglass "spaghetti" cone shade. Look underneath for an incised or stamped mark and a cork or felt bottom with the socket.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Kron (Texans Inc., Texas)**: elongated glossy black panthers, stylized; marked "Kron" incised.
- **Royal Haeger / Haeger**: gold-crackle, earth-wrap glazes, panthers and gazelles; paper labels or incised "Royal Haeger USA".
- **Lane & Co. (Van Nuys CA)**: horses, sea horses, deer; "Lane & Co. ©" incised.
- **Maddux of California**: deer, flamingos, horses; marked.
- **Claes Originals**, **Premco**, **Moss**, **Royal Fleet**: various.
- **Metro Ware / unmarked chalkware**: painted plaster figures (Asian couples, cowboys, ladies) with fiberglass cones — lowest tier.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Glazed ceramic (shiny, cold, heavy) beats chalkware (plaster, chalky white chips, flaking paint, light). Fiberglass cone shades in red/white/turquoise; brittle and often cracked. Original cord with cloth or early plastic, bakelite switch.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chalkware chips and paint loss (very common), cracked/yellowed fiberglass shades, glued-on figures, rewiring needed (frayed cords), broken ears/tails on ceramics.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Some modern ceramic reproductions of panthers; Chinese "MCM-style" lamps. Fiberglass shades reproduced.

## Market
Steady MCM decor demand; shipping fragility depresses eBay prices; local/Etsy sometimes better. 2–6 weeks.

## What sellers usually miss
Incised maker marks (Kron, Lane) underneath; often sold as "old lamp".

## Shipping / handling
Fragile (ceramic + fiberglass), 4–8 lb, needs double boxing; $20–35 to ship. High breakage risk.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Photos lit and unlit, all chips, marks, cord condition; say "tested working" only after testing; recommend rewiring.

## Buy rule
Profit = median × 0.87 − ship − ship − price. Chalkware median ~$70 → $61 − $50 shipping − price = loss. Pass. Kron/Haeger/Lane ceramic with intact shade and median ≥ $200: buy ≤ $40. Must be tested working (lamp is simple, but no repairs).
