## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Weltron 2001 (1970): white/cream ABS sphere ~13" with a black face (AM/FM dial, 8-track slot), ring base. Also 2005
(sphere with record player, rarer, $1,000+), 2007 (helmet-shape TV+radio). Colours: white is the common one; yellow,
orange, red, blue sell 1.5–2×. Tells: "Weltron" script on face, 8-track slot under the dial. Look-alikes: JVC Videosphere
(TV, helmet), Panasonic Toot-a-Loop and other space-age radios; generic "space ball" speakers (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Weltron 2001** (1970): AM/FM/8-track; white common, colours 1.5–2×.
- **Weltron 2005**: sphere with record player — rarer, $1,000+.
- **Weltron 2007**: helmet-shape TV + radio.
- Matching Weltron sphere speakers on chrome stands exist and add value when present (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
ABS plastic shell that yellows with UV — bright, even colour is the quality tell; cream-yellowed whites sell at the low
end. Original ring base, chrome trim and antenna should be present. Battery door at the rear/bottom — check for
corrosion.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Untested/dead, cracked shells, yellowing, battery-compartment corrosion, dead 8-track (belts perish — common and
fixable but Dylan doesn't repair), scratchy controls, missing antenna or base. Ask for a video of radio and 8-track
playing, and photos of the battery compartment and base.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No known Weltron reproductions; modern "retro sphere" Bluetooth speakers are not Weltrons (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Mid-century/space-age decor buyers and retro audio collectors; eBay, Etsy, 1stdibs/Chairish for top asks, and local
mid-century shops. Sells steadily; "still quite common" per historictech, so whites are not rare.

## What sellers usually miss
They rarely miss it ("space ball" is in every listing). Margin only on dead-cheap or coloured units.

## Shipping / handling
~10 lb, 16" cube, fragile ABS – $35–50 each way, double box.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Weltron 2001" (or 2005/2007) + colour + "space ball" + "8-track AM/FM" + "working" + "space age"; Junk: "retro radio", "sphere", "untested" if avoidable, "Bluetooth" (not a Weltron). Example: `Weltron 2001 Space Ball AM/FM 8-Track Radio Orange Working 1970s Space Age`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (space-age decor and retro audio buyers); 1stdibs/Chairish only for coloured or 2005/2007 asks; local mid-century shops take whites. eBay is the market; 1stdibs asks ($900) overstate.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (working white $250–350; working coloured $350–600; dead/cracked $80–150), accept −15%, floor p25; OBO; cut 10% after 3 weeks (whites are common).
- **Photos:** first photo the sphere face-on, lid open; then every side, base and stand, battery compartment (corrosion), 8-track slot, dial and controls, yellowing against a white card, any cracks; video of AM, FM and 8-track playing.
- **State:** model, colour, AM / FM / 8-track working status stated separately, yellowing, cracks, battery-compartment condition, base and antenna present, scratchy controls, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** ~10 lb, 16" cube; double-box, remove the base, immobilize the lid, 2" fill all round; $35–50. Carry-on size for local.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Weltron 2001 in [orange] — the coloured ones are 1.5–2× the white — AM, FM and 8-track all play (video), [no cracks, light yellowing]; working coloured units sell [$350–600]."
