# Weltron space-ball radios / 8-tracks
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: historictech.com (2001 retailed $160 in 1970; "still quite common"); 1stdibs Weltron creator page (asks $800–1,500); antiqueradio.org welt01; run12 sheet 56

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Weltron 2001 (1970): white/cream ABS sphere ~13" with a black face (AM/FM dial, 8-track slot), ring base. Also 2005 (sphere with record player, rarer, $1,000+), 2007 (helmet-shape TV+radio). Colours: white is the common one; yellow, orange, red, blue sell 1.5–2×. Tells: "Weltron" script on face, 8-track slot under the dial.
