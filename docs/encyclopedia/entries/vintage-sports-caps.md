## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Baseball caps (snapbacks, fitted, trucker) from the 1970s–90s team-licence boom, now heavily traded among streetwear collectors.
- **Sports Specialties (LA, 1950s–1993; Nike to ~1997)**: wool/acrylic, green undervisor, big embroidered script team names ("Script"), "Grid", "Shadow", "Wave", "Laser"; inside: "Sports Specialties" woven tag and "Pro" sweatband; snap often embossed. Bulls, Raiders, Lakers, Kings most valuable.
- **Starter (1980s–1999)**: "Starter" logo on back strap/snap, tri-power, arch, splash designs; tags "Made in Korea/Taiwan".
- **AJD**, **Annco**, **Logo 7**, **American Needle** (Blockhead), **Drew Pearson**, **Competitor**, **Twins Enterprise**: 1980s–90s.
- **Mitchell & Ness (2000s–)**: reissues of Sports Specialties designs ("Team Script", "Script 2.0") with green undervisor; M&N woven tag and "Hardwood Classics"/"Mitchell & Ness" logo on side or back.
- **Racing caps**: official Ferrari/Marlboro/Agip team caps (1980s–90s) carry sponsor patches and licence tags; "Scuderia Ferrari" souvenir caps with printed shield are low value; Michael Schumacher era Marlboro-sponsor caps sell to collectors.

## Makers, brands and variants
- Sports Specialties (Dave Warsaw): top vintage tier.
- Starter, AJD, Annco, American Needle, Logo 7, Nutmeg Mills (shirts), Twins Enterprise.
- New Era: vintage pre-2000 "Pro Model" with USA-made tags vs modern 59FIFTY.
- Reissues: Mitchell & Ness, '47 Brand, New Era Cooperstown.
- Racing: Ferrari (official merch), Marlboro team kit caps (Ferrari, McLaren), Goodyear, STP, NASCAR Chase Authentics (2000s, low).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Tags: country and brand tags inside (Korea/Taiwan 1980s–90s vs China/Bangladesh later); copyright years on tags.
- Embroidery: vintage SS script uses dense satin stitch with slight puff; reissues are flatter and cleaner.
- Snapback: older two-row snaps, sometimes embossed brand; modern snapbacks have brand-embossed straps.
- Sweatband: vintage foam-backed sweatbands degrade (crumbling foam = vintage tell, but condition hit).
- Brim: flat undervisor green on both vintage SS and modern reissues, so not diagnostic alone.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Sweat stains, sun fading, crushed or cracked brim, crumbling foam, missing snaps, washing shrinkage, odour. Condition cuts value 50–80%.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Mitchell & Ness reissues closely copy Sports Specialties Bulls/Lakers/Raiders scripts; counterfeit modern "vintage" caps from China with fake SS tags; bootleg Ferrari caps with printed shields; fake "deadstock" with reproduced tags.

## Market
Strong streetwear demand for 1980s–90s caps (Grailed, eBay, Depop); fast sales for authentic SS and Starter. Reissues flood listings titled "vintage".

## What sellers usually miss
- Sports Specialties tags inside caps bundled with modern hats.
- Rare teams (Seattle SuperSonics, Expos, Whalers, North Stars) and 1990s championship caps.
- Most "vintage" Bulls scripts on Craigslist are M&N reissues priced as vintage.

## Shipping / handling
Light (< 0.5 lb) but must not be crushed; small box $8–12 each way.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "vintage 90s" + team + brand (Sports Specialties, Starter) + style (Script, Grid, Shadow, tri-power) + "snapback" + "wool" + "Korea"/"USA" tag + "deadstock" if tagged. Junk: "retro", "throwback", "vintage" on Mitchell & Ness. Example: `Vintage 90s Sports Specialties Chicago Bulls Script Snapback Wool Korea Tag Black`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers and Grailed at the same price (streetwear buyers; authentic SS/Starter sells fast); Depop for worn decent-tier; nothing beats these.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median (worn Starter/AJD $20–60, SS Bulls script $80–200, Raiders/Kings scripts higher, M&N reissues ~$20); accept −10%, floor −20%; OBO; cut 10% after 21 days; deadstock firm.
- **Photos:** 1) front straight-on on a plain background; then inside tags (brand, size, country), sweatband, snap closure, undervisor colour, embroidery close-up, each side panel, any stains/fade/crushing.
- **State:** brand and era from the tag, team, style name, material, fit (snapback/fitted size), undervisor colour, tag country, condition (sweatband stains, fade, crushed crown) by photo number, "original not reissue" with the tag as evidence.
- **Pack / haul:** under 0.5 lb but must not be crushed; stuff the crown with paper, small box (8×8×6), 1-lb class, $8–12.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "An original [Sports Specialties Bulls script] with the [Korea] tag in photo [2], not the Mitchell & Ness reissue that sells for $20 — original SS scripts sell for [$X]; green undervisor and sweatband clean."

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − ~$10 to buyer − ~$10 to Dylan − price; pass unless ≥ max($50, price).
- Inside tag photo required before any buy.
- Sports Specialties/Starter/AJD in good shape: buy at ≤ $35 if resale ≥ $110; deadstock SS Bulls/Raiders at ≤ $70 if resale ≥ $200.
- Reissues (M&N, '47, New Era current) and souvenir racing caps: pass.
