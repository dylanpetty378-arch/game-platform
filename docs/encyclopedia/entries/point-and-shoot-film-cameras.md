## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Fully automatic 35mm compacts from 1977 (Konica C35 AF, first AF) through the 2000s. The money is in fixed-wide-prime models (28-38mm, f/2.8–3.5) with good optics: look for the lens marking (e.g., "Carl Zeiss Tessar T* 3.5/35" on Yashica T4/T5, "Carl Zeiss Sonnar T* 2.8/38" on Contax T2, "35mm 1:2.8" on Olympus mju II, "GR LENS 28mm 1:2.8" on Ricoh GR1), slim pebble shapes, titanium or magnesium bodies. (Correction: the old entry gave the T4 lens as 35mm f/2.8; it is a Zeiss Tessar 35mm **f/3.5**.)

## Makers, brands and variants
Olympus (XA, Stylus/mju, mju II "Epic"), Yashica (Auto Focus 1978, T AF, T2-T5 Zeiss), Contax T2/T3/TVS, Ricoh GR1/GR1s/GR1v, Nikon L35AF, 28Ti/35Ti, Konica Big Mini, Minolta TC-1, Canon AF35M/Sure Shot (WP-1 ~$150), Pentax Espio, Leica Mini/Minilux.
- **Yashica/Kyocera T-series**: T4 (base), T4D/Kyocera Slim T (date back), T4 Super = T5 = Kyocera T Proof (adds a waist-level "Super Scope" finder on top and is weather-sealed), T4 Super D (date back). All Zeiss Tessar 35mm f/3.5; DX to ISO 3200 (Blue Moon). The film-advance motor can fail without warning (PhotographyTalk).
- **Olympus**: mju I (~$100) vs **mju II / Stylus Epic** (~$250; 35mm f/2.8 fixed, weather resistant). Many zoom mju models (mju Zoom, Stylus Zoom) are worth far less; read the lens bezel.
- **Contax T2** (~$1,000, ~$2,000 serviced; Sonnar 38mm f/2.8, titanium; service $400–500 because parts are scarce), **T3** (higher).
- **Minolta TC-1** (~$925; G-Rokkor 28mm f/3.5, tiny titanium body, low production).
- **Ricoh GR1/GR1s/GR1v** (~$800 for GR1v; GR 28mm f/2.8, magnesium; the LCD data panel often fails with age and lowers resale).
- **Nikon 35Ti / 28Ti** (~$800; analog needle dials on top, titanium; 35mm f/2.8 Nikkor).
- **Nikon L35AF** (~$200), **Konica Big Mini** (~$200).

### Quick ID checklist
- Read the lens ring: fixed focal length (single number, e.g. "35mm") vs zoom ("38–105mm"). Zoom = low tier.
- Lens brand: Carl Zeiss (Yashica/Contax), Nikkor, GR, G-Rokkor, Leica Summar/Elmar → upper tiers.
- Model name on the front or top plate: "T4", "T5", "mju II"/"Stylus Epic" (not just "mju"/"Stylus"), "Big Mini", "GR1", "35Ti", "TC-1".
- Body: titanium (Contax T2, Nikon Ti, TC-1) or magnesium (GR1) vs plastic.
- Top of a Yashica: a small waist-level scope = T4 Super / T5.
- Back: data back (date imprint) present? LCD readable?
- Battery door: corrosion visible? Battery type (CR123A, CR2, 2CR5, AA).
- Ask: does the lens extend, flash fire, film advance? Any error blink?

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Fixed prime lens (no zoom ring), Zeiss/Leica/Nikkor branding = high value. Zoom compacts (38-140mm) are low value. Titanium or magnesium bodies and analog dials mark the premium class. Original case, strap and box add modestly; a recent service with warranty adds a lot (Contax T2 roughly doubles serviced).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dead electronics, corroded battery compartments, stuck lenses (error codes), flash failures, hazy lenses, sticky shutter. Very common in Craigslist cameras. Model-specific: Yashica T4 film-advance motor failure; Ricoh GR1 LCD panel failure; Olympus mju II deteriorating light seals around the lens and failing electronics; general slowing AF motors and battery drain on 25–35-year-old cameras; Contax T2 repairs $400–500 (PhotographyTalk).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not faked, but listings misidentify models (T4 vs T4 Super, mju vs mju II). Kyocera Slim T / T Proof are legitimate twins of the T4 / T4 Super, not fakes.

## Market
Very hot since 2018 (film revival, TikTok); premium models sell within days. Untested sells at 30-50% discount. Buyers prefer serviced bodies with warranties over raw listings (PhotographyTalk).

## What sellers usually miss
Fixed-prime compacts mixed in "old camera" lots; sellers price by size not model. Kyocera-badged T4/T5 twins listed without the Yashica name.

## Shipping / handling
Small, 1 lb, $8-12.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Model, tested functions (shutter, flash, AF, film advance), battery type, lens clarity.

### What to tell the buyer
"[Make model] 35mm point-and-shoot, fixed [focal length] f/[x] [lens brand] lens. Tested with a fresh [battery type]: powers on, lens extends, shutter fires, flash fires, film advances and rewinds [with a test roll/without film]. Lens clear, no haze or fungus [flashlight-checked]. Battery compartment clean. [LCD fully readable]. Light seals [fine/replaced]. Not serviced / serviced [date]." Photograph lens markings, the model name, the battery compartment and the LCD.

## Buy rule
Untested electronics rule: buy only with seller-confirmed operation, or at a price that makes "for parts" resale still clear $50 (only premium models).
