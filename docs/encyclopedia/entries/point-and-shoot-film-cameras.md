## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Fully automatic 35mm compacts from 1977 (Konica C35 AF, first AF) through the 2000s. The money is in fixed-wide-prime models (28-35mm, f/2.8 or faster) with good optics: look for the lens marking (e.g., "Carl Zeiss Tessar T* 35mm 2.8" on Yashica T4, "35mm 1:2.8" on Olympus mju II), slim pebble shapes, titanium bodies.

## Makers, brands and variants
Olympus (XA, Stylus/mju, mju II "Epic"), Yashica (Auto Focus 1978, T AF, T2-T5 Zeiss), Contax T2/T3/TVS, Ricoh GR1/GR1v, Nikon L35AF, 28Ti/35Ti, Konica Big Mini, Minolta TC-1, Canon AF35M/Sure Shot, Pentax Espio, Leica Mini/Minilux.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Fixed prime lens (no zoom ring), Zeiss/Leica/Nikkor branding = high value. Zoom compacts (38-140mm) are low value.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dead electronics, corroded battery compartments, stuck lenses (error codes), flash failures, hazy lenses, sticky shutter. Very common in Craigslist cameras.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not faked, but listings misidentify models (T4 vs T4 Super, mju vs mju II).

## Market
Very hot since 2018 (film revival, TikTok); premium models sell within days. Untested sells at 30-50% discount.

## What sellers usually miss
Fixed-prime compacts mixed in "old camera" lots; sellers price by size not model.

## Shipping / handling
Small, 1 lb, $8-12.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Model, tested functions (shutter, flash, AF, film advance), battery type, lens clarity.

## Buy rule
Untested electronics rule: buy only with seller-confirmed operation, or at a price that makes "for parts" resale still clear $50 (only premium models).
