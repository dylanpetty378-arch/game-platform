Buy rule (short): Only buy branded/graphic (oil company logo) cans ≤ $30; pass on plain red safety cans.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Metal containers for gasoline/kerosene. Safety cans have spring-loaded self-closing lids and flame arrestors (Justrite, Eagle, Protectoseal). Petroliana collectors want cans with printed or embossed oil-company branding.

## Makers, brands and variants
Justrite (Chicago, 1906-), Eagle Mfg (Wellsburg WV), Protectoseal, George W. Diener Mfg (Chicago), Gilbert & Barker, Dover, WWII Wehrmacht/US jerry cans (dated and maker stamped).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Terne plate or galvanized steel; old cans have soldered seams and brass caps; newer have welded seams and plastic parts. Labels/decals add value.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rust, dents, leaking seams, repaints. Residual fuel smell and contents - carriers forbid flammable residue.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reproduction branded oil cans and fake "Texaco" decals are common.
