## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Cardboard or wood boxes covered in fabric (satin, brocade) with zari (metallic thread), sequins, beads and couched gold cord: produced in huge volume for gifts and weddings since the 1970s. Older pieces use real gilt-silver wire and wood frames.

## Makers, brands and variants
Unmarked workshops (Agra, Delhi, Lucknow); Kashmiri papier-mâché (Srinagar); Sadeli (Surat/Bombay, 19th c.).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Plastic sequins, synthetic brocade, glued edges = modern. Tarnished real metal wire, hand-stitched, wood carcass = older.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Fraying, lost sequins, crushed corners, stains.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Mostly modern production sold as "vintage".
