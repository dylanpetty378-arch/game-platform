# Nikon F / F2 / F3 professional film SLRs
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: gearfocus.com sold listings (F2 Photomic black, "Good", sold $270.58), eBay sold browsing, KEH price knowledge

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Big, square-shouldered 1960s–80s pro bodies with a removable prism. Tells: the F2 has a tall "Photomic" meter head with a round window on the front (the dome under the name plate) and an advance lever with a plastic tip; the F (1959–73) has a plainer prism and a "Nikon F" nameplate; the F3 (1980–2001) has a red stripe on the grip and an LCD meter. Black bodies are worth more than chrome. Sellers write "Nikon camera", "vintage Nikon 35mm", "old Nikon with lens" – they rarely name F2. Lenses: pre-AI Nikkors with scalloped metal focus rings; the "Nikkor-S/-H/-Q" names mean 1960s–70s glass.

## What sellers usually miss
Colour (black > chrome), which finder is on it (DP-1 Photomic vs DP-12 "AS" – AS worth 2×), whether the meter works, and the lenses. A "lot of old cameras" from a retired photographer very often hides an F2 plus fast Nikkor primes (50/1.4, 85/1.8, 105/2.5 each $80–250).

## Shipping / handling
Body + lens 2–3 lb, box with foam: $15–20 ground. Resell on eBay (13%); mechanical shutter means no electronics-risk beyond the meter, which buyers accept as "meter untested".

## Buy rule
Buy when: an F2/F3/F body is visible in a lot under $120, or a black F2 body alone under $100. Pass when: Photomic head missing (body-only F2 ~$120), seller names "F2" and prices at $250+. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
