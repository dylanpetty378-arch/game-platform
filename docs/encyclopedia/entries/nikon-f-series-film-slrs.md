## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Big, square-shouldered 1960s–80s pro bodies with a removable prism. Tells: the F2 has a tall "Photomic" meter head with a round window on the front (the dome under the name plate) and an advance lever with a plastic tip; the F (1959–73) has a plainer prism and a "Nikon F" nameplate; the F3 (1980–2001) has a red stripe on the grip and an LCD meter. Black bodies are worth more than chrome. Sellers write "Nikon camera", "vintage Nikon 35mm", "old Nikon with lens" – they rarely name F2. Lenses: pre-AI Nikkors with scalloped metal focus rings; the "Nikkor-S/-H/-Q" names mean 1960s–70s glass.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Nikon F** (1959–73): plain prism $150–300; Photomic heads also exist (general knowledge, verify).
- **Nikon F2** (1971–80): finders DP-1 Photomic (standard), DP-11 Photomic A, DP-12 Photomic AS (AS worth ~2×), plain DE-1 prism (general knowledge for DP-11/DE-1, verify). Black > chrome. F2 Titan, F2AS black = top. Body-only F2 without Photomic ~$120.
- **Nikon F3** (1980–2001): F3HP (high-eyepoint) $250–400; F3P (press) top tier.
- **Accessories that multiply**: 250-exposure back (MF-1/MF-2), motor drives (MD-2/MD-3 for F2) (motor names general knowledge, verify).
- **Lower tiers**: FM, FE, Nikkormat FT ($80–180); EM, FG, N-series AF, FunTouch ($15–50).
- **Lenses often in the lot**: 50/1.4, 85/1.8, 105/2.5 ($80–250 each); see manual-focus-camera-lenses.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Brass/steel bodies with chrome or black-enamel/black-paint finishes; black paint wearing to brass on edges is desired "brassing" on F/F2 (general knowledge, verify). F2: fully mechanical shutter (works without battery); Photomic meter needs batteries. F3: electronic shutter with a mechanical backup speed (general knowledge, verify). Serial numbers on top plate date the body.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dead or erratic meter (common; buyers accept "meter untested"), sticky shutter at slow speeds, deteriorated light seals and mirror foam (cheap fix — the $270.58 comp had new seals), dented prism, desilvered prism (dark spots in viewfinder), missing Photomic head, corroded battery compartment. Ask for: shutter fires at all speeds, meter needle moves, viewfinder clear.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Low fake risk. Watch for repainted chrome bodies sold as factory black and fake "Titan" or commemorative bodies (general knowledge, verify). Married finders (wrong head on body) are normal and fine, but price by the actual head.

## Market
Liquid market on eBay, KEH, MPB and gearfocus; film photography revival keeps demand steady (general knowledge, verify). Buyers: film shooters, Nikon collectors. Working F2/F3 bodies sell within days to a couple of weeks.

## What sellers usually miss
Colour (black > chrome), which finder is on it (DP-1 Photomic vs DP-12 "AS" – AS worth 2×), whether the meter works, and the lenses. A "lot of old cameras" from a retired photographer very often hides an F2 plus fast Nikkor primes (50/1.4, 85/1.8, 105/2.5 each $80–250).

## Shipping / handling
Body + lens 2–3 lb, box with foam: $15–20 ground. Resell on eBay (13%); mechanical shutter means no electronics-risk beyond the meter, which buyers accept as "meter untested".

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Nikon F2" (or F / F3HP) + finder (Photomic DP-1 / DP-12 AS / plain prism) + "black" or "chrome" + "35mm film SLR" + "tested" + lens if included. Junk: "vintage Nikon camera", "old 35mm", "untested". Example: `Nikon F2 Photomic Black 35mm Film SLR Body DP-1 Tested Shutter Works`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (liquid, sells in days to two weeks); KEH/MPB trade-in is only a floor; FB local rarely beats eBay.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median for the exact finder/finish (F2 black Photomic ~$230–270, F3HP $280–380, chrome F2 $180–260); accept −10%, floor −20%; OBO; cut 10% after 14 days.
- **Photos:** 1) front three-quarter with lens cap off; then top plate with serial and finder name, back open showing shutter curtains and light seals, through the viewfinder, bottom plate, battery compartment, any dents/brassing; short video of the shutter firing at 1s and 1/1000.
- **State:** model, finder type, finish, serial, shutter fires at all speeds, meter works/needle moves or "meter untested", light seals new or original, viewfinder clear or desilvered, dents/brassing, included lens and caps, accessories, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 2–3 lb; body cap and rear lens cap on, foam-wrapped, 10×8×8 single box with 2" cushion, 2–4 lb class, $15–20.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "This is a [black F2 Photomic / F3HP], the pro Nikon of [1971–80]; fully mechanical shutter tested at every speed, [new light seals], and black working bodies sell for [$X] — chrome and dead-meter bodies are the ones at $150."

## Buy rule
Buy when: an F2/F3/F body is visible in a lot under $120, or a black F2 body alone under $100. Pass when: Photomic head missing (body-only F2 ~$120), seller names "F2" and prices at $250+. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (it does).
