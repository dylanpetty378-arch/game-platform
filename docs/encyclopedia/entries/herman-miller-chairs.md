## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Modern office seating from Herman Miller (Zeeland, MI). **Aeron** (1994–, Chadwick & Stumpf): mesh ("Pellicle") seat and back, curved graphite/black frame, comes in sizes A (small), B (medium, most common) and C (large) – the size letter is on a label/tag under the seat or on the back of the seat pan; Aeron Remastered (2016–) has "8Z Pellicle" with a different weave. **Setu** (2009–, Studio 7.5): one-piece "Kinematic" plastic spine/arm frame with a stretched mesh/elastomer back, no adjustment knobs; also counter/bar-height stool versions (general knowledge, verify). Other lines: Embody, Mirra/Mirra 2, Sayl, Cosm, Eames Aluminum Group (separate design-object market). A label under the seat gives model, date and serial. Seller words: "Herman Miller office chair", "Aeron size C", "mesh office chair".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Aeron Classic** (1994–2016): sizes A/B/C; fully loaded = PostureFit or lumbar pad, tilt limiter, adjustable arms. Size C is less common and sells higher (earlier summary: $300–500).
- **Aeron Remastered** (2016–): higher resale than Classic. (general knowledge, verify)
- **Setu**: chair and stool; earlier summary puts the stool at $200–350.
- Embody typically sells highest of the HM task chairs used; Mirra/Sayl lower. (general knowledge, verify)
- Look-alikes: Steelcase Leap/Gesture (different market, similar value), Chinese "ergonomic mesh" copies (no value).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Genuine: Herman Miller label under the seat with model/serial/date, "HermanMiller" moulded on the base or back, quality cast aluminium or glass-filled nylon base, smooth gas cylinder. Polished-aluminium bases are a premium option. Cheap copies: no label, flimsy mesh, generic casters. Mesh condition is the main quality tell.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Common damage: torn or sagging seat mesh (Aeron), cracked arm pads, failed gas cylinder (sinks), broken tilt mechanism, worn casters. Mesh tears kill value (replacement seat pan is costly); arm pads and cylinders are cheap fixes but count as repairs. Ask: does it hold height, any mesh tears, what size, what adjustments.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Copy chairs styled like Aeron exist widely online; no label and wrong frame details. Refurbished-dealer chairs (remanufactured with replacement parts) are a large market and set the ceiling. (general knowledge, verify)

## Market
Large, liquid local market (offices liquidating, WFH buyers). Sells best locally (FB Marketplace, Craigslist, OfferUp) because shipping a chair costs $60–120; eBay mainly for local pickup or freight. Office-liquidation dumps periodically depress prices. (general knowledge, verify)

## What sellers usually miss
The size letter (C commands more), Remastered vs Classic, the options (PostureFit, polished base). Casual sellers often list "mesh office chair" without naming Herman Miller.

## Shipping / handling
A chair is ~35–50 lb boxed and bulky (~30x30x30" box); $60–120 to ship. Local pickup strongly preferred.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State model, size, year (from label), options/adjustments, mesh condition, cylinder holds height. Photograph the under-seat label, mesh closeups, arms and base. Title keywords: "Herman Miller Aeron Size C fully loaded", "Setu stool", "Remastered".

## Buy rule
Buy when: Setu under $90, only if the listing has been relisted within a week (motivated seller); Aeron size C under ~$100 with intact mesh (general knowledge, verify). Pass when: torn mesh, copies, or seller already priced at used-dealer level. Most Herman Miller chairs are about the size of a passenger seat – confirm it fits the car, and plan a local resale. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
