## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Folding knives from Solingen, Germany. Puma (founded 1769, Lauterjung family) is the most collected: rounded bolsters, pinned handle scales (stag, jacaranda, white-tail stag, or green/black synthetic), model number stamped on blade (e.g. "230365", "977", "993"), "PUMA" with cat head logo, and a dot/symbol date code on older pieces. Example 7950989573: lockback with clip-point black-coated blade, olive-green checkered synthetic scales with round cat-head medallion, steel bolsters and pins — a Puma military/utility lockback (230 series family). Terapeak shows a NOS NIB 1994 Puma 230365 sold at $172.99 (n=1) and boxed Puma lockbacks median ~$150 (2026-10-13 pull).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Puma Werk (Solingen)**: 1769–present. Stamp "PUMA [cat] GERMANY" + model number. Older (pre-~1990) pieces have a date-code symbol near the tang (Puma dot code table — collectors use it to date). **Puma IP** = "Puma International Products", Spanish then Chinese manufacture since ~2000s: low value. **Puma SGB** (Solingen, 2010s) mid value.
- **Böker (Heinr. Böker & Co.)**: Tree Brand stamp; also US Böker (Böker USA) and Argentina.
- **J.A. Henckels (Zwillingswerk)**: twin logo, slipjoints and lockbacks.
- **Hen & Rooster** (Bertram, later Frost): rooster logo; later Frost imports from Germany/Spain — check stamp.
- **Hubertus, Linder, Herder, Otter, Carl Schlieper (Eye Brand)**: lower but collectible.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
German-made: crisp stamping, tight fitting liners, even walk-and-talk, carbon or high-carbon stainless steel. Imports: laser-etched logos, loose tolerances, "Made in China/Spain" or "IP". Stag scales from older production (natural antler) beat synthetic. Original boxes with paper "Puma" brochure and leather pouch add 30–60%.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Sharpened-down blades (profile changes), broken tips, lock play, rust pitting, cracked stag, missing medallion, buffed/polished blades (removes etch).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Puma White Hunter and Puma folder copies from Pakistan/China exist (wrong logo font, no model number, "Puma" without Germany). Puma IP is legitimate but cheap; sellers routinely omit "IP" from titles.

## Market
Strong collector base; eBay sells German Pumas within 1–3 weeks. Knife buyers scrutinize tang stamps.

## What sellers usually miss
"IP" vs Germany distinction (sellers both under- and over-price); Puma date codes; the original box and catalogue paper; stag vs synthetic.

## Shipping / handling
Pocket size; padded box $5–8. Ships legally domestically (folding knives fine via USPS).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Photograph both sides of tang stamp, model number, date code, lock-up, edge; state sharpened or factory edge; include box/papers.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold × 0.87 − $7 − $7 − price ≥ max($50, price). German-made Puma loose (~$90) → max price ~$20–25; boxed (~$150) → max price ~$60–70 (Tier A at ≤ $40). Puma IP: pass. Ask seller for close-up of tang stamp before buying. No professional authentication needed.
