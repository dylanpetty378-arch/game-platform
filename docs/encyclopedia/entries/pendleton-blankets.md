## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Pendleton Woolen Mills (Oregon). Money is in the JACQUARD TRADE BLANKETS: bold geometric Native-inspired patterns (Chief Joseph, Harding, Beaver State), felt binding, "Beaver State" label = 1920s–50s. Plain solid bed blankets with satin/sateen binding (pink, blue, camel) are common and cheap. Also valuable: Pendleton "Glacier Park" stripe (cream w/ 4 stripes), Hudson's Bay 4-point/6-point stripes (England, ivory with green/red/yellow/indigo), Cabin Craft, Oregon City trade blankets (pre-1930, rare). Sellers write "Pendleton wool blanket", "Indian blanket", "camp blanket".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Pendleton Woolen Mills** (Pendleton, Oregon): jacquard trade blankets — Chief Joseph, Harding, Beaver State patterns; "Beaver State" label (dated 1920s–50s here; the collectible tier below says 1920s–60s — both kept, verify with a label guide); "Round Up" label 1910s–20s and pre-1920 labels top tier; Glacier Park stripe (1940s collectible); modern (1990s+) jacquards decent tier; solid satin-bound bed blankets low.
- **Hudson's Bay Company** point blankets (made in England): ivory with green/red/yellow/indigo stripes; 4-point used $150–250, vintage 6-point collectible; the "points" (short black lines) indicate size (general knowledge, verify).
- **Oregon City Woolen Mills**: pre-1930 trade blankets, rare, top tier.
- **Cabin Craft**: trade-style blankets, collectible (general knowledge for value, verify).
- Size premium: queen > twin.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Virgin wool, jacquard-woven (pattern reversed on the back, same weave both sides) vs printed; felt binding on trade blankets vs satin/sateen binding on bed blankets. Label typeface and wording date the blanket (Pendleton label dating guides). Heavier, denser wool and wear-softened edges on old blankets; bright crisp colours and modern care labels on new ones (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Moth holes (the main value killer — ask for close-up photos held up to light), loose or replaced binding, stains, fading, felting from washing, cigarette burns, musty odour. Re-bound edges and darned holes reduce value (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Pendleton continually reissues heritage patterns (Chief Joseph, Harding, Glacier Park) — modern labels distinguish them (general knowledge, verify). Imported "Aztec"/"Southwest" acrylic blankets are listed as "Pendleton style" or "Indian blanket". HBC reproductions and licensed versions exist.

## Market
Steady demand from Western/Americana decor buyers, cabin owners and collectors on eBay and Etsy (general knowledge, verify). Good jacquards sell readily; solid blankets are slow. Fall–winter is the stronger season (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
The pattern name and the Beaver State / early labels; moth damage photos; size (queen > twin).

## Shipping / handling
3–6 lb, $15–20 each way. Fold into a poly bag inside a box. eBay/Etsy.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Pendleton" (or "Hudson's Bay") + "wool blanket" + pattern name (Chief Joseph, Harding) + label ("Beaver State") + "jacquard" + size (twin/queen, inches) + "vintage". Junk: "throw", "camp blanket", "Indian blanket". Example: `Vintage Pendleton Beaver State Chief Joseph Wool Trade Blanket Jacquard Twin`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (steady Western/cabin decor demand; good jacquards sell readily); Etsy at the same price reaches decor buyers; fall–winter is the stronger season.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median (Beaver State ~$169, modern jacquard/HBC 4-point $120–220, 6-point/Glacier Park $250–500); accept −10%, floor −20%; OBO; cut 10% after 30 days.
- **Photos:** 1) whole blanket flat, pattern full; then label close-up, binding/whipstitch edge, back side, held to window light for moth holes, any stain, tape measure on the width.
- **State:** maker, pattern name, label text and era, size in inches, weave (jacquard/stripe/solid), binding type, wool content, odour-free, holes/stains/binding wear by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 3–6 lb; fold into a poly bag, 16×12×8 box, 4–6 lb class, $15–20; no double box needed.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "This carries the [Beaver State / Round Up] label, the [1920s–60s] trade-blanket era, pattern [Chief Joseph]; vintage labelled jacquards sell for [$X] and a new Pendleton Chief Joseph retails well over that — no moth damage, photo [5] shows it held to light."

## Buy rule
Buy when: jacquard Pendleton/HBC with vintage label ≤ $80 and no moth holes. Pass when: solid satin-bound over $25, or binding/holes need sewing. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (folded, it does).
