## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Cast-iron (rarely bronze) andirons shaped as marching soldiers in tricorn hat and coat – "Hessians" or "Redcoats". 18th/19th-century originals are 18–22" tall, crude casting, often old paint. 20th-century reproductions (Virginia Metalcrafters, Rostand, unmarked) are 10–20" and crisp. Small 9–12" pairs are usually mid-century decorative pieces. Each andiron is a flat-backed figure cast in one or two halves, joined to a horizontal log bar (billet) at the back with a rear foot. Marks, when present, are cast on the back of the figure or underside of the billet (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- Unmarked 19th-century American foundries – most of the collectible tier.
- **Virginia Metalcrafters** (Waynesboro VA, Colonial Williamsburg licensed reproductions) and **Rostand** (CT) – 20th-century repros, sometimes marked. (general knowledge on marks, verify)
- Bronze pairs (1850s) – top tier, dealer market.
- Variants: painted (red coat, blue/black coat) vs bare iron; single-piece vs two-part casting; related figural andirons (owls, cats, Washington, George Washington's soldiers, Hessians with drums).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Older: shallow, soft casting detail, layered old paint, rust in recesses, hand-filed seams, square-cut or peened fasteners joining figure to billet. Newer: crisp detail, uniform modern paint, threaded bolts/nuts, smooth even rust. Weight: full-size iron pair 10–25 lb. Bronze shows gold-brown at worn high spots and is non-magnetic (magnet test). (general knowledge, verify)

## Condition, damage and repairs
Cast iron is brittle: cracked or broken legs/billets, welded repairs (bead lines at joins), missing billets (single figures), fresh repaint over old paint. A single andiron sells at a fraction of a pair (single = junk tier). Ask for photos of the back, the billet join and the underside.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reproductions are the norm: Virginia Metalcrafters, Rostand and unmarked mid-century castings; modern imports are also cast from originals (soft detail can fool – check fasteners and paint layers). Repainted 20th-c. pairs are sold as "antique". (general knowledge, verify)

## Market
Decorator and fireplace-season buyers plus early-American collectors. Sells best in autumn/winter. eBay with calculated shipping or local; Chairish/1stdibs for dealer-grade pairs. Moderate volume, slow for repros. (general knowledge, verify)

## What sellers usually miss
"Hessian" – they write "soldier andirons" or "red coats". Age tells: shallow casting and old paint layers = older.

## Shipping / handling
Heavy (10–25 lb pair), $30–50 to ship. Best sold locally or on eBay with calculated shipping. Durable but wrap legs and billets (cast iron can snap if dropped).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State height, width, depth (log-bar length), weight, iron vs bronze (magnet), paint (original/old/repaint), any marks, pair vs single, repairs. Photograph front, back, the billet join, undersides and any marks. Title keywords: "Hessian soldier andirons pair cast iron", "Redcoat", "19th century", "original paint".

## Buy rule
Buy when: a full-size (16"+) pair under $60. Pass when: 9–12" repro pair over $40 (sold ~$80–120, shipping eats it). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Fits a passenger seat.
