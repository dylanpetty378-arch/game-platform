# Hessian soldier (figural) andirons
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: chairish.com 1960s pair asking $300 (20" tall); 1stdibs listings of 19th-c. pairs $600–2,500; eBay listings 16" pairs

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Cast-iron (rarely bronze) andirons shaped as marching soldiers in tricorn hat and coat – "Hessians" or "Redcoats". 18th/19th-century originals are 18–22" tall, crude casting, often old paint. 20th-century reproductions (Virginia Metalcrafters, Rostand, unmarked) are 10–20" and crisp. Small 9–12" pairs are usually mid-century decorative pieces.

## What sellers usually miss
"Hessian" – they write "soldier andirons" or "red coats". Age tells: shallow casting and old paint layers = older.

## Shipping / handling
Heavy (10–25 lb pair), $30–50 to ship. Best sold locally or on eBay with calculated shipping.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a full-size (16"+) pair under $60. Pass when: 9–12" repro pair over $40 (sold ~$80–120, shipping eats it). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
