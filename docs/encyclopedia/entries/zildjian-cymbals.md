## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Brass-coloured disc with an ink logo near the edge and a stamped trademark around the bell. Value is in the series stamp,
not the brand. Read the logo ink: "Avedis Zildjian Co." + "Genuine Turkish Cymbals Made in USA" = A Zildjian (good).
"K Zildjian" = top line. "ZBT", "ZHT", "Planet Z", "ZXT", "S" = brass/budget (cheap). Older As: no ink, just the stamp
pressed in near the bell (1950s–70s "trans" and "hollow block" stamps) – collectors pay for these. The trademark stamp in
the metal under the ink identifies era (Zildjian "A" trans-stamps 1950s–60s, K Istanbul). Hi-hats sell as pairs; "Quick
Beat" = 1980s–90s heavy bottom with four holes. Cracks (even repaired/drilled), keyholing (oval centre hole) and hairline
cracks radiating from the bell or edge kill value. Sellers write "cymbal", "crash", "ride", "set of cymbals",
"Zildjian cymbals", "drum set cymbals", often with brand but no series.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Zildjian**: A (Avedis, bright, most common; 1950s–70s "Avedis" stamp variants collectible), A Custom (brilliant
  finish), New Beat and Quick Beat hats, A Sweet Ride 21–23" ($250–350), K (dark), K Custom, K Constantinople
  (pro cast bronze $150–500 each), K Zildjian 1980s Canadian, Istanbul K (pre-1977, Turkish stamp, $500–2,000);
  Oriental Trash Splash. Budget sheet lines: ZBT, ZHT, ZXT, Planet Z, S ($20–60).
- **Sabian**: HH, HHX, AA, AAX pro ($120–400; AA/HH $100–250); B20 crash-rides; budget B8, B8 Pro, B8X, SBR ($20–60).
- **Paiste**: 2002, Signature, Formula 602, Giant Beat, Innovations (1995–2004, discontinued), Traditionals — pro
  ($200–600; 2002/Signature $150–400; 602 and Giant Beat vintage are top tier). Budget: PST (3/5/X), 101, 302, 502.
- **Meinl**: Byzance (collectible); budget lines (general knowledge, verify).
- **Bosphorus / Istanbul Agop**: hand-hammered Turkish, large rides in the top tier.
- Bigger = more: 20–24" rides and 18"+ crashes carry the money. Weight in grams matters to buyers.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Cast B20 bronze (A/K, AA/HH, 2002 is CuSn8 but pro-grade, 602/Signature) vs sheet B8 bronze or brass (ZBT, B8, PST, 101)
(general knowledge, verify the Paiste alloy detail). Hand-hammering marks and lathing patterns; brilliant (buffed) vs
traditional finish. The stamp under the bell dates Zildjians: 1950s–60s "trans stamp"/"small stamp" and the small
"hollow" ink logo; see the contradiction note below about the hollow logo era.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Edge cracks, bell cracks, keyholing, cracks "repaired" by drilling or cutting (rounding the edge) — all take a cymbal to
parts value ($30–60 for a cracked 20" A). Heavy fingerprints/oxidation are cosmetic; aggressive buffing can remove ink
logos. Ask for an edge photo all round and a close-up of the bell and stamp.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Zildjian/Sabian/Paiste cymbals (often sold cheaply online from overseas) exist; tells are wrong logo fonts,
missing trademark stamp and very light weight. Logos can also be re-inked or a budget cymbal relabelled
(general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Drummers (gigging and home players) and a smaller vintage-cymbal collector market (trans-stamp A's, Istanbul K's,
Paiste 602/Giant Beat). Very liquid on Reverb (8%) and eBay; pro-series rides and hats sell fast at market. Sellers who
name the model usually price at market; the edge is in unnamed lots and "Zildjian cymbals" with no series.

## What sellers usually miss
The line (A vs ZBT) and the stamp era — sellers say "Zildjian" for everything, or say "drum set cymbals" and have an
A Custom. Titles like "Zildjian cymbal" with no model need a close-up of the ink; "unsure which series" – ask for the
stamp under the bell. Discontinued Paiste lines (Innovations 1995–2004, Traditionals). Mixed lots where one pro ride
carries the whole lot. Hairline cracks at the edge are deal-killers; keyhole or cracks = parts. Weight (grams) matters to
buyers.

## Shipping / handling
20" cymbal ~6 lb boxed flat, $18–25 (other write-ups: 20" ride 5–7 lb needs a 22x22 box, $25–35; 18–22" $20–30 each way in
a cymbal box; a 22" ride ~7 lb in a 24" square box $25–40 ground, oversize; from Hawaii/Alaska $50+). Pair of 14" hats
~5 lb, flat 16" box, $20–25. Pack between cardboard discs. Reverb (~8%) or eBay (13%).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker + series + model + size + weight in grams + era: "Zildjian A Medium Ride 20" 2450g", "K Custom Dark hats 14"", "1960s trans stamp", "Paiste 602"; + "no cracks"; Junk: "Zildjian cymbal" with no series, "drum set cymbals", "lot". Example: `Zildjian A Sweet Ride 23" 2900g No Cracks No Keyhole Vintage Stamp`.
- **Where:** Reverb first (~8%; pro rides and hats sell fast), eBay second; ZBT/B8 budget lines local or as a lot. Reverb beats eBay.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (A 20" ride $110–200; Quick Beat hats $150–220; K Custom hats $250–350; 23" Sweet Ride $380; 1960s trans-stamp 20" $250–400), accept −10%, floor p25; OBO; cut 10% after 3 weeks.
- **Photos:** first photo top face, logo up; then stamp under the bell macro, ink logos, an all-round edge photo (no cracks), bell and hole (keyhole), underside, scale showing grams; a short strike video.
- **State:** maker, series, model, size, weight in grams, stamp era, finish, "no cracks, no keyhole" or exactly where, oxidation/fingerprints, buffing, counterfeit check (stamp present, weight).
- **Pack / haul:** 20" ~6 lb flat in a 22×22 box between cardboard discs ($18–35); pair of 14" hats ~5 lb in a flat 16" box ($20–25); 22"+ is oversize.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[A Zildjian 20" Medium Ride], [2450 g], [1970s stamp], no cracks and no keyhole — the Avedis cast line, not the ZBT sheet line; new it's [$X] and used clean ones hold [$110–200]."

## Buy rule
Merged from the five write-ups:
- Buy when: un-cracked A/K Zildjian, Sabian AA/HH or Paiste 2002 for under half the sold price (20" A ride under $50);
  cast-bronze pro line ≤ 40% of Reverb sold and no cracks visible; vintage A/K rides ≤ $100; K/K Custom or Paiste 2002
  at ≤ $80, or any vintage small-stamp A at ≤ $100; pro-series 20"+ ride under $100, or a lot where the identifiable pro
  pieces resell for ≥ 2× the asking price plus shipping; trans-stamp A, 602, or Istanbul K under $120, or any clean
  A/2002 20"+ under $50.
- Pass when: any crack/repair/keyhole, budget or B8/B8-pro/sheet-brass line, seller quotes Reverb price or already lists
  per-cymbal market values, 20" A at $75 (market ~$100–150, margin < $50).
- Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees (Reverb ~8%) and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a
  passenger seat.
