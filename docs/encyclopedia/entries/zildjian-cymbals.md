# Cymbals (Zildjian, Sabian, Paiste)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: knowledge of Reverb/eBay sold ranges for A Zildjian 20" rides, ZBT/ZHT budget lines, 1960s–70s "A" stamps; no dated comp pulled

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Read the logo ink: "Avedis Zildjian Co." + "Genuine Turkish Cymbals Made in USA" = A Zildjian (good). "K Zildjian" = top line. "ZBT", "ZHT", "Planet Z", "ZXT" = brass/budget (cheap). Paiste 2002/Signature and Sabian AA/HH are the equivalents; Sabian B8 and Paiste PST are budget. Older As: no ink, just the stamp pressed in near the bell (1950s–70s "trans" and "hollow block" stamps) – collectors pay for these. Cracks (even repaired/drilled) and keyholing kill value.

## Additional research (earlier entry: Zildjian / Sabian / Paiste cymbals)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay/Reverb used browse Oct 2025 (A Zildjian Quick Beat 14", K Custom, New Beat); sweep listing 2754

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Brass-coloured disc with an ink logo near the edge and a stamped trademark around the bell. Zildjian lines: A (bright, most common), A Custom (brilliant finish), K (dark, "K Zildjian Istanbul"-style stamp), K Custom, Z/ZBT/ZXT (sheet brass = junk). Sabian AA/HH/HHX, Paiste 2002/Signature are the equivalents. Vintage tell: 1950s–60s "trans stamp"/"small stamp" A Zildjians sell to collectors. Hi-hats sell as pairs; "Quick Beat" = 1980s–90s heavy bottom with four holes. Cracks, keyholing or "repaired" = pass.

## Additional research (earlier entry: Drum cymbals (Zildjian / Sabian / Paiste / Meinl))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: drumcenternh.com used 23" Zildjian A Sweet Ride ("Good", $349.99, sold), Guitar Center used listings, Reverb price knowledge

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Value is in the series stamp, not the brand. Read the ink logo: **Zildjian** – "A" (Avedis), "A Custom", "K", "K Custom", "K Constantinople" are pro cast bronze ($150–500 each); "ZBT", "ZHT", "Planet Z", "S" are sheet-brass beginner ($20–60). **Sabian** – "HH", "HHX", "AA", "AAX" pro ($120–400); "B8", "B8 Pro", "B8X", "SBR" beginner ($20–60). **Paiste** – "2002", "Signature", "Formula 602", "Giant Beat" pro ($200–600, 602 and Giant Beat vintage are top tier); "PST", "101", "302", "502" beginner. Bigger = more: 20–24" rides and 18"+ crashes carry the money. Watch for cracks at the edge, "keyholing" (oval centre hole) and hairline cracks radiating from the bell. 1950s–60s Zildjian with the small "hollow" ink logo and hand-hammered "Trans stamp" are collector items.

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | ZBT, B8, PST3, Camber, no-name brass; any cracked cymbal | $10–50 | most listings |
| Decent | A Zildjian 14–16" crashes, Sabian AA, Paiste 2002 crashes | $80–180 | |
| Collectible | Zildjian A 21–23" Sweet Ride ($250–350), K rides ($200–400), Paiste 2002/Signature 20"+ ($250–450), Sabian HHX | $200–450 | the ones worth buying |
| Top | 1960s A Zildjian 22" with hollow logo, Paiste 602 rides, K Constantinople 22"+ | $400–1,200 | verify before paying |

### Comps (sold, dated)
- 2026 – Zildjian A Sweet Ride 23", used "Good", no cracks – $349.99 (Drum Center of NH, sold)
- 2026 – Zildjian A Medium Ride 20" used – $150–200 (Guitar Center used)
- 2026 – Sabian B8 Pro 16" crash used – $40–60

### What sellers usually miss
Series (they say "Zildjian cymbals" and mean ZBT, or say "drum set cymbals" and have an A Custom). Also mixed lots where one pro ride carries the whole lot. Confirm from the ink logo in the photo, and ask for an edge photo (cracks kill value).

### Shipping / handling
A 22" ride is ~7 lb but 24" square box: $25–40 ground (oversize); from Hawaii/Alaska $50+. Resell on Reverb (8%) or eBay (13%). Pack between cardboard discs.

### Buy rule
Buy when: pro-series 20"+ ride under $100, or a lot where the identifiable pro pieces resell for ≥ 2× the asking price plus shipping. Pass when: only beginner series, any visible crack/keyhole, or seller already lists per-cymbal market values. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: Reverb/eBay sold (agent knowledge Oct 2026)

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Series is stamped by the bell (ink logo) and the trademark stamp is in the metal under the bell. Zildjian A (1950s–70s "Avedis" stamp variants are collectible; 70s–80s hollow-logo A's $100–180 for 20" ride), K Zildjian (Istanbul K = $500–2,000; modern K $150–300), Sabian AA/HH ($100–250), Paiste 2002/Signature ($150–400), Paiste PST-X/101/Sabian B8/Zildjian ZBT/Planet Z = brass student lines ($20–60).

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

### What sellers usually miss
