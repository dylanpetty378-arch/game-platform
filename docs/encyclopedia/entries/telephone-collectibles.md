## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- **WE 302** (1937–1955, Henry Dreyfuss design): heavy die-cast metal (later some thermoplastic) body, sloped front with a dial set in a recess, F1 handset with round ends, bell inside the base. Black most common; ivory, red, green, gray, burgundy are rarer.
- **WE 500** (1950–1980s): plastic body, rounder, dial with letters/numbers on the outer ring under a clear finger wheel; date code stamped on base and handset (e.g., "8-62"). 2500 = touch-tone version. Colors: black, beige, ivory, pink, turquoise, light blue, green, red, yellow, white, gray.
- **WE 554** wall phone (1950s–80s) and 354 (1950s), **Princess** (1959–), **Trimline** (1965–).
- **Candlestick** (c. 1900s–1930s): tall black enameled brass stem, mouthpiece at top, separate receiver hanging on a hook, connects to a wooden or metal "subset" ringer box. WE 20B/20AL (no dial), 50AL/51AL (dial); Kellogg, Stromberg-Carlson, Automatic Electric (Strowger dial), American Electric.
- **Automatic Electric** Type 40/50/80 Monophone, **ITT/Kellogg** 500-style "lookalikes" – less valuable than WE.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Western Electric** (manufacturing arm of the Bell System; phones leased, so "Bell System – Property of" markings; "Western Electric" on base and handset). Most collected.
- **Automatic Electric** (Chicago/Northlake; GTE independent companies), **Kellogg** (Chicago), **Stromberg-Carlson** (Rochester), **North Electric**, **Leich** – independents; usually 20–50% below comparable WE except early/odd models.
- **Novelty phones**: Coca-Cola disc phone (1995), Mickey Mouse, Snoopy, hamburger, lips – low (Terapeak: Coca-Cola disc median $22).
- **Mouthpieces**: Bakelite/glass "Bell System" mouthpieces from candlesticks; small collector items $10–40.
- **Glass insulators** are a separate category (not here).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Weight: real 302 metal body is very heavy (~7–8 lb); repros are light plastic.
- Markings: "Western Electric" / "Bell System" cast in base, handset and dial card; date codes on 500s.
- Cords: original cloth-covered (302) or coiled with 4-prong or spade-lug connections; modular plugs suggest a later conversion (fine for users).
- Dial: real dials have metal finger stop and pulse mechanism; repros often use a push-button keypad disguised as a dial, or say "tone/pulse switch".
- Colors: original 500 colors are consistent through body, handset, and cord; a "pink 500" with a black handset or yellowed mismatched parts sells for less.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Cracked plastic bodies (500s), chipped paint on 302 metal, missing dial cards, broken/dead ringers, non-working dials, frayed cloth cords, worn candlestick enamel, missing candlestick subset/ringer box (drops value). Many are restored/repainted – repaint on 302s lowers collector value; sellers must disclose. Function: these are passive devices – they can be tested on a line or with a phone tester; untested sells for less.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- **Candlestick repros** (1970s–90s, Hong Kong/Taiwan/China; brands like "Crosley", "Radio Shack", "GPX"): lightweight, brass-plated plastic or thin brass, modular jack, push-button or fake dial, ringer inside the base. Real ones are heavy enameled brass with patent dates and WE/Kellogg stamps on the base and receiver.
- **302 repros** (Crosley, "Retro"), light plastic, keypad behind the dial, "Made in China".
- **Fake colors**: 302s and 500s repainted in rare colors; check inside surfaces and handset for original color plastic.

## Market
Big supply of 500s; real demand concentrates on 302s, colored sets, candlesticks and early wall phones. Decorators buy colored 500s; ATCA collectors buy rare/early. 302s sell in 1–3 weeks.

## What sellers usually miss
Owners don't distinguish 302 (heavy metal, more valuable) from 500 – they list both as "old rotary phone $25". Also, a real candlestick listed with "old repro candlestick" photos (or vice versa – most CL candlesticks are repros).

## Shipping / handling
302: heavy; pad well, 10 lb packed, $20–30. 500: 4–5 lb, $15–20. Candlesticks: tall, fragile mouthpiece, $20–25. All fit a passenger seat.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Western Electric" + model (302, 500, 554, Princess) + colour + "rotary" + "desk phone" + date codes + "tested works"; "candlestick" + "original" + maker. Junk: "old rotary phone", "antique telephone", "repro". Example: `Western Electric 302 Rotary Desk Phone Black Metal 1940 Dated Tested Rings Dials`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (302s sell in 1–3 weeks; coloured 500s to decorators; ATCA collectors for rare); common black 500s sell FB local since $15–20 shipping equals the price.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median (black 302 $70–200, coloured 500/554/Princess $40–90, candlestick original $120–400, black 500 $15–40); accept −10%, floor −20%; OBO; cut 10% after 30 days.
- **Photos:** 1) front three-quarter, handset on; then base underside with maker and date codes, handset date codes, dial face and number card, cord and plug, colour in daylight (original vs repaint), any cracks/chips; video of dialing and ringing.
- **State:** model, maker, date codes on base and handset, colour original or repainted, cord type and plug, tested (rings, dials, talks) or untested, restoration done, candlestick: dial or non-dial, subset included, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 302 ~10 lb packed, handset wrapped separately, single box with 2" foam, $20–30; 500 4–5 lb, $15–20; candlesticks mouthpiece padded, double-box, $20–25.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "A [1940 302], the heavy metal pre-500 model, date codes matching on base and handset — not the $25 plastic 500 it gets listed as; tested ringing and dialing in the video; working 302s sell for [$X]."

## Buy rule
Profit = median × 0.87 − ship (~$22) − ship to Dylan (~$22) − price ≥ max($50, price). Black 500: always pass. Black 302 at $140 median: works only ≤ ~$30 (local pickup helps). Colored 302 or real candlestick at $250+: works up to ~$80–100. Require photos of the base underside markings and handset. Pass any repro tells.
