## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Decorative angel figures, mostly cold-cast resin (1990s–2000s gift trade), plus porcelain (Lladró, Goebel, Lenox) and 1950s Japanese ceramic angels. **Seraphim Classics** (Roman Inc., 1994–) are tall resin angels with soft pastel matte paint, sculpted feathered wings, realistic serene faces with closed mouths and a gold foil/stamped base label with name and year. **Willow Tree** are faceless, hand-carved-look beige resin with wire wings. Generic resin angels have glossy, doll-like faces, painted open red lips, glitter, and lumpy grassy bases.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Roman Inc. – Seraphim Classics** (Dennis Gay sculptor, 1994–): named angels (Rebecca, Gloria, Cymbal of Joy, Angels of the Month); stamped bottom "Seraphim Classics © Roman Inc." with item number (e.g. 78085, 81480). $6–35 each.
- **Willow Tree (Demdaco, Susan Lordi, 2000–)**: $10–30.
- **Lladró / NAO (Spain)**: porcelain, blue mark; angels $40–250.
- **Goebel/Hummel (Germany)**: Annual angel bells/ornaments $10–40; large angels more.
- **Josef Originals, Napco, Lefton, Enesco (Japan 1950s–60s)**: birthday/month angels $8–40.
- **Sonny Angel (Dreams Inc., Japan)**: small vinyl blind-box babies; rare "Robby Angel" or secret figures $30–300 (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Porcelain = cold, glassy underside with maker mark; resin = lighter, felt or sticker bottom, warm to touch. Seraphim: tight sculpted detail, numbered gold sticker. Generic: rough seams, glitter, no marks.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chipped wing tips, broken fingers, glued wings, yellowing of resin, dust in crevices, missing boxes (box adds ~20–40% for Seraphim and Lladró).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Asian Lladró-lookalikes (glossy porcelain, no blue mark). Sonny Angel fakes are common. Seraphim are not usually faked, but copied in style by unmarked gift-shop resin.
