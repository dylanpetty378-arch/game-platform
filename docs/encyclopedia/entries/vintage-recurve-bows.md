## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
One-piece laminated wood bow, 48–70 in, with curved tips (recurve) or straight limbs (longbow); glass-backed limbs show a coloured stripe along the edge. Maker and model are silk-screened on the belly side of the riser near the grip, usually with draw weight ("45#", "52# @ 28") and a serial. **Bear Archery** (Grayling, Mich.) risers carry a coin/medallion in the riser: copper coin = 1959–61, aluminium = 1962–63, pewter = 1963–65, brass = 1965–69, nickel-silver = 1970–72, raised medallion = 1972+. **Damon Howatt** (Yakima, WA): Hunter, Mamba, Ventura, Monterey, Super Diablo – thin elegant risers, often phenolic tip overlays. **Wing** (Red Wing Hunter, Chaparral, Presentation II), **Browning** (Nomad, Wasp, Explorer), **Ben Pearson** (Palomino, Hunter, Colt), **Shakespeare** (Kaibab, Necedah, Condor), **Indian Archery / Hoyt / Root / Herter's / Fred Bear takedowns**.

Titles: "recurve bow", "vintage bow", "long bow", "hunting bow" – often with no brand because the seller never read the riser.

## What sellers usually miss
The riser silk-screen. "Old wooden bow" at $20–40 with a Bear coin or a Howatt script visible in the photo is the play. Check: limb tips for cracks, limbs sighted for twist, and draw weight (40–55 lb is the sweet spot). Left-hand bows take longer to sell (knock 20%).

## Shipping / handling
Long (54–70 in) but light (1–2 lb): needs a bow tube or 72-in box; $25–40 each way – this is the killer. Sell on eBay (13%) or Archery Talk / Leatherwall classifieds (no fee).

## Buy rule
Buy when: Bear coin-riser Kodiak/Super Kodiak/Grizzly under $90; Howatt, Wing, Shakespeare Condor, Pearson Palomino under $70; any recurve with a Top-tier name under $150 if limbs look straight. Pass when: no brand visible, draw over 60 lb, LH, or seller names the model and prices over $100. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (~$60 round trip).
