## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Abalone: ear-shaped single shells with a row of respiratory holes and an iridescent nacre interior. **Red abalone** (Haliotis rufescens, California) has a brick-red outer rim and is the largest (to 10–12 in). **Green/pink/black abalone** (California) smaller. **Paua** (Haliotis iris, NZ) has intense blue-green colour and is the main commercial import. Mexican abalone common in decor.
Specimen collections: labelled boxes/drawers, Latin names, locality cards — the value is the data.

## Makers, brands and variants
No makers; dealers/collectors. Grades: F+++ (gem), F++, F+, F (specimen grades used by shell dealers). Polished vs. natural surface.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Natural abalone: rough exterior with growth (barnacles, worm tubes), nacre interior; polished: exterior ground to show colour (decor, cheaper per size for imports). Large unbroken red abalone with good nacre is the best common piece. Specimen-grade: perfect lips, no drill holes, with data.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chipped edges, broken lips, sun-bleached nacre, glued repairs, holes, fading. Smell (organic residue) in recently collected shells.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Dyed/polished shells, resin replicas, composite "abalone" sheets. For fossils and minerals: fakes and composites are common.

## Market
High volume decor/spiritual market (smudge bowls); abundant supply from imports; price low.

## What sellers usually miss
Labelled collections with locality data; old natural-history cabinet specimens.

## Legal / restrictions (important)
- California: sale of sport-taken abalone is illegal; recreational fishery closed since 2018 and commercial fishing closed since 1997. Shells from legal/old harvest are widely sold but provenance questions exist — verify before buying or listing.
- CITES-listed: queen conch (Strombus gigas), giant clams (Tridacnidae), hard corals, chambered nautilus (Appendix II since 2017) — avoid buying/selling across borders; eBay restricts some.

## Shipping / handling
Abalone: 0.5–1.5 lb each; 4 large shells ≈ 4–6 lb packed, $15–22. Wrap individually; edges chip.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Species, size, natural vs. polished, origin (if known, and legal source), chips. Avoid medical/spiritual claims.
