## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Model on a plinth or in a styrofoam-lined box; brand on the base. Tells:
- **Franklin Mint / Danbury Mint** 1:24 (1980s–2010s): opening doors/hood, mostly $30–80 sold loose; with box, papers and display case $60–150; rare editions (Danbury Mint 1:12, pewter/sterling, Duesenberg, limited "Trucks" series) $150–400. "Precision Models" era (1990s) hold better than 2000s.
- **Carousel 1** (2000s, 1:18 Indy roadsters, Can-Am, Cobra Daytona): discontinued, $100–250 sold; Indy 500 winners (#4501 Sweikert 1955 etc.) $120–200; Novi/Watson specials up to $300.
- **Exoto** 1:18 (Ford GT40, Ferrari, Cobra, Chaparral): $250–800+; Exoto Motorbox Signature $500+.
- **AUTOart / Kyosho / Minichamps / CMC** 1:18: $80–300 (CMC $300–600).
- **Ertl / Maisto / Bburago / Jada / Welly / Hot Wheels 1:18** $10–40; Ertl American Muscle 1990s $20–50; **Ertl farm toys** (John Deere 1/16 1970s precision series) $40–150.
- **Neiman Marcus / dealership promos** (Ford Thunderbird 2002, Corvette promos): $20–60.
- **Hot Wheels Redlines (1968–77)** – separate topic; loose common $10–30, Rear-Loading Beach Bomb etc. $10k+.
- **1:64 small cars** (Hot Wheels, Matchbox, Yatming): mostly $1–15 each loose; Redline-wheel Hot Wheels and early Lesney Matchbox are the exceptions (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Franklin Mint** (US, 1:24/1:43): mass-marketed "collectible" subscriptions; high original retail, weak resale. Display case and certificate add modestly.
- **Danbury Mint** (US): similar to Franklin, but 1:12 scale cars, pewter/sterling editions and the trucks series are the exceptions.
- **Carousel 1**: discontinued 1:18 Indy roadsters, Can-Am, Cobra Daytona; collectible.
- **Exoto**: high-detail 1:18 racing cars; Motorbox Signature is the top line.
- **CMC** (Germany): very high-detail multi-part models, $300–600+.
- **AUTOart / Kyosho / Minichamps**: quality 1:18 / 1:43; racing-livery Minichamps lines collectible.
- **Ertl**: 1:18 muscle cars (common) and 1/16 farm toys (John Deere precision series collectible).
- **Maisto / Bburago / Jada / Welly**: budget toy-grade; junk tier.
- **Pocher** 1:8 kits built: top tier when built well (general knowledge, verify on build quality).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Zinc-alloy (Zamac) body with plastic interior and details. Quality tells: separate photo-etched parts, carpeted interiors, working steering and suspension, many opening panels, sharp panel gaps, real-rubber tyres with tread. Budget tells: thick panel gaps, toy-like chrome, molded-in details, oversized pins. Brand and scale are cast or printed on the chassis plate (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken mirrors, antennas and wipers; paint rub on display-case contact points; yellowed clear parts; missing small parts; zinc pest (crumbling/cracking alloy) on older castings; missing box/styrofoam/certificate. Box and paperwork add a lot at the upper tiers (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Little faking at the 1:18 level; Hot Wheels Redlines get restored or reproduced wheels and repaints; brands re-issue popular castings, so check the base plate and year (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
High volume on eBay; collectors buy by brand and subject (Indy, Ferrari, Mustang, John Deere). Franklin/Danbury market is soft and saturated with estate supply; premium brands (Exoto, CMC, Carousel 1) hold value as discontinued (general knowledge, verify). Farm toys sell well in the Midwest.

## What sellers usually miss
Carousel 1 / Exoto / CMC bases in a "diecast collection $5 each" lot. Franklin/Danbury Mint sellers overprice ("paid $150") – those are at best $60.

## Shipping / handling
1:18 in box ~3–4 lb, $15–20 each way; damage-prone (mirrors/antennas) – pack in original styrofoam. eBay 13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State brand, scale, model/year, edition number, box/styrofoam/certificate present, any broken or missing small parts. Photograph the chassis plate, all sides, the interior and the box. Title: brand + scale + car + year + "boxed"/"with certificate" (general knowledge, verify).

## Buy rule
Buy when: Carousel 1 / Exoto / CMC under $50; Danbury Mint 1:12 or truck series under $40 boxed; lots ≤ $5/car showing premium bases. Pass when: Franklin/Danbury 1:24 over $30, Maisto/Bburago/promos at any price, pickup-only sellers. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Fits a passenger seat.
