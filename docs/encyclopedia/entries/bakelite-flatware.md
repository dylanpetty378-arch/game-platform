# Bakelite-handled flatware & kitchen tools
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold memory 2024-25, collector tells (Simichrome/hot-water test)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1930s–50s stainless/chromium-plate knives and forks with moulded phenolic handles in butterscotch, marbled yellow/brown, cherry red, green, black. Real bakelite: deep saturated colour, marbling, no seam lines, heavy; smells of formaldehyde under hot water. Lucite/plastic is translucent and light. Seller words: "bakelite flatware", "butterscotch handles", "vintage silverware".

## What sellers usually miss
Colour rarity (red/green > butterscotch) and whether there are serving pieces. Many sellers hedge ("says bakelite online").

## Shipping / handling
2–3 lb, cheap to ship (~$10). eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 24+ pc marbled or red/green set ≤ $25. Pass when: 12-pc butterscotch at $25+ – profit ≈ $20.
