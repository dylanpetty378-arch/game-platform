## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1930s–50s stainless/chromium-plate knives and forks with moulded phenolic handles in butterscotch, marbled yellow/brown, cherry red, green, black. Real bakelite: deep saturated colour, marbling, no seam lines, heavy; smells of formaldehyde under hot water. Lucite/plastic is translucent and light. Seller words: "bakelite flatware", "butterscotch handles", "vintage silverware".

## Makers, brands and variants
Most sets are unmarked; blades may carry stamps such as "Stainless", "Chromium Plate", or "USA" (general knowledge, verify). Variants by value: plain yellow → butterscotch/marbled → two-tone/"end-of-day" marbled, cherry red, green → carved/figural handles and Art Deco fitted-box sets. Serving pieces and kitchen tools (spatulas, ladles, egg beaters) add to sets.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Bakelite: heavy, opaque-to-marbled, no mould seams, formaldehyde smell under hot water; Simichrome polish turns yellow on a cotton swab (common collector test, general knowledge, verify). Lucite/modern plastic is lighter and translucent. Handles are attached with pins or a tang; tight, unloose handles are better. Plated blades wear through to base metal.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Worn plating, pitted/rusty blades, loose or cracked handles, dishwasher-faded handles (dull, chalky). Mismatched colours in a "set" reduce value. Polishing handles restores gloss (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
"Fakelite" (modern resin imitating bakelite) exists mainly in jewellery; in flatware the risk is plastic/lucite sold as bakelite – sellers often hedge ("says bakelite online") (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Buyers are mid-century kitchen collectors, retro decorators, and Etsy shoppers; Etsy may suit colourful sets (general knowledge, verify). Steady, modest volume.

## What sellers usually miss
Colour rarity (red/green > butterscotch) and whether there are serving pieces. Many sellers hedge ("says bakelite online").

## Shipping / handling
2–3 lb, cheap to ship (~$10). eBay 13%.
