## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Mattel 1:64 die-cast cars 1968–77 with a **red stripe on the tire sidewall**. 1968–72 cars have **Spectraflame** paint — translucent candy colours over polished zinc (aqua, blue, gold, magenta, red, green, orange, purple, pink, brown, antifreeze, olive). 1973–77 cars have opaque enamel paint, often with tampo decals. Metal base stamped "©1967/68/69 Mattel Inc" + "Hong Kong" or "U.S.A.". Wheel hubs are cap-style with the red line.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **1968 "Sweet 16"**: Custom Camaro, Beatnik Bandit, Custom Barracuda, Python, Silhouette, Hot Heap, Custom T-Bird, Deora, Ford J-Car, Custom Cougar, Custom Corvette, Custom Firebird, Custom Mustang, Custom Eldorado, Custom Fleetside, Custom Volkswagen.
- **1969–72**: Classic '31 Ford Woody (1969), Boss Hoss (1970; Club Kit chrome mail-in 1971 with stripes), Heavyweights, Spoilers, Twinmill, Sizzlers (motorised).
- **1973–77 enamel**: low production 1973 cars are rarer.
- Colour, US vs HK base, interior colour and blister/button collectibles drive value.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Bright, glossy spectraflame with no toning = high grade. Wheel condition (straight axles, red line intact), windows clear, no paint loss on edges. Grade C9–C10 sells multiples of C6–C7.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Paint chips on edges (play wear), toning/fading, broken/bent axles, replacement wheels, reproduction parts, restored (re-plated/re-painted) cars — common and must be disclosed; restored cars sell much lower.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Hot Wheels Classics/Vintage reissues and "Real Riders" with redline tyres (2000s) — bases dated 2000s, Malaysia/China. Reproduction wheels and drilled/riveted bases indicate restoration.

## Market
Deep, liquid collector market; sells within days–weeks at fair prices. Condition grading drives price.

## What sellers usually miss
Owners don't distinguish redlines from later blackwalls, and don't know the colour/casting premiums. Garage-sale "old Hot Wheels" cases often hide redlines.

## Shipping / handling
Tiny; padded mailer $5. Wrap each in tissue to protect paint.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Four-side and base photos, wheel close-ups, note any restoration, US/HK base, colour.
