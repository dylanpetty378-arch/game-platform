## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hand-cranked pair of rubber rollers in a wood (pre-1930s) or cast-iron/steel frame, with tension screws on top, a gear train and a crank. Clamp-on models fix to a tub rim; bench models fold out with tub supports. Electric washing machines (Maytag, Speed Queen) had powered wringer heads.
Tells of age/value: wooden frame with black stencilled maker name and number on the top bar ("592", "12x1¾" = roller size), wooden drip board with maker name ("Horse Shoe Brand ... Wringer"), cast-iron tension screws with butterfly tops, horseshoe logo stamp. Steel-frame 1930s–50s are less wanted.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **American Wringer Co.** (New York, 1880s–1950s): Horse Shoe Brand, Eureka, Bicycle, Anchor (also Lovell), "Pioneer". Horseshoe-and-oval logo; size codes (e.g., No. 592, 12×1¾ rollers).
- **Lovell Manufacturing Co.** (Erie PA, 1881–1990s): Anchor Brand, Lovell; also made wringer heads for electric washers.
- **Metropolitan, Universal (Landers Frary & Clark), Keystone, Superior, Royal, Dowagiac**: other US makers.
- Wringer benches: often unbranded folding wooden stands sold with wringers (Lovell "Bench Wringer").

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original rubber rollers crack and harden; buyers are décor collectors so working rollers matter less. Hardwood (maple) frame with intact stencil = best. Repaint or replaced rollers reduce value. Rust on cast parts is acceptable.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Cracked/flattened rollers, missing crank (very common), broken clamp, split wooden bars, lost stencil, broken gears. Electric washer heads are often greasy.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Few fakes; Amish-made new hand wringers exist (Lehman's), and modern repro "décor" wringers. Steel Lehman's wringers are user tools, not antiques.

## Market
Local farmhouse-décor market (Facebook, antique malls). eBay sales are slow because of weight. Spring-summer peaks.

## What sellers usually miss
Owners overvalue them (Lens shows $500–$1,000 asking). Undervaluation is rare.

## Shipping / handling
15–25 lb, awkward; $35–60 ground. Benches are freight/local only and exceed the passenger-seat rule.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Maker, model number, roller size, crank present, rollers' condition, clamp works, stencil legibility, dimensions.
