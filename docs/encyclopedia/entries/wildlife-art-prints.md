# Wildlife / rural art prints (Terry Redlin, Bev Doolittle, Thomas Kinkade etc.)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: appraisily Terry Redlin auction profile (141 lots, median ~$80), eBay listings Oct 2026

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Framed lithographs, usually double-matted in oak/cherry frames, 1985–2005. Redlin: twilight farm/lake scenes, warm lit windows. Tells for the valuable version: pencil signature **and** edition number (e.g. 1234/9500) in the lower margin, often a remarque. Open-edition / "licensed" prints have a printed signature only. Seller words: "Terry Redlin print", "framed Redlin", "signed".

## What sellers usually miss
Whether the pencil number exists. Many sellers just say "Redlin print". Check the lower margin photo.

## Shipping / handling
Framed under glass 30x40" – $60–100 to ship, glass breakage. Only worth it local or if S/N and under $40.

## Buy rule
Buy when: pencil-signed & numbered confirmed in photo, price ≤ $40, local or driveable. Pass when: open edition, or seller quotes eBay asking prices (knows).
