# Vintage stereo receivers (Sansui, Pioneer, Marantz, Lafayette)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge 2024–25 and hifishark.com averages (Sansui R-70 $100–140; 8080/9090 $450–800; Pioneer SX-1250 $900+; Marantz 2270 $700–1,000; Lafayette LR-100 $40–70).

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1970s "monster" receivers: brushed-silver face, big tuning dial, wood case, weight 40–60 lb – Sansui G-series/9090/8080, Pioneer SX-x50 (SX-1050/1250/1280), Marantz 22xx (blue-lit dial, gyro-touch tuning wheel), Kenwood KR-9600. 1979–85 "slim" silver receivers (Sansui R-30/50/70, Pioneer SX-5, Technics SA-xxx) are 20 lb, low watt, worth $80–150. Model number is on the right of the dial glass or rear plate. Sellers write "vintage receiver", "Sansui receiver", "stereo", "in storage".

## What sellers usually miss
Model number – "Nice Sansui receiver" with one photo. Marantz 22xx are rarely mis-listed. "Been in storage 20 years" = untested; capacitors/lamps usually need work, which Dylan does not do, so only tested units qualify.

## Shipping / handling
Monster receivers 45–60 lb, double-box with foam – $60–90 and real damage risk; slim receivers 20 lb, $35. Resell on eBay/Audiogon/Audiokarma.

## Buy rule
Buy when: tested working Marantz 22xx, Pioneer SX-x50 (1050+), Sansui 8080/9090/G-series ≤ $200. Pass when: untested/"in storage", slim 1980s models, Lafayette/Realistic. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
