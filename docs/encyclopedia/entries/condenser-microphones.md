# Large-diaphragm condenser microphones (Rode, AKG, Neumann, Audio-Technica, Shure)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: equipboard Rode NT2 page (Reverb used listing $159, 3 available, 2026-10), Reverb NT2-A product pages, sweep listing photos (Rode NT2 original, Boise $75), general Reverb/eBay sold knowledge.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Fat cylindrical body (40–60 mm) with a mesh basket, usually on a shock mount; model name on the body under the logo. Needs 48 V phantom power. Tells:
- **Rode NT2 (original, 1997–2003)**: satin nickel body, three slide switches on the front (pattern/pad/HPF), "NT2" under the Rode logo, serial engraved on the lower barrel. Replaced by **NT2-A** (2004+, silver with "NT2-A", switches in a row). **NT1** original (satin) vs **NT1-A** (silver, 2004) vs **NT1 5th gen** (black, 2023). **NTK/K2** tube mics $250–400. **Rode Classic / Classic II** tube $800–1,500.
- **AKG C414**: EB (1970s, brass capsule CK12) $1,200–2,000; B-ULS $400–600; XLS/XLII $500–800. **C12VR** $2,500+. **C3000/C2000** $100–200.
- **Neumann**: U87 (any) $1,500–3,000; U87 Ai $2,000+; TLM 102/103 $400–800; KM84 $800+; U47/U67 $5,000–15,000.
- **Audio-Technica**: AT4050 $400–500, AT4040 $200–250, AT2020 $50–70, AT2035 $80–100.
- **Shure**: KSM44 $400–500, KSM32 $300–350, SM7B (dynamic) $280–330.
- **sE, Warm Audio WA-87/WA-47jr** $250–500; **MXL, Blue Spark/Bluebird** $50–150; **Blue Yeti** (USB) $40–60.

## What sellers usually miss
"Studio microphone", "recording mic" with no brand – a C414 or TLM103 in a $50 lot. Generic shock mount + "condenser mic $40" is worth a close look at the body engraving. Also tube mics listed without their power supply ($0) vs with ($300+).

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb in case; $12–18 each way. Reverb is the market (8% + processing); eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: AKG C414 under $200, AT4050/KSM44 under $150, Neumann TLM under $300, Rode NTK/K2 with PSU under $120. Pass when: Decent-tier mics over $60 (Rode NT1-A/NT2/NT2-A, AT2035), USB mics at any price. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
