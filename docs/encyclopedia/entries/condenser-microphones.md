## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Fat cylindrical body (40–60 mm) with a mesh basket, usually on a shock mount; model name on the body under the logo. Needs 48 V phantom power (tube mics need their own power supply). Tells:
- **Rode NT2 (original, 1997–2003)**: satin nickel body, three slide switches on the front (pattern/pad/HPF), "NT2" under the Rode logo, serial engraved on the lower barrel. Replaced by **NT2-A** (2004+, silver with "NT2-A", switches in a row). **NT1** original (satin) vs **NT1-A** (silver, 2004) vs **NT1 5th gen** (black, 2023). **NTK/K2** tube mics $250–400. **Rode Classic / Classic II** tube $800–1,500.
- **AKG C414**: EB (1970s, brass capsule CK12) $1,200–2,000; B-ULS $400–600; XLS/XLII $500–800. **C12VR** $2,500+. **C3000/C2000** $100–200.
- **Neumann**: U87 (any) $1,500–3,000; U87 Ai $2,000+; TLM 102/103 $400–800; KM84 $800+; U47/U67 $5,000–15,000.
- **Audio-Technica**: AT4050 $400–500, AT4040 $200–250, AT2020 $50–70, AT2035 $80–100.
- **Shure**: KSM44 $400–500, KSM32 $300–350, SM7B (dynamic, not a condenser) $280–330.
- **sE, Warm Audio WA-87/WA-47jr** $250–500; **MXL, Blue Spark/Bluebird** $50–150; **Blue Yeti** (USB) $40–60.

## Makers, brands and variants
See the list above for model-by-model values. Key multipliers: vintage AKG C414 EB with brass CK12 capsule; Neumann anything (U-series especially); tube mics with original power supply and cable; matched pairs; original cases/boxes. Rode is Australian; AKG Austrian (now Harman); Neumann German (Sennheiser-owned) (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Engraved serial numbers and model names on genuine bodies; solid metal bodies and grilles; quality shock mounts. Cheap clones have printed (not engraved) logos, light bodies and generic mounts. Capsule replacement history matters on vintage pro mics.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dropped mics (dented grille, damaged capsule – noise or dead), humidity-damaged capsules (crackling), missing tube power supply (huge value drop), corroded XLR pins. Testing needs phantom power and an interface – ask for a recording or video test. Ask for serial-number photo.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Neumann U87 and AKG C414 bodies and Chinese clones with copied branding exist; Shure SM7B fakes too (general knowledge, verify). Check serials with the maker and inspect engraving and internal boards.

## Market
Liquid: Reverb is the main market (about 8% fees with processing), eBay second. Buyers: home-studio musicians, podcasters, studios. Prices for mid-tier mics are stable; boutique/vintage pro mics hold value (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
"Studio microphone", "recording mic" with no brand – a C414 or TLM103 in a $50 lot. Generic shock mount + "condenser mic $40" is worth a close look at the body engraving. Also tube mics listed without their power supply ($0) vs with ($300+).

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb in case; $12–18 each way. Reverb is the market (8% + processing); eBay 13%. Pack in the case with foam; protect the capsule from shock.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** brand + model + generation ("Rode NT2 original", "NT2-A", "AKG C414 EB") + "large diaphragm condenser microphone" + "with shock mount/case/PSU"; Junk: "studio mic", "recording microphone", "podcast mic". Example: `Rode NT2 Original Large Diaphragm Condenser Microphone w/ Shock Mount Tested`.
- **Where:** Reverb first (main market, ~8% with processing), eBay second; tube mics only with the power supply. No local market worth the discount.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (NT2 original $170; C414 EB $200 median, p75 $900; U87 by serial), accept −10%, floor p25; OBO; cut 10% after 3 weeks.
- **Photos:** first photo the mic in its mount, logo facing; then model engraving, serial, pattern/pad switches, grille (dents), XLR pins, case/PSU/cable/box, a screenshot or clip of a test recording with phantom power.
- **State:** model, generation, serial, tested with phantom power (attach sample), pattern/pad/roll-off switches work, included mount/case/PSU/cable, cosmetic condition, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 1–2 lb in its case with foam, single sturdy box with fill around the case; protect the capsule from shock; $12–18.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Rode NT2] [original Australian run] — new a [NT2-A] is [$X]; this one's tested on phantom power with a clean sample and the original mount, no capsule noise."

## Buy rule
Buy when: AKG C414 under $200, AT4050/KSM44 under $150, Neumann TLM under $300, Rode NTK/K2 with PSU under $120. Pass when: Decent-tier mics over $60 (Rode NT1-A/NT2/NT2-A, AT2035), USB mics at any price. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat.
