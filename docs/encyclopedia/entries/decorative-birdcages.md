## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Ornamental cages used as décor, not for birds. Most on Craigslist are 1990s–2020s décor imports (distressed paint, rusted-finish wire tops with metal butterfly/bird ornaments, faux bamboo). Value tells:
- **Chinese songbird cages** (Qing to mid-20th c): round or dome-shaped, thin bamboo bars, carved hardwood base ring, hook at top (brass or carved), tiny porcelain feeding cups (often blue and white), brass fittings. Small (10–16" tall).
- **Hendryx** (New Haven CT, 1870s–1950s): brass or painted wire cages with a stamped "Hendryx" seed guard; 1950s brass dome cages with stands.
- **Victorian architectural cages**: hand-made wood-and-wire models of houses/churches; large; local sale only.
- **Pagoda bamboo cages**: tiered, cheap, 1970s–2000s Asian import décor (glued thin bamboo).

## Makers, brands and variants
Hendryx, Prevue (1960s+ wire, still made), Crown, Andrew B. Hendryx Co.; Chinese provincial songbird cages (Beijing/Shanghai styles); Mexican painted wooden cages; décor brands Pier 1, Hobby Lobby, Ballard, Grandin Road.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Old Chinese: smooth, darkened bamboo, mortised joints, porcelain cups with marks, hand-forged brass.
- Modern décor: MDF/pine with distressed paint, rough staples, rusted-look metal tops with cut-out ornaments, glued joints.
- Hendryx: soldered brass, stamped name, removable base tray.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken/missing bars, missing feeders and hooks, split bamboo, loose joints, dents in brass domes, missing doors.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Large volume of new "antique Chinese" cages with aged finishes; distressed "Victorian" décor cages (the Lens matches to $300+ are new retail from bol.com etc.).

## Market
Weak and local for décor cages (bulky, cheap new). Chinese songbird cages sell on eBay and LiveAuctioneers in weeks. Weddings drive some décor demand (card holders) in spring.

## What sellers usually miss
- The porcelain feeding cups inside old Chinese cages are often worth more than the cage.
- Hendryx stamps on the seed guard.

## Shipping / handling
Bulky and crushable: oversized boxes $25–50; many exceed passenger-seat size. Fragile bamboo.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Dimensions, materials, age evidence, fittings present (cups, hook, door, tray), honest note that décor cages are not for live birds.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − shipping both ways (~$40–60) − price ≥ max($50, price). Décor cages never qualify. Only small (≤ 16") antique Chinese songbird cages with porcelain cups at ≤ $40 qualify. Pass on everything else.
