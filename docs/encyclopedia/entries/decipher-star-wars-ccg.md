## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Decipher's Star Wars Customizable Card Game, 1995–2001 (general knowledge, verify). Cards are 2.5×3.5" with a Star Wars card back; fronts show film stills with Light Side (white/light) or Dark Side (black/dark) colouring, deploy/forfeit numbers and an expansion icon. First print "Limited" sets have black borders; "Unlimited" reprints have white borders (general knowledge, verify – the old summary says "black-border card backs", but the border distinction is on the card fronts). Sellers write "Star Wars cards", "Star Wars CCG", "Decipher", "box of Star Wars cards", "1995 Star Wars cards". Not to be confused with Topps Star Wars trading cards or Wizards of the Coast Star Wars TCG (2002–05).

## Makers, brands and variants
(general knowledge, verify)
- **Decipher Inc.** (Norfolk, VA) under Lucasfilm licence.
- Expansions: Premiere (Limited black-border / Unlimited white-border), A New Hope, Hoth, Dagobah, Cloud City, Jabba's Palace, Special Edition, Endor, Death Star II, Tatooine, Coruscant, Theed Palace; plus Reflections I–III foil sets, Enhanced sets, Official Tournament Sealed Deck, 2-player intro sets and starter decks.
- Later expansions (Death Star II through Theed Palace) had smaller print runs and higher card values; Reflections foils and key rares carry value.
- Sealed booster boxes (especially Limited printings and late sets) are the top tier.
- Players' Committee "virtual" cards are free print-outs – no value.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Standard card stock; foils in Reflections. Sealed product: factory shrink with Decipher branding; booster boxes contain 36 or 60 packs depending on set (general knowledge, verify). Played cards show edge whitening and sleeve wear.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Played/creased cards, water damage, smoke odour, binder dents. Sealed boxes: resealed shrink is the main risk. Ask for photos of rares, box seals and set symbols.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Resealed booster boxes/packs and proxy printed rares exist (general knowledge, verify). Check shrink seams and Decipher logos on wrap.

## Market
Small, dedicated player/collector community (the Players' Committee still runs the game); sealed product and late-set rares sell on eBay and in the community. Nostalgia demand rises with Star Wars releases (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Which expansion and printing (Limited black border vs Unlimited white border), late-set rares mixed into "box of Star Wars cards", Reflections foils (general knowledge, verify).

## Shipping / handling
Light: a 1,000-card box lot is ~3–4 lb, $10–18. Sealed boxes in a padded box (general knowledge, verify).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Star Wars CCG Decipher" + expansion name + "Limited black border"/"Unlimited" + "rare"/"foil"/"Reflections" + "sealed"/"booster box" + card count. Junk: "Star Wars cards lot", "trading cards", "vintage". `Star Wars CCG Decipher Death Star II Limited Rares Lot 40 Cards NM`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (list rares individually, bulk commons as a box lot); the Players' Committee community for sealed product. Nostalgia bump with Star Wars releases.
- **Price position:** at the sold median per rare or set (unknown here; $50–300 boxed sets/rares), accept −10%, floor 80%; OBO; cut 15% after 30 days; commons in bulk, firm.
- **Photos:** rares laid out individually front and back; set symbols close up; black vs white border edge; box seals for sealed product; foils under angled light; bulk box with count.
- **State:** set names, printing (Limited/Unlimited), counts by rarity, list of rares/foils, condition grade (NM/LP), sealed vs opened, played decks noted, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** shoebox; 1,000-card box lot 3–4 lb $10–18; rares in sleeves and toploaders, sealed boxes padded in a box, no double-box needed.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Decipher Star Wars CCG [expansion] [Limited] rares, [count], NM from a [1990s] collection; [expansion] had a small print run and singles run [$] each, so the lot is priced at [price]."
