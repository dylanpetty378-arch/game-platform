# US silver certificates & common silver coins (Morgan dollars, Mexican pesos, proof sets)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: spot silver + grey-sheet knowledge, eBay sold memory 2025

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Silver certificates: blue seal + blue serial numbers, $1 (1935, 1957 series), $5 (1934, 1953), $10 (1934, 1953). Star notes (★ in serial) and 1928/1934 $1 "Funnyback" are the premium ones. Morgan dollars: 1878–1921, 0.7734 oz silver; value = date/mint (CC mint, 1893-S, 1889-CC) + grade; graded by PCGS/NGC = trusted, ANACS/other = discount. Mexico 1947–48 5 pesos Cuauhtemoc: 30 g .900 = 0.868 oz ASW. US Mint proof sets 1980s–90s: $5–15 each (1989 ~$6). Seller words: "silver dollar", "old money", "blue seal dollar".

## Comps (sold, dated)
- 2025 eBay sold memory: 1935E $1 silver cert circulated $2–4; 1934D $5 cert VF $10–15; ANACS MS61 common Morgan $55–70

## What sellers usually miss
Nothing – coin sellers almost always overprice. The only arbitrage is key dates or star notes mislabelled, or bulk "old coins" lots sold by weight.

## Shipping / handling
Flat, cheap, insured. eBay 13% (coins category ~13%). Must authenticate before paying; counterfeit Morgans are rampant.

## Buy rule
Buy when: silver priced at or below melt (lots sold by weight), or a key-date/CC Morgan in a slabbed holder under grey-sheet. Pass when: single common silver cert priced over $5 or Morgan over $45 raw.
