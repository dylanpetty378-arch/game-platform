## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Cast-iron frame with swivel arm and guides holding a long backsaw (24–30 in); quadrant with angle stops; wood base; corrugated or flat fences.
- **Stanley**: "Stanley" cast on frame, No. 346/358/360; flat fences.
- **Millers Falls Langdon Acme**: corrugated fence and swivel, "Langdon" casting.
- **Goodell-Pratt**: similar, "GP" marks.

## Makers, brands and variants
Stanley (New Britain CT), Millers Falls (Langdon), Goodell-Pratt, Sargent, Disston (saws).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original Disston/Stanley saw with medallion; complete stops and guides; crisp castings.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Missing stops, cracked castings, rusted saws; missing saws halve value.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
None.
