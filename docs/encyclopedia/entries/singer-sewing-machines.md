## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Vintage Singer domestic machines, in two forms:
- portables: a head in a carry case or a bentwood "coffin" case
- heads set in a cabinet or treadle stand

The serial number is stamped on the bed. The prefix letters (AE, AF, AG, AH, AJ, AK, AL, AM, EE, EF, EG, EH, EJ, etc.) date the machine via the Singer serial database (general knowledge, verify). The model number is on a plate or tag, or must be identified by shape.

- **221 Featherweight** (1933–1964, US Elizabethport): small, about 11 lb, black (rarely white or tan 221K), scroll faceplate, fold-up bed extension, square carry case.
- **222K** (Scotland): free-arm version of the Featherweight.
- **15-91**: full-size black, potted motor behind the hand wheel.
- **201**: full-size, gear-drive, often brown/black. The 201-2 is the potted-motor version.
- **301**: slant-shank, beige or black long-bed.
- **500 Rocketeer**: 1960s slant-needle, space-age styling.
- **66 and 99**: Redeye/decal machines, the 99 being three-quarter size, often in bentwood cases.

Sellers write "vintage Singer", "antique sewing machine", "Singer in cabinet".

## Makers, brands and variants
All information below this line is general knowledge, verify.
- **221 Featherweight**: the money machine, $250–400 (median ~$335). The white/pale-green 221K (UK, 1960s) and the 1930s Centennial badge (1951) and scroll faceplates bring more. The 222K free-arm is $800+ (verify).
- **15-91**: $75–150 for the head, liked by quilters. The 15-88/15-90 are related.
- **201 / 201-2**: $40–100 per the summary; good gear-drive users' machines.
- **301 / 301A**: often $150–300 per general knowledge. Not covered by the earlier sweep summary, so verify.
- **500/503 Rocketeer**: $100–200.
- **66 / 99**: $40–100. Decals (Redeye, Sphinx, Lotus) and condition drive value.
- **Treadle and cabinet machines** (27, 66, 127, 128 etc.): low value as furniture-sized items. Excluded by the size rule.
- **Toy Singer Sewhandy (20)**: a separate small collectible (verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Cast-iron bodies (aluminium on the Featherweight and 301), black japanned finish with gold decals, chrome or scroll faceplates.
- Quality tells: decals intact (not silvered or worn), a smooth hand wheel, original motor and foot controller, bobbin case present.
- Bakelite foot controllers on 221/201s are period-correct. A frayed original cord needs rewiring (safety).

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Value loss: worn decals, a seized mechanism (from old oil), cracked foot controller, frayed wiring, missing bobbin case or accessories, and a damaged case.
- For Featherweights, a complete case, attachments and the manual add value. Look for cracks on the bed extension hinge.
- Ask for a video of it sewing or turning freely, the serial number, and photos of the decals and case.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake Featherweights are not common. Repainted "custom" Featherweights are a separate, decorated market. Reproduction or replacement cases and parts are common (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Quilters and sewists, especially for Featherweights, plus vintage-machine collectors. Featherweights sell quickly on eBay, Etsy and FB groups. Full-size heads sell best locally because of weight. There is a steady quilting-driven demand, with winter as quilting season (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Model numbers on full-size heads: a 15-91 or 201 gets listed as "old Singer". Featherweight sellers usually know the name. The serial date and accessories (attachments in the green box, manual) are often left out (general knowledge, verify).

## Shipping / handling
Featherweight in case ~15–17 lb shipped, $30–45. Pack the head out of the case or blocked inside it so it cannot shift. Full-size heads 30–40 lb shipped cost $50–80 and are prone to cracking cast parts. Treadles and cabinets are not shippable and are excluded (general knowledge, verify).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Title: "Singer [model] [year from serial] Sewing Machine [Featherweight/head] Serviced Sews". State the serial and year, that it was tested sewing, the motor and foot controller condition, the accessories included, decal condition, and whether it was serviced or rewired. Photograph the decals, bed, serial, bobbin area and case.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a Featherweight is under ~$120, or a 15-91 is under $40. Pass when: everything else, unless free. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat: portables and heads do; treadles and cabinet machines are bigger and excluded.
