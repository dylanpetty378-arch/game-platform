# Vintage plastic model kits (Hasegawa, Airfix, Monogram, Revell, Tamiya)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold browse Oct 2025 (sealed 1970s–80s 1/72 Hasegawa/Airfix, Monogram 1/48, Tamiya 1/35); sweep listing 2416

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Boxed unbuilt kits. Value tells: sealed/unstarted, box art era (1960s–70s Monogram/Aurora/Revell with painted box art), scale (1/48 and 1/32 aircraft, 1/24 cars, 1/35 armor > 1/72), and brand (Aurora figures/monsters, early Tamiya, Hasegawa "Minicraft" era, Airfix Series 1 bagged kits). 1980s–2000s 1/72 Hasegawa/Airfix are plentiful.

## What sellers usually miss
Aurora and 1960s box-art kits in a bin lot; everything else is a $15 kit that costs $10 to ship.

## Shipping / handling
Light but bulky; $10–15 each. Lots of listing work per dollar. eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: an Aurora/1960s Monogram/early Tamiya is visible in a lot at ≤ $5/kit average. Pass when: 1/72 1980s+ bins at ≥ $8/kit. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
