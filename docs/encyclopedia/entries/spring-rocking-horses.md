# Spring / Wonder Horses and painted wood rocking horses
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: agent knowledge of eBay/FB sold bands (verify); run12 sheet 59

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Wonder Horse (Wonder Products, 1950s–80s): moulded plastic horse on four springs in a tubular steel frame; name "Wonder Horse" moulded on the body/neck. Also Radio Flyer/Hedstrom spring horses (same market), Blazon. Separate, higher market: carved/painted wood dapple-grey rocking horses on bow rockers (English Ayres/Collinson $800–3,000) and 1950s Mobo (pressed-steel, UK) $150–400.

## What sellers usually miss
Age/model of Wonder Horse (early fibre-glass-era with glass eyes sell higher). Carved wood horses listed as "rocking horse $50".

## Shipping / handling
Frame 40"×20"×36", ~25 lb – $80+ each way; effectively local-only.

## Buy rule
Buy when: carved wood dapple-grey under $150 local. Pass when: spring horse at any shipped price. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
