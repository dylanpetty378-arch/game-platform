## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Estate clear-outs containing pressed glass (EAPG 1850–1910), Depression glass (1929–1940; thin, moulded, pastel), Elegant glass (Fostoria, Heisey, Cambridge; etched, fire-polished), 1950s–80s lead crystal (Mikasa, Gorham, Lenox, Waterford), and strays like pottery compotes. To spot hits, look for: an iridescent surface (Carnival/art glass), yellow-green glass that glows under UV (uranium), ground pontils, signatures on bases, cobalt or amethyst cut-to-clear, and unusual Depression colours (cobalt "Royal Lace", "Moderntone").

## Makers, brands and variants
EAPG: Duncan, Adams, US Glass. Depression: Jeannette, Hocking, Federal, Hazel-Atlas, Macbeth-Evans. Elegant: Fostoria, Heisey (H-in-diamond mark), Cambridge, Imperial. Art: Steuben, Loetz, Fenton, Tiffany. Pottery strays in glass lots: Lenox, Haeger, Abingdon, and English crackle-glaze pieces with gilt.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Lead crystal rings when tapped and is heavy; cheap soda glass is light and dull. Old pressed glass has soft mould seams and wear on the foot rim; reproduction glass is crisp but greasy-feeling.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips and flea bites on rims, sickness (cloudy etching inside), ground-down rims, cracks.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Depression glass reproductions (Cherry Blossom, Madrid/"Recollection"), fake Heisey marks, Fenton reissues of EAPG patterns.

## Market
Lots move slowly; single hits sell individually. Days to sell: 30–90 for common items.

## What sellers usually miss
Uranium glass (check with a UV flashlight); faint signatures; Heisey diamond-H marks; real cut crystal among moulded pieces.

## Shipping / handling
Glass is heavy and breakable: $15–30 per box, double box required. Lots are usually pickup-only on Craigslist.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Split lots into single pieces; ID the pattern via Replacements; state any chips.

## Buy rule
Price ≤ $200; profit = median × 0.87 − ship-out − ship-in − price ≥ max($50, price). Only buy when at least one piece is identified from the photo with resale ≥ ~$100 and the seller will ship (local pickup won't fit the nationwide model). Ask for bottom marks first.
