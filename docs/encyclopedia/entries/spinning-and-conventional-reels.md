# Vintage spinning and spincast reels (Mitchell, Penn, Zebco, Ryobi)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold browse Oct 2025 (Mitchell 300/301/3000, Zebco 33, Ryobi MG3); sweep listings 2542, 2619, 2194, 2820

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Egg-shaped reel hanging under the rod = spinning; closed cone with thumb button = spincast. Mitchell 300 (black, "Garcia Mitchell", made in France) is the classic – half-bail early 1950s versions and boxed examples are collectible, 1970s+ are common. Penn 700-series "greenies" and Penn Spinfisher 704/706 hold value. Zebco 33/202/404, Shakespeare, Daiwa budget = junk. Ryobi MG3 graphite (1980s) is minor collector interest.

## Shipping / handling
1 lb each, $8–10; rods don't ship economically (long box $30+). eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a visible Mitchell half-bail, boxed Mitchell, or Penn 704/706 is in a lot ≤ $30. Pass when: Zebco/Shakespeare lots, or Mitchell 300s priced ≥ $15 each. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).

## Additional research (earlier entry: Spinning and conventional fishing reels (Mitchell, Penn, ABU Cardinal, Quantum))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold listings (Mitchell 300/406, Penn 220 GTO, ABU Cardinal 3/4, Penn Senator), Mitchell Reel Museum, general tackle-collector pricing. Companion to the existing fly-reels entry.

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Spinning reels hang under the rod with a bail; conventional/level-wind sit on top. Read the foot/side plate: Mitchell (France: 300/301/308/400/406, "Garcia Mitchell" 1960s–70s = common; 1950s "half-bail" Mitchell and the 300 with "Made in France" and no "Garcia" are older), Penn (Senator, Squidder, Jigmaster, Spinfisher 700 series "greenie", International – US-made pre-1990s), ABU Garcia Cardinal 3/4/6 (Sweden 1970s – $100–250, Japan 1980s less), ABU Ambassadeur 5000/6000 (Sweden, red; 1950s "three-screw" and 1960s with leather case $100–400), Shimano Stella/Daiwa Saltiga (modern money), Zebco 33/Quantum (low). Boxes, wrenches, oil and manuals add 30–50%. Sellers' words: "fishing reel", "old reel", "Mitchell", "Penn".

- 2026 eBay sold – ABU Cardinal 4 Sweden – $120–250; Ambassadeur 5000 w/ case 1960s – $80–200

### What sellers usually miss
Sweden vs Japan on ABU; the "Garcia" prefix (post-1960) vs plain Mitchell; three-screw Ambassadeurs; boxed sets. Lots of "fishing tackle" often hide one Cardinal or Ambassadeur among Zebcos.

### Shipping / handling
1–3 lb, $10–15. eBay ~13%.

### Buy rule
Buy when: ABU Cardinal 3/4 (Sweden), Ambassadeur 1950s–60s with case, Penn International, Shimano Stella/Daiwa Saltiga at ≤ 35% of sold; tackle lots at ≤ $50 where one of those is visible. Pass when: Mitchell 300/406, Penn GTO, Senator under 114 at any price over $20. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
