## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- Spinning reel: egg-shaped reel hanging under the rod with a wire bail. Spincast: closed cone with a thumb button (Zebco). Conventional/level-wind: round reel sitting on top of the rod.
- Read the foot or side plate:
  - **Mitchell** (France): 300/301 (301 = left-hand)/308/400/406/3000. Black body, "Garcia Mitchell" on 1960s–70s reels = common. 1950s "half-bail" Mitchells, and 300s marked "Made in France" without "Garcia", are older and collectible. 302/402 = saltwater.
  - **Penn** (Philadelphia, US-made before the 1990s): Senator, Squidder, Jigmaster, 220 GTO level-wind, International (gold, lever drag), Spinfisher 700-series "greenies" (704/706, 704Z/706Z), 4400SS.
  - **ABU Garcia** (Sweden): Cardinal 3/4/6 spinning reels (Sweden 1970s worth most, Japan-made 1980s less); Ambassadeur 5000/6000 round baitcasters (red; 1950s "three-screw" and 1960s reels with a leather case are the best).
  - Modern money: Shimano Stella, Daiwa Saltiga, Van Staal.
  - Low: Zebco 33/202/404, Shakespeare, Quantum budget reels, Daiwa budget reels, modern Penn Fierce. Ryobi MG3 graphite (1980s) gets minor collector interest.
- Words sellers use: "fishing reel", "old reel", "Mitchell", "Penn", "tackle lot", "rods and reels".

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Older European reels (Mitchell, ABU Sweden, Alcedo) are cast metal with machined gears; budget reels are plastic or graphite with stamped parts (general knowledge, verify).
- Original box, wrench, oil can, spare spool and manual add 30–50%.
- Country of manufacture matters for ABU: Sweden is worth more than Japan. "Garcia" on a Mitchell means post-1960.
- Smooth, quiet cranking and a crisp bail flip are signs of a reel that has been looked after.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Common damage: seized gears from old grease, bent bail, worn anti-reverse, scratched or chipped paint, missing spool or handle (general knowledge, verify).
- Mechanical reels are easy to re-grease, but Dylan does not do repairs, so a "frozen" or "needs work" reel is valued as parts.
- Ask for photos of the foot (maker/country/serial), the bail and the spool, and a short cranking video.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Few outright fakes. The risk is reissues and later production: modern "retro" Mitchell and Penn models, and late Japanese-made Cardinals sold as "vintage Sweden" (general knowledge, verify).
- Married parts (wrong spool or handle) show up in lots.

## Market
Steady eBay volume. Thousands of common reels sell each month; collectible Mitchells, Cardinals and Ambassadeurs sell to European and US tackle collectors and to "user" fishermen. Spring (Mar–Jun) is the busiest season. Common reels are oversupplied and sell slowly at $10–30 (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
- Half-bail Mitchell 300s and Penn green 700s hidden in "old reels" lots. Everything else in a bucket lot is worth less than the shipping.
- Sweden vs Japan on ABU; the "Garcia" prefix (post-1960) vs plain Mitchell; three-screw Ambassadeurs; boxed sets.
- "Fishing tackle" lots often hide one Cardinal or Ambassadeur among the Zebcos.

## Shipping / handling
- 1–3 lb each, $8–15 shipped. eBay fees ~13%.
- Rods don't ship economically (a long box costs $30+). Buy reels, leave rods.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- State: maker, model, country of manufacture, left/right hand, serial or date code, whether it cranks smoothly and the bail flips, and what's included (box, spool, wrench, papers).
- Photograph: the foot stamping, both side plates, the bail, the handle and the spool, plus a cranking video.
- Title keywords: "Vintage Mitchell 300 Half Bail France", "ABU Garcia Cardinal 4 Sweden", "Penn 706Z Spinfisher Green", "Ambassadeur 5000 Three Screw".

## Buy rule
Buy when: a visible Mitchell half-bail, boxed Mitchell or Penn 704/706 is in a lot ≤ $30; or an ABU Cardinal 3/4 (Sweden), a 1950s–60s Ambassadeur with case, a Penn International or a Shimano Stella/Daiwa Saltiga is ≤ 35% of sold, including tackle lots ≤ $50 where one of those is visible.
Pass when: Zebco/Shakespeare lots; Mitchell 300s priced ≥ $15 each; Mitchell 300/406, Penn GTO, or a Senator under 114 at any price over $20.
Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (reels do; rods don't ship).
