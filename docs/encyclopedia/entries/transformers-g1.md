## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Transforming robot toys sold by Hasbro in the US from 1984 (moulds from Takara's Diaclone and Micro Change lines). Years and sub-lines identify the figure:
- **1984–85**: cars and planes (Optimus Prime, Jazz, Megatron gun, Soundwave tape deck, Jetfire), die-cast metal parts, "Made in Japan", chromed parts, rubber tyres.
- **1985–86**: Dinobots, Constructicons (Devastator), Insecticons, rubsigns (heat-sensitive faction symbols from late 1985).
- **1987**: Headmasters, Targetmasters (small partner figures), Fortress Maximus, Scorponok.
- **1988**: Powermasters, Pretenders (Year 1), **Seacons** (Snaptrap leader — turtle; combine into Piranacon with Seawing, Skalor, Overbite, Nautilator, Tentakil). 
- **1989**: Pretenders (Doubleheader, Pincher, Longtooth Autobots; Bludgeon, Octopunch, Stranglehold Decepticons; Monsters; Classic Pretenders), Micromasters, Ultra/Mega Pretenders.
- **1990**: Action Masters (non-transforming), Micromaster combiners.
Marks on legs/back: "© Hasbro Inc. [year] / Takara Co. Ltd [year]" with "Japan", "Macau", "Taiwan" or "China".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Hasbro** (US distributor) and **Takara** (Japan, maker; Japanese-market releases differ in colour/stickers and box).
- **Milton Bradley** (Europe), **Joustra** (France), **Estrela** (Brazil) variants — some valuable to specialists.
- **Reissues**: Takara Encore (2000s–), Hasbro Commemorative Series (2002), Walmart/Hasbro "Vintage G1" reissues (2018–2024; e.g., Optimus Prime, Soundwave, Grimlock, Jetfire, Starscream), Hasbro 2019 "Retro" packs. Reissue copyright stamps have 2000s/2010s dates or "China" with new legal text.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Plastic colour and stickers: original stickers yellow and lift; 1980s chrome dulls. Reissues have crisp new stickers and slightly different plastic shades.
- Die-cast feet/chests on 1984–85 cars.
- Completeness: every figure has a known accessory list (TFW2005/Seibertron). Pretenders include shell + inner robot + weapons (shell weapon and robot weapon); Seacons have two weapons (gun/missile/trident-type) and combiner hands/feet pieces.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken thumbs and hinges (famous weak points: Grimlock thumbs, Soundwave battery door, Jetfire missiles), loose joints, stress marks (white plastic), yellowed white plastic, chrome wear, missing stickers or damaged ones, sun fading, chewed parts. Replacement reproduction accessories and stickers sold on eBay — note in listings.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Chinese knock-offs (KOs) of Fortress Maximus, Metroplex, Jetfire, Optimus — off colours, blurry stamps, cheap plastic, no Hasbro/Takara stamp or a fake one.
- Reproduction accessories (3D-printed or injection-moulded) often in "complete" loose lots — slightly different colour, crisp edges, no stamp.
- Reissues as above.

## Market
Big, liquid market; nostalgia peak buyers (now 40–50 years old). Loose complete figures sell within days at fair prices. eBay is the main venue; TFW2005 forum for parts. Prices softened after the 2021 peak.

## What sellers usually miss
- Small partner figures and weapons — a Targetmaster gun or Headmaster head is often worth more than the main toy.
- Japanese Takara variants and European (Milton Bradley) variants.
- Reverse: sellers price incomplete loose figures as if complete.

## Shipping / handling
Light. Loose figures 0.5–1.5 lb $8–12; small box with bubble wrap; separate weapons in a zip bag.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Name, year, faction, sub-line, list of included accessories vs complete list, stickers condition, joints tight/loose, any breaks/stress marks, any repro parts. Photos in both modes.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold × 0.87 − ~$10 − ~$10 − price ≥ max($50, price). A $50 figure must sell for ~$140. So: buy only complete, unbroken loose figures whose sold median is ≥ 3× price, or boxed examples. Check copyright stamps (reissues sell for less). All fit in a seat; no electronics (except some Powermasters/lights — test).
