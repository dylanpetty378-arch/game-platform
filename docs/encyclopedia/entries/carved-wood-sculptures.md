## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- **Balinese (Mas, Ubud, 1930s–today)**: elongated stylised figures and busts in suar, crocodile wood, hibiscus, ebony, sawo (sapodilla); dancers, fishermen, Dewi Sri, busts with udeng headcloth and flower behind ear. 1950s–70s pieces from master workshops are elongated, finely finished, often signed with name and "Mas Bali" under base. Tourist pieces: shiny shoe-polish brown, shallow detail, stamped "Indonesia".
- **Chinese**: boxwood (huangyang, pale yellow to honey), rosewood/hongmu (dark), rootwood; Daoist immortals, Luohans, Zhongkui, Budai; 19th–early 20th-c. pieces show crisp undercut detail, softened worn high points and natural age cracks; modern ones are dark-stained with rough unfinished interiors.
- **Indian**: sandalwood (fragrant, pale) and rosewood/sheesham (dark), elephants, deities, warriors; tourist-grade mostly.
- **Andean (Peru, Ecuador)**: Inca warrior figures with shield and spear on brick-pattern plinths, flute players with poncho and hat; mahogany/cedar, 1960s–90s; $20–50.
- **Black Forest (Swiss/German, 1880s–1930s)**: bears, dogs, chamois in linden wood with knife-cut fur; valuable.
- **Anri (Italy)** and **Oberammergau (Germany)**: painted or natural linden-wood figures, signed/stamped; religious and genre.
- **Philippines/Kenya/Thailand**: monkeypod and ebony "tribal" figures and heads, airport art.

## Makers, brands and variants
- Bali: Ida Bagus Tilem, Ida Bagus Nyana, Ida Bagus Taman (Mas masters; signed, $500–5,000+); I Ketut Muja; workshop signatures.
- Chinese: no makers; age and quality drive value.
- Black Forest: Huggler (Brienz), Trauffer; Brienz school.
- Anri (Val Gardena): Juan Ferrandiz, Sarah Kay figures stamped "ANRI"; $20–150.
- Oberammergau: Lang family.
- Folk: Elijah Pierce, Wilhelm Schimmel (top tier, authentication).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Weight and density: ebony/sawo heavy; suar lighter; real boxwood is dense and fine-grained with no visible pores.
- Finish: old wax patina vs modern shoe polish or lacquer glaze; tourist pieces are uniformly stained including the base.
- Base: signatures, collection labels, old museum/dealer stickers; worn base edges.
- Detail: undercutting, fingernails, hair strands; even, sharp tool marks.
- Age cracks: radial shrinkage cracks in old wood; filled cracks in tourist pieces.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken and glued fingers, spears, ears, feathers; separated shields or weapons; cracks; insect damage (frass holes); missing parts on multi-part pieces; refinishing.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern Chinese "antique" boxwood with artificial patina and fake seals sold as Qing; Balinese tourist busts copying master styles; resin "Black Forest" bears; Anri composite resin pieces.

## Market
Liquid at low prices; collectors of Asian carvings bid well on old boxwood at auction. Tourist pieces sit.

## What sellers usually miss
- Old Chinese boxwood figures in "wood carving lots" (pale honey colour, worn detail).
- Signatures "Mas" / "Ida Bagus" under Balinese pieces.
- Black Forest bears listed as "carved bear".
- Lens hits on Etsy asks overstate tourist-piece value.

## Shipping / handling
Pad protrusions (spears, feathers, arms) and wrap separately; $15–25 each way; fits passenger seat under ~24".

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Name origin, wood species and approximate age conservatively; photograph base, signatures and any cracks; give height and weight.
