# Paul Flato compacts (1948–1950s)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: makeupmuseum.org (Flato compact history, patent Feb 1948, ads 1949–51), Heritage Auctions lot 668-39135 (signed Flato faux-pearl compact, c.1940), eBay.de active listing 126474240610 (Dogwood compact + lipstick w/ case, ask $350), Rau Antiques / Beladora (gold Flato compacts, four figures)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Paul Flato was the Hollywood jeweler (Katharine Hepburn, Rita Hayworth); after prison he launched a costume compact line in 1948. Gold-tone (sometimes silver) square or round compacts with a small jewelled figural motif: dogwood blossom, kitty with green eyes, key, teardrop, circus, bunny, crown/"crown jewels", seashell, stars. Sold with a MATCHING LIPSTICK TUBE and a cream cardboard box printed "Paul Flato" in red script. Signed "Flato" inside the lid or on the hinge. Sellers write "vintage jeweled compact", "gold compact with rhinestones", sometimes "Paul Flato" (they often know the name but not the price).

## What sellers usually miss
The "Flato" signature inside and the matching lipstick/box. A plain "jeweled gold compact and lipstick" listing at $20–40 is the arbitrage. Missing stones and plating wear halve the price.

## Shipping / handling
Under 1 lb; USPS Ground Advantage $6–10 each way. Sells on eBay (collectors) or Etsy. Fees ~13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: signed Flato (or box visible) with lipstick for ≤ $60; compact alone ≤ $35. Pass when: unsigned, heavy stone loss, or price > $100. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
