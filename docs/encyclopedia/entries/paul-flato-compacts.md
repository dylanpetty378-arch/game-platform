## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Paul Flato was the Hollywood jeweler (Katharine Hepburn, Rita Hayworth); after prison he launched a costume compact line in 1948. Gold-tone (sometimes silver) square or round compacts with a small jewelled figural motif: dogwood blossom, kitty with green eyes, key, teardrop, circus, bunny, crown/"crown jewels", seashell, stars. Sold with a MATCHING LIPSTICK TUBE and a cream cardboard box printed "Paul Flato" in red script. Signed "Flato" inside the lid or on the hinge. Sellers write "vintage jeweled compact", "gold compact with rhinestones", sometimes "Paul Flato" (they often know the name but not the price).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Paul Flato costume compacts** (patent Feb 1948; ads 1949–51): motifs — dogwood blossom, kitty with green eyes, key, teardrop, circus, bunny, crown/"crown jewels", seashell, stars. Figural (kitty, circus, key) boxed sets top the costume line.
- **Paul Flato fine jewelry** (1930s–40s, precious metal): gold Flato compacts at Rau Antiques / Beladora reach four figures — a different market.
- **Look-alikes (junk tier)**: Majestic, Elgin American, Volupté unsigned rhinestone compacts $10–40.
- Set completeness multiplies value: compact only < compact + lipstick < boxed set.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Gold-tone (sometimes silver-tone) plated brass with set rhinestones/faux pearls; signature "Flato" inside the lid or on the hinge; original powder puff and sifter add a little (general knowledge, verify). Quality tells: crisp plating, all stones present, signature visible, matching lipstick motif, red-script box.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Missing stones and plating wear halve the price. Also: broken or loose hinge, cracked/missing mirror, powder residue, dented lid, missing lipstick tube or box. Replaced stones show colour/size mismatch (general knowledge, verify). Ask for photos inside the lid (signature, mirror) and of each stone.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No known widespread fakes (general knowledge, verify). The risk is attribution: unsigned jeweled compacts listed as "Flato style" or "attributed to Flato". Only buy signed or boxed.

## Market
Niche but steady: vanity/compact collectors and Hollywood-jewelry enthusiasts on eBay and Etsy (general knowledge, verify). Low volume (~16 sold over the Terapeak window), modest prices, sells well as a gift item. The name is known, the price often isn't.

## What sellers usually miss
The "Flato" signature inside and the matching lipstick/box. A plain "jeweled gold compact and lipstick" listing at $20–40 is the arbitrage. Missing stones and plating wear halve the price.

## Shipping / handling
Under 1 lb; USPS Ground Advantage $6–10 each way. Wrap mirror side carefully. Sells on eBay (collectors) or Etsy. Fees ~13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State "signed Paul Flato", motif, c.1948–51, compact size, lipstick included and matching, original box, stone count/missing, plating condition, mirror condition. Photograph signature, inside lid, mirror, each side, lipstick tube, box with red-script logo. Title keywords: "Paul Flato Jeweled Compact Lipstick Boxed Set 1940s Crown Motif Signed".

## Buy rule
Buy when: signed Flato (or box visible) with lipstick for ≤ $60; compact alone ≤ $35. Pass when: unsigned, heavy stone loss, or price > $100. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (it does).
