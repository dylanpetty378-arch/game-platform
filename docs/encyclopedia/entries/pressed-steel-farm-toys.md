# Pressed-steel farm toys (Tru-Scale, Ertl, Carter, Doepke)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay 156942386214 (1950s Tru-Scale hay baler, $56 ask Oct 2025); eBay 205697031724 / 372645890589 (Tru-Scale hay elevators $40–90 asks); auctionzip farm-toy auction results; general knowledge of Ertl/Carter 1/16 values.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1/16-scale red (IH) or green (JD) steel tractors and implements, 1950s–70s. Tru-Scale (Carter Tru-Scale, Rockford IL): "TRU-SCALE" on the hood decal, slotted-spoke rubber tyres, red implements – hay elevator (long angled belt), baler (square box with tines), spreader, disc, plow, wagon. Ertl: cast aluminium tractors with "ERTL" under the seat. Doepke Model Toys: large 1/16 construction (Adams road grader, Barber-Greene loader) in orange/yellow – $150–400 complete. Sellers write "metal farm toys", "vintage tractor toy", "Tru-Scale" if the decal survives.

## What sellers usually miss
A bundle of "farm toys" is usually priced by the pile; the baler and elevator in a Tru-Scale lot are each worth as much as the tractor. Doepke road graders are listed as "metal toy grader" – even for parts they bring $75–150.

## Shipping / handling
Tractor ~3 lb, elevator 2 lb, baler 2 lb: a 7-piece lot is ~15 lb, $35–45 to ship in; resell individually on eBay.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Tru-Scale/Ertl lots ≤ $15/piece with original paint, or any Doepke complete ≤ $80. Pass when: repainted, heavy rust, or seller already prices per-piece at $40+. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
