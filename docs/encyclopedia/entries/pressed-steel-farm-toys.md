## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1/16-scale steel tractors and implements from the 1950s–70s, red (IH) or green (JD).
- **Tru-Scale (Carter Tru-Scale, Rockford IL)**: "TRU-SCALE" on the hood decal, slotted-spoke rubber tyres, red implements. Implements include the hay elevator (long angled belt), baler (square box with tines), spreader, disc, plow and wagon.
- **Ertl**: cast-aluminium tractors with "ERTL" under the seat.
- **Doepke Model Toys**: large 1/16 construction toys (Adams road grader, Barber-Greene loader) in orange/yellow. $150–400 complete.

Sellers write "metal farm toys", "vintage tractor toy", or "Tru-Scale" if the decal survives.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Tru-Scale / Carter Tru-Scale**: pressed steel. Tractors and implements are each $35–70 played-with. The 890/891 loader tractor is collectible ($90–200). Carter Tru-Scale boxed pieces are in the collectible tier.
- **Ertl (Dyersville, IA)**: 1950s–60s IH 560 / JD 630 originals $90–200. A 1950s JD 60 with box and the Precision series boxed reach $200–400. 1980s plastic-tyre Ertl tractors are junk tier. Modern collector issues (e.g. the Scale Models/Ertl McCormick-Deering 10-20) bring $60–90.
- **Doepke Model Toys (Rossmoyne, OH, c.1946–59)**: Adams grader, Barber-Greene loader and others. Complete examples $150–400; parts graders $75–150 (general knowledge on dates, verify).
- **Carter**: Carter Tru-Scale is the same maker as Tru-Scale. Look for boxes, which add a lot.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Originals are pressed sheet steel with tabs, factory enamel with even wear on edges, period decals, and rubber tyres (Tru-Scale slotted spokes).
- Repaints show overspray on tyres and axles, glossy uniform paint, reproduction decals that are too crisp, and paint in tab slots (general knowledge, verify).
- Ertl tractors are cast aluminium with the maker name under the seat. 1980s pieces have plastic tyres and plastic parts.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Value killers: repaints, heavy rust, missing wheels, broken belts on elevators, and missing tines or chutes.
- Played-with original paint with readable decals is acceptable and sells.
- Ask for photos of the underside, the tyres and wheels, the decals, and any repair welds.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reproduction decals and replacement tyres are sold for restorers, so restored pieces can look original (general knowledge, verify). Ertl has reissued many classic tractors as collector editions, so check for modern dates or "Precision"/"Collector" boxes. Modern issues are worth far less than 1950s originals.

## Market
Farm-toy collectors are a large, steady Midwest-centred market. Farm-toy shows and auctions drive demand, and winter and the holidays are stronger (general knowledge, verify). eBay sells individual pieces well. Local auctions are also good for buying. The buyer base is ageing, but 1950s originals hold value.

## What sellers usually miss
A bundle of "farm toys" is usually priced by the pile. The baler and elevator in a Tru-Scale lot are each worth as much as the tractor. Doepke road graders get listed as "metal toy grader", and even for parts they bring $75–150.

## Shipping / handling
Tractor ~3 lb, elevator 2 lb, baler 2 lb. A 7-piece lot is ~15 lb and costs $35–45 to ship in. Resell individually on eBay. Wrap protruding parts (elevator belts, tines).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker + model + "1/16" + "pressed steel" + piece (hay baler, elevator, loader tractor) + "original paint" + "vintage". Junk: "farm toys lot", "metal tractor", "repainted" (unless true). Example: `Vintage Carter Tru-Scale 1/16 Pressed Steel Hay Baler Original Paint Decals`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers, one piece per listing (collectors search by piece; winter/holidays stronger); FB local sells the pile for less.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median (Tru-Scale implements $35–70 each, 890/891 loader and original Ertl IH/JD $90–200, Doepke complete $150–400); accept −10%, floor −20%; OBO; cut 10% after 30 days.
- **Photos:** 1) three-quarter view on plain background; then decals close-up, underside with maker stamp, tyres, any rust/repaint spots, moving parts, box if present, ruler for length.
- **State:** maker, model, scale, original vs repainted finish, decal condition, tyres original or replaced, completeness (missing parts named), dimensions, box included, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 2–3 lb per piece; wrap elevator belts and tines, immobilize in a 12×8×8 box, 3-lb class, $12–15; lots of 7 are ~15 lb, $35–45.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Original-paint [Carter Tru-Scale / 1950s Ertl JD 630], not a repaint — decals readable in photo [2]; originals sell for [$X] and the 1980s plastic-tyre Ertls are the $15 ones."

## Buy rule
Buy when: Tru-Scale/Ertl lots cost ≤ $15/piece with original paint, or any complete Doepke costs ≤ $80. Pass when: repainted, heavy rust, or the seller already prices per piece at $40+. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (Doepke graders and loaders do).
