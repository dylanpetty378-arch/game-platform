## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hand-painted cast plaster ("chalkware") wall heads of characters, roughly 4–7" tall, with a flat back and hanging hole/loop. Bossons look: realistic, slightly caricatured male faces with crisp sculpted detail — pipes, beards, hats, uniforms — and a satin/low-sheen lacquered finish with smooth, careful paint. Back is usually stamped "BOSSONS CONGLETON ENGLAND © 19xx" with a gold/foil or paper label naming the character.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **W.H. Bossons Ltd (Congleton, Cheshire, 1946–1996)**: character heads from about 1958; designer Ray Bossons. Hundreds of characters: Kurd, Sardinian, Old Salt, Smuggler, Jock, Old Timer, Viking, Gurkha, Romany, Shogun, Tyrolean, Dickens characters (Mr Micawber, Bill Sykes, Fagin, Mr Pickwick), Mountie, Chef, Squire, Dutch girl/boy, Highwayman, Pirate. Copyright years 1958–1990s on backs.
- **Fraser Art (Bossons line, 1970s–80s)**: ivory-tone or colour figural plaques/figures; higher value.
- **Legend Products (Congleton, post-1980s)**: similar heads made by ex-Bossons staff; good but lower demand (general knowledge, verify).
- **Imitators**: Japanese/US chalkware heads, Naturecraft, hobby moulds — cruder paint, no back stamp.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Bossons: crisp sculpt, fine eye detail, consistent satin glaze, stamped back with year. Imitators: blobby detail, glossy enamel, crude eyes, no stamp. Back stamp + paper name label = most saleable.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chalkware chips easily: nose tips, hat brims, pipe stems, ears. Yellowed/smoke-stained lacquer, crazing, paint loss, missing hanging loop. Touch-ups are common; look under UV.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No modern fakes of note, but "Bossons-style" heads are sold as Bossons ($20–22 sales of "Bossons-style/like" in comps). Always check the back stamp.

## Market
Steady trickle of single sales, ~50+/yr per query; collectors are older, many in UK/Canada. Singles 20–60 days. Lots are discounted.

## What sellers usually miss
The per-head value: estate lots of 20+ heads are often priced at $1–3 each. Rare characters hidden in lots.

## Shipping / handling
0.5–1 lb each; wrap individually, double-box. Single $6–9 in USPS Ground Advantage. A 24-head lot boxed ~15 lb, $25–35.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Front, back stamp and label photo, year, height, call out every chip; group by theme (sailors, Dickens) for multi-head listings.
