## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- Hand-blown, mouth-shaped art glass in saturated colours (tangerine, sea green, jonquil, olive, cobalt, amethyst, ruby). Bubbles (seeds), pontil scars (rough or ground), slight asymmetry.
- Most pieces are unmarked; 1959–61 pieces have acid-etched "Blenko" script; foil/paper labels (often lost); recent pieces signed with year.
- Signature forms: tall floor vases (bottle shapes), decanters with oversized flame/teardrop stoppers, the #384 double-spout water bottle (since 1960s, still made), pinched and bent-neck decanters.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Winslow Anderson (1947–53):** bent-neck decanters, pinched vases.
- **Wayne Husted (1953–63):** bold large decanters, tall stoppers — most valuable MCM.
- **Joel Philip Myers (1963–70):** organic forms, floor vases (#6412, #7029 later attributed etc.).
- **John Nickerson (1970–74), Don Shepherd (1988–95):** 1970s floor vases, textured.
- **Look-alikes:** Rainbow, Pilgrim (crackle), Bischoff, Viking, Empoli (Italian), modern imports (Pottery Barn recycled glass, Spanish/Mexican recycled glass vases — thin, bluish bubbles, no pontil care).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Blenko: thick walls, pure strong colour, heavy weight; pontil mark or ground base; seeds.
- Spanish/Mexican recycled glass: greenish tint in all colours, many tiny bubbles, thin rim with rolled edge.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Base scratches (normal), rim chips (ground rims reduce value), sick glass (cloudy interior — can't clean), stopper chips/mismatched stoppers.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Blenko reissues classic designs (e.g., 384 bottle, Husted decanters in new colours). Dated signatures (e.g., "Blenko 2000") identify modern pieces (lower value).

## Market
- Strong MCM demand; decanters and floor vases sell steadily; 384 bottles are common.

## What sellers usually miss
- Unmarked Blenko sold as "green glass vase"; tall floor vases and decanters with big stoppers are the tell. Weight and pontil help confirm vs recycled-glass imports.

## Shipping / handling
- Floor vases 18–24" need large double boxes ($25–40); decanters with stoppers pack separately.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- Height, colour name, attribution reasoning (design number), seeds/pontil photos, condition.

## Buy rule
- Price ≤ $200; profit = sold median × 0.87 − ship to buyer (~$20–35) − ship to Dylan (~$20) − price ≥ max($50, price).
- A $200 decanter allows max buy ≈ $70. 384 bottles never.

## Field notes (learned in triage)
- 2026-10-13 (redo): cobalt ice-lip pitchers without a sandblast mark or label are unattributable; $40-70 even as Blenko.
