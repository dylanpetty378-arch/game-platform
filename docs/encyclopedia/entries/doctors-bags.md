## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Frame bag with a hinged steel jaw that opens wide, brass latches at both ends and a central lock, single top handle with rings. Gladstone = taller, split-open rectangular travel version. Leather types: smooth cowhide, embossed "bark grain"/"walrus grain" (regular repeating crinkle), genuine walrus (deep, irregular, thick hide with natural creases and pores, very heavy), alligator (scale pattern with umbilical-scale variation).

## Makers, brands and variants
Mostly unmarked American and English makers 1890s–1940s; trunk makers (Hartmann, Indestructo, Belber) and saddlers. Medical supply houses (Sharp & Smith, Feick Bros.) sold fitted bags with bottle loops.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Genuine walrus: thick (5+ mm), irregular deep creases following hide contours, varied texture across panels, heavy. Embossed cowhide: uniform pattern, thinner, pattern repeats. Brass hardware stamped "Pat." dates, steel frames. Inside: canvas/leather lining, bottle loops in medical versions.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dry rot and cracking, torn handles, broken latches, missing key, ripped linings, red rot (powdery leather). Restored bags with new handles are common; buyers discount.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern Indian goatskin "vintage doctor bags" with fake distress; embossed cowhide sold as "walrus". Ivory/walrus legality is not an issue for hide in most listings but some platforms flag it.

## Market
Steady decor/collector demand; good bags sell in 2–4 weeks.

## What sellers usually miss
Genuine walrus vs embossed cowhide; interior bottle-loop fittings (medical provenance adds value).

## Shipping / handling
4–7 lb, rigid frame, ships in a 18×10×12 box ~$18–25. Fits passenger seat.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "antique" + "doctor's bag"/"Gladstone bag" + hide (walrus / alligator / bark-grain cowhide) + era (1900s/1920s) + size + maker if marked + "fitted interior". Junk: "medical prop", "steampunk", "leather satchel" alone. `Antique Walrus Hide Gladstone Doctors Bag 1900s Brass Latches 16in Fitted`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (decor and collector buyers, 2–4 weeks); FB local for worn generic cowhide.
- **Price position:** at the sold median ($40–120 worn; $100–220 clean; $200–800 walrus/alligator), accept −15%, floor 75%; OBO; cut 15% after 30 days.
- **Photos:** bag closed side on; grain close up (walrus pores / alligator tiles); interior open with fittings and bottle loops; latches and lock; handle and stitching; base corners; maker stamp; next to a tape.
- **State:** hide ("walrus-grain" unless certain), length × height × depth, latches working, lock/key, handle and stitching condition, interior fittings present, odour (musty/smoke), repairs, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** carry-on, 4–7 lb rigid frame, 18×10×12 box $18–25; stuff the interior, pad the latches, no double-box needed.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[1900s–20s] [walrus/cowhide] Gladstone doctor's bag with working brass latches and the original fitted interior, [length]; embossed-cowhide copies sell for [$] and genuine [walrus] bags bring [$]."
