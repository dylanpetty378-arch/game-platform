## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Combo amps with two channels (mic + instrument), chorus/reverb, often a tan/brown or black tolex with a flat baffle. **Roland AC-33/AC-60/AC-90/AC-100** (AC-100 is the 1990s 100 W brown-grille one), **Fishman Loudbox Mini/Artist/Performer** (brown tolex), **AER Compact 60** (small black cube, the pro one), **Marshall AS50/AS100**, **Behringer/Crate** (junk tier). Sellers write "acoustic amp", "Roland amp". The model name is on the front panel; wattage and serial are on the rear plate (general knowledge, verify). Look-alikes: electric-guitar combos (one instrument input, no XLR mic input) and keyboard amps.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Roland** – AC-33 (battery-capable, small), AC-60, AC-90, and the 1990s AC-100 "Acoustic Chorus" with brown grille. AC-90/AC-100 are the ones worth buying ($250–400).
- **Fishman Loudbox** – Mini ($180–250), Artist and Performer ($300–500); brown tolex.
- **AER (Germany)** – Compact 60 (and /2, /3, /4 revisions; $500–800, $550–750 for a /3), Domino (top tier). Pro players' choice; light (14 lb).
- **Marshall AS50D/AS100D** – decent tier.
- **Schertler, Henriksen** – top tier, $800–1,500; verify before paying.
- **Crate, Behringer Ultracoustic, Kustom, Fender Acoustasonic 15** – junk/common, $30–70.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Higher-end units use birch-ply cabinets, twin-cone or cone+tweeter speakers and separate mic/instrument channels with phantom power; budget ones use particleboard and a single small speaker (general knowledge, verify). Weight is a quality proxy for the Roland range (AC-100 ~35 lb), but AER is light by design. Scratchy pots and a faint chorus are age wear on 1990s Rolands.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Common faults: scratchy pots, faint/dead chorus, crackling inputs, torn tolex or grille. A faint chorus or scratchy pot is a $30 fix that a buyer will still discount for. Untested electronics rule applies – only buy "works". Ask for a video of both channels and the effects working (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No fakes known to matter. Reissues/revisions (AER Compact 60/2, /3, /4) differ in features; state the revision (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Steady used market among gigging acoustic players, worship-band musicians and buskers; sells best on Reverb (~8%) and locally (general knowledge, verify). Local sale avoids the heavy-amp shipping problem. No strong seasonality.

## What sellers usually miss
Model number and wattage; that a faint chorus or scratchy pot is a $30 fix a buyer will discount for. Untested electronics rule applies – only buy "works".

## Shipping / handling
AC-100 ~35 lb: $60–90 ground, needs a double box. Fees eat a big share – the spread must be $200+ for a heavy amp. AER Compact 60 is 14 lb ($30) – the best shipper.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State model and revision, wattage, that both channels and all effects work (video link helps), any pot noise, and cosmetic flaws. Photograph front panel, rear plate/serial, grille, and corners. Title keywords: brand + model + "acoustic amp" + wattage + "tested working" (general knowledge, verify).

## Buy rule
Buy when: AER Compact 60 under $250; Roland AC-90/100 under $80 working; Fishman Artist/Performer under $120. Pass when: any fault described, or heavy amp priced at > 35% of resale. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (all of these do).
