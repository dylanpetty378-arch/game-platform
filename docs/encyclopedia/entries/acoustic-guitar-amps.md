# Acoustic guitar amplifiers (Roland AC, Fishman Loudbox, AER)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: Reverb/eBay sold knowledge, Guitar Center used pricing

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Combo amps with two channels (mic + instrument), chorus/reverb, often a tan/brown or black tolex with a flat baffle. **Roland AC-33/AC-60/AC-90/AC-100** (AC-100 is the 1990s 100 W brown-grille one), **Fishman Loudbox Mini/Artist/Performer** (brown tolex), **AER Compact 60** (small black cube, the pro one), **Marshall AS50/AS100**, **Behringer/Crate** (junk tier). Sellers write "acoustic amp", "Roland amp".

## What sellers usually miss
Model number and wattage; that a faint chorus or scratchy pot is a $30 fix a buyer will discount for. Untested electronics rule applies – only buy "works".

## Shipping / handling
AC-100 ~35 lb: $60–90 ground, needs a double box. Fees eat a big share – the spread must be $200+ for a heavy amp. AER Compact 60 is 14 lb ($30) – the best shipper.

## Buy rule
Buy when: AER Compact 60 under $250; Roland AC-90/100 under $80 working; Fishman Artist/Performer under $120. Pass when: any fault described, or heavy amp priced at > 35% of resale. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
