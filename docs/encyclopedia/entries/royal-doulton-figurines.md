## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Bone-china figures from Royal Doulton (Burslem) with an HN number (after Harry Nixon, 1913 to present). Base mark: the lion-on-crown backstamp, "Royal Doulton", "Made in England", the title, HN number and "© Royal Doulton" with a year. Thick, glossy, hand-painted glaze. Characters: crowd-pleasing street vendors (Mask Seller HN2103, 1953–1995), Balloon Sellers, the "Pretty Ladies" series.

## Makers, brands and variants
- Early HN (1913–1938, HN1–~HN2000): Art Deco ladies, hand-painted by artists; signed numbers, high value.
- Mid (1938–1975): character figures by Peggy Davies, Leslie Harradine, Mary Nicoll.
- Later (1975–2000s): huge volume of "Pretty Ladies", seasonal, Classics; low value.
- Production moved to Indonesia in 2004–05: "Made in Indonesia" (lower value).
- Limited editions with certificates; colour variations (same model, different HN number).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
English bone china with a heavy glaze. Older examples have finer face painting and more muted colours. Gold or printed backstamp; pre-1950s may have an artist's signature. "Potted by Doulton & Co" on very early pieces.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips on hands, fingers, hat brims and masks; restored fingers (check under UV); crazing on early ones; worn paint. Restorations are common on character figures with protruding parts.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Some Chinese copies and fake backstamps exist, but they are uncommon. Watch for "seconds": a scratched-through backstamp (factory reject) cuts value 30–50%.

## Market
Very large supply from estates; prices are down 60–80% from their 1990s peak. eBay: hundreds sold per month. Days to sell: 20–60.

## What sellers usually miss
A low HN number (under ~1500) or an artist-signed base; Art Deco 1920s–30s ladies; colourway variants with different HN numbers.

## Shipping / handling
A 9" figure needs a double box with its extremities padded; $16–22.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Photograph the base mark showing the HN number and copyright year. Disclose chips and restoration (UV check). Give the height.

## Buy rule
Price ≤ $200; profit = median × 0.87 − ~$18 out − ~$18 in − price ≥ max($50, price). Common characters at $30+ never qualify. Buy only low-HN/Deco/limited/rare colourway pieces, with no restoration, at ≤ ~30% of the sold median.
