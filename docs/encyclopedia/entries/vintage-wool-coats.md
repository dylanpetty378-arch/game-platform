## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Women's wool, cashmere or wool-blend coats from the 1940s–80s, often with fur collar/cuffs. Era tells: metal zipper-free closures, big buttons, bracelet sleeves, swing shapes (1950s–60s), union labels (ILGWU tag dates garment: pre-1955 "union made", 1955–63, 1964–74 red-white-blue).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Lilli Ann (SF, 1933–80s)**: sculptural 1940s–50s suits/coats; $300–1,500.
- **Bonnie Cashin / Sills**: leather-trimmed; $200–800.
- **Forstmann wool (fabric label)**, **Mr. Blackwell, Hattie Carnegie, Pauline Trigère**: $150–500.
- **Department store/unlabeled**: $25–100.
- **Marvin Richards**: modern (1990s–2010s) wool-cashmere with fur trim, $40–100.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Bound buttonholes, silk/rayon lining, hand-picked seams = quality. Fur: real has leather backing (part the fur); dyed fox/raccoon common on 1960s collars.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Moth holes (check under collar and arms), lining shredding, fur shedding/drying (brittle skins crack), odor, missing buttons.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern "vintage style"; check labels/union tags.

Seasonal (Sep–Jan). Fit-dependent; sizes small for modern buyers.

## What sellers usually miss
Label value; Lilli Ann and Cashin at estate sales priced as "old coat".

## Shipping / handling
Folds into a box; 4–6 lb; $15–25. Fur trim: some states' and international restrictions on certain furs.
