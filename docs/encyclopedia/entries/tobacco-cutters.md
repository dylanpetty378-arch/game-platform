Buy rule (short): Buy only advertising-marked plug cutters under ~$40; unmarked or unidentified cast pieces are a pass.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Counter-top lever cutters with cast iron base and blade, often embossed with tobacco brand advertising. Rotary shredders are rarer.

## Makers, brands and variants
Enterprise, Reading Hardware, Scotten-Dillon, P. Lorillard Climax, Star, Brown's Mule.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Embossed advertising, original paint, maker patents.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken lever, blade rust.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Many cast iron reproductions.
