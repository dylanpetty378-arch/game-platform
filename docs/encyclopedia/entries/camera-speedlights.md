## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hot-shoe flash with a tilting head. Model is printed on the front/side: Nikon SB-xxx (SB-600/700/800/900/910/5000), Canon Speedlite xxxEX (430EX, 580EX II, 600EX-RT), Godox/Flashpoint V860/TT685 (cheap new, little resale), Vivitar 283/285HV (1970s–80s, still used by film shooters), Metz 45/60 CT (handle-mount "potato masher"), Sunpak 383/544. Vintage flashes (Rollei, Sunset, Bolsey flashguns) are near-worthless except as props. Sellers' words: "camera flash", "speedlight", "flash gun".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Nikon**: SB-600 (decent), SB-700/800/900/910 (collectible tier, $90–200), SB-5000 (radio, top). SB-800 is still sought for its versatility (general knowledge, verify).
- **Canon**: 430EX/430EX II (decent), 580EX II, 600EX-RT (collectible), 600EX II-RT (top).
- **Profoto A1/A10** (top, $200–500).
- **Metz**: 45CL-4 / 45CT / 60CT handle-mount (collectible $90–200 for the 45CL-4 per ladder); see darkroom-equipment entry for Metz CT pricing overlap.
- **Vivitar** 283/285HV, **Sunpak** 383/544: film-era workhorses, $40–80 for 285HV/383.
- **Godox/Flashpoint, Yongnuo**: cheap new, little resale.
- **PocketWizard** triggers (e.g. Mini TT1) sell $40–70 – related accessory.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Plastic bodies; check the metal or plastic foot (cracked plastic foot common), battery door latch, swivel/tilt detents, and the LCD. Heavy pro use shows as worn paint on the head edges and a yellowed/scorched flash tube window. Newer models have metal hot-shoe feet and weather sealing (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Battery-door and foot cracks are the common faults. Battery corrosion from left-in AAs kills units – ask for a photo of the battery compartment. Flash tube failure or capacitor faults show only on test. Ask for a photo of the LCD lit and a test fire. Watch for "new" claims on 10-year-old models.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Canon/Nikon-branded flashes exist in small numbers, and third-party look-alikes are sometimes mis-titled (general knowledge, verify). Check fonts, serial label and the menu system.

## Market
High-volume, liquid category: pro speedlights sell weekly on eBay, KEH, MPB and Gearfocus. Buyers are working photographers and hobbyists. Prices drift down slowly as mirrorless/radio systems take over; no seasonality (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Little – model numbers are printed on the unit and sellers usually copy them. The arbitrage is only when a pro flash is priced like a generic one (≤ $40). Watch for "new" claims on 10-year-old models; battery-door and foot cracks are the common faults.

## Shipping / handling
<1 lb, $8–12. eBay ~13%. Test: power on with AAs – ask the seller for a photo of the LCD lit. Remove batteries before shipping.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Exact model and mount (Nikon/Canon), tested working with test-fire, battery compartment condition, foot condition, included items (stand, case, diffuser, box). Photograph the LCD lit, battery bay, foot and head. Title: brand + model + "Speedlight/Speedlite" + "tested".

## Buy rule
Buy when: SB-700/800/910, 580EX II, 600EX-RT at ≤ $40, or SB-5000/600EX II at ≤ $90. Pass when: priced ≥ 50% of eBay sold (the usual case). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat.
