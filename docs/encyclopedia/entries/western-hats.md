## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- Fur-felt (beaver, rabbit/hare or blends) or wool-felt hat with a tall creased crown and a wide brim; also palm and shantung straw.
- **Inside the hat** tells you almost everything: the leather sweatband (stamped maker, size, X rating), the satin liner (logo, scenes, union label), and size tags.
- **Stetson**: John B. Stetson Co., Philadelphia, 1865–1971 (factory closed; brand then licensed — western felts made by Stevens Hat Co., St Joseph MO, and later Hatco in Garland, TX). Pre-1971 sweatbands read "John B. Stetson Company Philadelphia"; the union label (United Hatters, Cap & Millinery Workers) sits under the sweatband or on the liner.
- **Resistol** (Harry Rolnick and E.R. Byer, Dallas/Garland TX, 1927): "Self-Conforming" sweatband system (a reed/strip that shapes to the head) on top grades; X ratings on the sweatband.
- **X ratings**: a fur-content/quality grade per maker; on old Stetsons 3X–5X were mid-grade and "100X", "Clear Beaver", "Royal Deluxe" were top. Modern X numbers aren't comparable across eras or makers.
- **Printed scenic liners** (cowboys, stagecoaches, horses) on Stetson 4X beavers = 1970s–1990s+, not vintage-premium.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **John B. Stetson Co.** — Boss of the Plains (1865 design), wide-brim "ten gallon" and Tom Mix-style tall crowns (1910s–30s), Open Road (1940s–60s; LBJ's hat, narrower brim), Stratoliner/Whippet (fedoras), Westerner, Gun Club, Royal Deluxe, 3X–100X; Philadelphia-made pre-1971 = collector tier.
- **Resistol** — Self-Conforming (1930s–60s), Pageant, Cattleman; 5X–100X beaver; Dallas/Garland labels.
- **Bailey** (Los Angeles, 1922) — film-industry westerns, later part of Bollman; mid-range.
- **American Hat Co., Charlie 1 Horse, Serratelli, Rodeo King** — modern (1980s–present) good hats, modest resale.
- **Custom hatters** — Rand's (Billings MT), Greeley Hat Works, Baron Hats, Montecristi straws, old Texas/Colorado custom shops; custom beaver with the shop's sweatband stamp sells to working cowboys and collectors.

### Quick ID checklist
1. Fur-felt or wool/straw? (fur felt is smooth, dense, "velvety"; wool is coarse and fuzzy.)
2. Sweatband stamp: maker, city (Philadelphia = pre-1971 Stetson), X rating, size.
3. Liner: plain satin with gold logo vs printed cowboy scene (1970s+); union label.
4. Size: 7–7⅝ sells best; under 6⅞ and over 7⅝ slow.
5. Condition: sweat stains through the crown, moth holes, brim flatness, cracked sweatband.
6. Box: original Stetson/Resistol hat box adds value.

### Golden-egg tells
- **Pre-1940 Stetson wide-brim / tall-crown "ten gallon" westerns** — ~5–10× a 1980s 4X ($400–1,500, estimate) — 5"+ brim, 6–7" crown, "John B. Stetson Company Philadelphia" sweatband, wide grosgrain band, bound edge, faded satin liner with the old Stetson crest.
- **1940s–60s Stetson Open Road (and Royal Deluxe Open Road)** — ~2–3× ($120–300, estimate) — 2½" brim, telescoping/cattleman pinch crown, narrow ribbon band, "Open Road" gold-stamped on the sweatband or liner, Philadelphia stamp.
- **Stetson 20X–100X / Clear Beaver / Royal Deluxe** — ~3–8× ($250–800, estimate) — X rating or "Royal De Luxe"/"Clear Beaver" gold-stamped inside, very smooth, heavy felt.
- **1950s Resistol Self-Conforming 5X–10X beaver** — ~2–3× ($100–250, estimate; eBay 1950s 5X Self-Conforming Cattleman listed) — "Self-Conforming" on the sweatband, Dallas label, plain liner.
- **Custom-hatter beaver westerns (Rand's, Greeley, Baron, older custom shops)** — ~2–5× ($200–600, estimate) — maker's name stamped on the sweatband or liner, 100% beaver noted, often personalised initials.
- **Original box with the hat (Stetson/Resistol)** — ~+$30–100 (see vintage-hat-boxes) — round printed box with store or maker graphics.
- No premium: straw, wool felt, 1980s–2000s Stetson 4X beaver with scenic liner ($60–110 used), Western-wear store brands.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Fur felt holds a crease, rebounds when pressed, feels dense and smooth; higher X = finer, lighter, smoother. Wool felt is coarse, stiff and pills. Old hats have hand-stitched leather sweatbands with a bow at the back, cotton or rayon-satin liners, and sometimes a leather or grosgrain bound edge. Modern hats: polyester liners, glued sweatbands, printed size tags.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Sweat stains through the crown/band (kills value for most buyers), moth holes, crushed or water-warped brims, cracked or rotted sweatbands (replaceable, but it's a repair), fading (silverbelly yellows), smoke smell, re-blocked shapes. Ask: inside photos (sweatband, liner), size, and a look at the crown from above.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake Stetson labels sewn into cheap wool hats; "Stetson" licensed wool hats sold as fur; modern hats with "vintage" liners. Check the sweatband stamping and felt quality. Not a heavy-fake market.

## Market
Steady; buyers are western-wear users (size and condition matter more than age), vintage-menswear collectors (Fedora Lounge crowd: pre-1970 Stetsons), costume/film. eBay main venue, Etsy for vintage, local western stores for consignment. Peaks before rodeo season (spring) and fall. Sizes 7–7½ sell fastest.

## What sellers usually miss
The Philadelphia stamp and union label (pre-1971), "Open Road" and high X ratings, custom-hatter stamps, and that hats can be stretched/re-sized. They overvalue modern 4X Stetsons because of the brand.

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb but bulky: a 16×16×10 box (dimensional weight) costs ~$18–28. Pack crown-down with paper inside the crown and around the brim; never let the brim carry weight. Fits a passenger seat.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Give: maker, model, X rating, size (inside measurement in cm too), brim width, crown height, colour, felt type, era tells (Philadelphia, union label), sweatband condition, stains/holes, smells. Photos: inside crown, sweatband stamp, liner, profile, top. Title: "Vintage 1950s Stetson Open Road 3X beaver fur felt western hat 7 1/4 Philadelphia".

## Buy rule
Buy when: pre-1970 fur-felt western (Philadelphia/union label, Open Road, Resistol Self-Conforming 5X+, 10X+ beaver, custom hatter) in size 7–7⅝, clean, ≤ $40, and profit = resale × 0.87 − ~$22 to buyer − ~$22 to Dylan − price ≥ max($50, price). Pass when: straw, wool, 1980s+ scenic-liner 4X over ~$25, stained or moth-holed, sizes under 6⅞. Fits a passenger seat.
