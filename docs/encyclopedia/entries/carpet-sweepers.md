## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
A box (8–14" wide) on four rubber wheels with a revolving bristle brush driven by the wheels, dust pans that tip open, and a long wooden handle on a wire bail. Melville Bissell patented his sweeper in Grand Rapids, Michigan, in 1876; Grand Rapids became the industry's center.
- Early (1880s–1910s): wooden case, often veneered (walnut, maple, burl, oak), brass or nickel corner trim, decals or gilt stencils with the model name ("Grand Rapids", "Cyco Bearing", "Gold Medal", "Parlor Queen"), cast-iron wheel housings.
- Mid (1920s–50s): painted wood or steel cases, decals, rubber bumpers, Bissell "Grand Rapids" decal.
- Late (1950s–80s): plastic and steel, pastel colors.
- Toy and salesman sample sweepers (4–7" case, short handle): Bissell "Little Queen", "Little Daisy", Grand Rapids toy; tin toy sweepers by Wolverine and Ohio Art.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Bissell Carpet Sweeper Co.** (Grand Rapids, 1876–present): models Grand Rapids, Cyco Bearing (1890s–1920s), Gold Medal, Parlor Queen; decal on lid. Most common.
- **Grand Rapids Carpet Sweeper Co.**, **Wolverine**, **Goshen Sweeper Co.** (Goshen, Indiana; "Goshen" and "Hotel" sweepers), **National Sweeper Co.** (Torrington CT), **Ewbank** (England, 1889–present), **Sweeper-Vac** (Boston; combined sweeper plus bellows vacuum, 1910s, larger, $60–150).
- **Toy/child**: Bissell toy sweepers 1890s–1930s $40–150; tin litho toy sweepers 1940s–60s $15–40.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Veneered or figured hardwood case with gilt decal and brass trim = early and decorative. Plain painted pine or plywood = later utility.
- Original decals legible; patent dates stamped inside the case or on the wheel plates (1880s–1910s dates).
- Natural bristle brush (horsehair) intact, wheels turn the brush.
- Wooden handle with original finish and turned end.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Missing or worn decals, broken bail, cracked wooden case, missing dust-pan springs, worn-out brushes, missing rubber tires, refinished cases (lower collector value), replacement handles.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Little faking. Some modern "vintage style" sweepers (Bissell still makes manual sweepers). Decorative repro decals on refinished cases.

## Market
Small, slow niche: primitives and farmhouse décor buyers, a few appliance collectors. Most sell locally for $20–50. Shipping a full-size sweeper (handle removed, 15x12x6 box plus a 4' handle, or handle omitted) costs $25–40, which kills eBay economics.

## What sellers usually miss
- Toy/child-size sweepers are the best value per box size.
- Patent dates and model decals on early cases; Sweeper-Vac's bellows mechanism.
- Reverse: sellers assume any wooden sweeper is a $100+ antique.

## Shipping / handling
Full size: unscrew handle; case alone ~5–8 lb, box 16x12x8, $18–28; handle requires a long box ($25+) — many sellers ship without handle. Toys: 1–2 lb, $10–14.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker (Bissell / Grand Rapids / Goshen / Sweeper-Vac) + model name from decal + "carpet sweeper" + "antique" + wood/oak + patent date; "toy"/"child's" if small. Junk: "primitive", "farmhouse decor", "vacuum". `Antique Bissell Cyco Bearing Oak Carpet Sweeper Patent 1890s Decal Works`
- **Where:** FB local for full-size (handle shipping kills eBay); eBay BIN with offers for toy/child sweepers and decaled early cases.
- **Price position:** at the sold median ($25–65 common), accept −20%, floor $20 local; OBO; cut 20% after 30 days.
- **Photos:** case top with decal; underside brushes and wheels; patent-date stamps; case ends/veneer; handle attached and full length; brush turning (short video).
- **State:** maker, model name from decal, case wood, width, patent dates, brush turns yes/no, decal condition, handle included or not, veneer lifting by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** passenger-seat; unscrew handle, case 5–8 lb in a 16x12x8 box $18–28, handle needs a long box ($25+) or stays home; toys 1–2 lb $10–14.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Maker model] from the [1890s/1910s] with the original decal and figured [wood] case, brush still turns; plain 1950s Bissells sell for [$] and the decaled early ones for [$]."

## Buy rule
A full-size sweeper selling for $60 nets ~$52 before ~$25 shipping each way: a loss. Buy only toy/child sweepers or premium early decorated sweepers at ≤ $20 where sold ≥ $100. Must fit in a passenger seat (handle off — it fits). Pass on 1930s+ utility sweepers.
