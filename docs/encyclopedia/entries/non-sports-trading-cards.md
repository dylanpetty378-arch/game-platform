## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Collector cards on entertainment subjects. Year is printed in the copyright line on the back/front. 1990s cards: glossy, full-bleed art, SkyBox/Impel/Fleer logos, holograms; produced in huge numbers. 1960s–70s cards: matte cardboard, gray or tan backs, gum stains, wax wrappers. Pre-war: thicker, often smaller, lithographed.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Topps (1950s–present)**: Mars Attacks (1962), Wacky Packages (1967, 1973+), Star Wars (1977–83), Garbage Pail Kids (1985–88), Batman (1966, 1989).
- **Gum Inc./Bowman (1930s–50s)**: Horrors of War, Wild West, Uncle Sam.
- **Impel/SkyBox (1990–)**: Marvel Universe Series I (1990, most valuable of the run), II (1991), III (1992), IV (1993, with "Super Heroes", "Villains", "Teams" subsets and hologram inserts), V (1994); X-Men Series 1 (1992, Jim Lee).
- **Fleer Ultra, Marvel Masterpieces (1992 SkyBox, 1994 Fleer)**: Masterpieces singles $1–10; Spider-Man/X-Men Masterpieces PSA 10 higher.
- **Comic Images (1990s)**, **Pro Set (1991)**, **Upper Deck**.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Value = scarcity + grade. Centering, sharp corners, clean edges, no creases/surface scratches. 1990s commons are only worth something as complete sets or PSA 10s of key characters (Spider-Man, Wolverine, Venom, Deadpool rookie-appearance cards e.g. 1992 Marvel Universe III Deadpool #?) — verify individual keys.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Creases, corner rounding, binder-ring damage, rubber-band dents, sun fading, gum stains (OK on 1960s–70s). Trimmed cards are worthless to graders.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reprints of Mars Attacks, Star Wars 1977, GPK; counterfeit unopened boxes (resealed). 1990s commons not commonly faked. Check card stock and print dot patterns for 1960s–70s.

## Market
Huge volume; 1990s commons have near-zero demand singly. Lots sell for pennies/card. Graded/vintage moves fast.

## What sellers usually miss
Sellers overvalue 1990s cards because of the "Marvel" name. They miss unopened 1990s boxes (MU I sealed boxes ~$200–400), pre-1985 wax packs, and early printing errors.

## Shipping / handling
Light; plain white envelope with toploader $1–5; eBay standard envelope for ≤ $20 cards.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State set, year, card numbers, condition honestly; sell commons as complete sets or lots.

## Buy rule
Profit = resale × 0.87 − ~$5 out − ~$5 in − price ≥ max($50, price). 1990s commons: always pass. Vintage/sealed only with verified sold comps; avoid "needs grading" bets (professional authentication excluded by rule).
