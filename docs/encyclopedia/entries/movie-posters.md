## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Original US one-sheet is 27×41" (pre-1985) or 27×40" (1985+), folded (pre-1985) or rolled (1985+). Originals carry an NSS number bottom right ("84/xxx") and studio/litho credits; reprints are 24×36" or have no NSS and glossy stock. Window cards 14×22" on card stock with a blank top strip; lobby cards 11×14"; publicity stills 8×10" B&W. Sellers write "original movie poster", "vintage poster", "framed".

Other formats (general knowledge, verify): inserts 14×36", half-sheets 22×28", three-sheets 41×81" (bigger formats are large and usually folded), international and re-release posters (marked "R" + year in the NSS line).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **National Screen Service (NSS)**: distributed US posters until the mid-1980s; the NSS number (year/serial, e.g. "84/xxx") and the "R"-prefix for re-releases identify originals vs re-releases (re-release detail general knowledge, verify).
- Studios and printers: litho credits at bottom (e.g. "Printed in U.S.A.", printer names) (general knowledge, verify).
- Style variants: Star Wars 1977 style A ($800+); advance/teaser vs regular styles; international versions (UK quad, Japanese B2) form separate markets (general knowledge, verify).
- Key collectible titles noted: 1984 Terminator ($75–130 sold; corrected 2026-10-27 from $200–400), Alien, Blade Runner, Jaws, Star Wars 1977.
- Video-store and commercial (retail) posters: low.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Originals: thin matte paper stock (pre-1990s), fold lines on pre-1985 one-sheets, NSS number and litho credit. Double-sided printing appears on many late-1980s+ theatrical one-sheets (general knowledge, verify). Reprints: 24×36", glossy heavy stock, no NSS, sharp uniform colours, sometimes pixelation under magnification.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Fold separations, pinholes, tape stains, tears, fading from sun, trimmed borders, water damage. Linen-backing (professional restoration) is accepted and common on older posters; amateur tape repairs reduce value (general knowledge, verify). Framed posters hide the back and size — ask for an unframed photo and measurements.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reprints are the dominant problem: 24×36" commercial reprints and 27×39/27×40 reproductions of popular titles (Terminator, Star Wars, Jaws, Scarface) sold as "original". Faked NSS numbers on reproductions exist (general knowledge, verify). Re-release posters (marked R) are worth a fraction of first release. Check size exactly, stock, fold lines and NSS line.

## Market
Deep collector market; eMoviePoster runs weekly auctions with knowledgeable buyers and lower fees, Heritage handles the top end, eBay for everything else (general knowledge, verify). Buyers: film collectors, horror/sci-fi fans, decorators (who care less about originality). Original vs reprint determines everything.

## What sellers usually miss
"Original" is used for any 27×40 poster; the 1984 Terminator original is 27×41 and folded with NSS 84/ number – a rolled 27×39 in a frame is usually a 1990s reprint. Framed posters hide the back; ask for an unframed photo.

## Shipping / handling
Rolled in a tube $15; folded in a flat mailer $8; framed – ship without glass. Resell on eBay or consign to eMoviePoster (lower fee, better buyers).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** title, year, "original one sheet" (only if it is), exact size (27x41), NSS number, "folded"/"rolled", style (A/B/advance), "single sided". Junk: "original" on a 27×40 reprint, "rare", "mint". `Terminator 1984 Original One Sheet Poster 27x41 Folded NSS 84 Style A`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for $20–200 posters; consign anything key (Alien, Star Wars 1977, pre-1960) to eMoviePoster — lower fees, better buyers; Heritage for the top end.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (Terminator 1984 $85, Star Wars style A $199 median but wide), mid-film 1970s–90s $20–80; OBO, accept −15%, floor $15; cut after 30 days or consign.
- **Photos:** full front flat; NSS line and printer credits at the bottom edge; fold lines; each corner and edge (pinholes, tears); back (single vs double sided, tape); any restoration or linen-backing.
- **State:** title, year, format and exact size, NSS number, folded vs rolled, single/double-sided, style, condition by edge/fold/pinholes, restoration or linen-backing, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** carry-on, ~1 lb; rolled in a tube $15, folded in a flat mailer $8; framed ships without glass.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "This is the [1984] [27×41 folded] original with the NSS [84/xxx] number, not the 27×39 rolled reprint; sold originals run $[75–130]."

## Buy rule
Buy when: a confirmed NSS-numbered 1970s–80s one-sheet from a cult/horror/sci-fi title ≤ $60. Pass when: framed with no back photo, 24×36/27×39 sizes, post-1995 titles. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (folded/rolled posters do; large framed ones may not).
