## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Modern kits: black or colored aluminum pen-style rotary machines, cartridge needle boxes, small ink bottles, transfer paper, practice skins - beginner bundles sold on Amazon for $40-120 new. Pro pens show logos (Cheyenne "C", FK Irons, Bishop wand shape). Vintage: iron/brass frame coil machines with two coils, springs, binding posts, often maker-stamped; hand-painted flash sheets.

## Makers, brands and variants
Pro: Cheyenne (Germany), FK Irons (USA), Bishop Rotary, Inkjecta, Stigma, Peak. Budget: Dragonhawk, Mast, Solong, Hummingbird, Wormhole. Vintage builders: Spaulding & Rogers, Percy Waters, Owen Jensen, Paul Rogers, Micky Sharpz (UK). Flash: Sailor Jerry, Lyle Tuttle, Bert Grimm - high value.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Pro pens: precise machining, branded packaging, serial numbers, replaceable stroke modules. Budget kits: generic laser-etched logos, cheap clip cords, off-brand ink. Vintage coil frames: cast or cut iron with stamped builder names.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Used needles/cartridges are biohazards and unsellable; opened ink is unsellable on eBay (and expiry-dated). Motors wear out; batteries in wireless pens degrade.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Cheyenne/FK cartridges and pens are widespread on AliExpress. Vintage machine replicas exist.
