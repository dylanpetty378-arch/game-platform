## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Rhinestone and enamel brooches, sets (parure = brooch + earrings + necklace), and cuff links. The value is in the **signature stamped on the back**, so ask for a back photo. Sellers who list "signed Boucher" know the name but usually price off old book values. For the best makers, those are below the 2024 market.

Tells by maker:
- **Boucher**: "BOUCHER" or Phrygian-cap mark, plus a 4-digit inventory number (post-1955).
- **Marboux**: Boucher's lower line.
- **Sherman** (Canada): script "Sherman", Swarovski stones, japanned or rhodium settings.
- **Eisenberg**: "Eisenberg Original/Ice", big stones.
- **Trifari**: crown-T (Crown Trifari 1950s is the good era).
- **HAR**: oriental and genie figurals.
- **Hobé**: double-struck mark, sterling.
- **Schiaparelli**: script signature, lava/moulded glass.
- **Juliana / DeLizza & Elster**: unsigned. Identified by 5-link construction and open backs.
- **Common makers** ($10–40): Weiss, Lisner, Coro, Sarah Coventry, Avon, Napier, Monet.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Quality pieces have prong-set (not glued) stones, foiled-back rhinestones and crisp castings.
- Rhodium or japanned (black) plating, sterling on 1940s wartime pieces, and a clear signature cartouche or stamp are good signs.
- Juliana: 5-link construction and figure-8 "puddling" of metal at the joins, with open-back stones.
- Cheap production: glued-in stones, thin stamped gold-tone, plastic "pearls" with peeling coating.
- Bakelite is a separate category: test with Simichrome.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Value loss: missing or dark (dead-foil) stones, worn plating, broken pin stems or clasps, enamel loss, green verdigris.
- Replaced stones that don't match in colour or size are visible in close-ups.
- Ask for back photos (signature), close-ups of all stones, and pin and clasp function.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake signatures on Eisenberg, Boucher and Haskell are reported. Modern reproductions of Trifari jelly bellies and HAR figurals exist. Trifari and others reissued vintage designs (e.g. Trifari for the Metropolitan Museum) (general knowledge, verify). Check that the signature style and construction fit the period.

## Market
A very high-volume market. Buyers are collectors, vintage-fashion buyers and resellers. eBay is best for signed pieces and Etsy for styled vintage. Top makers sell within weeks. Common makers are slow and mostly sell as bulk lots. Q4 gifting gives a seasonal lift (general knowledge, verify). Google Lens medians are asking prices and run well above sold. Don't use them as sold comps.

## What sellers usually miss
The back. "Vintage brooch lot" listings photograph only the fronts, so ask for backs. Unsigned Juliana is identified by construction (5-link, figure-8 puddling), not a mark. Bakelite is a separate category (test with Simichrome).

## Shipping / handling
Ounces; $5 first-class. eBay or Etsy. Photograph on black velvet.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Title: "Vintage Signed [Maker] [form] [stones/colour] [era]", e.g. "Signed Sherman Smoky Topaz Rhinestone Brooch Earrings Set 1960s". State the signature wording and location, dimensions, stone condition (none missing or dark), plating wear and clasp function. Photograph the front on black velvet, the signature close up and the side profile.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a Boucher/Eisenberg/Hobé/HAR/Schiaparelli/Haskell signature is visible and the price is ≤ 30% of tier, or Sherman parures are under $50. Pass when: Avon/Coventry/Monet lots, unsigned rhinestone "lots", or front-only photos. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat.

## Field notes (learned in triage)
