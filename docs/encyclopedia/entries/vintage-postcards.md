Summary: Common tourist views (white-border, linen, chrome) $0.50–3 each; small-town street scenes and good RPPCs $10–40; rare RPPCs (disasters, occupations, small depots, Black Americana), Halloween/Santa holiday, artist-signed $40–500+ (general knowledge, verify)
Buy rule (short): Never pay more than ~$0.25/card for tourist-view lots; look only for RPPCs of small towns/people at work and Halloween holiday cards.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Eras (back design dates them): Private mailing card / undivided back (1898–1907), divided back (1907–15), white border (1915–30), linen (1930–45, textured paper, saturated colours), photochrome/chrome (1939+). RPPC: actual photographic paper, "AZO", "VELOX", "CYKO", "EKC" stamp box on back. Example 7959421617: Niagara Falls white-border and early-linen views (illuminated falls, Maid of the Mist, Cave of the Winds) with Canadian/Niagara coat-of-arms, c. 1920s–40s, in plastic sleeves — mass-produced souvenir sets.

## Makers, brands and variants
Detroit Publishing (Phostint), Raphael Tuck (Oilette), Curt Teich (linen; code numbers date them), Valentine & Sons, Albertype, Hugh C. Leighton, Kropp; Niagara-specific: Niagara Falls Card Co., Ellis.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Value is subject, not age. RPPC shows silver-gelatin sheen/silvering, no halftone dots under loupe. Printed cards show dot pattern.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Corner wear, creases, album-paper residue, trimming, writing on face. Postally used with interesting cancels (DPO, RPO) can add.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern reprints of RPPCs (glossy, no stamp box or modern paper), reproduction Halloween cards.

## Market
Huge supply, deep buyer base, but common views barely move; dealers buy lots by the box at pennies.

## What sellers usually miss
Single RPPCs of small-town street scenes or businesses mixed into tourist lots; Halloween and Santa (non-red suit) holiday cards.

## Shipping / handling
Light; flat mailer $1.50–5.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Scan front/back, era, publisher number, posted/unposted, location.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold × 0.87 − $5 − $5 − price ≥ max($50, price). Tourist-view lots never qualify. A lot containing identifiable RPPC main-street or holiday cards at ≤ $20 might; ask for back photos.
