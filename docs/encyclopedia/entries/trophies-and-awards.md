Buy rule (short): Pass on industry statuettes and sports trophies; only buy sterling cups (hallmarks visible) under melt or Art Deco figural trophies under ~$30.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Telly Award statuette: stylized winged/leaping silver figure on a black cylindrical base (the example); given in large numbers each year for TV/video/commercial production. Clio: elongated abstract figure; Addy: silver/gold "A" form. Emmy (winged woman with atom), Oscar (knight on reel) — Oscars cannot legally be resold (post-1950 agreement) and Emmy has similar restrictions. Sterling loving cups: two- or three-handled, hallmarked "STERLING" + maker (Gorham, Tiffany, Reed & Barton). Figural Art Deco trophies: spelter/bronze sportsmen.

## Makers, brands and variants
Telly Awards (est. 1979, statuettes by RS Owens/Society Awards-style makers); Society Awards and R.S. Owens make Emmy/Clio/Grammy-type statuettes. Sports trophies: plastic columns (low). Sterling: Gorham, Tiffany, Reed & Barton.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Industry statuettes: cast zinc alloy/pewter with silver/gold plating, weighted bases, engraved plates naming winner and project (the name plate can raise interest if a notable production). Sterling = hallmark; plated cups "EPNS" low.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Plating wear, bent wings, missing name plate, base chips.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Replica statuettes (Emmy/Oscar novelty) common and worth $10–40.

## Market
Thin, novelty-driven; Telly statuettes appear regularly. 30–120 days.

## What sellers usually miss
Sterling under tarnish; notable winner names on plates; Deco figural trophies.

## Shipping / handling
1–4 lb, box with foam; $12–18.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Award, year, winner/plate text, material, height.

## Buy rule
Profit ≥ max($50, price). Telly at $30 needs ~$110+ resale — unlikely. Don't buy Oscars/Emmys (resale restrictions).
