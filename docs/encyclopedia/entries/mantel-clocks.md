# American mantel & shelf clocks (Seth Thomas, Gilbert, Waterbury, Ansonia, Ingraham)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: craigslist 1540, 1534, 1536, 1276, 1727, 1722; eBay sold pattern (knowledge 2024–25); NAWCC references

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1870s–1930s wooden or "adamantine" (celluloid faux-marble) cases with brass 8-day movements. Ogee = tall rectangular weight-driven box with a mirror/picture tablet (Gilbert, Seth Thomas, c.1850–90). Adamantine columned cases = Seth Thomas 1890–1917 (label inside back). Black marble/slate = French or Ansonia "enamelled iron". Tambour ("Napoleon hat") 1920s. Revere/Telechron Westminster electric = 1930s–50s. Sellers write "antique mantle clock", "grandfather style clock", "chime clock".

## What sellers usually miss
"Sonora chime" or "Chelsea" names; original glass tablet; whether it runs/strikes. Most mantel clocks are heavy, fragile and well-known — rarely arbitrage.

## Shipping / handling
10–25 lb, fragile, $30–50 each way double-boxed; movement should be removed or pendulum secured. eBay.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Chelsea/Sonora/Ansonia crystal regulator ≤ $100 running. Pass when: ordinary ogee/adamantine/tambour over $40 — shipping kills it. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

## Additional research (earlier entry: Seth Thomas and American mantel clocks)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold browse Oct 2025 (Seth Thomas Adamantine, 89 movement); sweep listing 2642; NAWCC general knowledge

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
American 8-day spring-driven mantel clocks 1880–1930. Seth Thomas "Adamantine" (celluloid veneer imitating marble, black with gold-painted columns) is the most common; also Ingraham, Gilbert, Sessions, New Haven, Ansonia. Label inside the back door names maker. Tells: adamantine columns intact, original dial and hands, movement stamped "Seth Thomas 89" etc. "Needs cleaning/doesn't run" = repair = pass. E. Howard banjo/regulator clocks and weights are a different, higher market.

### Comps (sold, dated)
- 2025-10 – eBay sold – Seth Thomas Adamantine 8-day, running – $100–170
- 2025-10 – eBay sold – Seth Thomas Sonora 4-bell chime – $250–400

### What sellers usually miss
Chime variants (Sonora) and crystal regulators; plain adamantines are correctly priced ~$100.

### Shipping / handling
15–25 lb, movement must be secured, pendulum removed – $35–50 boxed. Breakage risk high; prefer local.

### Buy rule
Buy when: running Sonora/crystal regulator ≤ $100 local. Pass when: plain Adamantine over $40, or anything "not running". Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
