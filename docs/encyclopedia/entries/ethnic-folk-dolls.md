## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Dolls dressed in national/ethnic costume, mostly made as souvenirs 1930s–present.
- **SE Asian hill-tribe dolls**: carved soft wood (unpainted or lightly painted face), handmade textile costume with real silver-alloy coins/bells; Hmong (indigo pleated skirts), Akha (headdress with coins/beads), Red Dao (red turban, black jacket with piping), Karen. Mostly 1980s–2010s Thai/Vietnamese tourist craft.
- **Japanese**: Hakata clay (painted fired clay figures, signed on base), Gofun/Ichimatsu (crushed-shell gesso faces, glass eyes, 1900s–40s), Kokeshi (lathe-turned wood, traditional vs creative, signed underneath), geisha souvenir dolls in glass cases (1950s–80s, $10–30).
- **Hopi Katsina (Kachina)**: carved cottonwood root, painted; signed carvings by known artists valuable; Navajo-made/"Kachina-style" and Asian imports cheap.
- **European**: Lenci (Turin, felt faces, 1920s–40s, side-glancing eyes), Klumpe (Spain, felt caricature 1950s), Roldan, Peggy Nisbet (UK, 1953–90s, tags), Paris souvenir "Poupée".

## Makers, brands and variants
Lenci, Klumpe, Roldan, Peggy Nisbet, Hakata Ningyo artists, Yoshitoku (Japanese dolls), Sato/Usaburo (creative kokeshi), Hopi carvers (signed), Native American doll makers (Lakota beaded, Seminole palmetto fibre, Navajo cloth), Chinese Cultural Revolution-era opera dolls.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Quality: hand-carved detail with tool marks, fine textiles (hand-embroidered, real silver), signed bases, original tags/boxes. Cheap: plastic heads, glued synthetic costumes, stamped "Made in China/Taiwan" stickers. The example has a simply carved soft-wood face and hands/feet, decent textile – typical tourist craft.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Moth damage on wool/felt, fading, missing accessories (hats, weapons), cracked gofun faces, insect damage in wood. Restoration lowers value.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
"Kachina" dolls imported from Asia/Mexico; fake Lenci; mass-produced "hill tribe" dolls sold in Chiang Mai night markets are the norm. Native American objects: watch for Indian Arts and Crafts Act misrepresentation – never describe as "Native American made" without proof.

## Market
Large supply of souvenir dolls; slow sales. Collector demand concentrated in Lenci, Japanese, Hopi.

## What sellers usually miss
Lenci and Japanese gofun dolls are often in "foreign doll" lots priced $10–20; signed creative kokeshi priced like generic wooden dolls.

## Shipping / handling
Light (1–2 lb), box with padding $10–14; protect accessories.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker/tradition (Lenci / Klumpe / Peggy Nisbet / Hakata / kokeshi / Hopi Katsina) + "doll" + "signed"/"tagged" + height + region "in the style of" if undocumented + era. Junk: "foreign doll lot", "souvenir", "ethnic" alone. `Lenci Felt Doll 1930s Italy 17in Original Tagged Costume Painted Face`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers; Etsy for kokeshi and Klumpe decor buyers; Native American pieces need provenance — pass if unclear. Tourist dolls lots locally.
- **Price position:** at the sold median ($25–100 Klumpe/Nisbet/Hakata/kokeshi; $150–2,000+ Lenci/Gofun/Hopi), accept −15%, floor 75%; OBO; cut 15% after 45 days.
- **Photos:** full doll front on white; face close up; signature/tag/label; costume details and fabric; back and base; any accessories; next to a ruler.
- **State:** maker or tradition ("in the style of" unless documented), materials (felt/clay/gofun/wood), height, signatures and tags transcribed, accessories, moth/fading/cracks by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** shoebox, 1–2 lb, padded box $10–14; wrap accessories separately, immobilize the head.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Lenci/Hakata/signed kokeshi] doll, [era], [signed/tagged] with the original costume, [height]; tourist costume dolls sell for [$] and [maker] pieces like this bring [$]."

## Buy rule
Tourist dolls resale $10–35 → never profitable. Pass. Buy Lenci/gofun/Hopi-signed only at ≤ $25 with resale ≥ $120, and pass Native American pieces without provenance (authentication/legal risk). Passenger-seat OK.
