## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Japanese porcelain and bisque giftware imported by George Zoltan Lefton (Chicago, company 1941–2001). Figures: "flower girls of the month", angels, colonial ladies, birds (bluebirds), Christmas holly items, Miss Priss kitten, Brown Heritage Fruit, Rose Chintz. Typical: matte bisque faces, glossy or matte dresses, applied porcelain roses, gilt edges.
Marks: red/gold foil oval "Lefton's Exclusives Japan" paper label, underglaze red/black "Lefton" crown mark, stock number with "KW" (e.g. KW4200), "Geo. Z. Lefton" mark, "Japan" or later "Taiwan/China/Malaysia".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Lefton** (1941–2001): Japan 1946–1970s best; later Taiwan/China/Malaysia.
- **Napco** (National Potteries Co., Cleveland): birthday angels (A1360 series etc.), "Napcoware".
- **Enesco**, **Inarco**, **Relpo**, **Norcrest**, **Lipper & Mann**, **Josef Originals** (California/Japan — collectible girls of the month, higher demand).
- Month series: each month has a flower (July = larkspur or water lily, often depicted with a rose on Lefton).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Japan-era pieces: fine bisque, delicate applied flowers, hand-painted features, crisp gilt. Later Taiwan/China pieces: heavier, glossier, simplified flowers. Original foil labels add a few dollars.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken applied flower petals (most common), chipped fingers, worn gilt, glued-on heads/hands, crazing. Repairs hide under paint touch-ups. Flower damage is near fatal for value.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not commonly faked; low value. Lookalikes from other importers sold as Lefton.

## Market
Oversupplied, slow; birthday-month figures sell steadily as gifts but cheap. Buyers often want a particular month.

## What sellers usually miss
Josef Originals sold as "Lefton-type"; Lefton cookie jars; complete month sets split up.

## Shipping / handling
0.5 lb; bubble wrap around flowers, double box, $8–10.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Give the stock number, month, Japan mark, height, any petal chips — sell month figures with the birthstone/flower angle.
