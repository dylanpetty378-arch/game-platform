# Record-store & advertising wooden crates (Peaches, brewery, fruit)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay listings for Peaches crates (Oct 2025, LP size asks $60–120, cassette size $25–45); thevinylfactory.com "10 best record crates"; general sold-price knowledge for pre-Prohibition brewery crates.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Peaches Records & Tapes (1970s–80s chain) crates: pine slat crate with a lithographed "Peaches" fruit label on the end. Two sizes – LP crate ~13×13×12" (the one people want) and the small cassette crate ~8×12×6". Napa Valley Box Co. crates are 1980s–90s cassette/CD racks, common. Brewery crates: stencilled or branded ends, dovetail or nailed corners, pre-1920 breweries (U.S. Brewing Co. Chicago, Schlitz, Pabst) bring the most; 1950s–70s Coke/Pepsi crates are $15–30.

## What sellers usually miss
Size: sellers say "Peaches crates" without saying cassette vs LP; the small ones are a third of the value. Label condition (full colour, not torn) matters.

## Shipping / handling
LP crate ~4 lb, 14" box, $15–20 to ship; cassette crates 1.5 lb, $10. Resell on eBay or Etsy.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Peaches LP crates ≤ $20 each; pre-Prohibition brewery crate ≤ $30. Pass when: cassette-size Peaches over $10 each, any Napa Valley crates (worthless). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
