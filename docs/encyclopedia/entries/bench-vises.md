# Bench vises (Wilton bullet, Columbian, Prentiss, Reed, Charles Parker)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: underpricedai.com aggregated eBay sales (Wilton 4" bullet: 43 sales, median $270, range $70–1,260; restored/unused examples $560–625), Garage Journal vise threads, eBay sold

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Wilton "bullet" machinist vise: round cylindrical body (looks like a bullet), hex nut on the front of the dynamic jaw, date stamped under the front jaw/key (month-year, e.g. "10-93"), "WILTON Chicago" or "Schiller Park, Ill." cast on the body (Chicago = 1941–57 earliest/most prized, Schiller Park = 1958–2000s, "Made in China" = pass). Models: 101/300S/400S/500S (3", 3.5", 4", 5" jaws), 825 "Toddler" 2.5", 9300/9400 swivel. Other money vises: Prentiss (Brooklyn, 1900s), Reed (Erie PA), Charles Parker (Meriden CT), Columbian/Athol/Starrett D-series, Yost, Morgan, Will-Burt Versa-Vise, and leg/post vises for blacksmiths. Cheap: Sears/Craftsman cast (China/Taiwan), Harbor Freight, Irwin, any red generic vise with "SEARS" cast in. Sellers' words: "machinist vise", "bench vise", "old vise".

## What sellers usually miss
"Wilton" in the title is usually enough for the seller to know. The arbitrage is a bullet vise titled "machinist vise" or "old vise" with no brand, or a Chicago-marked one priced as a Schiller Park. Also Parker/Prentiss/Reed names that mean nothing to most sellers.

## Shipping / handling
Heavy: 4" bullet ~40 lb, 5" ~60 lb. Ship UPS ground in a double box or on a half-pallet: $40–70 each way. This kills most of the margin, so only flag at ≤ 35% of sold value.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a real Wilton bullet (Chicago or Schiller Park), Parker, Prentiss, or Versa-Vise at ≤ $90, or any 5"+ bullet at ≤ $150. Pass when: the photo shows "SEARS"/import casting or the listing is at ≥ 50% of sold value. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after shipping both ways (~$100 total for a 4").
