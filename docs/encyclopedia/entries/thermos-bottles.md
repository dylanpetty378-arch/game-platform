## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Glass-filled or steel vacuum flasks. Example 7976489904 is the classic **King-Seeley Thermos quart, ribbed aluminum** with red plastic cup (model 2484 family), 1950s–70s.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **American Thermos Bottle Co.** (Norwich CT, 1907–1960) → **King-Seeley Thermos** (1960+): ribbed aluminum (Holtite), plaid steel, character lunchbox bottles.
- **Stanley (Landers Frary & Clark / Aladdin Industries)**: Stanley Super Vac steel unbreakable (1913+), hammertone green; older dated ones sought.
- **Aladdin**: lunchbox thermoses (Hopalong Cassidy, Disney) — value with the lunchbox.
- **Universal / LF&C** early nickel-plated flasks; **Icy-Hot** (Cincinnati) early leather-covered.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Age: nickel/brass body, leather case, cork stopper → 1910s–30s; ribbed aluminum + plastic cup → 1950s–70s; plaid steel → 1960s–80s. Glass liner rattles = broken refill.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken glass liner (shake test), dents, missing cork/stopper, cup cracks, faded decals.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Thermos reissued retro ribbed and character designs; Stanley classic is still made. Fakes not a concern.
