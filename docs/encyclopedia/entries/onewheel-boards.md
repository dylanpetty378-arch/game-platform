# Onewheel self-balancing boards (Future Motion)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: fallman.tech Onewheel+ spec page; eBay/owforum used listings 2025

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Single fat go-kart tyre in the middle, two footpads, aluminium rails. Models: V1 (2015, black rails, round-ish footpads), Onewheel+ (2017, blue rails, wooden footpads with grip, 130Wh LiFePO4, 5–7 mi), +XR (2018, 324Wh, 12–18 mi, grey/black rails, "XR" on rail), Pint (2019, small, yellow bumper), Pint X, GT (2021, big, 20+ mi), GT S-Series. Charger plug: 58.4V for V1/+, 63V for XR, 80+V for GT. Sellers say "Onewheel", "Future Motion", "electric skateboard".

## What sellers usually miss
Model identification (+ vs XR is a $300 gap – XR has "XR" on the rail and a bigger battery/63V charger); whether the charger is included (an original charger alone is $60–100).

## Shipping / handling
25 lb (+) to 35 lb (GT), lithium battery = hazmat ground only; ~$60–100 via UPS ground in the original-size box. Must be tested riding before buying (electronics rule). eBay fees ~13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: working Onewheel+ ≤ $150 with charger, XR ≤ $300, GT ≤ $500, tested in person. Pass when: no charger/untested/"won't balance", V1, or anything that looks like a stock/pawn photo at a too-good price (scam pattern). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
