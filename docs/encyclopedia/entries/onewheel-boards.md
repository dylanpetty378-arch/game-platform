Buy rule (short): Buy working, tested-in-person Onewheel+ ≤ $150 with charger, XR ≤ $300, GT ≤ $500; pass no-charger/untested/"won't balance" boards, V1s, and too-good pawn-style listings.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Single fat go-kart tyre in the middle, two footpads, aluminium rails. Models: V1 (2015, black rails, round-ish footpads), Onewheel+ (2017, blue rails, wooden footpads with grip, 130Wh LiFePO4, 5–7 mi), +XR (2018, 324Wh, 12–18 mi, grey/black rails, "XR" on rail), Pint (2019, small, yellow bumper), Pint X, GT (2021, big, 20+ mi), GT S-Series. Charger plug: 58.4V for V1/+, 63V for XR, 80+V for GT. Sellers say "Onewheel", "Future Motion", "electric skateboard".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Future Motion** (Santa Cruz, CA) is the only maker (general knowledge, verify).
- **V1** (2015): junk tier, pass.
- **Onewheel+** (2017): retail $1,499 new; used $300–500 with charger.
- **Onewheel+ XR** (2018): used $500–900; "XR" on rail, bigger battery. The + vs XR gap is ~$300.
- **Pint / Pint X** (2019+): smaller boards; value range not in this entry (general knowledge: roughly between + and XR used, verify).
- **GT / GT S-Series** (2021+): $900–1,800.
- Third-party aftermarket (Floatwheel, VESC conversions, custom rails/fenders/tyres) affects value — upgrades can help, conversions can scare buyers (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Aluminium rails, hub motor in the wheel, sealed battery/controller boxes. Original charger matters (alone $60–100). Check tyre tread, rail scrapes ("rail grind" from nose dives), footpad sensor function, bearing noise. Firmware/app shows mileage and battery health (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dead or degraded battery ("needs battery", "won't charge") = repair = pass. Error codes, "won't balance", footpad sensor faults, controller failures, water damage, cracked battery box, bent axle from crashes. Battery/controller repairs are expensive and parts are proprietary (general knowledge, verify). Must be tested riding before buying (electronics rule).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No real counterfeits; the risk is scams — stock or pawn-style photos at too-good prices — and stolen boards (ask for receipt/serial) (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Active used market on eBay, Facebook Marketplace and the owforum/Onewheel community; riders buy older XRs as daily boards (general knowledge, verify). Spring–summer is peak riding season. Shipping cost and hazmat rules push many sales local.

## What sellers usually miss
Model identification (+ vs XR is a $300 gap – XR has "XR" on the rail and a bigger battery/63V charger); whether the charger is included (an original charger alone is $60–100).

## Shipping / handling
25 lb (+) to 35 lb (GT), lithium battery = hazmat ground only; ~$60–100 via UPS ground in the original-size box. Must be tested riding before buying (electronics rule). eBay fees ~13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Onewheel" + model (+, +XR, Pint, GT) + "Future Motion" + "with charger" + "tested" + mileage. Junk: "electric skateboard", "self balancing", "as is". Example: `Onewheel+ XR Future Motion Electric Board 63V Charger Tested 450 Miles`.
- **Where:** FB local first (30 lb lithium hazmat, riders want to test-ride, pays near eBay without $80 shipping); eBay BIN with offers reaches more buyers if local stalls.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median for the model (+ $300–450 with charger, XR $550–850, GT $900–1,800); accept −10%, floor −20%; OBO; relist in spring if it stalls in winter.
- **Photos:** 1) full board side-on with rail model text visible; then both rails for scrapes, footpads, tyre tread, charger with voltage label, serial label, app screenshot of mileage and battery health; video of it balancing and riding.
- **State:** model, mileage from the app, battery health/range achieved, charger included and voltage, tyre condition, error codes ever shown, upgrades (fenders, rails, tyre), cosmetic damage by photo number, serial.
- **Pack / haul:** 25–35 lb, lithium battery = UPS ground hazmat only, original-size box with foam, $60–100; local it fits a car trunk or back seat.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "This is a [+XR] with [N] miles and [X] range on the app — retail was [$1,499/$1,800] new and working XRs with the original 63V charger sell for [$X]; it has been ridden and tested, no error codes."

## Buy rule
Buy when: working Onewheel+ ≤ $150 with charger, XR ≤ $300, GT ≤ $500, tested in person. Pass when: no charger/untested/"won't balance", V1, or anything that looks like a stock/pawn photo at a too-good price (scam pattern). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (it does).
