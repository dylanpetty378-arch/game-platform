## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Dolls with soft or rigid vinyl heads (from ~1950) and plastic/vinyl bodies, rooted saran or nylon hair, sleep eyes. Maker and year are moulded on the back of the neck ("HORSMAN DOLLS INC. ©1970", "IDEAL TOY CORP", "EFFANBEE", "Uneeda"). The valuable ones: complete boxed gimmick dolls (writing, talking, kissing, growing hair), and large companion dolls (Ideal Patti Playpal 35").

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Horsman** (1865–): Peggy Pen-Pal (1970, #7001, 18", draws on a yellow plastic desk via a cam mechanism, no batteries), Thirstee babies, Ruthie.
- **Ideal**: Crissy/Velvet growing hair (1969–), Patti Playpal (1959–), Kissy, Tearie Betsy Wetsy, Shirley Temple vinyl.
- **Madame Alexander**: Cissy, Cissette, Elise — higher value.
- **Effanbee**: Patsy reissues, Honey; **Uneeda**: Pollyanna, Dollikin; **American Character**: Tressy, Sweet Sue; **Mattel**: Chatty Cathy (pull-string), Baby Secret; **Remco**: Littlechap, Baby Laugh-a-Lot.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Bright factory cheek blush, unplayed rooted hair with original set, original clothes with maker tags, working mechanism, box with inserts. Sticky or shiny vinyl = plasticiser migration.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Cut or matted hair, ink/pen marks (fatal on vinyl), green ear (metal earring corrosion), cloudy sleep eyes, missing clothes/accessories, crushed boxes, broken mechanisms.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reissues by Effanbee, Ideal (Shirley Temple, Patti Playpal 1980s Ideal reissue), Madame Alexander; check neck mark years. Not commonly faked.

## Market
Slow, buyer pool is nostalgic women 55+. 30–90 days. Complete sets sell; loose dolls stack up.

## What sellers usually miss
Original box and accessories triple value; neck marks give exact identity.

## Shipping / handling
18" doll plus desk in large box: 6–9 lb, 20×14×10 box, $20–30.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Neck mark photo, whether mechanism draws, all accessories (pens, papers, templates), box damage, smoke-free.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − ~$25 ship to buyer − ~$20 ship to Dylan − price ≥ max($50, price). A $100 resale nets ~$42 at price $0, so even complete Peggy Pen-Pal sets fail shipped. Buy only collector dolls with resale ≥ $150 at ≤ $40.
