# Rookwood Pottery (Cincinnati, 1880–1967)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: knowledge of eBay/auction sold ranges (Humler & Nolan, Rago); no dated comp pulled

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Bottom mark: reversed "RP" monogram with flames around it – one flame added per year 1886–1900, then a Roman numeral below for the year (XIV = 1914, etc.). Shape number and size letter next to it. Artist-signed, hand-painted pieces (initials incised) are the valuable ones; moulded "production" pieces (matte glazes, florals, bookends, paperweights, candy dishes) are common and mostly $40–150.

## What sellers usually miss
The date numeral and whether it's artist-signed – and most sellers in Ohio DO know the Rookwood name. Arbitrage is outside Ohio where it's "pottery dish with flame mark".

## Shipping / handling
2–5 lb, double-box, $15–25.

## Buy rule
Buy when: artist-signed piece under $150, or production piece under $30. Pass when: production piece at $90. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
