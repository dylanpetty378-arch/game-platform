# Turkey & game calls (collectible box and pot calls)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold / Callmakers & Collectors Association (agent knowledge Oct 2026)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Wooden box calls (lidded, paddle), pot/slate calls, and duck/goose calls. Mass-market (Quaker Boy, Primos, Knight & Hale, Lynch pre-2000) = $10–30. Collectible = hand-made by named callmakers: M.L. Lynch early (Birmingham AL, pre-1960s), Neil Cost, Tom Turpin, Ben Rodgers Lee, Earl Mickel, D.D. Adams, Ike Ashby (Mississippi, "Ashby Gobbler Box", signed/numbered), Lamar Williams. Duck calls: Olt, Faulk's, Cajun, early Reelfoot (Johnny Marsh, Earl Dennison) $100–2,000.

## What sellers usually miss
Signature burned/pencilled inside the lid; the maker letter. "Old turkey call" with no name.

## Shipping / handling
Under 1 lb, $8.

## Buy rule
Buy when: signed Cost/Turpin/Lee/early Lynch under $100, or any signed call lot under $40. Pass when: Quaker Boy/Primos; Ashby at $100 (seller aware, ~$150 resale). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
