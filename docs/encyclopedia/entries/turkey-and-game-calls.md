# Turkey and game calls (collectible box, pot and duck calls: Lynch, Neil Cost, Turpin, Ashby, Olt, Reelfoot)

Summary: mass-market Quaker Boy/Primos/modern Lynch/plastic duck calls $8–30; 1960s–80s Lynch, Olt and signed modern makers $40–100; signed Ashby, Neil Cost, Turpin, early Lynch World Champion $100–1,500; pre-war Lynch, Cost-Lynch, Reelfoot checkered calls $1,500–5,000+.
Buy rule (short): Signed Cost/Turpin/Lee/early Lynch under $100, or any signed call lot under $40; pass Quaker Boy/Primos and seller-aware pieces.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- Wooden box calls (a lidded box with a paddle), pot/slate calls (a round pot with a striker), and duck/goose calls (wood or plastic barrels).
- Mass-market makers (Quaker Boy, Primos, Knight & Hale, Lynch pre-2000) are worth $10–30.
- The collectible ones are hand-made by named callmakers:
  - M.L. Lynch early (Birmingham AL, pre-1960s)
  - Neil Cost, Tom Turpin, Ben Rodgers Lee, Earl Mickel, D.D. Adams, Lamar Williams
  - Ike Ashby (Mississippi, "Ashby Gobbler Box", signed and numbered)
- Collectible duck calls: Olt, Faulk's, Cajun, early Reelfoot (Johnny Marsh, Earl Dennison), $100–2,000.
- Seller words: "old turkey call", "box call", "duck call".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **M.L. Lynch** (Birmingham AL): "World Champion" box calls. Early boxes (pre-1960s) are collectible. Later production, through the 1980s, is mid-range. Modern Lynch is mass-market.
- **Neil Cost** (Michigan): hand-made boxes, highly collected; Cost-Lynch pieces are the top.
- **Tom Turpin, Ben Rodgers Lee, Earl Mickel, D.D. Adams, Lamar Williams**: named callmakers.
- **Ike Ashby**: signed, numbered gobbler boxes, often with a letter; $100–250 with the letter.
- **Duck calls**: P.S. Olt (Illinois, 1904+), Faulk's (Louisiana), Cajun, Reelfoot-style checkered calls (Tennessee: Johnny Marsh, Earl Dennison) (general knowledge, verify for dates).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Hand-made boxes: figured hardwoods (walnut, cedar, poplar), hand-cut joints, a signature burned or pencilled inside the lid, sometimes dated and numbered.
- Mass-market calls carry printed logos, plastic parts and uniform machining.
- Duck calls: checkering by hand (Reelfoot) vs machine; hard rubber vs plastic.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Cracked boxes, replaced lids, worn or re-chalked paddles (normal), missing strikers or reeds. Original paperwork or a letter adds value.
- Heavy use is accepted. Refinishing lowers value (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Forged signatures on unsigned boxes in the style of Cost or Lynch exist. Collectors rely on provenance and the maker's handwriting (general knowledge, verify).
- Lynch reissued its classic models.

## Market
A small, knowledgeable collector community (Callmakers & Collectors Association, NWTF auctions) plus hunters. eBay moves common calls. Interest peaks before the spring turkey season (Feb–Apr) and duck season (Oct–Nov) (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
- A signature burned or pencilled inside the lid; the maker's letter.
- "Old turkey call" listed with no name.

## Shipping / handling
- Under 1 lb, $8.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- Maker, signature and number, date, wood, dimensions, original letter or box, how it sounds (a short audio clip helps).
- Photograph the inside of the lid, the signature, both ends and the paddle.
- Title keywords: "Neil Cost Turkey Box Call Signed", "Ike Ashby Gobbler Box", "Vintage Lynch World Champion Box Call", "Olt Duck Call".

## Buy rule
Buy when: a signed Cost/Turpin/Lee/early Lynch is under $100, or any signed call lot is under $40.
Pass when: Quaker Boy/Primos; Ashby at $100 (seller aware, ~$150 resale).
Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat.
