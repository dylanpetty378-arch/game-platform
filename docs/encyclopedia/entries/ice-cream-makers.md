## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
A wooden staved tub (pine or cedar) with metal hoops, a tin/galvanised canister inside, a dasher, and a cast-iron gear frame with a crank on top. Salt and ice go between tub and canister. Tells:
- Maker name and patent dates are cast into the iron gear frame ("White Mountain Freezer Co. Nashua NH", "Alaska Freezer Co. Winchendon Mass", "Shepard's Lightning", "Peerless Iceland", "Gem", "Kwik Freeze").
- Triple-motion (canister and dasher turn opposite ways) White Mountain = best quality.
- Original paint (blue/green/red), stencilled lettering on the tub, wooden crank handle.
- Size stamped on tub or frame: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 quart.
- 1950s–70s: galvanised steel or plastic tubs, Sears/Richmond Cedar Works "Rich-Freeze", electric motors.

## Makers, brands and variants
White Mountain (Nashua NH, 1853–; triple motion), Alaska Freezer Co. (Winchendon MA), North Pole, Peerless Iceland (Dana Mfg, Cincinnati), Gem (American Machine Co., Philadelphia), Shepard's Lightning (Nashua), Kwik Freeze, Auto Vacuum (no-crank 1910s), Richmond Cedar Works (Danville VA, largest producer 1900s–50s, also made Sears/Wards versions), Rival/Salton/Waring electric (1960s–80s).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Thick cedar staves with brass/iron hoops, heavy cast-iron frame with raised maker name, tinned (not galvanised) canister = old. Thin pine, stamped steel frame, galvanised can, plastic tub = later.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rusted canister (hole-through), shrunken/loose staves (hoops fall), missing dasher, missing wooden crank knob, flaking paint, salt corrosion of iron. Re-hooping/re-tinning = repair.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
White Mountain still makes the same design (modern ones have stickers, galvanised hoops, electric options). "Primitive-style" decor reproductions.

## Market
Local decor/farmhouse demand; shipped sales are few because of bulk (box 16×16×20" and 15–25 lb).

## What sellers usually miss
Miniature 1-pint freezers and early cast-iron table freezers; sellers usually price full-size buckets fairly.

## Shipping / handling
Full size: oversize/heavy, $35–60 shipped, frequent damage. Miniature: $15–20.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Give maker (from the cast frame), capacity, completeness (dasher, lid, crank, can), rust/holes, paint condition; local pickup preferred.

## Buy rule
Full-size freezers: shipping eats the margin and size approaches the passenger-seat limit with poor transit survival – pass. Miniatures/table models at ≤ $20 with resale ≥ $120 only.
