## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Just Andersen (Copenhagen, 1918–1970s): bronze or "diskometal" (grey-green pewter alloy) vases, bowls, bookends, busts and animal figures. Mark: "JUST A" or "JUST ANDERSEN DENMARK" stamped underneath, with a model number (D- prefix = diskometal, B- = bronze, e.g. D2111). Sculptor names appear on figural pieces (Carl Emil Ruge, Jean Gauguin, Gudrun Lauesen). Also Tinos, Ildfast, Buch & Deichmann, Bing & Grøndahl bronze dogs. Patina must be original – polished pieces lose half.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Just Andersen** (Copenhagen): diskometal (D- numbers) and bronze (B- numbers); figural busts like the Fanø girl (Carl Emil Ruge); animal figures; Art Deco vases and bowls.
- **Tinos** (Copenhagen bronze foundry/workshop), **Ildfast**, **Buch & Deichmann** – Danish Deco bronzes and pewter.
- **Bing & Grøndahl** bronze dogs.
- Sculptors: Carl Emil Ruge, Jean Gauguin, Gudrun Lauesen.
- Note: the Fanø girl bust D2111 is recorded here (and in the sweep) as "bronze", but a D- number per the entry's own scheme means diskometal – verify the alloy when one turns up.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Diskometal is a grey-green, soft pewter-like alloy with a matte, slightly greenish patina; bronze is heavier, warmer brown/gold under patina. Quality tells: crisp casting, clear stamped mark and number on the base, even original patina. Polishing to bright metal halves value. Modern copies are lighter, sharper-edged, with weak marks (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dents and bends (diskometal is soft), polished-off patina, corrosion spots, broken-off ears/tails, lead solder repairs. Ask for base mark photo, all-round photos of the patina, and close-ups of any dents.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Unmarked Deco pewter from other makers is often attributed to Just Andersen; some recasts of popular figures exist (general knowledge, verify). Insist on a stamped mark.

## Market
Scandinavian-design and Art Deco collectors; active in Denmark (Bruun Rasmussen, Lauritz) and on eBay/1stDibs/LiveAuctioneers in the US. Moderate volume; marked pieces sell steadily (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
The "JUST A" stamp – pieces get listed as "vintage pewter vase" or "bronze bust". Any grey-green metal Danish-looking Deco vase deserves a base photo.

## Shipping / handling
2–8 lb, $15–30 each way. Sell on eBay/1stdibs; Scandinavian design buyers are active. Wrap well – diskometal dents easily.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Maker, model number, alloy (diskometal vs bronze), sculptor if any, height/diameter, original patina (unpolished), any dents. Photograph the base mark and number, all sides, and close-ups of the patina. Title: "Just Andersen Denmark Diskometal Vase D-xxxx Art Deco".
