# Danish bronzes & diskometal (Just Andersen, Tinos, Buch)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: agent knowledge of LiveAuctioneers/Bruun Rasmussen/eBay sold bands (verify); run12 sheet 61

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Just Andersen (Copenhagen, 1918–1970s): bronze or "diskometal" (grey-green pewter alloy) vases, bowls, bookends, busts and animal figures. Mark: "JUST A" or "JUST ANDERSEN DENMARK" stamped underneath, with a model number (D- prefix = diskometal, B- = bronze, e.g. D2111). Sculptor names appear on figural pieces (Carl Emil Ruge, Jean Gauguin, Gudrun Lauesen). Also Tinos, Ildfast, Buch & Deichmann, Bing & Grøndahl bronze dogs. Patina must be original – polished pieces lose half.

## What sellers usually miss
The "JUST A" stamp – pieces get listed as "vintage pewter vase" or "bronze bust". Any grey-green metal Danish-looking Deco vase deserves a base photo.

## Shipping / handling
2–8 lb, $15–30 each way. Sell on eBay/1stdibs; Scandinavian design buyers are active.
