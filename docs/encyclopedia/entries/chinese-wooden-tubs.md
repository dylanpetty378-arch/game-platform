## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Coopered or slab-built wooden vessels exported in huge numbers from China since the 1990s (from Shanxi, Zhejiang, Fujian), many from c.1900–1960 village use, others newly made.
- **Rice/grain measures (dou, sheng)**: tapered square boxes, often with a centre divider/handle bar, iron or brass straps at corners, painted characters (shop name, "double happiness", "abundance") on sides. Standard sizes nest.
- **Baby bath / foot tubs**: oval coopered fir/cedar staves with a thick rolled rim, brass or iron hoops; often with red lacquer inside.
- **Wedding / dowry buckets**: lidded, red lacquer, brass mounts and handles, sometimes gilt and carved; part of a bride's dowry set.
- **Water and well buckets**, **lunch/food baskets** (tiered, lacquered).

## Makers, brands and variants
Anonymous village coopers. Value is driven by age, original lacquer/paint, brass fittings and decorative quality, not maker. Import-shop and decorator stock 1990s–2010s has dealer stickers (Pier 1, Cost Plus, Gump's).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Wood: Chinese fir (shan mu) or cypress, light and resinous; elm for measures.
- Age: worn edges, oxidised iron straps with rust bleed, hand-forged nails, softened paint characters, natural shrinkage gaps between staves. New ones: sharp stave edges, bright uniform varnish, machine nails, brass that is bright and thin.
- Lacquer: old red lacquer with fine craquelure; repainted red enamel looks thick and glossy.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Shrinkage gaps and cracked staves (common; tubs will not hold water), loose or missing hoops, split rims, worm holes, refinishing with modern varnish.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
New-made "antique" buckets and measures with artificial distressing are sold widely by import dealers; some carry new painted characters. Assume decor unless age tells are clear.

## Market
Decorator demand (planters, magazine bins, ice buckets); sells best locally or on Chairish. eBay sales are slowed by shipping cost.

## What sellers usually miss
Mostly the reverse: owners price imported decor as antiques. The rare miss is a genuine lacquered wedding bucket with original brass sold as a "wooden planter".

## Shipping / handling
Bulky: 18–24" long, 6–12 lb; box 24x18x14 → $30–50 each way. Fits a passenger seat.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Chinese", form (rice measure/wedding bucket/foot tub), wood (elm/fir), "red lacquer", "brass", size in inches, "early 20th century" only with age tells. Junk: "antique" on import-shop stock, "rustic farmhouse". `Antique Chinese Red Lacquer Wedding Bucket Brass Fittings 14" Elm`
- **Where:** FB local or Chairish (sells for more than eBay; decorator buyers); eBay BIN only for small measures where shipping stays under $30.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (measures $40–100, tubs $50–150), accept −20%, floor $40 local; OBO. Cut 20% after 30 days — decor buyers are slow.
- **Photos:** first: styled as decor in daylight; then stave edges and iron straps (age), interior, underside, lacquer craquelure close-up, every crack/gap, tape measure for scale.
- **State:** form, wood, dimensions, weight, lacquer original vs repainted, brass fittings, dealer sticker if any, cracks and "not watertight", suggested decor use.
- **Pack / haul:** 18–24" long, 6–12 lb; box 24x18x14, single box with corner padding, $30–50; fits a passenger seat.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Genuine [early-to-mid 20th c.] [elm rice measure] with hand-forged nails and oxidised straps — the import-shop copies are varnished and machine-nailed; this is the real one."
