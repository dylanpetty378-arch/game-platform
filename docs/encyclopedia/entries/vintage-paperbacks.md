# Vintage paperbacks (Signet, Bantam, Pocket Books, Ace, Avon, Dell, Popular Library, Perma Books — 1940s–60s)
Updated: 2026-10-28 · upgraded 2026-10-13 · Sources: eBay sold memory 2025, Signet Fleming series knowledge
Slug: vintage-paperbacks · Aliases: pulp paperbacks, mass market paperbacks, old paperbacks, James Bond books, Ace Doubles, Dell mapbacks, GGA paperbacks
Summary: 1960s–70s reprints $2–6 each; Signet Fleming Bond 14-title run $60–120/set; 1st printings, Ace Doubles, Avon pulps VG+ $25–100 each; Pocket Books #1–10, fine Dell mapbacks $200+ (Fleming UK hardcover firsts reach $5,000+).
Buy rule (short): Buy 1st-printing or pre-1955 pulps in VG at ≤ $3/book; pass 1960s reprint runs priced ≥ $50.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Mass-market paperbacks with painted or photo covers; publisher logo and catalogue number on cover top (Signet D2052
etc.). Value drivers: first paperback printings (check copyright page "First Printing" with no later printing listed),
pulp cover art (Avon, Popular Library, Ace Doubles), GGA ("good girl art"), early sci-fi/horror (Ace Doubles,
Ballantine), Fleming Signet first printings with the Barye Phillips/Hooks covers. Seller words: "vintage paperbacks",
"James Bond books", "pulp". Cover price is a dating tell: 25¢ = 1940s–mid-50s, 35¢ = mid-50s–early 60s, 50–60¢+ = 1960s
on (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Pocket Books** (1939–): kangaroo "Gertrude" logo; #1–10 of 1939 are the top tier.
- **Dell** (1943–): "mapbacks" (crime-scene map on rear cover, 1940s–early 50s) collected by number.
- **Avon**, **Popular Library**, **Lion**, **Gold Medal**: pulp crime and GGA covers.
- **Ace** (1952–): Ace Doubles (two novels back-to-back, tête-bêche) — early sci-fi (Philip K. Dick, etc.).
- **Ballantine**: early sci-fi/horror firsts.
- **Signet (NAL)**: Ian Fleming Bond run (the 14 Fleming titles) — mostly reprinted many times in the 1960s.
- **Perma Books**: M-3070 "Too Hot to Handle" = Moonraker US 1st paperback.
- **Bantam**: early numbered series collectible (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Cheap pulp paper that tans and gets brittle; laminated or varnished covers on some later books. Spine colour and
catalogue number on the cover/spine identify edition. A true first printing has no later-printing line on the
copyright page; reprints state "Fifteenth printing" etc. (e.g. 1964 15th printing). Book-club and digest editions
are low value.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Graded like books: Fine/VF/VG/G. Common faults: spine rolls/creases, cover tears and chips, tanned/brittle pages,
reading creases, stamped names, water damage, mould smell, detached covers. Tape repairs and colour touch-ups cut
value sharply. Pulp collectors pay a large premium for VG+ and Fine; Good copies are near reading-copy value.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Facsimile reprints and later printings with the same cover art are the main issue — check the printing line and cover
price rather than the artwork (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Steady collector market for pulp/GGA, sci-fi firsts and Bond; reprint runs sell slowly as lots. eBay is the main
venue; Heritage for top items; local book dealers pay little (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Printing statement. "Original Ian Fleming collection 70 years old" is almost always 1960s Signet 10th+ printings.
Conversely, sellers bundle a lone 1940s–50s pulp with GGA cover into a $1 box.

## Shipping / handling
Media mail, 14 books ~6 lb, $8. eBay 13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** author, title, publisher + catalogue number (Signet D1234, Dell 123 mapback, Ace Double D-36), "1st printing" only when the copyright page shows it, year, "vintage paperback", "GGA"/"pulp"/genre, grade. Junk: "70 years old", "original collection", "rare" on 10th printings. `Ian Fleming Moonraker Perma Books M-3070 Too Hot to Handle 1956 1st PB VG`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for firsts, mapbacks, Ace Doubles and GGA pulps; eBay lots for reprint runs; Heritage for Pocket Books #1–10; local book dealers pay little.
- **Price position:** reprint runs at the sold median (14-book Signet Bond $70–110 as a lot), OBO; firsts/pulps VG+ at the sold median ($25–100), accept −15%; cut 10% at 45 days. Good-grade copies at reading-copy price.
- **Photos:** first: front cover flat, even light; then back, spine straight-on, copyright page with the printing statement, each crease/chip/tear, page edges for tanning, any stamps or writing.
- **State:** publisher, catalogue number, printing statement verbatim, year, cover price, cover artist if known, grade with every flaw by photo number (spine roll, creases, chips, tanning, smell, tape).
- **Pack / haul:** Media Mail; single in a rigid mailer with a bag, $4; 14 books ~6 lb, $8.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Perma M-3070], the [1956] US first paperback of [Moonraker], copyright page shows no later printing, [VG] with a tight spine — the 1960s Signet reprints go for $5 each."

## Buy rule
Buy when: 1st-printing statement visible or pre-1955 pulps in VG at ≤ $3/book. Pass when: 1960s reprint runs priced
≥ $50 – profit evaporates. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Must fit a
passenger seat.
