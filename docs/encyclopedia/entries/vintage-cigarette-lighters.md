## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Petrol (wick) or butane pocket and table lighters, 1910s–1980s.
- **Lift-arm / semi-automatic petrol (1926–1950s)**: a hinged arm with a snuffer cap that lifts and strikes the flint in one push. **Ronson** (Art Metal Works, Newark NJ): Banjo (1926, banjo-shaped arm on top), De-Light, Princess, **Standard** (rectangular body, arm with a pointed cap), Whirlwind (windguard), Rondelight; cases in chrome, black or tortoise enamel stripes, engine turning. **Dunhill Unique** (1924–1950s; pointed lift arm, case in plate, silver, gold, often Swiss-made "Made in Switzerland" by La Nationale), **Dunhill Sports/Watch** (lighter with inset watch), **Thorens** single-claw (Swiss). **Evans** (US), **Golden Wheel**, **Parker Beacon**, Japanese copies.
- **Flip-top gas/petrol (1950s–80s)**: Dunhill Rollagas (roller wheel, lozenge or barley pattern), S.T. Dupont Ligne 1/2 (a "ping" when opened, lacquer or Chinese lacquer), Colibri, Cartier oval, and many Japanese copies (**Gladys**, Maruman, Zaima, Crown, Prince) that look like Dunhill or Dupont.
- **Zippo**: dated by bottom stamps (dots/slashes, year codes); pre-1950s and military/advertising Zippos are the valuable ones.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Ronson**: Banjo 1926–29 (rarest, $100–400 used; mint/boxed asks up to $3,000), Touch-Tip table lighters 1935–50s ($40–300), Mastercase and Twenty Case combos ($30–80), Standard and Princess pocket lighters 1930s–50s ($15–50), Whirlwind ($20–60), Varaflame butane 1960s–70s ($10–35).
- **Dunhill**: Unique (plated $100–300, sterling $300–900, gold $1,000+), Sports/Watch Unique (sterling £1,400 at Bonhams 2022), Square Boy (€78 Catawiki), Rollagas ($80–250 working; gold/lacquer more), Aquarium table lighters ($800–3,000).
- **S.T. Dupont**: Ligne 1/2, Gatsby; $80–400 working, Chinese lacquer and limited editions more.
- **Thorens**: single-claw automatic 1920s–40s, $40–150.
- **Evans**: enamel and guilloché cases, often with matching compacts; $10–40.
- **Japanese 1950s–80s**: Gladys, Maruman, Zaima, Crown, Prince – copies of Dunhill/Dupont designs, $10–35.
- **Precious metal**: sterling silver (hallmarks: lion passant for London, "Sterling" for US) and 9k/14k/18k gold cases from Cartier, Tiffany, Asprey, Van Cleef.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Weight and hallmarks: silver and gold cases have stamped marks on the base or inside the sleeve; plated pieces say "Silver plated", "Plaqué or", "EPNS", or nothing.
- Engine-turned (guilloché) cases and black enamel stripes are typical 1930s Ronson; tortoise enamel is Mastercase.
- Base stamps: "RONSON" with patent numbers (pre-1950 patents date it); "DUNHILL MADE IN SWITZERLAND/ENGLAND" + patent numbers; Dupont serial and "Paris"; Japanese "Made in Japan" or nothing.
- Original box and paperwork (Ronson boxes, Dunhill green boxes) add 20–50%.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Worn plating showing brass, dents, missing enamel.
- Mechanism: weak springs, seized flint wheels, corroded fuel chambers from leaked petrol. Butane valves on Rollagas/Dupont leak; service costs $80–150.
- Testability: a flint spark and a lit flame in the photo is a good sign, but many sellers fuel them only for the photo; Dylan can test with lighter fluid and a flint (simple, not a repair).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Japanese copies of Dunhill Rollagas and Dupont sold as "Dunhill-style"; check base marks.
- Fake Dupont and Dunhill (Chinese, 2000s) with wrong fonts and no "ping".
- Ronson reissue Banjos (Japan, 1980s–90s, gold plated) – sold as "Ronson Banjo Japan" ~$300 ask; not period.
- Zippo fakes from China (wrong bottom stamps).

## Market
Steady international collector market (Europe, Japan) for Dunhill, Dupont, Ronson Banjo and precious-metal pieces; days to weeks on eBay. Common Ronsons are plentiful and slow. Note: eBay allows lighters but prohibits shipping them with fuel; Dupont/Dunhill butane must ship empty.

## What sellers usually miss
- Hallmarks: a "silver lighter" that is sterling (or a "gold tone" that is 9k) is the main upside.
- "Dunhill" on the base of a plain-looking lift-arm lighter (sellers say "old lighter").
- Ronson Banjo shape (lever on the top like a banjo neck) vs the common Standard.
- The reverse is common: Japanese Gladys/Maruman copies listed as "1940s Dunhill-style" at $50–100.

## Shipping / handling
Light (under 1 lb). Must be **empty of fuel** (USPS/eBay rule); remove the wick-soaked cotton or let it evaporate a week; ship flint lighters with a note. $5–8 First Class/Ground Advantage.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker + model ("Ronson Banjo", "Dunhill Unique", "Dupont Ligne 1", "Thorens") + material ("sterling", "silver plate", "gold tone") + "pocket"/"table lighter" + decade + "sparks"/"works"; Junk: "old lighter", "Dunhill-style", "smoking", "lot". Example: `Dunhill Unique Sterling Silver Lift Arm Pocket Lighter 1930s Hallmarked Sparks`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers with international shipping on (Europe and Japan collectors); common Ronson Standard/Varaflame as a lot or local. No place beats eBay.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (Touch-Tip/Whirlwind $40–120; Rollagas/Dupont/Banjo/Unique plated $100–350; sterling Unique $300–900), accept −15%, floor p25; OBO; cut 10% after 3 weeks.
- **Photos:** first photo the lighter closed, front; then base stamps and patent numbers, hallmarks macro, mechanism open, a flame or spark photo, plating wear, dents, box; video of it lighting.
- **State:** maker, model, patent numbers, material and hallmarks, "sparks / lights / untested" honestly, plating wear and dents by photo number, valve leaks on butane, service history, box.
- **Pack / haul:** under 1 lb; must ship empty of fuel (USPS/eBay rule) — remove the wick cotton or let it evaporate a week, note "flint lighter" inside; small box, $5–8.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Dunhill Unique] with [London 1930] hallmarks on the case — sterling, not plate, and the real lift-arm, not the Japanese copy; [sparks and lights]; a plated Unique is [$100–300], this is the silver tier."

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − ~$6 to buyer − ~$6 to Dylan − price; pass unless ≥ max($50, price).
- Need a ≥ $75 sale even for a $10 buy: **pass on chrome Ronson Standard/Princess/Whirlwind, Evans and Japanese copies** unless ≤ $8 in a lot.
- Buy: Dunhill Unique, Dupont or Ronson Banjo at ≤ $50; hallmarked sterling/gold cases at ≤ 50% of melt-plus-premium.
- Butane lighters that don't light = untested mechanism, needs service → pass unless the price is ≤ $15.
- No authentication issue for common Dunhill/Dupont (marks are clear), but high-end gold/Cartier needs a specialist → pass.

## Field notes (learned in triage)
- 2026-10-13 (redo): "Gladys" = Swiss-made budget slim lift-arm petrol lighter (chrome barley pattern, box reads "Gladys / Swiss Made / Fabr. Suisse"); not Dunhill. The db comp row "Gladys Dunhill-style lift-arm lighter" ($425) actually returned Dunhill Uniques, so it is a bad comp. Gladys sells ~$30-60.
- 2026-10-13 (triage): Zippo value is on the bottom stamp: "PAT. PEND." + 3-barrel hinge (1930s) and "PAT. 2032695" + 4-barrel hinge (1937–50) sell $50–150+; 1970s+ date-code bottoms (dots/slashes/Roman numerals) $10–15. Always ask for the bottom photo of a Zippo lot.
- 2026-10-13 (triage): Butabloc (France) round butane lighters are common, $15-30; a Butabloc in a lot adds nothing.
- 2026-10-13 (triage): Lift-arm petrol lighters with mother-of-pearl checkerboard panels are Dunhill Unique only if the base is stamped "Dunhill Unique / Made in England" (or Pat. 143752); Japanese copies of the same form are unmarked or "Japan" and worth $10-25. Ask for the base before pricing a lift-arm.
- 2026-10-13 (triage): Unfired early-1960s plain brushed-chrome Zippo 200 in original box is $40-80 even sealed; value jumps only with a 3-barrel hinge (pre-1950), patent-pending base, or advertising/engraved lid. Billings seller lists the same lighter twice ($60/$70) and will not ship.
