# Hall China teapots & tea sets
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: kovels (donut shape history), Whitmyer Collector's Encyclopedia of Hall China (reference), eBay sold memory 2024-25

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Heavy vitrified pottery, glossy solid-colour glaze (often with gold decoration), "HALL" in a circle or "Hall's Superior Quality Kitchenware" on the base. Shapes are the key: common Boston, New York, Philadelphia, Windshield, Hollywood vs. novelty Aladdin, Airflow, Automobile, Football, Basketball, Donut, Rhythm, Nautilus, Birdcage. Seller words: "Hall teapot", "vintage teapot gold trim", "Jewel Tea Autumn Leaf".

## Comps (sold, dated)
- kovels: Donut shape introduced 1938, one of Hall's most popular; Chinese-made lookalike donut teapots sell $8–15
- eBay sold memory 2025: Boston-shape 3-pc green/yellow set ~$35–60; Nautilus cobalt ~$250; Automobile ~$600+

## What sellers usually miss
Shape names. A seller calling it "Hall teapot set" with an Automobile or Football shape is the win; colour (cobalt, Chinese red, emerald) multiplies.

## Shipping / handling
Pottery, 2–4 lb per piece, double box; ~$15–20 in. eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: novelty shape (Automobile/Football/Basketball/Donut/Nautilus/Airflow/Rhythm) ≤ $60, no chips. Pass when: Boston/New York/Philadelphia shapes at any price over $15.
