## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Original Tiger Electronics Furby, model 70-800 (1998–99), purple/magenta window box with "I will KEEP AMAZING you!" flap and the yellow-flower Furby logo. Later generations (1999–2000) have different box colours and model numbers (70-8xx). Furby Babies are smaller with a different box. 2005/2012 Hasbro reissues are a different toy (bigger, LCD eyes) and are not collectible. Sellers say "original Furby", "1998 Furby", "Furby new in box"; NIB ones often "untested" because the 4 AA batteries are long dead.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Tiger Electronics 70-800** (1998–99): first generation; common colours snowball/white, tuxedo, giraffe, leopard, brown/tan, grey ($40–80 NIB).
- **Rarer colours**: Dalmatian, Peacock/teal, Tiger, Gorilla – collectible tier.
- **Later Tiger generations** (1999–2000, 70-8xx) and **Furby Babies** (NIB collectible).
- **Special/limited editions** (name on the box front): Kid Cuisine ($500–2,000), Angel ($500–800), Millennium (reported $4,500 – single unverified report), Wizard/Jester/Royal/President ($100–300), Racing ($80–100), Hi-C, Tropical, Cubs, rainbow.
- **Hasbro 2005 / 2012** reissues: not collectible.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Faux-fur body over a plastic shell with motorised eyes/ears/beak; fabric tag on the body; window box with the flap. Box crispness (corners, window, no tears or sun-fade) drives NIB value (general knowledge for tag details, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Battery corrosion in the compartment (common on loose and occasionally NIB if batteries were left in – general knowledge, verify), stuck/grinding motors, matted or dirty fur, missing tags, crushed or faded boxes, torn windows. "Untested" is normal on NIB and doesn't hurt much.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Hasbro 2005/2012 reissues and knock-off "Furby-like" toys get listed as "original"; check the Tiger logo, model number 70-8xx and the box design.

## Market
Nostalgia market for late-90s kids; steady eBay/Mercari sales of NIB first-gen; press-reported 2019 highs were outliers, and the 2026 Terapeak median is $50. Nov–Dec gift peak likely (general knowledge, verify). Many buyers want one at a time.

## What sellers usually miss
That NIB first-gen boxes sell for 4–8x a loose one; the edition (look for a special-edition name on the box front); and that "untested" is normal and doesn't hurt much. Sellers price NIB common Furbys at retail-era $10–35.

## Shipping / handling
~2 lb boxed, box is the value – ship in an outer carton with padding, ~$10–14 each ground. eBay/Mercari; ~13% fees. Many buyers want one at a time; a lot of 8 sells cheaper per unit than singles.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State model number (70-800), year, colour/name, edition, NIB/sealed vs opened, box condition (corners, window, flap), untested/batteries. Photograph all box sides, the model number and the edition label. Title: "1998 Tiger Electronics Furby 70-800 [colour] NIB, original box".

## Buy rule
Buy when: NIB first-gen (70-800) at ≤ $20 each, or any special edition NIB at ≤ $60. Pass when: loose, Hasbro 2005+/2012, or NIB common colour over $35. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Fits a passenger seat.
