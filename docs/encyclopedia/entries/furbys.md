# Furbys (1998–2001 Tiger Electronics originals)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: today.com "Is Furby still in demand" (Bellomo sold examples), collectinsure.com Furby value guide (2023), eBay listings for 70-800 NIB, apartmenttherapy eBay roundup

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Original Tiger Electronics Furby, model 70-800 (1998–99), purple/magenta window box with "I will KEEP AMAZING you!" flap and the yellow-flower Furby logo. Later generations (1999–2000) have different box colours and model numbers (70-8xx). Furby Babies are smaller with a different box. 2005/2012 Hasbro reissues are a different toy (bigger, LCD eyes) and are not collectible. Sellers say "original Furby", "1998 Furby", "Furby new in box"; NIB ones often "untested" because the 4 AA batteries are long dead.

## What sellers usually miss
That NIB first-gen boxes sell for 4–8x a loose one; the edition (look for a special-edition name on the box front); and that "untested" is normal and doesn't hurt much. Sellers price NIB common Furbys at retail-era $10–35.

## Shipping / handling
~2 lb boxed, box is the value – ship in an outer carton with padding, ~$10–14 each ground. eBay/Mercari; ~13% fees. Many buyers want one at a time; a lot of 8 sells cheaper per unit than singles.

## Buy rule
Buy when: NIB first-gen (70-800) at ≤ $20 each, or any special edition NIB at ≤ $60. Pass when: loose, Hasbro 2005+/2012, or NIB common colour over $35. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
