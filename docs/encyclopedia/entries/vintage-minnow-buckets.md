## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
A two-part metal pail: an outer bucket and a perforated inner insert that lifts out (or floats) to hold live minnows. Galvanized steel with painted or printed logos, wire bail handle.
- **Old Pal** (Woodstream Corp., Lititz PA): red/orange "OLD PAL" lettering on galvanized steel, 1950s–80s; sizes 6, 8, 10 qt; "Floating Minnow Bucket".
- **Frabill** (Milwaukee): green or galvanized buckets with "Frabill" logo, 1940s–80s.
- **Falls City** (Louisville) and **Planet**: early, often painted green with gold stencilling.

## Makers, brands and variants
Old Pal / Woodstream (Lititz PA), Frabill, Falls City, Planet, Bill DeWitt (Ohio), Pflueger, Shakespeare (rare).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Bright original paint and graphics drive value. Insert present. Rust-free bottom. Pre-war pieces have stencilled paint, riveted seams, and wooden bail grips.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rust, holes in the bottom, missing insert, faded logo. No repairs worth doing.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Decorative "vintage-style" galvanized buckets are sold as decor. Not common fakes.
