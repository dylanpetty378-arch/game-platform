# Violin bows (antique / trade bows)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: Brompton's auction archive (Mirecourt makers: Bazin, Ouchard, Cuniot-Hury, Morizot), eBay sold "antique violin bow", Maestronet forum rules of thumb

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Value sits in three things: the stick wood, the maker's stamp, and the mounts. Pernambuco sticks are dense, red-brown, often octagonal at the handle and round at the tip; brazilwood is lighter/orangey and cheaper; carbon/fibreglass is modern. Stamp: look just above the frog on the player's side ("BAZIN", "OUCHARD", "TOURTE" (generic German trade stamp, not a real Tourte), "GERMANY", "NÜRNBERGER", "PFRETZSCHNER", "GLASSER" (student)). Mounts: silver or gold winding and ferrule = better grade; nickel = trade grade; plastic/whalebone-imitation winding = student. Frog: ebony with Parisian eye (pearl dot in metal ring) is a French tell; plain pearl dot is generic; tortoiseshell/ivory frogs are top-tier and have shipping/legal issues. Tip plate: ivory/bone (old) vs plastic. Missing hair is normal and cheap to fix (~$60–80 rehair) but a warped or cracked stick is terminal. Sellers' words: "fiddle bow", "antique bow", "old violin bow".

## What sellers usually miss
The stamp (they never photograph above the frog), pernambuco vs brazilwood, silver vs nickel. A bow that "came with grandpa's fiddle" is usually a $30 trade bow but 1 in 20 is a $500+ workshop bow. Ask the seller for a close-up of the stick above the frog and the underside of the frog slide.

## Shipping / handling
Under 1 lb; ship in a rigid tube; $10–15. Resell on eBay or via Tarisio/Brompton's for stamped makers. Cannot authenticate from photo alone – Dylan's rule says pass unless the stamp is legible in the photo.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a legible maker stamp (Bazin, Morizot, Cuniot-Hury, Nürnberger, Pfretzschner, Knopf) is visible, stick straight, price ≤ $150. Pass when: no stamp visible, brazilwood colour, plastic winding, warped stick. Lead when: good frog (Parisian eye, metal winding) but no stamp photographed – ask for one photo before deciding. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
