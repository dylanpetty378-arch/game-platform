## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Decorative metal wall sculpture, mostly 1960s–1980s American mid-century/brutalist design. "Curtis Jere" is a trade name (Curtis Freiler + Jerry Fels) of Artisan House, Los Angeles (founded 1963; Curtis Jere name used from the 1960s; production continued into the 1990s–2000s, and the name was later licensed to Artisan House decor).
- **Real C. Jere tells:** heavy brass, copper or bronze-finished steel; torch-cut, welded and brazed construction with visible weld beads; layered depth (pieces stand 1–4 in off the wall); finishes are patinated brass/copper, enamel accents, verdigris, gold leaf; a raised or welded signature "C. JERE" with a year (e.g. "C. Jere 1971") on the front edge or a brass tag, sometimes a paper Artisan House label on the back.
- **Look-alikes:** Homco/Home Interiors, Syroco (resin!), Burwood (plastic), and imported 1970s wire decor: thin painted wire bent into shapes, flat stamped sheet metal, faux-rust or gold spray paint, tiny hanging loops, no welding beads, no signature. Painted white/cream wire with spattered "rust" hulls is the classic cheap version.
- Sailboats are among the most-copied subjects. Real C. Jere sailboats use brass rods for rigging with brazed joints and copper/brass sails or a long hull with patina; the cheap ones use painted wire grids.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Curtis Jere / Artisan House (LA, 1963–)**: signed "C. Jere" + year; key lines: Raindrops (gold/silver drops, $800–2,500), Birds in Flight / flock of birds ($400–1,500), brutalist panels and sunbursts ($500–2,000), Koi and fish schools ($300–1,000), sailboat/clipper ships ($250–900 large, $100–300 small), Torch-cut abstract ($400–1,500), Tree of Life ($300–900), Flower/Daisy walls ($200–700), Sunburst clocks.
- **Artisan House later production (1990s–2000s)**: still signed, sometimes "Artisan House" tag; somewhat lower ($150–600).
- **William Bowie, Jere-era studio pieces, Marc Weinstein (Brutalist)**: other signed MCM metal; Weinstein $300–1,500.
- **Homco / Home Interiors & Gifts (1970s–80s)**: unsigned or "HOMCO" stamped; $15–50.
- **Syroco**: resin that looks like carved wood or gilt metal; tap it; $10–40.
- **Imported wire decor (Taiwan/Hong Kong/Mexico, 1960s–80s)**: painted wire, faux rust; $15–60/pair.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Weight: a signed C. Jere sailboat of 20–30 in typically weighs 3–10 lb; wire decor weighs under 1–2 lb.
- Welds: visible brazed beads at joints; cheap decor has crimped or spot-welded thin wire.
- Finish: real patina varies in tone; paint-only spatter finishes are look-alikes.
- Signature location: lower front edge, on the base/hull, or a brass plate; check back for "Artisan House" label.
- Old vs new: newer Artisan House have crisp lacquered brass and printed labels; 1960s–70s have dulled patina and handwritten-style signatures.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Bent rods, broken brazed joints, missing elements (birds, drops), lacquer flaking, verdigris over-cleaning. Re-brazing is visible as bright brass. Broken elements cut value 30–60%. Do not polish: buyers want original patina.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern "Curtis Jere style" imports from India/China sold new at HomeGoods/Wayfair; some bear fake "C. Jere" signatures. Fake signatures tend to be painted or engraved rather than welded/raised, and appear on light construction. Artisan House reissues are legitimate but worth less.

## Market
Steady MCM demand; signed pieces sell in 1–6 weeks on eBay/Chairish/1stDibs. Unsigned wire decor sells slowly (weeks–months) at low prices. Lens frequently returns $1,000+ Jere comps for any metal wall art, a big false-positive source.

## What sellers usually miss
Signature is often hidden on the lower edge or behind patina; sellers with "brass wall art" or "metal birds" titles may have signed pieces. Conversely, sellers often call any metal wall art "Curtis Jere".

## Shipping / handling
Fragile to bending; double-box with cardboard backer, wrap each element. Flat-ish but long: small pieces $15–30, 30–40 in pieces $30–60 (oversize).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Curtis Jere" / "C. Jere" + "signed" + year + subject (Raindrops / Birds in Flight / sailboat / Koi / brutalist) + "metal wall sculpture" + width + "MCM". Junk: "Jere style" (unless unsigned), "brass decor", "wall hanging". `Curtis Jere Signed 1976 Birds in Flight Brass Metal Wall Sculpture 36in MCM`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for signed pieces; Chairish/1stDibs sell signed classics for more to decorators (slower, higher fee). Unsigned wire decor FB local only.
- **Price position:** signed at the sold median for the subject/size, accept −10%, floor 80%; OBO; cut 10% after 30 days. Unsigned at the $15–60 tier, firm.
- **Photos:** full piece on a plain wall; signature and year close up; welds; back and any label; profile for depth; a bent/missing element close up; next to a tape.
- **State:** "signed C. Jere [year]" only if seen (else "mid-century style metal wall art, unsigned"), width × height × depth, weight, material/finish, hanging hardware, bent or missing elements and patina by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** passenger-seat, flat but long; double-box with a cardboard backer, each element wrapped and immobilized, small $15–30, 30–40" $30–60 oversize.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Signed C. Jere [year] [subject] from Artisan House, [width]; the same piece lists at [$] on 1stDibs and unsigned copies are what sell for [$]."

## Buy rule
Price ≤ $200, signature visible or confirmed, fits passenger seat (≤ ~40 in), complete (no missing elements). Profit = resale × 0.87 − ~$30 ship out − ~$20 ship in − price ≥ max($50, price). Example: signed 28 in sailboat, resale $300 → 261 − 50 = $211 headroom → buy at ≤ $105 (Tier A ≤ $70). Unsigned wire/tin: always pass. No authentication service needed but never trust Lens alone.
