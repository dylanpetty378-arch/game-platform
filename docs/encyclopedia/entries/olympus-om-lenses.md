## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Small black/silver-nose lenses marked "OM-SYSTEM ZUIKO" on the front ring. Common and cheap: 50mm f/1.8, 28mm f/3.5, 135mm f/3.5, 75–150 zoom. Money lenses: 35mm f/2.8 Shift, 24mm f/2, 21mm f/2 and f/3.5, 85mm f/2, 90mm f/2 Macro, 100mm f/2, 180mm f/2.8, 250mm f/2, 350mm f/2.8. Same logic for Minolta (MC Rokkor 58/1.2, 85/1.7, 35/1.8 valuable; "Celtic" line is the budget version), Yashica ML (55/1.2 valuable; 50/1.7 common), Pentax K/Takumar, Canon FD. Store-brand lenses (JC Penney, Sears, Vivitar non-Series-1) are $10–30.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Olympus OM Zuiko** (1972–2002, general knowledge, verify): early "Auto-S/Auto-W/Auto-T" labelled versions and later "MC" multicoated versions; silver-nose = earlier (general knowledge, verify). Money lenses listed above.
- **Minolta MC/MD Rokkor**: 58/1.2, 85/1.7, 35/1.8 valuable; MC 58/1.4 decent; "Celtic" budget line ($25–40 for 200/4.5).
- **Yashica ML**: 55/1.2 valuable; 50/1.7 common.
- **Pentax K / Takumar (M42)**, **Canon FD**: same tiering logic — fast primes, wides and macros carry value (general knowledge, verify).
- **Store brands** (JC Penney, Sears, Vivitar non-Series-1): $10–30.
- Note on the 180mm: this entry lists both "180mm f/2.8" (money lens) and "180/2" (top tier) — both existed as Zuikos; the f/2 is the rare, expensive one (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Compact all-metal barrels, engraved markings, smooth focus, aperture ring on the lens front (OM design). Multicoating ("MC") and later serials are optically slightly better (general knowledge, verify). Adapters to mirrorless keep demand up for fast and unusual lenses.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Fungus/haze is the one thing to confirm (shine a light through the glass); fungus halves value (OM 35 Shift fungus copies $180–260). Also: oily or sticky aperture blades, stiff focus, separation, front-element scratches, dented filter threads. Ask for a back-lit photo through the lens.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
None significant. Risk is misidentification (e.g. 21/3.5 vs 21/2, Celtic vs Rokkor) and store-brand lenses titled as the camera brand.

## Market
Steady eBay, KEH, MPB and kamerastore demand from film shooters and adapters (general knowledge, verify). Common lenses are abundant; specialty lenses (shift, macro, fast wides) sell quickly to knowledgeable buyers.

## What sellers usually miss
Which Zuiko it is – they write "Olympus lens". Also "Celtic" vs "Rokkor" for Minolta. Fungus/haze is the one thing to confirm in the photos (shine a light through the glass).

## Shipping / handling
Under 1 lb, $8–10. Caps on both ends, bubble in a small box. eBay (13%) or KEH/MPB trade-in as a floor.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State focal length, aperture, version (Auto-/MC/serial), mount, glass condition (back-lit photo), aperture and focus action, caps/hood. Title keywords: "Olympus OM Zuiko Shift 35mm f/2.8 manual focus lens", "Minolta MC Rokkor-PG 58mm f/1.2".

## Buy rule
