# Snap-on hand tools and tool boxes
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge, Snap-on retail catalogue pricing, vintage tool collector forums (Garage Journal)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
The red/black mechanic's brand sold off trucks. Tells: "Snap-on" logo in the script wordmark stamped on the shank; ratchets with a flat "swivel" head and the serial code; screwdriver sets with hard clear acetate handles (red/black or coloured "Classic") – an 8–12 pc set retails $240–350; soft-grip (Instinct) sets similar. Tool boxes: red/black (or custom colour) KRA/KRL-series roll cabs with the Snap-on badge are $1,500–6,000 new; old 1950s–70s KR-series top/bottom chests $200–600 depending on condition. Sellers write "Snap-on", so the information gap is small – value comes from sets priced as a single tool, old boxes "painted black", and ratchets/sockets in "misc tool" lots.

## What sellers usually miss
That old black-repainted boxes still bring $300+ on eBay/FB if drawers work (buyers restore); that a "misc tools" lot with a Snap-on ratchet is $60 right there.

## Shipping / handling
Screwdriver sets 3–5 lb ($15). Tool boxes are freight ($150–300) – local flips only. eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 12 pc screwdriver set under $60; ratchets under $25; sockets by the tray under 30% of eBay. Pass when: new-in-box sets priced at retail ($200), boxes needing freight unless local. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
