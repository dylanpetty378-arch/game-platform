# Snap-on hand tools and tool boxes (Snap-on, Blue-Point)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · upgraded 2026-10-13 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge, Snap-on retail catalogue pricing, vintage tool collector forums (Garage Journal)
Slug: snap-on-tools · Aliases: Snap-on, Snap on, Snapon, Blue-Point, mechanic's tools, Snap-on screwdriver set, Snap-on ratchet, Snap-on socket set, Snap-on tool box, KRA chest, KRL roll cab
Summary: Singles and loose sockets $5–20; used 12 pc screwdriver sets, ratchets and speed handles $60–180; tray socket sets, restored 1970s chests and T-handle ratcheting drivers $200–500; large KRL roll cabs and complete 3/4"–1" sets $800–4,000.
Buy rule (short): 12 pc screwdriver set under $60, ratchets under $25, sockets by the tray under 30% of eBay; pass on new sets at retail and boxes that need freight unless local.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
The red/black mechanic's brand sold off trucks.
- **Wordmark**: the "Snap-on" script wordmark is stamped on the shank.
- **Ratchets**: a flat "swivel" head and a serial code.
- **Screwdriver sets**: hard clear acetate handles (red/black or coloured "Classic"). An 8–12 pc set retails $240–350. Soft-grip (Instinct) sets are similar.
- **Tool boxes**: red/black (or custom colour) KRA/KRL-series roll cabs with the Snap-on badge are $1,500–6,000 new. Old 1950s–70s KR-series top/bottom chests are $200–600 depending on condition.

Sellers write "Snap-on", so the information gap is small. Value comes from sets priced as a single tool, old boxes "painted black", and ratchets/sockets in "misc tool" lots. See also snap-on-vintage-toolboxes, which overlaps this entry for boxes.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Snap-on**: the primary line. Collectible: full socket sets in trays, 1970s KR-562 chests restored, T-handle ratcheting drivers (TMRT8 $60–80). Anniversary and limited tools sit in the top tier.
- **Blue-Point**: the sister brand, sold off the same trucks. Singles are $5–20 and worth less than Snap-on equivalents.
- **Instinct**: soft-grip screwdrivers, priced like the Classic sets.
- **Tool boxes**: KRA/KRL roll cabs (new $1,500–6,000; used large KRL $800–4,000). KR-series top and bottom chests from the 1950s–70s.
- **Other truck brands** (Mac, Matco, Cornwell) have similar markets but lower resale than Snap-on (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Chrome-vanadium tools with a polished chrome finish. The stamped date code on sockets and wrenches dates them (general knowledge, verify).
- Fakes and look-alikes have a wrong-font logo, rough chrome and the wrong weight.
- Boxes: heavy-gauge steel, ball-bearing slides on later boxes, and a badge style that dates them (script badge = older).
- Black-repainted old boxes still sell if the drawers work.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Tools: worn ratchet pawls, chipped chrome, rounded sockets, cracked acetate handles. Snap-on's warranty makes even worn ratchets serviceable and kit-rebuildable (general knowledge, verify).
- Boxes: rust, dents, sticking drawers, missing handles and keys. Drawers that work matter more than paint.
- Ask for photos of the stampings, ratchet mechanism function, drawer slides open, and the box interior and badge.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Snap-on tools are sold online, mostly sets and ratchets, often new-in-box at too-good prices. Tells: logo font, stamped part numbers that don't match the catalogue, and finish (general knowledge, verify). Collector "limited edition" sets (e.g. NASCAR, Earnhardt) are a separate, lower-value market (see snap-on-vintage-toolboxes).

## Market
Very liquid. Mechanics and DIYers buy constantly on eBay and FB Marketplace. Hand tools sell in days. Boxes are local-only because of freight. No strong seasonality; tax-refund season (Feb–Apr) is mildly stronger (general knowledge, verify). Sellers almost always name the brand, so margin comes from lots and mispriced sets.

## What sellers usually miss
That old black-repainted boxes still bring $300+ on eBay/FB if the drawers work (buyers restore). That a "misc tools" lot with a Snap-on ratchet is $60 right there.

## Shipping / handling
Screwdriver sets 3–5 lb ($15). Tool boxes are freight ($150–300), so local flips only. eBay 13%. Top-and-bottom combos and roll cabs are bigger than a passenger seat and excluded by the size rule.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Title: "Snap-on [part number] [description] [set count] USA", e.g. "Snap-on SGDX60 12 pc Classic Screwdriver Set". State the part numbers, count and completeness, ratchet function, and any wear. Photograph the stampings and part numbers, and the whole set laid out.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a 12 pc screwdriver set is under $60, ratchets are under $25, or sockets by the tray are under 30% of eBay. Pass when: new-in-box sets priced at retail ($200), or boxes needing freight unless local. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (roll cabs and top+bottom combos are excluded).
