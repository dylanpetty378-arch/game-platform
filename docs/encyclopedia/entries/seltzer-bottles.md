## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
A heavy glass (or metal) pressure bottle with a lever-valve head that dispenses carbonated water. Two families: (1) bottler-returnable siphons - thick, often colored (cobalt, green, amber, clear) glass with acid-etched dairy/bottling company names, pewter/zinc heads; (2) home "charger" siphons - Sparklets (UK, British Oxygen Co.) and Soda King/Kidde (US) using CO2 cartridges, often encased in protective wire mesh with chrome heads, 1930s-60s.

## Makers, brands and variants
- Sparklets Ltd (London, 1900s-1960s, later BOC): wire-mesh "basket weave" covered bottles, chrome or brass heads stamped "SPARKLETS" and "Made in England"; red or blue fill line; colours clear, green, blue, amber (colors are worth more).
- Soda King (Kidde, USA, 1930s-50s): Art Deco chrome and colored-glass/bakelite designs, some by Walter Dorwin Teague-era designers; boxed sets with chargers sell well.
- Sparklets "Hostmaster", "Streamline"; Thermos; Hoffmann (Austria, later aluminum).
- Bottler siphons: Czechoslovakia-made glass for US bottlers 1900s-50s, acid-etched with city names (NYC, Brooklyn, Pittsburgh); cobalt blue is most popular.
- Modern: iSi, Mosa (Taiwan) - aluminum/stainless, $20-40.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Look for: maker name on the head; colored glass; acid-etched lettering; full mesh with no broken wires; chrome without pitting; intact syphon tube inside. Old heads are pewter, brass, or chrome-plated brass with screw-on caps; modern ones are aluminum. A red stripe painted on the glass inside the mesh is the Sparklets fill line.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips at the neck and base, cloudy/sick glass inside, missing or cracked glass tube, broken mesh wires, corroded heads, missing nozzle. Most buyers display only - working valves are a bonus. Do not pressurize old bottles (glass can burst).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reproduction etched "seltzer" bottles exist (2000s decor), with too-crisp etching and lightweight heads. Modern Sparklets-style mesh bottles are rare.

## Market
Steady barware/mancave demand; mid tier sells 2-6 weeks. UK buyers abundant on eBay UK (lots of supply there too).

## What sellers usually miss
Color: a green/blue/amber glass Sparklets is worth roughly double clear. Bottler names: small-town etched names sell to local collectors at a premium.

## Shipping / handling
Glass, 3-4 lb, tall (~13"); double box, $15-20. Fragile neck.
