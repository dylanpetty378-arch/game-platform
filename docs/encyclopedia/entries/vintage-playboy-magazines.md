## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
The men's magazine founded by Hugh Hefner (first issue December 1953, Marilyn Monroe cover, undated). Cover shows month/year and price ("ONE DOLLAR" = c. 1971–74; 50¢/60¢/75¢ = 1950s–60s). Value is set by year, the cover subject, and completeness of the centerfold.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **1953–1959**: 50¢ issues, smaller page counts; the first ~10 issues are scarce.
- **1960s**: more common; key issues with notable interviews (Beatles Feb 1965, Martin Luther King Jan 1965, Miles Davis Sep 1962).
- **1970s–80s**: printed in millions; most issues $5–15. Exceptions: Madonna (Sep 1985), Vanna White (May 1987), Farrah Fawcett (Dec 1978/Aug 1995), Bo Derek, Marilyn special issues, Jimmy Carter interview (Nov 1976).
- Foreign editions, "Playboy's Party Jokes", Newsstand Specials and Lingerie specials sell low.
- Related: Playboy Club keys, bunny items, early issues of Oui, Esquire, Gent, Rogue (1950s men's mags can be worth more).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Collectors want **centerfold attached** and intact, no address label or a light one, no clipped coupons, tight spine, bright cover. Check if pages were removed.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Missing centerfold (most common killer), loose/torn covers, mailing labels, water damage, mildew smell, cut-outs. Graded (CGC) copies only matter for key issues.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Facsimile/reprint of Issue #1 (1970s–2000s reproductions, "Collector's Edition") often mistaken for the 1953 original — real #1 has no date on the cover and is 44 pages; reprints usually say "reprint" or have modern paper and barcodes.

## Market
High volume on eBay but saturated; most 70s issues sell slowly, often in lots. Some categories on eBay restrict adult content (listed under Collectibles > Magazines, adult-only category; images must be compliant).

## What sellers usually miss
Notable non-cover content (interviews/fiction) adds value; 1950s issues mixed into later lots.

## Shipping / handling
Media Mail eligible? No — magazines with advertising are not Media Mail eligible; use Ground Advantage. ~1 lb each; lots of 20 = ~15–20 lb, $20–35.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
List issue, cover model, notable contents, centerfold present, labels, condition. Adult category settings required.

## Buy rule
Profit = median × 0.87 − shipping both ways − price ≥ max($50, price). Single 70s issues never qualify. A 1970s lot only works at ≤ $1–2/issue for 30+ issues sold as a lot ($100–150). 1950s lots worth asking about. Fits a car seat.
