## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Entertech/LJN **Photon** (1986): grey-and-red pistol phaser, chest/back sensor pod, helmet; "Photon" in italic script. Worlds of Wonder **Lazer Tag** (launched November 1986, ~$50 per unit at retail per 2warpstoneptune): white-and-red StarLyte pistol, StarSensor chest unit, StarHelmet, StarCap; later the "Starlyte Pro" rifle. Both rarely complete and rarely working (NiCd/contacts corrode). Infrared, battery-powered. Look-alikes: 2000s Tiger/Hasbro Lazer Tag reissues and modern IR tag sets (bright plastics, newer copyright).
- Moulded copyright line: "© 1986 Worlds of Wonder" or Entertech/LJN on the 1980s units; later units carry Shoot the Moon, Tiger Electronics, Hasbro or Nerf marks.
- Title words: "Lazer Tag 1986", "Worlds of Wonder", "StarLyte", "StarSensor", "Photon phaser", "Entertech", "80s laser tag".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Worlds of Wonder Lazer Tag** (1986–88 in the first-run note; Wikipedia gives WoW as owner 1986–1989, ceasing operations in late 1990): StarLyte pistol, StarSensor, StarHelmet, StarCap, Starlyte Pro rifle (higher value boxed), team sets. Created by Paul Rago (Wikipedia). A top hit toy of 1986–87 and underproduced when demand peaked (Wikipedia; 2warpstoneptune). Tie-ins: *Lazer Tag Academy* (NBC animated series 1986–87), gamebooks, and a late-1980s home-computer game (Wikipedia).
- **Entertech/LJN Photon** (1986): home version of the Photon arena game; arena gear (from Photon centres) is rarer. Arena gear was heavy, with about 15 lb of battery packs and equipment (Wikipedia). The Photon corporation sold its assets and ceased operations in 1989 (Wikipedia). LJN's Entertech toy-weapon line ran 1986–1990 (2warpstoneptune).
- **Later owners/licensees** (Wikipedia): Shoot the Moon Products (1990–96); Tiger Electronics (1996–98, including Star Wars-themed blasters and a poorly selling 1999 Episode I line); Hasbro (1998–present). Lazer Tag Team Ops (LTTO) launched at Toy Fair 2004 by Tiger, backward compatible with later sets; Nerf branding replaced Tiger labels from 2012, when an iPhone/iPod-based augmented-reality line launched. All low value (general knowledge, verify).

### Quick ID checklist
- Copyright line on the moulding: 1986 Worlds of Wonder or Entertech/LJN (vintage) vs Tiger, Hasbro, Nerf (later).
- Colour: WoW white-and-red; Photon grey-and-red.
- Pieces present: pistol/phaser, chest sensor, helmet or cap, rifle, belts/straps.
- Battery compartment photo: clean contacts vs green/white corrosion.
- Box and inserts, manuals, rule booklet.
- Video or statement of sound/light on trigger and sensor registering hits.
- For Photon: home-set phaser vs heavy arena pack/helmet (arena = rarer).

### Golden-egg tells
- Photon arena gear (centre equipment, heavy battery pack and helmet) — ~10–20x a loose home phaser; $250–400 — large pack with heavy battery compartment, arena-style helmet, no retail packaging.
- Sealed or new-in-box WoW Lazer Tag sets — ~5–10x loose; $250–400 sealed, $100–180 complete NOB — factory-sealed box, unpunched inserts.
- Boxed Starlyte Pro rifle — $120–250 — rifle-length WoW white-and-red unit in its box.
- Complete working WoW team set (StarLyte + StarSensor + StarHelmet) with box — $120–250 vs $10–30 for a single untested pistol — all three pieces with matching box, clean battery bays.
- Tested working phaser + sensor pair — $40–80 vs $10–30 untested — video of hit registration.
- Not golden: Tiger/Hasbro/Nerf reissues and LTTO (2004+) — low despite "Lazer Tag" name.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Moulded ABS plastic, IR LEDs/sensors, battery compartments. Working tells: clean battery contacts, sound and light on trigger, sensors register hits. Old vs new: WoW/LJN copyright 1986 moulded on units vs Hasbro/Tiger marks.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Battery-corrosion damage is the main killer (corroded contacts/boards); missing battery doors, broken straps/helmets, yellowed plastic, untested units. Ask for battery compartment photos and a working test (video).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No fakes; later Tiger/Hasbro reissues may be listed as "vintage". (general knowledge, verify) Check the copyright line.

## Market
1980s-nostalgia buyers; modest eBay volume; boxed working sets sell, loose untested units linger. Nov–Dec slight bump. (general knowledge, verify)

## What sellers usually miss
Nothing – both brands are always named. Only buy sealed/boxed working sets cheap. Occasionally Photon arena gear is listed as a home set.

## Shipping / handling
2–4 lb per set, bulky helmets – $15–25 each way. Remove batteries before shipping.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State brand, components included, box/inserts/manuals, tested working (how), battery compartments clean. Photograph each piece, battery compartments, box sides. Title keywords: "Worlds of Wonder Lazer Tag StarLyte StarSensor 1986 tested", "Photon phaser vintage 80s".

### What to tell the buyer
"Original 1986 Worlds of Wonder Lazer Tag [pieces], copyright 1986 moulded on each unit — not a later Tiger/Hasbro reissue. Tested [date] with fresh batteries: [sound/light on trigger, sensor registers hits] (video). Battery compartments [clean / light corrosion, shown]. Includes [box/inserts/manual]." Same structure for Entertech Photon, stating home set vs arena gear.

## Buy rule
Buy when: boxed complete working set under $40. Pass when: loose untested units at $50+ each (6850). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Fits a passenger seat.
