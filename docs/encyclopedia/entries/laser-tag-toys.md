## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Entertech/LJN **Photon** (1986): grey-and-red pistol phaser, chest/back sensor pod, helmet; "Photon" in italic script. Worlds of Wonder **Lazer Tag** (1986): white-and-red StarLyte pistol, StarSensor chest unit, StarHelmet, StarCap; later the "Starlyte Pro" rifle. Both rarely complete and rarely working (NiCd/contacts corrode). Infrared, battery-powered. Look-alikes: 2000s Tiger/Hasbro Lazer Tag reissues and modern IR tag sets (bright plastics, newer copyright).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Worlds of Wonder Lazer Tag** (1986–88): StarLyte pistol, StarSensor, StarHelmet, StarCap, Starlyte Pro rifle (higher value boxed), team sets. WoW went bankrupt 1988 (general knowledge, verify).
- **Entertech/LJN Photon** (1986): home version of the Photon arena game; arena gear (from Photon centres) is rarer.
- Later reissues: Tiger Electronics / Hasbro Lazer Tag (1990s–2010s) – low value (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Moulded ABS plastic, IR LEDs/sensors, battery compartments. Working tells: clean battery contacts, sound and light on trigger, sensors register hits. Old vs new: WoW/LJN copyright 1986 moulded on units vs Hasbro/Tiger marks.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Battery-corrosion damage is the main killer (corroded contacts/boards); missing battery doors, broken straps/helmets, yellowed plastic, untested units. Ask for battery compartment photos and a working test (video).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No fakes; later Tiger/Hasbro reissues may be listed as "vintage". (general knowledge, verify)
