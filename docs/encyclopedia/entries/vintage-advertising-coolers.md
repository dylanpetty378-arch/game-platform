## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- **1950s metal soda coolers**: embossed "Drink Coca-Cola in Bottles" sides, red paint, galvanized interior, bottle opener on the side, makers Progress Refrigerator (Louisville), Action Mfg, Cronstroms, Acton, Hemp. Small "6-pack" or "picnic" sizes.
- **Coleman/Thermos/Poloron metal** (1950s–70s): painted/plaid, often with brand names.
- **Plastic promotional** (1970s–2000s): Gott, Igloo, Coleman bodies printed with Snap-on, Mac Tools, Matco, Budweiser etc.; Gott made many Snap-on coolers (rounded lid, white top, red body). Fades to pink.

## Makers, brands and variants
Progress Refrigerator Co. (Coca-Cola licensed 1950s), Action Mfg (Arkansas City, KS), Cronstroms (Minneapolis), Hemp & Co. (Macomb, IL), Coleman (Wichita), Thermos (Norwich), Poloron, Gott (Winfield, KS; bought by Rubbermaid 1985), Igloo (Houston).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original paint with embossing, galvanized liner with drain plug, intact bottle opener and handles = value; repaint and rust-through = low. Plastic: sun fading, cracked lids, missing drain plugs.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rust-through floors, dents, repaint (halves value), broken hinges/latches, odor/mold in plastic.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Coca-Cola licensed reproduction coolers (1990s–2000s) and modern "retro" coolers. Check for maker stamps, wear, period construction.

## Market
Summer peak; tool-brand collectors buy locally. Shipping costs ($40–70) make plastic coolers poor eBay items.

## What sellers usually miss
Small Coca-Cola metal picnic coolers in garages listed as "old cooler".

## Shipping / handling
Bulky (20×14×14 in+); $35–70. Metal coolers ride well; plastic lids crack.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Maker, size, original paint, interior condition, drain plug, opener, odor.

## Buy rule
Price ≤ $200; profit = sold median × 0.87 − ~$45 ship out − ~$45 ship in − price ≥ max($50, price). Only small embossed metal soda coolers at very low prices clear this; plastic promos never. Most coolers barely fit a passenger seat. No authentication.
