## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Heavy outdoor workwear from C.C. Filson (Seattle, founded 1897) (general knowledge, verify). Tin Cloth: stiff, oil-finish waxed cotton canvas in tan/"dark tan"/otter green, often with a darker mottled waxed look. Mackinaw: dense virgin wool in plaid or solid. Label: "C.C. Filson Co. Seattle WA" inside the collar or on the waistband; older labels say "Since 1897" and "Made in USA". Chaps are tie-on leg covers for brush hunting.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Tin Cloth chaps** (pair $150–250; single replacement chap $40–70).
- **Mackinaw Cruiser** (wool, $150–300).
- Other Filson lines (tin cloth cruiser/packer coat, double Mackinaw, Mackinaw vest, Oil Finish Hat) likely sell but are not yet researched (general knowledge, verify).
- Older Made-in-USA labels are preferred over recent imports (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Tin Cloth is re-waxable; heavy weight, brass/metal snaps and buttons, double-layer seat/shoulders. Mackinaw: 24-oz-class wool, felted texture (general knowledge, verify). Fakes/knockoffs feel lighter and lack the label detail.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Cracked or dried-out wax (can be re-waxed), frayed cuffs, torn pockets, moth holes in wool, stains, mildew smell. Worn-in workwear is accepted; holes and odours cut value (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Some knockoff waxed jackets with Filson-like labels exist (general knowledge, verify).
