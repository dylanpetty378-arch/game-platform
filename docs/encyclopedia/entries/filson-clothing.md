## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- Heavy outdoor workwear from C.C. Filson (Seattle, founded 1897 by Clinton C. Filson; HistoryLink).
- **Tin Cloth:** stiff, oil-finish waxed cotton duck, the heaviest Filson canvas, in tan/"dark tan"/otter green, often with a darker mottled waxed look; smells waxy when warm. **Shelter Cloth:** medium-weight cotton duck, oil or dry finish, more tightly woven. **Cover Cloth:** lighter UK cotton poplin, lightly waxed, softer, dressier (Fedora Lounge 2010).
- **Mackinaw:** dense virgin wool in plaid or solid. Label: "C.C. Filson Co. Seattle WA" inside the collar or on the waistband; older labels are said to say "Since 1897" and "Made in USA" (general knowledge, verify).
- **Cruiser:** Filson's signature design, patented 1914: hip-length coat with a large rear "map pocket" across the back and multiple bellows chest pockets (general knowledge for the pocket layout, verify).
- Chaps are tie-on leg covers for brush hunting.
- Model numbers appear on labels and in catalogues (e.g. 620 "Tin Jacket", 420, #53 safari jacket, 641N Tin Cloth Field Jacket). Some 1990s–2000s labels carry a manufacture date (e.g. "July 96" Seattle-made, "Feb 2000") (Fedora Lounge).
- Look-alikes: Barbour (British waxed cotton, softer, corduroy collar, tartan lining), Carhartt duck, Woolrich and Johnson Woolen Mills plaid wool coats.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Tin Cloth chaps** (pair $150–250; single replacement chap $40–70).
- **Mackinaw Cruiser** (wool, $150–300; Terapeak median $250).
- **Tin Cloth jacket/cruiser** (Terapeak median $274; 620 Tin Jacket is unlined and was originally sold with a wool liner option).
- Other Filson lines (tin cloth cruiser/packer coat, Double Mackinaw, Mackinaw vest, Oil Finish Hat) likely sell but are not yet researched (general knowledge, verify). A vintage "Double Mackinaw Cruiser, Made in USA" is a listing title seen on eBay.
- **Signature/collab pieces:** e.g. Chris Stapleton Signature Mackinaw Cruiser (eBay title seen); collabs often carry a premium when new-with-tags (verify).
- Older Made-in-USA labels are preferred over recent imports (general knowledge, verify).
- **Ownership eras (dating context):** Filson family 1897–1965ish (Winifred Filson president from 1919); Ted Reed bought it in 1970 and switched wool supply to Oregon's Paris Woolen Mill and Jefferson Woolen Mill; Stan Kohls bought it in 1981; Brentwood Associates/Doug Williams 2005; Bedrock Manufacturing (Dallas) 2012 (HistoryLink). Collectors prize pre-2005 pieces (Fedora Lounge).

### Quick ID checklist
- Label: "C.C. Filson Co. Seattle" + "Made in USA"; any model number (e.g. 620, 420) and any manufacture date; any union label.
- Fabric: Tin Cloth (stiff, heavy, waxy, darker at wear points) vs Shelter/Cover Cloth (lighter) vs Mackinaw wool.
- Fibre tag: 100% virgin wool vs blends (85% wool / 15% Orlon is a 1950s blend; VFG thread).
- Measurements: pit-to-pit and length; vintage tag sizes are unreliable (tin jackets run large, older labels may be inflated) (Fedora Lounge).
- Wear: re-waxable fading at elbows/cuffs is fine; look for holes, moth damage, tears at pocket corners.
- Hardware: heavy snaps and buttons, double-layer shoulders/seat on tin cloth.
- Country of origin: "Made in USA" vs imported — check the inner tags.
- Smell: ask about mildew/smoke (wax and wool hold odour).

### Golden-egg tells
- **Pre-2005 Seattle-made Tin Cloth or Mackinaw (dated/older label)** — premium over current-production used (verify; Terapeak cruiser p75 $375 vs median $250) — older white "C.C. Filson Co. Seattle" label, model number, manufacture date, "Made in USA".
- **1950s-or-earlier Filson with union label** — collector/heritage premium (verify) — ACWA union label, wool/Orlon blend fibre tag, older label style.
- **Double Mackinaw Cruiser (double-layer wool)** — upper end of Mackinaw range and above (verify) — "Double Mackinaw" on label, very thick two-layer wool, made in USA.
- **Full pair of Tin Cloth chaps vs single chap** — ~3–4× ($150–250 vs $40–70) — two matching legs, belt/ties present.
- **Tin Cloth jacket vs plain waxed jacket** — Filson Tin Cloth median $274 vs no-name waxed jackets far lower — Filson label, very stiff heavy duck, map pocket.
- **Signature/collab Mackinaw Cruiser new with tags** — above standard Cruiser median (verify) — collab name on label/hangtag, NWT.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Tin Cloth is re-waxable (Filson Oil Finish Wax); heavy weight, brass/metal snaps and buttons, double-layer seat/shoulders. Mackinaw: 24-oz-class wool, felted texture (general knowledge, verify). Fakes/knockoffs feel lighter and lack the label detail.
- Tin Cloth is stiff new and must be broken in; heavy wear softens it and produces pale abrasion lines — that is desirable "patina" to heritage buyers.
- Wool and tin cloth can shrink with age (Fedora Lounge).
- Construction tells: felled seams, bar-tacked pocket corners, heavy-gauge thread.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Cracked or dried-out wax (can be re-waxed), frayed cuffs, torn pockets, moth holes in wool, stains, mildew smell. Worn-in workwear is accepted; holes and odours cut value (general knowledge, verify).
- Repairs: machine darns and patches are acceptable to workwear buyers if disclosed; re-waxing darkens colour.
- Ask: odours, moth holes (hold up to light), re-waxed or not, pit-to-pit and length, any repairs.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Some knockoff waxed jackets with Filson-like labels exist (general knowledge, verify).
- Filson's own reissues and current production use older patterns; dating relies on label and tags, not cut.
- Sellers sometimes call any waxed jacket "Filson-style"; insist on a label photo.

## Market

## What sellers usually miss
- Likely the label era, the size, and the specific model name (general knowledge, verify).
- Manufacture dates on labels, Made in USA, and "Double Mackinaw" vs single.

## Shipping / handling
2–5 lb, soft-pack poly mailer or box, ~$10–15 (general knowledge, verify).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State model, size, pit-to-pit and length, colour, label (Made in USA), wax condition, holes/stains. Photograph the label, front/back, and any wear. Title: "Filson [model] Tin Cloth / Mackinaw, size, Made in USA" (general knowledge, verify).

### What to tell the buyer
"Filson [model / style number] in [Tin Cloth waxed cotton duck / Mackinaw virgin wool], tagged size [X] — measures [pit-to-pit] in. across the chest and [length] in. long (vintage Filson sizing varies, please go by measurements). Label: C.C. Filson Co., Seattle, Made in USA[, dated ...]. Condition: [wax worn at elbows and cuffs, re-waxable / no moth holes / small repaired tear, shown]. No smoke or mildew odour." Add history only as fact: "Filson was founded in Seattle in 1897; the Cruiser design was patented in 1914."
