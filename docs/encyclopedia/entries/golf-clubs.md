## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Sets lean against a wall or sit in a bag; the model is stamped on the sole/cavity. Tells that matter:
- **Putters**: **Scotty Cameron** (Newport, Newport 2, Studio Stainless, Circa 62, Pro Platinum) $200–600 used, Tour-only/rare $1,000+; **Ping Anser** 1960s–70s "Scottsdale"/"Phoenix" address $150–800, 1980s Karsten Anser $40–80; **Odyssey** White Hot/Tri-Hot $40–90; **Bettinardi** $150–300; **Bullseye** $20–50. (See the separate `golf-putters` entry, which gives Scottsdale Ansers $300–1,000 and Scotty Newport/Studio $200–450.)
- **Irons**: **Ping Eye 2** (1982–90) sets $80–200 (BeCu copper-colour $200–350, "square groove" 1985–90 more); **Mizuno MP** forged (MP-33, MP-32, MP-14) $150–300; **Titleist 690/714/718 MB/CB** $200–400; **Hogan Apex** forged (1970s–90s) $80–200; Hogan "Bounce Sole" 1960s $100–250; modern game-improvement sets 5+ yrs old (Callaway Big Bertha, TaylorMade Burner) $60–150; **Ram, Northwestern, Wilson Ultra, Spalding, Dunlop, "Pro-line"/"Kit" clubs** $5–30 a set.
- **Woods/drivers**: current-gen (TaylorMade Qi10/Stealth 2, Callaway Paradym/Ai Smoke, Titleist TSR, Ping G430) $150–350; 2–4 gens old $50–120; 2000s titanium (Ping Rapture, Titleist 975D/983K, R7) $20–50; **persimmon** woods (MacGregor Tommy Armour 693/945, Toney Penna, Wood Bros, Louisville) $30–150 each for premium, $5–15 for Wilson/Spalding.
- **Hickory** (pre-1935): common smooth-face/line-scored irons by Nicoll, Spalding, MacGregor, Wilson, Burke $15–50 each; long-nose woods, Tom Morris, Forgan, smooth-face "cleek" marked rare makers $200–2,000+. "George Nicoll" with hand-forged stamp is common.
- **Bags**: Sunday/stand bags $20–60; Jones/Vessel/MacKenzie leather $100–300; Titleist/TaylorMade cart bags $40–80.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Scotty Cameron (Titleist)**: putters; Circle T / Tour-only top.
- **Ping (Karsten)**: Anser putters (sole address dates them), Eye 2 irons (BeCu copper and square-groove versions more), Rapture/G-series drivers.
- **Mizuno MP** forged blades, **Titleist MB/CB** (690/714/718): collectible irons.
- **Ben Hogan**: Apex forged (1970s–90s), Bounce Sole (1960s); Edge is the more common later line.
- **MacGregor / Tommy Armour** persimmon (693, 945, Eye-O-Matic): collectible to top.
- **Toney Penna, Wood Bros, Louisville**: premium persimmon.
- **Odyssey, Bettinardi, Bullseye**: putters across tiers.
- **Current OEM drivers** (TaylorMade, Callaway, Titleist, Ping): value drops fast by generation.
- **Hickory makers**: Nicoll, Spalding, MacGregor, Wilson, Burke (common); Tom Morris, Forgan, long-nose woods, rare cleek marks (top).
- **Budget**: Ram, Northwestern, Wilson Ultra, Spalding, Dunlop, "Pro-line"/"Kit".
- **Leather bags**: Jones, Vessel, MacKenzie.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Forged carbon-steel blades (Mizuno, Titleist MB, Hogan Apex) vs cast cavity-backs; BeCu (beryllium copper) Ping heads are copper-coloured; persimmon woods with inserts and whipping; hickory shafts with hand-forged heads and cleek marks. Grips and shafts matter: original vs regripped, steel vs graphite, stiffness (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Sky marks and dents on drivers, cracked faces (kills value), worn grooves, rusted heads, worn-out grips, bent shafts, missing headcovers; persimmon with cracked inserts or loose whipping; hickory with cracked shafts or re-shafting. Sets missing clubs sell well below full sets (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Scotty Cameron putters and fake current-gen drivers are common online; Tour/Circle T claims need authentication; hickory reproductions exist (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Very liquid eBay market for current clubs; prices fall each new release cycle. Spring (Mar–May) is the peak buying season (general knowledge, verify). Collectors buy Scotty Cameron, vintage Ping and persimmon; GolfWRX classifieds and 2nd Swing trade-in as alternatives.

## What sellers usually miss
"Golf clubs $50" set photos with a Scotty Cameron putter poking out of the bag, or Ping Eye 2 BeCu irons (copper colour) called "old Ping irons". Also Ping Anser putters: the address on the sole (Scottsdale/Phoenix vs "Karsten Mfg") separates $400 from $40.

## Shipping / handling
Single putter/driver $15–25 each way (long box); full set $50–80 – sets rarely clear the rule unless local. eBay 13%; GolfWRX classifieds / 2nd Swing trade-in as alternatives.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State maker, model, club numbers in the set, loft/lie if known, shaft brand/flex/material, grip condition, length, hand (RH/LH), headcover. Photograph sole stampings, face, back/cavity, shaft labels, grips. Title: "[Maker] [Model] irons 3-PW, [shaft] [flex], RH".

## Buy rule
Buy when: Scotty Cameron under $100, Ping Anser Scottsdale/Phoenix under $60, Mizuno MP/Titleist MB set under $80, current-gen driver under $80. Pass when: Ram/Northwestern/Wilson/Spalding sets, any 2000s driver, hickory singles by common makers, single Odyssey/Ping putters over $30. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Singles and a bagged set fit a passenger seat.
