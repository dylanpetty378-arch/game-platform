# Vintage saxophones (Buescher, Conn, King, Martin, Selmer)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold – Buescher Aristocrat alto, used, $195 (item 116757725844, 2026); saxophone.org forum threads on Aristocrat/S-40; knowledge of Conn/King/Selmer ranges

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Engraved bell tells the maker: "Buescher Aristocrat / True Tone / 400", "C.G. Conn 6M/10M/Naked Lady", "King Super 20 / Zephyr", "The Martin Committee", "Selmer Mark VI / Super Balanced Action / Bundy (student)". Serial number near the thumb rest dates it. Student horns (Bundy, Conn Director, Buescher Elkhart, Yamaha YAS-23) are the bulk of listings and worth $100–300. Pads/corks shot is normal – "needs repad" costs $400–700 and is why untested horns sell cheap.

## What sellers usually miss
Model and era: "Buescher saxophone" with a 1920s True Tone or a 400 series is worth 3–5x a 1960s Aristocrat. Also the Selmer "Bundy" vs Selmer Paris distinction (Bundy is the student line).

## Shipping / handling
In case ~10–12 lb, $35–50 each way. Rule: untested horns are a repair gamble – Dylan's rule says pass.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a pro-tier horn (Conn 6M/10M, King Super 20, Martin, Selmer Paris) is under $200 and the photos show keys complete. Pass when: student/mid tier untested – shipping both ways eats the margin. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
