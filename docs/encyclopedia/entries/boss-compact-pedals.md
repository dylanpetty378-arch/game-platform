## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Boss: the rectangular compact with hinged pedal and thumbscrew battery door. Model name on the front (CE-2, OD-1, DS-1, SD-1, DM-2, CE-3…). Flip it: silver thumbscrew + "Made in Japan" on the bottom label (1977–~1990, "black label"/"silver label") vs black thumbscrew + "Made in Taiwan" (later, cheap). Sellers write "Boss pedal", "chorus pedal", "overdrive pedal", often with the model.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Boss (Roland)** – Japan era (1977–~1990) vs Taiwan (later). Key collectible models: CE-2, OD-1, SD-1 silver screw, DM-2/DM-3, PH-1, SP-1, HM-2 Japan; top: VB-2, SG-1, DC-2 Dimension C, early DF-2, CE-1 (large box). Decent: MIJ CE-3, CH-1, PH-1r, GE-7, DD-3 (Japan).
- **Boutique** – Skreddy (Rubber Soul $200–260), Analog Man, Kasleder; Klon Centaur (top).
- **Others** – Seymour Duncan Tweak Fuzz ($70–100), Supro 1303 Boost ($60–90); Behringer clones (junk).
- Label colours (black/silver label) and serial numbers help date Japan units (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Die-cast metal housing, rubber footplate, bottom label with country and serial. Early Japan units: silver thumbscrew, "ACA" vs "PSA" power adaptor markings on some (general knowledge, verify). Taiwan: black thumbscrew. Original box and manual add value.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Scratches and paint wear are normal and accepted. Value-killers: non-working circuits, scratchy pots, failing switch (FET switching problems), modded circuits (sometimes a plus, often a minus), missing battery door/rubber. Housing-only (empty shell) is worth a fraction ($40–70 for an OD-1 shell). Ask for a demo video (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Boss Waza Craft reissues (e.g. DM-2W, SD-1W, CE-2W) are modern and distinct from originals (general knowledge, verify). Fake Japan labels and housing swaps (Japan shell with later board) exist – check the board and label (general knowledge, verify). Klon clones are common.

## Market
Very liquid; Reverb is the main venue, prices are well known to musicians. Fast sales at fair prices (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Japan vs Taiwan; silver screw; the model letters (CE-2 vs CE-3 is 2×); "Dimension C"; original box. Craigslist musicians usually DO know the model — margins are thin unless the seller misreads Japan/Taiwan.

## Shipping / handling
<1 lb, $6–9 each way. Reverb (fees ~8%) beats eBay.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State model, country (Japan/Taiwan), label colour, serial, screw colour, tested working (demo), any mods, box/manual. Photograph front, bottom label, inside (board) for Japan claims, and the box. Keywords: "Boss" + model + "MIJ"/"Made in Japan" + label colour + effect type.

## Buy rule
Buy when: MIJ CE-2/OD-1/DM-2/SD-1-silver ≤ $70, VB-2/DC-2 ≤ $150, boutique ≤ 40% of Reverb used. Pass when: Taiwan-era, housing-only, or seller already quotes Reverb price. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees (~8% Reverb, ~13% eBay) and shipping both ways. Must fit a passenger seat (it does).
