Summary: common 1920s–50s single ads $5–15; early (1900–1925) color full-page ads and covers $12–35; illustrator-signed (Parrish, Leyendecker, Rockwell, Coles Phillips) or auto/Coca-Cola $30–150 (general knowledge, verify)
Buy rule (short): Buy lots of pre-1930 full-page color ads/covers at ≤ $2 per page, especially Leyendecker/Parrish/Phillips/Rockwell and auto/tire/soda ads; pass single common ads.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Pages removed from magazines. Value drivers: date (pre-1925 color best), subject (cars, tires, soda, guns, toys, Black Americana, aviation), illustrator signatures (J.C. Leyendecker Arrow/Kuppenheimer, Maxfield Parrish Edison Mazda/Fisk/Jell-O, Coles Phillips "fadeaway", Norman Rockwell, Jessie Willcox Smith Ivory soap), cover vs inside page. Example lot (9 visible, sleeved and labelled): Rigaud "Mary Garden" perfume, Columbia Grafonola July 4th "Miss Columbia", Willys-Overland touring car in rain, a truck ad, Firestone non-skid tire with roadster, The Delineator June 1916 cover, Kellogg's? child-with-toothbrush/cereal ad, Egg-O-See cereal 10¢, Sunday Magazine of the Philadelphia Press cover (c. 1910s) — c. 1910–1925.

## Makers, brands and variants
Source magazines: Ladies' Home Journal, Saturday Evening Post, Delineator, Collier's, Woman's Home Companion, McClure's, Country Life, Life (humor). Illustrators listed above. Automotive (Packard, Willys, Pierce-Arrow), tires (Firestone, Fisk "Time to Re-tire"), phonographs (Victor/Columbia), soda.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Originals: period paper toning, text on the reverse (check for page numbers and other-side content), halftone dots under loupe, natural size (LHJ ~11×16). Reproductions: glossy modern paper, blank reverse, inkjet dot pattern.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Edge chips, tears, foxing, trimmed margins, tape, staining. Matted/sleeved items fine.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern reprints/posters of famous ads; "vintage ad" prints on blank-back stock. Check reverse.

## Market
Steady decorative demand (framing, man caves, automotive collectors); singles sell 20–90 days. Etsy strong for framed décor.

## What sellers usually miss
Illustrator signatures in the image; automotive/tire ads command more than the lot price implies; covers more than ads.

## Shipping / handling
Flat, rigid mailer with cardboard, ~$5–7 each; large lots in one flat box.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Magazine, issue date, advertiser, illustrator, size, reverse content, condition.

## Buy rule
Profit ≥ max($50, price). A lot must be sold individually: n pages × median $15 × 0.87 − n × ~$6 shipping − price. At ~$13 per page net of shipping ≈ $7, so you need ~10+ good pages per $15–20 spent to clear $50. Fits passenger seat.
