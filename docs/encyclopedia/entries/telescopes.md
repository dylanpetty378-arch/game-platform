# Telescopes (Edmund Astroscan, Celestron, Meade, Questar)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay/Cloudy Nights classifieds sold knowledge 2024–25 (Astroscan 2001 $150–250 complete; Celestron C8 orange tube $300–500; Questar 3.5 $1,500–3,500); craigslist listing body for 2889.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Edmund Astroscan: red plastic bowling-ball-shaped 4¼" reflector sitting in a three-point base, 1976–2010s – complete with base, RKE eyepiece, peep sight, strap and cap. Celestron C5/C8 orange-tube Schmidt-Cassegrains (1970s–80s, orange tube on a fork mount) $250–500. Meade/Celestron black department-store refractors on tripods: $20–60. Questar 3.5": small chrome/black Maksutov on a built-in fork with a leather case – $1,500+. Sellers write "telescope", often with brand.

## What sellers usually miss
Astroscans are usually named (the shape is unique). Vintage orange C8s and Unitron refractors get listed as "old telescope". Mirror coating condition matters – ask for a photo down the tube.

## Shipping / handling
Astroscan 12 lb with base, awkward – $35–45 to ship, double-box. C8s 40+ lb – local only.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Astroscan complete ≤ $70, Questar/Unitron/C8 at any price under $200. Pass when: department-store refractors, Astroscan over $100. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
