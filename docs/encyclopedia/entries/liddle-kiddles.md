## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Tiny vinyl dolls, 3/4" to 3½" tall, with rooted hair (often brightly coloured), painted wide eyes and a bendable wired body jointed only at the neck (Doll Reference). Made by Mattel 1966–1971 (Wikipedia). Each first-series doll came with a themed accessory (fire truck, crib, sailboat, horse, wagon), a comb and brush and a mini "Komic" book. Look for: oversized heads on tiny bodies, toddler proportions, bright mohair-like rooted hair, cotton/felt outfits, and pastel plastic accessories marked "Mattel".
Marks: head rim "Mattel, Inc."; back "© 1965 Mattel Inc. Japan" (later Hong Kong); some shoes "Japan"; accessories marked "© 1966 Mattel, Inc. Japan" or "Liddle Kiddles" (Doll Reference).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **First series 1966–68, ~3½"** (Doll Reference, stock nos.): 3501 Bunson Burnie (fireman + truck), 3502 Howard "Biff" Boodle (wagon), 3503 Liddle Diddle (baby + pink crib), 3504 Lola Liddle (sailboat), 3505 Babe Biddle (car), 3506 Calamity Jiddle (cowgirl + horse), 3507 Florence Niddle (nurse + pram), 3508 Greta Griddle (table set), 3509 Millie Middle (sandbox), 3510 Beat-A-Diddle (Sears exclusive), 3513 Sizzly Friddle, 3514 Windy Fliddle (airplane), 3515 Trikey Triddle, 3516 Freezy Sliddle, 3517 Surfy Skiddle, 3518 Soapy Siddle, 3519 Rolly Twiddle (only Black doll of the first 24), 3548 Beddy-Bye Biddle, 3549 Pretty Priddle (Sears), 3587 Baby Liddle (Sears), 3751 Telly Viddle, 3752 Lemons Striddle, 3753 Kampy Kiddle, 3754 Slipsy Sliddle. (Note: the triage spelling "Bunson Bernie" is wrong — it is Bunson Burnie.)
- **Kiddle Kolognes (1967–68)** — ~2" dolls inside a clear plastic perfume bottle with a coloured cap/stand, named for flowers: Sweet Pea, Honeysuckle, Lily of the Valley, Rosebud, Violet, Apple Blossom, Orange Blossom, Lilac and others (general knowledge, verify full list). Doll alone is a fraction of the bottle+doll.
- **Lucky Locket Kiddles (1967–68)** — ~2" dolls in a heart-shaped hinged plastic locket on a chain: Lorna, Larky, Lottie, Lois, Lorelei, Louise and others (general knowledge, verify).
- **Storybook Kiddles (1967–68)** — doll + fold-out book-shaped playcase/scene and accessories: Alice in Wonderliddle (with Rabbit/clock/castle pieces), Liddle Red Riding Hiddle (with wolf), Cinderiddle (pumpkin coach/clock), Sleeping Biddle, Liddle Middle Muffet (spider), Liddle Biddle Peep (lamb), Peter Paniddle (general knowledge, verify).
- **Skediddles (1967–69)** — larger 4" "Skediddle" Kiddles with a walking/push mechanism; also Disney Skediddles (general knowledge, verify).
- **Jewelry Kiddles, Kiddle Kones, Kola Kiddles, Sweet Treats, Animiddles, Zoolery, Kozy Kiddles, Tea Party, Kiddles 'n' Kars, Lenore Limousine, Santa Kiddle, Flower Kiddles, Kiddle Klub, playhouses (Pop-Up, Kiddle Kottage)** — 1968–70 lines; ring/pin/bracelet Kiddles are tiny (~¾"–1") and often lost in jewelry lots (general knowledge, verify).
- **Later revivals** — 1990s Tyco/Mattel and other reissues use different moulds and new markings (general knowledge, verify): check for "1965" back mark + "Japan/Hong Kong".

### Golden-egg tells
- **Sears exclusives — Beat-A-Diddle, Baby Liddle, Pretty Priddle, Beddy-Bye Biddle (1967 robe)** — ~2–4× a common first-series doll — Beat-A-Diddle: rock-star girl with guitar/mic in floral or (rarer) red cotton outfit; Baby Liddle with buggy; Doll Reference calls Baby Liddle "very hard to find".
- **Rolly Twiddle (#3519)** — ~2–3× — the only Black doll of the first series, with orange wagon; "hard to find and highly valued" (Doll Reference).
- **Complete Storybook Kiddles (book case + doll + animals/props)** — $60–200+ vs $10 doll alone (general knowledge, verify) — a book-shaped plastic case with printed scene; Alice with Rabbit and clock; Red Riding Hood with wolf.
- **Kologne doll with bottle and cap** — ~2× the loose doll (one sold complete at $19.95) — clear bottle shaped around the doll, coloured flower cap/stand.
- **Lucky Locket doll with locket and chain** — ~2–3× the loose doll — heart-shaped clear/coloured hinged locket with doll inside.
- **First-series doll with its original vehicle/accessory (Bunson Burnie's truck with "Liddle Kiddles" tyres, Calamity's marked horse, Liddle Diddle's pink crib)** — ~3× the loose doll — marked accessories in photos.
- **Mint in package (blister card or bubble)** — $100–400+ (general knowledge, verify) — original Mattel card with Kiddle name and number.
- **Jewelry Kiddles (ring/pin/bracelet) in costume-jewelry lots** — $20–50 each (general knowledge, verify) — 1" doll in a clear plastic dome on a ring or pin.

### Quick ID checklist
- Size: under 4"; big head, wired bendable body, rooted hair.
- Back mark "© 1965 Mattel Inc. Japan" (or Hong Kong); head rim "Mattel, Inc.".
- Accessories in photo? Count bottles, lockets, vehicles, books, combs/brushes.
- Hair original, uncut, unmatted; ponytails/bangs as issued.
- Outfit original cotton/felt (many are separated or swapped).
- Face paint intact (eye paint and cheek blush rub easily).
- Wires not broken (limbs that flop or poke through).
- Ask for each doll's name or close-ups; identify against the stock list above.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Soft vinyl with rooted saran hair, internal wire armature, painted features; outfits are glued/stitched cotton and felt. Kolognes and lockets are clear hard plastic that scratches and crazes. Accessories are hard plastic in bright colours, often marked Mattel. Vinyl can go sticky or discoloured; heat-yellowing and green spots (from wire/metal) occur.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Common: cut hair, missing outfits, broken wires, rubbed face paint, sticky vinyl, scratched/cracked bottles and lockets, missing caps and chains, broken locket hinges. Repairs: re-dressing with reproduction outfits, re-glued hair, repainted eyes — sold as "restored" at lower prices. Reproduction accessories (Kologne caps, combs, Komics) circulate among collectors.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Hong Kong knock-off "Kiddle-type" dolls (1960s–70s) without Mattel marks; 1990s Mattel/Tyco reissues; reproduction clothing and accessories. Check marks; unmarked = not Mattel.

## Market
Niche but steady collector base (Kiddle collectors are completists, buying missing accessories one at a time). Loose lots sell readily on eBay; complete accessories sell fast; common loose dolls sit. Prices have been flat to soft (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Tiny accessories (combs, Komics, bottle caps, locket chains) that complete a doll; Jewelry Kiddles buried in costume-jewelry lots; Storybook cases in toy boxes; and which dolls are Sears exclusives. Sellers often overrate a bag of loose undressed dolls as "rare 60s Mattel".

## Shipping / handling
Tiny and light; small box $5–7. Bag each doll and accessory separately; protect bottles/lockets from scratching.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Name and number each doll, list every original accessory, describe hair (uncut?), outfit (original?), paint, wires, and marks. Sell complete dolls individually; sell incomplete loose dolls as a lot.

### What to tell the buyer
"Vintage Mattel Liddle Kiddle, [name, #stock no.], 1966–68, marked © 1965 Mattel Inc. Japan on the back. Includes [original items]; missing [items]. Hair uncut/cut; outfit original/not; face paint [condition]; wire body bends normally. Not restored."

## Buy rule
Price ≤ $200. Profit = sold × 0.87 − ~$6 to buyer − ~$6 to Dylan − price ≥ max($50, price). A 9-doll loose lot at ~$10/doll ($90 resale) supports a price of only ~$15 — not a buy at $45+. Buy when the photos show original accessories or exclusives: e.g. a complete Storybook Kiddle at ~$120 sold → buy ≤ $40. Shoebox-size; no authentication needed.
