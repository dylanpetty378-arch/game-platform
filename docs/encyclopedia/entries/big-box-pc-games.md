## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Cardboard boxes 7x9" (Sierra "small box" 1984–89) or 9x12", 5.25"/3.5" floppies, manuals, maps, "feelies" (Infocom), registration cards. Publishers that carry value: Sierra (King's Quest, Space Quest, Leisure Suit Larry, Police Quest), Infocom (Zork, Hitchhiker's), Origin (Ultima, Wing Commander), LucasArts (Monkey Island), SSI gold-box D&D, id (Doom shareware boxes). Seller words: "vintage computer game", "floppy disk game", "IBM PC game".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Sierra On-Line** – King's Quest, Space Quest, Leisure Suit Larry, Police Quest; 1980s small box (collectible), 1990s big box (decent).
- **Infocom** – Zork, Hitchhiker's Guide; folios and "grey box" with feelies (collectible).
- **Origin** – Ultima I–V with cloth maps (collectible), Wing Commander; Akalabeth and Ultima I (California Pacific) top.
- **LucasArts** – Monkey Island, CD big boxes (decent).
- **SSI** gold-box AD&D; **id Software** shareware boxes.
- Platform variants: IBM PC/PCjr, Apple II, Commodore 64, Amiga, Atari ST – values differ by platform (general knowledge, verify). Sealed copies multiply value.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original printed cardboard boxes, floppy labels matching the publisher's style, manuals, code wheels, maps (cloth for Ultima), registration cards. Completeness (map, manual, code wheel) doubles value. Re-releases: budget "classics" packaging, jewel cases, later collections (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Crushed/torn boxes, sun fading, missing disks/feelies, water damage, mould on floppies. Untested floppies are normal; buyers mostly collect, not play (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Resealed boxes (fake shrink) and reproduction feelies/manuals exist; also later re-releases and anthology packs (general knowledge, verify). Graded sealed games carry their own authentication risk.

## Market
Retro-computing collectors and nostalgia buyers; sells on eBay with wide price spread by completeness and condition (KQII sold $22–265). Moderate volume (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Lots of "old computer games $20 for the box" where one is an Infocom or Ultima. Completeness (map, manual, code wheel) doubles value.

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb, boxes crush – rigid mailer, ~$10 in. eBay 13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State title, publisher, platform, disk size, contents inventory (disks, manual, map, feelies, reg card), sealed or not, box condition. Photograph all box sides and every included item laid out. Keywords: title + publisher + platform + "big box" + "complete" / "CIB" + disk size.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 1980s Sierra/Infocom/Origin complete ≤ $40, or lots of 5+ boxed games ≤ $50. Pass when: single title priced ≥ $100 (seller knows), CD-era re-releases. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (it does).
