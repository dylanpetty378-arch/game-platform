## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Kenner 1978–80 "Large Size Action Figures": Luke, Leia, Han, Chewbacca, Darth Vader, Ben Kenobi, C-3PO, R2-D2, Stormtrooper, Jawa (8"), Boba Fett, IG-88. Tells: soft cloth outfits, rooted hair on Leia, Luke with yellow lightsaber (telescoping, often lost), Leia with white hooded cape, belt, comb and brush; stand not original (Kenner didn't include stands). Later lookalikes: 1996–99 Hasbro/Kenner "Collector Series" 12" (boxed, stiffer vinyl, harder faces, $15–40 loose) and 1997 Electronic/Power F/X droids. The 1978 figures have a copyright on the back of the neck/buttocks "©GMFGI 1978 KENNER". Sellers' words: "vintage Star Wars 12 inch", "big Luke doll".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Kenner 1978–80 Large Size line** (12 figures above). Boba Fett (1979) and IG-88 (1980) are the scarcest and most valuable; Jawa and droids are smaller (general knowledge, verify).
- **Hasbro/Kenner Collector Series 1996–99** – mass-produced 12" figures, junk tier loose.
- **1997 Electronic / Power F/X droids** – lookalikes (e.g. R5-D4 seen in sweep).
- Modern Hasbro 12" (2000s–) and Sideshow 1/6 (separate, premium modern market).
- International variants (Palitoy UK, Meccano France) boxes – collector premium (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
1978: soft vinyl heads, rooted hair (Leia, Luke, Han), cloth outfits with Kenner tags, plastic accessories; copyright stamps "©GMFGI 1978 KENNER". 1996+: stiffer vinyl, sculpted hair/more detailed faces, "Hasbro" or 1990s copyright stamps. Original outfits have period velcro/snaps and Kenner-specific fabric. (general knowledge on details, verify)

## Condition, damage and repairs
Missing accessories (Luke's saber, Han's blaster, Leia's comb/brush/belt are 40–60% of the value), cut or frizzy hair on Leia, loose limbs, stained/faded cloth, broken boots, replaced/reproduction outfits. Ask for photos of the stamps, accessories, hair and tags.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reproduction accessories (sabers, blasters, Leia's comb/brush) and repro outfits are sold to complete figures – originals have correct plastic and stamps; repro boxes exist for MIB. 1996+ Collector Series lumped in as "vintage". (general knowledge, verify)

## Market
Strong nostalgia collector market (Gen X Star Wars fans); eBay sells several of these per week. Peaks around Nov–Dec and new Star Wars releases. Loose incomplete figures are plentiful; complete and boxed are what sell well. (general knowledge, verify)

## What sellers usually miss
1978 vs 1996: sellers lump them. Accessories (Luke's saber, Han's blaster, Leia's comb/brush/belt) are 40–60% of the value. Hair condition on Leia.

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb per figure, box well; $12–18. eBay ~13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Vintage 1978 Kenner Star Wars 12 inch", character, "large size action figure", "complete" with accessories named (cape belt comb), "loose"/"boxed", "original". Junk: "Collector Series" mixed with 1978, "rare", "action figure" without year. `Vintage 1978 Kenner Star Wars 12 Inch Princess Leia Complete Cape Belt Comb`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (several sell weekly; Nov–Dec peak); 1996+ Collector Series lotted on FB local.
- **Price position:** 1978 complete at the sold median ($144 Leia), OBO, accept −15%; incomplete at $50–120 median; boxed at the sold median ($425 Leia MIB), firm 3 weeks; 1996+ $10–40 lotted. Cut 10% at 30 days.
- **Photos:** first: figure standing with every accessory laid out; then copyright stamp ("©GMFGI 1978 KENNER"), outfit tags, face, hair close-up (Leia), each accessory alone, limbs/joints, boots, box all sides if boxed.
- **State:** figure, year from stamp, loose/boxed, each accessory original or repro, outfit originality, hair condition (cut/frizz), loose limbs/stains/broken boots by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 1–2 lb per figure, boxed with accessories bagged separately, $12–18.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[1978] Kenner stamp, original outfit and [saber/cape/belt/comb] all present — the accessories are 40–60% of the value, and the 1996 Collector Series lookalikes sell for $20."

## Buy rule
Buy when: a 1978 figure with its main accessory, price ≤ $70 per figure. Pass when: Collector Series (1996+) at any price over $15, or 1978 figures missing saber/cape. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Fits a passenger seat.
