# Kenner 12-inch Star Wars figures (1978–80 "large size")
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: actionfigure411.com sold-auction tracker (12" Leia avg $303, range $225–450, mixed boxed/loose; BIN low $100), thetoycollectorsguide.com 12-inch checklist, eBay sold

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Kenner 1978–80 "Large Size Action Figures": Luke, Leia, Han, Chewbacca, Darth Vader, Ben Kenobi, C-3PO, R2-D2, Stormtrooper, Jawa (8"), Boba Fett, IG-88. Tells: soft cloth outfits, rooted hair on Leia, Luke with yellow lightsaber (telescoping, often lost), Leia with white hooded cape, belt, comb and brush; stand not original (Kenner didn't include stands). Later lookalikes: 1996–99 Hasbro/Kenner "Collector Series" 12" (boxed, stiffer vinyl, harder faces, $15–40 loose) and 1997 Electronic/Power F/X droids. The 1978 figures have a copyright on the back of the neck/buttocks "©GMFGI 1978 KENNER". Sellers' words: "vintage Star Wars 12 inch", "big Luke doll".

## What sellers usually miss
1978 vs 1996: sellers lump them. Accessories (Luke's saber, Han's blaster, Leia's comb/brush/belt) are 40–60% of the value. Hair condition on Leia.

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb per figure, box well; $12–18. eBay ~13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a 1978 figure with its main accessory, price ≤ $70 per figure. Pass when: Collector Series (1996+) at any price over $15, or 1978 figures missing saber/cape. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
