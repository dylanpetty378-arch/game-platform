## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
A cast-iron hand pump with a pitcher-shaped open top and spout, a hinged handle on top, and a round flanged base that bolts onto a well/cistern pipe. Popular with garden and farmhouse decorators and with off-grid users (still functional).

## Makers, brands and variants
F.E. Myers & Bro (Ashland, OH), Fairbanks Morse, Goulds (Seneca Falls), Red Jacket (Davenport), Deming (Salem OH), Baker, Dempster, Aermotor. Modern Chinese/Indian imports (Tractor Supply, Amazon) are the majority of what's for sale, often painted red, green or black.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Old US pumps: raised maker names and size numbers cast into the body or cap, heavier iron, leather plunger cup. Imports: no maker name or "No. 1/2/3" only, rough casting, glossy paint, bolts with metric threads.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Missing handle/cap/plunger (like the example), cracked bodies (freeze damage), seized pivots. Decor buyers accept rust; functional buyers want parts complete.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern imports are routinely sold as "antique". The tell is the absence of maker castings and fresh, even paint.

## Market
Steady garden-decor demand, spring peak; heavy (15-25 lb) so shipping eats margin.

## What sellers usually miss
Maker marks on complete old pumps; most sellers price at $30-60 regardless.

## Shipping / handling
15-25 lb, $20-30 by ground; well packed with base protected.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Maker, completeness, whether it pumps (with a new leather), dimensions, base bolt pattern.

## Buy rule
Need resale ≥ ~$120 to clear $50 at a $20 price with $25 each-way shipping - only complete marked early pumps. Pass on parts.
