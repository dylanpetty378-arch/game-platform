# Shortwave / communications receivers (Radio Shack DX, Sony, Grundig, Hallicrafters)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge, eHam.net reviews/classifieds, QRZ swapmeet

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Tabletop or portable radios with a frequency display and bands beyond AM/FM (LW, SW, SSB). Tells: **Radio Shack DX-394/DX-394B** (1990s tabletop, black, with "LSB/USB/CW" on the front – $150–250 working), **DX-302/DX-160** (1970s–80s, $80–150), **Sony ICF-2010/ICF-SW7600GR/ICF-SW77** ($150–400; 2010 is the cult portable), **Grundig Satellit 700/800** ($250–500), **Yaesu FRG-7/FRG-100**, **Kenwood R-1000/R-2000**, **Drake R8/R8B** ($500–900), **Hallicrafters S-38/SX-** series tube (collector $80–400, the SX-28/SX-42 more). Sellers write "shortwave radio", "ham radio receiver".

## What sellers usually miss
Model desirability (DX-394 vs the cheaper DX-375 portable); "works some" = fault (capacitors, display) and electronics rule says pass.

## Shipping / handling
DX-394 ~8 lb, $20–25. Sony 2010 is a portable (known to die from leaking batteries – check the battery bay). eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: DX-394 fully working under $70; ICF-2010 working under $120; Satellit 800 under $150. Pass when: "works some", untested, missing knobs. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
