## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Kenner's 3¾" figures made 1977–1985 for Star Wars, The Empire Strikes Back, Return of the Jedi and the final Power of the Force (POTF) line, plus Droids and Ewoks cartoon lines (1985). **Five points of articulation only** (neck, two shoulders, two hips), straight or slightly bent arms, **no knee or elbow joints**, simple paint, hard plastic with a slightly chalky sheen; small removable vinyl/cloth capes and tiny weapons (blasters 1–2 cm).
Marks: on the back of a leg or inside a foot — "© LFL 1977"/"1980"/"1983"/"1984" (or "© G.M.F.G.I." on early figures) and a **country of origin (COO)**: Hong Kong (most common), Taiwan, Macau, later China (Appraisily).
Hasbro figures (1995+: POTF2, prequel, Revenge of the Sith 2005, Vintage Collection 2010+) usually have knee/elbow joints, more detailed sculpts, "Hasbro" and "China" stamps, and dates 1995+. Clone troopers, Anakin and Obi-Wan with articulated knees = 2005 Hasbro, $1–3 loose (triage). Hasbro also makes vintage-style reissues (Retro Collection 2019+, Vintage Collection) that imitate Kenner — they are stamped Hasbro and dated.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Kenner (US) 1977–85** — 92-figure mainline + POTF additions; vehicles and playsets are a separate type (kenner-star-wars-vehicles).
- **Palitoy (UK) / Meccano (France) / Clipper (Benelux) / Harbert (Italy) / Toltoys (Australia) / Takara (Japan)** — licensed figures using Kenner moulds; value mostly in the cards.
- **Lili Ledy (Mexico), Glasslite (Brazil), PBP (Spain), Uzay (Turkey, unlicensed), Top Toys (Argentina)** — foreign-made variants with different plastics/paint; collectors pay premiums for Lili Ledy and Glasslite (general knowledge, verify).
- **Last 17 (POTF 1985)** — the final wave: Amanaman, Barada, EV-9D9, Lumat, Paploo, Imperial Dignitary, Imperial Gunner, Han Solo in Carbonite, Luke Battle Poncho, Luke Stormtrooper, A-Wing Pilot, Anakin Skywalker, Lando General Pilot, R2-D2 pop-up lightsaber, Romba, Warok, Yak Face (list general knowledge, verify) — loose $75–250 good–excellent (Appraisily).
- **Droids and Ewoks cartoon lines (1985)** — Jann Tosh, Kea Moll, Thall Joben, Tig Fromm, Jord Dusat, Kez-Iban, Uncle Gundy, Sise Fromm, Vlix (Brazil only), Ewoks Dulok Scout/Shaman, Logray, Urgah, Wicket, King Gorneesh (general knowledge, verify).
- **Mail-away and early figures** — 1979 mail-away Boba Fett (non-firing), Bossk/IG-88 in "first 12" vs later; Sears/early-bird exclusives.

### Golden-egg tells
- **Vinyl-cape Jawa (1978)** — $5,000–28,000 vs $15 cloth-cape Jawa (Appraisily) — shiny brown vinyl short cape instead of cloth hooded robe; heavily faked → authentication required.
- **Double-telescoping lightsaber Luke, Vader or Ben (1977–78)** — $7,000–35,000 (Appraisily) — saber with a thin inner tip extending from a thicker tube; faked → authentication required.
- **Blue Snaggletooth (Sears Cantina, 1978)** — $750–5,000 vs $10 red Snaggletooth (Appraisily) — tall figure in blue suit with silver boots.
- **Yak Face (Canada/Europe only)** — $2,000–15,000 carded; loose with original staff far less but still several hundred (general knowledge, verify) — large-headed alien with long staff.
- **Original 1979 Boba Fett (non-firing, Hong Kong/Taiwan COO)** — $100–250 loose with original blaster (general knowledge, verify) vs $10 for a Hasbro Fett — blue-grey body, green chest, the leg COO; Fisher-Price Adventure People lots can hide one (eye-knowledge).
- **Last 17 loose with accessories (Amanaman, Barada, EV-9D9, Luke Stormtrooper, Imperial Gunner, Yak Face, Anakin)** — $75–250 (Appraisily) — 1985 dates on leg; unusual characters.
- **Droids cartoon figures (Jann Tosh, Kea Moll, Tig Fromm, Uncle Gundy)** — $30–150 loose (verify) — bright cartoon paint, 1985 date.
- **Original weapons (Boba Fett blaster, Vader saber, Stormtrooper rifle, Han blaster)** — $10–60 each separately (verify); loose figure with original weapon ~2× without — weapons are faked widely.
- **Kill: articulated knees/elbows, "Hasbro" on leg, dates 1995+** — $1–5.

### Quick ID checklist
- Joints: only 5? No knees/elbows?
- Leg stamp: "© LFL 19xx" + COO (Hong Kong/Taiwan/Macau/China) — ask for a close-up.
- Characters: name the key ones (Boba Fett, IG-88, Bossk, Hammerhead, Greedo, Last 17).
- Weapons: count originals; repro weapons look glossier, softer, sharper-edged or wrong colour (general knowledge, verify).
- Discolouration: yellowed white figures (Stormtroopers, Snowtroopers) lose value.
- Paint wear on faces/hands, loose limbs, broken thumbs (Vader, Han).
- Cases (Darth Vader case, mini-action collector case) and vehicles — note separately.
- Sealed cards: check for resealed bubbles; AFA/CAS grading for valuable ones.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Hard ABS bodies with softer heads, vinyl capes (early), cloth robes and capes later; paint wear on high points. Originals feel slightly lighter and have mould seams; Hasbro reissues are crisper and stamped differently. Yellowing (UV/heat) is common on white and light-grey plastics.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Loose limbs, broken thumbs and saber hands, paint wear, yellowing, chewed weapons, missing capes. Repairs: repainting, swapped limbs ("frankenfigures"), reproduction capes and weapons — sold as "repro accessories" at much lower values.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reproduction weapons, capes and Jawa vinyl capes are everywhere; fake COOs and fake double-telescoping sabers on high-end variants; resealed cards. Hasbro Vintage/Retro reissues imitate Kenner packaging ("updated version modeled after the vintage 1970s toy" — eye-knowledge). Uzay and other bootlegs. Any figure claimed above $500 needs a specialist (AFA/CAS) — outside Dylan's rule.

## Market
Very large and liquid: thousands of vintage Kenner sales per month on eBay; loose lots sell in days; collectors buy commons to complete sets, so weapons and completeness matter. Prices peaked 2021–22 and have softened (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Original weapons and capes hidden in bags; Boba Fett or Last 17 figures inside mixed toy lots; foreign (Lili Ledy, Glasslite) variants; vehicle parts and cases. Sellers overrate any "1977 Star Wars" figure and Hasbro figures as "vintage".

## Shipping / handling
Light; lots of 30 fit a medium flat-rate box, $10–15; bag weapons separately. Ship to Dylan $10–15.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
List each figure by name, year stamp and COO; state which weapons/capes are original vs reproduction; photograph leg stamps; describe yellowing, paint wear and loose joints; sell keys individually, commons as a lot.

### What to tell the buyer
"Original vintage Kenner Star Wars figure, [name], [year] — leg stamped © LFL [year], made in [Hong Kong/Taiwan]. Includes original [weapon/cape] (or: no accessories / reproduction blaster). Joints [tight/loose], paint wear [where], [no] discolouration. Not a Hasbro reissue."

## Buy rule
Price ≤ $200. Profit = sold × 0.87 − ~$12 to buyer − ~$12 to Dylan − price ≥ max($50, price). A 30-figure loose lot with no weapons at ~$10/figure ($300–400 resale) supports ≤ ~$110 asking; at $200 it needs ~$510 resale (triage) — pass. Buy at ≤ $3–5/figure, more only when photos show Boba Fett, Last 17, Droids or original weapons. Never buy vinyl-cape Jawa, double-telescoping saber, Blue Snaggletooth or carded high-value claims — they need professional authentication.
