## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Woven or pile wall textiles. Distinguish:
- **Hand-woven tapestry** (Aubusson, Flemish): weft-faced, slits between colour areas, wool/silk, colour shading by hatching, faded, often reduced or patched. Rare on Craigslist.
- **Machine jacquard tapestry** (Belgium, France, 1950s–today): uniform weave, cotton/poly, the back shows a reversed image with floats; usually a label "Made in Belgium" or "Tapisserie" and a rod pocket.
- **Tapestry throws** (Manual Woodworkers & Weavers, Goodwin Weavers, Pure Country): fringe on two or four sides, cotton, 50×60" or 50×70" size; a blanket, not a wall piece.
- **Velvet wall rugs** (Italy, Turkey, Belgium 1960s–80s): pile velvet with camel caravans, mosques, dogs playing cards, tigers; fringed ends.

## Makers, brands and variants
Aubusson, Gobelins, Beauvais (antique); Belgian jacquard mills (Art de Lys, Jules Pansu in France); Manual Woodworkers & Weavers (Hendersonville NC, 1932–); Goodwin Weavers; Italian velvet "Made in Italy" souvenirs; Hines of Oxford (England, panels).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Hand-woven: slits, uneven lines, wool; machine: perfect edges, cotton/chenille, label. Fringe on all four sides = throw. Polyester sheen = modern.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Fading (sun), moth damage on wool, stains, fraying, holes, odour (smoke). Hand-woven pieces are often rewoven or cut down.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Machine jacquards are often sold as "Aubusson" or "antique French tapestry". Velvet souvenirs are often called "antique Persian". Lens matched a modern floral jacquard throw to $795 listings: unreliable.

## Market
Slow décor market. Belgian jacquards with classical/medieval scenes sell steadily at modest prices; throws sell slowly.

## What sellers usually miss
Mostly they overvalue. The occasional real hand-woven fragment is identifiable by slits and hatching visible in close-ups.

## Shipping / handling
Light and foldable; roll on a tube for wall pieces. $8–15. Fits easily.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** origin + type + subject + size: "Belgian jacquard wall tapestry medieval 36x50 rod pocket", "Italian velvet camel scene", "Manual Woodworkers"; Junk: "Aubusson" or "antique French" on a jacquard, "Persian" on velvet, "boho". Example: `Belgian Jacquard Wall Tapestry Medieval Unicorn 36x52 Rod Pocket Made in Belgium`.
- **Where:** eBay or Etsy BIN with offers (slow décor market; Belgian classical/medieval scenes sell steadily); throws local. Etsy matches eBay for decor buyers. Hand-woven Aubusson/Flemish → specialist.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (Manual Woodworkers throws $20; Middle Eastern scene $25, p75 $36; Belgian/French jacquards $30–90), accept −15%, floor p25; OBO; cut 10% after 4 weeks.
- **Photos:** first photo the whole piece hung flat in daylight; then label (origin, fibre), back weave, rod pocket, close-up of the weave (machine vs hand — slits and hatching), any fading, stains, fraying, tape for dimensions.
- **State:** dimensions, fibre, machine jacquard vs hand-woven stated honestly, label text, rod pocket/lining, fading/moth/stains by photo number, smoke-free.
- **Pack / haul:** light and foldable; roll wall pieces on a tube, throws folded in a poly mailer; $8–15; carry-on size.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Labelled [Made in Belgium] jacquard, [36×52] with the rod pocket, ready to hang — these sell [$30–90] and this one's unfaded with no moth; it's the mill tapestry, not an Aubusson, and priced like one."
