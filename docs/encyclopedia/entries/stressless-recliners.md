## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- A leather recliner on a swivel base (a round "classic" base or a cross/star base), usually with a matching ottoman.
- Wraparound curved wood or upholstered arms, a high back with a separately adjusting headrest (the "Plus" system), and gliding recline set by body weight.
- Badge: a small "Stressless" logo plate or embroidery on the side or back. Ekornes labels sit under the seat (general knowledge, verify). The entry note says to look for the Ekornes badge on the arm.
- Seller words: "Stressless", "Ekornes", "Norwegian recliner", "leather recliner with ottoman".
- Dating features (Ekornes history): 1971 original sliding recline; 1981 wooden base introduced (Royal); 1991 Plus system (automatic headrest and lumbar adjustment); 1996 two or three sizes; 2004 logo redesigned; 2013 Star base; 2014 Signature base with BalanceAdapt (soft rocking); 2016 LegComfort (electric integrated footrest); 2019 Power motorisation replaced LegComfort on recliners.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Ekornes ASA** (Ikornnes, Norway). Stressless was launched in 1971 as the first TV recliner with a patented sliding system (Ekornes). 10 millionth seat sold in 2019; a North Carolina factory opened in 2012 (Ekornes).
- Models: Consul, Mayfair, Reno, Sunrise, Magic, Admiral (older, verify), Wing, Garda, View, Mayfair/Consul "Signature" bases and power versions. Sizes S/M/L (general knowledge, verify). Older and other recliner models named by Ekornes: Royal, Original, Governor, Senator, Savannah, Dream, Skyline, Metro, City, Crown, Tokyo, YOU and YOU James (Ekornes).
- Look-alikes: Himolla, Fjords and IMG Comfort (lower value), which are often sold as "Stressless-style". Ekornes won a copy case against Biva in 2006 (Ekornes).

### Quick ID checklist
- "Stressless" logo plate/embroidery on the side or back; Ekornes label under the seat.
- Base type: classic round wood base, Star base (2013+), Signature base (2014+), or power (cord/motor visible).
- Headrest that tilts automatically when reclining = Plus system (1991+).
- Ottoman present and matching leather.
- Size (S/M/L) from the label or seat width; model name from the label.
- Leather close-up: cracking, fading on arms and headrest, pet scratches.
- Recline video: smooth glide, lock knob on the side works.

### Golden-egg tells
(Furniture, not a collectible: the multiples are small and all general knowledge, verify.)
- Signature base (2014+) or Power / LegComfort models — upper end of $300–600 and above (general knowledge, verify) — wide base with integrated footrest or a cord to the wall.
- Premium aniline leather (Paloma, Noblesse) vs pigmented Batick/Cori — premium over the common version (general knowledge, verify) — softer, more natural grain and patina.
- Chair with matching original ottoman — more than the chair alone — same leather and base on the ottoman.
- Post-2004 logo and current model name (Mayfair, Consul, Magic) — easier resale than 1980s–90s chairs — newer logo plate.
- Pre-1991 chairs without the Plus system, fabric covers, and look-alikes — lowest tier — headrest that does not move with the back, no Stressless badge.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Leather grades: Batick and Cori (pigmented, durable), Paloma, Noblesse (aniline, premium). Wood bases in walnut, oak, cherry or black (general knowledge, verify). Water-based stain and varnish since 2011 (Ekornes).
- Fabric versions are worth less. Original ottoman = a matching set worth more.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Worn or cracked leather on the arms and seat, a faded headrest, sagging cushions, and broken recline glide or base.
- Pet scratches and smoke odour are common reasons for a pass.
- Power models: check the motor and cord.
- Ask for photos of the badge, the underside label, the leather close up, and a recline video.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- No fakes, but "Stressless-style" chairs from other makers are mislabelled (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Local resale on FB Marketplace and craigslist. Buyers are older comfort buyers and Scandinavian-design fans. Retail prices for new chairs are high ($2,000–4,000+ with ottoman), which supports the used market (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
- Model and size. Sellers call them "leather recliner" without the brand.
- Base type and leather grade.

## Shipping / handling
- Chair plus ottoman is bulky and heavy (60–90 lb, general knowledge, verify). Local pickup only; it does not fit a passenger seat as a whole.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- Model, size (S/M/L), leather type and colour, base type, ottoman included, condition of the arms and seat, a working recline video.
- Title keywords: "Ekornes Stressless Consul Recliner and Ottoman Leather".

### What to tell the buyer
"Ekornes Stressless [model] recliner, size [S/M/L], [leather name/colour], on the [classic/Star/Signature/power] base, with matching ottoman. Made in Norway [or USA]. Plus-system headrest and lumbar adjust automatically as you recline; glide and lock work (video). Condition: [wear on arms/seat], smoke-free, pet-free."
