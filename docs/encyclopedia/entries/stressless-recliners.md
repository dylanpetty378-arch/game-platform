## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- A leather recliner on a swivel base (a round "classic" base or a cross/star base), usually with a matching ottoman.
- Wraparound curved wood or upholstered arms, a high back with a separately adjusting headrest (the "Plus" system), and gliding recline set by body weight.
- Badge: a small "Stressless" logo plate or embroidery on the side or back. Ekornes labels sit under the seat (general knowledge, verify). The entry note says to look for the Ekornes badge on the arm.
- Seller words: "Stressless", "Ekornes", "Norwegian recliner", "leather recliner with ottoman".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Ekornes ASA** (Norway). Stressless was launched in 1971 (general knowledge, verify).
- Models: Consul, Mayfair, Reno, Sunrise, Magic, Admiral (older), Wing, Garda, View, Mayfair/Consul "Signature" bases and power versions. Sizes S/M/L (general knowledge, verify).
- Look-alikes: Himolla, Fjords and IMG Comfort (lower value), which are often sold as "Stressless-style".

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Leather grades: Batick and Cori (pigmented, durable), Paloma, Noblesse (aniline, premium). Wood bases in walnut, oak, cherry or black (general knowledge, verify).
- Fabric versions are worth less. Original ottoman = a matching set worth more.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Worn or cracked leather on the arms and seat, a faded headrest, sagging cushions, and broken recline glide or base.
- Pet scratches and smoke odour are common reasons for a pass.
- Ask for photos of the badge, the underside label, the leather close up, and a recline video.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- No fakes, but "Stressless-style" chairs from other makers are mislabelled (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Local resale on FB Marketplace and craigslist. Buyers are older comfort buyers and Scandinavian-design fans. Retail prices for new chairs are high ($2,000–4,000+ with ottoman), which supports the used market (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
- Model and size. Sellers call them "leather recliner" without the brand.

## Shipping / handling
- Chair plus ottoman is bulky and heavy (60–90 lb, general knowledge, verify). Local pickup only; it does not fit a passenger seat as a whole.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- Model, size (S/M/L), leather type and colour, base type, ottoman included, condition of the arms and seat, a working recline video.
- Title keywords: "Ekornes Stressless Consul Recliner and Ottoman Leather".
