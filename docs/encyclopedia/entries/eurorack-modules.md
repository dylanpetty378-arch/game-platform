## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
3U-tall aluminium panels with 3.5 mm patch jacks and knobs, mounted in a rack/case. Brand on the panel: **Make Noise** (Maths $200–250 used, Morphagene $350–400, Rosie $120–150, DPO $400+), **Mutable Instruments** (discontinued – Clouds/Rings/Plaits/Beads $250–500, originals with the MI logo are gold), **Erica Synths** (Pico series $60–120, Black series $200–400), **Endorphin.es** (Milky Way, Squawk Dirty, Grand Terminal – $150–400), **Intellijel**, **ALM Busy Circuits**, **Noise Engineering**, **Doepfer** (budget, $40–120), **Behringer** (junk tier $30–60). Sellers of eurorack are almost always expert and price at ModularGrid/Reverb levels; the only arbitrage is a non-musician estate seller calling it "synthesizer parts" or a whole case priced as a lump.
Width is measured in HP (1 HP = 5.08 mm); Buchla (4U) and Serge panels are different formats (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Make Noise** (US): Maths, Morphagene, Rosie, Function, DPO.
- **Mutable Instruments** (France, closed 2022 – general knowledge, verify): Clouds, Rings, Plaits, Beads, Elements. Open-source designs mean many clones; originals with the MI logo carry the premium.
- **Erica Synths** (Latvia): Pico (budget), Black series.
- **Endorphin.es**: Milky Way, Squawk Dirty, Grand Terminal.
- **Intellijel, ALM Busy Circuits, Noise Engineering**: mid-to-high, solid resale.
- **Doepfer** (Germany, format originator): budget utilities.
- **Behringer**: clones, junk tier.
- **Cases**: Intellijel, Make Noise, Doepfer powered cases add value to a full-case lot (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Branded silkscreened aluminium panels, quality jacks (Thonkiconn), proper power headers with keyed/stripe-marked ribbon cables. DIY builds show hand-soldered boards, mismatched knobs, unbranded or "uMI" clone panels (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Reversed power cable damage (dead module), bent jacks, missing knobs, scratched panels, DIY builds of uncertain quality. Only buy "works" listings.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Mutable Instruments clones (open-source; panels sometimes say "uClouds", "Rings clone") sold as originals; check for the original MI logo and PCB markings (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Liquid on Reverb, ModularGrid marketplace, and the r/modular / Muff Wiggler-style communities; experts buy and sell at known prices, so margins come only from non-musician sellers (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Nothing, usually – musician sellers know. Watch for estate/"parts" listings only.

## Shipping / handling
Light, $8–15. Reverb 8%. Electronics – only "works" listings.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State maker, module name, HP width, tested working, power cable included, original vs clone, box/manual. Photograph the panel front, PCB back (logo/serial) and power header. Title: "[Maker] [Module] Eurorack module, tested, w/ power cable".
