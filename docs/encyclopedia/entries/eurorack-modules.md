# Eurorack synthesizer modules
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: ModularGrid price index knowledge, Reverb sold knowledge

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
3U-tall aluminium panels with 3.5 mm patch jacks and knobs, mounted in a rack/case. Brand on the panel: **Make Noise** (Maths $200–250 used, Morphagene $350–400, Rosie $120–150, DPO $400+), **Mutable Instruments** (discontinued – Clouds/Rings/Plaits/Beads $250–500, originals with the MI logo are gold), **Erica Synths** (Pico series $60–120, Black series $200–400), **Endorphin.es** (Milky Way, Squawk Dirty, Grand Terminal – $150–400), **Intellijel**, **ALM Busy Circuits**, **Noise Engineering**, **Doepfer** (budget, $40–120), **Behringer** (junk tier $30–60). Sellers of eurorack are almost always expert and price at ModularGrid/Reverb levels; the only arbitrage is a non-musician estate seller calling it "synthesizer parts" or a whole case priced as a lump.

## What sellers usually miss
Nothing, usually – musician sellers know. Watch for estate/"parts" listings only.

## Shipping / handling
Light, $8–15. Reverb 8%. Electronics – only "works" listings.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a full case or "synth parts" lot from a non-musician at < 30% of ModularGrid sum. Pass when: musician seller pricing per module. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
