## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Any lidded wooden container. Valuable families and tells:
- **Georgian tea caddies** (1760–1840): sarcophagus or rectangular, mahogany/rosewood/satinwood veneer, inlaid stringing, lidded compartments and mixing bowl inside, lion-mask ring handles.
- **Writing slopes / lap desks** (1800–1880): open into a sloped writing surface with baize/leather, inkwells, hidden drawers; brass corners ("campaign").
- **Tunbridge ware** (Kent, 1830–1900): wood mosaic pictures of castles/flowers made of tiny tesserae.
- **Tramp art** (1870–1940): layered notch-carved cigar-box wood.
- **Victorian jewelry/dressing boxes**: burr walnut, brass inlay, fitted interiors.
- **Low-value**: 1950s–90s imports — Chinese red-lacquered boxes with embossed brass panels (junks/ships, dragons), Indian brass-inlaid boxes, Philippine carved, Hong Kong camphor chests (small).

## Makers, brands and variants
Makers rarely marked: Tunbridge makers (Edmund Nye, Thomas Barton), retailers (Asprey, Betjemann locks), lock makers (Bramah, Chubb, "Patent" stamped locks indicate quality). Chinese export (Canton lacquer, 19th c.) vs 20th-c. tourist.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Hand-cut dovetails, solid hardwood with veneer of figured woods, period brass hardware with slotted screws, original locks with keys, shellac/French polish finish. Cheap: plywood/MDF, Phillips screws, stamped brass sheet panels, wire hinges, spray finish, staples.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Lifted veneer, missing inlay, split lids, broken hinges, missing keys, replaced hardware, water marks. Veneer repairs are restorer work (Dylan avoids).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Many modern "antique-style" tea caddies and writing boxes from India/China (artificially aged, often with brass "campaign" corners). Tell: machine dovetails, new screws, uniform distressing, no wear where hands touch.

## Market
Good antique boxes sell steadily to decorators and collectors; import boxes are slow.

## What sellers usually miss
Hidden compartments and inkwells, Bramah/Chubb locks, Tunbridge mosaic on lids, tea caddy interiors.

## Shipping / handling
Shoebox to carry-on size, 2–10 lb; moderate fragility; $15–30.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Dimensions, wood, construction (dovetails), hardware/lock, interior, key, damage.

## Buy rule
Profit = median × 0.87 − ~$20 − ~$20 − price ≥ max($50, price). Import boxes never qualify. Georgian caddies/writing slopes/Tunbridge at ≤ $60 when median ≥ $200. No veneer repair jobs. Fits a car seat.
