## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- **Zenith H725/K725/Y723 (1951–55)**: maroon or brown bakelite, a big round gold dial with "Zenith" below, a carry handle. Working $70–130; untested $30–60.
- **Philco Transitone, RCA 8X, GE 400-series**: similar, $40–90.
- **Catalin** (swirled or marbled, translucent: Fada 1000 "Bullet", Addison, Emerson AU-190) is $800+. It never looks flat brown.
- Other forms (general knowledge, verify): wooden cathedral and tombstone radios (1930s), console floor radios (bigger than a passenger seat), and chairside sets.
- Seller words: "vintage Zenith radio", "tube radio", "bakelite radio", "antique radio".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Zenith**: H725/K725/Y723 brown bakelite AM/FM; Trans-Oceanic portables (separate market) (general knowledge, verify).
- **Philco** Transitone; **RCA** 8X; **GE** 400-series; **Emerson**; **Sentinel** (named in the original index row).
- **Catalin makers**: Fada (1000 "Bullet"), Addison, Emerson (AU-190 "Tombstone"). Colour combinations multiply the price (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Bakelite: an opaque, dense brown or black phenolic. Catalin: cast phenolic, translucent and marbled, often yellowed with age. Plaskon/urea: an opaque light colour (white, ivory, pastel).
- The chassis label on the back carries the model number. An original back panel and knobs matter.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Cracked cabinets (catalin shrinks and cracks), missing knobs or back, a frayed cord. Old capacitors need replacing before powering up ("recapping").
- Untested sets are a pass under Dylan's rule. Plugging in an unrestored set can damage it (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Reproduction catalin cabinets and knobs exist, and modern "retro" radios (Crosley, Thomas) are sold as vintage (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Collectors and decorators. Restored, working sets sell much better than untested ones. eBay, plus the local and antique-radio club market. Steady but aging buyer base (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
- Catalin vs bakelite: catalin is translucent and marbled.

## Shipping / handling
- Table radios 5–10 lb, fragile cabinets, double-boxed $20–35 (general knowledge, verify).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- Maker, model (from the chassis label), year, cabinet material, working or not, and whether it has been restored (recapped), cracks, knobs and back present.
- Title keywords: "Vintage Zenith H725 Bakelite AM FM Tube Radio Working".

## Buy rule
Buy when: coloured catalin (Fada, Addison, Emerson): stop everything and evaluate it.
Pass when: brown bakelite above $40; untested is a pass for Dylan regardless.
Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (table sets do; consoles don't).
