## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Small boxed speakers with grilles. Value tells: walnut veneer cabinets (vs vinyl wood-grain wrap), rear spring terminals plus binding posts, metal badges, tweeter level controls (AR), BBC LS3/5A label on back, heavy cabinets. Low-value tells: vinyl wood-grain wrap on particle board, plastic badge, center-channel + satellite combos matching a 1990s rack receiver, "SB-" model numbers on Technics small satellites.
- **Where the marks are**: a paper or metal label on the back (maker, model, impedance, serial); badges on the grille; AR has an AR emblem on the grille and, on 1970s models, arrows on the back marking level-control settings (AR-2ax page).
- **LS3/5A**: a very small (about shoebox-size) sealed box with a recessed front baffle, felt strips around the drivers and a grille; the back label names the licensee and carries the BBC LS3/5A designation (Stereophile).
- **Realistic Minimus-7**: a die-cast aluminium cabinet with a removable metal grille, about 180 × 113 × 110 mm and 2.03 kg each, made in Japan for Tandy/Radio Shack from c. 1977 (zstereo).
- Look-alikes: 1990s–2000s "bookshelf" units from rack systems that share brand names (Pioneer, Technics, Sony) with better 1970s lines; Bose small cubes and 301s (common) vs the 901 (larger, needs its equaliser).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Acoustic Research (AR)** AR-2ax, AR-4x, AR-7, AR-18; **KLH** Model 17/32; **Advent** Smaller/Baby Advent; **Boston Acoustics** A40/A60; **Polk**; **JBL** L-16/L-26, 4310 (big).
  - AR-2ax (c. 1970–71, $128 each in 1971): sealed acoustic-suspension three-way; cast-aluminium woofer basket with embossed AR logo held by six screws; **impregnated cloth woofer surround, not foam**; level-control pots on the crossover inside the cabinet (AR-2ax page).
- **British monitors**: Rogers/Spendor/Harbeth LS3/5A, KEF Cresta/Coda, B&W DM-series, Celestion.
  - **LS3/5A** (BBC design, early 1970s, for outside-broadcast trucks): KEF B110 4.5" Bextrene-cone bass/mid and KEF T27 19 mm Mylar-dome tweeter, 13-element crossover, veneered birch cabinet. Licensees: Rogers (first), Spendor, Harbeth, Goodmans; former Audiomaster, Chartwell, RAM (Stereophile). Rogers revised the driver in 1988 (synthetic surround replacing Neoprene). Collectors also separate the earlier 15-ohm from the later 11-ohm versions (general knowledge, verify on the back label). KEF supplied the drivers; a "KEF LS3/5A" search mostly returns parts (see Comps).
- **Japanese mass market**: Technics SB-, Pioneer CS-/S-, Sony SS-, Panasonic, Kenwood — mostly low value except 1970s large Pioneer/Technics (too big).
- **Realistic (Radio Shack/Tandy)**: Minimus-7 (8 ohm, 100 mm woofer, 25 mm dome tweeter, infinite baffle) and many later Minimus/Optimus variants; sound and build differ by year (Audiokarma thread on differences).

### Quick ID checklist
- Back label: maker, model, impedance, serial (photo).
- Cabinet: real wood veneer (grain wraps edges, end grain at corners) vs vinyl wrap on particle board.
- Grille off: woofer surround intact? Foam crumbling or missing = refoam needed; AR-2ax era cloth surrounds don't rot like foam.
- Dust caps and tweeter domes not pushed in.
- LS3/5A: licensee name and BBC designation on the label; impedance (15 or 11 ohm); pair serials close together.
- Matching pair (consecutive or close serials, same veneer, same grille).
- Terminals: spring clips (older/cheaper) vs binding posts; level controls present (AR, some JBL).
- Video of each driver playing, including the tweeter.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Real walnut veneer, sealed acoustic-suspension weight, cloth surrounds; foam surrounds rot (1970s–90s), needs refoaming = repair.
- Heavy, sealed, well-braced cabinets (AR, KLH, Advent, LS3/5A with damped birch) vs light, hollow-sounding particle-board boxes.
- Cast-aluminium woofer baskets (AR) vs stamped steel.
- Die-cast metal cabinets (Minimus-7) are a deliberate quality design, not a cheap one.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Foam rot, blown tweeters, pushed-in dust caps, veneer peeling, missing grilles.
- AR: corroded level-control pots leave mid and tweeter nearly silent or scratchy; dried putty seal around the woofer (AR-2ax page). Fixable, but it is repair.
- Replaced drivers (non-original woofers or tweeters) cut collector value; check driver part numbers match the model.
- Grille cloth stains and sun fade; veneer water damage at the base.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Unauthorized LS3/5A clones and kit versions; otherwise low.
- LS3/5A has also been reissued by modern makers (e.g. Falcon Acoustics, Stirling, Rogers reissues); these are new speakers and should be sold as such (Falcon review exists at https://www.stereonet.com/reviews/falcon-acoustics-ls3-5a-loudspeaker-review).
- AR and Advent names were later used on unrelated budget products; a modern "AR" or "Advent" sticker on a light cabinet is not the classic model.

## Market
Big category, fast for named classics; mass-market sets barely sell (shipping exceeds value). Buyers are vintage hi-fi enthusiasts (Audiokarma community), restorers who buy rotted-foam pairs to refoam, and users. Local pickup sells larger pairs best.

## What sellers usually miss
AR and KLH small speakers with faded grilles in garage sales; LS3/5A labels on the back.
- The licensee and impedance on an LS3/5A label; original cloth surrounds on early AR (no refoam needed).

## Shipping / handling
Pairs 10–25 lb; $25–45 to ship, double-boxed. Shipping often > value for low tiers. Protect the grilles and tweeter domes; a Minimus-7 pair is about 4.5 kg plus packing.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Video of each driver playing, surround condition, serials, cabinet photos.
- Title keywords: brand + model + "pair" + "tested" (e.g. "Acoustic Research AR-4x Speakers Pair Tested Walnut", "Rogers LS3/5A 15 Ohm Pair").

### What to tell the buyer
Give maker, model, impedance and serials from the back labels, and say whether they are a matched pair. State how they were tested (amplifier, all drivers playing, level controls working), with a video. Describe surrounds (original cloth, original foam intact, or refoamed and when), any replaced drivers, and cabinet finish (real veneer or vinyl) with flaws. For LS3/5A, name the licensee and the version as printed on the label.

## Buy rule
Speakers are electronics: tested on video required (no untested). Mass-market sets: pass. Classic small monitors with median ≥ $250 and price ≤ $80 only.
