# Vintage fishing reels (casting, spinning, saltwater) and bamboo rods
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge, Old Reel Collectors Association (ORCA) price knowledge, Lang's auction results; complements the existing fly-reels entry

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Casting (level-wind) reels: **Pflueger Supreme** (1920s–50s, $30–80), **Shakespeare President/Marhoff** ($15–40), **Abu Garcia Ambassadeur** (Sweden, red/black/green round reels – 5000/6000 1960s–70s $50–150, rare colours and the 2500C/1750 $150–400), **Penn** saltwater (Senator 4/0–12/0 $60–200, International gold lever drags $150–500), **Newell** (black graphite, $100–250 each), **Shimano Trinidad/Calcutta** ($200–450), **Daiwa Saltiga**, **Tiburon** frames. Antique brass/German-silver reels (pre-1910, Meek, Milam, Talbot, Vom Hofe) are the top tier ($300–3,000). **Olympic/Dohzuki** and most Japanese 1970s reels $15–40. Bamboo fly rods: **Montague**, **South Bend**, **Heddon** production rods $60–200 complete in tube with both tips; broken or missing tips $20–60 ("rod ends" = sections only). Tells: maker's name on foot or side plate; serial on Ambassadeur foot; original tube and bag for rods.

## What sellers usually miss
Ambassadeur and Penn models in a "box of old reels" lot; whether a bamboo rod is complete (two tips + tube = 3× the value of "ends"). Saltwater sellers usually know their reels (Newell/Tiburon owners price at market).

## Shipping / handling
Reels 1–3 lb ($10–15). Rods need a 60" tube ($25–35, oversize). eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a lot with a visible Ambassadeur/Penn Senator/Newell under $30; complete Montague/South Bend bamboo in tube under $40. Pass when: seller lists per-reel market prices; rods described as "ends/sections". Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
