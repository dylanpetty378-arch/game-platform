## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- **Casting (level-wind) reels**:
  - **Pflueger Supreme** (1920s–50s, $30–80)
  - **Shakespeare President/Marhoff** ($15–40)
  - **ABU Garcia Ambassadeur** (Sweden; red, black or green round reels. 5000/6000 1960s–70s $50–150; rare colours and the 2500C/1750 $150–400)
- **Saltwater reels**:
  - **Penn** (Senator 4/0–12/0 $60–200; International gold lever drags $150–500)
  - **Newell** (black graphite, $100–250 each)
  - **Shimano Trinidad/Calcutta** ($200–450)
  - **Daiwa Saltiga**, **Tiburon** frames
- **Antique brass and German-silver reels** (pre-1910: Meek, Milam, Talbot, Vom Hofe) are the top tier ($300–3,000).
- **Olympic/Dohzuki** and most Japanese 1970s reels: $15–40.
- **Bamboo fly rods**: **Montague**, **South Bend** and **Heddon** production rods are $60–200 complete in the tube with both tips. Broken or missing tips: $20–60 ("rod ends" = sections only).
- Tells: the maker's name on the foot or side plate; the serial on an Ambassadeur's foot; the original tube and bag for rods.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **ABU Ambassadeur** (Sweden): 5000/6000 standard; 5000C, 2500C and 1750A small reels; rare colours (green, black, blue) and early "three-screw" and 1950s serials are worth more (see spinning-and-conventional-reels for $100–400 three-screw). Japanese and later versions are worth less (general knowledge, verify).
- **Penn**: Senator 4/0–12/0, Squidder, Jigmaster, International (gold, lever drag; the 130 is top).
- **Newell** (California): graphite-frame big-game reels, P/C series (e.g. P229-F).
- **Shimano**: Trinidad (TN40 etc.), Calcutta.
- **Tiburon**: SST frames and 2-speed conversions.
- **Edward Vom Hofe** (NY), **B.F. Meek, B.C. Milam, Talbot** (Kentucky): antique, hand-made, top.
- **Hardy Perfect**: see fly-reels.
- **Rods**: Montague (production bamboo, Splitswitch and others), South Bend, Heddon. Higher-end bamboo makers are in bamboo-fly-rods.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- German silver, hard rubber side plates and hand-engraving = antique. Graphite and anodised aluminium frames = modern big-game (Newell, Tiburon).
- Bamboo rods: count the sections and tips (for example, a 3-piece with 2 tips), the original tube and bag, the maker's decal or stamp, the ferrule fit, and the varnish condition.
- Complete with tube and both tips = about 3× the value of "ends".

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Reels: corrosion (saltwater), worn drags, cracked side plates, missing star drags or handles, seized gears.
- Rods: broken tips, delaminated or set (bent) sections, loose ferrules, refinished rods (lower value), missing tube.
- Ask for the section count and length, and photos of the reel foot and serial.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- ABU reissued "Record" and "Classic" Ambassadeurs. Asian-made rods carry fake decals. Married reel parts are common (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Freshwater collectors (ORCA, NFLCC) and saltwater users. Saltwater big-game reels sell fast to fishermen, who know prices. Antique reels go through Lang's auctions. Spring peak (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
- Ambassadeur and Penn models in a "box of old reels" lot.
- Whether a bamboo rod is complete: two tips plus a tube = 3× the value of "ends".
- Saltwater sellers usually know their reels; Newell and Tiburon owners price at market.

## Shipping / handling
- Reels 1–3 lb ($10–15). Rods need a 60" tube ($25–35, oversize). eBay 13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- Reels: maker, model, serial, country, drag and gear condition, cranking video, box/papers.
- Rods: maker, length, line weight, number of sections and tips, tube and bag, ferrule fit, any repairs.
- Photograph the reel foot, the side plates, the rod decal or stamp, every section laid out and the tips.
- Title keywords: "Shimano Trinidad TN40", "Newell P229-F", "Montague Bamboo Fly Rod 9' 3/2 with Tube", "Ambassadeur 5000 Sweden".

## Buy rule
Buy when: a lot with a visible Ambassadeur/Penn Senator/Newell is under $30; a complete Montague/South Bend bamboo rod in its tube is under $40.
Pass when: the seller lists per-reel market prices; rods are described as "ends/sections".
Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (a 60" rod tube fits lengthwise).
