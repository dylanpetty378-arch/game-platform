## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Galvanised (zinc-coated steel) watering cans with a top handle and/or side handle, round or oval body, and spout with or without a sprinkler rose. Long tapered spouts suggest greenhouse use. French cans: zinc, oval, brass rose.

## Makers, brands and variants
Haws (UK, 1886–), Dennis, Wheeling Corrugating (US), Behrens, Dover, Rowell, French arrosoirs (Guillouard, Nantes).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Spangle pattern in zinc, hand-soldered seams on older cans; brass rose; stamped maker plates.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Leaks at seams, white rust, dents, paint remnants, missing rose.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern decorative galvanized cans (Target, Hobby Lobby), often thin with faux aging.
