## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Pressed glass with a metallic-salt iridescent finish sprayed on before annealing. Two eras:
- **Old carnival (c. 1907–1930)**: Fenton, Northwood (underlined N in circle mark), Imperial, Millersburg, Dugan/Diamond; marigold, purple/amethyst, cobalt blue, green, ice blue, white, peach opal. Thick, richly detailed molds, ground or collar bases.
- **New/contemporary (1960s–present)**: Imperial reissues (IG mark), Fenton (marked), Indiana Glass (Harvest Grape, Heirloom — very common, blue/marigold/purple 1970s–80s), Westmoreland, L.G. Wright, Boyd, St. Clair, Summit, Mosser; plus Taiwanese/Chinese reproductions.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Northwood**: underlined N mark; Grape & Cable, Peacocks, Good Luck, Poppy Show; best values.
- **Fenton** (old: unmarked; new: script Fenton mark from 1970s).
- **Millersburg** (1909–1912): highest quality, rare; Peacock, Hobstar.
- **Imperial**: old unmarked; 1960s–70s reissues with IG.
- **Indiana Glass** (Dunkirk, IN): Harvest Grape pitcher/tumbler sets (blue, amethyst, marigold) and Heirloom pressed series, 1970s–80s; huge numbers sold through retailers/Tiara. Low value.
- **Jeannette, Federal, Hazel-Atlas** "Depression carnival" — low value.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Old: base color visible when held to light (purple, cobalt), iridescence with depth and multi-colored "oil-slick" sheen; worn iridescence on high points; mold seams soft. New: thinner, lighter, very even "electric" iridescence over clear/blue glass, mold sharpness in a mechanical way, sometimes marked. Indiana Harvest Grape: footed pitcher with stylized grape clusters and leaves, flared tumblers; electric blue iridescence.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips on rims/feet, flakes on grape bunches, scratched iridescence, cracks in handles, ground-down rims (repair). Dishwasher-faded iridescence.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Many reproductions of Northwood patterns with fake N marks; Chinese "carnival" in old patterns. When unsure, assume new.

## Market
Old carnival collector base is aging; prices fell since 2000s, rare pieces still strong. Indiana sets sell slowly as decor.

## What sellers usually miss
Old Northwood marks; strong base colors (amethyst, cobalt) in old pieces; aqua opal and peach opal edges.

## Shipping / handling
Pitcher sets 8–12 lb boxed; high breakage risk; $25–40. Fits a passenger seat in a box.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Identify maker/age honestly (reissue vs old), dimensions, count, chips, iridescence condition, light-through-base color photos.
