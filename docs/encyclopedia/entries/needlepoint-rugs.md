## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Flat rugs with floral/medallion designs. **Needlepoint** = wool yarn hand-stitched in tent or cross stitch over a canvas grid; on the back you see slanting stitches and a canvas grid, colours mirror the front; often backed with cotton and edges bound. **Machine tapestry/jacquard** = woven on a loom; front has a fine "woven fabric" look with floats, back is a reversed-colour weave, edges serged; usually thin, light, and polyester/cotton/viscose blend. Room-size needlepoint is heavy (wool) and often sewn from panels (visible seams).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Chinese hand-stitched needlepoint (1980s–2010s)**: majority of what's sold; labels like "100% wool hand-stitched".
- **Portuguese Arraiolos**: cross-stitched wool on jute, folk designs.
- **English Victorian Berlin woolwork, 1940s–60s English needlepoint**: high value.
- **Stark Carpet, Patterson Flynn Martin, Elizabeth Bradley designs**: decorator-grade.
- **Machine tapestry**: Belgian/Turkish jacquard, chenille, "Aubusson style" mats from big-box stores.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Wool, irregular hand stitches, slight canvas distortion = hand. Uniform weave, polyester sheen, tag "Made in Belgium/Turkey, 100% polyester/olefin" = machine.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Moth damage, worn spots showing canvas, stains, colour run from washing, seam separation in panel-sewn rugs, dog chewing at edges.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Machine jacquard rugs and pillows constantly titled "needlepoint/Aubusson". Antique claims on 1990s Chinese production.

## Market
Decorator demand steady; room-size sells but shipping is heavy. Small rugs move in 30–60 days.

## What sellers usually miss
Hand-stitched small rugs sold as "mats"; Arraiolos labelled "embroidered rug".

## Shipping / handling
Small rugs roll into a 24" box, 3–6 lb, $12–20. Room-size 30–60 lb — beyond Dylan's size rule.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "needlepoint rug" + "hand stitched" + "wool" + style (Aubusson, Arraiolos, floral medallion) + size in feet + colour + "vintage". Junk: "tapestry", "mat", "French style" alone. Example: `Vintage Hand Stitched Wool Needlepoint Rug Aubusson Floral 3x5 Beige Rose`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for small rugs (decorators search by size, 30–60 day sell); Chairish lists higher for decorator-grade; room-size on FB local to avoid 30–60 lb freight.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median ($100–150 for a small Chinese hand-stitched; $500–950 room-size); accept −15%, floor $75 small; OBO; cut 10% after 45 days.
- **Photos:** 1) full rug flat, front, straight-on in daylight; then the back showing the slanting tent stitches and canvas grid, a corner with binding/backing, stitch close-up with a ruler, any wear/moth/stain spot, label if present.
- **State:** exact dimensions in inches, wool hand-stitched (tent stitch), backing and binding, panel seams if any, label/origin, no odours, moth/wear/stain/colour-run flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** roll around a tube, plastic sleeve, 24" box, 3–6 lb, $12–20; room-size 30–60 lb folds into a 24×24×24 or rides local in an SUV cargo area.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Hand-stitched wool needlepoint, not a machine jacquard tapestry — turn it over and you see the tent stitches; comparable [3x5 / room-size] hand-stitched Aubusson-style rugs have sold for [$X] and new decorator needlepoint from Stark runs far more."
