## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Bell-shaped glass or porcelain pieces that held wires on poles. Collectors classify glass by **CD (Consolidated Design) number** and porcelain by **U number**.
- **Glass:** embossed with maker and style (HEMINGRAY-42, BROOKFIELD, W.T.CO., ARMSTRONG). Colour is the main value driver. Aqua and clear are very common; sun-coloured (SCA) purple is mid; true cobalt, deep amber, yellow-green, and "milk" are better.
- **Porcelain:** glazed brown, white or blue-grey. Small spool and "saddle-top" types (Locke 8881 – the example) are common.

## Makers, brands and variants
Glass: Hemingray (Covington KY / Muncie IN, 1848–1972), Brookfield, Whitall Tatum, Armstrong, Kerr, McLaughlin, Lynchburg, California, Pyrex (Corning). Porcelain: Locke (Victor NY), Ohio Brass, Knowles, Thomas, Lapp, Pass & Seymour.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Check embossing, colour in daylight, and the base for "drip points" (some Hemingrays). Chips on the skirt or threads reduce value a lot.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips, flea bites, cracks, "base bruise". Some sellers grind or polish chips — look for frosted spots.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
**Altered colours**: clear glass irradiated or heat-treated to purple/amber; fake "rare" colours. Reproductions of rare CDs exist. Use the NIA guides.

## Market
Organised collector hobby (NIA shows), knowledgeable buyers. Common pieces glut.

## What sellers usually miss
A coloured glass insulator mixed with aqua ones in a box. Colour is visible from photos.

## Shipping / handling
Heavy and fragile; $8–15 for one or two; pack each in bubble.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker (Hemingray/Brookfield/Locke), CD or U number, embossing (Hemingray-42), colour in daylight terms (cobalt/SCA purple/amber/olive), "glass insulator"/"porcelain insulator", "threadless" if so. Junk: "telephone pole glass", "rare" on aqua, "vintage decor". `Hemingray CD 162 Cobalt Blue Glass Insulator Embossed Drip Points`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (NIA collectors search by CD number); NIA shows for rare CDs and threadless; aqua/clear and brown porcelain lotted on FB local.
- **Price position:** coloured glass at the sold median (cobalt Hemingray $21.50, threadless $85), OBO, accept −15%; aqua/clear lots $3–10 each local, firm; cut 10% at 45 days.
- **Photos:** first: insulator backlit in daylight for true colour; then embossing both sides, base/drip points, threads inside, skirt edge all round for chips, any frosted ground spots, ruler.
- **State:** CD/U number, maker, embossing wording, colour, chips/flea bites/base bruises/cracks by photo number, note any polished spots.
- **Pack / haul:** heavy and fragile; each in bubble, small box, $8–15 for one or two.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Hemingray CD 162] in true [cobalt], no skirt chips — the aqua ones are $5 box-lot glass; the coloured pieces are what NIA collectors pay $20–150 for."
