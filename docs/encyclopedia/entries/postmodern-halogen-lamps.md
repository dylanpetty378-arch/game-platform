# Postmodern / Memphis-era halogen lamps (Sonneman, Kovacs, Koch & Lowy)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: 1stDibs listings for Sonneman/Kovacs "Saturn" lamp (c.1980, three frosted glass rings on two columns, marble base, halogen, 22" h), design-mkt.com, eBay sold knowledge

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1978–1990 table and floor lamps: black or brass metal with frosted glass discs, cones or fins; halogen bulb under a dome with a dimmer. Key designs: **Robert Sonneman for George Kovacs "Saturn"** – stacked frosted-glass rings/dome on two thin black columns, round black marble or metal base; Sonneman **"Flying Saucer"** floor lamp; **Koch & Lowy** brass and glass desk lamps; **Memphis**-style Ettore Sottsass or Michele De Lucchi lamps (Artemide) are a tier above. Sellers write "space-age lamp", "80s modern lamp", "UFO lamp" and never the designer. Look for a Kovacs/Sonneman sticker under the base.

## What sellers usually miss
Designer attribution; that halogen fixtures are rewirable (buyers accept "works"). Condition tells: chips to glass rings, missing dimmer knob, marble base cracks.

## Shipping / handling
Heavy (marble base 10–15 lb) and glass: $40–60 ground double-boxed, rings wrapped separately. eBay 13%. Big risk of breakage – ask seller to ship rings and base in separate boxes.

## Buy rule
Buy when: an identifiable Sonneman/Kovacs Saturn under $80, or any designer-attributed 1980s halogen under $60. Pass when: over $120 (shipping and breakage eat the spread), glass chipped. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
