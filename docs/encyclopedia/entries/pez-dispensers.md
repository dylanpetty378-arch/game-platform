## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Spring-loaded candy dispensers with a character head on a stem. Dating tells visible in photos:
- **Feet**: stems with small "feet" at the bottom were introduced c.1987. **No feet** = older (pre-1987) — the single biggest value tell.
- **Patent numbers** on the stem: 2,620,061 (1950s–60s), 3,410,455 (1968–1980s), 3,845,882 (1970s–80s), 4,966,305 (1990s, footed), 5,984,285 (late 1990s+). Lower number = older.
- **Country**: Austria, Yugoslavia, Hungary, Hong Kong (older); China, Slovenia (newer).
- **Head detail**: early heads have softer plastic, painted details; die-cut "fins" on some.
- **Regulars**: 1950s dispensers with no character head (lighter-shaped) are the original product.

## Makers, brands and variants
PEZ (Austria 1927; US 1952). Character series: Disney, Looney Tunes (Tweety very common), Marvel (Hulk 1978 no-feet with dark green stem/black hair is the common version), Universal Monsters (1960s–70s), Halloween, Christmas (Santa), animals, Star Wars (1997+ footed, common), Bicentennial (1976, valuable), Make-a-Face (1972, rare), Psychedelic Eye/Hand (1967–68).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Cellulose acetate heads on old; brittle springs. Fake/repro heads exist on old stems ("marriages"). Check consistent colour/plastic.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken springs (common, minor), missing or broken head hinge pins, chewed heads, discoloured paint, swapped heads/stems (big value hit), missing card for carded items.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Re-releases of classic characters in footed versions; counterfeit rarities (Make-a-Face, Political, Elvis) with re-moulded heads; married stems. Rarities need collector authentication.

## Market
Active collector base; no-feet dispensers sell steadily in days to weeks. Footed common dispensers are close to worthless.

## What sellers usually miss
- "Vintage" Pez listings are mostly footed; real value is no-feet, identifiable from a side photo.
- Junk-drawer lots can include early no-feet Austrian or Yugoslavian pieces.

## Shipping / handling
Tiny and light: $5 in a padded mailer. Ideal.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Feet/no-feet, patent number, country, head condition, spring working, any swaps.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − ~$5 to buyer − ~$5 to Dylan − price ≥ max($50, price). Single common no-feet pieces never qualify. Buy only early no-feet rarities with confirmed ≥ $80 comps at ≤ $15, or lots with several no-feet heads at ≤ $3 each. Avoid rarities that need authentication unless very cheap.
