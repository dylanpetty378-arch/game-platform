Buy rule (short): Buy 1960s Japanese papier-mâché/composition nodders (gold/colored base, "Japan" sticker) at ≤ 1/3 resale; pass on 2000s licensed holiday/SGA bobbleheads unless a complete set or a hot player.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Figures with spring-mounted oversized heads. Vintage (1960–72): papier-mâché/composition, hand-painted, round or square
colored bases with team decals, "Japan" stamp. Modern (1999+): resin/polyresin, boxed. The modern era is usually dated to
the 1999 San Francisco Giants Willie Mays giveaway (~35,000 given away) (NAC Value). Typical vintage size: ~7" for
regular nodders (SCP lot), ~4½" for the minis (Keyman); 1950s figures ~4–5" (NAC Value).

Older non-sports "nodders" (pagods, Asian porcelain/pottery nodding figures) use wires or weights rather than a spring,
and are a different collecting field (NAC Value).

Where the marks are: under or on the base — "Japan" stamp or foil/paper sticker; distributor stickers such as "Sports
Specialties California/Japan" on late-1960s–70s gold bases (SCP); team decal on the front of the base; facsimile
autograph decal on 1961–63 player dolls (Keyman). Modern: year, team, sponsor and licence printed on the base or box.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **1960s baseball base series** (Keyman; Lelands):
  - **Colored base, 1960–61**: 12 MLB teams + 3 Pacific Coast League teams, each on a different colored square base; MLB
    dolls lack the city decal on the base that PCL dolls have.
  - **White base, 1961–63**: first comprehensive MLB series, $1.00 new, 20 franchises with variations, including nine
    mascot heads and four player dolls (Mantle, Maris, Mays, Clemente) with facsimile-autograph decals and picture boxes.
    All four player dolls share one face; only uniforms and numbers differ (NAC Value).
  - **Minis** (alongside the white base): ~4½", magnet on most bases, all MLB teams plus Mantle and Maris.
  - **1962 LA "Don Drysdale" series**: Dodgers and Angels fan dolls.
  - **Round green base, 1963–65** and **round gold base, later 1960s** (Lelands lot breakdown).
- **Sports Specialties** (Danny Goodman, Los Angeles): "Sports Specialties California/Japan" sticker on late-1960s–early
  1970s gold-base nodders, including the black-face variants (SCP, Lakers); a 1974 line of 7" dolls made in Taiwan on round
  gold bases in red windowed boxes (Keyman). Earlier entry text called them "Sports Specialties / Lego (Japan, 1960s)" —
  the Japan-made dolls were imported under several labels (Lego was a Japanese importer brand, not the Danish toy company;
  general knowledge, verify). MLB/NFL nodders $75–400; Mets, Senators, Colts, black-face variants more.
- **Beatles Car Mascots 1964**: four figures, each with a different head (NAC Value); $500–1,500 set.
- **Alexander Global, Bobble Dobbles, FOCO**: modern $10–50.
- **Toy Site / BD&A (2000–03)**: Rudolph and Island of Misfit Toys; singles $15–30, complete 10-piece sets $200–315 (comp).
- **Funko Wacky Wobblers**: $5–40.
- **Royal Bobbles**: modern licensed (general knowledge, verify).
- **Stadium giveaways (SGA)**: value set by player popularity and moment (e.g. the $105.91 Miguel Rojas Game 7 HR comp).

Value movers for vintage: team rarity (expansion/short-lived teams such as Colt .45s, Senators, Pilots), base series,
black-face variant, mascot vs player, original box, no neck cracks, decal intact.

### Quick ID checklist
- Base: square colored (1960–61), square white (1961–63), round green (1963–65), round gold (later 1960s–70s).
- Underside: "Japan" stamp/sticker or "Sports Specialties" sticker = vintage; printed year/licence/sponsor = modern.
- Material: hand-painted papier-mâché with fine crazing vs crisp, glossy polyresin/plastic.
- Size: ~7" regular, ~4½" mini with a magnet base.
- Face: black-face variant? Mascot head vs boy head vs player doll with facsimile-autograph decal?
- Neck: cracks or repaired breaks at the neck ring; spring present and nodding freely.
- Decal on base: original, worn but intact vs replaced/crisp.
- Box: original picture box (1961–63 player dolls) or window box (modern); SGA box with sponsor.
- Set completeness for modern holiday lines (Toy Site Rudolph 10-piece set).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Papier-mâché crazing and paint flaking on vintage; resin crisp modern. Vintage paint is hand-applied with slight
irregularity at eyes and lettering; the head is hollow and light. Older nodders used springs for sports dolls, while
older Asian nodding figures used wires (NAC Value). Plastic generally signals newer (NAC Value). Gloss coat on vintage
yellows and crazes evenly; a fresh glossy coat over crazing suggests restoration (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Neck cracks/head breaks, repaint, missing springs, box damage. Also decal nicks and chips at the base edge (SCP grades on
these). Ask for a close photo of the neck ring and the underside of the head, and a short video of it nodding. Near-mint
vintage is described as gloss intact, no paint nicks, nods consistently (SCP).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
1960s nodder repros (2000s Japan-style) exist; repro decals. Retro-style modern reissues are usually resin and heavier,
with printed (not hand-painted) detail and a copyright/licence line under the base (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
High volume; holiday items seasonal (Nov–Dec). Vintage sports nodders sell to team collectors year-round, best around
baseball season and the postseason (general knowledge, verify). Auction houses (Lelands, SCP, Grey Flannel) handle
large vintage collections.

## What sellers usually miss
1960s nodders in estate boxes priced like modern. Also: base colour/shape dates the doll; minis with magnet bases; the
"Sports Specialties" sticker; black-face variants; PCL teams on colored bases.

## Shipping / handling
Boxed $10–15; vintage needs careful padding. Immobilise the head (wrap head and body together or pad under the chin) so
the spring cannot snap the neck in transit.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Box condition, all sides, year/maker on base.

### What to tell the buyer
"[Team/character] bobbing head nodder, [~7" / 4½" mini], hand-painted papier-mâché, made in Japan, [square colored /
square white / round green / round gold] base, which dates it to c.[range]. Sticker/stamp under base: [text]. Condition:
[neck cracks none/hairline], [paint chips], [decal], nods freely. [Original box included/not]." For modern pieces: year,
team or licence, giveaway/sponsor, box condition, and whether it is part of a set.
