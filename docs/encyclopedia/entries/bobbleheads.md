## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Figures with spring-mounted oversized heads. Vintage (1960–72): papier-mâché/composition, hand-painted, round or square colored bases with team decals, "Japan" stamp. Modern (1999+): resin/polyresin, boxed.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Sports Specialties / Lego (Japan, 1960s)**: MLB/NFL nodders; $75–400; Mets, Senators, Colts, black-face variants more.
- **Beatles Car Mascots 1964**: $500–1,500 set.
- **Alexander Global, Bobble Dobbles, FOCO**: modern $10–50.
- **Toy Site / BD&A (2000–03)**: Rudolph and Island of Misfit Toys; singles $15–30, complete 10-piece sets $200–315 (comp).
- **Funko Wacky Wobblers**: $5–40.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Papier-mâché crazing and paint flaking on vintage; resin crisp modern.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Neck cracks/head breaks, repaint, missing springs, box damage.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
1960s nodder repros (2000s Japan-style) exist; repro decals.

## Shipping / handling

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
