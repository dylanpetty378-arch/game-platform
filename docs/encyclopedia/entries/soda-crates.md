Buy rule (short): Pass on standard wooden crates and plastic carriers (bulky, low margin); only early six-pack wooden/metal carriers or rare regional-bottler crates at ≤ $15.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Wood case with dividers for 24 bottles (or 12 big bottles), metal-banded corners, stencilled brand. Plastic crates and carriers are 1970s+. Date stamps (e.g., "1968") on end boards; bottler city stencils.

## Makers, brands and variants
Coca-Cola (yellow/red), Pepsi (blue), Dr Pepper, 7-Up (green), RC Cola; carrier makers (Ohio Box, etc.).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Real crates show wear, nail rust, metal edging; reproductions have uniform fresh stencils and staples.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Missing dividers, split boards, paint wear (accepted).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Decor-store repro crates common.
