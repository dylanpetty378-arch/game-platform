## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Embroidered cloth emblems. Age tells:
- **Twill/felt base with fully embroidered surface**: 1960s onward (fully embroidered = 1970s+ mostly).
- **Cut edge vs rolled (merrowed) edge**: cut-edge twill = generally pre-1970; merrowed (overlocked) edge = 1970s+.
- **Back**: plastic/glue backing = 1980s+; gauze/cheesecloth = older; no backing on chain-stitch felt = 1930s–50s.
- **Chenille/felt** letters = school and early sports.
- **Hand-embroidered bullion** (gold wire) = military officer insignia, sometimes theatre-made.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Scouting** (BSA, Scouts Canada, international): council shoulder patches (CSP), Order of the Arrow lodge flaps (first-issue "F1" flaps are valuable), National/World Jamboree patches (1937, 1950, 1953, 1957 BSA jamborees desirable), camp patches. Scouts Canada patches (Baden-Powell, Fleur-de-lis with maple leaf, district patches, "Trees for Canada") are a smaller market, mostly $2–10.
- **Presidential Physical Fitness Award** (1966–2012): eagle with numeral for consecutive years; extremely common, $3–8.
- **Snowmobile brands**: Arctic Cat (panther head), Ski-Doo, Polaris, Scorpion, Rupp, Boa-Ski, Moto-Ski; 1960s–70s club and race patches $10–40.
- **Motor/oil**: STP, Champion, Harley-Davidson, Gulf, Texaco, Mobil; uniform patches $10–50, older chain-stitched gas-station ones more.
- **Military**: WWII Army Air Forces squadron patches (Disney designs), theatre-made (Australia, India, Germany), Vietnam in-country made; high fake risk.
- **NASA/space**: original Apollo-era (AB Emblem, Lion Brothers, Crest Craft) $20–150, reproductions abundant.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Cut-edge twill, cotton thread with slight sheen loss, cheesecloth back = old. Shiny poly thread, merrowed edges, heat-seal backing = modern. Uniform-removed patches show stitch holes.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Fading, stains, moth holes, stitch holes from removal (acceptable), trimmed borders (reduce value), glue residue.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Rampant for WWII squadron, NASA, OA and Harley patches; reissues of jamboree patches. Military and OA rarities require collector authentication: outside Dylan's rules.

## Market
Large, liquid among collectors for scout and military; brand patches sell to nostalgia buyers and jacket customisers. Commons sit forever; good singles sell in 1–3 weeks.

## What sellers usually miss
- In mixed lots, a few brand or early OA patches carry the value; owners price lots by count.
- Cut-edge twill vs merrowed edge dates the patch at a glance.

## Shipping / handling
Light and flat: $5 in a rigid envelope. Ideal shipping category.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** type + issuer + name/date + edge: "BSA 1957 National Jamboree patch", "OA Lodge [number] flap cut edge", "Arctic Cat 1970s snowmobile patch", "WWII AAF squadron"; Junk: "patch lot", "iron on", "cool patch", "vintage" alone. Example: `Vintage 1970s Arctic Cat Panther Snowmobile Patch Cut Edge Twill Unused`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (scout, military and nostalgia buyers; good singles sell in 1–3 weeks); commons (fitness, modern rank, Scouts Canada district) only as a lot. OA/WWII rarities need authentication first.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (Arctic Cat $12, p75 $28; Presidential Fitness $9; brand/1970s jamboree $10–40; pre-1950 scout/cut-edge OA/Apollo originals $40–300), accept −15%, floor p25; OBO; cut 10% after 3 weeks.
- **Photos:** first photo the face flat, square-on; then back (backing, glue, stitch holes), edge macro (cut twill vs merrowed), ruler for size, any stains/moth holes, provenance item if removed from a uniform.
- **State:** size, edge type, backing, era estimate and why, stitch holes or unused, stains/trimmed border by photo number, provenance (removed from uniform, NOS), repro check.
- **Pack / haul:** flat and light; rigid envelope with cardboard, $5.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Arctic Cat] [1970s] cut-edge twill, [never sewn] — the period dealer patch, not the modern iron-on; these go [$10–40] and the cut edge is how you date it."

## Buy rule
