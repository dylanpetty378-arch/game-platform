# Pachinko machines (vintage Japanese, 1960s–80s)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: pachinkoplanet.com/machine-values, eBay listings 2025

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Upright pinball-like board ~32"x20", chrome frame, hundreds of brass pins, flipper-less; balls fed from a top tray. Makers printed on the face or back: Nishijin, Sankyo, Sanyo, Daiichi, Heiwa, Ginza. 1970s "Super DX" style = ball-feed tray on top; 1960s single-shot = thumb lever, no tray; electro-mechanical (Nishijin Powerflash/Circuit, 1974–75) have lights/relays. Seller words: "pachinko", "Japanese pinball", "slot machine".

## What sellers usually miss
Era. A single-shot 1960s or electro-mechanical machine listed as "old pachinko $80" is the only real upside. Themed faces are worth more than plain.

## Shipping / handling
~35 lb, glass face, pins bend – $60–90 to ship, high breakage risk. Local pickup only unless rare. Profit rarely clears Dylan's rule once shipping both ways is counted.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 1960s single-shot or EM model ≤ $100 and working, local. Pass when: "for parts or repair" (no repair rule), common 1970s at any price over $50 if it must ship.
