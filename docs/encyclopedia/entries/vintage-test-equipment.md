## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Analog meters with dial faces; tube testers in suitcase-style cases with roll charts; scopes with CRTs. Bakelite cases and printed scale faces with maker and city ("Marion Elec. Inst. Co., Manchester N.H.") date them to the 1930s–60s.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Hickok (Cleveland)**: tube testers — top tier for musicians/audio people ($200–800 serviced).
- **Simpson 260 (Elgin IL)**, **Triplett 630 (Bluffton OH)**: workhorse VOMs ($30–100).
- **Weston (Newark NJ)**: early meters; model 1 etc. $20–100.
- **Marion Electrical Instrument Co. (Manchester NH, 1920s–1960s)**: panel meters and Multi-Ranger VOM ($20–60).
- **Heathkit/Eico kits**: $20–80.
- **Tektronix/HP/General Radio**: lab gear, heavy.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Bakelite cases crack; check needle zero, mirror scales, roll charts present on tube testers.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Open meter movements (dead), corroded batteries, cracked cases, missing leads.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
None significant; decor "steampunk" meters.
