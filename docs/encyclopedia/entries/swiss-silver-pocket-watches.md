## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1870s–1920s Swiss pocket watches in silver cases, often marked 0.800, 0.935, "Argent", "Fine Silver", or with Swiss hallmarks (a bear or grouse for silver, after 1880). Ladies' (fob) sizes are 30–36 mm; gents' 45–52 mm. "Bijou", "Remontoir", "Cylindre 10 Rubis" and "Ancre" are generic dial or movement words, not makers. A white enamel dial with Roman numerals, blue-steel or Breguet hands, a sub-seconds dial, and an engine-turned bezel are typical of 1880–1900 exports. Large 45 mm+ with a sub-seconds dial are gents' size.

## Makers, brands and variants
Generic Swiss (Fontainemelon, A. Schild ébauches), Longines, Zenith, Omega, Waltham Swiss imports, Elgin (US). "Bijou" dials appear on Swiss export watches sold in the US c.1880–1900.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Silver cases tarnish; look for 0.800/0.935 marks inside the back and dust covers. A cylinder escapement is cheap, a lever escapement better. Jewel count is on the movement. An enamel dial with hairline cracks is common.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Not running (needs a service at $150–300), cracked dials, broken crystals, worn hinges, broken cylinder escapements (often uneconomic to repair), and dents.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern Chinese "antique" mechanical watches with fake old dials; marriages (mismatched dial, case or movement).

## Market
Steady; NAWCC collectors, steampunk and jewellery buyers. eBay: 500+ per month. Days to sell: 14–45.

## What sellers usually miss
Longines/Omega signatures on the movement (not the dial); gold cases mistaken for silver; enamelled backs.

## Shipping / handling
Small padded box, insured, $6–10.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Swiss", silver grade (800 / 0.935 / coin silver), movement (cylinder, key wind, stem wind), maker if signed on the movement (Longines, Omega), case style (open face, hunter, enamel), diameter, "ladies fob", running or not. Junk: "antique" alone, "rare", "Patek-style". `Antique Swiss 800 Silver Cylinder Key Wind Ladies Fob Pocket Watch 32mm Running`
- **Where:** eBay 7-day auction for running and signed pieces (500+ sold/month, NAWCC and steampunk buyers); BIN with offers for non-runners/parts.
- **Price position:** non-running generic $30–80, running $60–150, Longines/Omega silver $100–250 (sold €106–110), at the sold median, OBO, accept −15%, floor is silver weight; cut 10% after 3 weeks (14–45 days to sell).
- **Photos:** dial straight-on; movement with back open (maker signature lives here); case marks inside the cover; case back/enamel; crystal and hands; video of it running and the balance swinging.
- **State:** runs and keeps time or not, movement type, maker signature on movement, case marks and silver grade, diameter, key included, dial cracks, enamel loss, dents by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** pocket; small padded box, insured, $6–10.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "This is an [800 silver] [cylinder] Swiss fob watch, c. [1890], [running], with the [Longines] signature on the movement; running silver examples sell for $[60–150], non-runners half that."
