## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Unglazed matte stoneware tinted (pale blue, dark "Portland" blue, sage green, lilac, black, crimson, primrose) with white applied classical reliefs (dancing hours, muses, Cupid, portraits). Marks impressed on the base.

## Makers, brands and variants
- Marks (impressed): "WEDGWOOD" alone = pre-1891; "WEDGWOOD / ENGLAND" 1891–1908ish; "MADE IN ENGLAND" after ~1908; three-letter date codes (1860–1929) and later two-digit year codes (1930s–2000s, e.g., "75" for 1975). Post-1998 "Wedgwood Made in Indonesia" pieces exist (lowest value).
- Imitators: Adams, Turner, Dudson (England), Schafer & Vater and German bisque (1900s), Japanese imitation (Occupied Japan).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
High-quality: crisp undercut reliefs, fine details, uniform colour; old "dip" jasper (coloured slip over white body) shows white at worn edges. Lower: thick reliefs, small dishes, 1970s–90s standard production.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips on reliefs, rim chips, staining, crazing on glazed interiors, restoration (fills, colour touch-ups).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake "Wedgwood" marks rare; many imitators unmarked. Portland vase copies by Wedgwood itself across eras.

## Market
High volume, low price for common pale blue; strong for rarities.

## What sellers usually miss
Pre-1891 "WEDGWOOD" only marks; tricolour and coloured jasper.

## Shipping / handling
Small plate ~0.5 lb, $6–10; wrap reliefs.

Give mark text, date code, colour, diameter, subject, condition.
