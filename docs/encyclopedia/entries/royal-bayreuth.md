## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Royal Bayreuth (Tettau, Bavaria, 1794–present; collectible 1900–1920s) made figural novelties (tomatoes, lobsters, shells, animals), Tapestry ware (textured cloth finish), Sunbonnet Babies, and scenic pieces. Marks: a blue crest with a lion and "Royal Bayreuth Bavaria" (often "Tettau 1794"). R.S. Prussia (Reinhold Schlegelmilch, Suhl, c.1890s–1914) is famous for heavily moulded bowls with satin-finish floral transfers, a dry-brushed gold edge, and named moulds (Iris, Carnation, Point & Clover, Medallion); marked with a red wreath and star. Many identical unmarked German bowls from the same era exist.

## Makers, brands and variants
- Royal Bayreuth: figurals, Tapestry, Rose Tapestry, Sunbonnet Babies, Nursery Rhymes.
- R.S. Prussia (red mark), R.S. Germany (green/blue mark, lower value), R.S. Tillowitz (Silesia), E.S. Germany (Erdmann Schlegelmilch), Carl Tielsch (C.T. Altwasser).
- Unmarked German/Austrian bowls (most common at estate sales).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Translucent porcelain. Better pieces have a satin pastel ground blending into colour, crisp moulding, and rich hand-touched transfers. Grape, rose and fruit decals on a white ground with dry-brushed gold edges are the most common, least valuable type.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rim flakes on scalloped edges, worn gold, hairlines, restored rims, and scratch marks from use.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake R.S. Prussia red marks are rampant (decals applied to old or new blanks). Royal Bayreuth figurals have been reproduced. Genuine RS Prussia marks are under the glaze with clean edges; fake decal marks sit on top.

## Market
Soft for generic German bowls, steady for top molds and figurals. eBay: hundreds per month. Days to sell: 30–90.

## What sellers usually miss
Unmarked Tapestry pieces; portrait medallion R.S. Prussia molds listed as "old bowl"; Royal Bayreuth small figurals (creamers) in mixed lots.

## Shipping / handling
An 11" bowl needs a 15–16" double box; $18–25.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Photograph the mark close up, the rim for flakes, and light-through for hairlines. Name the mold if known.

## Buy rule
Price ≤ $200; profit = median × 0.87 − ~$20 out − ~$20 in − price ≥ max($50, price). Generic bowls never qualify. Buy only authenticated-looking red-mark R.S. Prussia premium molds, Tapestry or rare figurals at ≤ ~30% of the median, with no damage.
