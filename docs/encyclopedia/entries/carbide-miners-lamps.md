# Carbide miner's lamps and mining lights
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold browse Oct 2025 (Justrite, Auto-Lite, Guy's Dropper); sweep listing 2318

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Small brass/steel two-chamber lamp (water on top, carbide below) with a round reflector and a cap hook. Makers stamped on the top: Justrite (most common), Auto-Lite, Guy's Dropper, Dewar, Baldwin (early, big reflector). Larger hand/mounted carbide lamps (Dewar hand lamp, Justrite "streamlined") are a step up. Value tells: all-brass, original reflector, unusual maker, striker intact. Electric Edison/Koehler cap lamps with battery packs are a separate low-value niche.

## What sellers usually miss
Maker; most sellers just say "miner's lamp". Justrite is the Zebco of the category.

## Shipping / handling
1 lb, $8–10. eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Baldwin/Guy's Dropper/Dewar ≤ $30, or a box lot with several brass lamps ≤ $10 each. Pass when: single Justrite over $25. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
