## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Projectors for 35mm filmstrips (rolls of still frames) used in schools 1930s-1990s. Boxy wrinkle-finish (brown, gray, tan) housings with a lamp chimney, a lens barrel, a filmstrip channel and supply reel arm on top or side, sometimes a slide carrier. DuKane sound versions include a record turntable.

## Makers, brands and variants
DuKane (St. Charles IL), Standard Projector & Equipment Co. (Standard 500 series), SVE (Society for Visual Education, Picturol), Viewlex, Bell & Howell, Graflex/GAF, Spencer Lens Co. (Deco).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Cast aluminum bodies with wrinkle finish; original cases add value; working lamps (DEK/CZX bulbs) are increasingly expensive.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dead lamps, frayed cords, seized fans, hazed lenses. Electrical testing required.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
None.
