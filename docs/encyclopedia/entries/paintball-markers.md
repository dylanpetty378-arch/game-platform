## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Air-powered paintball guns. Value tells: milled aluminium bodies with brand engraving (Eclipse, Dye, Shocker), electronic grip frames, "Sheridan", "Nelson", "PMI Piranha", 12-gram stock-class guns. Cheap: plastic Brass Eagle bodies, rental Tippmanns, 12-oz/20-oz CO2 tanks with expired hydro-test date (5-year rule).

## Makers, brands and variants
Tippmann (98, A-5, Cronus), Brass Eagle (Talon, Stingray, Raptor), Spyder/Kingman, Planet Eclipse, Dye, Smart Parts/Shocker, WGP Autococker, Sheridan/Nelson (vintage).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Anodized aluminium vs plastic; braided macro-line; brand on body.

## Condition, damage and repairs
O-ring leaks, broken bolts, corrosion from CO2, expired tanks.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Clones of Autococker/Spyder bodies; no significant fake market.
