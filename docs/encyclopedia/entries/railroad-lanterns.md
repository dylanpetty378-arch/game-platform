# Railroad lanterns (Dietz, Adlake, Handlan, Armspear)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: appraisily.com antique lanterns value guide; eBay listings Dietz Vesta / No. 39 / SP-marked 2025

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Wire-guard kerosene lantern with a bail handle. Tall-globe (pre-~1920, globe ~5.5"+, brass-top or bell-bottom) vs short-globe (1920s–60s, Dietz Vesta, Adlake Kero, Handlan). Value tells: railroad initials stamped on the lid/frame (S.P. Co, UP, PRR, B&O…), the same initials cast/etched in the globe, coloured globes (red, amber, blue, green), and bell-bottom or brass-top frames. Plain Dietz barn lanterns (Blizzard, Little Wizard, D-Lite) with no railroad marks are farm lanterns, not railroad.

## What sellers usually miss
Whether the globe is cast with the railroad name (doubles value), colour of the globe, and tall vs short. Sellers who write "SP railroad lantern" already know.

## Shipping / handling
3–4 lb, globe fragile – wrap globe separately, ~$18–25. eBay ~13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: railroad-marked lantern with cast or coloured globe ≤ $50, or tall-globe ≤ $80. Pass when: unmarked, repro, or seller names the railroad and asks $75+. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
