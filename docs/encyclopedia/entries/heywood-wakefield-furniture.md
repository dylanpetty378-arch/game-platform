# Heywood-Wakefield furniture
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: modcitymad collector's guide, antiquetrader, 1stdibs/eBay sold memory

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Three distinct eras from the Gardner, MA company: (1) **wicker/rattan** 1880s–1930s; (2) **Old Colony / Colonial** 1930s–40s maple with turned legs, "Heywood-Wakefield" burned stamp in a shield/eagle cartouche (the stamp in 8797's photo); (3) **Streamline / Modern** 1936–66 solid birch in Wheat or Champagne blonde lacquer with rounded "bullet"/dogbone forms – this is the money line. Modern-line stamp is a burned-in "dogbone" (bowtie) mark with model number like M151G. Seller words: "Heywood Wakefield", "blonde MCM", "Hey-Wake".

## What sellers usually miss
"Vintage Heywood Wakefield" covers all three eras; buyers pay for the blonde Modern line only. Painted/refinished Modern pieces lose 30–70%.

## Shipping / handling
Furniture – local pickup or freight. Chairs/nightstands can ship Greyhound/uShip $80–150. Resell locally (FB Marketplace, no fee) or eBay local pickup.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Modern-line (blonde birch, dogbone/bullet) piece ≤ $100 and fits the car. Pass when: Old Colony maple or wicker unless free – resale ceiling ~$150 and it's heavy.
