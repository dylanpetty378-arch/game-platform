## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Single-action reel with a spool on a frame, foot to mount on the rod, handle on the spool face (general knowledge, verify). Maker name and model are engraved or stamped on the face plate or back plate; Hardy reels say "Hardy Bros Alnwick, Made in England". Size is given as a number (e.g. Marquis #6, System 2 78) matching line weight.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Hardy** (Alnwick, England): Perfect, Marquis – $150–400.
- **Scientific Anglers System 2** (made by Hardy): $100–180.
- **Pflueger Medalist** (USA, common): $20–50.
- Orvis (CFO, Battenkill), Ross, Abel, Bogdan, Vom Hofe likely matter but are not researched yet (general knowledge, verify; Vom Hofe and Bogdan are top-tier vintage).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Hardy/System 2: machined or cast aluminium with quality click-pawl or disc drag, crisp engraving. Pflueger Medalist: stamped/cast aluminium, common. Check for maker engraving, smooth spin and drag (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dropped/bent frames or spools, worn pawls/springs, corrosion (saltwater), missing line guards, replaced handles. Ask for a spin video and photos of both plates (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Hardy has re-issued Perfect models; check vintage details (general knowledge, verify).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Maker, model, size, weight, left/right retrieve, drag type, condition, spare spools and pouch/box. Photograph both plates, the foot and engravings (general knowledge, verify).

## Buy rule
