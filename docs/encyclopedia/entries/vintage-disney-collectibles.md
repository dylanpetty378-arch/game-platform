## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Licensed Disney merchandise and park souvenirs. The copyright line is the key date tell:
- **"© Walt Disney Enterprises"** (1930s–1939) and **"© Walt Disney Productions"** (1939–1986) = vintage. "WDP" on small items.
- **"© Disney"** and **"The Walt Disney Company"** (1986–) = modern; mostly retail-or-less.
Photo tells for old pieces: pie-eyed Mickey or Minnie (black oval eyes with a wedge cut, 1928–1938), rubber or vinyl faces with painted features (1950s–60s), felt collars, Japan or Hong Kong origin stickers, stamped tin, celluloid, and 1955–1985 Disneyland logos (Sleeping Beauty Castle, Mickey head "Disneyland" in red circle as on the fan in our sweep).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **1930s**: Charlotte Clark and Knickerbocker plush Mickey/Minnie, Steiff Mickey, Ingersoll watches, Fun-E-Flex and Borgfeldt bisque and wood figures, Lionel handcar. $100–3,000+.
- **1950s–60s**: Gund hand puppets (vinyl/rubber head with cloth body; Gund Mfg. Co., NY, "J. Swedlin" on tag): $15–60. Knickerbocker plush, Marx windups and playsets, Hasbro, Dakin vinyl figures (1960s–70s, $15–50). Japanese bean-bag/vinyl-head dolls (1960s–70s, $10–35). Disneyland ticket books, maps, guide books and Mickey Mouse Club items.
- **1970s–80s**: Walt Disney World opening items (1971), America on Parade (1976), EPCOT (1982), Disneyland souvenir fans, pennants, plates and plastic souvenirs. Mostly $10–40; maps and posters can bring $200+.
- **1990s–2000s**: Disney Store plush, WDCC (Walt Disney Classics Collection, 1992–2012; $20–150, a few higher), Poliwoggs (Santa/holiday resin figures; sold through Disney Store and gift shops, 2000s), Applause porcelain dolls, Hawaiian/Aulani merch and fabric bags. Typically $10–50.
- **Handbags and totes**: Disney Store and park bags, Loungefly (2010s–, some limited ones $40–120), Harveys/Dooney & Bourke Disney (needs authentication: Dooney Disney bags $80–300).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Vintage vinyl/rubber heads harden and develop paint rubs; fabric bodies fade. Check tags: "Gund Mfg. Co.", "Walt Disney Productions", "Made in Japan".
- Composition and bisque (1930s) vs resin (1990s–2000s). Poliwoggs pieces are cast resin with a "hand-carved wood" look.
- Park souvenirs: printed "Disneyland" with "© Walt Disney Productions" = pre-1986.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Vinyl head splits and paint loss, faded felt, moth damage, cracked celluloid, rusted tin, missing parts (puppet bodies, figure accessories, fan cord or tassel), stained fabric bags, resin chips (Poliwoggs marionette strings and fingers).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- 1970s–90s reproductions of 1930s Mickey toys (Schmid music boxes, Mickey "vintage style" tin) and Chinese pie-eyed fakes.
- Disney Store "vintage style" re-releases (e.g., 2000s Mickey retro plush).
- Fake Dooney & Bourke Disney bags and fake Loungefly. Avoid without authentication.
- Fantasy items (unlicensed folk-art "Minnie" figures) sold as Disney.

## Market
Very large, very liquid at the low end. Vintage Disneyana has a dedicated collector base (Disneyana Fan Club, Van Eaton Galleries). Sell-through is high for authentic pre-1970 toys in good condition and for park ephemera; low for modern merch. Holidays lift Christmas items such as Santa Minnie.

## What sellers usually miss
- They don't read copyright lines. A "Walt Disney Productions" tag on a puppet, figure or souvenir means pre-1986.
- Early Disneyland paper (maps, ticket books, guidebooks, Mickey Mouse Club) is often thrown into "old Disney stuff" boxes.
- Conversely, sellers price modern Disney Store and Poliwoggs pieces as "antique" or "vintage".

## Shipping / handling
Most items are light and small: $6–12 USPS Ground Advantage. Resin figures (Poliwoggs, WDCC) need double boxing. Puppets and plush go in poly mailers. Fans and paper go in rigid mailers.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Walt Disney Productions" + character + maker ("Gund", "Dakin", "Knickerbocker", "Marx") + item + decade/park ("1970s Disneyland"); Junk: "old Disney stuff", "vintage" on post-1990 merch, "rare". Example: `1950s Walt Disney Productions Gund Mickey Mouse Hand Puppet Vinyl Head Tagged`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for pre-1986 toys and park paper (ticket books, maps); modern Disney Store/park merch (median $20) as lots or local; 1930s pieces and cels to Van Eaton/auction. Christmas items in Q4.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (modern $12–36; 1960s–80s souvenirs/Gund/Dakin $15–60; ticket books/maps/Mickey Mouse Club $40–300), accept −15%, floor p25; OBO; cut 10% after 3 weeks.
- **Photos:** first photo the item front in daylight; then the copyright line exactly, maker tag, marks, vinyl/paint wear, fabric stains, all parts/accessories, park and attraction shown on paper items, back.
- **State:** copyright line as printed, maker, approximate year, size, paint/vinyl/fabric condition, missing parts, park and era for souvenirs, repro check (modern © date), flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** light, $6–12 Ground Advantage; plush/puppets in poly mailers, paper and fans in rigid mailers, resin (WDCC/Poliwoggs) double-boxed.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Tagged '[Walt Disney Productions]' — that line stopped in 1986, so this [Gund puppet] is the [1950s] original, not the Disney Store retro version; [vinyl head unsplit, cloth body clean]."

## Buy rule
Price ≤ $200. Profit = sold median × 0.87 − shipping to buyer − shipping to Dylan − price. Pass unless profit ≥ max($50, price). Tier A: profit ≥ 2× price.
- Only pre-1970 licensed toys or pre-1986 park ephemera with a sold median ≥ $80, priced ≤ $25.
- Never modern bags, plush, or Poliwoggs/Disney Store resin at > $10.
- Designer Disney bags (Dooney & Bourke, Coach) need authentication: excluded.
- Everything fits a passenger seat.
