## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
600 box cameras (OneStep, Sun 600/660, Cool Cam, Impulse) are hinged flash-bar boxes in black/grey plastic — millions made 1981–2000s. SX-70: flat folding SLR with brown leather and chrome (or black/white plastic Model 2/3), very distinctive. SLR 680/690: folding with flash bar. Land pack-film cameras: bellows, rangefinder, metal body; 180/195 have manual Tominon lens.

## Makers, brands and variants
Polaroid Corporation (1948–2008), Impossible Project / Polaroid Originals refurbs (2010s), Fuji Instax (current, separate).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original box adds $10–20 on 600 cameras. SX-70 leather lifting, chrome pitting reduce value.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Untested: battery is in film pack (600/SX-70), so seller can't test without film; rollers dirty, shutter issues, folding mechanism issues on SX-70. Untested electronics — Dylan rule: pass.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Refurbished "Retro Polaroid" resellers; modern Polaroid Now. No fakes per se.
