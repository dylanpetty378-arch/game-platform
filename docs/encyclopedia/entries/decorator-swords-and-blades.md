# Decorator swords, "antique" machetes and tourist blades
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: agent knowledge, eBay sold categories Oct 2026

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Craigslist "antique cavalry sword / Indian sword / machete antique" is almost always a 1970s–2000s Indian or Pakistani decorator: **"MADE IN INDIA" stamped at the ricasso**, red/green velvet-covered scabbard, acid-etched scroll "engraving", wire-wrapped or dyed-leather grip, thin guard. Hand-forged "tribal" sickle blades with scratched runes are the same category.
Real military swords have: maker/arsenal stamps (Wilkinson, Ames, Solingen, Klingenthal, proof marks), steel/leather scabbards (never velvet), unit numbers on the guard, and a serial on Wilkinsons.

## What sellers usually miss
Nothing on the junk tier; on the real tier they miss the maker stamp. The velvet scabbard and "MADE IN INDIA" are instant passes.

## Shipping / handling
3–5 lb, long box ($25–40). eBay allows swords; Facebook Marketplace does not.

## Buy rule
Buy when: arsenal/maker stamp legible in photo and price ≤ 25 % of comps. Pass when: velvet scabbard, "Made in India/Pakistan", loose handle, "collectible" with no maker. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
