## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Paired shirt-cuff fasteners: face plus back mechanism (bullet/whale-back toggle, chain link, fixed back, snap). Vintage US makers stamped the back of the toggle. Value is mostly metal and maker.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Swank** (Attleboro, MA, 1897–2010s): huge volume; marks "SWANK" on the toggle; most $8–25. Exceptions: novelty/figural (cars, golf), Swank "Disney", jade/stone sets.
- **Anson**, **Hickok**, **Shields**, **Foster**, **Dante**, **Speidel** — similar mid-market; Anson gold-filled better.
- **Krementz** (gold overlay/14k): $25–80 base; 14k $200+.
- **Sterling designer**: Georg Jensen (Denmark), Hans Hansen, Danecraft, Mexican Taxco, Ed Wiener, modernists.
- **Luxury**: Tiffany, Cartier, Dunhill, Hermès, Van Cleef — need authentication.
- **Military/organizational**: Masonic, corporate, NASA/aerospace pieces — niche demand.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Stamp check: "14K", "18K", "STERLING", "925", "1/20 12K GF". Silver-tone with toggles stamped only "SWANK" = base metal (rhodium/nickel plated brass). Heft and stone quality (onyx, lapis, MOP) help.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Plating wear (brassing), broken toggles, chipped enamel, missing stones, mismatched pairs.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake Tiffany/Cartier common; fake gold marks rare but exist. Luxury brands need authentication — Dylan avoids.

## Market
Very high volume, mostly low prices; lots of 20–50 pairs sell to crafters/resellers at $1–3/pair.

## What sellers usually miss
Small "14K" or "STERLING" stamps on the toggle post; designer signatures on the face back.

## Shipping / handling
Pocket size, $5 envelope/small box.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Photograph stamps, dimensions, mechanism, any wear; say plated if not marked.

## Buy rule
Profit = median × 0.87 − ~$5 − ~$5 − price ≥ max($50, price). Base-metal pairs never qualify. Stamped 14k qualifies if price ≤ melt/2. Luxury brands need professional authentication → pass. Fits easily.
