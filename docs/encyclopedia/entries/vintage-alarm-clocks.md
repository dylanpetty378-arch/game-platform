## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Spring-wound (or synchronous-electric) bedside clocks with an alarm-set hand or small sub-dial. Westclox (Western Clock Co., La Salle/Peru IL, 1885–2001) produced the Big Ben (1910–), Baby Ben, and dozens of cheap models (Bachelor, Dawn, Moonbeam, Spur, Countess, Drowsy). Glowing numerals: radium paint before ~1950s (risk), later promethium/tritium/phosphor.
Photo tells: the dial name, back-plate model names and patent dates, case material (steel enamel, brass, Bakelite, wood), feet style, keys and alarm-set knobs.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Westclox**: Big Ben "Style 1–7" (1910–1950s) and Baby Ben, both datable from back and dial; plain models in ivory/white enamel, cream, brown; 1950s–60s square/streamline cases. Back usually reads "Westclox La Salle Ill" / "Made in USA" and model name.
- **Ingraham, Gilbert, New Haven, Sessions, Seth Thomas**: American wind-ups.
- **Telechron / GE / Hammond**: electric; Bakelite Art Deco models sell well when working.
- **Lux / Keebler / Mastercrafters**: animated "blinking eye" and novelty clocks.
- **Character clocks**: Ingersoll Mickey Mouse (1930s), Hopalong Cassidy, Roy Rogers — high value, much faked.
- **German twin-bell** (Junghans, Kienzle) and modern repro "twin bell".

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Old = thicker steel, crisp printed or painted dial, glass crystal (not plastic), brass or nickel bezel. Westclox ivory enamel on steel chips at edges. Bakelite cases (Telechron) are heavier, brown/black, no mould flash. Original keys add value.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Not running/needs service (very common), overwound spring, rust on case, crazed or yellowed plastic crystals, flaking luminous paint, re-dialled faces, missing alarm-set knob. Radium dials: luminous paint dull/brown and still radioactive — do not grind or open; disclose.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern Chinese "retro" twin-bell alarms; reproduction Mickey Mouse/character clocks, especially Bayard and Ingersoll; replacement printed dials.

## Market
High volume, slow for common models, quick for animated/character clocks. Decorator buyers pay for colour and deco shapes.

## What sellers usually miss
Back-plate model and style numbers; animated movement (blinking eyes, rocking figures) not shown in photo; Telechron working condition.

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb; box with crystal protected, $10–14. Let spring run down before shipping.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Video of it running and alarm ringing, keep time over 24h, model and date from back, luminous paint disclosure ("possible radium — sealed, do not open").
