## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
19" rack units and stage boxes.
- **dbx graphic EQs**: 231/2231 (2×31 band) $100–180; 215/1215 (2×15 band) $70–130; 131 $50–80.
- **Radial passive DIs**: ProDI $50–70, ProD2 stereo $90–130, J48/JDI $120–180.
- **Rapco/Whirlwind DIs**: $15–30.
- **Shure 520DX Green Bullet** harmonica mic: $70–100. The vintage 520 with a crystal element is $100–200.
- **Numark DMC2 / old controllers**: $20–40.
- **TASCAM DP-03SD Portastudio**: $120–180.
- **Arturia BeatStep Pro**: $150–200.

The model number is printed on the front panel. Radial boxes are heavy steel "I-beam" enclosures with the Radial logo. Behringer copies the dbx front-panel layout, so read the logo.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **dbx**: 2x31/2231/231 > 2x15/2215/215 > 131 for EQs. The 160A compressor is collectible ($150–300) and the 160VU (vintage) is top tier ($500+).
- **Radial Engineering (Canada)**: the J48 (active) and JDI (Jensen transformer) are the premium DIs ($120–180). ProD2 > ProDI.
- **Shure**: 520DX current Green Bullet. Older 520 with a crystal element, sought by harp players ($100–200).
- **TASCAM, Numark, Arturia**: user gear with a steady used market.
- **Top**: API/Neve modules and the Urei/Universal Audio 1176 ($500–3,000+). Reissues from UA and clones exist, so verify the version.
- Junk: Behringer EQs, Rapco DIs, early USB DJ controllers.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Pro gear has steel chassis, rack ears, quality pots and sliders, and Neutrik XLRs.
- Behringer and clones look similar but are lighter, with a plastic-feeling knobs/fader feel (general knowledge, verify).
- On vintage units (1176, dbx 160VU, Neve), check for original transformers and faceplates. Recaps and mods are common and change value (general knowledge, verify).
- Shure crystal elements fail with age and humidity. A dead crystal element drops the 520 to parts value (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Must be power-tested. Scratchy pots, dead sliders, bent rack ears and missing power supplies (DP-03SD, controllers) are common. Smoke or nicotine film is common on bar/PA gear. Ask for a video of the unit powered and passing audio. Ask whether the power cord or adapter is included.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Shure mics exist, mostly SM58s; Green Bullet fakes are less common (general knowledge, verify). Urei 1176 has UA reissues and many DIY clones, and Neve/API modules have clones. Check the serial and internals on anything over $300.

## Market
Liquid, with steady demand from bands, churches, home studios and harp players. Reverb is the reference price and sellers know it. Items sell in days to a few weeks at fair price (general knowledge, verify). No strong seasonality. Margin only appears in estate lots or from non-musician sellers.

## What sellers usually miss
Model numbers. But pro-audio sellers nearly always know and price at Reverb asking. Margin only appears in estate lots.

## Shipping / handling
Rack units 5–10 lb, $20–30; DIs 2 lb, $10. Reverb 5–8% fees, eBay 13%. Must power-test.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Title: brand + model + type, e.g. "dbx 231 Dual 31-Band Graphic EQ Rack Tested". State that it was tested working and how, all sliders and pots OK, what is included (power, case, cables) and cosmetic wear. Photograph the front, back panel and serial, and the unit powered on.
