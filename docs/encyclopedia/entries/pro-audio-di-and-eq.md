## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
19" rack units and stage boxes. dbx graphic EQs: 231/2231 (2×31 band) $100–180, 215/1215 (2×15 band) $70–130, 131 $50–80. Radial passive DIs: ProDI $50–70, ProD2 stereo $90–130, J48/JDI $120–180. Rapco/Whirlwind DIs $15–30. Shure 520DX Green Bullet harmonica mic $70–100 (vintage 520 with crystal element $100–200). Numark DMC2/old controllers $20–40. TASCAM DP-03SD Portastudio $120–180. Arturia BeatStep Pro $150–200.

## What sellers usually miss
Model numbers; but pro-audio sellers nearly always know and price at Reverb-asking. Margin only appears in estate lots.

## Shipping / handling
Rack units 5–10 lb, $20–30; DIs 2 lb, $10. Reverb 5–8% fees, eBay 13%. Must power-test.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Radial/dbx units at ≤ 35% of Reverb sold median in a lot. Pass when: single items at > 60% of sold median (all of today's). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
