## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Canvas or cotton duck bag with two handles. Value tells: L.L.Bean Boat and Tote with contrast bottom/handles and sewn-in "L.L.Bean Freeport Maine" label (older without size code); USPS letter-carrier satchels with leather straps and "U.S. Mail" stamping; screen-printed graphics from known events, bands or brands.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **L.L.Bean Boat and Tote** (since 1944). Old labels: "L.L.Bean Freeport, Maine" with no RN/size; colours like Kelly green/navy.
- **USPS carrier satchels** (leather, then canvas/vinyl) — postal collectors.
- **NALC / union convention totes** — low value, niche.
- **Corporate/conference giveaways** (1990s) — near nil.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Heavy 24-oz duck canvas, double-stitched, leather/vinyl trim = quality; thin cotton canvas with screen print = promo.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Stains, yellowing, fraying handles, print cracking, mildew odour.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
L.L.Bean reissues (current production) look similar; label details date them.

## Shipping / handling

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Label close-up, measurements, stains, print condition.

## Buy rule
