## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Boxed games with board, pieces, rules. Value drivers you can see in photos: pop-culture licences (TV shows, monsters, space, Beatles, Batman), period box art (1950s–60s illustrated), unpunched cardboard or shrinkwrap, intact box corners. Small "travel" magnetic games (1970s–90s Hong Kong/Taiwan) and standard titles (Monopoly, Scrabble, Clue, Sorry, Parcheesi, Chinese checkers) are mass-produced and cheap.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Milton Bradley** (Springfield MA, 1860–1984 then Hasbro): logo "MB" in a circle/oval; 1960s–70s licensed TV games are the sweet spot.
- **Parker Brothers** (Salem MA, 1883–): Monopoly (1935+), Clue (1949 US), Risk (1959); early wood-box Monopoly and 1935–36 white-box editions are valuable.
- **Selchow & Righter** (Scrabble, Parcheesi); **Ideal** (Mouse Trap 1963, Operation-style); **Transogram**, **Hasbro**, **Whitman**, **Lowell**, **Hassenfeld Bros** (1960s TV games, valuable).
- **Cadaco** (Chicago): published the first US **Mastermind** (1972, "a great Cadaco game", photo cover of a seated man and standing woman). **Invicta** (UK, the originator, 1970/71) and **Pressman** (US from late 1970s). Mastermind is very common; the 1972 Cadaco edition is the only one with modest collector interest.
- **3M Bookshelf series** (1962–1970s): Acquire, Twixt, Feudal, Oh-Wah-Ree in book-shaped boxes; $20–60.
- **Avalon Hill** (Baltimore 1958–1998) and **SPI** wargames; early "flat box" Avalon Hill titles (Tactics II, Gettysburg 1958) are collectible; unpunched counters are worth more.
- **McLoughlin Bros** (NYC, 19th c), **Bliss**, **Chaffee & Selchow**: lithographed paper-on-wood games, $100–1,000+.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Unpunched cardboard counters/cards = unplayed (premium). Original shrinkwrap = sealed (verify, re-shrinkwrapping is common).
- Old games: linen-hinged boards, wooden pieces, paper-litho box labels, prices printed in cents.
- Mass-market reprints: "Classic"/"Nostalgia" retro editions of Clue, Monopoly, Mastermind (2000s, Winning Moves) look old but are cheap.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Missing pieces (most common), split box corners (taped), water damage, mildew smell, writing on boxes, faded box lids. Completeness matters more than box condition for players; collectors want both.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Retro reissues (Winning Moves, Target "vintage edition") of Monopoly, Clue, Operation, Mastermind; replica early Monopoly sets; re-shrinkwrapped "sealed" games. Check copyright dates and manufacturer addresses.

## Market
Large and slow for common titles; supply far exceeds demand. Licensed 1960s–70s games and wargames sell in 1–4 weeks. Boxes are bulky (shipping $15–25), which kills margins on cheap games.

## What sellers usually miss
- They price all "vintage" games the same; the valuable ones are licences (TV, monsters, space), early wargames and pre-1940 litho games.
- Replacement-part buyers: complete sets of original pieces of a sought game (Mouse Trap, Stratego) sell even when the box is bad.

## Shipping / handling
Standard games 2–4 lb in 20" boxes: $15–25 ground. Small travel games ship $5–8 but are worth little. Pack box corners to avoid crushing.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** game title + publisher + year + "board game" + "complete" + licence/series (TV, 3M Bookshelf, Avalon Hill) + "unpunched"/"sealed" if so. Junk: "vintage games lot", "family night", "retro". Example: `Vintage 1965 Milton Bradley Mystery Date Board Game Complete Original Box`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for licensed 1960s–70s, wargames and pre-1940 litho (1–4 weeks); common titles (Monopoly, Mastermind) in a FB local lot, since $15–25 shipping exceeds them; complete piece sets of sought games sell as parts.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median (common $5–20, Mastermind ~$9, complete licensed/3M $20–60, sealed/early Avalon Hill $40–150); accept −15%, floor −25%; OBO; cut 10% after 30 days.
- **Photos:** 1) box lid straight-on; then box corners and edges, board open, all pieces laid out against the rules inventory, counters punched/unpunched, rules sheet, any tears or water stains.
- **State:** year from box/board copyright, publisher, completeness inventory against the rules list (missing pieces named), punched/unpunched, box condition (corners, tears, split), smoke/mildew status, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 2–4 lb in a 20" box; pad the box corners, single outer box, 3–5 lb class, $15–25; travel games $5–8.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[1965 Mystery Date / 1958 flat-box Avalon Hill Gettysburg], complete against the rules inventory, [unpunched] — the valuable vintage games are the licences and early wargames, not Monopoly; complete examples sell for [$X]."

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − ~$18 shipping to buyer − ~$15 to Dylan − price ≥ max($50, price). Shipping both ways (~$33) means only games selling ≥ $100 work shipped. Buy only sealed or complete licensed 1955–85 games with a confirmed ≥ $100 comp, pre-1940 litho games, or early Avalon Hill/SPI unpunched, at ≤ $20. Pass on everything else. All fit the passenger seat.
