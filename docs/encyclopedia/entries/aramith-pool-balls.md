## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Belgian phenolic balls; the box or the cue ball says "Aramith". Lines by value: Crown Standard (budget, ~$110 new), Premium (~$220), Super Pro / Tournament ($300+), Black (~$400). Fakes: generic "Vigma/Iszy" sets with the same colours sell for $40 new – look for the Aramith logo on the cue ball and the moulded box. Used Premium sets sell $100–150 if unchipped; a yellowed/chipped set $40–60. Standard set = 16 balls, 2 1/4" (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Aramith (Saluc, Belgium)** – Crown Standard (budget), Premium, Super Pro / Super Pro Cup, Tournament (incl. TV set with alternate colours), Black. Measles cue balls and practice sets are add-ons (general knowledge, verify).
- **Generic imports** (Vigma, Iszy etc.) – same colours, $40 new.
- **Vintage clay/composition and ivory sets (pre-1930)** – niche collectible, $300+; ivory has legal sale restrictions in many US states (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Aramith phenolic resin: very hard, high gloss, consistent weight and colour, logo on the cue ball. Cheap polyester/resin sets yellow, chip, and vary in weight. Super Pro/Tournament have different numbering/colour schemes and higher-grade resin (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips, yellowing, and dull/pitted surfaces knock a Premium set down to $40–60. Missing balls (especially the cue ball or 8 ball) kill set value. Polishing restores gloss but not chips (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Generic sets in the same colours sold as "Aramith" – check for the logo on the cue ball and the Aramith moulded box.

## Market
Steady demand from home pool-table owners and league players; sells on eBay and locally, especially alongside table sales (general knowledge, verify). Not seasonal in a known way.

## What sellers usually miss
The line (Premium vs Super Pro). Sellers who say "Aramith" already know; the arbitrage is a photo of a Super Pro set in a listing titled "pool balls".

## Shipping / handling
~6 lb, $15–20. eBay (13%).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State line (Premium/Super Pro/Tournament/Black), complete 16-ball count, chips and yellowing, and whether the original box is included. Photograph the full rack, the cue ball logo, and any chips close-up. Keywords: "Aramith" + line + "pool balls" + "billiard ball set" + "2 1/4".

## Buy rule
Buy when: Super Pro/Tournament set under $60. Premium under $35 was the earlier threshold, but by Dylan's rule a ~$120 Premium sale nets only ~$35 profit at a $35 cost (fees ~$16, ~$36 shipping both ways) – so Premium only at ≤ ~$20. Pass when: Premium at $75 (sold ~$120 → profit <$50). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Must fit a passenger seat (it does).
