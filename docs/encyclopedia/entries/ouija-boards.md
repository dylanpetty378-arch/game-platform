## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
A flat rectangular board, ~15 × 22 in (Fuld standard; also a 12 × 18 "junior"), with the alphabet in two arcs, numbers 0-9 below, YES/NO in the top corners with a sun and moon, GOOD BYE at the bottom, and a heart-shaped planchette (pointer) on three legs with a window or a pointed tip. Fuld boards are wood (pine with a printed/stencilled face, later three-ply veneer, then Masonite from the 1940s) with the maker's name, address and patent/trademark text printed in the bottom margin or the centre; Parker Brothers (1966-) boards are printed cardboard-faced Masonite, then pure cardboard, then "glow in the dark". Planchettes: early Fuld = wood with a round glass window and brass/nickel legs; 1920s-60s = wood or Bakelite-brown plastic with a glass or plastic window; Parker = cream or clear plastic with a plastic magnifier window; some late-1960s Parker planchettes still show "Wm. Fuld" moulded. The box: Fuld boxes are plain lithographed cardboard with the "WM. FULD TALKING BOARD SET" label and a Baltimore address; Parker boxes say "Parker Brothers, Salem, Mass." (1967-1985) or "Beverly, Mass." (1986-1991) or "Hasbro/Pawtucket". Title words: "antique Ouija", "vintage Ouija board", "Wm Fuld", "1920 Ouija", "Mystifying Oracle", "Parker Brothers 1972". Look-alikes: 1990s-2020s reproductions ("Ouija" licensed Hasbro wood boards, Winning Moves "Classic Ouija" 2016+, Etsy laser-cut boards, Spirit Halloween "antique" boards), novelty boards (Hasbro pink "Ouija for girls" 2008, Stranger Things, Mystic Skull).

## Makers, brands and variants
**Kennard Novelty Company (Baltimore, 1890-1892) and Ouija Novelty Company (1892-c.1898).** The first boards: Bond patent 10 Feb 1891 ("Pat. Feb. 10, 1891" printed); the name was coined 1890 (Wikipedia). Boards are solid wood, hand-stencilled, with "Ouija Novelty Co." or "Kennard Novelty Co., Baltimore, Md." and the patent date; planchette a plain wood heart with a pin pointer. Value $800-2,500+ (estimate; very rare).

**William Fuld (Baltimore, 1898-1966).** Took over production 1898-1901, with the 1891 patent and later his own: "Pat. Sept. 19, 1920" (a planchette patent) and design patents; the board says "OUIJA — WM. FULD — BALTIMORE, MD. U.S.A." and later "Wm. Fuld Talking Board Set". Dating by address printed on the board/box: no address or "Baltimore" only (c.1898-1911); 1226-1306 N. Central Avenue / Lamont Street (c.1911-1919; "factory label c.1911-1917" per williamfuld.com); 1508 Harford Avenue (c.1920-1966, the landmark factory). Face variants: the classic stencilled "Ouija" with the mystical sun/moon faces; "Mystifying Oracle" (Fuld's second brand, 1900s-1950s, black-and-white "ORACLE" lettering, sold as a separate board — $80-250); junior-size boards; boards made for Copp Clark (Toronto), W.S. Reed, Northwestern Toy, International Novelty and American Toy under licence (maker name printed — $150-500). Fuld boards with the Masonite face and a plastic planchette (1940s-1966) are the "common vintage" board: $60-150 bare, $100-200 boxed, estimate. Pre-1920 solid-wood Fuld boards with the glass-window planchette: $300-800.

**Isaac Fuld & Brother "Oriole" talking board (Baltimore, 1901-1920s).** William's brother, after the family feud; identical board with "ORIOLE" in place of "OUIJA" (the Oriole stencil was a Ouija stencil with the trademark cut out — williamfuld.com). Injunction 1920 ended production. $400-1,200 (estimate; scarce).

**Parker Brothers (1966-1991: Salem MA, then Beverly MA; made at the Bay Shore, NY? — box says Salem) and Hasbro (1991-).** Bought the rights from the Fuld heirs in 1966; the 1967-1972 Parker boards keep the "Wm. Fuld" text and Fuld face art on a Masonite board with a plastic planchette ("Parker Brothers, Salem, Mass." box) — $25-50 boxed; 1972-1990s cardboard boards $15-30; 1998 glow-in-the-dark $10-25; 2010s Hasbro and Winning Moves wood-look $10-20. The Parker board outnumbers everything else 50:1 on eBay.

**Competitors and scarce makers (the golden eggs).** American Novelty Company (Baltimore, 1920s-40s) "Ouija Queen" — a board with a crowned female figure and "OUIJA QUEEN" lettering, often a lithographed paper face on board; $150-400 boxed (a $200 asking copy seen in sweeps). Haskelite Manufacturing (Chicago, 1920s-30s) "Haskelite Mystic Board"/"Ouija" licensed boards on plywood with a Haskelite stamp on the back; $100-300. Mystic Hand (Shell/Shel-Ray/Lee Industries, 1940s) — a board with a pointing-hand planchette; $100-300. Swami (Gift Craft, 1940s), "Wonderful Talking Board" (Baltimore Talking Board Co., 1920s), "Volo" (Kennard's rival board, 1890s) and "Espirito" (Bowie/Fuld, 1890s) — $200-1,500, the Volo/Espirito at the top. Hasko (Haskelite) "Mystic Tray" boards: $60-150. Regional 1920s boards (Milton Bradley "Spirit Board", Rajah, Wizard) $60-250. All estimates.

### Quick ID checklist
1. Maker and city printed at the bottom or centre of the face: "Wm. Fuld Baltimore" (good), "Parker Brothers Salem/Beverly" (common), anything else (research it).
2. Address/patent text: "Pat. Feb. 10, 1891" only = pre-1920; "Pat. Sept. 19, 1920" or "Pat. 2 - 1 - 21" style = 1920s-60s; "© 1972 Parker Brothers" = 1972-91.
3. Material: solid wood with visible grain through the stencil = pre-1920; three-ply veneer = 1920s-30s; Masonite (smooth brown, dark edge) = 1940s-72; cardboard = 1972+.
4. Planchette: wood with glass window and metal legs = early Fuld; brown Bakelite-style = 1930s-50s; cream/clear plastic = Parker; a pointing hand or odd shape = competitor board.
5. Board size: 15 × 22 standard; smaller "junior" boards and odd sizes point to competitors.
6. Back of the board: Fuld stencils/stamps, Haskelite stamp, retailer labels (Copp Clark) date and identify.
7. Box present and its address.

### Golden-egg tells
- "Kennard Novelty Co." or "Ouija Novelty Co., Baltimore" with "Pat. Feb. 10, 1891" on a solid pine board — $800-2,500+ vs $20-40 for a Parker board — hand-stencilled letters that are slightly uneven, grain showing through, plain wood heart planchette with a pin.
- "ORIOLE" talking board (Isaac Fuld & Brother) — $400-1,200 vs $60-150 for a Fuld Masonite board — identical layout to a Fuld but "ORIOLE" where "OUIJA" sits, "Isaac Fuld & Bro." text.
- Wm. Fuld with no Harford Avenue address (Baltimore only, or N. Central Ave/Lamont St, c.1898-1919) and a glass-window wood planchette — $300-800 vs $60-150 for a 1508 Harford Ave Masonite board — read the address line; solid-wood face; brass-legged planchette.
- Licensee boards marked "Copp Clark Co., Toronto", "W.S. Reed Toy Co.", "Northwestern Toy & Mfg.", "International Novelty Co." or "American Toy Co." — $150-500 — the maker name replaces or sits beside Fuld's in the bottom text.
- "OUIJA QUEEN" (American Novelty Co., Baltimore) with the crowned woman — $150-400 vs $20-40 Parker — distinctive female figure/crown artwork; lithographed paper face.
- Haskelite-stamped plywood boards ("Haskelite Mfg. Corp., Chicago" on the back) and "Mystic Hand" boards with a pointing-hand planchette — $100-300 — look at the back for the Haskelite diamond stamp; hand-shaped pointer.
- "Mystifying Oracle" (Fuld) in its box — $100-300 vs $60-150 for the same-era Ouija — black "ORACLE" banner with a mystic face at the centre.
- Original early Fuld glass-window planchette alone (wood, glass lens, three brass legs with felt/ivory tips) — $40-100 by itself — the glass lens is round and thick; Parker lenses are flat plastic.
- 1967-72 Parker Brothers board still printed "Wm. Fuld" in the box marked "Salem, Mass." — $30-60 boxed vs $15-30 for the 1972+ copyright board — "Wm. Fuld" text present but Parker box; Masonite board.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Early boards: solid wood (pine/poplar), hand-stencilled or stamped with ink, varnished; the varnish crazes and yellows, stencil edges are soft and slightly uneven; the back is unfinished wood with saw marks. 1920s-30s: three-ply with a lithographed paper face or stencil on veneer; edges show the plies. 1940s-66: Masonite with a screen-printed face; the back is the rough Masonite texture; edge dark brown. Parker: cardboard-faced Masonite (1967-72), then printed chipboard with a laminated face (feels like a game board; corners delaminate). Reproductions (2010s wood boards): laser-crisp lettering, modern plywood, uniform "antiqued" stain, often a "Made in China" label or a Hasbro © line; Etsy boards have maker's own text. Planchette quality: glass lens + brass legs (early), Bakelite/urea plastic with glass (1930s-50s), injection-moulded plastic (1960s+). Old vs new: period wear is on the planchette's felt feet and the board's surface in a track pattern across the letters, corner bumps on the box and sun-fading on the face; fake age is uniform brown staining.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Common: warped Masonite, water stains, face scratches from the planchette, chipped/worn edges, box lid split, missing planchette (a Fuld board without its planchette loses ~30-40%; a Parker board without is almost worthless), mismatched planchettes (Parker plastic on a Fuld board — ask), paint/varnish crazing (acceptable on early boards), insect damage to pine, mould on cardboard, replaced planchette legs/felt, "cleaned" boards that lose varnish. Fatal: cracked/split wood boards, heavy water damage, delaminated plywood faces, repainted lettering. Ask: is the planchette original to the board (window material, leg metal), measurements, back-of-board photo, box present, any warp.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Hasbro licensed "Classic Ouija" boards in wood (2016+, Winning Moves) and countless "antique style" Etsy/Amazon/Spirit Halloween boards; fakes of early Fuld boards exist (printed on new pine and distressed). Tells: modern print sharpness, perfectly even stain, new-wood smell/lack of oxidation on the back, Phillips screws or modern plastic feet on the planchette, barcode or © lines, and faces copied from the 1972 Parker art rather than Fuld's. Reissues of the 1972 Parker board (Hasbro 1990s-2020s) are near-identical to the 1972-91 boards — the copyright line tells the year, and none are valuable. Mystifying Oracle reproduced by Hasbro 2010s (laser-crisp).

## Market
Parker boards: hundreds sell monthly on eBay at $15-40, sell-through high but margin nil. Fuld boards: dozens per month, $60-200, 1-3 weeks; buyers are Halloween decorators (September-October spike, 1.5-2× prices), occult/spiritualism collectors, game collectors and Baltimore-history buyers. Scarce makers sell in days to collectors (Talking Board Historical Society community) and do best in September-October eBay auctions or at Hake's/Morphy's for the 1890s boards. Trend: steady with annual October spike; horror-film tie-ins (2014-16) lifted interest, which has held.

## What sellers usually miss
The address line and patent dates (they say "antique Ouija board" for a 1972 Parker, and "Parker Brothers Ouija" for a 1967 Fuld-art board), the maker name when it is not Fuld/Parker (they read "Ouija" and stop), the planchette's window material and originality, and the back of the board (Haskelite stamp, factory labels). They also miss that a lone early glass-window planchette has value. Conversely they overprice 1972 Parker boards as "antique".

## Shipping / handling
15 × 22 board: 2-4 lb (Masonite) to 5 lb (solid wood) boxed flat with corner protection and the planchette wrapped separately; USPS Priority/UPS Ground $15-25. Fragility medium (corners, warping if damp). Fits a passenger seat. Never ship the planchette loose on the board (scratches the face).

## Buy rule
Buy when: a wood/plywood/Masonite board shows a Baltimore Fuld address other than 1508 Harford (pre-1920), a licensee or competitor maker name, or a Mystifying Oracle/Ouija Queen/Haskelite/Mystic Hand face, with original planchette, uncracked, at ≤ $60-80 (resale $150-400+), or a Harford Ave Fuld with box and original planchette at ≤ $30. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after 13% fees and ~$20 shipping each way. Pass when: Parker Brothers/Hasbro of any year above $15, missing or mismatched planchette on a common board, warped/water-damaged/repainted boards, modern reproductions, and 1890s boards whose authenticity would need expert review at prices above $200. Must fit a passenger seat (all do).
