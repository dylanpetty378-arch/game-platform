## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- **Maiolica (tin-glazed earthenware):** opaque white tin glaze with hand-painted cobalt, yellow, green, ochre; Deruta Ricco and Raffaellesco patterns (dragons, grotesques); signed with town + painter initials on base.
- **MCM Italian art pottery (1950s–70s):** Bitossi Rimini Blu (stamped geometric patterns, bright cobalt/turquoise), Fratelli Fanciullacci and Fantoni (thick lava glazes, abstract figures), Raymor imports with paper labels and incised numbers ("Italy 453").
- **Vietri:** naive bright animals, Amalfi coast.
- **Modern import decor:** printed (decal/transfer) designs, perfectly uniform; vintage-label trays (e.g., "Peeled Tomatoes") are decorative reproductions.
- **Portuguese faience (Coimbra, Alcobaça):** similar look to Italian majolica — sponged blue, raised leaves/grapes, sacred-heart and religious motifs; marks "Portugal", "Coimbra", "Pintado à mão".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Deruta (Umbria):** dozens of workshops (Grazia, Sberna, Maioliche Artistiche); marks "Deruta Italy" + painter initials; modern pieces $20–100, older quality $100–400.
- **Bitossi / Aldo Londi (Montelupo):** Rimini Blu, Seta, Cat figurines; marks incised numbers, "Italy", Raymor labels. $60–500.
- **Fratelli Fanciullacci, Fantoni, Guido Gambone (signed "Gambone Italy" with donkey):** $150–3,000.
- **Capodimonte-type:** flower-encrusted porcelain (see capodimonte-porcelain entry).
- **Taormina-style modern import trays:** retail housewares (Pier 1, Home Goods). $5–15.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Hand painting shows brushstrokes, slight drips, unglazed foot with pink/red earthenware body; decal printing shows dots under loupe.
- MCM pieces: heavy, thick glazes, often incised numbers, crazing.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Glaze chips (tin glaze chips easily at rims), flakes on high points, hairlines, restored rims.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Bitossi still produces Rimini Blu; reissues are lighter and cleaner; Raymor label suggests period.
- Fake Gambone signatures exist.
