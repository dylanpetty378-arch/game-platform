# Breast drills and hand drills (Millers Falls, Goodell-Pratt, Stanley, Yankee)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: knowledge of eBay sold ranges for Millers Falls No. 120B/97/12 and Goodell-Pratt; no dated comp pulled

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hand-cranked drill with a curved breast plate at the top (breast drill) or a wooden knob handle (egg-beater hand drill). Millers Falls (Massachusetts) is the collectible name: red/black paint, two-speed gear, "No. 120B" or "No. 97" cast in the frame, wooden crank handles. Goodell-Pratt, North Bros "Yankee", Stanley also collected. Sellers write "antique hand drill" or "vintage chest drill".

## What sellers usually miss
The maker and model number cast in the frame; "egg-beater" drill collectors pay for original paint and the correct wooden handle.

## Shipping / handling
