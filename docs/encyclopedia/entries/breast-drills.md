## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hand-cranked drill with a curved breast plate at the top (breast drill) or a wooden knob handle (egg-beater hand drill). Millers Falls (Massachusetts) is the collectible name: red/black paint, two-speed gear, "No. 120B" or "No. 97" cast in the frame, wooden crank handles. Goodell-Pratt, North Bros "Yankee", Stanley also collected. Sellers write "antique hand drill" or "vintage chest drill". Look-alikes: braces (U-shaped crank, no gears) – a different type (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Millers Falls** – No. 120B, No. 97, No. 12 breast drills; No. 2 and No. 5 egg-beater drills (original finish collectible).
- **Goodell-Pratt** – 2-speed drills collectible.
- **North Bros "Yankee"** (later Stanley) – 1530 and other models; boxed examples collectible.
- **Stanley** – 965 and others, decent tier.
- **19th-century patent drills** – top tier.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Cast-iron frames, cut steel gears, japanned or painted finish (red/black on Millers Falls), hardwood (often rosewood/cocobolo) handles, three-jaw chucks. Original paint and the correct wooden handle are what egg-beater collectors pay for. Two-speed gearing and smooth action are quality tells (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rust, missing/replaced handles, broken gear teeth, missing chuck jaws or springs, seized mechanism. Repainting lowers collector value but may help user sales (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
None known. Later reissues/budget versions of the same model numbers exist (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Woodworkers (hand-tool users) and tool collectors; steady eBay demand, moderate prices (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
The maker and model number cast in the frame; "egg-beater" drill collectors pay for original paint and the correct wooden handle.

## Shipping / handling
3–6 lb, $12–18. eBay (13%).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State maker, model number, speeds, chuck type and jaw condition, gear action, paint percentage, handle material/originality. Photograph casting marks, gears, chuck, and handles. Keywords: maker + model + "breast drill"/"hand drill" + "two speed" + "vintage" + "woodworking".
