# Disc music boxes (Thorens AD30, Regina, Symphonion, Polyphon, Reuge/Porter)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · upgraded 2026-10-13 · Sources: Harry Rinker col. 1371 (Thorens AD30 2013 sold prices), 1stdibs/chairish listings, collectorsweekly, Terapeak (Thorens AD30, Oct 2026)
Slug: disc-music-boxes · Aliases: music box with discs, interchangeable discs, vintage music box, Thorens music box, Sorrento music box, Regina music box
Summary: Sankyo cylinder boxes $10–30; Thorens AD30 with 5–10 discs $150–250 (Terapeak median $130); AD30 with 15–30 discs $250–400; Porter/Reuge 10" $800–2,500; antique Regina tabletop $1,500–4,000.
Buy rule (short): Thorens AD30 + 15+ discs ≤ $75–100 (playing); 10"+ disc box with discs ≤ $200; pass broken teeth / no discs / "needs work".

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Box with a steel disc ~4.5" (Thorens AD30, Swiss 1940s–70s) or 11"–27" (antique Regina/Symphonion/Polyphon, 1890s–1910s) sitting on a comb movement. Thorens: small inlaid/marquetry box (often Sorrento "Made in Italy" inlay cases, or Thorens-branded walnut), a stack of 4.5" black discs with a yellow Thorens label, hand crank underneath. Antique Regina: large oak/mahogany case, big punched discs, serial plate. Seller words: "music box with discs", "interchangeable discs", "vintage music box".
Look-alikes: Sankyo/Reuge cylinder movements (pinned cylinder, no disc) in the same Sorrento boxes are the cheap tier.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Thorens AD30** (Sainte-Croix, Switzerland, 1940s–70s): 4.5" discs, hand crank; sold in Thorens walnut cases and in Italian Sorrento inlaid boxes. Value scales with disc count.
- **Thorens gramophone-style** variants (horn-gramophone-shaped case) – one sold at $388 in 2013.
- **Reuge / Porter** (Porter Music Box Co., Vermont): modern high-quality 10"+ disc boxes, $800–2,500.
- **Regina** (Rahway NJ, 1890s–1910s): 15.5" tabletop $1,500–4,000; 27" upright/changer $8,000–25,000.
- **Symphonion / Polyphon** (Leipzig, Germany, 1890s–1910s): similar antique disc machines; value by disc size, case and comb condition (general knowledge, verify).
- **Sankyo** (Japan): 18-note cylinder boxes, junk tier.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Steel comb with tuned teeth; damper wires under teeth on antique machines; steel punched discs. Thorens discs carry a yellow Thorens label and are interchangeable across AD30 boxes. Antique cases: solid oak/mahogany, often carved or with a lithograph lid print. Sorrento boxes: olive-wood/marquetry inlay, "Made in Italy". Quality tell: full, clean tone with every note sounding (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken or missing comb teeth (kills value; re-tipping is specialist work), rusted discs, bent disc projections, broken springs/governors, cracked inlay or veneer lifting. Ask for a video of a full disc playing. Regina restoration costs run into hundreds of dollars (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Few fakes; modern reproduction discs exist for Regina/Polyphon (lower value than originals), and married cases/movements occur (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Steady eBay demand from music-box collectors and gift buyers; the Thorens market has softened since 2013. Antique Regina/Symphonion sell best through specialist auctions and the Musical Box Society community (general knowledge, verify). Mild Nov–Dec gift peak.

## What sellers usually miss
Thorens movement inside a Sorrento inlay box – they list the box maker, not the movement. Disc count drives price (each disc $4–7). Antique Regina owners usually know; the Thorens ones don't.

## Shipping / handling
Thorens box ~5 lb + discs; wrap discs separately, ~$20–25 in. Regina tabletop 40+ lb, crate – local only. eBay 13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State movement maker (Thorens AD30), disc count and titles, whether it plays every note, box/instructions present, case maker and condition, dimensions. Photograph the movement label, a disc label, the comb (all teeth) and include a playing video link. Title: "Thorens AD30 disc music box + N discs, Sorrento inlay, works".

## Buy rule
Buy when: Thorens AD30 + 15+ discs ≤ $100 (2026 Terapeak data says it clears comfortably only at ≤ $75), or any 10"+ disc box with discs ≤ $200 (confirm it plays). Pass when: broken comb teeth visible, no discs, or "needs work". Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Regina tabletops (40+ lb) are local-only; 27" uprights are bigger than a passenger seat and excluded.
