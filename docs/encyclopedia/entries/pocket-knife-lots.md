# Pocket-knife lots (Case, Buck, Boker, Schrade, Camillus)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge 2024–25; Buck 898 Impact S30V auto retail ~$180 / used $100–130; Case XX tang-stamp dating from Case collector guides.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Case: "CASE XX" shield in the handle, tang stamp with dots (1970–2000) – jigged bone, red/blue bone bring the most; Case "American flag" shield knives are 2000s factory issues ($40–60). Buck: 110 folding hunter with brass bolsters (1964+, $30–60), 898 Impact (auto, black handle, $100–130 used). Boker: tree-brand shield, "Boker USA" stockman ($25–45). Craftsman/Camillus camp knives $15–30. Marttiini: Finnish birch-handle fixed blade ($25–50). Sellers write "vintage pocket knives", "Case knife lot", "Buck knife".

## What sellers usually miss
Tang stamps (dots/dating) on Case; first-variation Buck 110s (no brass bolster rivets visible, 1964–67). Big 40-piece lots are mostly junk tier – value is in the 3–4 named pieces.

## Shipping / handling
Light – $8–12 per knife. Automatic knives (Buck 898) can't ship to several states; sell locally or via dealers.

## Buy rule
Buy when: lot ≤ $8/knife with visible Case XX bone or Buck 110 pieces, or Case XX 1960s–70s singles ≤ $25. Pass when: seller names Case/Buck and prices at $25+/knife; auto knives (shipping rules). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
