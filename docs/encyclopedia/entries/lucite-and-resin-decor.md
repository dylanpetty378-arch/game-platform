## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Clear cast polyester resin or acrylic (Lucite, Plexiglas) used for decorative objects 1950s–1980s.
- **Embedments**: real butterflies, dried flowers, scorpions, seahorses, coins cast in clear resin — paperweights, domes, cubes; huge 1970s souvenir trade from Taiwan, Philippines, Malaysia. Also "corporate embedments" (company products/awards in Lucite — collectable to niche buyers).
- **Lucite design objects**: thick clear acrylic boxes, trays, ice buckets, lamps, bookends, obelisks — Hollywood Regency/MCM.
pid 7957073117: ~5.75 in. dome of clear cast resin containing dried everlasting flowers (helichrysum/rhodanthe) and a brown-and-white Graphium-type butterfly (swordtail-like; common SE-Asia species) — 1970s souvenir embedment; slight yellowing at base, minor surface scratches.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Lucite (acrylic): crystal clear, polished edges, lighter than glass, no yellowing. Polyester resin: yellows with age, may have bubbles, slightly sticky or cloudy surfaces. Quality embedments: no bubbles, well-centred, colourful specimens intact.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Yellowing, crazing, scratches, internal cracks, delamination around specimens, faded insects. Polishing can remove light scratches (but is a repair).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern Chinese resin embedments (2000s–present) are identical in type; "vintage" claims unverified. Charles Hollis Jones fakes exist.

## Market
Moderate decor demand (cabinet of curiosities, oddities buyers). Sell-through ~35%.

## What sellers usually miss
Corporate embedments with rare products; signed Lucite pieces called "plastic box". Sellers often over-price butterfly souvenirs as "taxidermy".

## Shipping / handling
1–3 lb solid, $8–12; wrap to prevent scratches.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Lucite" or "resin", maker if signed (Charles Hollis Jones, Ritts, Grosfeld House, Dorothy Thorpe), form (box, tray, bookends, paperweight, dome), "embedment", specimen (butterfly) or corporate product, "MCM", size. Junk: "taxidermy" on souvenir domes, "designer" unsigned, "rare". `Charles Hollis Jones Signed Lucite Box MCM 1970s 8 in Hollywood Regency`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for signed Lucite (CHJ median $120); Etsy for oddities/cabinet-of-curiosities buyers on butterfly domes and corporate embedments.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (butterfly domes $12.50, large clear domes/corporate $30–100, Ritts/Grosfeld/Thorpe $80–300, CHJ $70–300), OBO, accept −20%, floor $6; cut after 6 weeks (sell-through ~35%).
- **Photos:** piece backlit on white for clarity; signature/label; yellowing against a white card; bubbles and crazing; scratches under raking light; specimen or product close-up; ruler.
- **State:** dimensions, weight, clarity, yellowing, bubbles, scratches, specimen type (no protected species named unless verified), "souvenir embedment, c. 1970s" where it is one, signature, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 1–3 lb solid, wrapped to prevent scratches, $8–12; shoebox class.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "This is a signed [Charles Hollis Jones] Lucite [box], 1970s, [no yellowing or crazing]; signed CHJ accessories sell for $[70–300], and the unsigned plastic boxes are $20."
