# Wicker fishing creels & tackle baskets
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: craigslist 1057 (Eurosporta ARCA wicker fishing basket seat, $40); eBay sold pattern (knowledge 2024–25); Lang's Auction creel results (knowledge)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Split-willow/rattan creels with a hole in the lid (to drop trout in), leather harness. Value makers: George Lawrence (Portland OR, leather-trimmed, stamped "Geo. Lawrence Co."), McMonies, Turtle-brand, Peters, Japanese Hong Kong export creels (common, $20–50). European tackle baskets/seat-boxes (French ARCA/Eurosporta, English Brady bags) are "cottagecore" decor, $40–120. Sellers write "fishing basket", "wicker creel", "trout basket".

## What sellers usually miss
The Lawrence/McMonies stamp on the leather; leather-bound rims; centre vs side hole.

## Shipping / handling
2–5 lb, bulky, $20–25 each way. eBay/Etsy.

## Buy rule
Buy when: leather-bound American creel with any maker stamp ≤ $80. Pass when: Hong Kong/Japan creels or European seat-baskets over $30. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
