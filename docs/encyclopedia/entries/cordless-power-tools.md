## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Brushless ("Fuel", "XR", "BL") > brushed. Battery capacity printed on the pack (2.0/5.0/8.0/12.0 Ah). Genuine batteries carry holograms and date codes; fakes are light, have poor labels and wrong cell counts. Wear: scuffed housings are fine; burnt smell, sparking, chuck wobble and cracked housings are not. Serial-number labels intact (removed labels = theft red flag).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dead or weak batteries, burnt motors, worn brushes (corded), slipping clutches, broken chucks, missing guards/blades, cracked housings. Ask for a video running and the battery fuel gauge lit. Stolen-tool risk is real on CL: missing serial labels, engraved initials ground off, no charger/case, cheap bulk listings.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Milwaukee/DeWalt/Makita batteries are widespread on eBay/Amazon/marketplaces; fake Festool/Milwaukee tools less common but exist (general knowledge, verify). Only buy batteries with hologram/date-code photos.

## Market
Huge and liquid on eBay, FB Marketplace and OfferUp; buyers are trades and DIYers and they know prices. Batteries and Festool/Hilti move fastest. Spring/summer construction season is slightly stronger (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Almost nothing – tool sellers know brands. The only spreads: estate lots ("box of tools $50") containing Festool/Hilti, or batteries listed without noting capacity (a 12.0 HD listed as "Milwaukee battery $40"). Stolen-tool risk is real on CL; prefer sellers with the charger/case.

## Shipping / handling
Drills 3–6 lb ($12–18); saws/band saws 10–20 lb ($25–40); miter saws 40–60 lb ($60–90 – local only). eBay 13%. Lithium batteries must ship ground with terminals protected (general knowledge, verify carrier rules).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Brand, exact model number, tool-only vs kit, battery Ah and date code, tested (video link), included accessories/case/charger, cosmetic condition. Photograph the model/serial label, battery label and hologram, running shot, accessories.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Festool/Hilti under 40% of sold; Milwaukee/DeWalt estate lots ≤ $5/tool; genuine HD12.0/XC8.0 batteries under $40 with hologram photo. Pass when: Ryobi/Craftsman/B&D, single Milwaukee/DeWalt tools priced within $40 of sold, anything over 40 lb, batteries without hologram/date-code photos. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (large bench/stationary tools are "bigger" and excluded).
