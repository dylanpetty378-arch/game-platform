## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Colour tells the brand: Milwaukee red, DeWalt yellow, Makita teal, Ryobi lime, Hilti red/grey, Festool green/black, Bosch blue, Craftsman red/grey. Model number is on a label on the housing or battery.

## What sellers usually miss
Almost nothing – tool sellers know brands. The only spreads: estate lots ("box of tools $50") containing Festool/Hilti, or batteries listed without noting capacity (a 12.0 HD listed as "Milwaukee battery $40"). Stolen-tool risk is real on CL; prefer sellers with the charger/case.

## Shipping / handling
Drills 3–6 lb ($12–18); saws/band saws 10–20 lb ($25–40); miter saws 40–60 lb ($60–90 – local only). eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Festool/Hilti under 40% of sold; Milwaukee/DeWalt estate lots ≤ $5/tool; genuine HD12.0/XC8.0 batteries under $40 with hologram photo. Pass when: Ryobi/Craftsman/B&D, single Milwaukee/DeWalt tools priced within $40 of sold, anything over 40 lb, batteries without hologram/date-code photos. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
