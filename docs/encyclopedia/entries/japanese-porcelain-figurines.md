## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Inexpensive export porcelain and bisque from Japan, 1921–1980s: courting couples, animals, shoes and boots (planters), angels, Hummel-alikes (Stauffer), Christmas pieces. Marks: "Japan", "Made in Japan", "Occupied Japan" (1945–1952), foil labels (Lefton red/gold "Exclusives Japan", Napco, Enesco), inked numbers.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Lefton** (George Zoltan Lefton, Chicago importer, 1941–2000s): numbered with foil labels; Christmas, Brown Heritage, Bluebird $15–80.
- **Napco** (National Potteries, Cleveland): lady head vases $50–300; Christmas angels.
- **Inarco, Relpo, Rubens, Parma**: head vases $40–300 (size, lashes, pearls).
- **Erich Stauffer / Arnart** (1950s–60s): Hummel-style children, often large 7–9", "Barnyard Frolics", "Mother's Helper" etc. numbered 86/xxxx, 8xxx. $15–40.
- **Holt Howard** (1950s–60s): pixie condiments $40–200, Christmas items.
- **Kreiss** "Psycho-Ceramics" (1950s) $40–150.
- **Occupied Japan**: bisque colonial couples, small figures $10–60.
- Generic shoes/boots planters (gold trim, applied roses) $5–15 each.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Fine detail, delicate applied flowers, crisp painted faces, bisque with good tinting = better. Thick glossy glaze, blurry molding, rough paint = cheap. Foil labels intact add value.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chipped applied flowers and fingers, worn gold, crazing, repaired heads. Value drops 50%+ with damage.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake "Occupied Japan" stamps on newer Chinese porcelain; head vase reproductions (lighter, poorly painted lashes). Hummel-alikes are not fakes of Goebel, just imitations.

## Market
Saturated; slow. Head vases and Holt Howard are the liquid exceptions.

## What sellers usually miss
Head vases priced as "vase"; Holt Howard pixie jars mixed with kitchen junk.

## Shipping / handling
Light (1–2 lb), fragile; $10–15.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker (Lefton/Napco/Inarco/Erich Stauffer/Holt Howard/Kreiss), line or item (lady head vase/pixie/Psycho-Ceramics/Barnyard Frolics), model number, "Made in Japan"/"Occupied Japan", height, "foil label". Junk: "Hummel" for Stauffer, "antique", "rare", "cute". `Napco Lady Head Vase 6" Lashes Pearls Blue Hat C3815 Japan 1959`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for head vases, Holt Howard and Kreiss (the liquid lines); Etsy for Lefton Christmas and Bluebird; shoes/animals/Stauffer lotted local.
- **Price position:** head vases/Holt Howard/Kreiss at the sold median ($40–300 by size and lashes), accept −15%; Lefton named lines $15–60, OBO; Stauffer at $25 median, shoes $8 — lot them. Cut 10% at 45 days.
- **Photos:** first: figure front; then base mark and foil label, model number, applied flowers and fingers close-up, gold trim, lashes and pearls on head vases, crazing, repaired head joints, ruler.
- **State:** maker/label, model number, height, country mark, chips on flowers/fingers, worn gold, crazing, repairs by photo number; label intact noted.
- **Pack / haul:** 1–2 lb, fragile; boxed with flowers and lashes padded, $10–15.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Napco C3815] head vase, [6"], lashes and pearls intact with the foil label — the generic Japan figurines go for $8; the lashed head vases are what collectors pay $50–300 for."

## Buy rule
Price ≤ $200; profit = sold median × 0.87 − ~$12 ship out − ~$12 ship in − price ≥ max($50, price). Only head vases, Holt Howard and Kreiss qualify, at very low prices. Fits a passenger seat. No authentication.
