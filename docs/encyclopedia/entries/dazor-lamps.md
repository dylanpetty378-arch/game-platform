# Dazor floating-arm lamps
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: napoleonexhibit.com eBay mirror (Dazor floating fixture $150; model 1102 $257; model 2008 saucer $479; M-210 $100–195; M-209 magnifier $120); 1stdibs Dazor creator pages (asks $300–1,200); run12 sheet 56

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
St Louis-made industrial task lamps, 1940s–70s. Two families: (1) "Floating Fixture" – long twin-tube fluorescent head on a spring-balanced double arm, clamp or weighted base, tan/brown/grey hammertone; label "Dazor Mfg. Corp. St. Louis" on the head. (2) "Flying saucer" / UFO incandescent desk lamps (models 2004, 2008, 1102) – domed round shade on a slim arm, 1950s, the ones designers pay for. Also M-209/M-210 magnifier lamps.

## What sellers usually miss
The word "Dazor" – sellers write "vintage articulating fluorescent lamp" or "drafting lamp". Check the head label. Saucer shape = top tier.

## Shipping / handling
Floating fixture: ~10 lb but 3 ft long and springs – box 40"×10"×8", $30–45 each way. Saucer desk lamps ~6 lb, $20. Sell on eBay/Etsy; fees 13%.
