## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Natural sandstone or bonded abrasive wheels on an iron axle. Floor-standing farm grindstones (wooden or cast-iron frame, 12-30" wheel, foot treadle and/or crank, water trough) were standard on 1880-1940 farms. Bench hand-crank grinders clamp to a table with gear housings; made from the late 1800s into the 1950s, when electric grinders replaced them (Farm Show). Variants: kitchen knife/tool grinders, scythe and sickle grinders (some only for horse-drawn mower sickle bars), chain-drive grinders for drill bits and chisels, rotating-head models that tilt to angles, and heavy industrial ones (Farm Show). The Luther "Hummer" seated grinder has a bicycle-style seat and pedals.

## Makers, brands and variants
Luther Grinder Mfg (Milwaukee, seated/pedal "Dimo-Grit"; "The Luther Lines" hand-crank tool grinders, 1911 patent), Keen Kutter (Simmons Hardware, emblem castings), Sears "Craftsman"/"Dunlap", Red Devil, Montgomery Ward ("Wards"), Bonney, Hubbard. Collectors also name Griswold, Universal (Landers, Frary & Clark, New Britain CT), Climax, Modern Merit and Winchester (Farm Collector). Many farm grindstones are homemade frames with a purchased Ohio sandstone wheel.

### Quick ID checklist
- Size first: floor/treadle/seat units are "bigger" than a passenger seat → pass, regardless of brand.
- Look for cast lettering on the gear housing or frame: maker name, patent dates (from 1887 on), model number.
- Brand emblems: Keen Kutter "K" in a keystone/shield; Winchester script; Griswold; Luther name/"Luther Lines".
- Gear drive enclosed (better) vs exposed gears; chain drive and tilting heads are scarcer types.
- Original paint, pinstriping or decals vs sandblasted or "stone paint" refinish (Farm Collector warns of fake-aged finishes).
- Wheel: natural sandstone (tan, gritty, may be dished) vs grey bonded aluminium-oxide (later replacement).
- Handle: original wood handle on the crank, tool rest present, clamp screw intact.

### Golden-egg tells
- **Winchester-marked hand-crank grinder** — $65–300 vs $15–25 typical (Farm Collector) — "Winchester" cast or stamped on the housing; Winchester hardware line was 1920s–30s (general knowledge).
- **Keen Kutter emblem grinder** — premium over unmarked (the old entry's $30–120 tier) — Keen Kutter keystone logo cast in the frame.
- **Luther seated/pedal grinder, restored** — $150–400 local — bicycle seat and pedals, Luther castings; local only.
- **Luther "Luther Lines" hand-crank tool grinder (1911 patent)** — shippable bench tier — patent date cast in, Luther name on the frame.
- **Sickle/mower-bar grinder or chain-drive type** — scarcer types sought by grinder collectors (Farm Show) — sickle-bar clamp, chain visible between crank and stone.
- **Tormek (modern wet grinder)** — $250–500 — Tormek name, horizontal 10 in grey stone, water trough, leather honing wheel.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Cast iron with embossed maker names and patent dates is the value driver; original paint/pinstriping adds value. Sandstone wheels crack and are heavy (a 12" x 2" stone ~20 lb, a 24" ~80+ lb). Modern bonded aluminum-oxide wheels indicate later replacements. English-made grinders sometimes have porcelain-lined parts (Farm Collector). Collectors prefer unrestored with original labels; sandblasted or "stone painted" pieces are made to look older and are worth less.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Cracked or out-of-round stone, seized axle bearings, missing treadle linkage, rotten wood frames. A cracked stone can explode under power - don't motorize. Also missing tool rests, stripped gears, broken clamp screws, replaced handles.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Little faking. Cast-iron "Keen Kutter" decor reproductions exist in small items. Watch for fake-aged finishes (Farm Collector).

## Market
Local/farm-decor market; eBay sales limited by freight. Slow. A small collector community for hand-crank bench grinders (Farm Collector, Farm Show).

## What sellers usually miss
Little - prices are local and fair. Marked Keen Kutter bench grinders sometimes underpriced; Winchester marks rarely called out.

## Shipping / handling
Treadle grindstones: freight or local only, 50-150 lb. Bench grinders 8-15 lb, $20-30.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Maker castings, wheel diameter and condition (no cracks), local pickup terms.

### What to tell the buyer
"Hand-crank bench grinder by [maker], cast marks '[text/patent date]'. Wheel [diameter × width], [natural sandstone/bonded], [no cracks, runs true]. Gears turn freely [yes/no]; tool rest and clamp present. [Original paint/finish]. Treadle units: local pickup only." Photograph castings, the gear side and the wheel edge.

## Buy rule
Floor/treadle units fail the passenger-seat and shipping rules - always pass. Bench hand-crank grinders: buy only marked brand units ≤ $25 with resale ≥ $100.
