## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Embossed steel (or pre-1915 porcelain-on-steel) registration plates. Year is stamped on the plate or on a tab/sticker. Collectors pay for age, condition (original paint, no rust holes), pairs (same number front and rear), low numbers, and specific years for "Year of Manufacture" (YOM) registration of classic cars in states that allow it. Decorators buy rusty plates in bulk for signs and walls.

## Makers, brands and variants
State-issued, mostly made in prisons. Key types: porcelain (1903-1920s), early embossed painted steel, 1940s fibreboard/soybean (WWII shortages), 1950s-70s painted steel with colour changes every year, reflective sheeting from late 1970s onward, motorcycle (small), dealer, truck, and specialty plates.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original paint vs repaint is the key quality issue: repainted plates show brush marks or overly glossy uniform colour and paint in the recesses; original has even wear on raised characters. Rust holes and bends lower value. Old: thicker steel, bolt slots; newer: reflective film and stickers.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rust, bent corners, enlarged holes, faded paint. Repaints acceptable to YOM buyers if DMV-legal but devalue for collectors.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reproduction "vintage" tin plates (thin, stamped, novelty) and recently repainted plates passed as original. Porcelain repros exist.

## Market
Large and liquid: plates sell steadily on eBay to collectors, car restorers and decorators; lots move within 1-4 weeks. Ohio is a populous state (common), so values are middle of the range.

## What sellers usually miss
Treating a mixed box of 1950s-60s plates as a $10-20 wall-decor lot. Each plate individually is $10-30, and years that match popular classic cars (1955-57, 1965-69) attract YOM buyers.

## Shipping / handling
Flat, durable: a single plate ships in a large envelope for $5-8; 5-10 plates ~3-5 lb, $10-15. Cardboard both sides to protect corners.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** state + year + "license plate" + "pair" if matched + number + "original paint" + type (porcelain / passenger / motorcycle / dealer) + "YOM". Junk: "wall decor", "man cave", "plate lot" for early plates. `1957 Ohio License Plate Matched Pair Original Paint YOM Classic Car`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (collectors, restorers, decorators; lots move in 1–4 weeks); pairs for classic-car years to YOM buyers. No local premium.
- **Price position:** at the sold median ($12–35 1950s–60s singles; $40–150 pairs/YOM; $75–500 pre-1930/porcelain), accept −10%, floor 80%; OBO; cut 15% after 30 days; 1970s–90s in lots.
- **Photos:** front straight on in daylight; back; corners and bolt holes; paint close up (original vs repaint); porcelain chips; pairs side by side; lot laid out with count.
- **State:** state, year, number, colours, original paint or repaint, bends/rust/chips by photo number, pair matched, YOM eligibility to be checked with the buyer's DMV.
- **Pack / haul:** shoebox; single plate in a large envelope with cardboard both sides $5–8; 5–10 plates 3–5 lb $10–15; corners protected.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Year] [State] [pair], original paint, numbers match, the year restorers need for a [1957] car's YOM registration; single 1970s plates go for [$] and matched [year] pairs bring [$]."

## Buy rule
Profit = lot resale × 0.87 − ~$12 ship out − ~$12 ship in − price ≥ max($50, price). A 6-10 plate lot of 1950s-70s original-paint plates at ≤ $25 typically clears this. Singles rarely do unless pre-1930.
