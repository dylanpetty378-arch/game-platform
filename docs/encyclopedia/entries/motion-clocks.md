## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1940s–60s American electric (or Lux wind-up) clocks with an animated feature: a waterfall (a printed rotating cylinder behind a curved transparent scene), a flickering fireplace, a girl on a swing, a boy fishing, a blacksmith, or a rocking chair grandma. Mastercrafters (Chicago) used brass-plated plastic cases with a dial marked "MasterCrafters", "Made in U.S.A." Waterfall models (#344 Niagara Falls, 1950s) have green/gold plastic tree trim and a lit cylinder.

## Makers, brands and variants
- Mastercrafters Clock & Radio Co. (Chicago, 1940s–60s): waterfall #344/#345, fireplace, swinging girl, carousel, church, blacksmith.
- United Clock Co. (Brooklyn): fireplace, "Bubbles" lamp clock, ballerina.
- Haddon (Chicago): rocking chair grandma, Haddon "Rite Time" motion clocks.
- Spartus, Sessions, Rite-Time, Electric Clock Co.
- Lux / Keebler (Waterbury CT): pendulette wind-up novelty clocks with moving eyes or bobbing figures; very collectible.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Brass/gold paint over plastic or metal cases; synchronous motors (Hansen, Telechron-type). Painted plastic tends to flake. Original cords are often brittle cloth or rubber.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Motors run noisy or stall; motion linkages break; the animation cylinder sticks; bulbs are out; crazed bezels; flaking gold paint; brittle cords (a safety hazard). Many are "time works, motion doesn't".

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Few reproductions. Some modern Chinese "waterfall" novelty clocks exist (clearly modern).

## Market
Moderate; collectors want working examples. eBay: roughly 100+ motion clocks per month. Days to sell: 20–60.

## What sellers usually miss
Lux/Keebler pendulettes in "old clock" lots; United "Bubbles" lamp clocks.

## Shipping / handling
About 10×8×5 in, 3–4 lb packed; $15–20. Remove and secure the bulb; pad the motion cylinder.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Post a video showing time and motion running. Give the model number from the back label and describe the cord condition.

## Buy rule
Price ≤ $200; profit = median × 0.87 − ~$17 out − ~$17 in − price ≥ max($50, price). Dylan's no-untested-electronics rule applies: only buy if the seller shows a video of it running, and even then common Mastercrafters waterfalls don't clear the profit bar. Buy rare models only.
