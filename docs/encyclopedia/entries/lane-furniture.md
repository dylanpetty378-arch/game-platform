## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
The Lane Company (Altavista, Virginia; founded 1912 as a cedar-chest maker) made mass-market mid-century modern lines in walnut and walnut veneer. **Acclaim** (1958–60s, André Bus): walnut tables and case pieces with distinctive inlaid "dovetail" key joints across the top. **Rhythm** (1960s): rounded, sculpted walnut forms. **Perception** (1960s): glass-topped walnut tables with wood frames. Lane also made "Mosaic", "Tuxedo", "Copenhagen" and Brutalist/1970s lines (general knowledge, verify). Serial/stamp inside a drawer or under the piece (per earlier summary). Lane cedar chests carry a stamped serial number on the underside that reads in reverse as the manufacture date (general knowledge, verify). Seller words: "Lane MCM", "Lane Acclaim end table", "dovetail table", "walnut mid century".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Acclaim** – most recognised; step tables, end tables, coffee tables, cube tables.
- **Rhythm**, **Perception**, **Copenhagen**, **Tuxedo**, **Mosaic** and 1970s Brutalist "Pueblo"/"Staccato" lines (general knowledge, verify names).
- **Cedar chests** – very common, low-to-mid value except MCM-styled ones.
- Look-alikes: Broyhill Brasilia, American of Martinsville, Drexel – similar walnut MCM.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Walnut veneer over solid/engineered core with solid walnut edges; Acclaim's contrasting inlaid dovetail keys; Lane stamp with model number and serial. Quality tells: original lacquer with even patina, intact veneer. Refinishing is common and acceptable to many buyers if well done. (general knowledge, verify)

## Condition, damage and repairs
Veneer chips and lifting at edges, water rings, sun-faded tops, scratched glass (Perception), refinish quality, wobbly legs. Ask for top surface photos, edges, the stamp, and dimensions.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Lane-style reproductions and modern "mid-century" pieces imitate the dovetail inlay; no Lane stamp. (general knowledge, verify)

## Market
Strong mid-century demand; sells best locally (FB Marketplace, Craigslist) and on Chairish; pairs sell faster and higher than singles. (general knowledge, verify)

## What sellers usually miss
Line name (Acclaim vs generic "Lane"), the stamp/serial inside the drawer, matched pairs.

## Shipping / handling
End tables 25–45 lb; freight/Greyhound or local pickup; shipping $80–150 per table makes eBay shipping uneconomic. (general knowledge, verify)

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State line, model/serial, dimensions, original vs refinished finish, condition of top/veneer. Photograph the stamp, dovetail inlays, top surface, legs. Title keywords: "Lane Acclaim walnut end table dovetail", "mid century modern", "pair".

## Buy rule
Buy when: cube/end tables under $60 that fit the passenger seat. Pass when: case pieces, cedar chests and coffee tables – bigger than a passenger seat and **excluded** by the size rule; damaged veneer. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
