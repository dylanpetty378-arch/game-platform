## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1970s woven jacquard straps with leather ends, rivets and swivel clips or split rings, often in Mexican/Andean or geometric patterns, sold widely in camera shops. Value only for OEM brand-name straps or very vivid designs.

## Makers, brands and variants
Unbranded Japanese/US 1970s; Ace, Optech; OEM Nikon, Canon, Pentax, Olympus, Leica, Hasselblad.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Cotton/acrylic woven bands, real leather ends, nickel hardware.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dry/cracked leather, fraying, stained bands.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern reproductions sold as "vintage style" on Etsy/Amazon.
