## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Licensed merchandise from 1988–2015 boy bands. 'N Sync (1995–2002) dominates US Y2K nostalgia: 2000 Living Toyz "No Strings Attached" marionette dolls (each member on strings, ~12"), 2001 Best Buy exclusive bobbleheads (boxes labelled "2001 Collectible" with member name and Best Buy logo), Mattel UNO 'N Sync edition (2000), Got Milk items, lunchboxes, Pop! era; NKOTB 1989–90 Hasbro dolls and lunchboxes; BSB 1999 dolls; Jonas/One Direction dolls by Play Along/Hasbro.
Photo tells: original boxes with intact windows, all five members, sealed packaging, store-exclusive marks.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Boxes: shelf wear, sun fading (pink to white), crushed corners lower value 30–50%. Justin Timberlake figures sell highest.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Marionettes tangle; bobblehead springs break; cards missing. Complete vs incomplete matters.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Unauthorized dolls and bootleg tees; signatures widely faked.

## Market
Strong 1990s/Y2K nostalgia since ~2022 ('N Sync reunion buzz 2023). Moderate liquidity; lots sell in 2–4 weeks.

## What sellers usually miss
Sellers price whole lots as "old toys" at $20–40 when individual boxed pieces sum to $80–150.

## Shipping / handling
Boxed bobbleheads light; lot box ~16×12×10, 4–6 lb, $15–22.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
