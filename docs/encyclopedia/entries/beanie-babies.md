## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Small bean-filled plush by Ty Inc. (Oak Brook IL), launched 1993, craze 1996–99. Recognition: red heart-shaped "ty" hang tag (swing tag) in the ear, white fabric "tush tag" sewn at the seam. Tag generations (dating tool):
- 1st gen (1993): single heart, no poem, "ty" in thin letters, narrow.
- 2nd gen (1993–94): fat "ty", single page, no poem.
- 3rd gen (1995): bubble letters "ty", single page.
- 4th gen (1996–97): opens like a book, yellow star "Original Beanie Baby", poem and birthday.
- 5th gen (1998–99): same, birthdate written out, "Ty Inc. Oakbrook IL" etc. Later generations through the 2000s.
Princess bear (1997): royal purple bear with an embroidered white rose on the chest, purple ribbon – made in the millions after Diana's death; the "1st edition, PVC pellets, Indonesia" story is an internet myth for value.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Ty Beanie Babies** (1993–): ~2,000 styles. Original Nine (1993): Legs, Squealer, Spot, Flash, Splash, Chocolate, Patti, Pinchers, Cubbie – early versions with 1st/2nd-gen tags are the real value.
- **Known valuable variants** (general knowledge, verify): Royal Blue Peanut the Elephant (dark blue, 1995), Brown "Teddy" old-face/new-face early (1994–95), Nana the monkey (pre-Bongo), Spot without a spot, Humphrey the camel, Peking panda, Chilly the polar bear, Slither, Web the spider, Quacker "wingless", Derby with no star, Kiwi, Tank 7-line – these $100–1,000+ only with early tags and authentication.
- **Employee/insider bears**: Ty Employee Bear (red/white no-name 1997), Billionaire Bears 1–15 (given to Ty staff) $200–2,000.
- **Sports promotion bears**: MLB/NFL/NBA stadium giveaway Beanies (1997–2000) with commemorative cards, and later 2000s–2010s Ty "NFL" team bears (red 49ers bear in example) – $5–20 unless with the event card and a key player.
- **Beanie Buddies** (larger, 1998–), **Teenie Beanies** (McDonald's 1997–2000, $1–3), **Beanie Boos** (big eyes, 2009–).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Polyester plush filled with PVC (to 1997) or PE (polyethylene, from ~1998) pellets – tush tag states which. PVC alone does not make a bear valuable. Real rarity tells: tag generation, tush tag "©1993", style-specific variation (colour, missing feature). Tag protectors suggest the owner thought it valuable; most are still $3.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Value drops 50–90% without the hang tag; creased/torn hang tags, smoke odour, fading, matted plush. Clipped tush tags = near worthless. Re-tagged bears (genuine old tag swapped onto later bear) are a known fraud.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Beanies flooded the late 1990s (especially Princess, Peace, Garcia, Erin, Britannia, Royal Blue Peanut, Brown Teddy) – thin tags with wrong fonts, poor stitching, wrong pellet feel. High-value examples need professional authentication (Peggy Gallagher Enterprises / True Blue Authentication historically) – which is outside Dylan's rules.

## Market
Massive oversupply; eBay has hundreds of thousands of active "rare" asks at fantasy prices; actual sold prices low. Sells slowly except genuine early tags. Bulk lots move at $0.50–1 per piece to crafters/flippers.

## What sellers usually miss
They overvalue, not undervalue. The only edge: early-tag (1st/2nd gen) Original Nine pieces or Ty employee bears mixed into a cheap toy lot – identifiable by the thin single-page heart tag without a poem.

## Shipping / handling
Light, soft; poly mailer, $5–8. Protect hang tags with plastic tag protectors.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Photograph hang tag inside and out and tush tag both sides; state tag generation, PVC/PE, smoke-free; do not call common bears "rare".

## Buy rule
Profit formula with resale $3–20 and $10+ shipping never clears max($50, price) for common Beanies – pass all lots and Princess/49ers/NFL bears. Only a 1st/2nd-gen-tag early style or Ty employee/Billionaire bear at ≤ $10 is worth a look, and even then value depends on authentication (excluded by Dylan's rules for high-value items) – treat as "ask seller for tag photos", rarely a buy.
