Buy rule (short): Only 1956-57 Elvis Presley Enterprises-marked merchandise or concert ephemera ≤ $40; pass on statues (size), autographs (authentication) and post-1977 souvenirs.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Everything from 1950s fan merchandise to the endless memorial items after his 1977 death. The valuable core is the 1956 Elvis Presley Enterprises (EPE) licensed line (marked "© 1956 Elvis Presley Enterprises"), original concert programs, tickets and early RCA records. Life-size statues are 1990s-2010s resin/fiberglass decor.

## Makers, brands and variants
EPE 1956 licensees (Teen-Age Lipstick, charm bracelets, scarves, Magnolia shoes, record cases), McCormick Distilling decanters (1978-80s, music boxes), Bradford Exchange plates, Hallmark ornaments, Vandor, Franklin Mint, Graceland souvenirs, life-size statues by various decor makers.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
1956 items: period printing, EPE copyright line, aged materials. Modern: bright printing, "Elvis Presley Enterprises Inc." with later dates, ABG (Authentic Brands Group) licensing.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Paper items: tears, tape, fading. Statues: chipped paint, broken guitar necks/fingers. Decanters: music box works may be dead.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Forged autographs are endemic - requires PSA/JSA/Beckett, outside Dylan's rules. Reproduction concert posters and tickets common.

## Market
Aging collector base; souvenirs glut. 1956 EPE remains strong.

## What sellers usually miss
"Old Elvis stuff" boxes with EPE 1956 merchandise mixed with souvenirs.

## Shipping / handling
Small paper/merch cheap. Statues: freight $300+.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Copyright line, date, condition; never sell autographs without authentication.

## Buy rule
Statues fail the size rule. Autographs require professional authentication - pass. Buy only EPE-1956-marked items at ≤ 1/3 of comps.
