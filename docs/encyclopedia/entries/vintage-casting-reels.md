# Vintage casting reels & tackle lots (level-wind baitcasters, minnow traps)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold-price knowledge 2024–25 for Pflueger Supreme/Shakespeare/Penn/Langley reels; glass minnow trap sold ranges (Orvis, Joy, C.F. Orvis marked); fishing-collectibles (NFLCC) guides.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1930s–70s round-sided metal casting reels with a level-wind bar across the front, knurled end plates, pearl or black handle knobs. Makers stamped on the foot or end plate: Pflueger (Supreme, Akron, Nobby), Shakespeare (Criterion, Tournament), Langley, South Bend, Penn, Heddon, Ocean City, Bronson. Great Lakes Products "Great Lakes" single-action trolling reel is a big spoked aluminium reel. Glass minnow traps: a gallon-size clear jar with a funnel neck moulded in – "Orvis" or "Joy" embossed ones bring the most. Sellers write "vintage fishing reels lot", "old reels", "antique tackle".

Lures: see vintage-fishing-lures – common plastic Rapala/Rebel/Beetle Spin tackle-box contents are $1–3 each.

## What sellers usually miss
Reel makers are only stamped on the foot or inside the end plate; "lot of old reels" hides a Pflueger Supreme or Ambassadeur. Glass minnow traps are listed as "glass jar" or "minnow bottle" and are worth $40–80 alone.

## Shipping / handling
Reels ~1 lb each; a 10-reel lot ships for $20–25. Glass minnow traps need double-boxing ($20). Resell individually on eBay; fees 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: lot price ≤ $6/reel and at least one Pflueger Supreme / Ambassadeur / Heddon visible, or a glass minnow trap ≤ $25. Pass when: the lot is plastic tackle boxes of modern lures, or seller already names Supreme/Ambassadeur. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
