## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- 1930s–70s round-sided metal casting reels with a level-wind bar across the front, knurled end plates, and pearl or black handle knobs.
- The maker is stamped on the foot or end plate: Pflueger (Supreme, Akron, Nobby), Shakespeare (Criterion, Tournament), Langley, South Bend, Penn, Heddon, Ocean City, Bronson.
- The Great Lakes Products "Great Lakes" single-action trolling reel is a big spoked aluminium reel.
- Glass minnow traps: a gallon-size clear jar with a funnel neck moulded in. The ones embossed "Orvis" or "Joy" bring the most.
- Seller words: "vintage fishing reels lot", "old reels", "antique tackle".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Pflueger** (Akron OH): Supreme (the best mid-century casting reel; worth more in a leather case with wrench), Akron, Nobby, Medalist (fly; see fly-reels).
- **Shakespeare** (Kalamazoo): Criterion, Tournament; President (higher).
- **Langley** (San Diego): Streamlite.
- **Penn**: Peer.
- **Heddon**: 3-25 and other casting reels.
- **Meisselbach**: Tripart.
- **South Bend**: mid-range.
- **Bronson, Ocean City**: low.
- **Kentucky reels** (German-silver, hand-made, 1800s–early 1900s): B.F. Meek, B.C. Milam, J.F. & B.F. Meek, Talbot. Top tier: stop for anything engraved "Meek" or "Talbot".
- **ABU Ambassadeur 5000** red (Sweden, early serials) is top tier per this entry; see the note under Value ladder.

### Golden-egg tells
- B.F. Meek & Sons (Louisville) / Meek & Milam (Frankfort) German-silver Kentucky reel — $500–3,000+ vs $20–45 Pflueger Supreme (~25×+) — engraved "B.F. MEEK & SONS LOUISVILLE KY" or "MEEK & MILAM FRANKFORT KY" on the head plate, size number (No. 2/3/5), jewelled bearing caps, no level-wind (https://antiquefishingreels.com/?p=2058)
- "MEEK 44" German-silver fly reel — $1,000+ vs $20–45, the most collectible Meek — "MEEK 44" stamped on the foot/plate, no level-wind, click button on the back plate (https://antiquefishingreels.com/?p=2058)
- Talbot (Nevada MO / Kansas City) "Ben Hur", "Star", "Meteor" — $300–1,500 vs $20–45 — "Wm. H. Talbot" engraving, German silver, often a star or model name on the plate (https://amff.org/portfolio/in-reel-time-fly-reels-past-present/wm-h-talbot-ben-hur-trout-reel/)
- Later Horton/Bluegrass-marked Meek No. 3 (Bristol, Conn.) — $100–300, well below hand-made Louisville/Frankfort reels but 5× a Supreme — "THE HORTON MFG CO. BRISTOL CONN." or "BLUEGRASS REEL WORKS" beside the Meek name (https://antiquefishingreels.com/?p=2058)
- Pflueger Supreme in its leather case with wrench and oiler — $50–120 vs $20–45 bare (~2.5×) — brown leather snap case with "Pflueger" embossed, small flat wrench inside (entry comp)
- ABU Ambassadeur 5000 three-screw (1950s, Sweden, low serial) — $100–400 vs $50–150 ordinary 1960s–70s red 5000 — three screws on the sideplate (later four), serial under 100000, round "ABU Record"-style foot stamp (entry comp)
- Orvis-embossed glass minnow trap — $50–120 vs $20–45 unmarked jar — "ORVIS MANCHESTER VT" moulded into the shoulder of the gallon funnel-neck jar (entry comp)
- Shakespeare President or Heddon 3-25 — $50–150+ vs $5–15 plain Shakespeare Criterion — model name stamped on the foot or end plate (entry comp)

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Nickel-plated brass, German silver and aluminium. Jewelled bearings and quadruple-multiplying gears in better reels (general knowledge, verify).
- Hand-made Kentucky reels: fine machining, a maker's name and town engraved, serial numbers.
- Leather cases, wrenches, oil and boxes add value. Pearl handle knobs are original on many better reels.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Seized gears, worn level-wind pawls, cracked pearl knobs, missing click buttons, heavy plating loss, dented pillars.
- Check that the spool turns and the level-wind tracks. Ask for photos of the foot stamping and end plates.
- Glass traps: chips on the funnel neck, cracks, missing wire bail or lid.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Reproduction glass minnow traps with "Orvis" embossing exist, and modern Asian casting reels are styled as vintage (general knowledge, verify).
- Married parts are common in lots.

## Market
Fishing-collectible buyers (NFLCC, ORCA members) and users. eBay is the main venue, plus Lang's auctions for high-end reels. Spring is the peak. Common reels are oversupplied (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
- Reel makers are only stamped on the foot or inside the end plate. A "lot of old reels" can hide a Pflueger Supreme or an Ambassadeur.
- Glass minnow traps are listed as "glass jar" or "minnow bottle" and are worth $40–80 by themselves.

## Shipping / handling
- Reels ~1 lb each; a 10-reel lot ships for $20–25. Glass minnow traps need double-boxing ($20). Resell individually on eBay; fees 13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker + model + "casting reel"/"level wind" + "with case"/"wrench"/"box" + era + patent date; traps: "Orvis glass minnow trap". Junk: "old fishing reel", "antique tackle", "lot" (unless a lot). Example: `Vintage Pflueger Supreme Casting Reel with Leather Case Wrench Level Wind Working`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (NFLCC/ORCA collectors and users; spring peak); Kentucky reels (Meek, Milam, Talbot) and German-silver pieces to Lang's auction; sell lots individually, not as a pile.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median (Supreme/Langley/Penn Peer $20–45, cased Supreme/Heddon 3-25/Orvis trap $50–120, early Ambassadeur/President $150+); accept −15%, floor −25%; OBO; cut 10% after 30 days.
- **Photos:** 1) reel in profile, handle side; then the foot with maker/model stamp, both end plates, handle and knobs, level-wind and pawl, spool, case/wrench/box, any plating loss; short video of the spool spinning and the level-wind tracking.
- **State:** maker, model, patent dates or serial, spool turns freely, level-wind tracks, click/drag works, plating condition, case/wrench/box included, line removed, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** ~1 lb each; bubble in a 6×6×6 box, 1–2 lb class, $8–12; 10-reel lots $20–25; glass minnow traps double-boxed, $20.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "A [Pflueger Supreme in its leather case with the wrench / Heddon 3-25], maker stamped on the foot in photo [2] — the Bronson and Ocean City reels are the $10 ones; cased [model] examples sell for [$X] and it spins free in the video."

## Buy rule
Buy when: the lot price is ≤ $6/reel and at least one Pflueger Supreme / Ambassadeur / Heddon is visible; or a glass minnow trap is ≤ $25.
Pass when: the lot is plastic tackle boxes of modern lures; the seller already names Supreme/Ambassadeur.
Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat.
