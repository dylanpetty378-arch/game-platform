# Steuben glass (post-1933 colourless)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: agent knowledge of eBay/LiveAuctioneers sold bands (verify); run12 sheet 60

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Corning NY, 1933–2011 colourless lead crystal. Heavy, optically perfect, often with a thick disc foot or teardrop/"bubble" stem. Signed "Steuben" in script, acid-stamped or diamond-scribed, on the base – ask for a base photo. Pre-1933 Carder-era coloured Steuben (Aurene gold/blue iridescent, Rosaline, Jade) is a different, much higher market ($300–3,000+).

## What sellers usually miss
Carder-era coloured pieces listed as "iridescent glass vase"; animals listed as "crystal figurine". Plain colourless vases are correctly priced almost everywhere.

## Shipping / handling
Heavy for size (2–5 lb), fragile – $15–25 each way double-boxed.

## Buy rule
Buy when: signed Carder colour under $100, or animals under $40. Pass when: colourless vase/bowl at $75+ (7287 math: $110 × 0.87 − $100 − $40 shipping < 0). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
