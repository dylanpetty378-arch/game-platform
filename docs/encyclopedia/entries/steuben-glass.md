## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- Corning NY, 1933–2011, colourless lead crystal. Heavy and optically perfect, often with a thick disc foot or a teardrop/"bubble" stem.
- Signed "Steuben" in script on the base, acid-stamped or diamond-scribed. Ask for a base photo.
- Pre-1933 Carder-era coloured Steuben (Aurene gold/blue iridescent, Rosaline, Jade) is a different and much higher market ($300–3,000+).
- Seller words: "Steuben vase", "crystal vase", "iridescent glass vase", "crystal figurine".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Frederick Carder era (1903–1932)**: coloured art glass: Gold and Blue Aurene, Calcite, Jade, Rosaline, Tyrian, Intarsia. Often marked with a fleur-de-lis acid stamp or "Aurene" with a shape number (general knowledge, verify).
- **Arthur Houghton era (1933–2011)**: colourless "10M" crystal. Designers include Sidney Waugh, Walter Dorwin Teague, Lloyd Atkins and Donald Pollard.
- Common forms: trumpet and bouquet vases, olive dishes, bud vases, bowls.
- Better forms: hand coolers (apple, pear, egg), animals (pigs, owls, bears, eagles, frogs), large bowls, spiral and handled vases.
- Top forms: engraved presentation pieces and big sculptural works.
- Steuben was revived briefly under new owners after 2011; later pieces are less collected (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Very clear, heavy lead crystal with no seeds or tint, precise polished bases and crisp facets. Signature: small script "Steuben", diamond-point engraved on the underside.
- Look-alikes: Orrefors, Kosta, Baccarat (marked), Hawkes, and unsigned "Steuben-style" crystal. Unsigned pieces sell at the bottom tier.
- Carder Aurene shows gold or blue iridescence with a smooth satin lustre; compare against Tiffany Favrile and Quezal.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Rim chips, flea bites on the foot, and scratches inside bowls and vases.
- Ground or polished rims (a repair) lower value; look for an uneven rim height or a sharp bevel.
- Cloudiness inside vases ("sick glass") is hard to remove.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Forged diamond-point "Steuben" signatures on unsigned crystal exist; compare the handwriting.
- Fake Aurene and "Aurene"-signed iridescent pieces from the Czech Republic and the US circulate (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Colourless Steuben is plentiful because it was a corporate and wedding gift staple, so prices are flat to soft and buyers are mostly decorators and gift-buyers. Animals and Carder colour have a dedicated collector market at LiveAuctioneers and Jeffrey S. Evans-type auctions (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
- Carder-era coloured pieces listed as "iridescent glass vase".
- Animals listed as "crystal figurine".
- Plain colourless vases are correctly priced almost everywhere.

## Shipping / handling
- Heavy for its size (2–5 lb) and fragile: $15–25 each way, double-boxed.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Steuben" + "signed" + form ("hand cooler", "trumpet vase", "spiral bowl", animal name) + size + designer if known + "crystal"; Carder: "Gold Aurene" + shape number; Junk: "crystal figurine", "iridescent glass vase", "clear glass". Example: `Steuben Crystal Signed Apple Hand Cooler Paperweight 3" with Red Box`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for signed crystal and animals; Carder Aurene and engraved pieces to LiveAuctioneers/Jeffrey S. Evans. Plain trumpet vases are flat everywhere.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (6" trumpet vase $75–120; hand cooler $100–180; Aurene 8" vase $600–1,500), accept −15%, floor p25; OBO; cut 10% after 3 weeks (colourless is plentiful).
- **Photos:** first photo the piece on a dark background; then the signature macro (diamond-point style), rim, foot, interior for scratches/cloudiness, raking light for flea bites, red Steuben box/bag, tape for height.
- **State:** signature and its style, height and diameter, designer/model if known, rim and foot chip-free (or where), ground rim if suspected, interior condition, box/bag included, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 2–5 lb, heavy for size; double-box with 2" fill, piece wrapped and floating; $15–25.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Signed Steuben [hand cooler] in the original red box — the colourless 10M crystal from Corning, with the real diamond-point signature; retail was [$X] and these hold [$100–180]."
