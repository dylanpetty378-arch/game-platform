## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1983–2010 mobile phones. Valuable tells: brick shape with long antenna (DynaTAC), small clamshell with external antenna and grey/black "StarTAC" logo, chrome slider Nokia 8810/8850/8210, Motorola RAZR V3 in gold/pink boxed, factory sealed boxes. Commodity: Samsung/LG prepaid flips, Nokia 6010/3390, Kyocera.

## Makers, brands and variants
Motorola, Nokia, Ericsson, Samsung, LG, Kyocera, Audiovox, BlackBerry, Apple.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Boxed with manuals and chargers adds value. Batteries swollen in many.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dead/swollen batteries (fire risk in shipping), carrier locks, scratched screens; untested.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Refurb/new-housing Nokia 8800 and Vertu fakes; re-housed RAZRs.
