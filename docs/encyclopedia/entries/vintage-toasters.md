## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Chrome 2-slice toaster, 1930s–60s, bakelite base/handles. The one that matters: **Sunbeam Radiant Control T-20 / T-20A /
T-20B / T-35 / AT-W / VT-40 (1949–1990s)** – no lever at all; bread lowers itself when dropped in, rises when done.
Tell in a photo: rounded chrome body, two slots, **no push-down lever**, just a small "light/dark" knob at the base front;
model on the bottom plate. Others: **Toastmaster 1B14 / 1B9** (deco, push lever, 1940s–50s), **GE 139T / 129T** (chrome,
deco), **Universal/Landers Frary & Clark** flip-side toasters (1920s, you flip the bread by hand), **Manning-Bowman**,
**Hotpoint**, **Sunbeam T-9** (1937–40, streamlined, art deco). Titles: "vintage toaster", "chrome toaster",
"mid century toaster".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Sunbeam**: T-1, T-9 (1937–40 deco), T-20/T-20A/T-20B (1949+), T-35, AT-W, VT-40 Radiant Control — the cult line.
- **Toastmaster**: 1A1 (1926, top), 1B9, 1B14 deco.
- **GE**: 129T, 139T.
- **Universal / Landers, Frary & Clark**: 1920s flip-sides; E947 with porcelain (top).
- **Manning-Bowman**: deco designs incl. "Flat Iron" (top).
- **Hotpoint**, **Sterling**, **Kwikway**, **Handy Hot**, **Proctor-Silex** — mostly common.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Heavy chrome-plated steel/brass bodies, bakelite handles and feet, mica-wound nichrome elements. Quality tells: bright
chrome without pitting, intact bakelite, original cloth-covered cord. Repro or replaced cords are acceptable if done
safely. Porcelain inserts (Universal E947) add value.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chrome pitting and dents, cracked bakelite, frayed or brittle cords (safety), broken elements, crumbs/burnt residue,
stuck auto-lower mechanism on Sunbeams (often fixable by cleaning). "Tested working" and "lowers automatically" double
the price of a Radiant Control. Ask for a video of it lowering and toasting.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No significant fakes. Modern retro-styled toasters (Smeg, Dualit, Sunbeam retro lines) are not collectible
(general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Mid-century kitchen collectors, users who want the Sunbeam auto-lower, and decorators. Working Radiant Controls sell
steadily on eBay; common lever toasters barely sell. Etsy works for deco display pieces (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
The Sunbeam auto-lower feature. A "chrome toaster $15" with no lever is a T-20 worth $100–200 working (and they almost
always work or need a $0 element clean). Also the model plate underneath – sellers never photograph it.

## Shipping / handling
4–6 lb, $15–22 each way. eBay (13%). Test before listing; "works, lowers automatically" doubles the price.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State maker, model (bottom plate photo), year, tested status (video of auto-lower if Sunbeam), chrome and bakelite
condition, cord condition. Title: maker, model, year, "Radiant Control", "auto-lower", "tested working", "chrome",
"mid century".

## Buy rule
Buy when: Sunbeam Radiant Control (no lever) under $40; deco Toastmaster/GE under $20. Pass when: any lever toaster over
$20, untested lots at $35+ each, or shipping-heavy lots with no Sunbeam. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees
and shipping both ways. Must fit a passenger seat.
