# Vintage chrome toasters (Sunbeam Radiant Control, Toastmaster, GE, Universal, flip-side 1920s–30s)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay Sunbeam toaster category 2026-10 (T-9 1939 "tested working" $130 ask; AT-W Radiant Control $120 ask; T-20B listings $150–300 restored), gigazine.net "1949 toaster still the best" (Sunbeam T-20 cult), appraisily antique toaster value guide, sweep listing (NJ antique dealer, 6 toasters at $35 each).

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Chrome 2-slice toaster, 1930s–60s, bakelite base/handles. The one that matters: **Sunbeam Radiant Control T-20 / T-20A / T-20B / T-35 / AT-W / VT-40 (1949–1990s)** – no lever at all; bread lowers itself when dropped in, rises when done. Tell in a photo: rounded chrome body, two slots, **no push-down lever**, just a small "light/dark" knob at the base front; model on the bottom plate. Others: **Toastmaster 1B14 / 1B9** (deco, push lever, 1940s–50s), **GE 139T / 129T** (chrome, deco), **Universal/Landers Frary & Clark** flip-side toasters (1920s, you flip the bread by hand), **Manning-Bowman**, **Hotpoint**, **Sunbeam T-9** (1937–40, streamlined, art deco). Titles: "vintage toaster", "chrome toaster", "mid century toaster".

## What sellers usually miss
The Sunbeam auto-lower feature. A "chrome toaster $15" with no lever is a T-20 worth $100–200 working (and they almost always work or need a $0 element clean). Also the model plate underneath – sellers never photograph it.

## Shipping / handling
4–6 lb, $15–22 each way. eBay (13%). Test before listing; "works, lowers automatically" doubles the price.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Sunbeam Radiant Control (no lever) under $40; deco Toastmaster/GE under $20. Pass when: any lever toaster over $20, untested lots at $35+ each, or shipping-heavy lots with no Sunbeam. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
