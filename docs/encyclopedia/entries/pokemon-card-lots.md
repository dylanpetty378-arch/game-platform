Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: sweep listings 2026-10 (XY-era binder 200+ cards $125 Klamath Falls; "Pokemon cards and collection" $175 Williamsport; "Pokemon cards OLD ONES" $1 St Louis; "Pokemon cards" $1 Atlanta); TCGplayer/PriceCharting market knowledge 2025–26.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Binder pages or stacks. What sets era and value:
- **Card back**: all eras share the blue Pokéball back; **1999–2000 Base/Jungle/Fossil** have no set symbol (Base) or a Jungle/Fossil symbol, thick black border bottom text "©1995, 96, 98 Nintendo". **Shadowless / 1st Edition** stamps (left of the art box) on Base Set = the money cards.
- **Era by bottom-left/right symbols**: e-Reader bar (2002–03), EX era (2003–07) with "ex" cards, Diamond & Pearl (2007–10), HGSS/Black & White (2010–13), **XY (2014–16)** – "XY" set symbols, full-art EX and "Break" cards, Sun & Moon (2017–19) GX, Sword & Shield (2020–22) V/VMAX, Scarlet & Violet (2023+) ex.
- **Value lives in**: holo/reverse-holo rares, full-art, secret rares (card number higher than the set size, e.g., 110/108), "Rainbow", Gold, alt-art, and any **Charizard**. Commons/uncommons in bulk: $0.02–0.10 each.
- **Japanese cards** and sealed packs/boxes: separate, often higher.

## What sellers usually miss
Parents/kids list by count ("500 cards $50") not by the rare cards. Look for: 1st Edition stamp, shadowless (no drop shadow right of the art box), Charizard, secret-rare numbering, full-art textured cards. Ask for close-ups of holos only.

## Shipping / handling
Binders 2–4 lb ($12–18); singles in a bubble mailer $4. Sell singles on eBay/TCGplayer (13%/10%); bulk to local card shops.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a lot ≤ $100 shows ≥ $250 of identifiable singles (Charizards, 1st Ed, secret rares) in close-up photos. Pass when: no close-ups of rares, 2010s+ binders priced at $100+, "mystery lot". Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
