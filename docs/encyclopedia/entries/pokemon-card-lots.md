## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Binder pages or stacks. What sets era and value:
- **Card back**: all eras share the blue Pokéball back; **1999–2000 Base/Jungle/Fossil** have no set symbol (Base) or a Jungle/Fossil symbol, thick black border bottom text "©1995, 96, 98 Nintendo". **Shadowless / 1st Edition** stamps (left of the art box) on Base Set = the money cards. (Note: English cards of this era have yellow borders; the "thick black border" refers to the art-box frame and text on shadowless prints — general knowledge, verify.)
- **Era by bottom-left/right symbols**: e-Reader bar (2002–03), EX era (2003–07) with "ex" cards, Diamond & Pearl (2007–10), HGSS/Black & White (2010–13), **XY (2014–16)** – "XY" set symbols, full-art EX and "Break" cards, Sun & Moon (2017–19) GX, Sword & Shield (2020–22) V/VMAX, Scarlet & Violet (2023+) ex.
- **Value lives in**: holo/reverse-holo rares, full-art, secret rares (card number higher than the set size, e.g., 110/108), "Rainbow", Gold, alt-art, and any **Charizard**. Commons/uncommons in bulk: $0.02–0.10 each.
- **Japanese cards** and sealed packs/boxes: separate, often higher.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Wizards of the Coast era (1999–2003)**: Base Set (1st Edition, shadowless, unlimited), Jungle, Fossil, Team Rocket, Gym, Neo, e-Reader sets. 1st Edition and shadowless prints carry the premium.
- **Nintendo / The Pokémon Company era (2003+)**: EX, Diamond & Pearl, HGSS, Black & White, XY (incl. Evolutions, 2016 Base Set reprint set), Sun & Moon (incl. Hidden Fates), Sword & Shield, Scarlet & Violet.
- **Money variants**: 1st Ed Shadowless Charizard ($5k–$300k graded), 1st Ed Base holos, Gold Star cards, Crystal cards, SM Hidden Fates Shiny Charizard GX, alt-art Umbreon VMAX; XY/SM full-art Charizards and Evolutions Charizard (collectible).
- **Japanese** cards and **sealed product** — separate, often higher.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Holo foil, reverse holo, textured full-art and gold cards. Shadowless = no drop shadow right of the art box. 1st Edition stamp left of the art box. Secret-rare numbering (e.g. 110/108). Card stock: genuine cards have a dark inner layer visible at the edge and consistent print (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Whitening on edges and corners, creases, scratches on holos, bends, water damage, binder-ring dents, sun fading. Condition drives everything for vintage singles; graded (PSA/BGS/CGC) prices are multiples of raw (general knowledge, verify). Ask for close-ups of holos front and back.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake cards are very common (Chinese counterfeits): wrong font, washed-out or overly glossy colours, wrong back blue, no dark inner layer (rip/light test), misspellings, wrong energy symbols (general knowledge, verify). Fake graded slabs exist for top cards. Reprints: 2016 Evolutions reprinted Base Set art (with the Evolutions set symbol); Celebrations (2021) reprinted classic cards (general knowledge, verify). Altered/fake 1st Edition stamps.

## Market
Enormous, fast, data-rich market (TCGplayer, PriceCharting, eBay sold); buyers are collectors, players, investors and card shops (general knowledge, verify). Prices are volatile and hype-driven; singles sell within days, bulk sells to local shops at low per-card rates. Holiday season and new-set releases move demand (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Parents/kids list by count ("500 cards $50") not by the rare cards. Look for: 1st Edition stamp, shadowless (no drop shadow right of the art box), Charizard, secret-rare numbering, full-art textured cards. Ask for close-ups of holos only.

## Shipping / handling
Binders 2–4 lb ($12–18); singles in a bubble mailer $4 (penny sleeve + top loader). Sell singles on eBay/TCGplayer (13%/10%); bulk to local card shops.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Sell valuable cards as singles: name, set, card number, edition (1st/shadowless/unlimited), holo type, condition (or grade), language. Photograph front and back, corners and holo surface in good light. Bulk: state count, eras, and that rares were pulled (or not). Title keywords: "Pokemon Charizard 4/102 Base Set Shadowless Holo", "Pokemon card lot 500 bulk with holos".

## Buy rule
Buy when: a lot ≤ $100 shows ≥ $250 of identifiable singles (Charizards, 1st Ed, secret rares) in close-up photos. Pass when: no close-ups of rares, 2010s+ binders priced at $100+, "mystery lot". Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (it does).
