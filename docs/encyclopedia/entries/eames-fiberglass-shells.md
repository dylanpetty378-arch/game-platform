## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Moulded fibreglass-reinforced polyester seat shell designed by Charles and Ray Eames, made for Herman Miller from 1950 (general knowledge, verify). Side shell (no arms) or arm shell; visible glass fibres in the surface; rubber shock mounts glued to the underside to take a base. Bases: Eiffel wire (DSR/DAR), dowel leg (DSW/DAW), X-base (DSX/DAX), rocker (RAR), H-base, stacking/tandem. Underside marks: early shells may carry a Zenith Plastics stamp or Herman Miller embossed logo/label; later shells have a moulded Herman Miller logo and sometimes a date (general knowledge, verify). Sellers' titles: "Eames chair", "mid century fiberglass chair", "Herman Miller shell".
Look-alikes: 2000s+ polypropylene Herman Miller shells (smooth plastic, no fibres) and countless fibreglass-look knockoffs.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Zenith Plastics** (1st production, c. 1950–53): **rope edge** (a wire rod moulded into the rim) on early arm shells – most valuable, $250–450 per the earlier sweep summary.
- **Herman Miller** later fibreglass (1950s–1980s, reissued 2014–2020s): $120–250.
- **Modernica** (licensed/contested fibreglass shells, 2000s): mid-value (general knowledge, verify).
- **Knockoffs / polypropylene look-alikes**: $20–60.
- Colours: early greige, parchment, elephant grey, sea foam, red-orange, yellow; rare colours bring more (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Real vintage: visible random glass-fibre strands under the gelcoat, slightly irregular thickness, shock mounts with age, Herman Miller or Zenith marks. Knockoffs: smooth uniform plastic, screws straight into the shell, no marks, wrong proportions (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Sheared or missing shock mounts (common; re-glue with new mounts is accepted), cracks around mounts, fibreglass "bloom" (fibres poking out), fading, stains, chips on the rim. Rope-edge damage and cracks hurt most. Original vs replaced base matters less than the shell (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Many unmarked fibreglass knockoffs; Herman Miller reissued fibreglass shells (2014+) and Modernica made them in the 2000s, so a mark doesn't always mean vintage (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Strong mid-century modern demand among decorators and collectors; sells best locally or via Chairish/1stdibs/eBay local pickup because shipping is bulky (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
unknown – likely the Zenith/rope-edge distinction and the underside marks (general knowledge, verify).

## Shipping / handling
Shell alone ~8–12 lb, bulky box; complete chairs ship freight-like ($60–100+) (general knowledge, verify). Shells stack.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State maker, side/arm, colour, base type and whether original, shock-mount condition, marks on the underside, any cracks. Photograph the underside marks and mounts. Title: "Herman Miller Eames fiberglass [side/arm] shell chair, [colour], [base]" (general knowledge, verify).

## Buy rule
Buy marked Herman Miller/Zenith under $100 only if it fits the car. Pass knockoffs and cracked shells. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. A single shell or side chair fits a passenger seat; a complete arm chair on a rocker or bigger multi-chair sets may not – check before buying.
