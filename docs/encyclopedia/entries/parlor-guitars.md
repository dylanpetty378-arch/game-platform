## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Small-bodied flat-top acoustic (lower bout ~12–13.5"), slotted or solid headstock, often ladder-braced. 1930s cheap catalog versions have **stencilled or painted decorations** (Hawaiian palms/flamingos, cowboys, Spanish dancers), birch or mahogany tops, painted or stained finish, wooden bridge with pins or tailpiece. Over 36–38" long — fits a passenger seat.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Harmony** (Chicago, 1892–1975): made most Sears Supertone (1914–1941) and Silvertone (1941+) guitars; model numbers stamped inside (e.g. "H1270", date codes like "F-63").
- **Regal** (Chicago), **Kay** (Chicago), **Oahu** (Cleveland, Hawaiian-style), **Gene Autry/Roy Rogers** cowboy stencil guitars (Harmony).
- **Stella / Oscar Schmidt** (Jersey City, 1899–1939): 12-string and parlor guitars favored by blues players — $500–3,000.
- **Washburn / Lyon & Healy**, **Bay State**, **Bruno**: 1880s–1920s, often Brazilian rosewood back/sides, $400–2,000.
- **Martin** (size 0, 1, 2-17), **Gibson L-00/L-1** — high value, need authentication.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Solid wood tops (grain continues across) vs plywood; ladder bracing in 1930s; tells of quality: bound edges, rosewood fretboard, ebony bridge, tuners with ivoroid buttons, labels/stamps visible through the soundhole. Stencils on unfinished birch = budget; still collectible.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Top cracks, lifting bridges, neck pulled forward (needs neck reset — expensive), warped necks, high action, loose braces, missing tuners. Neck resets usually exceed value on stencil guitars — "wall hanger" condition halves price.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern reissue stencil guitars (Recording King, Gretsch, Loar) and Chinese "vintage-look" parlors. Tell by truss-rod covers, modern tuners, laser-cut stencils.

## Market
Steady vintage-acoustic interest; stencil guitars are decor + player buys, weeks to sell. Shipping a guitar box ($40–70) eats margin; local sale (Reverb local pickup, Facebook) helps.

## What sellers usually miss
Stella/Oscar Schmidt labels or stamps inside, Brazilian rosewood backs, original cases.

## Shipping / handling
Guitar box 42×17×6", 8–12 lb boxed: $40–70 to buyer; fragile (headstock breaks). Passenger-seat OK.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Model/date stamps, measurements, playability (action at 12th fret), cracks, neck angle, repairs, tuners original.
