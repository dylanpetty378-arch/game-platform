# West Point cadet uniforms (full-dress coats, 1900s–1950s)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: napoleonexhibit.com/eBay 1937 named cadet tailcoat (damaged, asked $74), milivault.com Cadet Store–tailored officer coat (sold $295), westpoint.edu uniform history

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Cadet grey wool tailcoat ("full dress" / "dress coat with tails") with three rows of brass acorn/eagle buttons, black mohair braid frogging across the chest, standing collar, long tails with braid and buttons at the back. Inside a cloth label: "CADET STORE, WEST POINT" with No., date, name and maker lines, often in ink. Plebe/upperclass chevrons on the sleeve if not removed. Also seen: grey wool "dress gray" short jackets, overcoats with capes, white summer blouses – all worth less than the tailcoat.

## What sellers usually miss
The Cadet Store label date and name (lets a buyer research the cadet – a later general or WWII casualty doubles the price). Sellers list under "costume" or "military jacket" at thrift prices. Photograph the label.

## Shipping / handling
Wool coat ~3 lb, $12–18 to ship. Sell on eBay militaria (13%). Check for moth holes in every photo.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a complete tailcoat (all buttons, braid intact, label legible) is under $75. Pass when: buttons missing or moth damage visible, or price already at $150. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
