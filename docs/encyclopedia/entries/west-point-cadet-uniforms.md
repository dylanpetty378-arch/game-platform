## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Cadet grey wool tailcoat ("full dress" / "dress coat with tails") with three rows of brass acorn/eagle buttons, black
mohair braid frogging across the chest, standing collar, long tails with braid and buttons at the back. Inside a cloth
label: "CADET STORE, WEST POINT" with No., date, name and maker lines, often in ink. Plebe/upperclass chevrons on the
sleeve if not removed. Also seen: grey wool "dress gray" short jackets, overcoats with capes, white summer blouses – all
worth less than the tailcoat. Look-alikes: VMI, Citadel and military-school/band coats use similar grey with different
buttons (state seals, school buttons) (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Cadet Store, West Point** (USMA's own tailoring shop): label with number, date and cadet name — the key variant.
- Officer dress coats tailored at the Cadet Store (e.g. M1902) are a related collectible.
- Variants by garment: full-dress tailcoat (best), dress gray jacket, overcoat with cape, white summer blouse.
- By era: 19th-century coatees (top), pre-WWII (collectible), 1940s–50s (decent), 1960s–90s (common).
- Buttons by Waterbury, Scovill or Horstmann may be backmarked (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Heavy cadet-grey wool, black mohair braid, gilt brass ball/acorn/eagle buttons (count them — three rows on the chest
plus tails), silk or cotton lining. Hand-inked label with date and name is the provenance tell. Older coats have
hand-finished buttonholes and period labels (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Moth holes (check every photo), missing buttons (big value hit), frayed or detached braid, stained linings, removed or
faded labels, alterations. Replacement buttons that don't match lower value. Ask for photos of the label, both sides of
the chest, the tails and the lining.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Costume and theatrical reproductions exist; they lack the Cadet Store label and use lighter wool and stamped buttons.
Labels can be transferred, so check that wear on the label matches the coat (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Militaria collectors, West Point alumni and families, and theatrical/display buyers. Sells on eBay militaria, milivault and
militaria dealers; named, researched coats sell best. Graduation season (May) and alumni events may lift demand
(general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
The Cadet Store label date and name (lets a buyer research the cadet – a later general or WWII casualty doubles the
price). Sellers list under "costume" or "military jacket" at thrift prices. Photograph the label.

## Shipping / handling
Wool coat ~3 lb, $12–18 to ship. Sell on eBay militaria (13%). Check for moth holes in every photo.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State garment type, era, label text (number, date, name), research on the named cadet (class year, career), button
count and condition, braid condition, moth holes, measurements (chest, sleeve, length). Photograph label, front, back
tails, buttons and any damage. Title: "West Point cadet full dress coat", year, "named", "Cadet Store", "USMA".

## Buy rule
Buy when: a complete tailcoat (all buttons, braid intact, label legible) is under $75. Pass when: buttons missing or moth
damage visible, or price already at $150. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Must
fit a passenger seat.
