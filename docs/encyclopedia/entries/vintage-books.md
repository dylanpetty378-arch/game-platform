## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Any pre-1990 book. Value depends on title, edition (first printing), dust jacket and condition — almost never age. A 1890 Bible is usually $10; a 1970 paperback can be $100.
Photo tells:
- **Dust jacket** present, with the price on the front flap (not clipped) — required for most modern firsts.
- **Copyright page** — "First Edition", "First Printing", a number line ending in "1", or no later-printing statement. Ask for this photo every time.
- **Book-club editions (BCE)**: smaller, thinner boards, no price on jacket flap, "Book Club Edition" on flap, a blind-stamped dot or square on the back board. Worth little.
- **Children's illustrated**: famous illustrators (N.C. Wyeth, Jessie Willcox Smith, Arthur Rackham, Garth Williams, Leonard Weisgard, Maurice Sendak, Tasha Tudor, Eloise Wilkin).
- **Counterculture/cookbooks**: Whole Earth Catalog, Tassajara Bread Book (Shambhala 1970), Moosewood (1974 hand-lettered first), Alice's Restaurant, early Rodale.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Shambhala Publications** (Berkeley, 1969–): Tassajara Bread Book by Edward Espe Brown (1970), kraft-brown paper cover with hand-lettered title and sunflower/bread drawing. Later printings keep a similar cover; first printing must be confirmed on the copyright page. Estimate first printing $40–120, later 1970s printings $8–20 (general knowledge, verify).
- **World Publishing "Rainbow Classics"** (Cleveland, 1940s–50s): illustrated classics (Heidi illustrated by Leonard Weisgard 1946, Little Women by Louis Jambor, etc.) with intro by May Lamberton Becker. $8–25 with jacket.
- **Grosset & Dunlap**: Nancy Drew, Hardy Boys, Tom Swift. Early tweed or blue-cloth editions with matching jackets $20–150; 1960s+ picture covers $3–8.
- **Little Golden Books** (Simon & Schuster 1942–): first printing marked "A" on the last page or spine; early titles with jackets $20–200.
- **Reilly & Lee** Oz books, **Whitman Big Little Books** (1932–), **Scribner Illustrated Classics** (N.C. Wyeth), **Heritage Press / Limited Editions Club** (slipcased, LEC signed by illustrators $50–1,000).
- **Modern firsts**: Hemingway, Fitzgerald, Steinbeck, Salinger, Tolkien, Harper Lee, Vonnegut, King (early). Only with jacket and correct points.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- First-edition statements differ by publisher (e.g., Random House "First Edition" until 1970s; Scribner "A" code 1930–70s; Knopf "First Edition"). Check a points guide for the publisher.
- Jacket price and the "points" (typos, ads, binding colour) separate firsts from early reprints.
- Library discards (stamps, pockets, barcodes) cut value 50–90%.
- Cloth vs paper boards, gilt titles, illustrated endpapers, tipped-in colour plates (count them — missing plates kill value).

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Foxing, water stains, mould (smell), loose or detached hinges, missing pages/plates, writing (pen scribbles on the cover of our Tassajara example), torn or chipped jackets, price-clipped jackets, sun-faded spines.
- Repairs: tape on jackets or hinges (bad), rebacked spines, facsimile jackets (must be disclosed; often sold as originals).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Facsimile dust jackets on real firsts, and book-club copies with a real-looking jacket.
- Publisher facsimile editions (e.g., Hemingway, Tolkien, Little Golden Books 50th anniversary reprints with "A" in the code).
- Forged signatures on modern firsts — signed copies of famous authors need provenance or a reputable dealer's guarantee (outside Dylan's lane).

## Market
Huge, slow market for most books; values are compressed by AbeBooks supply. Specific titles sell quickly when underpriced. eBay works for illustrated/collectible; AbeBooks for firsts. Media Mail shipping makes books cheap to move.

## What sellers usually miss
- The copyright page: sellers price on age ("from 1946!") and miss that the valuable one is the 1970s paperback first printing.
- Original jackets on series books; "A" printings of Little Golden Books.
- Ephemeral counterculture print (Whole Earth, zines, small-press poetry, early Shambhala/City Lights).
- Signed/inscribed copies with the author's signature on the half-title.

## Shipping / handling
Media Mail $4–8 for 1–2 lb; padded mailer or box with board stiffeners; wrap jackets in Mylar. Low damage risk if boxed.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Title, author, publisher, year, edition/printing with copyright-page photo, jacket state (price-clipped or not), size, and an honest condition list (hinges, foxing, writing). Use standard grades (Fine, Near Fine, Very Good, Good).

## Buy rule
Profit ≥ max($50, price) after 13% fees and ~$5 shipping each way means the book must sell for ~$75+ at a $10 buy. Buy only with a copyright-page photo proving the valuable printing (or signed), condition at least Very Good, at ≤ 30% of sold. Pass on "old" with no title value. Signed copies of famous authors need professional authentication — pass unless dirt cheap and the inscription is to a traceable person.
