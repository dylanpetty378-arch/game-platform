## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Scotty Cameron: "Titleist" on the sole, "Scotty Cameron" script, milled face, Newport/Studio Style – used $200–450, Circle T tour $1,500+. Ping Anser/Zing (1970s–80s, "Scottsdale" or "Karsten Mfg" on the sole – Scottsdale Ansers $300–1,000). Bettinardi: honeycomb-milled face, $150–350. Sacks Parente: modern ultra-low-balance putters, model names "Series 39/91 The Duke", $150–300 used. Bobby Grace/Cobra HSM: 1990s, $40–80. Hickory-shaft antiques: "Hand Forged in Scotland", George Nicoll Leven (hand mark), Tom Stewart (pipe mark) – $50–150, $300+ for rare marks. Sellers list model names most of the time.
See also `golf-clubs`, which gives Scotty Cameron $200–600 used and Ping Anser Scottsdale/Phoenix $150–800 with 1980s Karsten Ansers $40–80.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Scotty Cameron** (Titleist): Newport, Newport 2, Studio Style, Studio Stainless, Pro Platinum, Circa 62; Circle T / Tour = top.
- **Ping**: Anser and Zing; sole address dates them – Scottsdale and Phoenix (early, valuable) vs Karsten Mfg (later, common); Ping 1A and Redwood top; Ping Sigma G Kinloch $80–110.
- **Bettinardi**: honeycomb-milled faces, $150–350.
- **Sacks Parente**: Series 39/91 The Duke, retail $400–500, used $150–300.
- **Odyssey**: two-ball (junk), Toulon (decent).
- **TaylorMade Spider**: decent.
- **Bobby Grace / Cobra HSM** (1990s): $40–80.
- **Hickory**: George Nicoll Leven (hand mark), Tom Stewart (pipe mark), 1800s long-nose wood putters (top).
- **Junk**: Wilson, Ray Cook, Cobra, department-store, Gorilla.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Milled carbon or stainless steel (Cameron, Bettinardi) vs cast heads; Ping BeCu (copper) or manganese bronze vs steel; hickory shafts with hand-forged heads and cleek marks. Original grips, headcovers and the Cameron "Circle T" stamp add (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Bag chatter, dings, refinished heads (lower collector value), replaced grips/shafts, bent hosels. Ask for sole, face and shaft label photos (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit Scotty Cameron putters and fake Circle T/tour stamps are widespread; Ping has reissued vintage-style Ansers (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Liquid on eBay, GolfWRX classifieds and SidelineSwap; Scotty Cameron has a deep collector market. Spring peak (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Ping sole stampings (Scottsdale/Phoenix/Karsten Mfg = pre-1970s). Scotty Cameron Pro Platinum/Studio Stainless models under "old putter". Hickory antiques listed as "antique golf club" – check the cleek mark.
[Note: the original line groups "Karsten Mfg" with the pre-1970s stamps, but `golf-clubs` treats "Karsten Mfg" Ansers as the later, $40–80 ones. Generally Scottsdale and Phoenix stamps are the early ones and "Karsten Mfg" is later (general knowledge, verify).]

## Shipping / handling
2 lb, 40" box – $20–25. Resell on eBay/GolfWRX/Sidelineswap.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State maker, model, length, loft/lie if known, sole stamping, grip, headcover, RH/LH, any refinish. Photograph sole, face, top line, shaft band and headcover. Title: "Scotty Cameron [model] putter 34in RH w/ headcover".

## Buy rule
Buy when: Scotty Cameron ≤ $100, Ping Scottsdale ≤ $80, Sacks Parente ≤ $60, hickory Nicoll/Stewart ≤ $25. Pass when: seller names the model and asks within 30% of market. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Fits a passenger seat.
