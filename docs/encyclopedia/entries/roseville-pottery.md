# Roseville Pottery (Wisteria, Pine Cone, Futura, etc.)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: underpricedai.com sold-price aggregation for Wisteria 634-7 (median $158.75 over 62 sales; range $58–485; blue examples $300–460), antiquesandthearts.com auction reports, Kovels knowledge

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Ohio art pottery 1890s–1954. Matte, moulded floral or geometric relief on vases, jardinières, wall pockets. Marks: 1930s pieces carry only a foil/paper triangle sticker ("Roseville Pottery") or an impressed shape number; post-1936 pieces have raised "Roseville U.S.A." plus shape number (e.g. "634-7" = shape 634, 7" tall). Lines worth knowing: **Wisteria** (1933, bark-textured body with purple wisteria clusters; blue ground > brown), **Pine Cone** (pine needles and cones; blue > brown > green), **Futura** (Art Deco geometric), **Sunflower**, **Blackberry**, **Falline**, **Ferella**, **Baneda** – these are the money lines. Common lines (Zephyr Lily, Freesia, Magnolia, Clematis, Snowberry, Peony, Apple Blossom) are everywhere. Reproductions from China (1990s–2000s) copy Wisteria, Pine Cone, Futura: tells are a blurry/soft mould, heavy glossy glaze, wrong size, and "Roseville" raised mark with crude letters.

## What sellers usually miss
Line name and colour (blue vs brown Wisteria is 2–3×). Many list as "vintage pottery vase". The sticker/mark photo decides authenticity.

## Shipping / handling
2–4 lb, double-box with 2" foam all round: $20–30 insured. Resell on eBay (13%). Chips/hairlines cut value 50–70%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: blue Wisteria/Pine Cone/Futura under $120 with a clear mark photo; brown Wisteria under $60; any "unknown pottery vase" that matches a money line under $40. Pass when: seller names line + shape number and prices at eBay level; any repro tell. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
