## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Everything for drinks at home: shakers, ice buckets, decanters, glass sets, carafes, bar tools, trays. 1950s–60s "Mad Men" style is the heart of the collector market, with Art Deco chrome (1930s) at the top.
- **MCM glass sets:** 22k gold-decorated highballs and rocks glasses. Signatures in gold on the side: "Culver" (Culver Ltd.), "Georges Briard" (script), "Fred Press", "Cera" (Cera Glass), "Dorothy Thorpe" is unsigned but known by the sterling or silver band and sandblasted flowers. Full sets of 8 with a matching ice bucket or carafe bring the most.
- **Cocktail shakers:** Napier (Meriden CT) figural penguin ($300–1,000+), bell, and "Hour-Glass" shakers; West Bend penguin; Chase Brass & Copper and Revere Copper & Brass Art Deco chrome (marked with a centaur or the Revere logo); Kromex and Atomic-star aluminium; Hazel-Atlas recipe shakers with printed cocktail recipes on glass ($30–80).
- **Ice buckets:** Kromex aluminium, Georges Briard and Culver gold-decorated, Irvinware and Kraftware vinyl/woodgrain/plaid (1960s–70s), Ice Tub insulated, Tiffany & Co. and Baccarat crystal (acid-etched mark on the base), Dansk Quistgaard teak ($100–300).
- **Decanters:** Blenko and Holmegaard art glass, Kosta, Orrefors, cut crystal with a stopper, Jim Beam figurals (low).
- **Trays:** 12–13" round lithographed beer and soda trays. Pre-Prohibition and 1930s–40s trays with pictures sell high ($50–500). 1950s–70s logo trays (Schlitz Real Gusto, Hamm's, Falstaff) $10–40.
- **Novelty:** oversized "giant" martini, margarita and brandy glasses (1970s–2000s gag gifts and display pieces) $10–30.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Culver Ltd.** (1950s–80s): 22k gold patterns (Valencia, Tyrol, Scroll, Bristol Bay). Signed "Culver" on the side. Sets of 8: $60–150.
- **Georges Briard** (1950s–70s): gold-leaf and enamel glass, Pyrex-lined ice buckets, aluminium trays. Signed in gold script.
- **Dorothy Thorpe** (CA, 1950s–70s): silver-band (sterling overlay) highballs, sandblasted floral. Sets of 6–8 sell $30–75 (project deal-sweep, Sept 2026).
- **Fred Press**, **Cera**, **Libbey (Silver Leaf, Golden Foliage)**, **Federal Glass**, **Hazel-Atlas** (recipe shakers), **Anchor Hocking**: low to mid.
- **Napier Co.** (Meriden CT): penguin shaker (1936) $500–1,500; bell shaker $200–400; "Tells You How" recipe shaker $150–300. Stamped "Napier" and "Pat. Pend."
- **Chase Brass & Copper** (Waterbury CT, 1930s–42): centaur mark. Shakers, cocktail sets $100–400. **Revere** (Rome NY): Norman Bel Geddes "Manhattan" set $500–1,500.
- **Kromex** (Cleveland, 1950s–60s): spun-aluminium ice buckets $20–50.
- **Irvinware** (Irwin Corp., NY, 1960s–70s): vinyl-wrapped plastic ice buckets, woodgrain, plaid, butcher-block. $15–35.
- **Tiffany & Co. crystal** (1980s–present): ice buckets and wine coolers, acid-etched "TIFFANY & CO." script on the underside, thick clean lead-free crystal. Resale $80–200 (estimate). Heavy look-alikes (Crate & Barrel, Global Views, generic Polish and Romanian) are unmarked.
- **Breweries:** Schlitz (Milwaukee) "Real Gusto in a Great Light Beer" campaign 1961–68 trays. Hamm's bear, Falstaff, Pabst Blue Ribbon, Ballantine.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Gold decoration: 22k gold on Culver and Briard is bright and slightly raised, with a signature. Cheap pieces have thin gold that wears to bare glass.
- Crystal: real lead crystal or fine crystal rings when tapped, is heavy, and has a cut or polished base. The maker's acid etch is on the underside. Pressed soda-lime glass has mould seams and a dull ring.
- Chrome: Chase and Revere are heavy chrome on brass/copper with crisp stamping. Later chrome-plated steel rusts.
- Trays: original lithography with crisp printing and a rolled rim. Reproduction trays are thin and very glossy.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Gold and silver wear from dishwashers (the most common value-killer on MCM glass), chips on rims, cloudiness ("sick glass") that can't be cleaned.
- Shakers: dents, missing strainer or cap, pitted chrome, worn printed recipes.
- Ice buckets: cracked vinyl, missing liner or lid knob, rusted lid hardware, water rings on teak.
- Trays: rust, scratches through the litho, dents. Most beer trays survive in poor condition.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Coca-Cola and pre-Prohibition beer trays are widely reproduced (thin steel, bright, a modern copyright line).
- Napier penguin shakers have been reproduced. An original has the Napier mark under the beak flap and a heavy silver-plate body.
- Tiffany-style crystal is everywhere. Only the acid-etched base mark (plus quality) counts. Pieces sold "Tiffany style" are not Tiffany.
- Mass-retail "MCM-style" gold-rim glasses (2010s, Target/World Market) are unsigned and current.

## Market
A large, steady market. MCM barware and gold glass sell well to décor buyers on eBay and Etsy, mostly as sets. Singles and generic glass are slow. Breweriana trays have a collector base, but 1960s+ trays are abundant. There is a holiday bump in Nov–Dec (bar carts, gifts) and a smaller one before summer.

## What sellers usually miss
- **Small gold signatures** ("Culver", "Georges Briard") on glasses listed as "gold rim glasses".
- **Acid-etched base marks** on crystal ice buckets and decanters (Tiffany & Co., Baccarat, Orrefors, Steuben). Sellers photograph the side, not the base.
- **Napier marks** on silver-plated figural shakers listed as "silver bell".
- **Chase centaur** on Art Deco chrome.
- The reverse: sellers inflate woodgrain vinyl ice buckets and 1960s beer trays.

## Shipping / handling
- Glass sets: heavy and fragile, 4–8 lb, double-box, $18–30.
- Ice buckets: 2–5 lb, $14–22. Crystal ice buckets need double-boxing.
- Shakers: 1–2 lb, $10–14.
- Trays: flat 13" round, 1 lb, $10–14 in a 14x14 box.
- Giant glasses: tall, fragile, $18–25.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- Lead with the maker and the signature photo, the count of pieces, the dimensions, and the pattern name if known.
- Show gold and silver wear under raking light. Say "hand-washed only" for decorated glass.
- For crystal, include a base photo showing the etched mark, and the weight.
- For trays, give the brewery, the campaign or slogan and the era ("Schlitz Real Gusto campaign, 1960s"), and show the rim.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold median × 0.87 − shipping to buyer − shipping to Dylan − price, and it must be ≥ max($50, price). Glass and crystal ship at $18–30 each way, so the resale needs to be at least about $110–140 on a cheap buy. Buy only:
- an acid-etched Tiffany, Baccarat or Steuben ice bucket or decanter, with the mark confirmed in a base photo, at ≤ $35
- a Napier figural shaker at ≤ $60
- Chase or Revere chrome at ≤ $40
- a complete Culver or Briard set of 8 with an ice bucket or carafe at ≤ $25

Pass on beer trays from the 1960s on, vinyl and woodgrain ice buckets, unsigned glass, novelty giant glasses and anything "Tiffany style". No authentication service needed, but the mark must be visible before paying.
