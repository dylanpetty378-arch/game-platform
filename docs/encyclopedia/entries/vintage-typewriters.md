## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Portables in cases are the market: Royal Quiet De Luxe (1939–59, $100–200; 1950s colour versions $250–400),
Smith-Corona Silent/Sterling/Clipper ($80–150), Olympia SM3/SM4/SM9 ($150–300), Hermes 3000 seafoam ($300–500), Hermes
Baby/Rocket ($150–250), Olivetti Lettera 22/32 ($120–200), Royal Model P/O 1930s ($100–200, colour $300+). Desktop
standards (Underwood 5, Royal 10/KMM, Remington 16) are 30 lb, $40–100 unless 1920s glass-sided Royal 10 ($150–250).
Painted-over or spray-bombed machines are worth $20. Sellers write "antique typewriter", "vintage typewriter", "works".
The serial number (under the carriage or on the frame) dates the machine via typewriterdatabase.com.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Royal**: Quiet De Luxe (1939–59; colours 1950s), Model P/O (1930s), Arrow; desktop Royal 10 (glass sides 1920s),
  KMM.
- **Smith-Corona / L.C. Smith / Corona**: Silent, Sterling, Clipper, Skyriter; Corona 3 folding (1912–), top.
- **Olympia**: SM3/SM4/SM9 portables; SG1/SG3 desktops.
- **Hermes** (Paillard, Switzerland): Baby/Rocket, 3000 (seafoam is the prized colour).
- **Olivetti**: Lettera 22/32, Valentine (red, design icon) (general knowledge, verify Valentine values).
- **Underwood**: No. 5 standard, Universal/Champion portables.
- **Remington**: No. 12/16 standards, Remington Portable; early Remington No. 2 (1880s) top
  (general knowledge, verify).
- **Early/rare**: Blickensderfer, Oliver (1890s), index typewriters.
- Script/cursive typefaces add 50–100%.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Steel frames with enamel/crinkle paint, glass keytops with chrome rings (older) or plastic keys (later). Original
decals intact; colour paint must be factory (Royal colours, Hermes seafoam). Repainting erases decals — "no brand seen"
usually means a decal lost to repainting. Smooth carriage travel and crisp type are quality tells.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Sticky/frozen segments and keys (often just dried oil), worn platen (hard, cracked), dead ribbon, broken drawband,
missing feet, cracked case, rust, mouse damage in case. Missing keys or broken type slugs are hard to fix. Ask for a type
sample and a photo of the serial.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Few fakes; repainted machines sold as "colour" and Frankenstein machines (parts swapped) are the risk. Modern
decorative "typewriter-look" items are not typewriters (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Writers, decorators and a strong typewriter-collector community ("typosphere"); portables sell steadily on eBay and
Etsy, standards mainly locally. Serviced/cleaned machines sell for 1.5–2x untouched ones (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Model and colour (colour = 2–3× value); "no brand seen" usually means a decal lost to repainting. Script/cursive typeface
machines bring 50–100% more – ask for a type sample.

## Shipping / handling
Portable in case 15–20 lb, $30–40, lock the carriage and wrap the case. Standards 30+ lb – local only.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State maker, model, year (from serial), typeface (pica/elite/script, with a type sample photo), colour, working status
(all keys, carriage advance, bell, ribbon), case condition. Title: maker, model, year, colour, "manual portable
typewriter", "working", typeface.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Olympia SM/Hermes/colour Royal ≤ $60, Royal QDL/Smith-Corona ≤ $35, with a type sample. Pass when: standards
(freight), repainted, seller quoting "worth $100 online". Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping
both ways. Must fit a passenger seat.
