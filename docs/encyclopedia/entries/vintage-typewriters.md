# Vintage typewriters (Royal, Underwood, Smith-Corona, Olympia, Hermes)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge 2024–25; typewriterdatabase.com; craigslist phoenix listing body (Royal Quiet De Luxe "over $100 online").

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Portables in cases are the market: Royal Quiet De Luxe (1939–59, $100–200; 1950s colour versions $250–400), Smith-Corona Silent/Sterling/Clipper ($80–150), Olympia SM3/SM4/SM9 ($150–300), Hermes 3000 seafoam ($300–500), Hermes Baby/Rocket ($150–250), Olivetti Lettera 22/32 ($120–200), Royal Model P/O 1930s ($100–200, colour $300+). Desktop standards (Underwood 5, Royal 10/KMM, Remington 16) are 30 lb, $40–100 unless 1920s glass-sided Royal 10 ($150–250). Painted-over or spray-bombed machines are worth $20. Sellers write "antique typewriter", "vintage typewriter", "works".

## What sellers usually miss
Model and colour (colour = 2–3× value); "no brand seen" usually means a decal lost to repainting. Script/cursive typeface machines bring 50–100% more – ask for a type sample.

## Shipping / handling
Portable in case 15–20 lb, $30–40, lock the carriage and wrap the case. Standards 30+ lb – local only.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Olympia SM/Hermes/colour Royal ≤ $60, Royal QDL/Smith-Corona ≤ $35, with a type sample. Pass when: standards (freight), repainted, seller quoting "worth $100 online". Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
