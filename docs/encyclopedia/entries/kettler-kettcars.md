# Kettler Kettcar pedal go-karts (and other German pedal cars)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay listings "Kettcar Original Pedal Race Car Kettler Made in Germany" (US listings typically $150–300 asking, local pickup/freight), Kettler shop (new Kettcar Evolution ~$250–450), Eames Institute (Kettcar as design object)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Steel-tube pedal kart with chain drive, hand-pull brake, adjustable bucket seat, pneumatic or solid rubber tyres, "KETTLER" / "Kettcar" decal on the nose or frame. Made in Germany 1962–2010s; newer Chinese-made Kettler Evolution/Daytona from 2016. Colour tells: 1970s–80s red/black and blue/yellow frames with chrome steering; 1990s "Kettcar Super" with roll bar. Also valuable: Berg (Netherlands) karts, Puky, 1950s–60s Murray/AMF steel pedal cars (different market, $150–500). Sellers' words: "pedal go kart", "Kettcar", "German pedal car".

## What sellers usually miss
"Made in Germany" vs the current Chinese-made line; 1970s–80s originals with chrome and the adjustable-seat rail; completeness (brake lever, chain guard, pedals).

## Shipping / handling
Heavy and bulky: 25–40 lb, ~40x24x24" box – $60–100 freight each way. This is a local-flip item; not a ship-in buy unless ≤ $40.

## Buy rule
Buy when: German-made original at ≤ $40 and the car fits a trip, or NOS/boxed 1970s–80s at ≤ $100. Pass when: $75+ (the usual Craigslist price) – profit never clears after freight. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
