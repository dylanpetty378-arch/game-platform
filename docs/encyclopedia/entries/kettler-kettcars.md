## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Steel-tube pedal kart with chain drive, hand-pull brake, adjustable bucket seat, pneumatic or solid rubber tyres, "KETTLER" / "Kettcar" decal on the nose or frame. Made in Germany 1962–2010s; newer Chinese-made Kettler Evolution/Daytona from 2016. Colour tells: 1970s–80s red/black and blue/yellow frames with chrome steering; 1990s "Kettcar Super" with roll bar. Also valuable: Berg (Netherlands) karts, Puky, 1950s–60s Murray/AMF steel pedal cars (different market, $150–500). Sellers' words: "pedal go kart", "Kettcar", "German pedal car". Typical size ~40–50" long × 24–28" wide (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Kettler** (Ense-Parsit, Germany) – Kettcar introduced 1962; German-made through the 2010s, then Chinese-made Evolution/Daytona lines (2016+).
- **Berg** (Netherlands) – large karts, strong resale in Europe.
- **Puky** (Germany) – smaller children's ride-ons.
- **Murray / AMF** (USA, 1950s–60s) – stamped-steel pedal cars, separate collector market ($150–500).
- Budget imports: Hauck, Costway – junk tier.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
German Kettcars: powder-coated steel tube, chrome steering wheel (older), chain drive with freewheel, adjustable-seat rail, quality moulded seat, "Made in Germany" decal. Chinese versions: lighter tubing, plastic-heavy. Old vs new: chrome parts and decal style on 1970s–80s; roll bar on 1990s Super. (general knowledge, verify)

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rust on frames, cracked seats, flat/perished tyres, missing brake lever, chain guard or pedals, bent axles, sun-faded plastic. Completeness (brake lever, chain guard, pedals) drives value.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No fakes; the risk is current Chinese-made Kettler models sold as "original German". (general knowledge, verify)

## Market
Local-pickup item bought by parents/grandparents; in the US, scarce and bought by people who grew up with them in Europe (military families). Spring/summer and pre-Christmas peaks. (general knowledge, verify)

## What sellers usually miss
"Made in Germany" vs the current Chinese-made line; 1970s–80s originals with chrome and the adjustable-seat rail; completeness (brake lever, chain guard, pedals).

## Shipping / handling
Heavy and bulky: 25–40 lb, ~40x24x24" box – $60–100 freight each way. This is a local-flip item; not a ship-in buy unless ≤ $40.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State model, decade, country of manufacture, tyre type, brake works, seat adjusts, rust, completeness. Photograph decals, frame, chain, brake, seat rail, tyres. Title keywords: "Kettler Kettcar Made in Germany pedal go kart", "vintage 1980s", "chain drive".

## Buy rule

Earlier rule (kept for reference): Buy when German-made original at ≤ $40 and the car fits a trip, or NOS/boxed 1970s–80s at ≤ $100.
