## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
A box with a speaker grille and a control panel.
- **Tube amps**: a back panel with glowing tubes visible and a heavier chassis. Fender (Deluxe Reverb, Princeton, Twin, Blues Jr, Hot Rod), Vox AC15/AC30, Marshall JCM/DSL, Mesa, Ampeg, Peavey Classic 30.
- **Solid-state/modelling**: Line 6 Spider (all generations), Fender Frontman/Mustang/Champion, Peavey Bandit/Rage/Vypyr, Marshall MG, Crate, Boss Katana, Orange Crush, Roland Cube, Blackstar ID.
- **Vintage solid-state exceptions worth money**: Roland JC-120 ($500–900), Fender Power Chorus / Princeton Chorus ($150–250), Peavey Bandit 80s "red stripe" ($120–200), Polytone Mini-Brute ($300–450), Lab Series L5 ($400–700), Music Man (hybrid, $600–1,200), Yamaha G100 ($150–250), Acoustic 360/370 bass ($800–2,000).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Fender**: blackface (1963–67) and silverface (1968–early 80s) Deluxe Reverb, Princeton and Twin are top tier, as are tweed-era amps (general knowledge on years, verify). Blues Jr and Hot Rod Deluxe are collectible ($350–700). Frontman, Mustang and Champion are solid-state; Mustang GTX is mid-tier. Power Chorus and Princeton Chorus are $150–250.
- **Vox**: AC15 collectible; AC30 top tier.
- **Marshall**: JCM800 and plexi top tier; DSL tube is mid; MG solid-state is junk tier.
- **Roland**: JC-120 $500–900; Cube decent.
- **Peavey**: Classic 30 (tube) collectible; 80s red-stripe Bandit $120–200; Rage and Vypyr junk tier.
- **Ampeg**: B-15 top tier.
- **Music Man**: hybrid, $600–1,200.
- **Polytone Mini-Brute** ($300–450), **Lab Series L5** ($400–700), **Yamaha G100** ($150–250), **Acoustic 360/370** bass ($800–2,000).
- **Line 6 Spider** (all generations) and Amplifi, Crate, Smarvo, Diago, no-name: junk tier. Spider IV 120W sells $100–150 at most.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Tube amps have a heavy steel chassis, transformers, visible tubes, and hand-wired or eyelet-board construction in vintage Fender.
- Original speakers (date-coded) and transformers add value. Replaced speakers and transformers lower collector value (general knowledge, verify).
- Fender date codes appear on the tube chart and on speakers and pots (general knowledge, verify).
- Solid-state amps are lighter, with PCB construction and digital modelling displays on modern units.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- "Needs work", "hum", "no sound" means pass.
- Common issues: dead filter caps, scratchy pots, blown speakers, torn grille cloth, broken handles, missing footswitches.
- Recaps on vintage tube amps are accepted. Non-original mods reduce collector value.
- Ask for a video of it playing at volume, back-panel photos (tubes, speaker), and the serial and tube chart.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fender and Vox reissue classic models (e.g. '65 Deluxe Reverb Reissue, AC30 reissues). Reissues are worth far less than originals, so check serials and construction (general knowledge, verify). Clones in old cabinets exist. "Vintage" Spider-era amps are not vintage.

## Market
Reverb is the market. Gigging musicians, hobbyists and vintage collectors buy there. Common solid-state amps are oversupplied and slow. Vintage tube amps sell fast at fair prices. Local sales avoid freight. The holidays and tax season give mild bumps (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
"Old Fender amp $150" with a silverface panel and tubes in the back photo. A "Roland amp" that is a JC-120. A "bass amp" that is an Acoustic 360 or Ampeg B-15. Solid-state is almost never a flip.

## Shipping / handling
Combos are 25–60 lb: $40–90 each way. Only local pickup or very high-value tube amps clear the rule. Reverb is the market. Large bass rigs (Acoustic 360/370 cabinets, 4x12 and 8x10 cabs) exceed passenger-seat size and are excluded.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Title: "[Year] [Brand] [Model] [tube/solid-state] [watts] [speaker config] Combo Amp Tested". State the serial and date codes, original or replaced speaker and transformers, service history (recap, tubes), working features (reverb, tremolo, channels), included footswitch and cover, and weight. Photograph the front panel, the back with tubes, the speaker codes and the serial plate, and post a sound clip.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a Fender blackface/silverface, Vox AC, Marshall tube, Roland JC-120, Music Man or Ampeg B-15 is under $250 and the photo shows it complete. Pass when: any solid-state practice/modelling amp, any amp that "needs repair"/"hum"/"no sound", or anything over 40 lb unless profit ≥ $300. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat; large bass cabinets and stacks are excluded.
