# Guitar & bass amplifiers (solid-state practice/modelling vs tube)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: Reverb/eBay sold knowledge, sweep listings 2026-10 (Line 6 Spider IV 120 $169, Spider III $40, Line 6 "Guitar Amp" $175, Fender Power Chorus $195, Smarvo Avenger 150 $125, Diago Little Smasher $70, Peavey PVI 10 $100, Peavey XR-600B $50, Line 6 Amplifi TT free, BP mini amp $100).

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Box with a speaker grille and a control panel. Tells: **tube amps** have a back panel with glowing tubes visible, heavier chassis, brands/models like Fender (Deluxe Reverb, Princeton, Twin, Blues Jr, Hot Rod), Vox AC15/AC30, Marshall JCM/DSL, Mesa, Ampeg, Peavey Classic 30. **Solid-state/modelling**: Line 6 Spider (all generations), Fender Frontman/Mustang/Champion, Peavey Bandit/Rage/Vypyr, Marshall MG, Crate, Boss Katana, Orange Crush, Roland Cube, Blackstar ID. Vintage solid-state exceptions worth money: **Roland JC-120** ($500–900), **Fender Power Chorus / Princeton Chorus** ($150–250), **Peavey Bandit** 80s "red stripe" ($120–200), **Polytone Mini-Brute** ($300–450), **Lab Series L5** ($400–700), **Music Man** (hybrid, $600–1,200), **Yamaha G100** ($150–250), **Acoustic 360/370** bass ($800–2,000).

## What sellers usually miss
"Old Fender amp $150" with a silverface panel and tubes in the back photo; "Roland amp" that is a JC-120; "bass amp" that is an Acoustic 360 or Ampeg B-15. Solid-state is almost never a flip.

## Shipping / handling
Combos are 25–60 lb: $40–90 each way. Only local pickup or very high-value tube amps clear the rule. Reverb is the market.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Fender blackface/silverface, Vox AC, Marshall tube, Roland JC-120, Music Man, Ampeg B-15 under $250 and photo shows it complete. Pass when: any solid-state practice/modelling amp, any amp "needs repair"/"hum"/"no sound", anything over 40 lb unless profit ≥ $300. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
