## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- Mass-produced American art and kitchen pottery. Glossy solid glazes (aqua, pink, yellow, green, white) on 1940s–50s pieces; matte green and brown Onyx on 1920s–30s stoneware.
- Marks: "McCoy" in block or script, "NM USA" (1930s–40s), "McCoy USA" with LCC (Lancaster Colony 1974+). Many unmarked.
- 1926 stoneware line (Nelson McCoy Sanitary & Stoneware): embossed pitchers like Buccaneer, Grape, Pine Cone, Indian; green or brown glaze; often marked shield/clover or unmarked.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Nelson McCoy (1910–1990):** majority of pieces.
- **J.W. McCoy / Brush-McCoy (1899–1925):** Loy-Nel-Art, Olympia, Rosewood — Arts & Crafts art pottery, higher value.
- **Lines:** Butterfly, Blossomtime, Leaf & Berries, Hobnail, Pine Cone, Sunburst gold, Floraline (low), Hobnail planters, Brocade; strap/ribbon vases (1948).
- **Cookie jars:** Mammy (reproduced), Hillbilly Bear, Clyde Dog, Kookie Kettle (common), Apple, Strawberry (common), W.C. Fields, Covered Wagon.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Crisp molds, clean glaze, matching colour inside/outside. Later LCC pieces lighter and duller.
- Glaze crazing normal on older pieces.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Chips on rims and handles, hairlines, crazing, restored rims (look for matte areas/blacklight).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- McCoy cookie jars (Mammy, Aunt Jemima, Hillbilly) have widespread repros, including fake "McCoy" marks (the trademark lapsed; reproductions in the 1990s marked "McCoy" by Mt. Clemens/Ohio). Smaller size and soft detail.
- Vases rarely reproduced.

## Market
- Huge supply; mid-century pieces sell slowly at modest prices. Jardinieres and pedestals are heavy and sell locally.

## What sellers usually miss
- Unmarked 1920s stoneware and Loy-Nel-Art pieces are sometimes unidentified.
- Most sellers overprice common planters using asking-price data.

## Shipping / handling
- Pottery: double box; 12" vase 5–7 lb boxed, $18–25. Pitchers 3–4 lb, $14–18.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- Height, colour name, mark photo, line name, crazing/chip disclosure.
