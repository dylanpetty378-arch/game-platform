## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Square printed scarves: carré 90 cm (Hermès), 70 cm, 50–55 cm neckerchiefs. Brand usually printed in the design or corner and on a woven or satin label. Coach scarves of the 2000s use the "COACH est. 1941" circles, "C" signature or op-art patterns on silk twill or silk/cotton, with a small black tab label — the example is exactly this (Coach "Op Art"/circle print, c. 2008–2010, ~50 cm).

## Makers, brands and variants
- Hermès: hand-rolled hems rolled toward the front, signature "Hermès Paris" and designer name, © mark; titles. Top value. Requires authentication.
- Salvatore Ferragamo, Gucci (horsebit, flora), Emilio Pucci (signed "Emilio" repeat), Chanel, YSL, Dior: $60–250.
- Vera Neumann (ladybug signature), Echo, Liberty of London: $15–60.
- Coach, Anne Klein, Liz Claiborne, Talbots: $10–40.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Silk twill feels heavy and crisp; hand-rolled hems are plump and stitched visibly; machine-hemmed flat edges indicate lower-end or fakes. Color registration sharp on genuine luxury pieces.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Holes, pulls, stains, fading, perfume smell. Dry-cleaning is not a repair you should plan on.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Hermès, Chanel, Gucci and Louis Vuitton scarves are heavily counterfeited — needs authentication; Coach fakes exist but are low value either way.

## Market
Huge supply; mid-tier sells in weeks, low-tier slow. Poshmark and eBay.

## What sellers usually miss
Vintage Pucci/Vera signatures on scarves in "silk scarf lots"; Ferragamo signature woven into the border.

## Shipping / handling
Flat envelope, ~$5.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Brand, size (measure), fiber tag, hem type, flaws.

## Buy rule
Profit ≥ max($50, price): mall brands fail. Hermès/Chanel need professional authentication — not for Dylan. Only underpriced Ferragamo/Pucci/Gucci lots.
