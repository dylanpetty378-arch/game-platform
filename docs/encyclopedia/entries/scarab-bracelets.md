# Egyptian-revival scarab bracelets (1950s–70s)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge (gold-filled scarab link bracelets), Etsy/Ruby Lane listing ranges

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Link bracelet with 6–8 oval carved "scarab" stones in different colours (carnelian, tiger eye, dyed green/blue chalcedony, onyx, aventurine) set in prong cups. Mid-century costume jewelry sold by the million (Krementz, Van Dell, Winard, Curtis, Hong Kong). Marks on the clasp or links: "1/20 12K GF" (gold-filled – most of them), "14K" (solid gold – the money ones), "925/sterling", or nothing (gold-tone base metal). Sellers write "vintage scarab bracelet", "Egyptian revival". Real Victorian faience or hardstone scarabs set in 14K/18K are a different, rare thing.

## What sellers usually miss
The karat mark on the clasp tongue. A "vintage scarab bracelet $40" is only interesting if the photo shows "14K" not "1/20 12K GF".

## Shipping / handling
