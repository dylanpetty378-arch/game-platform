## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Collectors buy helmets as display/decor (never for riding – EPS liners degrade after ~5 years). Era tells:
- **1950s–60s**: leather "pudding basin", Everoak, Cromwell (UK), Buco "Guardian" (Buco Products, Toledo OH), early Bell 500 (1954–) – fibreglass shell, sparse snaps, chin strap with D-rings, leather trim.
- **1966–1980**: Bell 500-TX open face (big Bell "BELL" decal), Bell Star (1966, first full-face), Bell R-T, Shoei open faces, Arai; metalflake paint finishes highly desirable.
- **1980s touring**: full-face with intercom cords for Gold Wing/Aspencade (Honda "GL" wing badge), Harley-Davidson "Tour Pak" branded; smoked visors, ear speakers.
- **Modern** (1990s+): DOT/Snell stickers with dates (Snell M90, M95, M2000...), EPS liners; dated sticker inside the comfort liner.

## Makers, brands and variants
Bell (Los Angeles 1954–), Buco (Toledo), McHal (UK/US), Everoak, Cromwell, Davida (UK), AGV (Italy 1947–), Nava (Italy), Shoei (Japan 1959–), Arai (Japan), Simpson (race helmets, Bandit full-face 1980s – $150–500), Nolan, Shark, KBC, HJC (low value). OEM branded: Honda (made by Shoei/Arai/Nolan), Harley-Davidson (Bell/HJC made), Yamaha, Kawasaki, Polaris/Arctic Cat (snowmobile).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Fibreglass shells with hand-painted or metalflake finishes, leather trim, cloth liners, original Bell "Toptex" label = desirable. ABS/polycarbonate shells and foam liners = lower. Look inside for maker labels, Snell sticker dates, size labels. The Honda GL helmet in the example is a production touring helmet (likely Shoei-made, polycarbonate or fibreglass).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Crumbling foam/liner, sticky deteriorated vinyl trim, cracked shells, scuffs, missing visor/snaps, faded decals. Re-lining or repaint destroys collector value.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Bell reissued the "Bell Star Classic" and "500-TX" (modern labels, DOT stickers, Chinese manufacture); repro Bell decals; modern "novelty" German/WWII-style helmets. Collector-grade Bell Stars need careful label checking.

## Market
Steady decor/collector demand for 1960s–70s Bell/Buco; slow, thin market for 1980s OEM touring helmets (many asks at $150–250, few sales). Sold as "display only".

## What sellers usually miss
Early Bell 500 and Star helmets in garages priced $20–40; Buco helmets with original snap visors; Bell "Magnum" racing helmets. Sellers over-price 1980s OEM helmets.

## Shipping / handling
Helmet box ~14×12×12", 4–5 lb dim-weight, $20–30. Fits passenger seat.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Always state "for display/collection only, not for riding". List maker, size, date sticker, liner condition, visor and accessories (intercom cord), and shell damage.

## Buy rule
Resale for 1980s OEM helmets ~$60–100; profit = 80 × 0.87 − $25 − $20 − price → negative at any non-trivial price. Pass. Buy pre-1975 Bell/Buco/Shoei with intact liners at ≤ $40 when conservative resale ≥ $150. Passenger-seat OK. No authentication needed except "racer used" claims – pass on those.
