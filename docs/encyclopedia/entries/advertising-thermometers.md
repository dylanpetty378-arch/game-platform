## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Vertical rectangular signs (9–39 in.) or round 12 in. dial thermometers with a glass tube or dial, printed with brand advertising. Originals: lithographed or embossed steel, porcelain enamel (glossy, chipping to black/blue steel), or glass-faced dials with metal rims; tube held by metal clips; often a small maker's line at the bottom edge ("Stout Sign Co. St. Louis", "Robertshaw", "Taylor").

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Stout Sign Co. (St. Louis)**, **Robertshaw-Fulton**, **Taylor Instrument**, **Springfield**: tin vertical thermometers 1940s–70s.
- **Pam Clock Co.**, **Ohio Thermometer Co. (Springfield OH)**: 12 in. round glass-dome dials (1950s–60s) — high value for Coke, Pepsi, Dr Pepper, 7Up.
- **Porcelain**: Prestone, Marvel Mystery Oil, Mail Pouch, Hills Bros (1915–50) — the big money.
- **Nostalgia repros**: Ande Rooney (porcelain-look repros 1990s+), unnamed 1980s–2000s plastic/thin-tin "gasoline filling station" types, Coca-Cola licensed 1990s.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original: heavy steel, natural rust at holes/edges, fading consistent with outdoor use, period phone numbers/addresses, maker imprint. Repro: plastic or thin stamped tin with perfect print, molded raised frames, "Made in USA/China" with tiny modern printing, generic slogans ("Clean Rest Rooms", "Gasoline Filling Station"), fake distress. Porcelain repros are thinner, too bright, with even fake chips.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken or separated mercury/spirit tubes (replaceable but buyers discount), rust-through, porcelain chips (especially at mounting holes), dent/bend, faded print. Repainted touch-ups lower value.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Very high repro risk — petroliana and Coke are the most faked advertising categories. Ande Rooney and "Old Time Signs" style repros flood the market.

## Market
Big, active collector category (petroliana + soda). Originals sell in days to weeks; repros sit.

## What sellers usually miss
Small-town dealer/feed-store thermometers have regional collectors; round dial thermometers are much more valuable than vertical tin.

## Shipping / handling
Vertical tin 1–3 lb, long box; dial thermometers need double box (glass dome). $10–20 to ship.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State original vs reproduction plainly, dimensions, maker imprint, tube intact and working.

## Buy rule
Profit = median × 0.87 − ship − ship − price. Repros: pass at any price over $5. Originals: buy if comp median ≥ $150 and price ≤ $50 (tin) or ≤ 1/4 comps (porcelain/dial). Ask for close-ups of the bottom edge imprint and mounting holes.
