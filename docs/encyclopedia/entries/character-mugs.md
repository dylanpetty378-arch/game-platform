## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- **Figural head mugs**: whole mug moulded as a face (Popeye with pipe, Santa, Garfield, Star Wars by California Originals 1977, Batman by Applause). Popeye 1980 King Features mug: flesh-coloured head, white sailor hat rim, brown pipe, "© 1980 King Features Syndicate" on base, made in Japan/Korea.
- **Printed licensed mugs**: straight cylinder with decal (Peanuts by Determined Productions, Garfield by Enesco, Disney).
- **Gift/stationery company mugs**: Potpourri Press (Greensboro NC), 1980s Korean stoneware with dated base stamps ("© 1987 Potpourri Press, Korea"); cobalt interior with floral/botanical decals; low value.
- **Arabia Moomin (Finland, 1990–)**: straight white porcelain mug with Moomin character illustration; seasonal and discontinued editions command $60–200.
- **Royal Doulton character jugs**: English ceramic heads (Old Charley, Sairey Gamp etc.), not drinking mugs but often called "mugs".

## Makers, brands and variants

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Vintage pieces have copyright dates and country of origin (Japan pre-1980 better than Korea/China). Crisp moulding and hand-painted details; reissues have glossy uniform glaze.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips on rims and protruding parts (pipes, ears), crazing, stained interiors; paint rubs on cold-painted figurals.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Popeye mugs were reissued in the 1990s–2000s (e.g. Vandor); Moomin mug counterfeits from China exist (check Arabia backstamp and year).
