## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hard hats: shell + suspension (webbing/headband). Valuable ones are **aluminum** (riveted brim, dull silver, often with "Hard Boiled" or "Aluminum" embossing — 1930s–60s) or **early fiberglass** (yellowish, visible fiber texture, 1940s–60s). Decals or painted lettering for oil companies (Humble, Texaco, Gulf, Shell), utilities, steel mills, unions, Hoover Dam/bridge projects add most value. Bullard's 1919 "Hard Boiled" hat (steamed canvas, glue, black paint) is the origin; aluminum versions 1938+, fiberglass 1940s+. MSA "Skullgard" (phenolic, brown, 1940s–60s) and "Comfo-Cap". McDonald T (B.F. McDonald Co., LA) aluminum ribbed hats, often with "T" logo.
Pith helmets: cork or pith core covered in khaki/white cloth with a puggaree band; leather sweatband; vent on top. Hawley (U.S., 1940s–present) made many lightweight fiber helmets for US military/WPA and later tourist use; "Vero" (Vero Beach) also. British/colonial (Christy's, Hawkes & Co., Army & Navy Stores) with officer insignia are the valuable ones.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **E.D. Bullard Co.** (SF, est. 1898): Hard Boiled (canvas 1919), aluminum (1938), fiberglass (1950s); model 502 and 1950s white/red. Stamp inside brim.
- **Mine Safety Appliances (MSA)**: Skullgard (Bakelite-type, brown), Comfo-Cap, V-Gard (1960s+ plastic, low value).
- **B.F. McDonald Co. "McDonald T"**: aluminum ribbed caps and hats (1940s–60s).
- **Willson Products** (Reading PA): aluminum/fiberglass hats, mining caps.
- **Jackson, Fibre-Metal, Cesco, Superior**: secondary.
- **Pith helmets**: Hawley Products (St. Charles, IL; USMC/Army WWII fiber "pith" helmets $40–120 with WWII markings), Vero, Christy's, Hawkes, Army & Navy Stores (British), French colonial "casque colonial".

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Aluminum: lightweight, dull gray, rivets, may have yellow/white paint over it. Early fiberglass: amber with visible fibers; later fiberglass uniform gloss. Plastic (polyethylene) = 1960s+ and low value. Original suspensions are leather/cloth-webbed; replacement plastic ratchet suspensions reduce value. Decals: water-slide (period) vs vinyl sticker (later).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Dents in aluminum, missing suspension, flaking paint, cracked fiberglass, rotten sweatbands on pith helmets, moth/insect damage to cork, crushed brims. Repainted hats lose value.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Decals applied later to plain hats to fake oilfield provenance; reproduction WWII pith helmets (Indian-made); modern Hawley-style "safari" helmets sold as vintage. Union/company decals are reproduced as stickers.

## Market
Steady; Americana/workwear and man-cave décor buyers; ~50–150 sales/month for vintage metal/fiberglass hard hats on eBay (estimate). Texas/oilfield decals sell well.

## What sellers usually miss
Aluminum vs plastic (they call all "old hard hat"); maker stamps inside the brim; small company decals; WWII stamp inside Hawley helmets.

## Shipping / handling
Bulky but light: 14×12×9 box, 2–3 lb, $12–18 Ground Advantage. Fits a passenger seat easily. Stuff with paper; don't crush brim.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker (Bullard Hard Boiled / MSA Skullgard / McDonald T / Hawley) + material (aluminum / fiberglass / cork) + "hard hat"/"pith helmet" + decals or company + era + model. Junk: "old hard hat", "safari costume", "man cave decor" alone. `Vintage Bullard Hard Boiled 502 Aluminum Hard Hat Oilfield Decals 1950s`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (Americana/workwear buyers; Texas oilfield decals sell well). No local premium.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (Bullard Hard Boiled ~$85; decaled $100–250), accept −10%, floor 80%; OBO; cut 15% after 30 days; plastic hats $10–35 firm or local.
- **Photos:** hat three-quarter on plain background; maker stamp inside the brim; each decal close up; suspension inside; crown top; dents and paint loss; WWII stamp inside Hawley helmets.
- **State:** maker, material, model stamp, each decal/company stencil, suspension included and original, dents/cracks by photo number, "collectible, not for safety use".
- **Pack / haul:** shoebox, 2–3 lb in a 14×12×9 box $12–18; stuff with paper, brim not crushed, no double-box.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Bullard [model] [aluminum] hard hat, [1950s] stamp inside the brim with original [company] decals; plastic hats go for [$] and decaled metal Bullards bring [$]."
