# Anamorphic projection lenses (Kowa, Sankor, Isco, Bell & Howell)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay listings (Kowa 16-H ask $1,190 item 235161786436; 16-D asks $300–500), kamerastore.com 16-D spec page, vintagelensesforvideo.com Kowa Vidoscope review + 2024/25 reader comments ($200 for a 16-H in 2024, $280 Vidoscope), seller statements in sweep listings.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Short, fat chrome-and-black cylinder (60–90 mm long) with a glass front, no aperture ring, a single focus ring, and a rear thread (43 mm on Kowa). Originally bolted to the front of 16 mm/35 mm cinema projectors to un-squeeze CinemaScope film; indie filmmakers now mount them on camera lenses for 2x "anamorphic" flares and oval bokeh. Titles usually say "projection lens", "anamorphic", "cinemascope", "Prominar". The model is engraved on the barrel: **Kowa Prominar 8-Z / 16-H** (same optics, the grail), **16-D** (smaller, 2x, cheaper), **16-S**, **Vidoscope**, **Bell & Howell Anamorphic** (rebadged Kowa 8-Z or 16-D), **Sankor 16C/16D/16F**, **Isco Ultra Star / Iscorama** (Iscorama 36/54 are $2–5k), **Schneider Cinelux**, **Elmoscope**, **Singer/Kollmorgen**.

## What sellers usually miss
Estate/garage sellers list these as "projector lens" or "movie projector part" for $10–40 because they don't know filmmakers want them. The model number on the barrel is everything: 8-Z/16-H = 3–4x the price of a 16-D. Check photos for the engraving and for clear glass (haze/fungus halves the price).

## Shipping / handling
Under 1 lb, small box, $10–15 either way. Sell on eBay (13%) or Reverb (~8%) – Reverb buyers are the filmmakers.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 8-Z / 16-H under $300; 16-D under $120; Iscorama at any price under $800 (then verify). Pass when: large theatre projector anamorphics (>1 kg), any visible haze, or a 16-D over $150 (profit can't reach max($50, price)). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
