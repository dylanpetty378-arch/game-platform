# Pocket transits (Brunton, K&E "Natural Sine")
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay listings via search (Brunton F-5008 new ~$400; vintage brass Brunton with box ~$150–250), oldtopographer.net; K&E values knowledge-based

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hinged square compass with a mirror in the lid, clinometer needle, and a peep sight – the "Brunton". Brunton (Wm. Ainsworth, Denver) in brass or black/green aluminum; Keuffel & Esser made a near-identical "Natural Sine" pocket transit in cream/white-painted aluminum with a black card. Leather belt case. Sellers call them "surveyor compass" or just "compass".

## What sellers usually miss
Maker (Ainsworth vs. Brunton Co. vs. K&E) and the brass vs. aluminum distinction. Brass + "Wm. Ainsworth & Sons" = top tier.

## Shipping / handling
