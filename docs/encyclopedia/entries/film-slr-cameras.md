## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Metal-bodied (chrome-and-leatherette or black) manual SLRs 1960s–80s with a pentaprism hump; model engraved on the prism/front/top plate, lens name on the front ring: Minolta SRT/X-700/XD, Canon AE-1/A-1/F-1, Nikon F/F2/FM/FE, Pentax K1000/Spotmatic, Olympus OM-1/OM-2. 1985+ autofocus plastic bodies (Minolta Maxxum 7000/5000, Canon EOS 650, Nikon N-series) are near-worthless — the LENSES are the value. Minolta AF 70-210 f/4 "beercan" (fat black barrel, "MACRO" ring) and AF 50 f/1.7 still sell because they fit Sony A-mount. Sellers write "old 35mm camera", "vintage Minolta/Canon", "film camera with lens".
Model tells:
- **Pentax Spotmatic** (SP/SPII/SPF, "Asahi Pentax" on prism, M42 screw lens, 1964–76); **Pentax K1000** (K-mount, 1976–97; the student camera, plain chrome, holds value).
- **Olympus OM-1/OM-2/OM-4**: small, shutter-speed ring around the lens mount.
- **Canon AE-1 / AE-1 Program / A-1 / F-1**: black-and-chrome, FD lenses; black top plate with "Program" = the better AE-1.
- **Minolta SRT-101 / X-700 / XD-11**; **Nikon FM/FE/FM2** (top of the consumer tier).
- **Yashica FX-3 Super 2000**: plastic-top Contax/Yashica-mount body – valued for the mount, not itself.
- **Ihagee Exakta VX / Varex (Dresden)**: trapezoid body, left-hand shutter release, waist-level or prism finder.
- **Kodak Retina Reflex S / III / IV**: leaf-shutter SLR with Schneider Xenon 50/1.9; shutters often stuck.
- **Nikkorex F / Nippon Kogaku**: Nikon's budget SLR.
Value tells: light seals, mirror desilvering ("flaking mirror" = pass), "shutter squeak" on AE-1s (cheap fix but Dylan's rule says pass), winder/motor drive adds little.
See also the shorter sibling entry `film-cameras` (overlapping scope; cine and compacts).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
All-metal mechanical bodies (Spotmatic, K1000, OM-1, F/F2, FM2) work without batteries; "meter doesn't work" is normal on Spotmatics/OM-1s (mercury batteries) and buyers accept it. Electronic bodies (OM-2, A-1, AE-1, X-700) must work. Fast normal lenses (f/1.4, f/1.2, f/2 Zeiss) and named glass (Biotar, Pancolar, Nikkor-S 1.4, Summicron, Rokkor 58/1.2) are the money. Plastic-top bodies (FX-3, Maxxum, Rebel) are low value.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Sticky shutters, stuck leaf shutters (Retina), mirror desilvering, degraded light seals and mirror foam (cheap but count as repair under Dylan's rules), AE-1 shutter squeak, lens fungus/haze (= pass), dead meters (acceptable on mechanical bodies). "Untested" is normal on Craigslist but caps resale at ~60%, and halves it per the sibling entry; mechanical shutters are acceptable untested, electronic bodies are not. Lots of "3 bodies for parts" are only worth it if shutters fire.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake Leicas (Soviet FED/Zorki bodies re-engraved) are common – authenticate serials (general knowledge, verify). Otherwise little faking.

## Market
Film-camera boom since ~2018; strong sell-through on eBay for tested bodies and fast primes. KEH/MPB buy outright at ~60% of retail (fast, no fees). Common bodies (AE-1, K1000) are widely known, so Craigslist sellers price at eBay level.

## What sellers usually miss
The lens, not the body: lens model (fast primes) is often worth more than the body – "Old Minolta camera $30" with a 58/1.2 or 85/1.7 Rokkor attached; "Nikon camera with lenses $100" including a 105/2.5 or 28/2.8 AIS; Nikkor primes, Rokkor 58/1.2, Canon FD 85/1.8, Biotar, Pancolar, Nikkor-S 1.4, Summicron. Point-and-shoot cult models (mju-II, Yashica T4, Contax T2) listed as "small film camera"; camera bags/lots with one thrown in. Leica/Contax/Hasselblad names. Medium format listed as "old camera" (TLRs, Mamiya). The Program vs plain AE-1 difference. "Extras" bags sometimes hide a good prime. "Three cameras $20 each" lots. "Meter doesn't work" is normal and accepted. "Untested" is normal; light seals and foam are cheap but count as repair for Dylan's rules.

## Shipping / handling
Body + lens 1–3 lb boxed, $10–18 each way (padded); medium format $20–30. eBay ~13%; strong sell-through. Resell with a shutter-fires video. Must be film/shutter tested for electronic bodies (electronics rule). KEH/MPB buy-back is the fast exit.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Body and lens model and serials, shutter fires at all speeds (video), meter status, light seals, mirror condition, glass clear (no fungus/haze/separation), film-tested or not, accessories. Photograph top plate engraving, lens front ring, glass with a light behind it, mirror and film chamber. Title: "[Brand] [Model] 35mm film SLR + [lens] f/x, shutter tested".

## Buy rule
Buy when (any of):
- a named fast lens (Zeiss Biotar/Pancolar, Nikkor 1.4, Takumar 50/1.4, Zuiko 50/1.4) or a collectible body is visible and the lot/kit price ≤ $60, and the seller will ship;
- tested body + prime ≤ $40, or a bag of gear with a visible premium prime ≤ $60;
- a working OM-1/K1000/F-1 under $40; a lot of 3+ bodies with shutters firing under $15/body; any fast prime (f/1.4 or faster) under $30;
- cult compact or pro body ≤ 35% of sold; cult point & shoots under $100; beercan + body ≤ $25; medium format under $200.
Pass when: AF plastic SLRs, disc/110, Polaroid 600 at any price > $10; anything "doesn't fire"; "untested"/"as is" bodies over $40 and untested electronic bodies; "sticky"; mirror/seal issues; any fungus/haze visible; single common bodies (SRT, AE-1, K1000) at $70+ (or seller already at $75+ / eBay level); Kodak folders; pickup-only; bodies without lenses; point-and-shoots not on the stop list. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Fits a passenger seat.
