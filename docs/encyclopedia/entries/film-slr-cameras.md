# 35 mm film SLRs & lenses (Minolta, Canon, Nikon, Pentax, Olympus)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: craigslist 1910 photos (Minolta Maxxum 7000 + AF 70-210 f/4), 1522 (Nikon D80), 1008 (Polaroid 100); KEH / eBay sold pattern (knowledge 2024–25)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Metal-bodied manual SLRs 1960s–80s: Minolta SRT/X-700/XD, Canon AE-1/A-1/F-1, Nikon F/F2/FM/FE, Pentax K1000/Spotmatic, Olympus OM-1/OM-2. 1985+ autofocus plastic bodies (Minolta Maxxum 7000/5000, Canon EOS 650, Nikon N-series) are near-worthless — the LENSES are the value. Minolta AF 70-210 f/4 "beercan" (fat black barrel, "MACRO" ring) and AF 50 f/1.7 still sell because they fit Sony A-mount. Sellers write "old 35mm camera", "vintage Minolta/Canon", "film camera with lens".

## What sellers usually miss
Lens model (fast primes), point-and-shoot cult models (Mju-II, Yashica T4, Contax T2) listed as "small film camera", Leica/Contax/Hasselblad names. "Untested" is normal; light seals and foam are cheap but count as repair for Dylan's rules.

## Shipping / handling
2–3 lb, $12–15 each way. eBay or KEH buy-back. Fees 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: cult compact or pro body named above ≤ 35% of sold; beercan + body ≤ $25. Pass when: AF plastic SLRs, disc/110, Polaroid 600 at any price > $10; anything "doesn't fire". Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

## Additional research (earlier entry: Film SLR cameras, 1970s–90s (Canon AE-1, Pentax K1000, Yashica FX-3, Minolta X-700))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay/KEH used price browse Oct 2025; sweep listings 2509, 2623, 2631, 2609

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Chrome-or-black 35mm SLR with a pentaprism hump. Models that still sell: Canon AE-1 / AE-1 Program / A-1 (black top plate with "Program" = the better one), Pentax K1000 (student classic, plain chrome), Minolta X-700 / SRT-101, Olympus OM-1/OM-2, Nikon FM/FE/FM2 (top of this tier). Yashica FX-3 Super 2000 is a plastic-top Contax/Yashica-mount body – valued for the mount, not itself. Value tells: light seals, mirror desilvering ("flaking mirror" = pass), "shutter squeak" on AE-1s (cheap fix but Dylan's rule says pass), winder/motor drive adds little.

## Additional research (earlier entry: 35mm film SLRs – consumer/enthusiast bodies (Pentax, Olympus, Canon, Minolta))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge, KEH used price knowledge, gearfocus sold listings

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1960s–90s manual-focus SLRs. Tells: **Pentax Spotmatic** (SP/SPII/SPF, "Asahi Pentax" on prism, M42 screw lens, 1964–76), **Pentax K1000** (K-mount, 1976–97; the student camera, holds value), **Olympus OM-1/OM-2/OM-4** (small, shutter speed ring around the lens mount), **Canon AE-1 / AE-1 Program / A-1 / F-1** (black-and-chrome, FD lenses), **Minolta SRT-101 / X-700 / XD-11**, **Nikon FM/FE/FM2**. Lenses matter as much as bodies: fast 50mm f/1.4s, 85mm, 135mm f/2.8 primes from Takumar, Zuiko, FD, Rokkor are each $40–200. Vivitar/Sears/Kalimar zooms are $5–20.

### What sellers usually miss
That the fast prime on the body is worth more than the body; that "meter doesn't work" is normal (mercury batteries) and buyers accept it. Lots of "3 bodies for parts" are only worth it if shutters fire.

### Shipping / handling
1–2 lb each, $10–15. eBay 13%. Mechanical shutters are acceptable "untested"; electronic bodies (OM-2, A-1, X-700) must work.

### Buy rule
Buy when: a working OM-1/K1000/F-1 under $40; a lot of 3+ bodies with shutters firing under $15/body; any fast prime (f/1.4 or faster) under $30. Pass when: priced at eBay level, "sticky", electronic bodies untested. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

## Additional research (earlier entry: Film SLR & rangefinder cameras (1950s–80s))
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay listings Oct 2026, 35mmc.com (Exakta VX, 2023), agent knowledge

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Chrome-and-leatherette 35 mm bodies. Name is engraved on the prism/top plate. Value is mostly in the **lens** and a few cult bodies; most bodies are $20–60.
- **Ihagee Exakta VX / Varex (Dresden)**: trapezoid body, left-hand shutter release, waist-level or prism finder. Body only $40–90; with Zeiss Biotar 58/2 or Pancolar $150–300 (the lens is the money).
- **Kodak Retina Reflex S / III / IV**: leaf-shutter SLR with Schneider Xenon 50/1.9; $40–90 working; shutters often stuck.
- **Nikkorex F / Nippon Kogaku**: Nikon's budget SLR; $50–120 body; Nikkor-S 50/1.4 adds $60–100.
- Stop for: Nikon F/F2/F3 ($150–500), Leica M (anything, $700+), Contax/Zeiss Ikon, Olympus OM-1/OM-2 ($80–200), Pentax LX, Canon F-1, Hasselblad/Rolleiflex ($400–2,000), Minolta CLE.
- Pass: Pentax K1000 ($60–100 but everyone knows), Canon AE-1 ($50–100), Minolta Maxxum 7000 ($20–40), any point-and-shoot except Contax T2/T3, Olympus mju-II, Yashica T4 ($150–400).

### What sellers usually miss
