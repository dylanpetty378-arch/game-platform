# Beer / soda neon signs and neon clocks
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: knowledge of eBay sold ranges (Coors, Budweiser, Miller 1990s–2000s bar neons; Cheerwine/Coca-Cola neon clocks); no exact dated comp pulled this run

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Real glass-tube neon on a metal or acrylic backer, with a transformer brick on the cord – distinct from LED "neon-look" rope signs (flat, uniform glow, no visible glass bends). Brewery promo signs (Coors, Bud, Miller, Pabst, Shiner, Lone Star) from the 1980s–2000s are the bulk of what shows up. Neon wall clocks (Cheerwine, Coke, Route 66) are mostly 1990s–2010s licensed reproductions.

## What sellers usually miss
The brand and the fact it's real neon. Rarely an arbitrage item – sellers see "Coors neon" value. Only worth it when the sign is a scarce regional brand under $40.

## Shipping / handling
Fragile glass – shipping neon is the deal-killer ($40–70 with proper boxing, high breakage). Treat as local-only unless profit is $150+.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 1970s-or-earlier or scarce-regional neon under $40 and the sign is local. Pass when: common 1990s brewery neon over $40, any neon that must ship. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
