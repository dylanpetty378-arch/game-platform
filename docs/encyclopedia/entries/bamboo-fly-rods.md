# Bamboo (split-cane) fly rods
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: midcurrent.com "Are my old bamboo rods worth anything" (AMFF maker tiers), appraisily bamboo rod value guides; eBay sold pattern (knowledge 2024–25)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Six-strip hexagonal cane, honey/brown colour, 2–3 sections usually with a SPARE TIP (3 pcs + extra tip = quality rod), nickel-silver ferrules, cloth bag and aluminium tube. Maker is stamped/inked on the flat above the cork grip or on the reel-seat hardware; decals near the grip. Sellers write "old bamboo fishing pole", "split cane rod", "antique fly rod", often with the tube.

## What sellers usually miss
The maker stamp on the butt flat; 3/2 configuration; length (≤ 8 ft brings more); original tube and bag. "Needs new wraps"/refinished halves value.

## Shipping / handling
Tube ~4 ft, 3–5 lb; USPS/UPS oversize $25–40 each way. Sell on eBay or Classic Fly Rod Forum classifieds.

## Buy rule
Buy when: a named Heddon/Granger/Phillipson/Orvis 3/2 rod w/ tube ≤ $120, or any Leonard/Payne/Thomas at any price ≤ $200. Pass when: unmarked, missing tip, set/delaminated, or seller already prices per maker. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price); always ask for a photo of the butt stamp first.

## Additional research (earlier entry: Bamboo (split-cane) fly rods)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: MidCurrent "Are my old bamboo rods worth anything", appraisily bamboo rod value guide, Classic Fly Rod Forum

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Six-strip hexagonal cane blank (look for the flats), nickel-silver ferrules, cork grip, silk wraps, usually 2–3 pieces + spare tip in a cloth bag and aluminum tube. Maker's name is stamped/written on the flat just above the grip or on the reel-seat hardware; serials on each section. Seller words: "split bamboo", "cane rod", "antique fishing pole".

### Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | Unmarked, Montague, Horrocks-Ibbotson, Shakespeare, bait-casting/boat rods, missing tips | $20–75 | most CL listings |
| Decent | South Bend, Heddon lower models, Phillipson, Montague top grades, complete with tube | $100–300 | |
| Collectible | Granger (Goodwin/Wright & McGill), Heddon #17/#20, Orvis Battenkill, Winston, Leonard 8' | $300–1,500 | shorter (≤8') worth more |
| Top | Payne, Garrison, Gillum, Dickerson, Paul Young, Leonard pre-1920 | $2,000–20,000 | Lang's Auction territory |

### Comps (sold, dated)
- appraisily tiers (estimates): Montague $50–300; South Bend $75–400; Heddon $150–600; Wright & McGill Granger $300–800; Leonard $3,000–15,000; Payne $5,000–20,000+
- MidCurrent: most old rods sell under $50; quality cane $500–3,000; rods ≤8' bring top money

### What sellers usually miss
The name on the flat above the grip. "Vintage bamboo fishing rod $60" with a Granger/Heddon/Orvis stamp is the hit. Also: a 6' two-piece "fly rod" is usually a bait-casting rod (worth less).

### Shipping / handling
Tube ~3 lb, up to 50" long – oversize but light, $25–35 in. Resell eBay (13%); photograph the stamp, ferrules and every tip.

### Buy rule
Buy when: Granger/Heddon/Phillipson/Orvis/Winston/Leonard stamp visible and price ≤ $150 with all tips. Pass when: Montague/H-I/unmarked, missing tip, refinished, or casting rod length (≤6.5').
