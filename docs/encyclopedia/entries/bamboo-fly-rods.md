## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Six-strip hexagonal cane blank (look for the flats), honey/brown colour, 2–3 sections usually with a SPARE TIP (3 pcs + extra tip = quality rod, "3/2" = 3 pieces, 2 tips), nickel-silver ferrules, cork grip, silk wraps, cloth bag and aluminium tube. Maker is stamped/inked on the flat just above the cork grip or on the reel-seat hardware; decals near the grip; serials on each section. Sellers write "old bamboo fishing pole", "split cane rod", "split bamboo", "cane rod", "antique fly rod", often with the tube. A 6' two-piece "fly rod" is usually a bait-casting rod (worth less).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Mass-produced / common**: Montague (incl. "Sunbeam", Redwing top grade), Horrocks-Ibbotson, Shakespeare, unmarked Japanese, bait-casting/boat rods. Appraisily estimate Montague $50–300; Terapeak Montague median $90 (n=49).
- **Decent**: South Bend (290/359; appraisily $75–400), lower-model Heddon.
- **Collectible**: Heddon (#10, #14, #17 "Black Beauty", #20; appraisily $150–600), Granger (Goodwin Granger: Victory/Special/Aristocrat; Wright & McGill Granger, appraisily $300–800), Phillipson, Orvis Battenkill, Winston.
- **Top**: Leonard (appraisily $3,000–15,000; pre-1920 highest), Payne ($5,000–20,000+), Thomas, Powell, Paul Young, Dickerson, Garrison, Gillum.
- Variant tells: shorter rods (≤ 8 ft) bring the most; 3/2 configuration; original tube and bag.
Note on tiers: the two earlier write-ups disagree on placement – one puts Phillipson in Collectible and Winston/Leonard 8' in Collectible ($300–1,500), the other puts Phillipson in Decent and Leonard in Top. The first write-up (the newer one) is used for the buy rule; both views are kept in the value ladder.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Tonkin cane, hexagonal (some quad/penta on custom rods), nickel-silver ferrules, silk wraps under varnish, cork grip, reel seat of nickel-silver or wood spacer. Quality tells: spare tip, maker stamp, fine even wraps, crisp flats, original varnish, matched serials on all sections. Cheap production: thick glossy varnish, heavy ferrules, painted-on decals only (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
"Set" (permanent bend), delamination of the glue lines, broken/short tips, loose ferrules, missing tip. "Needs new wraps"/refinished halves value. Ask for a photo of each section sighted down its length and of the butt stamp (general knowledge, verify on the sighting photo).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Re-stamped or "upgraded" rods with forged maker names are rare but possible on top makers; mismatched (married) tips from another rod are more common – check matching serials (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Classic fly-fishing collectors and anglers who fish cane; strong on eBay, Classic Fly Rod Forum classifieds, and Lang's Auction for top makers. Volume is healthy for common makers (Terapeak Montague n=49) (general knowledge for the rest, verify). Spring may be stronger (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
The maker stamp on the butt flat just above the grip; 3/2 configuration; length (≤ 8 ft brings more); original tube and bag. "Vintage bamboo fishing rod $60" with a Granger/Heddon/Orvis stamp is the hit. "Needs new wraps"/refinished halves value. A 6' two-piece "fly rod" is usually a bait-casting rod.

## Shipping / handling
Tube ~3–4 ft (up to 50"), 3–5 lb; USPS/UPS oversize, $25–40 each way. Sell on eBay (13%) or Classic Fly Rod Forum classifieds; photograph the stamp, ferrules and every tip.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State maker and model, length, line weight if marked, piece/tip count (e.g. 3/2), all tips full length, set/straightness, finish (original/refinished), wraps condition, tube and bag. Photograph the stamp, each section and tip, ferrules, reel seat, grip, tube and bag. Keywords: maker + model + "bamboo fly rod" + length + "3/2" + "split cane" + "with tube".

## Buy rule
Buy when: a named Heddon/Granger/Phillipson/Orvis/Winston/Leonard 3/2 rod with all tips and tube ≤ $120 (newer write-up; the earlier one allowed ≤ $150), or any Leonard/Payne/Thomas at any price ≤ $200. Pass when: unmarked, Montague/H-I, missing tip, set/delaminated, refinished, casting-rod length (≤ 6.5'), or seller already prices per maker. Always ask for a photo of the butt stamp first. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Must fit a passenger seat (a rod tube does).
