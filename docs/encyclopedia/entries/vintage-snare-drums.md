# Vintage snare drums (Ludwig, Slingerland, Rogers, Gretsch, Leedy, MIJ Pearl/Star/Tama)
Updated: 2026-10-28 · upgraded 2026-10-13 · Sources: reverb.com listings, vintagedrumforum.com ID threads, agent knowledge (Oct 2026)
Slug: vintage-snare-drums · Aliases: vintage snare, chrome snare, Supraphonic, Acrolite, Radio King, Dynasonic, Black Beauty, marching snare
Summary: MIJ/no-badge chrome snares $40–90; Ludwig Acrolite/Rocker, Pearl Export $100–200; Supraphonic 60s–70s, Radio King, Powertone $250–600; Dynasonic, Black Beauty, pre-war Radio King, COB Supra $600–2,000.
Buy rule (short): Buy Ludwig/Slingerland/Rogers/Gretsch with badge visible at ≤ $100; pass no-badge or MIJ tapered-nut-lug snares.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
14" metal or wood shell with 8 or 10 lugs. The badge (small metal plate on the shell) and the lug shape identify the maker:
- **Ludwig**: Keystone (pre-1970) or Blue/Olive oval badge; "Imperial" bow-tie lugs. Supraphonic LM400/402 =
  chrome-over-aluminium, slight pitting = normal. Acrolite = grey/anodised aluminium, 8 lugs.
- **Slingerland**: "Sound King" / Radio King badge; Stick Saver hoops; streamlined "beavertail" lugs.
- **Rogers**: Dynasonic (brass, with snare cradle frame) or Powertone; "beavertail" lugs; script badge.
- **Gretsch**: round badge, die-cast hoops, lightning-bolt throw-off.
- **MIJ 1960s–80s** (Pearl, Star/Tama, Apollo, Stewart): tapered "lug nut" lugs, cheap throw-off, often
  chrome-over-steel. These are the "nice vintage chrome snare" on Craigslist.
Decorative/marching snares (deep shells, rope tension or rod tension, often painted) are a separate, mostly decor market.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Ludwig**: Supraphonic (LM400 5x14, LM402 6.5x14), Acrolite, Black Beauty (brass, black nickel, engraved = top),
  Jazz Festival, Super Sensitive, COB (chrome-over-brass) Supra. Badge dates: Keystone pre-1970, Blue/Olive 1970s+.
- **Slingerland**: Radio King (pre-war/solid-shell = top), Sound King, Artist.
- **Rogers**: Dynasonic (top), Powertone; Covington/Dayton badges matter (general knowledge, verify).
- **Gretsch**: round badge; wrap/finish rarity matters.
- **Leedy**, **WFL**, **Camco** (general knowledge, verify values).
- **MIJ**: Pearl, Star/Tama, Apollo, Stewart — junk tier; Pearl Export is decent.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Shell material (brass, aluminium, steel, wood ply, solid wood) drives value; chrome-over-brass and chrome-over-aluminium
are prized over chrome-over-steel. Wraps (pearl, sparkle) on wood shells: rare colours add. Original snare wires,
throw-off and heads add; date stamps inside wood shells help date them (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Pitting on chrome, rust on steel, cracked or bent hoops, stripped lugs, broken throw-off, out-of-round shells, wrap
shrinkage and cracking, redrilled holes (big value hit). Ask for photos of the badge, throw-off, inside the shell and the
bearing edge.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Ludwig has reissued Black Beauty and Supraphonic (modern ones have modern badges and serials); badge swaps onto
generic shells exist (general knowledge, verify).

- Agent knowledge 2025–26 (undated): 1960s Ludwig Supraphonic 5x14 keystone badge $350–500 Reverb; Acrolite $120–180; MIJ "Pearl" 1970s chrome $50–80.

## Market
Drummers and vintage-drum collectors; Reverb is the best venue (fees ~8%). Ludwig Supraphonic and Acrolite are
liquid; MIJ snares sell slowly and cheaply. Decorative marching snares sell to decorators at low prices
(general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
"Vintage chrome snare, great for a student" with no badge photo. Ask for a badge/throw-off photo; Supraphonic/Acrolite
badges and Imperial lugs are the tell.

## Shipping / handling
8–12 lb, needs a box ~16x16x8, $20–30. Reverb (fees ~8 %) beats eBay for drums.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** maker + model + size ("5x14", "6.5x14") + badge era ("Keystone badge", "Blue/Olive") + shell ("COB", "aluminum") + "vintage snare drum"; Junk: "chrome snare", "student drum", "great for a beginner". Example: `1960s Ludwig Supraphonic LM400 5x14 Keystone Badge Vintage Snare Drum`.
- **Where:** Reverb first (~8% fees, drummers and collectors; Supraphonic/Acrolite are liquid), eBay second; MIJ no-badge snares local (slow and cheap). Reverb beats eBay for drums.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (1960s Supraphonic keystone $350–500; Acrolite $120–180; Radio King/Powertone $250–600; MIJ $50–80), accept −10%, floor p25; OBO; cut 10% after 3 weeks.
- **Photos:** first photo the drum from the front at a slight angle, badge visible; then badge and serial macro, throw-off, inside the shell, bearing edges, hoops (bent?), each lug, wrap/chrome pitting, snare wires and bed; short video of a hit.
- **State:** maker, model, depth × diameter, shell material, badge type and serial (era), lug count, throw-off model, chrome/wrap condition, what's original vs replaced, redrilled holes, heads, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 8–12 lb, box ~16×16×8; loosen or remove snare wires, pad the hoops, fill the shell; $20–30.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Ludwig [Supraphonic LM400], [Keystone badge, serial dates it 1965] — the aluminum shell the whole genre was recorded on; original throw-off and lugs, no extra holes; a new LM400 is [$X]."

## Buy rule
Buy when: Ludwig keystone/blue-olive, Slingerland, Rogers, Gretsch badge visible and price ≤ $100. Pass when: no badge
visible or MIJ lug style (tapered nut lugs). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees (Reverb ~8%) and shipping
both ways. Must fit a passenger seat.
