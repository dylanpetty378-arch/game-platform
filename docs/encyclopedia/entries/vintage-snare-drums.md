# Vintage snare drums
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: reverb.com listings, vintagedrumforum.com ID threads, agent knowledge (Oct 2026)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
14" metal or wood shell with 8 or 10 lugs. The badge (small metal plate on the shell) and the lug shape identify the maker:
- **Ludwig**: Keystone (pre-1970) or Blue/Olive oval badge; "Imperial" bow-tie lugs. Supraphonic LM400/402 = chrome-over-aluminium, slight pitting = normal. Acrolite = grey/anodised aluminium, 8 lugs.
- **Slingerland**: "Sound King" / Radio King badge; Stick Saver hoops; streamlined "beavertail" lugs.
- **Rogers**: Dynasonic (brass, with snare cradle frame) or Powertone; "beavertail" lugs; script badge.
- **Gretsch**: round badge, die-cast hoops, lightning-bolt throw-off.
- **MIJ 1960s–80s** (Pearl, Star/Tama, Apollo, Stewart): tapered "lug nut" lugs, cheap throw-off, often chrome-over-steel. These are the "nice vintage chrome snare" on Craigslist.

## Comps (sold, dated)
- Agent knowledge 2025–26: 1960s Ludwig Supraphonic 5x14 keystone badge $350–500 Reverb; Acrolite $120–180; MIJ "Pearl" 1970s chrome $50–80.

## What sellers usually miss
"Vintage chrome snare, great for a student" with no badge photo. Ask for a badge/throw-off photo; Supraphonic/Acrolite badges and Imperial lugs are the tell.

## Shipping / handling
8–12 lb, needs a box ~16x16x8, $20–30. Reverb (fees ~8 %) beats eBay for drums.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Ludwig keystone/blue-olive, Slingerland, Rogers, Gretsch badge visible and price ≤ $100. Pass when: no badge visible or MIJ lug style (tapered nut lugs). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
