# Sports card lots & singles (craigslist pattern)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: craigslist 1099 (Ja'Marr Chase Prizm RC auto $145), 1336 ("vuntage sports cards" $130), 1359 (1990 Topps Nolan Ryan $25), 1535 (5,000 cards $30), 1896 (3,000 cards $85), 1077 (1977 Topps uncut strip $7); eBay/130point sold pattern (knowledge 2024–25)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
"Junk wax" era (1987–94 Topps/Donruss/Fleer/Upper Deck) = worthless in bulk ($10–30 per 5,000). Value needs: pre-1980 (Topps 1950s–70s stars, 1952 Topps, 1975 minis), graded cards (PSA/BGS slabs), rookie autos/parallels of current stars (Prizm Silver, numbered /25), or complete vintage sets. Sellers write "baseball cards", "card collection", "box of cards".

## What sellers usually miss
Rarely anything — card sellers on craigslist are usually collectors. Arbitrage only in estate lots where a 1960s–70s binder is listed as "old baseball cards" at $20–50.

## Shipping / handling
5,000 cards ≈ 25 lb; singles in bubble mailers $4. eBay.

## Buy rule
Buy when: visible pre-1980 stars in a lot ≤ $40, or a graded card ≤ 40% of 130point. Pass when: junk-wax bulk, "1990 Nolan Ryan $25", raw modern autos (needs auth). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
