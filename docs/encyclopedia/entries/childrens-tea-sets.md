## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Miniature tea services for children/dolls. Japan lusterware: iridescent tan/peach/blue glaze, hand-painted flowers, "Made in Japan" or "Hand Painted Japan" (1921–41) or "Made in Occupied Japan" (1945–52). Akro Agate: marbled opaque glass in bright colours, boxed sets "Little American Maid". German: thin porcelain with transfer prints, gold trim, often unmarked. Tin: lithographed metal (Ohio Art, Wolverine, Chein).

## Makers, brands and variants
Japan (Noritake child sets, generic), Occupied Japan, Akro Agate, Moderntone "Little Hostess" (Hazel-Atlas), Germany/Bavaria, Disney-licensed 1930s.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Moriage or detailed hand painting > crude floral; complete service for 6 with plates > partial.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Lustre wear, chips on lids/spouts, missing lids.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Modern Chinese lusterware-look sets; reproduction Akro boxes.
