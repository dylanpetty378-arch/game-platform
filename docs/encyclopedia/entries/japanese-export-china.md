## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Ceramics made in Japan for US import. Marks date items: "Nippon" (1891–1921), "Made in Japan"/"Japan" (1921–41, 1952–), "Made in Occupied Japan" (1945–52), paper labels for Thames, Lefton, Napco, Norcrest, Enesco (1950s–70s). Thames pieces: relief-moulded fruit with twig handles, white body, often foil/paper "Thames Hand Painted" label. Nippon: raised slip (moriage), gold beading, scenic painting.

## Makers, brands and variants
Thames, Lefton, Napco, Norcrest, Enesco, Holt Howard (US importer), UCAGCO, Noritake/Morimura, Nippon unmarked factories.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Fine hand painting vs decal; raised gold; heavy moriage. 1950s–70s giftware is earthenware with crazing.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips on lid/spout, crazing, paint wear on fruit.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fake Nippon marks (common reproductions from 1980s); compare mark guides (maple leaf, M-in-wreath).
