## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Red-and-black Snap-on cabinet on a steel cart with a green CRT screen and keypad: MT1665 Counselor (1980s), MT3000 Counselor II (1990s), MT2500 Scanner (hand-held red brick), Vantage/Vantage Pro (hand-held graphing meter). Sun 1015/1115 and Allen Smart Engine Analyzers are grey roll-around towers. Sellers write "Snap-on oscilloscope", "engine analyzer", "diagnostic machine". Bench electronics test gear (e.g. Sencore analysers) is a different type – not automotive (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Snap-on** – MT1665 Counselor (1980s cart), MT3000 Counselor II (1990s cart), MT2500 "red brick" scanner (needs vehicle cartridges/keys), Vantage/Vantage Pro graphing meters, modern Solus / Solus Edge / Verus / Zeus ($800–2,500 with current software).
- **Sun Electric** – 1015/1115 and other roll-around analysers.
- **Allen** – Smart Engine Analyzer towers.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Steel carts with CRT displays (heavy, 80–120 lb). Hand-helds: rugged plastic housings with rubber boots; value depends on included cartridges/keys/leads and software year. Model and serial on the rear plate (general knowledge, verify for hand-helds).

## Condition, damage and repairs
CRT failure, missing lead sets, outdated software, cracked screens on hand-helds, missing cartridges. Untested electronics are a gamble; old CRT units are rarely repairable economically (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
None for the cabinets. Cloned scan tools exist in the modern market (general knowledge, verify) – not relevant to vintage units.

## Market
Buyers are small repair shops, hobby mechanics and some nostalgia collectors; cabinet units sell locally. Hand-helds move on eBay. Old CRT analysers have little practical use on modern cars (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Model number is on the rear plate. Working MT3000s with the full lead set ask $500+, but these are 80–120 lb cabinets on carts: freight $200+ kills any arbitrage unless the buyer is local.

## Shipping / handling
Cabinet units: local pickup only (freight $200–350). Hand-helds (MT2500, Vantage, Solus) ship in a 5-lb box for $15–20.
