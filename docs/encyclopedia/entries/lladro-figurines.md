# Lladró / NAO porcelain figurines
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge, Lladró retired-piece dealer pricing (A Retired Collection, Replacements), Kovels

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Spanish porcelain figures in pale blue/grey/tan matte or glossy glazes, elongated faces, often with flowers. Backstamp on the base: blue "LLADRÓ" with the bellflower logo and "Made in Spain" (1974+), or impressed early marks; **NAO** (Lladró's budget line – same look, blue "NAO" mark) is worth 20–30% of a Lladró; **Daisa** on the base is the design-copyright line, not a maker. Sellers write "Lladro angel", "Spanish porcelain figurine". Value is in retired limited editions, large groups, and perfect condition – chipped flower petals or fingers kill value.

## What sellers usually miss
That NAO ≠ Lladró; that a "lot of Lladro" sometimes contains one large retired group worth the whole lot. Boxes add 20–30%.

## Shipping / handling
Fragile; double box, $20–35 insured. eBay 13%. Photograph every finger and petal before shipping.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 12"+ groups or boxed retired pieces under $100; lots under $5/piece with large pieces visible. Pass when: small angels/children over $30 each, any chips, NAO at Lladró prices. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
