## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1930s–50s California makers with marks on the base: "METLOX Poppytrail", "Metlox Made in USA" + "Des. Patent 125,xxx" (Carl Romanelli figural vases/nudes/Zodiac, 1939–42), "BAUER" (ringware in orange-red, jade, cobalt – oil jars and large bowls are the money), "Catalina Island/Catalina Pottery" (1927–37, red/brown clay, Descanso green, Toyon red), "Brayton Laguna" (figurines, Disney licence 1938–40), "Hedi Schoop", "Kay Finch" (hand-painted animals), "Franciscan" (Gladding McBean), "Pacific Pottery", "Vernon Kilns" (Rockwell Kent/Disney plates), "Roselane", "Sascha Brastoff" (signed, gold). Later Metlox dinnerware (Poppytrail, Red Rooster, California Provincial, 1950s–80s) is $5–20 a piece. Sellers' words: "vintage California pottery", "Metlox vase", "nude lady vase", "Bauer bowl".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Metlox** – Carl Romanelli figural line 1939–42 (Des. Patent numbers; Zodiac vases, nudes, "Dancer" 1941); later Poppytrail dinnerware (low).
- **Bauer** – ringware (orange-red, jade, cobalt and others), oil jars (#100 etc.), large bowls. Lookalikes: Pacific, Garden City.
- **Catalina Island** (1927–37, island clay; Descanso green, Toyon red) vs **Catalina Pottery** (Gladding McBean, post-1937) – island pieces are the valuable ones.
- **Brayton Laguna** – figurines; Disney licence 1938–40.
- **Hedi Schoop, Kay Finch, Sascha Brastoff** – signed decorative wares; Kay Finch large animals top.
- **Franciscan (Gladding McBean), Vernon Kilns, Pacific Pottery, Roselane, Maurice of California** – mostly dinnerware/decor; Vernon Kilns Rockwell Kent/Disney plates higher.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Marks on base (incised, ink stamp, or moulded); Bauer ringware has thick walls and concentric rings; Catalina Island has red/brown island clay visible at the foot; Romanelli pieces have crisp moulding and the design-patent number. Glazes: solid California colours (orange-red, jade, cobalt, Descanso green). Sharp mould detail and even glaze = early production; soft detail = later or lookalike (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips/repairs at the base halve value. Rim chips, crazing, glaze flakes, hairlines; repaired chips show as matte or colour-mismatched under UV/raking light (general knowledge, verify). Ask for base and rim close-ups.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Bauer lookalikes (Pacific, Garden City) and later Bauer reissues (the brand revived in the late 1990s) exist (general knowledge, verify). Catalina Pottery (GMB, post-1937) sold as Catalina Island. Unmarked nude vases from other makers sold as Romanelli.

## Market
Strong West Coast collector base and mid-century decorators; eBay and Etsy for small pieces, regional auction (e.g. California design sales) for large Bauer and Catalina Island (general knowledge, verify). Dinnerware is slow.

## What sellers usually miss
"Romanelli" and the design-patent number on the base; Bauer vs lookalikes (Pacific, Garden City); Catalina Island (pre-1937, island clay) vs Catalina Pottery (GMB, post-1937). Chips/repairs at the base halve value.

## Shipping / handling
2–8 lb, fragile – double-box; $15–30. eBay ~13%. Large oil jars (20"+) are heavy and need custom crating.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
State maker, mark text (including Des. Patent number), form/model, glaze colour, height, condition (chips, crazing, repairs). Photograph the base mark, all sides, rim and foot, and glaze close-ups. Keywords: maker + designer ("Romanelli") + form ("nude vase", "oil jar", "ringware") + glaze colour + "California pottery".

## Buy rule
Buy when: marked Romanelli/Catalina Island/Bauer large forms at ≤ 35% of sold; unmarked-looking "nude lady vase" at ≤ $40 (check base in a second photo). Pass when: seller names the maker and prices at ≥ 60% of sold. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Must fit a passenger seat (the largest oil jars may not – excluded if bigger).
