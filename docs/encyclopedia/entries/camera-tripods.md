# Camera tripods & gimbals (Manfrotto, Gitzo, DJI)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: agent knowledge of eBay/MPB sold bands 2025 (verify); run12 sheets 56–58

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Manfrotto/Bogen (black legs, red or silver accents; model number on the leg collar: 3001/3021/3205 aluminium, 055/190 series; carbon = 055CX/190CX/MT055CXPRO); Gitzo (grey carbon, "Gitzo" badge – the valuable ones, $300–800); Really Right Stuff (RRS, $500+); heads: Manfrotto 410 geared ($150–200), 804RC2 ($40–60), ball heads 496/498 ($40–80), Arca-Swiss ($200–400). Gimbals: DJI Ronin-SC $130–200, RS2 $250–350, RS3 $300–450.

## What sellers usually miss
Gitzo and RRS names ("carbon fiber tripod $40"). Manfrotto is always named.

## Shipping / handling
3–6 lb, long box – $20–30 each way.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Gitzo/RRS/Sachtler under $100, or Manfrotto carbon + head under $80. Pass when: aluminium Manfrotto at $40+ (6817), carbon at $199 (6892). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
