## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Manfrotto/Bogen (black legs, red or silver accents; model number on the leg collar: 3001/3021/3205 aluminium, 055/190 series; carbon = 055CX/190CX/MT055CXPRO); Gitzo (grey carbon, "Gitzo" badge – the valuable ones, $300–800); Really Right Stuff (RRS, $500+); heads: Manfrotto 410 geared ($150–200), 804RC2 ($40–60), ball heads 496/498 ($40–80), Arca-Swiss ($200–400). Gimbals: DJI Ronin-SC $130–200, RS2 $250–350, RS3 $300–450. Carbon legs show a woven or matte black fibre texture; aluminium is painted/anodised tube.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Manfrotto / Bogen** (Italy; "Bogen" was the US importer name on older units): aluminium 3001/3021/3205 legs, 055/190 series; carbon 055CX/190CX/MT055CXPRO. Heads 410 geared, 804RC2 pan-tilt, 496/498 ball.
- **Gitzo** (France, now Vitec/Manfrotto group): carbon Systematic, Mountaineer, Traveler series – the premium name.
- **Really Right Stuff (RRS)**: US-made legs and Arca-style heads, $500+.
- **Arca-Swiss** ball heads $200–400; **Sachtler / Vinten / O'Connor** video fluid heads (top tier).
- **DJI Ronin-SC / RS2 / RS3** handheld gimbals.
- Junk: Velbon, Sunpak, Ambico, Star-D, Slik budget aluminium, 1980s video tripods.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Carbon fibre (light, matte weave) vs aluminium (heavier, painted). Quality legs have metal castings at the top (spider), twist or flip locks that hold firm, spiked/rubber feet, and engraved model labels. Cheap tripods have plastic spiders, built-in non-removable heads and thin legs. Gitzo spiders carry serial numbers (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Cracked carbon tubes, stripped leg locks, missing feet, seized twist locks (sand/salt), missing quick-release plates (a missing RC2 plate costs $15–25), loose ball-head tension. Gimbals: motor faults, firmware lock, missing battery grip. Ask for a photo of all legs extended and the head locked.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit "Gitzo" and "Manfrotto" carbon tripods and clone heads are sold on AliExpress/eBay (general knowledge, verify). Check fonts, serials and casting quality.

## Market
Liquid on eBay/MPB/KEH; buyers are hobby photographers, video shooters and landscape pros. Manfrotto is everywhere and priced near market; Gitzo/RRS move fast at fair prices. Gimbals depreciate quickly as DJI releases new models (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Gitzo and RRS names ("carbon fiber tripod $40"). Manfrotto is always named.

## Shipping / handling
3–6 lb, long box – $20–30 each way. Pack heads separately or locked; protect leg tips.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** brand + legs model + head model + "carbon fiber"/"aluminum" + "tripod" + max height. Junk: "camera stand", "heavy duty", "pro". `Gitzo GT3542LS Systematic Carbon Fiber Tripod Legs 3 Series`
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers for Gitzo/RRS/Manfrotto carbon (buyers search by model); FB local for aluminium Manfrotto and video tripods (shipping eats margin). MPB buys outright.
- **Price position:** at the sold median for the model, accept −10%, floor 80%; OBO; cut 10% after 21 days; aluminium Manfrotto priced to move locally.
- **Photos:** fully extended on plain background; leg label/model plate; spider and centre column; leg locks and feet; head with plate (RC2/Arca); folded length next to a tape.
- **State:** brand, legs model, head model, material, max height, folded length, weight, load capacity, plate type, lock function, scratches/stuck sections by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** long box, 3–6 lb, $20–30 each way; lock or remove the head and pack separately, pad leg tips, no double-box needed.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "[Brand model] carbon legs with [head], new they're [$] + [$]; this set is [height] max, locks all firm, plate included — the setup landscape shooters actually use."

## Buy rule
Buy when: Gitzo/RRS/Sachtler under $100, or Manfrotto carbon + head under $80. Pass when: aluminium Manfrotto at $40+ (6817), carbon at $199 (6892). Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (studio/cine tripods that don't are excluded).
