# Manual-focus camera lenses (Nikon AI/AI-S, Vivitar, Kodak projection)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: kamerastore.com (Nikon 200mm f/4 Micro-Nikkor IF AI, €259 average-condition price, sold out, Oct 2025); kenrockwell.com 200mm f4 micro AI-S page; eBay sold knowledge for Vivitar Series 1 / Komine lenses.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
All-metal barrels with engraved focus scale and aperture ring. Nikon: "Nikkor" on the front ring, AI-S has a scalloped rabbit-ear prong and a small lug on the mount. The Micro-Nikkor 200mm f/4 IF is long and slim with a built-in tripod collar. Vivitar: "Vivitar Series 1" is the valuable line (70–210 f/3.5, 90mm f/2.5 macro, 135 f/2.3); plain "Tele Vivitar" 400mm f/5.6 preset T-mount lenses are $20 lenses. Kodak Ektanar projection lenses are near-worthless ($10). Sellers write "vintage lens", "Nikon lens", "telephoto".

## What sellers usually miss
Series 1 vs plain Vivitar; Micro vs plain Nikkor 200mm (plain 200 f/4 AI-S is $60, Micro is $250+). Fungus/haze: ask for a back-lit photo.

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb, $12–15 to ship, double-box. Resell on eBay/KEH consignment.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Nikon Micro/ED lenses ≤ 40% of kamerastore price; Vivitar Series 1 ≤ $30. Pass when: plain Tele Vivitar, projection lenses, or seller already quotes "legendary" and asks market. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
