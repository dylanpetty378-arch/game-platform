## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Electric table lamps with a glass chimney shade over a socket on a glass base. Lead crystal: very heavy, brilliant, deep sharp-edged hand cutting (wedge cuts), acid-etched signature. Pressed glass: mold seams, rounded "cuts", lighter, less sparkle, diamond-quilt or "cane" pattern formed in the mold.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Waterford (Ireland)**: etched "Waterford" or seahorse; lamps $120–400.
- **Baccarat**: $300–900.
- **Godinger, Lenox, Gorham, Marquis**: $50–150.
- **American pressed (Fostoria, Imperial, L.E. Smith, Westmoreland, Indiana)**: $20–100.
- **Generic boudoir (1950s–80s)**: $20–60/pair.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Check mold seams on shade and base; turned wooden/brass stem pieces are typical of inexpensive pressed lamps.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips on rims, cracked chimneys, old cord, missing finials.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Unmarked "Waterford style" imports; seller "Waterford" claims without etched mark.
