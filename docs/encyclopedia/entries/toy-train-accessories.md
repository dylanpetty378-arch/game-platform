# Toy train accessories (Lionel / Marx / Noma postwar O-gauge)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: binnsroad.co.uk Lionel postwar accessory listings; OGR Forum threads on 154 crossing signal and 394 beacon; eBay sold-price knowledge (2024–25).

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Tinplate and bakelite trackside pieces: silver lattice towers with red/orange top (Lionel 394/494 rotary beacon), black semaphores with red/green lenses (Lionel 151), crossbuck crossing signals on a black base (Lionel 154), gateman shed (Lionel 45/145), water tower (Lionel 138), street lamps (Lionel 64/71), Marx lithographed crossing gates and signals (thinner tin, brighter litho), Noma "Lites" electric stations in a brown Noma box. Sellers write "vintage train accessories", "Lionel pieces", "train set parts".

## What sellers usually miss
Pieces are sold by the box lot "as-is"; the 394 beacon, water towers and operating accessories inside the lot carry most of the value. Noma stations in the original box are scarce ($40–80). Lionel transformers only sell if tested (ZW/KW $80–150; 1033/1044 $15–30).

## Shipping / handling
A 25-piece lot is ~20 lb in two boxes, $40–60 to ship in; sells best split into 10–15 eBay lots (lots of listing time). Fees 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: lot price ≤ $4/piece and at least one 394 beacon / water tower / operating accessory visible, or any Lionel 313/350/97/164 under $80. Pass when: all Marx tin and plastic, or transformers only. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
