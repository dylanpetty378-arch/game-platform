## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Flat-seated riding saddle (no horn) with knee flaps, stirrup bars, and girth billets under the flap (general knowledge, verify). Types: close contact/jumping, all-purpose, dressage (long straight flaps). Maker name and model are usually stamped on the cantle or the flap near the stirrup bar; seat size (e.g. 17") and tree width (narrow/medium/wide, or cm) are often stamped under the flap or on the cantle (general knowledge, verify).

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Stübben** (Germany): Siegfried used $300–500.
- **Pessoa** (sold by Dover; made in various countries) and **Prestige** (Italy): $400–900.
- **No-name / Indian-made leather**: $50–150.
- Premium French makers (CWD, Devoucoux, Antares) and Passier (Germany) resell far higher; synthetic Wintec lower (general knowledge, verify – not in the earlier summary).
- Vintage US makers (Crosby, Hermès older pieces) vary widely (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Quality: thick supple calfskin or buffalo leather, neat even stitching, wool- or foam-flocked panels, a solid (spring) tree, maker stamp. Cheap: thin stiff leather with painted edges, crude stitching, stamped "Made in India" (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Broken or twisted tree (kills value – check by flexing pommel to cantle), worn billets, cracked dry leather, lumpy panels needing reflocking, torn knee rolls. Ask for photos of the gullet/tree area, billets and panels (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Counterfeit/badged no-name saddles with fake maker stamps exist in the low end (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Riders buy used saddles constantly via eBay, tack-shop consignment, Facebook groups and Saddlefitter-type sites; fit (seat size, tree width) decides sales (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
unknown – likely the maker/model and tree size (general knowledge, verify).

## Shipping / handling
~$40 to ship (earlier summary); saddle box ~15–20 lb (general knowledge, verify).

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Maker, model, seat size, tree width, flap length/type, panel type, condition of tree and billets, serial. Photograph the stamps, gullet, panels and billets (general knowledge, verify).

## Buy rule
Named German/French makers under $120 (shipping ~$40). Pass broken trees and no-name. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways. Fits a passenger seat.
