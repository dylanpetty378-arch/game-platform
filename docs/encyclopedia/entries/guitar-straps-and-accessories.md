## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Accessories that came with or around guitars: woven/jacquard straps, leather straps, hardshell and chipboard cases, pick tins and boxes, string envelopes, cords, tremolo arms, hang tags. Collectors want "case candy" that completes a vintage guitar.
**1970s Fender monogram strap:** black (or brown) woven strap with a repeating white "F" monogram (Fender "F" logo) pattern; leather ends stitched; vintage examples have thin, somewhat stiff weave, leather tabs with slot holes, and often a small Fender tag. Fender sells a modern reissue (Fender Monogrammed strap, ~$25–35 new) — the reissue is wider-feeling, softer polyester, with logo "Fender" printed/woven tag and modern ends; check for "Made in USA/Mexico/China" tags.
**Ace straps (1960s):** woven jacquard with bold patterns (flowers, stars, geometric, "Ventures"), leather ends with "ACE" stamp; Ace Tone/Ace Strap Co.; sold through Mosrite and Fender dealers; Keith Richards association drives reissue demand. Original Ace straps $100–300.
**Hippie/Woodstock straps:** embroidered, peace/flower motif; 1960s–70s unknown makers $20–80.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Fender**: monogram straps (1970s), woven Fender logo straps, Fender "F" pick boxes and tins, Fender picks in 1960s–70s plastic boxes ($50–200 sealed), Fender tweed (1950s), brown/white Tolex (1960s), black "Fender" Tolex with orange lining (1960s), molded black "Fender" (1970s), and SKB-made (1980s) cases.
- **Gibson**: Lifton and Stone cases (brown/pink plush), Gibson pick tins/boxes, "Gibson Sonomatic" string envelopes (NOS $10–50 each), Gibson hang tags.
- **Gretsch**: alligator-pattern straps and Gretsch "G" strap ($50–200), orange/grey cases.
- **Ace**: woven straps (see above) — reissued by D'Andrea in 2010s ($30–40 new).
- **Levy's** (Canada, from 1973): leather straps, often embossed/printed; vintage Levy's $30–100; modern Levy's are common.
- **Mosrite, Rickenbacker, Danelectro**: branded accessories small market.
- **Picks**: D'Andrea, Fender, Gibson celluloid tortoise picks; tortoiseshell picks (real) $30–100 each — CITES issues.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Vintage woven straps: cotton/nylon, tight weave, faded colors at edges, original leather ends with hand stitching, roller-buckle adjusters with patina. Reissues: polyester sheen, crisp bright colors, laser-cut ends, modern plastic or "Fender" metal logos. Cases: vintage Fender cases have specific hardware (Lifton or Fender latches, leather handle with brass "Fender" plate), lining color dates them; chipboard cases have little value except early Washburn/Martin types.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Straps: cracked/torn leather ends, fraying, stains, moth damage. Cases: broken handles/latches, mold, smell, torn Tolex, crushed chipboard. Smell (smoke/mildew) hurts sale and is hard to fix.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Fender Monogrammed strap reissued constantly; D'Andrea Ace reissue; Chinese "vintage style" hippie straps; reproduction pick tins and decals. Fake "case candy" made to complete vintage guitars exists at high end (hang tags, warranty cards). Not an authentication-heavy category at low price.

## Market
Small but steady; guitar-collector buyers on eBay and Reverb. Straps sell in weeks if correctly described as period. Reverb Price Guide has data for straps and cases.

## What sellers usually miss
- Fender, Ace or Gretsch straps lumped into "guitar strap lot".
- Old pick boxes/tins and NOS string envelopes in a case pocket.
- Original case of a cheap guitar may outvalue the guitar (e.g. 1960s Fender case).

## Shipping / handling
Straps and pick tins ship in a poly mailer, $5–6 Ground Advantage. Guitar cases are big (~42×16×6) — fits a passenger seat but ships $40–80; avoid unless high value.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Say "vintage, believed 1970s" only with tells shown (photos of both leather ends, back side, any tags). Note fraying, cracking, length, adjustment. Distinguish from current reissues explicitly.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold × 0.87 − $6 ship to buyer − $6 to Dylan − price ≥ max($50, price). A genuine 1970s Fender monogram strap at ~$90 sold → buy at ≤ $25 (profit ≈ $51 at $15). Ace 1960s straps at ≤ $60. Cases only if documented Fender/Gibson original at ≤ 25% of sold. Pass on reissues and generic lots.
