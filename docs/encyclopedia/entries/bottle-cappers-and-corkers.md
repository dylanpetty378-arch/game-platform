## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hand tools for sealing bottles: lever crown cappers (rack-and-pinion, bench or floor), vertical plunger cork presses (tap the plunger or lever to drive the cork), and Italian-style floor corkers. Home brewing and winemaking during Prohibition (1920-33) made them common.

## Makers, brands and variants
Crown Cork & Seal, Bevis, Hamilton, Wisconsin, Mason, Imperial; many are unmarked; modern Red Baron/Emily cappers (plastic/aluminum) are worthless as antiques. Italian Portuguese brass corkers decorative.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Old: cast iron with patent dates, wooden handles, rack-and-pinion gears, red or black original paint. New: aluminum, plastic bells, chrome.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Rust, missing bell/jaw, bent levers. Most still work.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Not faked; modern working tools are mislabeled "vintage".
