# Polaroid SX-70 folding cameras
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge 2025 (SX-70 Alpha 1 tested $180–280, untested $90–150, Sonar OneStep $150–250); Retrospekt/Polaroid refurbished prices ($350–450).

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Folding SLR with chrome body and tan leather (original 1972), all-chrome/black (Model 2/3 plastic bodies, less), "Alpha 1" with a tripod socket and neck-strap lugs, "Sonar OneStep" with a round gold sonar disc on top. Big Swinger 3000, Pronto, OneStep rainbow box cameras are $10–30. Sellers write "Polaroid Land Camera", "SX-70", "tested with film".

## What sellers usually miss
Model (Alpha 1 vs Model 2) and whether it's been tested; "untested" originals at $50–80 are the play – but Dylan passes on untested electronics, so buy only "tested with film".

## Shipping / handling
1.5 lb, $12 to ship, pad the leather. Resell on eBay; fees 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: SX-70 original/Alpha 1/Sonar tested ≤ $90. Pass when: untested, mirror haze, or ≥ $150. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.

## Additional research (earlier entry: Polaroid SX-70 folding cameras)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold-listing searches (SX-70 Sonar OneStep tested), retailer price knowledge (Retrospekt / Brooklyn Film Camera refurb pricing)

### What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
The flat folding SLR that opens into a wedge. Tells: chrome body with tan leather = original SX-70 (1972); black body with a big gold-mesh circle on the front = Sonar OneStep (autofocus, 1978) or Model 2/3 (fixed-focus, lower value); "SX-70 Land Camera" printed on the front plate. Sellers call it "old Polaroid", "fold-up Polaroid".

| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Top | Refurbished with warranty, Sonar in box, black "Alpha 1 SE", Model 2 "Mildred Scheel" | $300–600 | dealer-refurb prices |

### Comps (sold, dated)

- 2026 – SX-70 chrome/tan, untested, cosmetically good – $90–130 (eBay sold)

### What sellers usually miss
That the folding SLR models are worth 3–5× the plastic box OneSteps; that leather condition (no peeling) and the shutter firing with an empty pack matter. Untested examples still move because refurbishers buy them.

### Shipping / handling
<2 lb, $10–15 ground. Electronic shutter – Dylan's "no untested electronics" rule applies if buying stand-alone; acceptable as a bonus item inside a lot.

### Buy rule
Buy when: folding SX-70 (any) under $40 as part of a lot, or a Sonar OneStep under $60 described as working. Pass when: stand-alone untested over $70. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
