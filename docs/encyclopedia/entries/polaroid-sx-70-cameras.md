## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
The flat folding SLR that opens into a wedge. Folding SLR with chrome body and tan leather (original 1972), all-chrome/black (Model 2/3 plastic bodies, less), "Alpha 1" with a tripod socket and neck-strap lugs, "Sonar OneStep" with a round gold sonar disc on top. "SX-70 Land Camera" printed on the front plate. Big Swinger 3000, Pronto, OneStep rainbow box cameras are $10–30. Sellers write "Polaroid Land Camera", "SX-70", "tested with film", "old Polaroid", "fold-up Polaroid".

The earlier write-up describes the tells differently: chrome body with tan leather = original SX-70 (1972); black body with a big gold-mesh circle on the front = Sonar OneStep (autofocus, 1978) or Model 2/3 (fixed-focus, lower value). The two descriptions disagree on where the sonar disc sits ("on top" vs "on the front") and on Model 2/3 colour; see the note under Makers.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **SX-70 original** (1972): chrome with tan leather; collectible when tested ($150–280).
- **SX-70 Alpha 1** (c.1977, general knowledge, verify): adds tripod socket and strap lugs; collectible tier. **Alpha 1 SE** (Special Edition, black) top tier.
- **SX-70 Model 2**: plastic body (ivory/white per the earlier write-up); decent tier ($80–150 per the newer write-up, $40–90 per the earlier one). German "Mildred Scheel" Model 2 edition listed in top tier.
- **SX-70 Model 3**: lower-value variant.
- **SX-70 Sonar OneStep** (1978): sonar autofocus, gold sonar disc; tested $150–250 (knowledge) / $150–220 (2026 eBay sold) / Terapeak median $125 (n=41) — the Terapeak number is the most recent and measured.
- **MiNT SLR 670** (modern Hong Kong refurb/upgrade of SX-70 bodies, general knowledge, verify): top tier.
- **Polaroid 180/195** pack-film cameras: top tier (different product line).
- **Box cameras** (Big Swinger 3000, Pronto, OneStep rainbow, Sun 600, Spirit 600): $10–30.

Note on Model 2/3 (general knowledge, verify): the Model 2 is an SLR with ivory/grey plastic body and manual focus; the Model 3 is the one with a non-SLR (direct) viewfinder and zone focus. The earlier write-up's "Model 2 (white/ivory, non-SLR viewfinder)" and "Model 2/3 (fixed-focus)" appear to mix these up. Likewise the Sonar OneStep's gold sonar transducer is on the front, above the lens (general knowledge, verify), not "on top".

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Chrome-plated plastic and metal body, leather (original/Alpha 1) or leatherette (Model 2) skins, bellows, electronic shutter powered by the film pack battery. Quality tells: leather not peeling, bellows light-tight (no pinholes), mirror clean (no haze), shutter fires with an empty pack. Refurbished cameras often carry new leather in colours (Retrospekt) (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Mirror haze, peeling leather, bellows pinholes (light leaks), dirty or misaligned rollers (streaky prints), dead electronics, sonar focus failure, corroded contacts. "For parts" Sonars sell $40–70. Electronic shutter — Dylan's "no untested electronics" rule applies if buying stand-alone; acceptable as a bonus item inside a lot. Test: insert an empty film pack (its battery is often dead) or a fresh pack and fire.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No fakes. Refurbished and re-skinned cameras (Retrospekt, MiNT, Brooklyn Film Camera) are sold at dealer prices ($300–600; $350–450 refurb); modified "MiNT SLR 670" bodies are a separate higher tier (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Strong demand driven by the instant-film revival; buyers are film photographers and gift buyers; refurbishers buy untested examples, so even untested units move (general knowledge plus entry text). eBay is the main venue; refurb dealers set the ceiling. Holiday season likely lifts prices (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
Model (Alpha 1 vs Model 2) and whether it's been tested; "untested" originals at $50–80 are the play – but Dylan passes on untested electronics, so buy only "tested with film". That the folding SLR models are worth 3–5× the plastic box OneSteps; that leather condition (no peeling) and the shutter firing with an empty pack matter. Untested examples still move because refurbishers buy them.

## Shipping / handling
1.5–2 lb, $10–15 ground (one write-up: $12). Pad the leather; ship folded. Resell on eBay; fees 13%.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Polaroid SX-70" + variant (Sonar OneStep, Alpha 1, Model 2) + "Land Camera" + "folding SLR" + "tested with film" + "working". Junk: "instant camera", "OneStep" alone (box camera), "untested". Example: `Polaroid SX-70 Sonar OneStep Land Camera Tested with Film Working Leather Good`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (film-revival buyers and refurbishers; sells in days when tested); FB local does not beat it.
- **Price position:** list at the sold median (Sonar ~$125–180 tested, original/Alpha 1 $180–280 tested, untested $90–150); accept −10%, floor −20%; OBO; cut 10% after 21 days; holiday season lifts it.
- **Photos:** 1) camera open, front plate, on a plain background; then folded, leather close-ups (no peeling), mirror and viewfinder, rollers clean, bellows, a test print from it; video of the shutter firing and sonar focusing.
- **State:** exact model, tested with a film pack (print shown) or "fires with empty pack", shutter and sonar status, mirror/viewfinder clear, bellows light-tight, roller condition, leather condition, any refurbishment, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 1.5–2 lb; ship folded, leather padded, bubble in an 8×8×6 box, single box fine, 2-lb class, $10–15.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "A [Sonar OneStep / Alpha 1] folding SLR, the one worth 3–5× the plastic box OneSteps — tested with film (photo [7] is its print); tested examples sell for [$X], refurb dealers ask $300+."

## Buy rule
Two earlier rules, both kept (the newer Terapeak median of $125 for Sonars supports the stricter limits):
- Buy when: SX-70 original/Alpha 1/Sonar tested ≤ $90. Pass when: untested, mirror haze, or ≥ $150.
- Buy when: folding SX-70 (any) under $40 as part of a lot, or a Sonar OneStep under $60 described as working. Pass when: stand-alone untested over $70.
Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (it does).
