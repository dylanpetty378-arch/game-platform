## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Hollow rubber or vinyl figures with a squeaker in the base, 1940s–70s. Marks moulded on the base: "Sun Rubber Co. Barberton O.", "Rempel Akron", "Edward Mobley Arrow Rubber", "Sanitoy". Later Hong Kong/Taiwan vinyl and hard-plastic accordion-body "squeeze" toys (bellows torso) are common.

## Makers, brands and variants
Sun Rubber (Barberton OH), Rempel (Akron), Arrow/Edward Mobley, Sanitoy, Oak Rubber, Irwin, Knickerbocker, Hong Kong vinyl.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Early rubber hardens and cracks; vinyl stays soft. Original paint vs repainted.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Hardened/cracked rubber, missing squeakers, paint loss.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
"Repro-1955 Sun Rubber" items exist (Terapeak shows one at $10.59, Feb 2026).
