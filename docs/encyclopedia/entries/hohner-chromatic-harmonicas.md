# Hohner chromatic harmonicas (Chromonica 270/280, CX-12) and holders
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay/Reverb browse Oct 2025 (Hohner 280C 64, 270 Super Chromonica); sweep listings 2878, 2024

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Chromatic = slide button on the right end. Hohner 270 Super Chromonica (12-hole, 3 octaves, wood comb) and 280C "64 Chromonica" (16-hole, 4 octaves, the Stevie Wonder model) are the pro standards; CX-12 is the modern plastic-shell version. Pre-1960s German ones with "Made in Germany" and the old box are collectible. Diatonic "Marine Band"/"Special 20" are $20–40 used. Flex-Rack holders $10–15.

Little; "Chromonica 64" sellers have looked it up. Estate "box of harmonicas" lots are where a 280 hides.

## Shipping / handling
Under 1 lb, $6–8. eBay 13%. Used mouth instruments sell slower.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 280C ≤ $40, 270 ≤ $25, or a lot with several Hohner chromatics ≤ $10 each. Pass when: seller names the model at ≥ $90. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule).
