# Collector Barbies (Holiday, Hollywood Legends, I Love Lucy, Marilyn)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge 2024–25 (Holiday Barbie 1988 NRFB $150–300, 1990s $20–40; Hollywood Legends Marilyn 1997 $40–70; I Love Lucy "Job Switching" 1999 $25–40; Silkstone BFMC $150–400); craigslist listings this sweep.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Boxed 1990s–2000s Mattel collector lines in pink-and-white or black boxes: Happy Holidays (1988–98; only the 1988 first issue and 1989 are worth real money), Hollywood Legends (Marilyn, Scarlett, Dorothy – $40–80), Timeless Treasures I Love Lucy episodes ($25–50), Bob Mackie ($80–250), Silkstone Barbie Fashion Model Collection (2000+, black box – $150–500), Dolls of the World ($15–30). Vintage 1959–66 ponytails/bubblecuts in stripe swimsuit: $100–2,000+ (loose). Sellers write "Barbie collectors", "NRFB", "in box", "Holiday Barbie".

## What sellers usually miss
Silkstone (black box, hard-body) vs ordinary collector lines; 1988 vs later Holiday. "Big lot of Barbies" from the 1990s is $2–5 a doll.

## Shipping / handling
Boxed doll 2 lb, $12–15; keep boxes crisp. Resell on eBay; fees 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Silkstone BFMC ≤ $50, Bob Mackie ≤ $40, 1988 Holiday NRFB ≤ $60, any 1959–66 vintage ≤ $100. Pass when: 1990s Holiday, Hollywood Legends over $25, lots of played-with 1990s dolls. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after fees and shipping both ways.
