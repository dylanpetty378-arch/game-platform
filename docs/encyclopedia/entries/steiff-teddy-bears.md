# Steiff teddy bears and animals (Margarete Steiff GmbH, Giengen, Germany)
Updated: 2026-10-28 · upgraded 2026-10-13 · Sources: eBay sold listings (modern Steiff plush bears $30–80; 1950s mohair Original Teddy $200–600), Steiff collector guides (button/tag chronology), Christie's/Morphy Steiff results
Slug: steiff-teddy-bears · Aliases: Steiff bear, button in ear, Knopf im Ohr, German teddy, mohair bear, Steiff animal
Summary: modern plush $20–60; 1970s–80s mohair $60–150; 1950s–60s mohair Original Teddy and white-tag limited replicas $200–600; pre-1930 bears and rare pieces $1,000–20,000+.
Buy rule (short): Mohair, felt paws, raised-script or earlier button ≤ $60, or boxed white-tag limited edition ≤ $50; pass synthetic yellow-tag plush over $15.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
- "Knopf im Ohr": a metal button in the left ear, usually with a yellow cloth tag (standard line) or a white one (limited edition). Post-1950s pieces also have a chest tag.
- Button chronology (era tells):
  - elephant button: 1904–05
  - blank button: 1905–09
  - "Steiff" with underscored final F (FF underscored): 1905–1950s
  - raised script: 1952–70s
  - incised script / brass: 1970s–80s
  - gold brass "Steiff" on a lentil button: 1986+
- Mohair with felt paws, glass eyes, long arms, a humped back and a growler = pre-1960s and valuable.
- Modern (1980s+) plush or synthetic-fur "cuddly" bears (Fynn, Lotte, Jimmy, Soft Cuddly Friends) are retail toys worth $30–80 used.
- Limited-edition replicas (white tag, numbered certificate, boxed) sell for $100–400.
- Seller words: "Steiff bear", "button in ear", "German teddy".

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Original Teddy** (mohair, jointed, many sizes and colours: gold, caramel, white, cinnamon): the core collectible line.
- **Zotty** (shaggy-tipped mohair, open mouth, 1950s+), **Jackie** (1953 jubilee bear), animals such as Jocko the monkey, Cosy-series dralon animals, hedgehog "Mecki", Peter Rabbit, Mickey Mouse (general knowledge, verify).
- **Limited-edition replicas** (1980s+): white tag, numbered certificate, often boxed.
- **Rare colours** (black 1912 "mourning" bears, apricot, blue) and very large shop-display "studio" pieces are top tier.
- Look-alike German makers (Hermann, Schuco, Clemens, Grisly) are collectible but worth less (general knowledge, verify).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
- Mohair: a woven backing with an upright, slightly coarse pile; wear shows as bald patches over the backing. Synthetic plush: soft, shiny, dense, often polyester.
- Felt paw pads; glass or early boot-button eyes; excelsior (wood-wool) stuffing that crunches; growler or squeaker voice box; five-way jointing.
- Steiff stitching: a "Steiff nose" stitched vertically on early bears (general knowledge, verify).
- Old vs new: a faded tag or no tag, button style, the weight of the stuffing, and the wear pattern.

## Condition, damage and repairs
- Moth damage, bald mohair, replaced paw pads, re-stitched noses, lost buttons, and dead or missing growlers.
- Restoration lowers value but is common on early bears; ask whether the pads and eyes are original.
- A missing button or tag makes identification harder and lowers value, but construction can still identify a Steiff (general knowledge, verify).

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
- Fake buttons are added to non-Steiff bears; check that the button era matches the construction.
- Steiff's own replicas (white tag) are legitimate reissues of early designs. They are not antiques, so don't pay antique prices.
- Chinese "artist" bears with stuck-on buttons appear on eBay (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Steady eBay demand from European, US and Japanese collectors. Early bears go to Christie's, Morphy and Teddy Dorado-type auctions. The limited-replica market softened after the 2000s peak. Q4 gift season helps modern plush (general knowledge, verify).

## What sellers usually miss
- Mohair vs plush, the button type, and the white tag.
- A modern plush bear called "Steiff from Germany" and priced at $50 is retail, not a find.

## Shipping / handling
- 1–2 lb, $10–15. eBay ~13%. Ship in a box, not a bag, so the fur and nose aren't crushed.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
- **Title words:** "Steiff" + "mohair" + "teddy bear"/animal name + size in cm + button type ("raised script button") + decade + "growler" or "white tag limited edition replica"; Junk: "plush", "stuffed animal", "cute", "Steiff from Germany" alone. Example: `Steiff Mohair Original Teddy Bear 35cm Raised Script Button 1950s Growler Works`.
- **Where:** eBay BIN with offers (European, US and Japanese collectors); pre-1930 bears to Christie's/Morphy/Teddy Dorado auction; modern plush local or Q4 eBay.
- **Price position:** at the sold median (1950s mohair 35cm $250–500; boxed white-tag replica $120–350; modern plush $30–70), accept −15%, floor p25; OBO; cut 10% after 3 weeks (replica market is soft).
- **Photos:** first photo the bear seated, face on; then ear button macro, tag/chest tag, paw pads, face and nose stitching, back seam, joints, bald/moth spots, box and certificate for replicas.
- **State:** height in cm, material (mohair/alpaca/plush), button style, tag colour and article number, growler working, jointing, pads original/replaced, repairs, flaws by photo number.
- **Pack / haul:** 1–2 lb; box, never a bag — fur and nose crush; tissue around the face; $10–15.
- **Quote to a buyer:** "Steiff [Original Teddy], [35] cm mohair with the [raised script] button — [1950s] Giengen production, not a replica; growler works and the pads are original."

## Buy rule
Buy when: mohair (visible nap), felt paws, raised-script or earlier button, ≤ $60; or a boxed white-tag limited edition ≤ $50.
Pass when: synthetic plush with a yellow tag at any price over $15.
Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat.
