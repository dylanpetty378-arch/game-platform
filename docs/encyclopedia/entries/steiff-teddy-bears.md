# Steiff teddy bears and animals
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold listings (modern Steiff plush bears $30–80; 1950s mohair Original Teddy $200–600), Steiff collector guides (button/tag chronology), Christie's/Morphy Steiff results

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
"Knopf im Ohr" – a metal button in the left ear, usually with a yellow (standard line) or white (limited edition) cloth tag; a chest tag on post-1950s pieces. Era tells: elephant button (1904–05), blank button (1905–09), FF underscored (1905–50s), raised script (1952–70s), incised script / brass (1970s–80s), gold brass "Steiff" on lentil button (1986+). Mohair with felt paws, glass eyes, long arms, humped back, growler = pre-1960s and valuable. Modern (1980s+) plush/synthetic-fur "cuddly" bears (Fynn, Lotte, Jimmy, Soft Cuddly Friends) are retail toys worth $30–80 used. Limited-edition replicas (white tag, numbered certificate, boxed) sell $100–400. Sellers' words: "Steiff bear", "button in ear", "German teddy".

## What sellers usually miss
Mohair vs plush, button type, and the white tag. A modern plush bear is called "Steiff from Germany" and priced $50 – that is retail, not a find.

## Shipping / handling
1–2 lb, $10–15. eBay ~13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: mohair (visible nap), felt paws, raised-script or earlier button, ≤ $60; or boxed white-tag limited edition ≤ $50. Pass when: synthetic plush with a yellow tag at any price over $15. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).
