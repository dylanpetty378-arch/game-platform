## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Anything a brewery made to advertise. Value depends on age, brand (regional and defunct beat national), graphics, and rarity.
- **Coaster sets / bar-ware:** 1970s plastic giveaways, like a bottle-shaped holder of six cork coasters (the example, Schlitz).
- **Signs:** tin, TOC, reverse-painted glass, lighted (plastic bubble, "motion" signs with rotating scenes like Hamm's bear).
- **Trays:** lithographed tin, pre-Prohibition (before 1920) trays are prized.
- **Tap knobs:** 1930s–50s enamel "ball knobs" are a strong collectible.
- **Cans:** cone-top and flat-top cans (1935–60s).

## Makers, brands and variants
National: Schlitz, Pabst, Budweiser, Miller, Hamm's, Olympia, Falstaff, Blatz, Old Style. Regional/defunct names multiply value (Grain Belt, Lucky Lager, Rainier, Iron City, Stroh's earlier, etc.). Sign makers: Price Brothers, Everbrite, Lackner (Cincinnati, motion signs), American Art Works (trays).

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Original paint, no fading, and legible maker marks. Lighted signs must work. Pre-1960 items often carry maker credit lines.

## Condition, damage and repairs
Fading, rust, scratches, cracked plastic, non-working lights or motion. Repaints and reproductions reduce value heavily.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
Reproduction tin signs ("Ande Rooney" and many others) are common and often sold as vintage. Reproduction trays exist. Breweries reissue retro designs.

## Market
Strong organised collector base (NABA, BCCA) for good pieces; common promos glut.

## What sellers usually miss
Regional brewery names unknown to the seller, and old ball tap knobs in mixed bar lots.

## Shipping / handling
Coaster sets: light, $8–10. Signs: oversize, expensive.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Brand, era, maker credit, dimensions, condition, working status for electricals.

## Buy rule
Profit = sold × 0.87 − shipping each way − price ≥ max($50, price). Common coaster sets never qualify. No untested lighted signs. Must fit in a passenger seat.
