## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Spode Christmas Tree (green-banded, tree with presents, England – the most common and most liquid); Waechtersbach Christmas Tree (West Germany, bright red rim, square-ish plates, red/white checker box); Lenox Holiday (ivory w/ holly, gold rim); Johnson Brothers Merry Christmas; Pfaltzgraff Winterberry; Fitz & Floyd figural. Seller words: "Christmas dishes", "holiday china set", "Xmas plates". Backstamps on the underside name the maker and pattern.

## Makers, brands and variants
- **Spode Christmas Tree** (pattern since 1938; made in England, later production moved to China/other countries after Portmeirion took over c.2009 – general knowledge, verify). England backstamps preferred. Serving pieces (tureens, cake plates, punch bowls, 3-tier servers) carry the value.
- **Waechtersbach Christmas Tree** (Germany; "W. Germany" marks are older and preferred).
- **Lenox Holiday** (US, ivory bone china with holly and gold rim; also Holiday Nouveau, Holiday Gatherings).
- **Johnson Brothers Merry Christmas / Victorian Christmas** (England).
- **Pfaltzgraff Winterberry / Christmas Heritage**, Home Trends, unbranded stoneware – junk tier.
- **Fitz & Floyd** figural holiday pieces – serving pieces/figurals sell individually.

## Materials, finishes and quality tells
Spode/Johnson Bros: transfer-printed earthenware; Lenox: bone china with gold trim (gold wears in dishwashers); Waechtersbach: glazed earthenware/stoneware. Made-in-England/Germany marks are older and better than later import marks. Gold-rim wear, utensil marks and crazing show use (general knowledge, verify).

## Condition, damage and repairs
Chips, crazing, utensil scratches, faded transfers and worn gold. Ask for backstamp photos and close-ups of rims. Sets are often incomplete – count pieces.

## Fakes, reproductions and reissues
No significant fakes; the issue is later, cheaper production of the same patterns (Spode non-England backstamps) and look-alike patterns from other makers (general knowledge, verify).

## Market
Strongly seasonal: demand peaks Oct–Dec and collapses in January. Buyers are households completing sets and replacement buyers (Replacements Ltd. sets the retail ceiling – general knowledge, verify). Heavy, so full sets are a local (FB Marketplace) market; individual serving pieces sell on eBay.

## What sellers usually miss
Brand – listed as "Christmas dishes $40". Serving pieces (Spode tureen, 3-tier server) are where the margin is; plates alone are freight-heavy.

## Shipping / handling
A 20-pc set is 20+ lb and fragile – $35–50 to ship and 30 min packing. Sell local (FB Marketplace, no fee) or part out servers on eBay. Double-box serving pieces.

## Selling it (for the buyer-facing listing)
Maker, pattern name, backstamp country, piece count and list, sizes, chips/crazing/gold wear. Photograph the backstamp, each serving piece, a stacked plate shot. List in October–November. Title: "Spode Christmas Tree [piece] England" etc.

## Buy rule
Buy when: Spode/Waechtersbach/Lenox set ≤ $25 locally, or serving pieces ≤ $15 each. Pass when: must ship a full plate set – margin is gone. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price) after ~13% fees and shipping both ways (Dylan's rule). Must fit a passenger seat (a boxed 20-pc set does).
