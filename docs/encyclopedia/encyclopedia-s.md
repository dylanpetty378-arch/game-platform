# Egyptian-revival scarab bracelets (1950s–70s)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge (gold-filled scarab link bracelets), Etsy/Ruby Lane listing ranges

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Link bracelet with 6–8 oval carved "scarab" stones in different colours (carnelian, tiger eye, dyed green/blue chalcedony, onyx, aventurine) set in prong cups. Mid-century costume jewelry sold by the million (Krementz, Van Dell, Winard, Curtis, Hong Kong). Marks on the clasp or links: "1/20 12K GF" (gold-filled – most of them), "14K" (solid gold – the money ones), "925/sterling", or nothing (gold-tone base metal). Sellers write "vintage scarab bracelet", "Egyptian revival". Real Victorian faience or hardstone scarabs set in 14K/18K are a different, rare thing.

## Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | Gold-tone base metal, glass or plastic "scarabs" | $10–25 | |
| Decent | 12K gold-filled, genuine stones, marked Krementz/Van Dell/Winard | $30–60 | most listings |
| Collectible | 14K solid gold scarab bracelets (weight 8–15 g) | $300–900 (metal value drives it) | the ones worth buying |
| Top | Antique Egyptian-revival with genuine faience scarabs in 18K, signed | $1,000+ | verify before paying |

## Comps (sold, dated)
- 2025–26 – 12K GF scarab bracelet 7", Winard – $25–45 (eBay sold)
- 2025–26 – 14K scarab bracelet, 7.5", ~10 g – $400–700 (eBay sold, tracks gold price)

## What sellers usually miss
The karat mark on the clasp tongue. A "vintage scarab bracelet $40" is only interesting if the photo shows "14K" not "1/20 12K GF".

## Shipping / handling
Letter weight, $5. eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 14K mark visible under $150. Pass when: GF or unmarked at any price above $10. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

# Ship engine-order telegraphs & nautical brass
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay/auction listings (agent knowledge Oct 2026)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Pedestal or wall "Full Ahead / Slow / Stop" dial with brass handles. **Reproductions** (India, 1980s–now, often sold as "Brelco"-style decor, lightweight brass, modern screws, printed dial, ~12" tall) sell $80–200. **Genuine** Chadburn (Liverpool), Robinson, Bendix, Henschel, A. Robinson, Siebe Gorman units are heavy (40–150 lb), have cast maker plates, mechanical chain drive, glass dials, and sell $800–3,000. Same split applies to binnacles, portholes (real = 10–20 lb cast bronze/brass with dogs), and sextants (real: Plath, Hughes, Tamaya $300–800; Indian repro $30–60).

## Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | Indian repro telegraph/sextant/compass, "Brelco" decor | $40–150 | |
| Decent | Real brass portholes, Perko lamps, WWII US Navy clocks (Chelsea/Seth Thomas) | $150–500 | |
| Collectible | Chadburn/Robinson/Henschel telegraph, Chelsea ship's bell clock, real sextant boxed | $500–2,000 | |
| Top | Named-vessel telegraphs, Kelvin & Hughes binnacle complete | $2,000–6,000 | |

## What sellers usually miss
Maker plate photo. Weight ("heavy" in ad = real). Cracked glass is minor on a genuine unit.

## Shipping / handling
Repro 10 lb ($25); real pedestal unit freight ($150+). Pickup-only on real ones.

## Buy rule
Buy when: maker plate visible (Chadburn, Robinson, Henschel, Bendix) and ≤ $200 (rare). Pass when: "vintage ships telegraph" 12" with printed dial, any Indian repro. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: collectingcostumejewelry101.com (2024 value revisit of Mayer collection), eBay/Etsy listings Oct 2026, agent knowledge

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Rhinestone/enamel brooches, sets (parure = brooch + earrings + necklace), cuff links. Value is in the **signature stamped on the back** – ask for a back photo. Sellers who list "signed Boucher" know the name but usually price off old book values, which are below 2024 market for the best makers.
Tells: Boucher = "BOUCHER" or Phrygian-cap mark + 4-digit inventory no. (post-1955); Marboux = Boucher's lower line; Sherman (Canada) = script "Sherman", Swarovski stones, japanned or rhodium; Eisenberg = "Eisenberg Original/Ice", big stones; Trifari = crown-T (Crown Trifari 1950s = the good era); HAR = oriental/genie figurals; Hobé = double-struck mark, sterling; Schiaparelli = script signature, lava/moulded glass; Juliana/DeLizza & Elster = unsigned, 5-link construction, open backs; Weiss, Lisner, Coro, Sarah Coventry, Avon, Napier, Monet = common ($10–40).

# US silver certificates & common silver coins (Morgan dollars, Mexican pesos, proof sets)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: spot silver + grey-sheet knowledge, eBay sold memory 2025

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Silver certificates: blue seal + blue serial numbers, $1 (1935, 1957 series), $5 (1934, 1953), $10 (1934, 1953). Star notes (★ in serial) and 1928/1934 $1 "Funnyback" are the premium ones. Morgan dollars: 1878–1921, 0.7734 oz silver; value = date/mint (CC mint, 1893-S, 1889-CC) + grade; graded by PCGS/NGC = trusted, ANACS/other = discount. Mexico 1947–48 5 pesos Cuauhtemoc: 30 g .900 = 0.868 oz ASW. US Mint proof sets 1980s–90s: $5–15 each (1989 ~$6). Seller words: "silver dollar", "old money", "blue seal dollar".

## Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | 1935/1957 $1 silver cert circulated; 1934 $5 cert circulated; modern proof sets | $1.50–4 ($1); $8–15 ($5); $5–15 (sets) | CL sellers routinely ask $100+ |
| Decent | Common-date Morgan VF–AU; 1948 5 pesos | ~1.0–1.3x melt (Morgan $35–50 raw; peso $30–35 at $35/oz) | |
| Collectible | Morgan MS63+ PCGS/NGC common date $80–150; star notes CU $20–60 | | |
| Top | Key dates (1893-S, 1889-CC, 1895), CC mints in MS | $500–50,000 | |

## Comps (sold, dated)
- 2025 eBay sold memory: 1935E $1 silver cert circulated $2–4; 1934D $5 cert VF $10–15; ANACS MS61 common Morgan $55–70

## What sellers usually miss
Nothing – coin sellers almost always overprice. The only arbitrage is key dates or star notes mislabelled, or bulk "old coins" lots sold by weight.

## Shipping / handling
Flat, cheap, insured. eBay 13% (coins category ~13%). Must authenticate before paying; counterfeit Morgans are rampant.

## Buy rule
Buy when: silver priced at or below melt (lots sold by weight), or a key-date/CC Morgan in a slabbed holder under grey-sheet. Pass when: single common silver cert priced over $5 or Morgan over $45 raw.

# Snap-on hand tools and tool boxes
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold knowledge, Snap-on retail catalogue pricing, vintage tool collector forums (Garage Journal)

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
The red/black mechanic's brand sold off trucks. Tells: "Snap-on" logo in the script wordmark stamped on the shank; ratchets with a flat "swivel" head and the serial code; screwdriver sets with hard clear acetate handles (red/black or coloured "Classic") – an 8–12 pc set retails $240–350; soft-grip (Instinct) sets similar. Tool boxes: red/black (or custom colour) KRA/KRL-series roll cabs with the Snap-on badge are $1,500–6,000 new; old 1950s–70s KR-series top/bottom chests $200–600 depending on condition. Sellers write "Snap-on", so the information gap is small – value comes from sets priced as a single tool, old boxes "painted black", and ratchets/sockets in "misc tool" lots.

| Collectible | Full socket sets in trays ($200–500), 1970s KR-562 chests restored, Snap-on T-handle ratcheting drivers | $200–500 | the ones worth buying |
| Top | Large KRL roll cabs, complete 3/4" and 1" drive sets, anniversary tools | $800–4,000 | verify before paying |

## Comps (sold, dated)

## What sellers usually miss
That old black-repainted boxes still bring $300+ on eBay/FB if drawers work (buyers restore); that a "misc tools" lot with a Snap-on ratchet is $60 right there.

## Shipping / handling
Screwdriver sets 3–5 lb ($15). Tool boxes are freight ($150–300) – local flips only. eBay 13%.

## Buy rule
Buy when: 12 pc screwdriver set under $60; ratchets under $25; sockets by the tray under 30% of eBay. Pass when: new-in-box sets priced at retail ($200), boxes needing freight unless local. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

Red steel top chests with a script "Snap-on" nameplate (1940s–60s have a raised script badge; later a rectangular logo). Model stamped on a plate inside (KRA-53, KRA-55, K-60 etc.). # Vintage casting reels & tackle lots (level-wind baitcasters, minnow traps)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold-price knowledge 2024–25 for Pflueger Supreme/Shakespeare/Penn/Langley reels; glass minnow trap sold ranges (Orvis, Joy, C.F. Orvis marked); fishing-collectibles (NFLCC) guides.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
1930s–70s round-sided metal casting reels with a level-wind bar across the front, knurled end plates, pearl or black handle knobs. Makers stamped on the foot or end plate: Pflueger (Supreme, Akron, Nobby), Shakespeare (Criterion, Tournament), Langley, South Bend, Penn, Heddon, Ocean City, Bronson. Great Lakes Products "Great Lakes" single-action trolling reel is a big spoked aluminium reel. Glass minnow traps: a gallon-size clear jar with a funnel neck moulded in – "Orvis" or "Joy" embossed ones bring the most. Sellers write "vintage fishing reels lot", "old reels", "antique tackle".
