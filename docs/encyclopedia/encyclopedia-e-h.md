# Electric guitar pickups (Gibson, Seymour Duncan, DiMarzio, vintage PAF)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: Reverb sold knowledge, eBay sold knowledge

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Loose humbuckers/single coils with leads. Tells: **Gibson** modern (490R/498T, Burstbucker, '57 Classic) have "Gibson" stamped on the baseplate and a sticker with the model; a used 490R/498T pair sells $120–180, Burstbucker Pro pair $150–220, '57 Classic pair $180–250. **Seymour Duncan** (JB/59/Pearly Gates, logo on the bobbin/baseplate) $50–90 each; **DiMarzio** $50–90; **EMG** active (black plastic covers) $50–80 each; **Fender** vintage-style singles $30–60 each, **Fender Custom Shop** sets $150–250. **Vintage money**: Gibson PAF (1957–62, "PATENT APPLIED FOR" sticker on the base, $3,000–8,000 each), Patent-number stickers 1962–75 ($500–1,500 each), T-Top ($400–800), 1950s Fender Strat/Tele pickups ($300–1,000). Unmarked ceramic pickups from import guitars $5–15.

## Value ladder (sold prices, not asking)
| Tier | Examples | Typical sold price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Junk / common | Import ceramic humbuckers, Squier singles | $5–20 | |
| Decent | Duncan/DiMarzio singles, EMG, Gibson 490/498 pair ($120–180) | $50–180 | most listings |
| Collectible | Gibson '57 Classic/Burstbucker sets, Fender CS sets, Lollar/Fralin/Bare Knuckle boutique | $150–350 | the ones worth buying |
| Top | PAF, Patent-number, pre-CBS Fender | $500–8,000 | verify before paying |

## Comps (sold, dated)
- 2025–26 – Gibson 490R + 498T pair, long leads – $120–170 (Reverb sold)
- 2025–26 – Seymour Duncan JB – $55–80
- 2025–26 – Gibson Patent-number humbucker, 1960s – $600–1,200

## What sellers usually miss
A "humbucker from an old Gibson" without the base photographed – ask for the baseplate (sticker/patent stamp). Modern Gibson sets are priced at market by their sellers.

## Shipping / handling
< 1 lb, $6–9. Reverb 8%. Electrically simple – a multimeter reading in the listing (7–9 kΩ for PAF-style) is a plus.

## Buy rule
Buy when: any "old Gibson pickup" with a patent-number or PAF sticker under $300; boutique sets under $80. Pass when: modern Gibson/Duncan at market. Profit must be ≥ max($50, price).

## Seen in sweeps

1940s–70s store-model gloves with a facsimile player signature stamped in the palm/pocket and a model number (Rawlings: MM, MM5, MM9, MMF, DF1, XPG; Wilson A2000 series; Spalding; MacGregor). # French gilt-bronze andirons (chenets)
Updated: 2026-10-12 · Sources: eBay sold Jul–Oct 2026 (Chrome), Run 13 Lens pass. See also hessian-soldier-andirons.

## What it is / how to recognise it in a photo
Pairs of decorative fireplace fronts in Louis XV (rococo: asymmetric C-scrolls, acanthus leaves, rocaille), Louis XVI (urns, rams' heads, laurel swags, fluted columns) or Napoleon III style, with an iron log bar bolted to the back of each. Real 19th-century ones: gilt bronze (ormolu) or gilt brass, hand-chased detail (crisp leaf veins, matte vs burnished contrast), rough sand-cast backs with file marks, verdigris/green corrosion where the gilt is worn, 11–18" tall, heavy (6–12 lb each). 20th-century repros (1950s–80s Italian/Spanish/Virginia Metalcrafters): smooth cast backs, soft detail, bright lacquered brass, often no log bars. Titles sellers use: "antique brass andirons", "French style andirons", "fireplace dogs", "fire dogs", "brass fireplace decorations".
