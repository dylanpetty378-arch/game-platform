# Golden-egg eye brief — DFW estate-sale photo galleries (in-person sales this week)

Sheets: 12 × 10 thumbnails of photos estate-sale companies posted for sales near Keller TX this week. The `$0`
label is meaningless (no prices online). Companies research and price what they recognise and often *caption* it;
the eggs are in photos of shelves, tables, walls and boxes the company did NOT single out.

A golden egg = something worth **$1,000+** that is likely priced as ordinary household goods. Look at every object
in each photo, especially in the background: paintings on walls, pottery on shelves, jewelry trays, desk tops,
garage shelves, boxes of "smalls".

Flag (0–3 per sheet): good original paintings/works on paper (confident hand, period frame, regional schools —
Texas bluebonnet/early Texas artists, Taos/Santa Fe, California), art pottery (Rookwood, Grueby, Newcomb, Van
Briggle early, studio masters), Native American pottery/baskets/textiles/old pawn silver, Asian works of art
(bronze, jade, cloisonné, scrolls, early porcelain), signed art glass (Tiffany, Steuben, Lalique, Loetz), early
silver, fine jewelry/gold watches, rare cameras/lenses, pre-1970 guitars and amps, antique scientific instruments,
militaria (no Nazi items), folk art/carvings, antiquarian books/maps, mid-century design icons (Eames, Nakashima,
Noguchi lamps — small enough to carry), rare toys/advertising.

Skip: furniture and anything big (passenger-seat rule), mass-market decor, figurines, crystal stemware, linens,
tools in common use, modern electronics.

Output, one line per flag, appended after each sheet:
`<sheet>\t<cell>\t<what it might be, the $1,000+ version, ≤ 15 words>`
