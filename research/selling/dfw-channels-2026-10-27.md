# DFW selling channels other than a plain FB Marketplace listing
Research date: 2026-10-27. Seller in Keller, TX. Items: Home Zone "Hollywood" sofa + loveseat (2019), Orian 8x11 rug, Ashley Porter TV console + tables (2013), custom pine queen platform bed, two 1960s Lane "Rhythm" walnut tall chests.

Note on sources: Several store sites (The Consignerie, Furniture Consignment Gallery, Area 25, Kaiyo, Chairish /sell, Trinity Habitat donate page, Yelp) block automated reading, so their terms are marked "not verified; call." Facebook group names could not be verified from the open web (Facebook groups aren't indexed), so none are named below as fact.

General pattern: DFW consignment runs 50/50 on 90-120-day terms with automatic markdowns. Shops want current styles in excellent condition. NE Tarrant shops mostly don't pick up for free, so the seller delivers.

Verdicts:
- Sofa + loveseat (2019 Home Zone): **Weak.** It's a budget brand, and these shops screen for "current" pieces in excellent condition. Even if accepted, a 50% markdown floor and a 50% split means you could net about 25% of the tag.
- Orian 8x11 rug: **Possible** at The Consignerie (takes rugs). Elsewhere, unknown.
- Ashley Porter console/tables (2013): **Weak.** Ashley pieces 13 years old rarely meet the "current, name brand" bar.
- Pine platform bed: **No.** It's custom and unbranded.
- Lane Rhythm chests: **Yes at Again & Again** (MCM specialist, 50/50, 120 days). **No at Furniture Consignment Gallery** (stopped taking MCM).

## 2. Mid-century dealers and online MCM platforms (for the Lane chests)

- **Mid Century Warehouse** (Franklin Park, IL) makes cash offers from photos and advertises "Free Pickup Anywhere in USA" with professional movers at its expense. Pays at pickup by Zelle, PayPal, Venmo, cash or check. Store credit is offered at a higher value. https://midcenturywarehouse.com/sell-your-mcm-mid-century-modern-furniture/
- **Chairish:** Consignor plan (1-9 listings, free) takes 40% commission on vintage. The Professional plan (10+ listings) takes 30%. Chairish arranges shipping, or the seller can opt into local pickup and delivery. Large furniture requires seller availability during business hours. Payout is at least 70% of sale price via PayPal after a 48-hour return window. https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/115006460407-Chairish-Selling-Plans-Commission-Rate-O ; https://www.chairish.com/pages/consign-with-us
- **Comparable listings (asks, not sales):** Lane Rhythm tall chests are listed on 1stDibs and Chairish. One 1stDibs example (refinished tops and sides) is from a Wilmington, DE dealer. https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/storage-case-pieces/dressers/mid-century-modern-lane-rhythm-sculptural-walnut-tall-chest/id-f_26438282 ; https://www.chairish.com/product/28800964/1960s-lane-rhythm-walnut-tall-chest-mid-century-five-drawers (prices not readable).
- **Lula B's:** 50+ booths across two locations. The Design District location carries more furniture and is "a good stop for larger mid-century modern pieces." It's a vendor mall, so the options are to rent a booth or sell to a booth dealer, not consign. https://www.dallasobserver.com/best-of-dallas/2025/shopping-and-services/best-antique-mall-23282174 ; https://thegaston.com/vintage-shopping-in-dallas
- **Kaiyo:** Serves NY, Philadelphia and DC (2022), with a West Coast/LA expansion. No Texas or Dallas pickup was found. **Not a DFW channel.** https://www.homeaccentstoday.com/e-commerce/used-furniture-marketplace-kaiyo-launches-new-instant-offer-feature ; https://www.modernretail.co/platforms/secondhand-furniture-marketplace-kaiyo-expands-to-the-west-coast/
- **AptDeco:** Says sellers can be anywhere in the contiguous US. AptDeco picks up, inspects and delivers, and the seller is paid after delivery. Listing is free; the commission isn't shown on the public pages. Its Dallas page showed 78 listings, so buyer traffic is thin. https://aptdeco.com/how-it-works ; https://www.aptdeco.com/locations/city/dallas

Verdicts: Lane chests: **best channel group.** Do three things in parallel: get a Mid Century Warehouse cash offer (free pickup), get an Again & Again consignment quote, and post a Chairish listing (40% commission, or 30% with 10+ listings). Sofas, rug, console, bed: **No.** These platforms are for designer and vintage pieces, and AptDeco's DFW traffic is minimal.

## 4. Donation with tax value

- **Habitat ReStore (national policy):** Most ReStores pick up furniture. Upholstered and leather pieces "must be free of any tears, stains or pet damage." https://www.habitat.org/stories/does-habitat-offer-furniture-donation-pickup
- **Trinity Habitat ReStores** (Tarrant): North FW, 4433 River Oaks Blvd (817-626-5000); Arlington; South FW; Cleburne. All open Tue-Sat. The site doesn't state pickup or accepted items. https://trinityhabitat.org/restore/ . A 2019 roundup listed Trinity Habitat pickup as building materials only, not furniture, so call to confirm. https://fortworth.culturemap.com/news/city-life/08-08-19-charities-fort-worth-pick-up-donations-from-home
- **Salvation Army:** Takes "chairs, couches." You book pickup by ZIP. https://fortworth.culturemap.com/news/city-life/08-08-19-charities-fort-worth-pick-up-donations-from-home . Free pickup depends on ZIP; some ZIPs get drop-off only, and you don't choose the time slot. https://lugg.com/fort-worth-tx-salvation-army-donation-pickup
- **Goodwill of Fort Worth:** Takes household furniture, with pickup limited to certain ZIPs (2019). https://fortworth.culturemap.com/news/city-life/08-08-19-charities-fort-worth-pick-up-donations-from-home . The nearest NE Tarrant store is 701 N. Industrial Blvd, Bedford. Thrift Giant (Irving) offers free pickup. https://www.olympusproperty.com/apartments/tx/fort-worth/the-davis/blog/5-places-in-northeast-fort-worth-to-donate-furniture-home-goods
- **Tarrant County Furniture Bank:** Takes new or gently used furniture. Pickup by appointment, usually Saturdays. https://fwtx.com/news/fort-worth/tarrant-county-furniture-bank-fills-new-homes-with-hope/ . Dallas Furniture Bank runs a warehouse in Carrollton (2017). https://www.fox4news.com/news/hometown-hero-dallas-furniture-bank
- **IRS rules (Pub 561):**
  - FMV is what a willing buyer and willing seller would agree on. Used household goods are "usually much lower than the price paid when new" and may have "little or no market value."
  - No deduction unless the item is in "good used condition or better."
  - Form 8283 is required for more than $500 in noncash gifts.
  - A qualified appraisal is generally needed only at $5,000 or more, or for a not-good-condition item claimed at more than $500.
  - https://www.irs.gov/publications/p561
- **2026 tax law:**
  - The new non-itemizer deduction ($1,000 single / $2,000 joint) is **cash only**. Furniture doesn't qualify.
  - Itemizers face a new floor of 0.5% of AGI.
  - So for a standard-deduction household, donated furniture is worth **$0 in tax**.
  - https://wiss.com/charitable-donation-tax-deductions-2026/

Verdicts:
- Sofas: Salvation Army or Goodwill pickup, if clean and undamaged.
- Rug, console, tables and bed: furniture bank or Salvation Army.
- Lane chests: don't donate; they have resale value.
- Tax value: real only if you itemize, and then at thrift-store FMV.

## 5. Buyer pools for cheap used furniture

- **Craigslist DFW furniture by owner** is still active. On 2026-10-27 the search returned 355 listings, about 20 of them sofas or sectionals. Most asks were $65-$375 (for example, sectionals at $200 in Fort Worth and a leather sofa + loveseat at $600 in Fort Worth). No Lane pieces were listed, so a Lane chest would face no direct competition there. https://www.craigslist.org/search/area/dallas?cat=fua&purveyor=owner
- **AptDeco:** 78 Dallas listings (thin). https://www.aptdeco.com/locations/city/dallas
- **Short-term-rental hosts, staging companies, TCU/UNT move-in, Nextdoor, OfferUp, Buy Nothing and Keller/Southlake/Roanoke FB groups:** No verifiable 2025-26 data was found on activity levels or specific DFW group names. Host-community directories exist but list national groups. https://keynest.com/blog/10-essential-facebook-groups-and-forums-every-airbnb-host-should-join . Dallas has also restricted STRs, which shrinks that buyer pool. https://www.essence.com/news/money-career/dallas-banned-airbnb-rentals/ . Treat these as low-cost cross-posts, not proven channels.

Verdicts:
- Sofas, console, bed: cross-post free to Craigslist, OfferUp and Nextdoor.
- Lane chests: Craigslist plus Chairish.
- Rug: Craigslist or Nextdoor.

## 6. Delivery as a lever

- **Lugg Dallas/Fort Worth rates:**
  - Pickup truck with 2 movers: $38 base + $2.24/mile + $1.62/minute labor, plus a booking fee.
  - Van: $68 + $3.08/mile + $2.02/minute.
  - Real Dallas couch deliveries cost $89-$140 by pickup truck and $202 by van.
  - Same-day service, often under 60 minutes.
  - https://lugg.com/dallas-tx-big-furniture-pick-up ; https://lugg.com/fort-worth-tx-salvation-army-donation-pickup
  - A 20-mile sofa run (pickup truck, about 30 minutes of labor) works out to $38 + $44.80 + $48.60, or about **$130 plus the booking fee**. That's consistent with the observed $89-$140.
- **TaskRabbit Delivery (formerly Dolly)** serves Dallas and Fort Worth with up-front pricing; prices aren't published. https://www.taskrabbit.com/delivery/cities-dallas
- **GoShare** serves the Dallas and Fort Worth metros with pickups, cargo vans and box trucks; quote only. https://goshare.co/?p=19276
- **Does "delivery available" lift sales?** No quantitative study found. Lugg's own case study is anecdotal ("within a few days, he had found a buyer"). https://lugg.com/blog/how-lugg-helped-justin-miller-sell-and-buy-vintage-furniture . The logistics friction is documented, though: a couch needs two people and a truck, and "many sales fall through when a buyer arrives alone." https://clearlist.me/blog/how-much-should-i-sell-my-couch-for
- Verdict: For the sofa set, listing it as "delivered within 20 mi for +$100," or as a delivered price with buyer-booked Lugg, removes the main reason sales fall through. At $130 it eats a large share of a $200-$400 sale, so build it into the price instead of absorbing it.

## 7. What used sofas really sell for, and why upholstery is hardest

- **Pricing rules of thumb:**
  - 30-50% of what you paid.
  - Name brands in excellent condition: 40-50%.
  - Flat-pack: 30-40%.
  - Pets or smoke: cut about 20%.
  - At the right price expect inquiries within 48 hours. A listing that sits 3 weeks is overpriced.
  - https://clearlist.me/blog/how-much-should-i-sell-my-couch-for
- **Condition bands for all furniture:** "good but visibly worn" sells at 35-50% of retail. https://reperch.com/blog/secondhand-furniture-pricing-guide-how-to-know-if-its-a-good-deal
- **Live DFW asks (not sold data):** most Craigslist sofas and sectionals are listed at $65-$375. https://www.craigslist.org/search/area/dallas?cat=fua&purveyor=owner . No public sold-price dataset for DFW sofas was found; FB and Craigslist don't publish sold data.
- **Competition from cheap new:** IKEA US sells a new MANNARP 3-seat sofa for $449, and KIVIK and UPPLAND for $899, delivered new. https://www.ikea.com/us/en/cat/fabric-sofas-10661/ . A used 2019 budget-brand sofa set has to undercut a new $449 sofa by a wide margin.
- **Why upholstery is hardest:**
  - Buyers distrust fabric that has absorbed dust, pet hair, odors and spills.
  - There's no public price index.
  - Sellers anchor to retail while buyers compare against new, delivered furniture.
  - Source: https://clearlist.me/blog/how-much-should-i-sell-my-couch-for
  - Bed bugs make buyers inspect secondhand furniture (an NPMA survey found 16% inspected furniture brought home). https://www.news-medical.net/news/20110112/Americans-taking-bed-bug-resurgence-seriously.aspx
  - Charities reject upholstery with any tears, stains or pet damage. https://www.habitat.org/stories/does-habitat-offer-furniture-donation-pickup
- Verdict: Price the sofa + loveseat as a pair for roughly $250-$400, offer delivery, and set a 2-week clock. If unsold, book a Salvation Army pickup or have it hauled off. Holding it costs more than the cash at stake.

## Summary verdicts by item
- **Lane Rhythm chests (x2):** Highest-value items. Get a Mid Century Warehouse cash offer (free US pickup), an Again & Again consignment quote (50/50, 120 days) and a Chairish listing (40%). Also cross-post to Craigslist, where no Lane competition is listed. Don't donate.
- **Sofa + loveseat:** Craigslist/OfferUp/Nextdoor cross-post with a delivered price via Lugg (about $130 for 20 miles). Fall back to Salvation Army or Goodwill pickup. Consignment is unlikely to accept it.
- **Orian 8x11 rug:** Ask The Consignerie, then Craigslist/Nextdoor, then donate.
- **Ashley Porter console + tables:** Cross-post, then a Rusty Estate Sales "Sell a Few Things" or auction lot, then furniture bank.
- **Pine platform bed:** Cross-post locally, then furniture bank (beds are a top need), then haul.
