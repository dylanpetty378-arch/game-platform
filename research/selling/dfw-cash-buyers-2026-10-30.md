## 1. Cash furniture buyers ("we buy furniture")

### We Buy Ugly Furniture (top lead)
- Phone/text: (214) 833-7182. Email: hello@webuyuglyfurniture.com. Online offer form on site.
- Site: https://webuyuglyfurniture.com/ (© 2026 footer)
- Service area: "All cities within 50 miles of Dallas." Keller, Southlake, Grapevine, Fort Worth and Weatherford are named.
- How it works: you send photos by form or text, they reply "within hours," resellable pieces get a cash offer, everything else gets a flat haul-away quote. Pickup is same-day or next-day, and their crew does the lifting.
- Won't take: mattresses (so the Murphy mattress is out), broken or hazardous items, large appliances. Bed frames and rugs aren't mentioned, so ask.
- Typical offer: no price list. One testimonial cites $200 cash for a sectional. Expect flipper pricing (~20–35% of resale).
- Caveat: no street address or independent reviews found. Confirm cash or Venmo in hand before loading.
- What to send: the whole list with photos. Ask for one cash offer for everything except the mattress, and a separate haul-away price for the mattress and anything they won't pay for.

### Cash for Couches (Addison; same operator as CouchCycled Dallas)
- Phone: 469-599-4481 (texting preferred). Address: 4650 Ratliff Ln, Addison, TX 75001.
- Form: https://cashforcouches.com/sell-your-couch/ (up to 10 photos, zip code, condition, brand, retail price, pickup timing, desired payout)
- Pays: average purchase $50–$300. Their formula is resale estimate (35–50% of retail) minus about $250 in labor and costs, then the remainder split with you. That works out to roughly 5–15% of retail. Payment is Venmo or Zelle at pickup.
- Takes: full-size couches, couch sets and sectionals that are "gently used, fairly new, and neutral in color." Blue denim may lower the offer or be declined. Items that don't qualify may be removed free.
- Speed: in-home pickup, "typically the same week."
- Service area: the site lists none; the form checks your zip. Keller is about 35 miles from Addison, so confirm.
- Sources: https://cashforcouches.com/ and the sell page above.

### Asherfield (marketplace, not a buyer)
- https://asherfield.com/sell-furniture/texas/fort-worth/ is a free listing site with 0% commission where buyers pick up and pay cash. It's low-traffic compared with FB Marketplace. Worth one free listing at most.

### Not usable

- Junk haulers (LoadUp, Junk King, 1-800-GOT-JUNK) charge you; they don't pay.

## 3. Murphy bed (frame, mechanism, mattress, no cabinet)
- No DFW Murphy-bed specialist found that buys used mechanisms. The local showrooms and installers sell new: Classy Closets Dallas (https://classyclosets.com/dallas.php), More Space Place Dallas (https://info.morespaceplace.com/dallas-wall-bed), and Breda Beds DFW assembly (https://bredabeds.com/blog/dallas-fort-worth-murphy-bed-assembly).
- The cash buyers exclude mattresses, so the Murphy mattress won't sell to #1.
- Best route: FB Marketplace or Craigslist aimed at DIY builders. Title it "Queen Murphy bed mechanism/hardware + frame + mattress, no cabinet – build your own." New hardware-only kits (e.g., Create-A-Bed queen) are a common DIY buy, so price well under new. Realistic $100–250. AptDeco lists used Murphy beds nationally (https://www.aptdeco.com/product/deluxe-cabinet-murphy-queen-bed), but only complete cabinet units.
- If nothing sells by Sunday, CouchCycled FW or a junk hauler takes it as removal only.
