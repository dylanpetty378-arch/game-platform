## 1. Upholstered furniture (sofas, sectionals, chairs)

**channel** FB local · **fee** 0% · **photo** straight-on at seat height (3–4 ft), plus 45° and side shots, daylight, no 0.5x lens · **price** 10–60% of retail by age, and upholstery is the weakest holder · **days** expect weeks to months; one couch sat 9 months · **drop** small steps; at ~30 days delete and repost instead of renewing · **don't** smoke, pet odor, any stain you don't disclose, ultra-wide lens

- **Channel.** FB Marketplace, local. Bulky goods belong local (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/how-to-sell-on-facebook), and eBay advises local pickup for sofas (https://itemvaluechecker.com/blog/how-to-sell-furniture-on-ebay).
- **Price.** Upholstered pieces sell for far below original price. A $2,000 1stDibs table goes for ~$400–$500 on Marketplace (https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/how-to-use-facebook-marketplace-37394670). Resale by age: like-new under 1 yr 40–60%; 1–3 yrs 25–40%; 3–5 yrs 15–25%; 5+ yrs 10–20% (https://spottable-lp.pages.dev/guides/furniture). By condition: like-new 70–90%, gently used 50–70%, visibly worn 35–50% (https://reperch.com/blog/secondhand-furniture-pricing-guide-how-to-know-if-its-a-good-deal). **[inference]** For sofas, use the lower age-based table. The condition table describes quality furniture in general.
- **Photos.** Shoot from 3–4 ft high, centered and straight-on, plus a 45° and a side view. Use standard photo mode, never 0.5x ultra-wide. Flash and overhead lights off; soft window light. Clear the clutter (https://www.whataroom.com/pages/how-to-photograph-a-sofa).
- **Days and drops.** One couch sat about 9 months through incremental price cuts. Deleting and reposting it as a new listing got it sold about a month later, at $100 under asking (https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/how-i-sold-my-couch-on-facebook-marketplace-37410429). A top seller also prefers delete-and-relist to Facebook's 7-day "renew" (https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/how-to-use-facebook-marketplace-37394670).
- **Stigma.** Buyers and guides warn off upholstered pieces from smoke or pet homes ("smoke smell never fully leaves") and dirty homes, because of bed bugs (https://spottable-lp.pages.dev/guides/furniture). Upholstery is hard to inspect for bed bugs, and guides tell buyers it "may not be worth the risk" (https://insectlab.russell.wisc.edu/2016/08/15/think-twice-about-bed-bugs). **[inference]** State "non-smoking, no pets" only when it's true, and show the cushions and zippers removed.

## 2. Rugs

**channel** hand-knotted or vintage: eBay or Etsy shipped; machine-made: FB local · **fee** 0% local / 13.6% eBay · **photo** whole rug flat, then pile, back and fringe close-ups · **price** machine-made under 50% of new; hand-woven holds value · **days** no sourced figure · **drop** standard (§15) · **don't** ship 9x12 and up as a parcel; undisclosed pet or smoke exposure

- **Where.** eBay, Craigslist, Etsy, FB, Mercari, AptDeco, OfferUp (https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/yes-sell-used-rug-online-225500946.html).
- **Price.** A used machine-made rug may bring "less than 50 percent" of a comparable new one, while one-of-a-kind hand-woven rugs hold value. Professional cleaning can raise the price on good rugs (https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/yes-sell-used-rug-online-225500946.html).
- **Title.** Size, style, material and colour: "8x10 Shag Faux Sheepskin White Area Rug", not "White Rug". Disclose smoke or pets and whether it has been professionally cleaned (https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/yes-sell-used-rug-online-225500946.html).
- **Shipping.** Roll pile-side out and strap at 3–4 points, then wrap in plastic and kraft paper. A 5x8 rolls to ~60–70" and 10–20 lb, about $25–$50 by UPS Ground. An 8x10 rolls to ~96–108", close to the limit. A 9x12 runs 140"+ and goes LTL freight at ~$80–$200 (https://shippinglabel.co/how-to-ship/rugs).

## 3. Case goods (dressers, consoles, tables, bookcases)

**channel** FB local; eBay shipped only for small pieces with freight under 25% of price · **fee** 0% / 13.6% · **photo** straight-on whole piece first, then sides, back, underside, hardware and grain · **price** solid wood 40–60% of retail for quality brands; IKEA/Target $20–$80 · **days** no direct figure; FB-sourced furniture "within a week" is not reliable for case goods · **drop** small steps, 5%+ to trigger alerts · **don't** freight a $200 dresser (≈$80 freight eats the margin)

- **Price.** Used quality brands bring ~40–60% of original retail. Illustrative bands: solid-wood antique dresser $200–$600; Pottery Barn/West Elm side table $80–$200; IKEA/Target/Wayfair $20–$80 (https://itemvaluechecker.com/blog/how-to-sell-furniture-on-ebay). Solid wood holds value better than particleboard (https://spottable-lp.pages.dev/guides/furniture). Brand only helps when the construction backs it up (https://reperch.com/blog/secondhand-furniture-pricing-guide-how-to-know-if-its-a-good-deal).
- **Shipping maths.** A $200 dresser with $80 freight nets about $93.50 on eBay. Ship side tables, lamps, chairs, mirrors and art; keep the big pieces local. Use 10–12 photos with measurements (https://itemvaluechecker.com/blog/how-to-sell-furniture-on-ebay).
- **Photos.** Window light in the morning or early evening, not midday sun and not flash. Lead with a straight-on shot of the whole piece, then front, back, sides and underside, plus close-ups of hardware, grain and legs. Stage lightly in context with a neutral background and no personal items (https://www.thisoldhouse.com/furniture/5-photography-tips-to-help-you-sell-your-used-furniture-online).

## 4. Mid-century / designer furniture

**channel** FB local for the first 1–2 weeks; Chairish for pieces with collector names · **fee** 0% FB / Chairish 40%, falling to 30% · **photo** maker's mark or label plus whole piece · **price** see brand bands · **days** no sourced figure · **drop** collectors aren't price-elastic, so a small drop does little; make a big one or none · **don't** call it "MCM" without a maker and line name; reproductions are common

- **Brand bands.** Broyhill Brasilia pieces $150–$900, sets $1,500–$5,000+. Lane Staccato/First Edition dressers $200–$800. Kent Coffey Perspecta $300–$900. Drexel Declaration $400–$1,500. Knoll $600–$3,000+. Eames side chairs $300–$800. Complete sets and original hardware add value (https://modcitymad.com/blogs/news/most-collectible-mid-century-modern-furniture-brands).
- **Title pattern.** "[Maker] [Line] Vintage Mid-Century Modern [piece], c. 1960s" (https://modcitymad.com/blogs/news/most-collectible-mid-century-modern-furniture-brands).
- **Chairish.** New accounts start at 40% commission and move to 30% at 10 live listings (https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/115006460407-Chairish-Selling-Plans-Commission-Rate-O). Shipping runs white-glove or freight and can take weeks (https://www.kashew.com/blog/top-10-places-to-shop-used-furniture-2025-guide). **[inference]** At 30–40% plus freight hassle, Chairish only beats FB on pieces local buyers won't pay up for.
- **Elasticity.** Collectibles aren't price-elastic "unless you make a **big** markdown" (https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Pricing-Strategy-Start-high-and-then-lower-Need-your-thoughts/m-p/34350074).

## 5. Beds and mattresses

**channel** FB local for frames · **fee** 0% · **photo** assembled frame, plus the slats and hardware bag · **price** a quality bed frame in excellent condition holds value (https://reperch.com/blog/secondhand-furniture-pricing-guide-how-to-know-if-its-a-good-deal) · **days** no source · **drop** standard · **don't** sell a used mattress as a reseller

- **Mattress rule.** Texas: "the used mattress must be cleaned and has a tag to indicate the mattress is used." Colour-coded tagging applies to businesses, not one-off individuals (https://www.nolahsleep.com/blogs/blog/is-it-legal-to-sell-a-used-mattress). The Texas bedding law is Health & Safety Code ch. 345 (https://law.justia.com/codes/texas/2009/health-and-safety-code/title-5-sanitation-and-environmental-quality/chapter-345-bedding/). **[inference]** A one-off household mattress sale is lower risk, but a repeat reseller looks like a business. Don't flip mattresses, and give away or haul off your own.
- **Bed bugs.** Mattresses carry the same inspection fear as upholstery (https://insectlab.russell.wisc.edu/2016/08/15/think-twice-about-bed-bugs).

## 6. Exercise gear

**channel** FB local (eBay as local pickup only for high-value pieces); Peloton Repowered for Pelotons · **fee** 0% / Repowered 30% · **photo** 6–10 daylight shots, powered on · **price** lightly used 60–70% of current retail; normal wear 40–55%; older 25–40% · **days** a clean, honest listing moves "within a week" · **drop** 15% at 2 weeks · **don't** ship treadmills or racks

- Channels, pricing table, the 15%-at-2-weeks rule, and a $20–$50 Venmo deposit to hold pickup (https://plategym-com.pages.dev/how-to-sell-home-gym-equipment-fast-and-for-more/). Exercise equipment is classic FB-local inventory (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/how-to-sell-on-facebook).
- Peloton Repowered pays sellers 70% and suggests a price with AI. It launched in a few cities and is rolling out nationwide (https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/peloton-launching-resale-market-used-bikes-treadmills-rcna210631).

## 7. Electronics

**channel** eBay shipped (Swappa for phones) · **fee** 13.6% + $0.40 / Swappa ~3% + PayPal · **photo** powered on with the screen lit; serial or model plate; every accessory · **price** use sold comps · **days** BIN averages ~22 days vs auction ~9 (cross-category) · **drop** 10% at 45 days, 10% more at 90 · **don't** take FB payments you haven't verified in the app; never "pay to unlock" funds

- **Swappa vs eBay.** A $500 phone netted $466.54 on Swappa vs $451.95 on eBay (2022 rates) (https://swappa.com/blog/swappa-fees/).
- **Parts / not working.** Since Oct 2025, eBay blocks remorse returns on "For Parts / Not Working" listings marked no-returns, and cuts the not-as-described claim window to 3 days with photo proof. Chargebacks still run 60–120 days (https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-for-parts-not-working-updates/).
- **FB scam pattern.** A buyer offers full price instantly and moves to text. A fake Zelle or Venmo "held funds" email then asks you to pay to unlock the money. Check the payment in the app itself, and never pay to get paid (https://www.wrdw.com/2026/08/17/what-tech-facebook-marketplace-scam-tricks-sellers-into-refunds/).
- **Markdown cadence** (used stock): 10% at 45 days, then 10% more at 90 (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/repricer-for-ebay).

## 8. Clothing, shoes, bags

**channel** eBay for brands (Poshmark/Mercari optional); FB local lots for unbranded · **fee** eBay 13.6% (bags 15%, sneakers ≥$150 at 8%) / Poshmark 20% / Mercari 10% / Depop 3.3% + $0.45 · **photo** flat lay or hanger, then tag, label and flaws · **price** ~20–30% under retail for used; undercut sold comps · **days** 1–3 weeks to 1–3 months on Poshmark (the source contradicts itself) · **drop** Poshmark: 10% Closet Clear Out drop notifies likers; offers to likers at 10%+ off · **don't** bother with unbranded singles

- **Poshmark mechanics.** A 10% price drop notifies all likers and gives $4.99 shipping for 6 hours. Offers to likers need at least 10% off. Pad the list price 5–30% for discounts. Buyers pay $7.45 shipping (https://oneshop.com/blog/price-items-poshmark).
- **Speed.** Poshmark sell time is quoted as "1–3 weeks" and "1–3 months" on the same page. A used pair listed at $50 sells far faster than at $80 (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/how-long-does-it-take-to-sell-something-on-poshmark).
- **eBay Authenticity Guarantee.** Sneakers $100+, other shoes $500+, apparel $200+ (eligible brands), handbags $500+ (https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/selling-tools/ebay-authenticity-guarantee?id=4644).
- **Local.** Branded jeans that sell for $48 on eBay draw $28 offers locally. Box unbranded tees into lots (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/how-to-sell-on-facebook).

## 9. Books and media

**channel** BookScouter for ISBN buybacks; eBay for firsts, signed and lots; FB lots locally · **fee** eBay 15.3% / Amazon 15% + $1.80 · **photo** cover, spine, copyright page, and the flaws · **price** see triage · **days** not sourced · **drop** n/a · **don't** list $2 paperbacks singly

- **Triage.** Sell on BookScouter when the top offer is over $10. Use eBay for first editions, signed copies, or recent comps of $20+. Bundle $2–3 paperbacks by genre. Donate textbooks three or more editions old. Decluttr is reported **closed**. Media Mail starts at $4.39 and does not cover video games (https://www.underpriced.app/blog/where-to-sell-used-books).

## 10. Toys / LEGO

**channel** eBay for complete or retired sets; BrickLink for minifigs and parts; FB local for bulk and fast cash · **fee** 13.6% / BrickLink 3–5% · **photo** built set, plus the instructions and box · **price** complete used sets 50–80% of original retail; bulk $5–$15/lb · **days** not sourced · **drop** list in Oct–early Nov for peak toy demand · **don't** sell bulk before pulling the minifigs

- All of the above, and "sell minifigures separately" (https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/make-money/how-to-sell-legos). BrickLink fees and the seller-account requirement (one positive rating as a buyer first) (https://brickfact.com/blog/guides/bricklink-shop-guide-step-by-step-guide).
- Bundles beat singles: one Barbie is hard to sell, ten together sell faster and earn more (https://www.mother.ly/life/tips-for-using-facebook-marketplace).
- Check recalls. FB bans recalled goods, which mostly hits baby and kids' items, and car seats have expiry dates (https://blog.vendoo.co/reseller-guide-to-facebook-marketplace-prohibited-items).

## 11. Kitchen

**channel** FB local for heavy items (KitchenAid mixers at 25+ lb); eBay for rare colours, discontinued models and complete sets · **fee** 0% / 13.6% · **photo** rating plate (model, serial, voltage), then the flaws, then a completeness list · **price** like-new 60–80% of retail, good 40–60%; Vitamix $200–$350; KitchenAid bowl-lift $200–$400; Artisan $150–$250; Le Creuset / All-Clad $50–$200 · **days** not sourced · **drop** price 10–15% under sold comps for a quick turn · **don't** list generic small appliances

- All of the above, plus the test protocols (https://www.underpriced.app/blog/kitchen-appliances-flipping-guide). Kitchen lots locally: "five kitchen items at $25" (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/how-to-sell-on-facebook).

## 12. Tools

**channel** cordless under ~30 lb: eBay by model number; over ~30 lb (table saws, compressors, chests): FB/Craigslist · **fee** 13.6% + $0.40 (charged on shipping too) · **photo** cleaned, battery charge indicator lit, all batteries and chargers shown, model number in the title · **price** bare-tool and kit are separate markets (DeWalt 20V drill kit $88 vs bare $59; Milwaukee M18 FUEL kit $164 vs bare $71); parts-only runs ~½ of a working bare tool · **days** n/a · **drop** standard · **don't** ship loose batteries outside the rules; keep each battery ≤100 Wh, at most 2 per box, ground only

- All of the above (https://www.underpriced.app/blog/where-to-sell-used-power-tools). A $45 Ryobi kit nets ~$38.48 on eBay before shipping, so sell cheap kits locally (same source).
- A whole-workshop clear-out goes as one bundled listing on Craigslist and FB (same source).

## 13. Jewelry and precious metals

**channel** scrap gold: local coin shop or an online refiner (not a pawn shop); branded or $500+ pieces: eBay with Authenticity Guarantee; vintage: Etsy or Ruby Lane · **fee** eBay 15% · **photo** hallmark or karat stamp, plus a scale reading · **price** pawn ~40–80% of melt (sources differ); "We Buy Gold" 50–70%; coin shop 85–90%; refiner 90–95%; mall diamonds 10–20% of what you paid · **days** n/a · **drop** n/a, metal prices off spot · **don't** treat an appraisal as the price

- Payout ranges and the "appraisals mean nothing" point (https://closo.co/blogs/blog/the-cold-hard-truth-about-reselling-jewelry-why-your-appraisal-value-means-nothing). Pawn 60–80%, "We Buy Gold" 50–70%, eBay fees 12–15% (https://findbullionprices.com/blog/where-to-sell-gold-silver-for-the-best-price-a-practical-guide-for-bullion-investors/).
- Sort by stamp, weigh in grams at home, and ask for the payout % and any deductions. Get a second quote (https://learn.apmex.com/selling-guide/where-to-sell-scrap-gold/).
- eBay Authenticity Guarantee covers jewelry $500+ and coins $500+ (https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/selling-tools/ebay-authenticity-guarantee?id=4644).
- Auction sold ~$24 higher than BIN on average for jewelry (vendor data) (https://uprightlabs.com/blog/the-best-reselling-methods).

## 15. Cross-platform tactics

### Markdown rhythm: "anchor then drop" vs "price low from day 1"
- **Evidence for anchoring high.** Starting high and lowering "works a lot better than starting low and raising" (https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Pricing-Strategy-Start-high-and-then-lower-Need-your-thoughts/m-p/34350074). Exercise gear: list slightly high and drop 15% at 2 weeks (https://plategym-com.pages.dev/how-to-sell-home-gym-equipment-fast-and-for-more/).
- **Evidence against.** Views spike at listing and taper, so early viewers never see the later cut (https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Pricing-Strategy-Start-high-and-then-lower-Need-your-thoughts/m-p/34350074). Price at market when many comparables exist. Price high only when yours is clearly the best one available (same thread). A sofa sat 9 months under incremental cuts (https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/how-i-sold-my-couch-on-facebook-marketplace-37410429).
- **Synthesis [inference].** Commodity goods with many comps (IKEA, mass-market sofas, kitchen, tools): price at or just under sold comps on day 1 and pad only 10–15% for haggling. Unique or collector items: anchor high, enable offers, and cut in big steps.
- **Scheduled cadence.** FB: a 5%+ price drop notifies savers, while delete-and-relist suits listings 30+ days old with low engagement. Renew works from 7 days old, up to 5 times. Don't run two copies of the same listing, and keep delete-and-relists to about 10 per hour (https://blog.vendoo.co/facebook-marketplace-delete-and-relist). eBay used stock: −10% at 45 days, −10% at 90 (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/repricer-for-ebay). Bundle at 21 days; past 90 days it's dead stock, sold in $25–$75 lots (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/how-to-sell-on-facebook). On eBay, revising a listing rather than ending and relisting can lift views (https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Pricing-Strategy-Start-high-and-then-lower-Need-your-thoughts/m-p/34350074).

### "Firm" vs "OBO"
- Use OBO on stale items (2+ weeks), uncertain value, or volume. Use firm when the item is at or below market, since OBO there invites lowballs. Don't write "OBO" in the title when the platform has an offer button. Pad 15–20% over your floor. eBay auto-accept at 85–90% of asking, auto-decline at 70%. Offers 10–15% below asking are normal; 30% below is usually ignored (https://blog.vendoo.co/what-does-obo-mean).
- Enable eBay Best Offer from day 1, because buyers who passed early never see an offer option added later (https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Pricing-Strategy-Start-high-and-then-lower-Need-your-thoughts/m-p/34350074).
- Local norm: offers come in 10–15% under asking (a $100 ask draws $85–$90) (https://justagirlandherblog.com/?p=10593). FB guides say to add 10–25% for negotiation (https://tools.oneshop.com/blog/facebook-marketplace-sellers-guide). List at round numbers, e.g. $60 expecting about $50 (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/how-to-sell-on-facebook).

### Bundling
- Bundle only things that sell as a group (toy trains, same-size clothes), never a coffee maker with encyclopedias (https://justagirlandherblog.com/?p=10593 · https://www.mother.ly/life/tips-for-using-facebook-marketplace). Lot price points: $25 for 5 kitchen items, $50–$75 for 40–50 pieces (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/how-to-sell-on-facebook). Poshmark: 10% off bundles of 3+ (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/how-long-does-it-take-to-sell-something-on-poshmark).

### Free with pickup / clearing space
- The household-clearing floor is to donate anything worth under $25 rather than list it (https://justagirlandherblog.com/?p=10593).
- When posting free upholstered items, expect bed-bug wariness (https://insectlab.russell.wisc.edu/2016/08/15/think-twice-about-bed-bugs). **[inference]** A "Free, must pick up by Saturday" post on FB or Buy Nothing clears space faster than a $10 price. I found no hard data on this.
- Set a firm time and place and don't wait on vague buyers. Keep backup buyers in your messages (https://justagirlandherblog.com/?p=10593). Take a $20–$50 deposit for large pickups (https://plategym-com.pages.dev/how-to-sell-home-gym-equipment-fast-and-for-more/).

### Phone photography of furniture (the first-photo rule)
1. **Light.** Large window, morning or early evening; no flash; overheads off (yellow cast); diffuse harsh sun with a sheer curtain (https://www.thisoldhouse.com/furniture/5-photography-tips-to-help-you-sell-your-used-furniture-online · https://www.whataroom.com/pages/how-to-photograph-a-sofa).
2. **Lens and height.** 1x lens, never 0.5x. Camera 3–4 ft high for a sofa, phone level, gridlines on, tap to lock focus and exposure, both hands or a tripod (https://www.whataroom.com/pages/how-to-photograph-a-sofa).
3. **First photo.** The whole piece, centered, filling the frame, straight-on or at 45° (https://www.thisoldhouse.com/furniture/5-photography-tips-to-help-you-sell-your-used-furniture-online · https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/listings/photo-tips). On FB, a video goes after the lead static image (https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/how-to-use-facebook-marketplace-37394670).
4. **Clutter.** Remove personal items, use a neutral backdrop, and keep props light and proportionate (https://www.thisoldhouse.com/furniture/5-photography-tips-to-help-you-sell-your-used-furniture-online).
5. **Set.** Shoot all sides, underside, labels, hardware and every flaw. Label flaws in the text ("see photo #4 for stain") (https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/how-to-use-facebook-marketplace-37394670). eBay allows 24 photos at 500px minimum (1600px recommended), with no text or watermarks (https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/listings/photo-tips). Use 10–12 photos plus measurements for furniture (https://itemvaluechecker.com/blog/how-to-sell-furniture-on-ebay).
6. **Title.** Brand, item, colour, size, condition (https://tools.oneshop.com/blog/facebook-marketplace-sellers-guide). Include dimensions and material keywords like "oak" (https://www.mother.ly/life/tips-for-using-facebook-marketplace).

### Platform bans (household goods)
- FB bans used or opened cosmetics, supplements and medicines, weapons including knives, live animals, licensed medical devices, and recalled items. Too many removals can get you permanently banned. The automated filter misfires on words like "gun" in toy listings (https://blog.vendoo.co/reseller-guide-to-facebook-marketplace-prohibited-items). The CPSC says FB's recall policy is "widely circumvented" (https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/facebook-marketplace-banned-recalled-products-amazon-walmart-ebay-cpsc).
